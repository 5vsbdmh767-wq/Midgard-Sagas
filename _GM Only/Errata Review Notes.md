Kobold Press errata dated 29 May 2025, applied on 15 Nov 2026. Every change carries a hidden `%% errata 2025-05-29 %%` note (a few small fixes like a renamed word don't).

## How this was done, and what to check

I could not download the errata page, so I read it through a web reader that only returns summaries and short quotes. Where the errata changed a word, a number, a level or a spell, the fix is exact. Where the errata replaced a whole feature, I wrote the new text from the summary, keeping every number and condition it gave. Those rewrites are marked **(rewritten)** below. If anything reads oddly at the table, compare it with koboldpress.com/errata.

Your PDFs (Player's Guide May 2024, Lineages & Heritages Nov 2024) are older than the errata, so none of these fixes were in them.

Entries I skipped because the wiki already said the same thing, or because the page isn't on the wiki, are not listed.

## Lineages & Heritages
- Ratfolk: swimming speed 15 feet (was 10).
- Shadow fey: gained Magic Ancestry and Trance.
- Shadow Court heritage: Gloam Sight now only covers dim light.
- Zobecker heritage: gained a Languages trait (Common plus two).

## Player's Guide
- Leveling Up: character level is the total of all class levels. Multiclass spell slots now count warlock at half and a spellcasting subclass (Spell Blade) at a third.
- Barbarian: Fast Movement and the Lizard aspect say "walking speed". The 14th-level table entry is Relentless Rage. Frenzy is automatic when you rage. Mindless Rage lost "while raging" and "during a Frenzy".
- Bard: Bardic Inspiration die is a d10 at 9th level (was 10th).
- Cleric: Lux Malediction wording.
- Druid: starts with a sickle (was a scimitar). Potent Forms uses the creature's own hit points.
- Fighter (Spell Blade): spells learned from 8th level on can be any school, including replacements.
- Mechanist: Shard of Creation costs 1 charge to transform. Augment lets you swap one augment per level. Metallurgist's Heavy Hitter (d8) and Juggernaut are 11th level (was 9th).
- Monk: Unarmored Movement says "walking speed". Obsidian Eyes works on one creature at a time.
- Paladin: Divine Smite can use any spell slot. Aura of Devotion and Aura of Vigilance point to the 17th-level aura feature.
- Ranger: beast spirit Perception and Stealth now scale with PB. Avian Form flies at twice walking speed.
- Rogue: Sneak Attack is once on your turn. Kill Shot wording.
- Sorcerer: Enlarged Spell example. Volatile table entries 47–48, 55–56, 93–94. Both draconic spell tables moved up one circle.
- Warlock: Pact Magic (2/Rest) at 3rd level. Pact of the Blade needs a melee weapon.
- Wizard: copying a scroll doesn't destroy it. Rite of the Source Master is for non-ritual spells.
- Backgrounds: "common clothes" in Criminal, Homesteader, Maker, Rustic. Homesteader gets navigator's tools plus one tool or vehicle.
- Talents: Ritualist (same source, can be taken again for another source), Armor Training, Hand to Hand, Spell Hunter, Two Weapon Mastery, Covert, Quick.
- Equipment: AC from different sources doesn't stack. Spear lost Thrown and gained Reach. Net lost the ghost sentence. Galley, keelboat, rowboat, sand skimmer and war wagon show "—" for INT, WIS, CHA. New keelboat and war wagon numbers.
- Magic items: mailbreaker (any bludgeoning melee weapon), apparatus of the crab (full stats), cape of the mountebank 8,400 gp, onyx dog, fire giant strength potion 650 gp, potion of resistance 100 gp, ring of jumping (longstrider), ring of spell storing, staff of the woodlands (wildsense), ring of the flamekeeper DCs, spell scroll (two paragraphs removed).
- Rules: sunlight, psychic damage, Luck on death saves, one reaction per trigger, unseen attackers, underwater ranged weapons, cantrips scale with character level, Primordial "source".
- Spells: conjure minor elementals (no M), fire under the tongue, moonbeam and prayer of healing (above 2nd), spiritual weapon (1 minute), symbol (1 minute).
- Conditions: Frightened (while it can sense the source), Incapacitated (no bonus actions).
- Fixed while there: the giant strength potion table was scrambled and is rebuilt.

## Book of Blades
- Frostborn Raider: Bitter Animus, Home in the Wild, Summon the Rampant Snowstorm **(rewritten)**, Raging Blizzard **(rewritten)**.
- Undying Warrior: save DCs now 8 + CON + PB. Feast of Suffering and Rejected by Death **(rewritten)**.
- Iron Bastion: Additional Talent, Barrier Shield and its four exploits, Barrier Shield Expert, Impenetrable **(all rewritten)**.
- Skirmisher: second description paragraph cut. Honed Reflexes moved to 7th level, Additional Fighting Style gone. Agile, Crossbow Finesse, Focused Aim, Honed Reflexes, Bleeding Strike **(rewritten)**.
- Fighter martial actions: Arbalest Skirmish and Springheel **(rewritten)**.
- Clockmaker: description, Countdown augment (renamed, new trigger rules), Stasis Trap, "Saved Time".
- Fleshsmith: typo, Third Arm, Understanding **(rewritten)**. The Book of Blades "Absorbing" augment is now "Storing" so it no longer clashes with the Metallurgist's Absorbing.
- Monk: Horse Archer, Blazing Strike (Fiends too), Soothing Flare 2d6, Cleansing Soul, Soul Burst.
- Paladin: both oath spell tables now have one spell per level. Scorched Earth Brand.
- Ranger: Preternatural Advancement opening, Trip Rope barbs 1d4.
- Rogue: Ever-Ready, Nimble Strider, Momentum Mastery, Tactical Guidance **(rewritten)**, Setup, Blind.
- Heritages: Horse Lord (Expert Trainer, Herd Master; Master of Horse removed), Martial Law (Basic Training; Militia Drill removed; Small Unit Tactics), Privileged (Social Maneuvering).
- Backgrounds: Ascetic, Conscript and Grave Keeper talent lists. Avatar's table is now "Divine Purpose". Judicial Champion was already fixed.
- Talents: Poisoner (resistance, not immunity), Unarmed Maneuvers (replaces one attack; save DC).
- Weapons: the Book of Blades boomerang and war scythe are removed, as the errata says (the core boomerang and scythe stay). The Hooked property is gone. New weapon options on the sai, tonfa, butterfly sword, ji and hook sword. Rope dart is 1 gp, 1d4 piercing. Disarming Twist and Weapon Trap **(rewritten)**, Ensnare, Wall Waster **(rewritten)**.
- Chain of hexed souls removed from the "magic items for mounts" line (it's a weapon).

This settles two earlier flags: "Boomerang, rebounding" and "Scythe, war" no longer exist.

## Book of Ebon Tides
- Umbral human size is Medium only.
- "shadow walk" became "ebon tide" on A Brief History of Shadow.
- Shadow goblin and quickstep sizes were already right. The other entries cover spells, items and monsters that aren't on the wiki.

## Lore books
- Midgard Worldbook: Rogglett and Mynnasgard ages (60 and 90 years). Brescia is now Brixia and Schio is now Schion. Hengist Staffbreaker is a barbarian. Yngvarr Ironsides (the wiki had "Vngvarr"). Gunhilda the Lefthanded, jarl of Amberhall, swears fealty to Uffi Toothless. Varnerhall shelters a goblin clan. Kesh is now Kush (Bastet page).
- Southlands Worldbook: Nuria Natal population adds 15,000 basteti. The errata's own numbers add up to 13,511,000, not the 13,495,000 it states. I kept its total.
- The Old Margreve: this is the Tales of the Valiant book (it mentions Monster Vault and origin spells). Roses druid Bonus Proficiencies, Verdant druid Grasping Vines and Plant Form levels, Flora sorcerer 7th-level spell (freedom of movement, was cloudkill).
- Zobeck and Tales from the Shadows: nothing on the wiki matches (real estate tables and adventure encounters).
