Every magic item now has its own page. Done on 22 Nov 2026. No rules text was added or rewritten; this is a structure change only.

## What changed
- 610 item pages, in folders under Rules/Magic/Magic Items. Each page has the item's text, a linked type line (Weapon goes to Weapons, Armor to Armor, the rarity and attunement words to Using Magic Items), the first mention of each condition linked to Conditions, and a line at the bottom pointing back to its list.
- The old type pages (Magic Armor, Magic Weapons, Potions, Rings, Rods, Staffs & Wands) are now tables: Item, Type, Rarity, Attunement, Price.
- Magic Tattoos, Fabled Magic Items, Arcanepunk Items and Weapons of Legacy keep their rules text, with an item table under it.
- Wondrous Items A–L and M–Z are gone. In their place: a Wondrous Items page listing 14 categories, one table page per category, and one table of every wondrous item.
- New pages: Magic Items A–Z and Magic Items by Rarity. Both are linked from the Magic Items hub.
- 288 links across the wiki that pointed at item headings now point at the item pages. Recommended Magic Items by Class is included.
- Autolink added 286 links, mostly item names on the pricing page and other item pages, plus class names in attunement lines. The autolink tool no longer links text inside `%% %%` notes (it had started linking book names in source notes).

## Merges and renames
- Weapons of Legacy: Destiny, Mankiller, Second Chance and Soul Reaver each had two sections (stats, then story and suggested deeds). Each is now one page, stats first.
- Figurine of Wondrous Power: the core entry and the second list of figurines are now one page. The second list sits under "More Figurines". Its intro paragraphs said the same thing as the core intro in slightly different words, so I kept only the core intro. **Check:** your rule is to keep the newer book's version of near-identical text. If you'd rather have that wording, say so and I'll swap it.
- "Ioun Stone: Reciprocation" is saved as "Ioun Stone, Reciprocation" because Windows can't use a colon in a file name. Its old name is an alias, so links still work.
- Jarlpact Rings is the intro to the four jarl rings, not an item itself. It has its own page and is listed in Rings as "Ring (set of four)".

## Fixed while there
- Cape of the Mountebank: its type line and first sentence had run together onto one line. They're split.

## Left alone
- Story So Far: autolink wanted to add a link there. I skipped it because it's a campaign page.
- The Wondrous categories come from a word list. A few items may sit in the wrong one; "Tools, Keys & Curiosities" is the catch-all (52 items). Move anything you like.
- Prices are copied exactly as written. Vault of Magic items will show "pending" until we do the pricing pass.
