How each Vault of Magic potion, food, honey, bezoar, troll blood and scroll got its price. Done 22 Nov 2026. Change any price on the item page if it feels wrong at the table.

## How the prices were set

- **Permanent items** use the five-step method on [[Magic Item Pricing]]: start at the lowest price for the rarity (common 25, uncommon 500, rare 2,000, very rare 15,000, legendary 25,000 gp), double it if the item is mainly for combat, double it if its benefit lasts (always on, or longer than 1 minute), double it if it can affect several creatures at once, then add 10 gp per circle of the best spell it casts, or 10% if it uses charges for other powers. Flight, invisibility, teleporting, a pocket dimension or an endless resource move an item up one rarity first.
- **Enchanted items** (a simple power that fits any weapon or armor of a kind) use the table on [[Using Magic Items#Item Type and Value|Using Magic Items]], plus the cost of the base item.
- **Consumables** are priced like the spell they copy (cantrip 10, 1st 50, 2nd 100, 3rd 200, 4th 350, 5th 650, 6th 1,500, 7th 2,500, 8th 5,000, 9th 10,500 gp), or by rarity when no spell fits. The official potions do the same, which is why a very rare *potion of flying* costs only 200 gp.
- **Cursed items** are listed at half the price of what they pass for. They carry a hidden note saying so.
- No setting adjustment. [[Magic Item Prices]] has the regional rule.
