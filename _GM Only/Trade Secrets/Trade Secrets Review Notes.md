## Trade Secrets: Review Notes

Source: *Trade Secrets* (Kobold Press). Rules text is carried over as written, the same way the Player's Guide and Book of Blades pages were. Nothing is invented unless marked **Mine**.

### Batch A1: Chapter 1, Mercantile Adventures

New pages:
- `Rules/Trade Secrets/Trade Secrets` (hub). The one-line description of the book is **Mine**.
- `Rules/Trade Secrets/Shopping & Markets` (player): Shopping downtime activity, Settlement Economy Limits, Shopping Resolution, the "Why Not CHA?" sidebar, and the descriptions of markets, auctions, the black market, fairs, and festivals.
- `Rules/Trade Secrets/Running a Business` (player): investing, building from the ground up, securing supply, hiring staff (full hireling table and descriptions), going out of business, and the Running a Business downtime activity.
- `_GM Only/Trade Secrets/Mercantile Adventures (GM)`: shopping-at-the-table advice, shop descriptors, currency-crime villains, the four quest tables, shopping encounters by settlement size, all market, fair, and festival events, the three magical markets, and the Investment Opportunities table.

Changes to existing pages:
- `Rules/Rules`: added the Trade Secrets line to the list and the credit line at the bottom.
- `Rules/Playing the Game/Between Adventures`: one line under Activity Descriptions pointing to the two new downtime activities.

Edits to the book's text:
- Fixed plain typos: "Maxium", "Bargian", "merchants''guild", "tables n the", "marge number", "You spend their downtime", a missing full stop, and a misplaced "the" inside the italics of *modify memory*.
- Settlement Economy Limits: the book prints it as two side-by-side pairs of columns. It is one two-column table here. Same values.
- "Why Not CHA?" sidebar: the second paragraph opened with a pointer to the GM-only section "Incorporating Shopping in Your Game". The pointer is removed on the player page; the rule is unchanged.
- Hireling table: the book splits it over two pages. It is one table here. "special" for Overseer reads "Special (see description)".
- Running a Business: the paragraph introducing the Investment Opportunities table is on the GM page with the table.
- Section titles "New Downtime Activity: Shopping" and "New Downtime Activity: Running a Business" are shortened to "Shopping" and "Running a Business".
- Event lists (auction, market, black market, fair, festival) are GM-only. The player page keeps the descriptions and the Fair Games table.

Open items:
- Two places on Running a Business point to "Structures in Chapter 4". That section comes with the gear batch; the pointers get linked then.
- The Shopping activity mentions the Random Magic Item tables in the *Game Master's Guide*. Left as written.

### Batch A2: Chapter 2, Creating a Business

New pages:
- `Rules/Trade Secrets/Creating a Business` (player): the five steps, choosing a location, choosing a trade (every industry with its capital, labor, supplies, goods, proficiencies, and risks), acquiring inventory for a player-owned business, the inventory ledger, naming a business with the random name tables, and security (nonmagical measures and useful spells).
- `_GM Only/Trade Secrets/Creating a Business (GM)`: NPC businesses, the stock check table and other ways to decide what is in stock, magic items for sale, the six sample inventories, making a merchant, the four example merchants, all eleven merchant generation tables, store interior and shop quirk tables, and the five ready-to-use shops.

Changes to existing pages:
- `Rules/Trade Secrets/Trade Secrets`: added the Creating a Business line.
- `Rules/Trade Secrets/Running a Business`: one sentence at the end of the opening paragraph pointing to Creating a Business. **Mine.**
- `Creating a Business` opening paragraph ends with one sentence pointing to Running a Business. **Mine.**

Edits to the book's text:
- Fixed plain typos: "Workship", "SalePrice", "available for buy", "an accessible spells", "Joyfer's drives", "in in", "No se of magic", "Arcan Emporium", a stray bold on "Labor" in three trade entries.
- Merchant Names: the book prints 1 to 50 and 51 to 100 side by side. It is one table here. Merchant Lineage and Merchant Attitude are also unfolded into single tables. Same values.
- Three staff names that wrapped onto a second line in the book's sample tables (Cindra Longhair, Bumbottle Brown, Thadius the Brave) are rejoined.
- Sample tables sit under their own headings with their descriptions; the book scatters them across the page.
- Page references ("see page 54") are removed.
- On the player page, the Labor section drops one sentence that pointed to the GM-only merchant tables.

Fixed errors in the book (Bryant said to use judgement):
- Shop Name Formula is labeled d6 but has four entries. Changed to d4. **Mine.**
- Interior Style (GM page) is a d10 table with no result for 4. Canvas now covers 4–5. **Mine.**

Spells named under Useful Security Spells link to their entries where the wiki has one. *Antipathy* links to the wiki's Antipathy/Sympathy entry. The Forbiddance entry never names the spell in its text, so a linked name in brackets is added there (**Mine**). *Private sanctuary* has no entry on the wiki, so it is unlinked.

### Batch A3: Chapter 3, Fantasy Finance

New pages:
- `Rules/Trade Secrets/Trade Goods & Money` (player): methods of exchange, currency, stores of value, financial crimes (how societies handle them), the full trade goods price list with units of measure, buying and selling trade goods with the market modifier tables, and merchant organizations.
- `Rules/Trade Secrets/Batch Crafting` (player): all fourteen trade activities (brewing, farming, foraging, hunting and trapping, mining, pottery, processing, prospecting, sewing, smelting, smithing, spinning and weaving, tanning, woodcutting).
- `_GM Only/Trade Secrets/Fantasy Finance (GM)`: Trade Events, Wartime Trade Events, and the random goods tables.

Changes to existing pages:
- `Rules/Trade Secrets/Trade Secrets`: new "Money and Trade" section with both pages.
- `Rules/Equipment/Currency & Trade`: one line pointing to Trade Goods & Money.
- `Rules/Playing the Game/Between Adventures`: the Trade Secrets line now also points to Batch Crafting.
- `Batch Crafting` ends with one sentence pointing to Trade Goods & Money, and its opening links the core Crafting activity. **Mine.**

Edits to the book's text:
- Typos fixed: "Tree essential factors" (Three), "should paying", "the apple owner desire", "High inflation are", "refined good", "The Cloth", "Your ruin the hides", "to make complete", "If the time is to be made" (item), "functional and textiles work", missing spaces after two event names.
- Buyer Type table: its second column was headed "Type of Seller" in the book. Changed to "Type of Buyer".
- Hide Cost and Fiber Cost tables: the book heads the first column "Cloth". Changed to "Hide" and "Fiber".
- Sewing Cost, Fiber Cost, and Hide Cost are printed side by side in the book. They are single two-column tables here.
- Processing Cost and Resolution is one four-column table in the book; the PDF splits it, so it was rebuilt and checked against the page.
- Random goods tables: the book nests a d6 list inside each row. Each row here reads "1–2: Furs; 3: Hair…". The book's "Firs" is "Furs", and the Arcane Ink row's "1–4, 4–6" is "1–4, 5–6". **Mine** (both fixes).
- Page references removed.

Kept as written: the Syreena example's numbers, and "Malchonite" with its pointer to the *Labyrinth Worldbook*.

### Batch B: Chapter 4, Equipment Expanded

Placement follows the wiki's existing pattern for optional books (Player's Guide 2 and the Northlands Worldbook): new gear is added as a "More … (Trade Secrets)" section at the end of each core equipment page.

Changes to existing pages:
- `Rules/Equipment/Adventuring Gear`: new section "More Adventuring Gear (Trade Secrets)": provisions, alchemical items, herbal concoctions, mechanical devices (with the Timer Duration sidebar), and fourteen equipment packs.
- `Rules/Equipment/Tools`: new section "More Tool Uses (Trade Secrets)": Big Projects, and New Tool Challenges for every tool set (new goods with prices, and new example tasks with DCs).
- `Rules/Equipment/Weapons`: new section "More Weapons (Trade Secrets)": weapon descriptions, table, three new weapon options (Brand, Distract, Grappling), the Shoddy material, and the Disguised, Hot, and Secure properties.
- `Rules/Equipment/Armor`: new section "More Armor (Trade Secrets)": nine armors and shields, the Decorative material, and six armor properties.
- Each of those four pages gets the Trade Secrets credit line at the bottom.
- `Rules/Trade Secrets/Running a Business`: the four pointers to "Structures in Chapter 4" now link to Setting Up Shop.
- `Rules/Trade Secrets/Trade Secrets`: Setting Up Shop line, and a new Gear section.

New pages:
- `Rules/Trade Secrets/Setting Up Shop` (player): the book's Structures section: financing, shop components, location, other considerations, mobile shops, permanent shops, workshops, factories, and farms, and renting. The opening sentence is reworded to introduce the page (**Mine**).
- `_GM Only/Trade Secrets/Equipment Expanded (GM)`: Tool Limitations, Creating Tools, and Homebrew Tool Proficiencies (advice for the GM on adjusting and inventing tool sets).

Skipped as duplicates (Bryant: skip obvious duplicates):
- Hook swords and rope dart: already on the wiki from Book of Blades, with different stats.
- Garrote: already on the wiki from Book of Blades as gear, with its own rules.
- Net: already in the core Adventuring Gear.
- The Linked and Strangling properties, used only by hook swords and the garrote.

Edits to the book's text:
- Crafting tables: the book prints cost and weight in one table and crafting time and DC in a second one beside it. They are one table here, headed Crafting Time and Crafting DC (**Mine**). Same values.
- Shop Components: the book prints the Effect column apart from its table. Rejoined and checked against the page images.
- Investor Funding, Guild Funding, and Management Difficulty tables were side by side pairs of columns; unfolded into single tables. All three skip 100 gp (51−99, then 101−200). Changed to 100−200 (**Mine**).
- Weapons and Armor tables rebuilt by hand from the page images: "Marital" (Martial), "piecing" (piercing), "Cat o nine tails" (Cat o’ nine tails), "4 gp." and "14 _ DEX" (14 + DEX) fixed.
- The second "Iron Goods" table under Tinker Tools lists clocks, locks, and toys. Retitled "Tinker Devices", column "Device" (**Mine**).
- "Navigation Tools" is "Navigator Tools", the name the wiki's Tools page uses (**Mine**).
- "Alchemical Tools New Example Tasks" is "Alchemist Tools New Example Tasks".
- Several typos fixed: "lures insect", "potion provide", "checks … has advantage", "can be trigger", "1 days of rations", "colder climbs", "instantly extinguished", "Safes. A safe or keeps", "boats of this size are have", "The hook us used".

Kept as written:
- The book gives no description for the sabre or the scutum, only their table rows. Disappearing ink is in the alchemist table with no description.
- Brand says the target is "scared by the weapon". It probably means the Frightened condition; left as written.
- The pistol sword lists a Gunpowder property, which isn't defined on the wiki.
- Commerce Cost, Commerce Land Cost/Rent, and Frontage tables each have two columns headed just "Modifier". The book doesn't say which modifier is which. **Question for Bryant.**
