## Trade Secrets: Review Notes

Source: *Trade Secrets* (Kobold Press). Rules text is carried over as written, the same way the Player's Guide and Book of Blades pages were. Nothing is invented unless marked **Mine**.

### Batch A1: Chapter 1, Mercantile Adventures

New pages:
- `Rules/Trade Secrets/Trade Secrets` (hub). The one-line description of the book is **Mine**.
- `Rules/Trade Secrets/Shopping & Markets` (player): Shopping downtime activity, Settlement Economy Limits, Shopping Resolution, the "Why Not CHA?" sidebar, and the descriptions of markets, auctions, the black market, fairs, and festivals.
- `Rules/Trade Secrets/Running a Business` (player): investing, building from the ground up, securing supply, hiring staff (full hireling table and descriptions), going out of business, and the Running a Business downtime activity.
- `_GM Only/Trade Secrets/Mercantile Adventures (GM)`: shopping-at-the-table advice, shop descriptors, currency-crime villains, the four quest tables, shopping encounters by settlement size, all market, fair, and festival events, the three magical markets, and the Investment Opportunities table.

Changes to existing pages:
- `Rules/Rules`: added the Trade Secrets line to the list and the credit line at the bottom.
- `Rules/Playing the Game/Between Adventures`: one line under Activity Descriptions pointing to the two new downtime activities.

Edits to the book's text:
- Fixed plain typos: "Maxium", "Bargian", "merchants''guild", "tables n the", "marge number", "You spend their downtime", a missing full stop, and a misplaced "the" inside the italics of *modify memory*.
- Settlement Economy Limits: the book prints it as two side-by-side pairs of columns. It is one two-column table here. Same values.
- "Why Not CHA?" sidebar: the second paragraph opened with a pointer to the GM-only section "Incorporating Shopping in Your Game". The pointer is removed on the player page; the rule is unchanged.
- Hireling table: the book splits it over two pages. It is one table here. "special" for Overseer reads "Special (see description)".
- Running a Business: the paragraph introducing the Investment Opportunities table is on the GM page with the table.
- Section titles "New Downtime Activity: Shopping" and "New Downtime Activity: Running a Business" are shortened to "Shopping" and "Running a Business".
- Event lists (auction, market, black market, fair, festival) are GM-only. The player page keeps the descriptions and the Fair Games table.

Open items:
- Two places on Running a Business point to "Structures in Chapter 4". That section comes with the gear batch; the pointers get linked then.
- The Shopping activity mentions the Random Magic Item tables in the *Game Master's Guide*. Left as written.
