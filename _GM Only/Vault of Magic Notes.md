## Armor & Weapons (22 Nov 2026)

- 177 new item pages: 56 in Magic Armor, 121 in Magic Weapons. Every one has a hidden `%% price pending %%` note and shows "pending" in the Price column until we do the pricing pass.
- Four were skipped because the wiki already has them: Goblin Shield, Impaling Weapon, Sand Arrow, Sickle of Thorns.
- Converted to Tales of the Valiant wording: saves ("DC 15 CON save"), ability abbreviations, PB, AC, spell circles. Spells link to their wiki entries, including ones with different names here (*locate creature* and *locate object* become *locate*, *Tenser's floating disk* becomes *floating disk*).
- Text came from OCR of your scan, then checked against the page images where it looked wrong. A few spots I repaired by hand: Akaasit Blade, Buzzing Blade, Candied Axe, Hellfire Armor, Hexen Blade, Moonsteel Weapon, Pistol of the Umbral Court, Scimitar of the Desert Winds, Spearbiter, Trident of the Vortex, Tyrant's Whip, Vestments of the Bleak Shinobi. The tables on Dragonstooth Blade, Phidjetz Spinner, Rain of Chaos and Tarian Graddfeydd Ddraig were rebuilt. If any wording reads oddly, these are the first places to check.
- Bonebreaker Mace's type line came through as "(1 requires attunement)". I read it as Requires Attunement.

## Needs your call
- **Madness:** spirit honey gives a "long-term madness", and Brain Juice and Tonic for the Troubled Mind mention short-term and long-term madness. Those are 5th Edition rules that Tales of the Valiant doesn't have, and the wiki has no madness rules. Options: pick a Horror or Dread effect to stand in for madness, write a small madness table as a house rule, or leave it to your call at the table.
- **Troll Blood vs. the alchemy ingredient:** the alchemy recipes use "Troll Blood" as an ingredient. I stopped autolink from linking those mentions to the new Troll Blood page. Say if they should link to it.
