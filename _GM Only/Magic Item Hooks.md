Adventure hooks that came with magic items, in my own words. Items link to their pages.

## Asp's Kiss
%% source: Vault of Magic %%

The [[Asp’s Kiss|asp's kiss]] is a haladie, a double-bladed knife common in Khandiria and the Mharoti Empire. A few have turned up as far west as Nuria Natal.

- **Weeping victims.** Someone is killing members of the Antars, a basteti merchant family in Nuria Natal. Each victim sheds a single blue tear as they die. Neerlop Antar, head of the family, shrugs it off. His sister Tahani is afraid for her children, believes the killer carries the asp's kiss, and wants adventurers to find the assassin.
- **The old soldier's heir.** Ghazi Taslimi, an aged dragonborn veteran, took the blade in the wars against Khandiria under Sultan Makbule Khezmir. Near death, he is testing people to find a worthy heir for it. So far every candidate has failed, and each one has died with a blue tear on their cheek.

## The Crook and Flail
%% source: Vault of Magic %%

The [[Shepherd's Flail|shepherd's flail]] and its partner, the [[Crook of the Flock|crook of the flock]], were made for the god-king Hekhmet, who was famous for his way with people and animals. They were buried with him after a long, prosperous reign.

- **A shaky crown.** Hekhmet's descendant Ibris is losing his grip on the throne. His people find him unimpressive, predators are killing the herds, and an ambitious rival is waiting to take over. Ibris wants adventurers to bring the crook and flail out of his ancestor's tomb, both to prove his right to rule and to set things right for his people.

## Moxug the Fear-Eater
%% source: Vault of Magic %%

Moxug is a bugbear necromancer who started out as hired muscle for a dark wizard named Varrus. She was clever enough that he made her his apprentice. She is obsessed with fear, and people say she lives on her victims' terror instead of food. When Varrus tried to become a lich, she sabotaged the ritual and savored his fear as he died. Rumor says the first [[Craven’s Heart|craven's heart]] she made came from his body. Good for: the maker of the [[Crawling Cloak|crawling cloak]], [[Fear-Eater’s Mask|fear-eater's mask]] or craven's hearts the party finds.

## The Eye of the Golden God
%% source: Vault of Magic %%

The [[Eye of the Golden God|eye of the golden god]] was the left eye of a pit fiend statue worshipped by a small cult of Mammon. An adventurer named Slick Finnigan pried it out while his friends chased down the fleeing cultists. A hidden cultist attacked him, and he got away with the gem but not the second eye. Devils and cultists hounded him within days, so he sold it to merchants in town, including a jeweler who set it into an amulet for a local noble. It has changed hands many times since.

- **The second eye.** The idol still has its right eye, and its power is weakened. Devout cultists want the left eye back in place.
- **Mammon's faithful.** Other worshippers of Mammon can almost sense the amulet and want it for themselves.
