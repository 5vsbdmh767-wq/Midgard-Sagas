Notes on the "Made with" lines on magic item pages. The *Vault of Magic* items were done on 24 November 2026; the core and *Player's Guide 2* items, and the Field Guide recipe fixes, on 25 November. The rule itself is on [[Crafting Magic Items]] and in the bible (v5.32). Everything here is for your call; nothing has been resolved silently.

## 25 November: core items and official recipes

- 344 more item pages got a "Made with" line: every core and *Player's Guide 2* magic item. 326 have ingredients; 18 are fabled (including the four weapons of legacy) and can't be crafted; [[Tattoo of the Emissary]] can't be crafted either (see below).
- Same rule and same checks as the Vault pass, now run against the Field Guide text and both Monster Vaults: every named material exists in the Field Guide, counts match rarity (consumables one fewer), and every main ingredient can come from a creature at the rarity's floor. All 326 pass; nothing in this batch is flagged for a weak main ingredient.
- Still to do: the Northlands, Labyrinth and Old Margreve items (about 156), then Trade Secrets, Book of Blades and Night Hunters (about 107), then the 77 Field Guide monster items that aren't on the wiki yet.

### Calls in this batch

- [[Tattoo of the Emissary]]: its text says powerful Fey give these tattoos to those who serve or court them, so it's treated as given, not made, and can't be crafted. Your call.
- Some weak creatures have the perfect material, but they're under the floor, so they went second instead of first: the astralsupial pouch (a pouch into the Astral Plane) on [[Handy Haversack]], [[Portable Hole]] and [[Valendra’s Voluminous Sheath]]; the gluckshund heart (the Field Guide says to use it in dice, cards and other things of chance) on [[Lucky Tattoo]]. On the one-ingredient items that would have wanted them ([[Bag of Holding]], [[Efficient Quiver]], [[Coin of Wandering Luck]], [[Stone of Good Luck (Luckstone)]]) I used a material that meets the floor instead. If you'd rather let a clear fit beat the floor, say so and I'll switch them.
- [[Lucky Roller Dice]] (Common, no floor) use gluckshund heart, as the Field Guide suggests.
- Items whose rarity varies (+1/+2/+3 gear, potions of healing, spell scrolls and so on) list ingredients in order; use as many as the version's rarity calls for. Their main ingredient isn't held to a floor, the same as in the Vault pass.

## 24 November: Vault of Magic

### What was done

### Things I noticed

- Named Field Guide materials thin out at the top. In Monster Vault, very few creatures of CR 13 and up drop named materials, so many Very Rare and Legendary items lean on Elemental Gemstone, Celestial Feather, Aberrant Brain, Throat Scales and generic parts.
- Players won't know what a named material like Voltaic Organ does or who drops it. If you want them able to plan, a player page listing the materials (in my words) would fix that. Not built; your call.
- Three materials appear in the Field Guide's tables with no entry in either book: Chaos Fruit, Psychic Shard and Tough Hide.
