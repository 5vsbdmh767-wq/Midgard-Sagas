## Wiki Review Notes

Review of the wiki's organization and navigation, 22 Nov 2026. Changes are listed by batch.

### Batch 1: book references and optional wording (done)

Changes:
- Removed the visible book captions from 23 setting pages: "*Map from the Midgard Worldbook.*" (25 lines across Midgard and Cosmology pages) and "*Material on this page is from Tales of the Old Margreve by Kobold Press.*" on Magic in the Margreve. Books are credited only on the Credits page.
- Dread Expanded: heading "Optional Rule: Dread Exposure" is now "Dread Exposure".

- Between Adventures: "ask your GM to select" is now "let your GM select".

Left alone on purpose:
- Rules text where a player asks the GM a question, such as Cleric "Location" and Technical Talents "examining an object". These are game mechanics, not labels.
- Ship Statblocks.md and Potions & Consumables.md are empty stubs with publish: false, not linked from anywhere. Waiting on Bryant's decision.
- "Monster Vault" pointers stay until the Monster Vault pages are added.

### Batch 2: more hotlinking (done)

Only the first mention of each condition on each page is linked (blinded, charmed, deafened, exhaustion, frightened, grappled, incapacitated, invisible, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, prone, restrained, stunned, surprised, unconscious). The Conditions page itself is not changed.
