Trombei sits coiled like a serpent where the roads to [[Valera]], [[Triolo]], and [[Melana]] meet, with marshes on both sides, so nothing moves north or south through the [[Seven Cities]] without passing it. Its farms make it rich, and its heavy cavalry makes it dangerous. Every time it comes close to beating one rival, the others band together against it. Its Golden Ceres Fair is the place to go for beer and horses.

The Commune, made up of representatives of the 24 guilds and ten landholders, elects a First Speaker. Fallani Vil, daughter and niece of past speakers, has held the post for over ten years by knowing something embarrassing about everyone. The army is split between the young Master of Horse Alessandra Stefano and the dwarf Field Marshal Waldbeck, a former free company captain who wanted the post for his own protégé.

Trombei's great warhorse bloodlines belong to the state, and taking one over the border is a hanging offense. Two years ago a disease called the black strangles began killing the herds, and it has since spread to the ponies of the [[Khazzaki]] and the [[Centaurs|centaurs]] of the [[Rothenian Plain]]. Nobody agrees where it came from.

## The Republic

Trombei's strategic place and the bounty of its farms have made it an economic juggernaut and, through its cavalry, a military power. But graft and corruption are sapping its strength, the gap between rich and poor is widening, and the disease in its precious herds threatens the very foundation of the Republic. Its neighbors mock Trombeians as large, well fed, and loud, and they are certainly bigger and bolder than the quiet Friulans or the polite Valerans.

The First Speaker appoints Trombei's two great military officers, the field marshal and the master of horse. After the untimely death of Triadano Gardano, the young and fiery General Alessandra Stefano became master of horse, to the dismay of much of the army, above all the field marshal, the dwarf Ursli Waldbeck. A former free company captain, Waldbeck grew so influential that the city had little choice but to give him his post, and he wanted master of horse for his protégé, Captain Matteo Moretti.

## Horses of Trombei

Trombei's cavalry depends on its horses: big, powerful animals bred to carry a fully armored rider and trained to trample foes. The two great bloodlines are the black baccanera and the roan barbata, both prized and owned entirely by the state. They are clever and well trained, and fast enough to defend several borders without tiring. General Stefano is rightly proud of her mounted legions. Private breeders may buy the right to breed the lines, and Trombei often lends trained horses to mercenaries, but taking any black or roan heavy cavalry horse over the border is punished by death. A third line, the Valeran whitemane, is never bred privately and is reserved for Trombei's officers.

### The Black Strangles

Two years ago a stubborn, deadly disease appeared among Trombei's horses, spread by the bite of another horse. It begins with blackened hooves and lines of infection creeping up the legs, then fever and lengthening teeth. In the middle stage rotting abscesses open on the neck and flanks. At the end the lungs bleed, breathing fails, and the horse dies. Spells can cure it, but there are not enough clerics for the thousands of horses in the herds. Even so, First Speaker Vil is spending a large share of the treasury on a cadre of priests of Ceres to fight it. Lately Valibar Fetch has won great fame selling a cure of his own, Roggsothof's Balm, brewed from orange mushrooms scraped off a Waste Walker. It is as likely to kill the horse outright as to heal its abscesses.

## Ceres-of-the-Harvest

Most Trombeians revere Ceres-of-the-Harvest, goddess of the fields, the flocks, and all that brings life, and celebrate her at the solstices and the harvest by burning straw effigies as symbolic sacrifices. Merchants and bankers honor her as Ceres-in-Gold, a noblewoman in cloth-of-gold. But not everyone shares in the growing wealth. Many farmers and laborers see little of it, since the grain goes to the army and the best fodder to the cavalry horses. To them the gilded Ceres is a symbol of runaway luxury, and the black strangles a punishment for greed. Her cult has become both a refuge for the discontented and a cauldron of unrest.

A few follow Ceres Vindicator, robed in black with striking adders twined around her arms. These Reapers strike by night with poisoned sickles, destroying property and attacking landowners, merchants on the highways, and even officials. The Commune once thought them a nuisance, but their attacks grow bolder, their sympathizers more numerous, and some say they have infiltrated the city guard.

## Places in Trombei

| Place | Description |
| --- | --- |
| The Brawls | The docks along both banks of the Arnesse River in the heart of the city, home to every vice money can buy. Anyone looking for peace and quiet should stay away. The Golden Horn Ale House, built over the water on a disused pier, is the hall of the powerful Brewers' Guild, the guild most welcoming to dwarves, many of whom travel to Trombei to enjoy its hospitality. The brewers proudly honor [[Ninkash]] as their patron and jealously guard their recipes, some of which have magical effects. A small temple of [[Mavros]] on the docks keeps the relic called the Hand of St. Ulvert, to which brawlers and sailors pray to "guide their fists" or "guide their ship." |
| The Broken Reeds | Marshes north of the city, named for an old legend of gods trampling the land into the sea. Fed by the Arnesse, their shallow waterways are a maze of drifting reed islands that confuse and trap travelers. Scattered tribes of grippli, lizardfolk, and swamp creatures live there among the ruins of ancient manor houses and older civilizations. |
| The Wreck | One of the great ruins of the Broken Reeds, a crumbling castle that lists at a dangerous angle like a sunken ship. It is home to the Ladies of the Wreck, a coven of cannibal hags who worship the Hunger and prey on travelers between Trombei and [[Melana]]. |
| Carpere, City of Horses | The large city at the southern end of the highway before Valeran territory, a customs stop for caravans. Companies of mercenary centaurs, descendants of the Valeran Rhoetian Guard, patrol the border against bandits and smugglers. Every year in Last Leaf, Carpere holds the tremendous ten-day Trombei Horse Fair, the highlight of the social calendar, with tens of thousands of horses, grooms, and buyers, quick trades, and fortunes made and lost. Nothing is too exotic, from a fey walker to a sleipnir. |
| The Old Hold | An immense elven fortress south of Carpere, headquarters of the centaurs, which some humans call the Stable. Strangely, since the black strangles appeared, the centaurs have shut its gates and drive away visitors with words and arrows, and those who still patrol keep far from other travelers and explain nothing. |
