Kyprion is a volcanic island of wheat fields and olive and apple orchards, and the homeland of the minotaurs since the dragons burned their southern cities three hundred years ago. It owes fealty to [[Triolo]] in exchange for protection.

Queen Kitane rules from the Palace of the Bull at the heart of the Great Labyrinth, which is both fortress and temple to the minotaurs. By day, walking its halls is a way to commune with Hecate and the ancestors. By night its traps wake and beasts hunt in it, and several times a year condemned criminals are hunted through it. They say the queen invites friends and enemies alike to sleep in her palace, and only her friends wake up.

Two months ago, in revenge for their lost fleet, [[The Mharoti Empire|Mharoti]] fire dragons burned the city of Gramvar to ash, and while the minotaurs rushed to help, a dragonkin legion took Chamiras, the City of Rope. The dragonkin have dug in and are fortifying it with its own people as slave labor. The queen is begging her allies for help and struggling to keep her furious people from throwing themselves at the trenches.

## The Queen of Kyprion

The title of queen on Kyprion is largely hollow, though saying so is a sure way to provoke a nearby minotaur to a duel, or worse. As vassals of Triolo, the minotaurs are subjects without a true nation of their own, and that rankles most of the island. Queen Kitane presents herself as a gentle priestess, kind and merciful to her people, who honors the alliance with Triolo. She takes comfort in the military alliance forming between Triolo, [[Kammae Straboli|Kammae]], and Kyprion, especially with signs of support from Nuria Natal, and sees it as her people's best hope.

But Triolo has refused to send troops, and everyone can see why: the foothold on Kyprion is a ploy to draw forces away from Triolo, and if the First Duke-Admiral sends his army, the Dragon Empire will fall on Triolo in force. That does little to ease the suffering of Chamiras. Kitane's hardest task is keeping her people from sinking into fury and returning to the old ways of blood-fueled dunamiphagy, the eating of an enemy's heart to gain spiritual power, and from throwing off Triolo's gentle yoke, which would leave Kyprion wide open to the dragons.

## Vespras, City of Song

The capital is a truly great city of wide, well-paved avenues, lush foliage, and fragrant groves outside its walls. Wells near the top of its main hill send magically raised water cascading through fountains and channels to the markets and rich houses on the waterfront. [[Humans]] and gearforged are welcome, especially those who share the minotaurs' faith.

## Occupied Chamiras

Chamiras made the finest hempen rope in the world, a staple of the sailor's trade, and was known for its great necropolis, the City of the Honored Dead. Two months ago, while most of its guard rushed to help burning Gramvar, a small Mharoti legion landed under magical fog and invisibility and quickly took the city. Four hundred minotaurs were put to death at once as an example, and the rest are slave labor for the fortifications. Defensive trenches studded with traps now ring the city, its walls grow taller, and the old governor's manor, the Palm Palace, is being rebuilt under scaffolding into a fortress. The minotaurs are cowed by brutal reprisals for the smallest offense and pray to any god who will listen for rescue, even [[The Hunter]].

The legion is commanded by Aarush Vedula, a huge red dragon and protégé of Lashmaraq Talshah, the Morza of Hariz in [[The Mharoti Empire]], who calls the minotaurs "jambuka," jackals, and unworthy. She is furious at her orders to hold position, convinced her rivals are holding her back from glory, and she keeps the former Horned Governor, Yikashata, chained in her quarters. His howls echo through the night. Her Sultan's Foothold Legion is more than enough to hold one city and could conquer the whole island if unleashed. It may simply be strengthening its grip on Chamiras before doing exactly that.
