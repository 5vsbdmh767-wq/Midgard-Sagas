Kammae is a theocracy ruled by the Oracle of Hecate, the moon goddess, and everything about it is foretold. Its people are zealous about public piety, charity, and great deeds of devotion. Magic and government are bound together here, and the Oracle's seminary-college, Hecate's Chambers, trains priests and mages side by side.

Eight years ago the sea god Nethus, long imprisoned in his temple in Kammae, was set free, and instead of taking revenge he declared himself Hecate's true husband. The Oracle revealed her daughter Dalyora as the child of the two gods and made her warleader. Kammae has been on the march ever since. With the help of Nethus's sea-titans, merfolk patrols, and new cavalry riding ghostly boars, it has seized the northern half of [[Capleon]] as punishment for the baron's dealings with the dragons, and it sailed in the fleet that broke the [[The Mharoti Empire|Mharoti]] armada.

Kammae fields excellent archers and a hammer of priests, paladins, and inquisitors. Its spies are said to be the best informed of the [[Seven Cities]] along with [[Triolo]]'s. It also trades, discreetly, in requiem, a drug that lets the user speak with the dead.

## A Time of Change

The inquisitors of Kammae know how to strike at their foes' weakest points. The priests of the Moon Goddess know where and when to attack. The Oracle knows how defeating those foes leads, link by link, to Kammae's final triumph. It is all foretold.

With Nethus freed and standing beside Hecate as her husband and equal, Kammae Straboli is rising on a new swell of fanatical faith and grim resolve. Gone are the days when Hecate held her people to nonviolent means. Now the goddess demands that the nation grow and be strong. Guarded by Nethus's scylla, new eastern shipyards race to build vessels while merfolk patrol the coast. The recent conquests in Capleon, the alliance that sank the Mharoti armada, and gods walking among them have convinced the people of Kammae that victory is certain.

## Kammae Straboli, City of the Oracle

The city is a fine sight of red-tiled roofs and well-kept whitewashed houses, its people learned and fanatically pious. It is the only place in the [[Seven Cities]] where [[Mavros]] is not among the great gods, for Hecate and Nethus guide its destiny in person. Some doubt the sea god's change of heart, but most of Kammae basks in his love and his shared rule.

Kammae has turned to war. Its forges turn out weapons and armor while legions of priestesses make healing potions, scrolls, and other magic for the army, and in the fields outside the walls the ghost-boar cavalry, the "cavallos," drill. Warleader Dalyora Podella readies the army to face the Mharoti while the Oracle works to build alliances among the other Septime states.

### The Oracle

The blind daughter of the moon, Yeneva Podella, is a woman of fifty, unmarried as is the custom of her priestesses, blessed or cursed with divine insight and great gifts of healing and prophecy. Like many of her sisters she was an orphan raised in the temple, and her gifts shone from an early age. With the voices of spirits, angels, and shrewd counselors around her, and direct communion with Hecate and Nethus, she rarely errs in matters of fact or faith. Once cautious, she now believes the Mharoti, with their advance on Capleon and their conquest of [[Rumela|Illyria]], are the gravest threat the Seven Cities face, and she is quick to act and quicker to take risks. She leaves the army to her foster daughter, Dalyora, and concerns herself with larger matters.

### The Eyeless

The inquisitors and paladins of the Moon Goddess belong to a divine order called the Eyeless, answerable only to the Oracle, the gods, and their messengers. Their symbol is a white eye without a pupil, and the people both love and fear them. Their training and zeal make them dangerous to anyone they consider an enemy of the Temple, though a few are corrupt. The Oracle recently gave command of the Eyeless to Dalyora Podella, a decision that has split the ranks. Many swear loyalty to the new warleader, while others call it favoritism and stand behind their former leader, the Dark Eye of the Moon, Lygren Ullos. The quarrel simmers and could boil over at any time.

### The Ringwood

The largest forest on the peninsula has a reputation as a dark refuge for bandits, outlaws, and anyone who opposes the Oracle. Several small villages and fortified keeps lie within it, among them Crescent Manor, where the Oracle's bloodhounds are bred. Unlike many forests of Midgard, the Ringwood has no elven or fey inhabitants, but drakes, wyverns, and other monsters live there and sometimes attack travelers.
