Friula grew up around the one monastery on its coast to survive the destruction of Caelmarath. It took in exiles and refugees and sent them out to plunder the ruined keeps and towers of the coast, and within a decade it held more books and scrolls than anywhere outside [[Magocracy of Allain|Allain]] and the temples of Thoth-Hermes. It still trades in knowledge, vellum, rare inks, and dyes from its reef, and once a year at the Scribe's Fair it opens its gates and plays the good host.

The city is ruled by the bibliotori, a closed circle of scholar-mages who compete to expand their private collections, now led by Primus Balack Giolan, who has forced bitter rivals to work together. Its neighbors distrust it. It trades books with [[The Mharoti Empire|Mharoti]] arcanists, gives safe harbor to ships from Allain, and keeps a small enclave of elfmarked and even elves. Luckily, any army would have to march through basilisk-infested hills to reach it, so its enemies send spies instead.

Something is wrong with the Great Library. Its halls have grown dark, endless, and mazelike, scholars vanish or come back changed, and things prowl the stacks. The bibliotori are still arguing about it in committee.

## The City of Secrets

The stone spires of Friula rise along the shore and throw their shadows over a great barrier reef. This coast was once lined with the private keeps of Old Verrayne and the hidden refuges of great mystic orders, and the cataclysm that destroyed Caelmarath shattered all but one of them. Friula grew up around that last monastery. Its wealth of knowledge draws scholars, arcanists, and secret-seekers from everywhere, and its success has made its neighbors suspicious. A city of exiles that trades tomes with Mharoti arcanists and shelters the ships of Allain cannot help but unnerve [[Kammae Straboli|Kammae]] and [[Trombei]]. Friula balances the shifting political tides to keep its isolation, and besides lore it trades in vellum and in the rare inks and dyes of its reef.

Today the City of Secrets trembles. The Great Library has gone dark, its vast halls now mazelike, endless, and twisting. Scholars vanish or come back changed, and there are whispers of darker things prowling the stacks. The bibliotori argue endlessly in committee while the darkness grows.

## The Bibliotori

For most of its history Friula has been ruled by the bibliotori, a closed circle of collectors who guard the city's books and scrolls and compete tirelessly to grow their private collections. No one knows how many there are, but twelve openly run the government and act as diplomats. Membership is by invitation, offered only to those who can add substantially to the members' collections. For all their influence, the bibliotori do not control the Great Library, and they often clash with its Keeper and with Ulsavus the Golden Voice.

That changed somewhat three years ago when Balack Giolan became primus, first among equals. He has vastly expanded the bibliotori's gathering of lore, even forcing bitter rivals to work together. It has won him few friends, but as long as he keeps bringing in lore and artifacts, his power is secure.

### The Darkening of the Library

The Library's corruption began as a wonder. The Keeper found tomes unheard of or thought lost forever. Then scholars and visitors began to vanish, turning up days later with their spirits permanently broken, and soon only bodies were found, eaten away by patches of inky blackness. The Keeper closed all but the most public halls and sent in trusted agents to find the cause. Most never came back. The Library is now far larger than its building, its halls impossibly twisting, and visitors speak of cannibal books, prowling shadows, and creatures torn from the pages and given life. The Keeper fights the shadows every day, desperate to keep her precious lore from sinking further into darkness.
