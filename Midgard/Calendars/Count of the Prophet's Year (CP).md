The prophet of the Southern monotheistic deity [[Aten]] was martyred 52 years ago, and since that time his cult has grown substantially, especially among women, southern dwarves, and the poor. For religious reasons, these people disregard the [[Mharoti Dragon Ages (MDA)]] and use the Count of the Prophet’s Years instead. This does not endear them to the [[The Mharoti Empire|Mharoti]] officials and governors who encounter [[Nurian]] pilgrims, preachers, and merchants doing business with an unofficial calendar.
