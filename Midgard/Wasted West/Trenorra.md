Trenorra is the only human town south of the Pytonne Mountains, a bandit barony behind a huge wooden palisade, built on top of old goblin mines and surrounded by the burial cairns of forgotten giant tribes. The roads are dangerous because of the undead giants in the hills.

The Trenorrans go out of their way to get along with their enormous neighbors. A few giants walk its streets, many businesses cater to both sizes, and goblins are let inside the walls as miners. In return the giants seem to keep the worst raiders and horrors of the Wastes away. Trenorra sends ore along the Mage Road to [[Magocracy of Allain|Bemmea]] and gets leather, wool, and potions back. Visitors should be careful about asking the giants about their troubles, and more careful still about admitting to friends in Bemmea.

Its arms are four vertical bars of gold and black, and it trades in adamant, gold, and artifacts. Its fields are fertile, and its huge wooden palisade can be seen for miles from the mountain road. Hill giants free of their ancestors' hauntings appear here more often than anywhere else in Balinor; they are peaceful if left alone and quick to rage if pestered about their people's troubles. Adventurers come to capture unusual creatures or help a stone giant oracle in exchange for some broken artifact from Andarre's workshops. The giant villagers of Sorrowsown sell their crafts here (see [[Haunted Lands of the Giants]]).
