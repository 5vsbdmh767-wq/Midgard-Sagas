The Green Duchy is a surviving fragment of old Caelmarath, and it stands guard on the northern edge of the [[Western Wastes]]. Its gorges and hills make good defensive ground, and its villages and the fortresses of the Nine Towers watch the border constantly. Most years the mule trains and pilgrims get through with few losses. When the goblin hordes do come, it is terrible.

The druids of the Oaken Ring chose Duke Valis, a hardened general and former castellan of the Nine Towers, when the last duke died without heirs. He has done things the druids don't love: brought in Bemmean wizards to strengthen the towers' wards and made deals with the more "civilized" goblin tribes to hold off the rest. Since the rise of the goblin warlord Braagezz, he has grown afraid of assassins and keeps four members of the Black Band, a famous company of heroes, beside him at all times.

Verrayne distrusts arcane magic, which tore its land apart once already. Its Order of Seekers watches every wizard and sorcerer in the duchy, and visitors from [[Magocracy of Allain|Bemmea]] and [[Friula]] are assigned a minder. The capital, Savoyne, sits behind double walls, and its Duke's Gardens are said to hold a passage to the Elflands.

The duchy's arms are a black tower on a white field above a green curved hill. It trades in mules, donkeys, oak casks and barrels, copper, fine masonry, pigs, goats, and barley. Besides Savoyne (18,700), it has the merchant city of Barenna (9,000), the port of Goasta Cliffs (1,500), the castle of Bael-Tor, and the Nine Towers.

## Savoyne, City of Blood
Watch castles and signal fires on the surrounding hills lead to Savoyne's double walls. It is small by southern standards but well kept. [[Goblins]] enter rarely and under close watch, bringing news of the Wastes, and the Seekers execute humans and goblins alike who smuggle ancient treasures. The city takes no goblin prisoners: executions happen on Raven's Wall, the northern wall, stained red by the blood of beheaded goblins whose heads draw great flocks of ravens. The Duke's Gardens host the Rites of Spring, High Summer, and Harvest festivals and hold a magical passage to the Elflands whose opening ritual is a state secret. At the city's heart, the living Temple of the Twins, woven of trees and flowers, is a peaceful place where priests bless goats, seed corn, and the earth's bounty; its head is the young druid Green Priestess Losaneta, who reports on the city to Blood Mother Margase.

## The Nine Towers
Nine castles and watchtowers hold the line against the Goblin Wastes and garrison most of Verrayne's army, well stocked and well trained. Every commoner owes the duke's corvée, a period of mandatory service that repairs and improves them each year. The commander of the Tower Watch is the ranger Olfen Abromo, a member of the Order of Seekers who leads a hunt into the Wastes every month.

## The Order of Seekers
This elite order of rangers and fighters holds the borders against goblins and aberrations and watches wizards and sorcerers throughout the duchy for magic that might open new holes in its defenses. Visitors from Bemmea and Friula are given a personal minder, and with the Oaken Ring the Seekers track the most powerful arcanists using blood wards and druid marks that warn them when summoning or diabolic magic is used. Many dukes have led the Seekers, Valis among them, and they muster the militia in times of danger: every man over 16 trains with spear, bow, or axe, and unmarried women serve as archers, scouts, or drovers. Since Braagezz's rise, Valis has doubled the order's recruiting.

## The Oaken Ring
Verrayne's druids are stronger than in most lands, because they hold the line against the Wastes. They see arcane casters as more dangerous than useful and persecute them; only the duke's protection keeps some wizards in the land, and they even distrust the scholars of Friula. They favor harsh measures, reputedly including a company of werewolf soldiers, human sacrifice to purify the corrupted west, and divination from the entrails of pegasi and unicorns, on the principle that one can argue morality only while alive and untainted. Many claim ties to the fey and the Arbonesse elves, and they work toward restoring the Wastes over many generations.

Their leader, Blood Mother Margase, answers to no one but the gods, and it is not always clear how much authority she thinks the duke has. She is as old as many of Verrayne's oaks, and some believe she is related to [[Baba Yaga]]. Under her, Verrayne has held against every horde, and no invading army has fared well in her forests. Her spies are spiteful clurichauns and far darrig who hate wizards and will gladly kidnap one. Her ziggurat temple to [[Hecate]], on a wooded hill in the wilderness and guarded by werewolves, is said to be where captured sorcerers and wizards are held until the new moon and sacrificed.
