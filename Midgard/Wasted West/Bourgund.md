## The Gleaming City
Against the stark western edge of the Wastes stands Bourgund, gleaming and impossibly intact, without a crumbled tower or pocked wall. Order is everything here. White Knights in intricate armor and spotless banners patrol pristine streets. Outside the walls lies Zhergthoth the Fallen One, the only Dread Walker ever killed by mortal magic and sharp steel, slowly mummifying in the dry wind, the one foulness the city cannot erase. Bourgund's perfumes, the strongest and longest-lasting anywhere, are made on the edge of that stench, an irony not lost on buyers.

Bourgund is not large, but its citizens are privileged: the duke sees that everyone lives comfortably, there are no slums, and crime is rare. The price is constant watching. No one knows when a wizard might be scrying by spell, crystal, or familiar, vagrancy is a crime, and the citizens' foppish smiles hide real terror of the laws. A thriving black market sells amulets and other items that block scrying. Each commercial street has a severe market warden with White Knights within call, businesses open and close like clockwork, and residential districts have overseers too. Bourgundians prize mithral, and a rich, tightly regulated vein pays dwarf miners well. The castle of Elserin guards the Mage Road south of the city.

The city's symbol is a white sun on a blue field with golden stars. Its trade is banking and finance, grain, horses, mithral, and perfume, and its people are about 40,000 humans with a few hundred dwarves, halflings, and elves.

## Government
Duke Ignatius Martenne II is the latest of a long line. His ancestor, the Sun Duke Karlott Martenne, broke Bourgund away from Allain after defeating Dizzerax, the last king of the united goblins. Ignatius lacks his ancestor's prowess; his battlefield glory comes from carefully managed jousts, and no one dares tell him. He is, however, a square-jawed, charismatic leader, and his hereditary hold over Bemmea's mages and the White Knights' power let him believe he has no equal. He is advised by the Council of White Counts, hereditary lords of the counties outside the walls who oversee the duchy's farms, and served by many mages, including Bemmeans indentured by ancient oaths. The White Knights' orders, including the duke's elite Rose Knights, answer to the combined council.

## The White Knights and Mageholme
Ever since the White Knights slew Zhergthoth, perhaps before it could crush Bemmea, the mages of Bemmea have been bound to them, one wizard to one knight. Within the secluded citadel of Mageholme, 250 wizards live in service: most are armor-bonded mages who watch their knight from scrying cells in the high spires and can cast spells through their armor, scry on them, and speak to them mind to mind; a few dozen elders advise the White Council; and the rest are apprentices culled from the least promising of Bemmea's schools, who in Bourgund are valued. Its methods are a state secret, and many of its mages learn both arcane and divine arts.

Within the city the knights enforce every law, and many citizens have seen a knight appear in a flash of arcane fire to punish a law they didn't know they were breaking, often discussing the secrets of perfumery, which is forbidden on pain of death. Few will talk about it. Beyond the walls, on the White Counts' estates, enforcement is looser. Knights escort important caravans, especially perfume, to [[Barsella]] and [[Verrayne]], but rarely patrol the Wastes, since their mages can teleport them to any threat. That reliance has made them soft, better at court intrigue than war, and with the bonds now failing (see [[Wasted West#The White Knights' Bonds|The White Knights' Bonds]]) and only 250 armor-bonded knights left, they are poorly prepared for the undead giants of the Haunted Lands or the rumored dragons of the Ghostlight Forest. The new Master of the Armor Bonded, Tellanis Yazaron, is loyal to the duke and watches the other mages closely. The White Council is worried; the duke, not yet.

## Aurgelmir's Teeth
A dozen huge monoliths of crimson stone stand around the city, the largest, the Centerstone, in the Grand Plaza before the duke's palace. A few years ago Sir Markus Alavaine, the youngest of the Rose Knights, eager to match the heroes who slew Zhergthoth, persuaded his superiors to let him bring the monoliths down, and his men stopped the priestesses of [[Marena]] from performing their rites there. Giants broke out of several of the stones and rampaged through the Grand Plaza until a band of adventurers and High Priestess Lethana's priestesses held them back long enough for one of the Red Sisters to bind them again, cutting her own throat to do it. Sir Markus was demoted and still broods over his disgrace.

## The Ducal Perfumeries
The most exclusive district is the Bouquet District, which locals whisper is Odor Alley, where small incense shops and great perfume emporiums stand under heavy guard. [[Halflings]] are common here, prized for their sensitive noses, skill, and devotion. Shadow elves come to arrange shipments to the Courts and spend fey coin freely. Merchants also sell delicate bottles, magical censers, and rings with tiny compartments that work as well for poison as for perfume.
