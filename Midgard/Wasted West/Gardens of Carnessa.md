South of the [[Haunted Lands of the Giants]] stands the Wall, an impossibly tall unbroken barrier of stone grown out of the bedrock by the stone giants of Andarre. Beyond it lies a jungle. The wizards of Caelmarath once grew magical gardens there, and nobody is sure why: an academy, a king's summer palace, or an army of thinking plants. Whatever they were for, the plants got loose. An alien jungle swallowed the whole peninsula and the old port of Al-Rassor, and its roots and vines are working at the Wall from both sides. Most people assume the giants built the Wall to keep the [[Western Wastes|Dread Walkers]] out of the gardens. It is older than that, and it was built to keep the jungle in.

Lately, explorers who have climbed or flown over the Wall come back to the taverns of [[Barsella]] and [[Bourgund]] with stories of tendril puppets, mindless people with green vines growing out of their eye sockets and mouths, driven by long-limbed, bark-skinned vine lords. The explorer Tavereen Windrider claims to have found what drives them all: a new Dread Walker, Veth-Shoon, a six-legged mountain of vines and moss throwing seed pods as it walks, the twin of the Green Walker of Kush far to the south. No mage has slowed it, and wherever it treads the jungle grows. [[Trenorra]], Barsella, and Bourgund are all very worried.

Greenhouses of magic-infused glass and strange basalt cultivation beds still stand among the jungle ruins, which suggests the gardeners turned their work toward darker ends, perhaps an early attempt to enslave the giants to the north. Unlike the Walkers of the Wastes, Veth-Shoon has never been slowed, and it has made dozens of vine lords, whose spore pods turn the jungle's people into tendril puppets. No one knows for certain why Midgard now has two such Walkers. Tavereen Windrider, the elfmarked explorer who first found it, is compiling an almanac of the [[Wasted West]].
