Perhaps no region inspires more tales of terror across the face of Midgard than its ruined western reaches, a once-verdant land permanently ravaged by the Great Mage Wars. A puckered, ragged scar of desiccated land that tears along the edge of the Green Duchy of [[Verrayne]] and down the coast of the giant-haunted Carnessian Peninsula marks its southern border. The desolation extends north to the elven courts of the [[Arbonesse]], where it gives way to lush forests. In the east, the proud, towering [[The Ironcrags|Ironcrags]] crumble into the foothills of the [[Western Wastes|Goblin Wastes]]. As one moves west toward the [[Leukos River]], the terrible devastation slowly lessens before finally halting at the vast expanse of the [[Western Ocean]].

For centuries, tyrannical arcanists dueled over the [[Ley Lines & Shadow Roads|ley lines]] of Midgard’s western lands. Tremendously powerful mages built and abandoned principalities as the fickle ley lines shifted, and the constant magical strife among the rival magocracies slowly drained the life from the land. Only the bones of these cities remain today, their tumbled stones and spires covering a landscape pockmarked by acrid lakes and stunted trees. This is not the whole of the magocracies’ legacy, however. 

Worst of the surviving relics are the colossal, unimaginable alien creatures summoned during the war’s desperate final years. The [[Western Wastes|Dread Walkers]] slowly sleepwalk across the horizon, held outside of time by powerful spells woven by the combined might of the surviving wizards. Violent rag-wrapped dust goblins, their minds obsessed with the artifacts of past days, haunt the ruins and salvage lost items of power. Weary pilgrims plod over the Bone Road to the Seat of [[Mavros]] while trying to avoid hostile druids, river goblins, and the horrors of the Wastes. Undead wizards work in crumbling towers to awaken slumbering monstrosities. Oasis-like forests beckon in the wasteland, only to prove more dangerous than the dunes. To the south, dead-eyed giants haunted by their ghostly ancestors wander the land, and sentient plants creep over a great wall to transform the desert into lush, fecund jungle.

But civilization does continue. Midgard’s most acclaimed magic users thrive in Allain. White Knights from [[Bourgund]] offer protection from the badlands. Far [[Barsella]] supports an entire economy devoted to explorations of the Western Seas. From across Midgard come those who would pluck fame, wealth, and fortune from the shriveled corpse of these dead lands, and those who would remake the world with magic best left undisturbed.

## History
Some 3,000 years ago the island nation of Ankeshel, humanity's first great civilization, sank beneath the Western Ocean with its vril magic and orichalcum temples. Dark centuries of barbarism followed until the elves came to the West, and humans lived under elven rule until they mastered magic again and built nations of their own. The mightiest was Caelmarath, south of the [[Arbonesse]]. Its most gifted mages listened to the whispers of [[Mammon]] and his servant Totivillus, bred devilish half-bloods, and schemed to control the region's ley lines.

Eight hundred years ago, hungry for knowledge the elves refused them, Caelmarath and its tieflings rose in the Black Sorceress's Revolt. The empire did not survive its freedom. It split into nine magocracies that fought to seize the elf-held ley lines, and some histories say the struggle corrupted them, opening doors to shadowy realms and to stranger places between the stars. When the elves left Midgard, the magocracies lost their common enemy and turned on each other.

### The Great Mage Wars
The contest stayed half hidden until, more than 400 years ago, the mad archmage Tycho Luz summoned the Isonade, the creature that sank Ankeshel, against the coast of Allain. Only the combined mages of Bemmea put it back to sleep. After that the war was open. Dar Ramek of Vael Turog called acid rain down on Cuculla, tiefling sorcerers of Caelmarath summoned fiends in the streets of Cassilon, and Herkest Galbrion of Molovosch created the Fellmire with a vampiric fog the size of a lake.

As the ley lines strained to breaking, the mages of Allain bent them into unnatural curves toward anchors of their own. Enkada Pishtuhk, a traitor within the Fulgurate Society, a peace-seeking circle drawn from every magocracy, used the twisted lines to tear a hole in reality and summon the Dread Walker Pah'draguusthlai against hated Uxloon. Rivals summoned another horror to stop it, too late, and the Walkers multiplied, laying city after city to waste. The surviving archmages could not banish them, so they stopped time around them instead. This Great Slumber left the Walkers sleepwalking a few steps a year across the ruins, and the lands west of the Ironcrags have changed little since.

## The Lost Magocracies
Of the nine magocracies, only [[Magocracy of Allain|Allain]] survives. [[Barsella]] endures as a city ruled by sea merchants and pirates rather than mages.

Caelmarath answered with hellfire and a portal to the Realms Beyond that loosed shoggoths in its streets. ## Twisted Fey
Not every fey servant left with the elves, and those who stayed were warped by what leaked through the doors to the Realms Beyond. The selang, dark satyrs, serve the Walkers as heralds and diplomats and have settled at the feet of several of them; the sorcerer Raak Talberoth, guided by the babble of his mamuras, seeks to bring a "transformed" Caelmarath back. Tentacled dorreqi swarm around the Walkers as guards. At the tent village of Quan-Ved, which follows Uthul-Vangslagish, selang piping, the Walker's wailing, dorreqi tootling, and goblin drums blend into a din that has driven dozens mad. The hag-like qwyllion dominate with a deadly gaze and enslave the slain as specters. One, Seelys, haunts the Melano River and preys on pilgrims bound for the Seat of Mavros.

## The Black Goat's Flock
When the magocracies summoned the Walkers, the church of Bacchana, god and goddess of night, wealth, fertility, and power, blessed their wars, and she was corrupted along with the land into the [[Black Goat|Goat of the Woods]]. Paladins of Khors burned her groves, her worship was outlawed, and her faith dwindled. Now dozens of new cult cells are springing up across the Wastes, popular among the dust goblins and among giants of the Haunted Lands who hope the Black Goat will quiet their ancestors. Their leaders, often depraved wizards, hunt the lost rites of the magocracies.

## The White Knights' Bonds
Ever since the White Knights of [[Bourgund]] slew the Dread Walker Zhergthoth, Bemmea's mages have been bound to them, each wizard sworn to a single knight and protecting them from afar by magic until death parts them. That bond is failing. Count Tremvar Yellete, Master of the Order of Rose Knights, led his knights into the Ghostlight Forest while escorting a caravan to Barsella, and they died there among will-o'-wisps and an ancient green dragon. Their deaths freed a dozen bonded mages, including the former Master of the Armor Bonded, who deserted Mageholme Citadel for Allain. Other bonded wizards now feel their shackles loosening, and no one is sure which of them will stay loyal.
