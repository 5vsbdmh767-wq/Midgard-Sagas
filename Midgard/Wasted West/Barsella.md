Barsella is the city at the edge of the world, the last free port on the Western Ocean before the water runs off into nothing anyone has mapped. It is cut off from the rest of the West by the Ghostlight Forest, the Pytonne passes, and the giants, and it lives by adventure. Expeditions leave from its harbor for the [[Haunted Lands of the Giants]], [[Magocracy of Allain|Allain]], the [[Western Wastes]], and out across the ocean, and every tavern argues over which kills more people, east or west.

The founding families take a cut of everything. Merchants call the taxes on supplies, docking, and repairs extortion, and pay them anyway. The city is full of gambling halls and does a quiet trade in vril artifacts headed for the [[Seven Cities]], but it also has fine shipwrights, good provisioners, and arguably the best-run port in Midgard. For a long time fewer than one ship in ten came back from the far ocean. Lately more have been returning.

Its symbol is a silver galleon on a calm blue sea. It trades in imported silks, exotic spices, lumber, ships, and gold, and holds the small towns of Maravahr, Vurflix, and Goatshead and the castle of Algos.

## Voyages West
Barsella's fortunes rise and fall with its families' obsession with the Western Ocean. When a ship comes back, sometimes years later, its holds of spices, gold, magic metals, beasts, and strange artifacts repay investors a hundredfold, the streets roar with festivals, and talk of the next voyage starts before the parties end. Strangest of all, no two reports agree. Hundreds of logs, bottled messages, and magical messages describe wildly different destinations on the same headings: ghost ships, giant merfolk, cannibals, leviathans, burning islands, shadow currents. Sailors who return are heroes, but sponsors can't plan a second voyage from the first. Still, every month of fair weather a new ship sails over the horizon, and most never come back.
