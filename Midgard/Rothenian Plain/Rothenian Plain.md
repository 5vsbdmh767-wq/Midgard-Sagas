East of the [[The Margreve|Margreve]] and the Cloudwall Mountains, the grass runs to the horizon. The Rothenian Plain smells of wild thyme in summer and lies under snow all winter, and almost nothing on it stays put. [[Kariv]] bands roll across it in painted wagons (see [[Wandering Realm of the Kariv]]), the [[Centaurs|centaurs]] keep an uneasy peace with them, and the [[Khazzaki]] ride their tough little ponies wherever there is plunder to be had. The few cities are held by iron-fisted lords, robber barons control the river roads, and bandits own the trails between.

The Plain has no real towns of its own, only the gulyay-gorod, wandering towns built of wall-sized shields mounted on wagons and sleds. Misto Cherno is a bandit town of outcast Kariv and centaur raiders. Misto Ellel, the town of runes, floats without wheels and is pulled by swans or reindeer. Misto Tilla is drawn by a hundred oxen and carries ballistae on a tower. The stories about them grow with every telling.

##### Powers of the Plain
The Khanate of the [[Khazzaki]] claims the open grasslands from the Cloudwall to the far east. Six great centaur clans roam the steppes as nomads, mercenaries, and bandits, and when one clan is wronged badly enough, the whole centaur nation rides out to burn its enemy's border. A sickness called the Long-Teeth has lately been spreading among them.

North of the Plain, [[Vidim]], the Kingdom of Ravens, is ruled by Tsar Alexor Yodorovic, whose boyars and [[Ravenfolk|ravenfolk]] compete for his favor. Farther north, the Silver Mountain Kingdom of Domovogrod has fallen to giants, trolls, and ogres out of the [[Northlands]]. To the south lies the slaving [[Despotate of the Ruby Sea]], and in the east stands Demon Mountain, whose Master has haunted the region for centuries.

##### Grandmother
Above all of them is [[Baba Yaga]]. Her hut walks the Cloudwall valleys, the Plain, and the deep Margreve, and she plays the khan, the tsar, and the centaur chiefs against each other, a friend for a month or a year and then a bitter enemy. Her daughters, the beautiful fey called vila, are sometimes seen at the courts of the [[Magdar Kingdom|Magdar]], the Tsar of Vidim, and the Despot of the Ruby Sea. Koschei the Deathless, whose soul is hidden in an egg that she is said to control, serves her whether he likes it or not.

## Kharalang, the Great Dragon of the Plain
When travelers think of the Plain, they think of its tribes, and of Kharalang, the wind dragon. Some say its home is on Demon Mountain, but no one has found its lair. Scudding clouds, rising wind, falling temperatures, and often hail and thunder announce it. It slew the drakes of the one [[The Mharoti Empire|Mharoti]] army ever sent onto the grass. Those who have attacked it with spells and arrows have not yet reported back. Its hoard is a running joke: the people of the Plain love to give visitors a perfectly straight-faced account of where the treasure lies, involving long rides, icy rivers, and dug-up hills, and always ending "beyond the reach of a coward like me; beware the dragon's wrath!" No one laughs until the visitor has left.

## Demon Mountain
It hides behind wooded slopes and unnatural clouds that roll down into the valleys, and the barren hills at its feet are full of howling things that dance on cloven feet. Its name is known but rarely spoken. Those who climb it come back with sulfur on their breath and madness in their eyes, whispering of the Master and a palace of bone, wine, and the wicked. Rumor says the mountain sends smiling, listening spies out across the Plain and beyond, and more than one innocent has been put to death as one. Even Baba Yaga avoids the place and the subject, though some believe she keeps a room in the Master's palace. Daring visitors who reach the peak may be invited to dinner with the Master. What happens after the dishes are cleared is anyone's guess.

## Domovogrod
The Silver Mountain Kingdom, once the bright star of the north at the feet of the Riphean Mountains, has fallen to the ogres, trolls, and giants of the [[Northlands]]. Its capital, Vellarsheim, the Mountain City, is now held by Mensnark Obsbane, a Thursir warlord grown too fat to move, who commands his giants and ogres from the ruined throne room while they stamp out the survivors. The winterfolk halflings fled into the wolf-haunted forests and wage a hard, scattered war from hiding. The Winter Tree, once tended by the All Mother, stands untended; explorers say the giants hoped to use its branches to raid the Elflands, and lately shadowy creatures have been seen stalking its valley. A few winterfolk rangers and druids guard it and its healing well. The fur-trading town of Olersheim still holds out, led by Nicosai the Fleet, the maimed, revenge-obsessed former captain of the Drakon Guard, with a few hundred survivors.

## The Thin Trail
Far out in the empty east of the Plain runs the Thin Trail, a shifting path the color of smoke, said to be older than Baba Yaga and perhaps as old as the world. Ash rises from the dark grass beside it, and from inside its bounds the Plain looks like a charred waste under a red sky, with monstrous leviathans moving on the horizon. Step off, and the vision ends. To find it, go east to a tall hill crowned by four trees, burn something precious to you in their midst, and eat the ashes; then you might glimpse it from the hilltop. It has no beginning and no end, only a maddening pattern across the land. Baba Yaga avoids it, and those who ask her about it tend to be turned into chairs; it is said she once saw its shape from the air by moonlight and found something in it that frightened even her. When a wicked blizzard blows in from the east, the Kariv spit in the snow and blame the Kalder, who they believe know the trail's shape and power.
