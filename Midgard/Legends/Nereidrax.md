Old enough to rival [[Sorros the Sea Sage]] and [[Mharot]] himself, [[Nereidrax]] was involved in the early years of [[The Mharoti Empire]] but due (circumstances) has separated herself from the political jostling of the other dragons and has instead imbedded herself in the courts of [[Sea Elves]], [[Sahuagin]], and [[Merfolk]]. She sows several plots at once.  

However, even she sees the [[Deep Ones]] as an existential threat. 

[[Nereidrax]] has the statblock of a [[Sea Dragons]] with the Sovereign template from [[Warlock]] #26.

She is the warlock patron of [[Captain Starless]] and she uses him for plots that necessitate fetching things on land or getting involved in land dweller matters. He is not her first, last, nor only currently active warlock she uses for such matters. Though now that he has acquired Tidecutter, he is her primary agent, and the fact that her goals currently line up with the goals of [[Bey Addakala]] is only a coincidence that will surely not last locating and entering the submerged temple of Thoth.
