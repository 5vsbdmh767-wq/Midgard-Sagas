Derro are distant cousins of the smallfolk and the dwarves, most often found in cavernous, lightless realms deep beneath the earth. Early in their history, the derro were changed forever by an encounter with horrific entities that dwell between the stars, and to this day many derro carry the same mental scars their ancestors did. Every derro has an innate sensitivity to the Void and to other eldritch horrors. Those raised in insular derro communities tend toward fragile states of mind and unpredictable, fanciful behavior, though they are as likely to be brooding or sullen as wild.

Life underground has shaped them. Derro often have semitranslucent gray, purple, or blue skin webbed with blue veins, and white or pale straw-colored hair worn in elaborate braids, with equally prodigious mustaches. Their large eyes are usually pure white, but some have a vivid orange, red, or blue hue that glows in the dark of their homes. They eat glowing cave mushrooms and the fish and kelp of underground lakes, and they often befriend giant slugs, worms, and bats, which carry them across the underworld.

## In Midgard

Derro are known best as a danger from below. In the [[The Ironcrags|Ironcrags]], the dwarven canton of Sargau was destroyed from beneath by ghouls and derro, and when new gold was found in the abandoned Golden Citadel of Friundor, the gold rush ended in disaster as derro and devils in the caverns set the miners against one another. Derro speak Aklo, a language they share with aboleths and linnorms.

Not every derro is an enemy. In the kobold warren of Lillefor, derro come up to sell ore and ingots, and in [[Zobeck]] derro are counted among the city's many peoples.

Among the gods, derro are some of the primary worshippers of [[Addrikah]], alongside duergar and deranged dwarves and humans.
