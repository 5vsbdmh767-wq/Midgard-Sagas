**==Dhampir in the Southlands==**

With some notable exceptions, Dhampir are far less common in The [[Southlands]] than they are further north. Most Dhampir in Midgard are of bloodlines coming out of the [[Blood Kingdom]] in [[Central Midgard]], and few of them have come so far south, fewer still would openly admit it. 

This is a bit of an unfortunate circumstance though, as The Southlands -- [[Nuria Natal]] especially, has a relatively accepting stance towards the undead compared to the rest of Midgard. The [[God Kings of Nuria Natal]] are themselves undead beings, in a state that might seem like lichdom to the ignorant, and the residents of Nuria Natal know this. Dhampir and even fully realized vampires fleeing the evils of the [[Blood Kingdom]] would likely be welcome in Nuria Natal to some extent. 

The Southlands does have it's own host of vampiric residents with no ties to the [[Blood Kingdom]], and 'native' Dhampir do exist, especially in the cities of [[Nuria Natal]] and The Free City of [[Free Cities of the Desert|Siwal]]. Most notably, Lady [[Alashra al-Zania]] of [[The Pallid Court]] in [[Nuria Natal|Per-Bastet]], has produced multiple Dhampir sons & daughters, something thought to be impossible by many for a female vampire to do. 

**==Non-Human Dhampir==**

Though not nearly as common, or known about, vampires can reproduce with other lineages than humans. 

**Dwarves**
Extremely rare, and only recorded in The Black Hills of Grisal in the [[The Ironcrags|Ironcrags]] of Central Midgard, it is possible for a vampire and a dwarf to produce a variant of Dhampir called a [[Glogken]]. Glogkens have sallow skin and yellow eyes, and lack most of the glamour of a human Dhampir. The one or two official records that exist also mention a supernatural ability to traverse rocky terrain, or "glide" across earth & stone. 

The dwarves of the Ironcrags, and especially Grisal, have no tolerance for the undead, and Glogken are killed on discovery. There are no exceptions. It's likely the records omit hundreds of discovered Glogken and the phenomenon is more common than any Ironcrag Dwarf is willing to admit. 

**Trollkin**
A dhampir of trollkin origin, [[Vzakhil]] are only encountered in regions where trollkin are numerous such as the kingdom of [[Trollheim]]. Resembling a normal trollkin with ashen, almost translucent skin, a vzakhil makes up for its lack of beauty with preternatural brawn. They lack the supernatural charm of a typical dhampir and have an increased appetite for blood and violence, known to go into a blood frenzy similar to full-blooded fledgling vampires.
