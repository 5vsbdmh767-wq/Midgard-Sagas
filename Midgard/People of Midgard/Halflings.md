Halflings and [[Gnomes|gnomes]] are the two branches of the smallfolk. In Midgard, halflings are rarely seen and keep close to home. Travelers meet them as bargefolk on the great rivers and as the most discreet of servants to the River Court of the [[Arbonesse]] and in the [[Grand Duchy of Dornig]]. They are widely believed to have been bound in service to the elves before the Great Retreat, and they still show great deference to elves and the elfmarked. Most halflings left Midgard for the Elflands or the River Court when the elves withdrew. Those who stayed behind were a little more adventurous than the ones who stayed loyal to their elven masters, and they were suddenly on their own.

## In the Crossroads

Riverfolk halflings work the barges on the rivers around [[Zobeck]]. They long steered clear of the city's Bargeman's Fellowship as too shady and too dangerous to deal with, but the guild has since been reformed and is now run by a council of three, one of them the halfling Quinnie Wisewater. Halflings also live in the Grand Duchy alongside its humans, elfmarked, and dwarves, and along the [[Great Northern Road]], where the grooms and stable hands at [[Great Northern Road#The Bluebell|the Bluebell]] are all gnomes and halflings in robin's-egg blue. Halflings are among the members of the Argent Rats, a mercenary company of former bandits.

## Winterfolk

The halflings of the North, also called winterfolk, are a different people from their southern cousins. They are so used to the rugged life of ice and fang that they seem to relish hardship. Some live in isolated communities in the lowlands of the Tunturi Range or pockets of the Reaching Mountains; others spend time in the towns and cities of the [[Northlands]], though they never stay long. Community and family are strong bonds among the winterfolk, and they give no quarter to anything that threatens the freedom and security of their people. They are often superstitious, with many rituals they follow every day.

Life is tricky when you are three feet tall in a world of twelve-foot snowdrifts. Winterfolk believe they must be twice as strong as the "big folk" to survive, and they seek out challenges with a zeal that looks suicidal to everyone else.

## Courtfolk

When the sable elves came into the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]], they brought courtfolk halflings with them as servants.
