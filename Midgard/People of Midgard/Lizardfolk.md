Lizardfolk appeared in the [[Southlands]] only 160 years ago, after the appearance of the Egg of Veles, but they have multiplied at an astonishing rate. Most are content to stay in the jungles and swamps and take little interest in human civilization.

## Veles-Sa

At the heart of lizardfolk civilization is an enormous egg covered in shimmering flecks of gold and green. The lizardfolk revere and protect it in a great marble temple at Azmull-Kre, deep in the Zobanu Jungle. They say it came from Veles, the Great Serpent, and believe it is the source of their power and of their remarkably rapid rise.

The lizardfolk of Veles-Sa, the Velesborn, believe they are the World Serpent's chosen people, destined to conquer the lands around them in his name, though progress toward that goal has been slow. They are cold, alien builders and engineers who have raised cities and temples in the deep jungle, and they capture and tame the dinosaurs of the mangrove marshes and hardwood jungles as beasts of burden and weapons of war. Outsiders call the heart of their territory the Land of Terrible Lizards.

Distance and terrain keep Veles-Sa isolated. It gets few visitors, and fewer still come back to tell of it; most end up as slaves or in the larders. That has begun to change over the last ten years, since the realm's ruler, the Vessel of Veles, allowed merchants from Sudvall and Morreg to bring their goods to the edge of the territory to trade.

## Elsewhere

The Velesborn's cousins, the Murkscale lizardfolk, live mostly in the marshes and swamps of the Spice Coast and the Fendeel Jungle. Far to the north, scattered lizardfolk tribes share the Broken Reeds, the marshes north of [[Trombei]], with tribes of grippli, and lizardfolk are counted among the many peoples who have lived in [[Zobeck]].
