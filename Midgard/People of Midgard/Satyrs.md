From the waist up, satyrs look much like other humanoids apart from the ram-like horns on their foreheads. From the waist down they look like overgrown goats, with dark cloven hooves and thick fur that matches the hair on their heads. Many cultures call them "goatmen" and dwell on their bestial, masculine look, a reputation that could hardly be further from the truth. Satyrs of every gender grow facial hair, and it means nothing about gender to them. Their long, dexterous fingers suit the pipes they are known for.

Satyrs are fey. They often live in forests, but they can be found anywhere they can reach the fey world, ideally somewhere they can dance, revel, and make mischief.

## In the Crossroads

In [[The Margreve]], satyrs, dryads, plant creatures, and sentient animals guard the forest and regularly clash with loggers along its edges. Satyrs turn up at the Meadow of Fey Revels in the [[Central Margreve]], where Lord Cedartail, the Piping Lord of Birds and Song, is one of the nobles who sometimes presides. He is a minor noble of the fey courts, far more interested in song and a good time than in court intrigue, and a chorus of songbirds heralds his arrival.

Further south, the Winewood east of Droisha in [[Rumela]] was long a home of smallfolk such as gnomes and satyrs, and it remains largely untouched by the [[The Mharoti Empire|Mharoti]], though its edges have been heavily logged.

## The Selang

Not every satyr is a reveler. When the elves left Midgard, not all their fey servants went with them, and those who stayed in the West were warped by the alien radiations leaking through the doorways from the Realms Beyond into darker, twisted mockeries of what they had been. These are the selang, the dark satyrs.

Bands of selang have settled at the feet of several of the Dread Walkers in the [[Western Wastes]]. At the tent village of Quan-Ved, which follows one Walker's ponderous route, the selang's hypnotic, alien piping joins in twisted harmony with the wailing from the Walker's fluted tentacles. Selang also haunt the Stale Wood of [[Blood Kingdom|Morgau]], the realm of the King in Rags, a huge, red-eyed satyr said to be a bastard son of the [[Court of the Moonlit King|Moonlit King]] of the shadow fey. Far to the east, the Grove of Silence near Galna is home to selang who worship the [[Black Goat|Goat of the Woods]] and similar dark gods; their pipes under the dark branches keep most woodcutters away.

Not all selang are lost to darkness. In the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]], Prince Valendan, a noble selang wizard, rules the [[Court of One Million Stars|Starry Court]] at the heart of the Court of One Million Stars with aloof wisdom, and he is far less bound to chaos and evil than most of his kind.
