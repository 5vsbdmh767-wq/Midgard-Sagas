Werekin are born of ancient bloodlines touched by the wild essence of [[Fenris]], and they bridge the worlds of beast and man. They descend from those once cursed, or blessed, with natural lycanthropy, and they now breed true as a people of their own. Werekin don't suffer the madness of the full moon as their ancestors did, but they can still call up the beast within.

Most werekin look human, with touches of their bestial ancestry: sharp teeth, pointed ears, large eyes, hair or bristles on the face and limbs. When the beast rises, it shows mostly in posture and poise, though some werekin show subtler signs too, such as yellowing eyes, lengthening nails or teeth, or sudden hair growth. In the cold of the North most werekin are wolfish, perhaps because of Fenris, but some carry the blood of other lycanthrope lines: bears, boars, rats, and even arctic foxes.

## The Kingdom of the Wolf

Werekin are common in [[Vargrike]], the harsh Kingdom of the Wolf, where the law of fang and strength rules. They are hunters, scouts, warriors, and survivors, filling the ranks of the great wolf packs that roam the taiga. Ignorant outsiders distrust them and take them for full-blooded werewolves, but werekin are fiercely proud of their heritage and gladly embrace the beast within, proving each time that it is not a curse but power and freedom.

Werekin turn up elsewhere in the [[Northlands]] too. In [[Skaldholm]], the City of Bards, the skaldic school called Rjodr's Grove draws many werekin and beastkin students.
