The dragonborn are the youngest people of Midgard, and their rule is growing fast. They are common only in [[The Mharoti Empire]], and in fact they are a product of its founding: nobody had heard of dragonborn before the dragons united. Since then they have spread beyond the Dragon Empire and now flourish across the south and east. "Dragonborn" names this people; "dragonkin" means all the scaled races of the empire together.

Other peoples see the dragonborn as having great presence and ability, but also greed and arrogance, and a people so young ought to be humbler. The dragonborn answer with the battles they have won. Their success at war and plunder makes many wonder whether they are especially favored by Veles, the World Serpent, and the dragonborn are quick to claim his blessing and the blessings of their draconic gods.

## Appearance

Dragonborn are powerful scaled humanoids with clawed hands and feet, short snouts, and deep golden or black eyes. Most have gray, brown, tan, gold, silver, or black scales; a rare few have red-orange, green-yellow, or starlike blue-and-black. Males are larger and heavier, horned or crested, with short and often spiked tails. Females are smaller, faster, and wiry, with longer lashing tails and sometimes a frill or collar of a contrasting color. Their scales protect them little better than skin, so they wear armor like anyone else. All dragonborn are heavy and slow for their size, but they carry a commanding presence, a sense of destiny that scaled and unscaled people alike find compelling.

## The Four Elemental Kinds

The dragonborn divide themselves into four supposedly purebred kinds, though there is plenty of mixing and hybrids are common.

| Kind | Scales | Crest | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flame or fire | Yellow, golden, or orange | Red | The most common |
| Wind or storm | Blue, white, silver, or gray | Black | |
| Stone or cave | Brown, gray, black, or rarely white | Purple or white | Uncommon |
| Wave or tide | Gold, blue, or green | Bright green or yellow | The rarest |

More common than any of the purebreds are the edjet, or soldier dragonborn, with brassy or tan scales and black or rust-colored crests. They stand outside the elemental divisions.

## Language and Magic

Dragonborn speak Draconic, the language of the Dragon Empire, as well as Common. Their magic tends toward the raw and simple: elemental power and the magic of scale and claw. Some of it is transformative, such as rituals said to turn a kobold into a dragonborn, or a dragonborn into a drake.

## Beyond the Empire

In the [[Southlands]], dragonborn are among the typical worshippers of [[Baal-Hotep]]. In the [[Northlands]] they are barely known.
