Gnolls are most common in [[Nuria Natal]] and the lands south of it, but they are also found in [[The Mharoti Empire]], in Khandiria, and even on the [[Rothenian Plain]]. They range the deserts and plains as hunters and scavengers, and they are expert rangers, scouts, and trackers. Their culture is violent and largely unfriendly to outsiders, but they have a small kingdom in the distant south, and they serve as guardians and avengers throughout Nuria Natal.

## In the Southlands

Gnolls live in large numbers throughout the [[Southlands]]. The largest population is in Nuria, and there are more in the Kingdoms of Salt and Steel, in [[Ishadia]], and along the Spice Coast. Other peoples often write them off as feckless and craven, but the truth is more complicated. In the cities of Nuria and elsewhere, gnolls find work as mercenaries, guards, and traders, thanks to their strength and their talent for flattering whoever is in charge. Even their wilder cousins in Dabu and the deserts have a well-defined society with strong traditions. Still, most gnolls are bullies, brave only when they have the advantage and hardworking only when they can't make someone else do the work. In recent years the zealous cult of Laughing Nkishi has spread among them, bringing more conflict with other peoples.

In Dabu, the Land of the Gnolls, a dozen or so loosely organized packs dominate the land. From a distance their camps all look alike, rings of hide or canvas tents and stolen caravan wagons drawn up around pens of livestock, but each clan has its own customs, laws, and practices, shaped mostly by the god it serves and by where it lives. Dabu is two realms: Dabu Zavoa in the north, reaching into the windswept Mukupe Sahel and west to the Jagged Coast, and Dabu Uhlza in the south, along the Mbazha range and the eastern Djibbar Plains. The [[Dominion of the Wind Lords]] to the north is a perennial enemy of gnoll raiders.

## Elsewhere

Clans of tundra gnolls range the glaciers and windswept ice of the far [[Northlands]] as hunters and scavengers. Their culture is matriarchal, violent, and largely unfriendly to outsiders, and some clans bend the knee to [[Boreas (North Wind)|Boreas]]. On the edge of [[The Margreve]], the Forest King, a roadside inn looking out on the [[Eastern Margreve]], has a gnoll owner. A few gnolls are even found in the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]], usually as servants of the gods of death such as [[Anu-Akma]].
