Elemental scions carry the primordial power of air, earth, fire, or water in their blood. Most owe it to unions between genies and mortals, which is why they are often called jinnborn, the name Midgard knows them by.

## The Jinnborn of the Deserts

The jinnborn are nomads who follow well-trodden ancestral paths through the Stone and Crescent deserts of the [[Southlands]]. They are a little smaller and stockier than humans, with skin that runs from sky blue or pale violet to gold or brick red, and they often show otherworldly features such as small horns or gleaming eyes.

Insular jinnborn tribes are scattered throughout the [[Dominion of the Wind Lords]], and to a lesser extent the Crescent Desert. They claim to be the first mortals to walk the world. In the beginning, they say, all the world was a vast desert beneath a fiery sun, and the jinnborn wandered where they pleased and ruled all they saw. They call themselves the sab siraat, the "people of the path."

Each tribe tends to follow a single elemental path, and the path shapes its people. Air jinnborn are proud and aloof, fire jinnborn impulsive and harsh, water jinnborn carefree but mercurial, and earth jinnborn stoic and determined. Within those broad tendencies, the tribes vary widely in their customs, laws, lore, and manners. Some jinnborn leave their path behind; the water jinnborn Ormzhad abandoned hers long ago to join [[Shibai]]'s merchant houses, and now governs the port of [[Alhijr]].

The gods of the jinnborn include Yemaja, Eshu, Patron of the Winds, [[Baal-Hotep]], [[Nakresh]], and Shu, Invisible Lord of Wind and Sky.

## The City of Brass

In the City of Brass, on the Plane of Elemental Fire, the jinnborn are the workers, team bosses, clerks, guard captains, and stewards who keep the city running like clockwork. They are mortal and primordial at once, and in the city's hierarchy they rank above the mortals who serve them.
