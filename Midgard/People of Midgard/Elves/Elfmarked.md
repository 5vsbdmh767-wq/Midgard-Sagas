Elfmarked is the name Midgard gives to those of mixed human and elven blood.

Around [[Zobeck]], most elfmarked have deep ties to the [[The Margreve|Margreve]], through direct ancestry or cultural connection. They understand fey nature in ways shorter-lived peoples struggle with, and many serve as rangers, druids, or go-betweens for the forest and the world outside it.
