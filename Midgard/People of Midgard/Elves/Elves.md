The elves are a people apart, as they are quick to remind others. They were not the first settlers of the forests and 
fields, but they taught humans and dwarves and others the arts arcane and the art of civilization. The empire they founded at [[Thorn]] and in the [[Arbonesse]], which later spread as far east as [[Perunalia|Sephaya]] and south to [[Valera]], was a wonder for the ages. Its magical roads, its slim towers, and its speedy and lethal armies maintained an age of peace that lasted until a few centuries ago.

Now the elves are a splintered race. Some few with elven blood—the so-called “elfmarked” humans—remain and can claim descent from the great elves of old. The greatest of these is the Imperatrix of [[Grand Duchy of Dornig|Dornig]], an aging queen without a clear heir. But the elfmarked are as much human as elf. The remaining true elves are rarely seen, and defined by five groups: 

- The elusive [[Windrunner]] elves of the [[Rothenian Plain|Rothenian Plains]]
- The [[Shadow Fey]] of [[The Shadow Realm]]
- The reclusive [[River Elves]] of the [[Arbonesse]], who might be the strongest and wisest of the three groups.
- The [[Dunewalker]] elves of The [[Southlands]] have never known the green shade of the Arbonesse, never seen a tree outside an oasis. They call the scorching sands of both the Crescent and Sarkland deserts their home.
- The [[Frostfell Elves]] live as nomads in the northern reaches of Midgard, from the [[The Bleak Expanse|Bleak Expanse]] eastward to the tundra north of the Tunturi Range of mountains.

## Elves of the North

Elves are exceedingly rare in the [[Northlands]]. The northern tale is that elves were created in the Vanir War between the gods, and that the dwarves drove most of them out of their old Northland territories after the Reaving, forcing them south to join their kin before the Great Retreat. The elven courts of the North were abandoned and left to ruin. In the North today, true elves belong to three groups: the alfar, the ice elves, and visitors from southern lands.

The alfar, or hidden elves, are an extremely rare people who stayed behind when their kin fled south, to protect their ancient holy sites. With no help from anyone and pressure from the dwarven empire, they withdrew into the North's most remote forests and hid their communities with camouflage and illusion. They are suspicious of outsiders, and their customs are far more insular than those of other elves.

The ice elves, also called the [[Frostfell Elves]], are nomads of the Bleak Expanse and the tundra beyond it.

## Drow

The dark elves of the underworld have long been among the enemies of the ghouls of [[The Darakhul Imperium]], whose conquest of the underworld peoples, dark elves, dwarves, svirfneblin, and dark creepers among them, has been no accident. Twenty-two years ago, drow assassins slew Vermesail, and Nicoforus the Pale rose to rule the Ghoul Imperium.

## Sea Elves

Along the coasts of the North, sea elves are counted among the aquatic threats, with selkies, that trouble coastal settlements. The ancient sea dragon [[Nereidrax]] is said to have embedded herself in the courts of the sea elves, the sahuagin, and the merfolk.
