The Frostfell elves, called ice elves in the [[Northlands]], live as nomads in the northern reaches of Midgard, from the [[The Bleak Expanse|Bleak Expanse]] eastward to the tundra north of the Tunturi Range. They roam the tundra and icy hills in small bands, hunting caribou and mammoths from the backs of reindeer and white-furred saber-toothed tigers, and they shun "civilized" lands.

The ice elves are largely insular and fiercely self-sufficient. That has earned them a reputation for secrecy, though the truth is mostly that they have little interest in the wider world. They expect no help from outsiders, and they don't give it freely either. Still, they will sometimes trade with humans, dwarves, and trollkin.

Ice elves are counted among the typical worshippers of [[Boreas (North Wind)|Boreas]], and ignorant outsiders believe they all serve him. In truth only a few ice elf communities worship Boreas and do his bidding, and most want nothing to do with the malevolent god.

Along with the hidden alfar and the occasional elven visitor from the south, the ice elves are one of the three kinds of true elves found in the North. See [[Elves#Elves of the North|Elves of the North]].
