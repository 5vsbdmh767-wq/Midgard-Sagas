The tosculi are winged wasp-folk with a reputation for overrunning and consuming every culture in their path. Their sculpted mud-and-resin hives and bulbous towers are a dark omen everywhere in the [[Southlands]], above all in the Abandoned Lands, and when the tosculi threaten to spread into a neighbor's territory, every other people involved sets its quarrels aside to stop them.

## History

The first tosculi appeared in the Southlands nearly 3,000 years ago, after the fall of Glorious Umbuso. While the titans ruled, gods and demon lords found it hard to gain a foothold in the south, but once the Great Web lay in tatters the way was open. Arbeyach, Prince of Swarms, opened portals on the broken ley lines from Bzeletalet, the Buzzing Hell of Insects, and the first tosculi colonists poured through. Several minor kingdoms, among them the realms of the Molioba Kings and the Ivory Queens of Ylla-Maurefan, were wiped out entirely as the wasp-folk stripped them of food, slaves, magic, and everything else. The tosculi built the Great Hive of Arbeyach first, then spread, raising hives across the continent.

The powerful southern kingdoms of [[Nuria Natal|Nuria]], [[Ishadia]], [[Aksaba]], Narumbeki, Lignas, and Morreg eventually checked their conquest, and so did the hive-queens' own failure to work together. The portals to the Eleven Hells fell dormant but still exist, scattered around the Abandoned Lands and elsewhere. A new portal from Bzeletalet opened about 200 years ago in the badlands near Dabu, creating the hive of Bahima Azar, and it remains active.

## The Hives

Thousands of tosculi live in each hive under a hive-queen, attended by jeweled drones who serve as her advisors and sometimes her consorts. Every queen is certain her way is best, so the major hives stand far apart and rarely cooperate. There are six major hives, among them the Great Hive and Titan's Hive, and at least a dozen lesser hives scattered across the Southlands, each ruled by its own queen. If they ever began to act together, things would go very badly for everyone else.

Following the designs of Arbeyach, the tosculi raid nearby communities for living creatures to serve as hosts for their eggs. Arbeyach is revered above every other god in the hives. Wadjet, the Mother of Cobras, is honored for her knowledge and prophecy, and Selket, Goddess of Scorpions, for her ferocity in the hunt and her healing touch. A queen's preferences are imprinted on every tosculi who hears her song, and a hive's temple can change its faith overnight if the queen decides it will.

## The Hiveless

Not every tosculi follows the queen or shares the thoughts of the hive mind. Some drones resist her orders; others are simply unsuited to the work they were bred for. Such nonconformity is not allowed, and these renegades must flee or be killed. The other tosculi call them "hiveless," and spit the word as an insult. The hiveless make their way as best they can in a world that fears them, often as adventurers or mercenaries, and Wadjet, the "wild winged prophet," is a favorite among those trying to forge a new path.

Some outcasts find their way to the Haven of the Outcasts, a hidden refugee community in the Utunga Mountains of the Abandoned Lands, where several hundred tosculi live together in a single tall chimney. They make goods to trade with the ramag and the people of Munayo, and some have become alchemists. The Haven has no rulers; its people share food and goods and settle disputes without violence. Small groups travel out on trading expeditions, but they keep the Haven's location secret.
