Eonics come from a dying civilization in the far future. To save their people, a few took to the timestream to find help in the ages before and after their own. The first of them traveled with crystal belts, but a flaw in the belts kept them from ever returning to their own time and place. They have become a displaced, desperate people, though over the generations they have outgrown the belts and gained an innate affinity with time.

Little is known for certain about eonic civilization. By their own account, their people had wiped out disease, overcome the limits of mortality, and ended war and oppression. Nobody knows what disaster befell them, though eonics speak of nova-portal reality destabilization, post-harmonic gravitic collapse, sunward drift, and other ominous, baffling things.

Eonics feel the flow of time keenly, along with their past and future selves, and they know that disaster can fall on even the strongest society. They were human once, but moving through and outside of time across generations has aged their skin and flesh unevenly, and they now look like mummified humans. Flashes of the near past and future flicker constantly across their minds, so many eonics speak in a stilted, formal way and leave long pauses in conversation.

## In Midgard

In the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]], eonic drifters who step between past and future as easily as walking into the next room are among the strange visitors seen in and around [[Oshragora]], the City Fallen into Shadow, a place with a famously troubled relationship with time.

In Midgard proper, eonics are rare enough that most people who meet one take it for one of the walking dead, and more than one has been chased out of a village by folk with torches. Those who settle tend to be drawn to [[Zobeck]], where the clockwork and the temples of [[Rava]], goddess of gears and fate, feel to them like an echo of home. The scholars of the [[Arcane Collegium]] would dearly like to question them about what is coming, and eonics are generally careful about what they say.

Eonics rarely agree on exactly when their civilization fell or how far ahead it lies, and none of them will say whether Midgard's own future leads there.
