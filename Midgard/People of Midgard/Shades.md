Most creatures pass at the moment of death and hand their souls to the afterlife. A few are caught between the world of the living and the spirit realms, and these are the shades. They come from every living people. Scholars say shades are bound by nearly unbreakable ties to friends and family; priests say the gods gave them an echo of life to serve some deeper purpose. Shades themselves give all sorts of reasons, which suggests there is no single path.

A shade looks, moves, and feels much as it did in life, but it is not flesh and blood. Its body is built from memories of the life before. As a shade is wounded, or falls out of the routines of its old life, it grows less solid: bright colors dull, voices grow distant, and memories bleed away with the pain. A shade that isn't anchored by healing magic eventually passes, its memories collapsing like an old house.

Shades are found anywhere mortals live. They are fairly well known in the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]], though few in number, and they are far more common in the realms of the darakhul there than anywhere else.

## Stygian Shades

The [[The Ever River|River Styx]] winds through the Shadow Realm, full of mindless shades that live only to consume the memories of anyone they can drown. A few of the shades bound there keep their minds but must struggle every day to remember who they were. These are the Stygian shades, the Shadow Realm's own kind. Unlike the shades of the mortal world, they come out of the water with almost no memory of their former lives, and the need to find what was lost drives them.

A new Stygian shade is hard to tell from a ghost, but most settlements in the Shadow Realm have seen enough of them to recognize one stumbling into town with no idea where it came from. Few know their own names. Those who rediscover theirs use them with pride; the rest try on name after name, looking for one that feels familiar. Like other shades, they fade as the memory of them grows distant.

The city of [[Soriglass]], a haven for the undead, has so many shades that it is called the City of Shades, and its shades are wild with curiosity about how the [[Twilight Empire|Road of Bones]] to the [[Evermaw]] was built. A small community of shades has also camped by the Styx, hoping to find and guide the unfortunate souls who wander out of its waters and to help them learn who they once were. The bearfolk druids of the Grove of the Three Rivers, who call themselves shade whisperers, tend pools fed by the Styx and have sent about twenty rescued souls on their way, some to pass on and some as Stygian shades seeking the truth of their lives and deaths.
