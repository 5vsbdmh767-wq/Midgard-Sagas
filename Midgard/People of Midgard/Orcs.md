Orcs are rare in Midgard. They were once a great people, and the tales say they were the dominant one, but now the few that remain eke out a living in the depths of the earth and in the farthest reaches of the mountains, forests, and deserts. Most surface folk have never seen an orc and know them only from frightening stories. Those who have describe a pale people, many of them nearly white.

## The White Goddess

Orcs are the first worshippers of the [[White Goddess]], a battle goddess who embodies the harshness of life and the cruel heat of the sun. She appears as a massive albino orc with ember-red eyes and carved tusks, and her faithful say she climbed out of her people's first home under the skin of the world and chased the sun god from the sky. Every solar eclipse is her holiest day, when the sun turns its empty socket toward the lands below, and the orcs come up out of the wilds to raid and kill. Her followers raise altars of bones where they make their kills. Orcs also claim that Xevioso and [[Mot]] are her slave-husbands, a title humans and dwarves find less familiar than the orcs do.

Tales of the orcs' ferocity draw others to her worship too: goblins, derro, ogres, and now and then even kobolds and centaurs.

## The Green Abyss

The best-known orcs in Midgard live in the Green Abyss beneath Yawchaka, the jungle beyond the walls of the great cities of Kush in the [[Southlands]]. They are mostly subterranean, living out of reach of the blighted plant life that rules the daylight above, and crawl up out of the Great Sinkhole into the jungle in the cooler night. The Ashen Bats, an ancient line who have learned to thrive at the jungle's edges and were once allies of the vine lords, are the largest orc tribe of the Green Abyss. Like all Green Abyss orcs, they are immune to the spores of the vine lords, who cannot turn them into tendril puppets.

Deeper down, in the Ravenous Craw, a pit that drops more than two miles into the earth, live the pale orcs, who move like albino spiders and climb out each night to hunt and forage. The largest of their many tribes is the Milk-Eye Scrugs, whose chiefs scar their bodies with poison thorns to prove their strength. The orcs of the Green Abyss know a great deal about making poisons from jungle plants. Orcs who rise again as undead are called the Chosen of Mot, and living orcs regard them with terror and awe.

The current high priestess of the White Goddess, the war prophet and general Jabresha Khemsal, is a power among the orcs of the Green Abyss.

## Orcs Among Other Peoples

Now and then an orc turns up on the surface and stays: a captive who escaped a raid and found no way home, a child of a tribe broken by war, or one who simply walked away from the White Goddess and her altars of bone. These orcs are almost always alone, and they spend their lives being stared at. Most keep to the night, the deep woods, or the lower levels of cities like [[Zobeck]], where a pale face under a hood draws less notice.

Orcs who leave the White Goddess are few, but they are not unheard of, and some of them turn to the very gods she hates. An orc who has given up the dark for the road is usually the most determined person in any room, and has to be.
