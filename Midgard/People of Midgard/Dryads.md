The lands of the fey, whether they are called the [[Silendora|Summer Lands]], the Wall Beyond, the Plane of the Fey, or simply Faerie, are thick with dark forests, and growing in those forests are countless heart trees. Each one is a dryad's home and soul. To a casual eye a heart tree looks like any other of its kind, but a practiced observer can tell at a glance: heart trees run slightly small, and they never show signs of pests or disease. So long as a dryad's heart tree stands, death cannot stop the dryad for good.

There are no settlements among the dryads as mortals reckon them. The dryads whose trees stand within one forest think of themselves as one community, and they divide themselves into neighborhoods by the species of their trees rather than by where the trees stand. Each neighborhood is overseen by a ternion of its oldest adult, its youngest adult, and its median member; the eldest seat rarely changes hands, but the other two change often enough to keep the body from growing stagnant. The ternions of a forest meet once every century or two to discuss the matters of the whole community.

Most dryads spend their long lives alone or with their neighbors. Those who go adventuring are thought of as thrill seekers and daredevils, and their communities urge them out into the mortal world to spend their excitement before it infects anyone else. They are eager to live among the shorter-lived peoples and feel the pace of their lives and the quick shifts of their moods.

## In Midgard

In [[The Margreve]], dryads are among the forest's guardians, clashing with the loggers who work its edges alongside the satyrs, plant creatures, and sentient animals of the wood, and every gathering of dryads keeps its own ancient rituals in deference to the forest. The dryads of the [[Central Margreve]] are represented in the fey courts by Willow Oakthistle, the Flowering Lady of the Feral Heart, whose hair changes with the seasons and who is more politically savvy than her sisters.

Dryads are also commonly seen in the Tomierran Forest, among the guardians the elves left to watch over their secrets, and they turn up as far afield as the baobab groves of the [[Southlands]].
