Alone among the shorter peoples, goblins refuse to be conquered or to keep up even the pretense of diplomacy, so dwarves and men treat them as vermin and drive them out wherever they meet. Pushed into the wilderness by larger peoples, goblins have become opportunistic scavengers. Many tribes fall under the sway of worgs and nightgarms, which they worship as totem beasts, and the relationship runs the opposite way to what outsiders expect: the goblins are the pets and servants of the wolves, not their masters.

Some believe goblins are the warped remnants of the gnomes who did not escape to the Wormwood, or the twisted results of experiments on halflings or humans during the Great Mage Wars. Goblins are hardy travelers and turn up thousands of miles from home, from the [[Northlands]] to the [[The Mharoti Empire|Dragon Empire]]. They have a knack for finding ancient artifacts and lost magic, and spend much of their time digging through the dust for such things to sell to the mages of [[Magocracy of Allain|Allain]].

## The Dust Goblins

The largest numbers of goblins live in the [[Western Wastes|Goblin Wastes]] at the heart of the [[Wasted West]], where they came up out of the dark after the Mage Wars, fleeing the dwarves, and made the Wastes their own. Some dust goblin tribes worship the Dread Walkers, the colossal alien things frozen across the Wastes, building towns in their shadows and reading omens in their every twitch. Others scavenge the ruins for relics. A warlord named Braagezz has lately set herself up as ruler of all the goblins.

## Across the Crossroads

Goblins turn up all over the [[The Crossroads|Crossroads]]. Forest goblins raid out of [[The Margreve]], some goblins live among the humans and dwarves of [[The Ironcrags]], and goblins are counted among the many peoples who have lived in [[Zobeck]]. In the kobold warren of Lillefor, goblins hire themselves out as bat riders and scouts. Dust goblins also serve in the Argent Rats, a band of former bandits trying to make itself a proper mercenary company.

## Shadow Goblins

When the shadow fey came into the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]], they brought servitor peoples with them, and shadow goblins were among them. Shadow goblins remain one of the realm's peoples, and hobgoblins often arrive there as mercenaries or as leaders among them. The gods of the Shadow Realm lean on the shadow goblins and the shadow fey more than the human pantheons do. Goblins are among the peoples who avoid stepping into shadow by way of the [[Yggdrasil|World Tree]].
