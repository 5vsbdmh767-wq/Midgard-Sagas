Dark-skinned and eagle-nosed, the Roshgazi are associated with their friends the minotaurs. They roam 
the western deserts of the [[Southlands]] as well as dwell in small villages in [[Capleon]], [[Kyprion]], and [[Cindass]]. They speak Roshgazi, a language closely related to [[Minotaurs|Minotaur]]. Speakers of either language can understand the other.
