The Morregi humans include the people of [[Lignas]], the tree-dwelling [[Mafri]] in the [[Abandoned Lands]], and the inhabitants of the [[Kingdom of Morreg]]. They are renowned for their love of learning, for their joyous festivals and celebrations, and for their prophetic talents, but they also have a reputation for being sharp-tongued. Dark-skinned, the Morregi speak the Southern Trade Tongue; some also speak the argot of the Western Ocean islanders.
