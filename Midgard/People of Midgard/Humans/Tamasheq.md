Widespread throughout the northern deserts, the  nomadic Tamasheq have bloodlines that reach back to the sunken empire of [[Ankeshel]] and the ancient peoples of the [[Dominion of the Wind Lords|Stone Desert]]. They are usually black-haired, and their skin ranges in color from light tan to deep brown. They speak 
Tamasheq, and the Southern Trade Tongue
