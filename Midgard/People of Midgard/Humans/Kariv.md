The Kariv are a nomadic human people who travel between the forest and civilization. Their bands range across the [[Rothenian Plain]] and along the [[Great Northern Road]] through the [[The Margreve|Margreve]], and in the forest they often serve as cultural bridges, understanding both worlds in ways settled folk rarely do.

Small caravans of Kariv travel the Northern Road more often than any other merchants dare. Each train of four or five wagons, painted with wards and sigils, carries a trickle of goods and what pass for luxuries. Somehow, more wagons arrive in [[Zobeck]] than a traveler heading the other way would pass. Some claim the Kariv have a bargain with the [[Shadow Fey|shadow fey]] that lets them use a shadow trail running alongside the road. Others say Kariv witches can make their most valuable wagons invisible to bandits.
