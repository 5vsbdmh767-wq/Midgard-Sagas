Sharp-featured and tall, with tightly curled hair, typically worn short, the zwana live in [[Narumbeki]], [[Omphaya]], and [[Terrotu]] on the edges of the Abandoned Lands, and in the floating village of Munayo on Lake Debari. They have a variety of dark skin and hair colors, and their customs and culture vary by area. Zwana speak the [[Southern Trade Tongue]] and their own regional dialects.
