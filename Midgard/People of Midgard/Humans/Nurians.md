In addition to [[Nuria Natal]] itself, Nurians reside in the desert cities of   [[Free Cities of the Desert|Siwal]], [[Free Cities of the Desert|Makuria]], and [[Free Cities of the Desert|Saph-Saph]], as well as in [[Kush]] and [[The Spice Coast]]. Nurians have mahogany colored skin and straight, dark hair, although many choose to shave their heads and wear wigs. They exude an air of confidence that stems from their long magical tradition, their sense of history, and their closeness to their gods. They speak Nurian and the Southern Trade Tongue.
