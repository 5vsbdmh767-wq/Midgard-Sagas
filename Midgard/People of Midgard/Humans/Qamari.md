Close cousins to the [[Tamasheq]], the more settled Qamari share similar origins, but settled in the [[Moon Kingdom]] under [[Minotaurs|minotaur]] rule. Some have since joined their brethren in the mountains, while others toil to rebuild [[Cindass]]. They are usually black-haired, and their skin ranges in color from light tan to deep brown. They speak [[Tamasheq]], [[Minotaurs|Minotaur]], and the Southern Trade Tongue.
