Smallest of the six major human ethnicities, the resilient, independent-minded Kushites dwell predominantly in the jungle kingdom of [[Kush]], but they can also be found in the desert around [[Free Cities of the Desert|Makuria]] and the plains to the south. Most have dark skin, but albinism is not infrequent. Kushites speak the Southern Trade Tongue; those who deal with demons or devils also speak Infernal.
