Gnomes appear in two major varieties around [[Zobeck]].

In the Free City, gnomish tinkers and inventors thrive in the culture of innovation, and their curiosity and technical aptitude make them valuable members of the guilds.

The gnomes of [[Niemheim]], in the Wormwood Forest east of [[Krakovar|Krakova]], are a different people. They are known for their red caps and their long feud with [[Baba Yaga]], and for the dark protectors they turned to in order to survive her. Niemheim gnomes keep close ties to the forest and its old magic, and outsiders tend to treat them with suspicion.
