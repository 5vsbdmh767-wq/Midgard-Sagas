Kobolds are a cunning people with draconic features. Many kobolds believe ancient dragons made kobolds in their likeness, to serve their draconic masters. Cagey and strategic, these diminutive draconic humanoids instinctually seek safety in tunnels and similar enclosed spaces such as collapsed architecture or rubble. Kobolds display an inborn mechanical aptitude which often manifests as trap-making and tinkering. The crafting of traps became such a monumental part of kobold civilization that those rare kobolds born away from their own people still gravitate toward occupations and hobbies that allow them to craft small, potentially dastardly, creations. Though they can live for decades, a penchant for curiosity claims as many kobold lives as time does.
