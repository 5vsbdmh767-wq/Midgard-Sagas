Giantkin descend from the unlikely pairing of giants and humans, and they tower over most other Northerners, standing 6½ feet or taller. Neither fully giant nor truly human, they walk between worlds: mistrusted by some, respected by others, and always seen as formidable. They are not as common as humans, but they can be found from glacier to fjord across the [[Northlands]], living hard lives of labor, war, or wandering.

The skalds don't admit to knowing any stories of the first giantkin, and few giantkin care much where they came from. Today they are a true lineage, with children of their own who take after either parent. They know they must walk their own path in lands where many look on their ancestry with disfavor, and that their deeds alone will make their name.

Every giantkin carries some sign of the giants behind them, such as the blazing hair of the fire giants or the gray skin of the stone giants, and all share the strength of their blood and a sense of fate tied to ancient powers. Some carve jotun symbols into their gear or hunt for lost relics of the Vanir Wars. Others try to put their blood behind them. Culturally, most giantkin draw more from the people who raised them than from their ancestors.

## Among Giants and Smaller Folk

Giantkin live mostly in communities on the fringes of giant-held lands like the [[The Bleak Expanse|Bleak Expanse]], [[Thursrike]], and [[Jotunheim]], or in and around human and dwarf settlements such as [[Björnrike]] and [[Jomsborg]]. True giants put giantkin at the bottom of their order, barely a step above the smaller peoples. [[Humans]] and dwarves make use of their strength, but many distrust them for their obvious ties to the giants. Caught between the two, giantkin have spread throughout the North, even into frozen lands they would otherwise avoid, looking for a place of their own.

## Ancestries

Frost giantkin are perhaps the most common in the North, descended from the nomadic glacial giants, and that connection earns them a good deal of suspicion; yet they tend to be hardworking and diligent, and often artistic. Fire giantkin are thought hot-tempered but are mostly stoic and practical. Cloud giantkin carry something of their cosmopolitan, learned ancestors, who live on flying islands. Hill giantkin have a reputation for quick tempers and big appetites, though they vary widely. Stone giantkin are reserved and slow to anger, and sometimes decorate their faces and scalps with woad. Storm giantkin are moody, and in moments of stress small sparks of their ancestors' lightning can escape them.
