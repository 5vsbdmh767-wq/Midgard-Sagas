## Leadership
- [[High Forge-Priest Korgath Ptah-mar]] (LN male spindrift dwarf cleric), Supreme Religious Authority and Keeper of the Floating Temple
## Religious Structure
**Primary Deity:** [[Ptah]] 
- The [[Spindrift]] [[Dwarves]] particularly venerate Ptah's aspects as *Shipbuilder of the Gods* and *Lord of the Eternal*
- They maintain that [[Ptah]], not [[Volund]], brought black powder knowledge to dwarves
- Their ships incorporate religious symbolism: bull-headed prows, ankh-marked sails

**Major Temples:**
- The Floating Temple of Ptah ([[Arkhos Navis]]): Mobile grand temple incorporating traditional pillared architecture with naval engineering.
- House of the Eternal Wave ([[Wavemeet Haven]]): Training center for priest-craftsmen.
- Sanctuary of the Sacred Forge ([[Tidehome]]): Traditional forge-temple with underwater shrines.
## Specialized Priesthoods

### Forge-Priests
- Traditional metalworking clergy, backbone of Spindrift religious hierarchy.
- Master both sacred metallurgy and ship component crafting.
- Maintain the eternal flames aboard all vessels.
### Powder-Priests
- [[Keepers of the Keys|Keepers]] of black powder lore.
- Maintain sacred powder magazines.
- Perform blessing rituals for ammunition.
- Train naval gunners in religious observance.
### Wave-Priests
- Specialists in maritime blessings.
- Maintain underwater shrines.
- Perform ship consecrations.
- Guide souls of drowned crafters.
### Shell-Priests
- Experts in underwater construction.
- Maintain submerged temples.
- Preserve ancient building techniques.
- Train underwater craftsmen.
## Daily Observances

### The Three Tides of Prayer

1. **Dawn Forge-Lighting (Morning Tide)**
    - Sacred lighting of ship forges using flint and steel (*never* magical fire.)
    - Recitation of the [[Shipwright's Verses]] from the [[Scrolls of Creation]]
    - Blessing of daily tools and workspaces.
    - Marking of foreheads with sacred ash from the forge.
2. **Midday Anchoring (Still Tide)**
    - Brief work stoppage for communal prayer.
    - Sharing of water blessed by [[Wave-Priests]].
    - Reaffirmation of crafting vows.
    - Traditional striking of ship's bell in Ptah's rhythm. (seven strikes)
3. **Evening Reflection (Night Tide)**
    - Inspection of day's craftsmanship.
    - Offerings of completed work at shrine.
    - Ritual cleaning of tools.
    - Burning of incense made from sacred woods.
## Sacred Spaces

### Ship-Board Shrines
- Located near the forge
- Must contain:
    - Bull-headed staff or ram symbol
    - Eternal flame (carefully maintained)
    - Craftsman's altar
    - Collection of sacred tools
    - Vessel of blessed seawater
### Floating Temples
- Built according to Ptah's sacred geometries.
- Features multiple forges at cardinal points.
- Underwater viewing chambers for meditation.
- Libraries preserving crafting lore.
- Special chambers for black powder blessing.
## Seasonal Observances

### Festival of the Deep Forge

- Annual celebration of Ptah's underwater forges
- Features:
    - Underwater forge-lighting ceremony
    - Display of year's finest crafts
    - Religious diving competitions
    - Ritual shipbuilding demonstrations

### Time of Remembrance

- Commemoration of Ptah's death and rebirth
- Marked by:
    - Temporary dousing of forges
    - Period of silence and reflection
    - Ceremonial relighting of forges
    - Feast of Renewal
