In ancient times, ogres, trolls, and fey sometimes took humans as mates, and the children of those unions are the trollkin. They combine the strength of giants with the arcane might of the darker fey, and they are strong in body and mind: warriors and raiders with a long history of taking what they want from the humans and dwarves of the [[Northlands]] and the wilder parts of the [[Dark Kingdoms]]. They have power over spirits and a deep understanding of the roads between worlds, and their warriors are known for ferocity, courage, and persistence. Even in the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]], the trollkin share an ancient bond of blood with the creatures that dwell in shadow.

Trollkin are usually 6 to 7 feet tall, and their skin favors green, ranging from green-tinged gold to aquas and blues. Those with strong troll blood are rangy, thin and wiry with long arms and legs. Others are broad and ogre-like, with thick skin and heavy jaws. Some have tusks or horns or a coat of coarse hair; others are nearly hairless, or spotted or patterned.

Customs vary from settlement to settlement and are handed down from the ancestors. Trollkin heal too quickly and too fully for tattoos to last, so some brand or scar themselves in careful patterns with acid or fire instead. Many are musical, with a taste for heavy percussion and sinuous, mournful melodies.

## In the North

Trollkin have carved out many settlements in the cold North. Their clans once brought untold misery to the North's isolated villages and farmsteads, and they earned a name as powerful but unreliable mercenaries. They were rarely welcome in the so-called civilized lands. In recent years, though, many trollkin have moved into communities across the Northlands and settled in, especially in [[Trollheim]], [[Wolfheim]], and even [[Vargrike]]. Many [[Northlanders]] speak the Trollkin tongue.

## In the Southlands

In the hills and valleys of the Southern Fringe live the trollkin septs. The southern trollkin trace their origins to the fey and other immortal or monstrous races who lived at the edges of the titan empire and took mortal lovers. When the fey fled the [[Southlands]], they left their cities and their half-fey children behind. Those first trollkin, abandoned, fell into leaderless chaos and bred among themselves and with others, which is why southern trollkin vary so widely. Zimwi-kin can make fierce lion-like leaps, harpy-kin have vestigial wings and a luring song, and gorgon-kin have petrifying breath.

For a time the civilized nations looked on the trollkin kindly, since they seemed to carry the blessings of their fey ancestors. Fear and suspicion have long since replaced that goodwill, and the septs keep to the outskirts of settled lands, hunting and raiding. Most trollkin still respect and protect the ruined fey cities in their territory, though a few loot them. They keep no written records and take little interest in their past, apart from defeats that still need avenging.

Between forty and fifty septs hold the trollkin lands of the Southern Fringe, and there are trollkin territories elsewhere in the south too, including the Trollkin Kingdom of Nmabi and the lands of the Gorgonkin. Trollkin are not very religious, but those who pray do so to the Battlelord, the Stalker, the [[White Goddess]], or Xevioso.
