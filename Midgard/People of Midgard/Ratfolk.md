Ratfolk are small, rodent-like humanoids with twitching snouts, whiskers, and long pink tails, and they work hard to avoid attention. Because they look so much like rats, they tend to gather on the fringes of human society, nesting in slum tenements, abandoned warehouses, and sewers.

They try to keep out of other people's way, but ratfolk love collecting trinkets and baubles, whether picked up off the ground, scavenged from trash, or lifted from unwary owners. Sometimes they sell their finds, and just as often they hoard them at home, to the dismay of the former owners.

Family matters a great deal to ratfolk. Whatever others think, they don't gather in packs out of necessity but for the sake of community. The leader of a ratfolk community is often called a "rat king," and is usually the most powerful among them.

## In Zobeck

The most famous rat king is the Mouse King of [[Zobeck]], who commands a sprawling circle of thieves and robbers despite the ill-fitting name. Myzi I, called the Mouse King and Lord of the Undercity, sits as one of the city's consuls, and most Zobeckers believe him a corrupt rogue. Ratfolk are the most common residents of Ragman Alley, a stretch of crumbling buildings where even the city guard goes only in large numbers and never after dark. Ratfolk also serve in the Argent Rats, a company of former bandits trying to make themselves a proper mercenary band.

The rat cult of Chittr'k'k, least of the demon cults of the cities, is widespread among ratfolk, as it is among beggars, sewer dwellers, kobold rogues, and smugglers. Dock workers, stevedores, bargefolk, and sailors all know of it, though most are wise enough to distrust its claims.

In the [[Southlands]], ratfolk are among the typical worshippers of Arbeyach, Prince of Swarms.
