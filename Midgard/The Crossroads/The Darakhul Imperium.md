The Ghoul Imperium is a subterranean kingdom of darakhul, ghouls who keep their minds and wills after death. Its tunnels stretch from beneath [[The Ironcrags]] and the [[The Margreve|Margreve]] to southern [[Krakovar]].

Raiding bands of militant ghouls sometimes harry [[Zobeck]]'s trade, though the ghouls send no ambassador to the city. Both ordinary ghouls and darakhul rise from the infected corpses of other peoples, spread by darakhul fever.

The Imperium is now ruled by Sardea the Vile, the Ghoul Emperor, from a throne in the White City. Before Sardea, the Imperium was ruled by Emperor Nicoforus the Pale.

Vilmos Marquering, the Black Fang, once commanded the Iron Legion of the Imperium. After a failed coup against Emperor Nicoforus the Pale, he fled to the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]] with his surviving ghouls.

## Rise of the Imperium
The Imperium rose more than 100 years ago, when the first darakhul answered a diabolist's summons. Hidden deep under the earth, it has bided its time, growing in strength, knowledge, and numbers. Speed and ferocity won its first conquests, and cunning magic and ruthless rage have held it together. Its people are flesh-eating worshippers of the gods of death, hunger, and darkness who come to the surface only to feed.

Its conquest of the dark elves, dwarves, svirfneblin, dark creepers, and other peoples of the underworld was no accident. Ghouls paralyze their foes and turn them into food or into replacements for their own losses. Their armies include crawling bone colossi, demonic rams, bat-winged devices trailing fire and smoke, burning skeletons, liquid zombies, and tunneling undead purple worms. Lightly armored ghoul legions march 72 miles a day through small tunnels, without resting at night; heavy legions still manage 48 miles a day through difficult ones. They carry their provisions or march beside them, when the provisions are zombies, and in a frenzy they can strip a battlefield to cracked bones.

## The Alliance with Morgau
Only their aversion to sunlight once kept the darakhul from swarming the surface. That changed when the Ghoul Emperor and the Council of the Darakhul sealed a closer alliance with [[King Lucan|Lucan]] of the [[Blood Kingdom]], and ghoul legions marched into Krakova beside the vampire armies (see [[Krakovar]]). Duke Leander [[House Stross|Stross]] serves as the Imperium's ambassador to Morgau. Lady Darvulia, the vampire Voivodina of Cloudwall, keeps closer ties with the Lords Subterranean than any other vampire; Leander Stross and Duke Drago Blackfly, ruler of the near-surface trade town of Fretlock, are regular guests at her court. The main surface entrance to the Imperium runs from the catacombs under the Great Necropolis of the Cloudwall Mountains to Fretlock, and a night market among the tombs trades the underworld's goods for blood, flesh, and slaves.
