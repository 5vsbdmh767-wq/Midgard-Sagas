The princes of Morgau and Doresh are exclusively ghouls, vampires, and other intelligent undead. Their cold hands control a nightmare realm where peasants suffer without hope or sanctuary. The principality recently conquered [[Krakovar]], its neighbor to the north, which has only escalated tensions with its other neighbors: the [[Rothenian Plain]] to the east, [[The Ironcrags|the Ironcrag Cantons]] to the southwest, and the city of [[Zobeck]] to the south.

The rulers of Morgau and Doresh believe themselves surrounded by a sea of enemies. The undead hold the passes when they must and raid the lowlands whenever they can. Morgau and Doresh draw special hatred for their tendency to wage winter wars and to fight by night, since neither condition bothers their undead soldiers.

For the most part, the wars remain small: holding a village for a season, despoiling a graveyard for new troops, laying waste to crops. The principality does not wish to make friends, only to terrify its neighbors. The peasants of Morgau, restless and always fearful, long to shake off their masters.

The [[Great Northern Road]] runs some 200 miles through the [[The Margreve|Margreve]] from Zobeck toward the Blood Kingdom.

The Greater Duchy's arms combine three provinces: a gold boar's head on dark green for Doresh, a red crown on black for Morgau, and a divided green and silver field for Krakovar.

## A Kingdom of Enemies
The Greater Duchy of Morgau, to use its proper name, joins the twin Principalities of Morgau and Doresh with [[Krakovar]], the conquered Electoral Kingdom of Krakova. It is beset on every side: reaver dwarves to the north (including [[The Wolfmark]] on its doorstep), [[Grand Duchy of Dornig|Dornig]] to the northwest, the [[Rothenian Plain]] to the east, the Ironcrag cantons to the southwest, and Zobeck to the south. [[Baba Yaga]], the Daughters of [[Perun]] in [[Perunalia]], and the exiled queen of Krakova are its enemies too. The vampires could handle any one of these alone, but together they force a careful strategy. The undead's enemies return the favor of night warfare by striking in daylight, when vampire officers rest and ghoulish sergeants hide from the sun.

The constant raids also keep the neighbors from spreading rebellion among the living. The one war the undead nobles can never win is the one for the hearts of their people. Most living folk obey and fight out of fear, since anything else brings reprisals against their families or forced enlistment in the "bone company." The army's real strength is its undead troops and its darakhul mercenaries from the [[The Darakhul Imperium|Ghoul Imperium]] below, who are both thoroughly evil and among the best soldiers the Greater Duchy can field.

## Living under Vampire Rule
In the Blood Kingdom, vampires roam openly and rule proudly, and the living are little more than property. Many slaves long to be drained and raised, to escape the "tortures of the living" and gain status. A new vampire is enslaved to the vampire that sired it until the sire is destroyed, and can sire and enslave vampires of its own, building a pyramid of control called the Tree of Chains with a free-willed master at the top. A clever slave plans ahead, hiring assassins (or do-good heroes) to destroy the sire after the bite and so break free of the Tree.

The kingdom stages hunts in its hills and forests and gladiatorial fights in its cities, where the living fight for hours while vampires cheer at the spraying blood, and the winner is drained and raised into the ruling class. Once a year, tradition demands that an elder free the winner instead. An arcane taboo marks that champion forever and protects them from all vampire predation. One such champion, the fighter Emmon Trond, has recently moved to [[Zobeck]], where he fights in the Pit of the Fierce Lynx under the [[Zobeck#Kobold Ghetto|Kobold Ghetto]].

### The Red Goddess
After Lucan's rise, the worship of [[Marena]] spread through the Principalities. Every noble took it up, though some kept a fondness for [[Charun|St. Charon]] or for Marena's husband [[Mavros]]. The common folk came to her too, fearing her as goddess of death and honoring her as goddess of lust and fertility. Worship is frequent, public, loud, and messy. Every village of any size has a blood-stained altar stone, and her name is invoked at every birth, funeral, and battle. Her greatest zealots are the Flagellants of the Red Goddess, also called the Order of the Rosy Salvation.

### The Ghost Knights
The Order of the Knights Incorporeal recruits living men and women, who serve a vampire, darakhul necromancer, or priestess of Marena. After 5 or 10 years of good service they may be "raised up" as undead foot soldiers, roughly a squire's rank. Those who survive ghoul fever or the vampire's bite without madness or blood frenzy can advance through the grades of the Order of the Red Shield: Initiate Brother or Sister, Honest Brother or Sister, Master of Arms, Captain of Arms, General at Arms, Commander, and Grand Marshall. The order provides a dappled gray or white warhorse, two lances, a red banner, a mace or longsword, and a tabard with its skull-on-red insignia; knights supply their own armor, from leather or chain for initiates to full plate for masters. Its Ghostrider Templars ride flying horses that phase through castle walls.

## Recent Events
Lucan and the Ghoul Emperor have long kept a pact of mutual aid; neither wants the other to rise too high, but both want allies if an army of paladins comes. In recent years the alliance grew closer. Duke Leander [[House Stross|Stross]], the Imperium's ambassador to Morgau, was a constant presence at Lucan's court, while the vampires sent the silver-tongued Countess Urzana Dolingen to the City of White Bone. No one knows the terms. [[Orlando|Guildmaster Orlando]] of Zobeck's [[Arcane Collegium]] believes the vampires traded ancient forbidden tomes for elite darakhul troops against Krakova, and darakhul certainly fought at every Krakovan city that fell.

At the same time, Lileshka had visions of Marena's supply of blood running dry and urged Lucan to war. Without waiting for him, she readied Sonye's warrior-priests and sent the Blood Sisters out from Cantri Abbey to spread the faith across the [[The Crossroads|Crossroads]], and they are said to have opened secret temples disguised as brothels in Zobeck and other cities. Then came the fall of Krakova (see [[Krakovar]]), after which Lucan named himself king.

Emboldened, the vampires have raided as far as [[Perunalia]] to carry off its women, among them a young archer of Clarsaya taken to Hengksburg as a bride for its Lord Mayor. Aglaii Soulay led a White Lion counterattack around the eastern Margreve into the Cloudwalls, where it ran into a mated pair of two-headed rocs and then a vampire noble's hunt; few survived, though Aglaii returned. Duchess Vasilka has since strengthened the Margreve border with archers, paladins, and priests of Perun.

### The Silent War with Baba Yaga
The vampires and [[Baba Yaga]] generally don't get along, but she has sometimes aided them for her own reasons. Thirty years ago she warned Prince Lucan about the rebellious cleric Kjord, and Lucan crushed him. Every year she is invited to celebrate the Winter Solstice in Bratislor with the Elders, and every year she sends her apologies. Her hut is sometimes seen wandering the Cloudwall valleys, but she mostly keeps away. That fragile peace is fraying: the Greater Duchy now trades with, and tentatively allies with, the gnomes of [[Niemheim]], her hated foes, while the exiled Queen Urzula has sent her "mice" to find Grandmother's hut and seek an alliance.

## Lay of the Land
Morgau and Doresh sit on the forested Brom Plateau, between the Ironcrags and the Cloudwall Mountains, with the Margreve to the south and the Rothenian Plain beyond the Cloudwalls to the east. The fast, icy River Runnel, too narrow for boats and full of trout and perch, divides Doresh in the west from Morgau in the east before running north through Krakovar to the sea at Lodezig. The land is richly fertile, with small but abundant fields, excellent red wines, and woods full of deer and boar, all of it dotted with misty villages, crumbling ruins, and the keeps and manors of the vampire nobility.
