Krakovar was the northern neighbor of Morgau and Doresh until it was conquered by [[King Lucan]]. It is now part of the [[Blood Kingdom]].

The people of these lands were known for folklore and customs against the undead long before Lucan's rise. Some 350 years ago, vampires preyed on the farmland around the Krakovan city of Lodezig, retreating into the Cloudwall Mountains whenever pursued. The charms the Krakovars handed down for generations against such creatures are now forbidden by Lucan's edicts.

The exiled Queen Urzula and her court fled to [[Grand Duchy of Dornig|Dornig]] and still work to raise resistance in the province, and the reaver dwarves of [[The Wolfmark]] hold its northern coast. See also [[Dark Kingdoms]].

## The Fall of Krakova
The vampires had infiltrated the "mice," Krakova's spy network, months before and fed it false reports. Then, on a single night, the Ghost Knights and the warrior-priests of [[Marena]] and [[Mavros]] stormed Yarosbirg Castle while a darakhul legion burst from a tunnel beneath Tannenbirg Castle to the west. Neither fortress could help the other. Tannenbirg's defenders were massacred and the castle burned; Yarosbirg fell the same night to Ghostrider Templars whose flying horses passed through its walls.

The Ghost Knights pushed north to Varshava while the darakhul marched on Wallenbirg, and neither city held long. As King Eynryk prepared to lead the Order of the Storm Knights south from Heiderbirg, a vampire spy assassinated him in his throne room. Grand Marshall Jolenta Ludmiska led the army out against the undead, but coastal soldiers unused to fighting the dead, at night, were beaten soundly. Krakova, City of the Mermaid, surrendered a few days later to Princess Hristina. Queen Urzula and the surviving royals fled into exile.

## Under Vampire Rule
Princess Hristina, Grand Marshall of the Ghost Knights, rules as Protector from Krakova. The Slahta, the thousands of landholding nobles who once elected the king and made the laws, still run much of the government, but after a series of executions they toe her line. She abolished the annual Gathering of Envoys and had the most senior Slahta, the High Posol and the Magnates, killed for treason. Wotan's temples were razed, Marena's priestesses built shrines in every town, and Perun's temples were rededicated to his mask as [[Mavros]]. New Ghost Knight commanderies stand at Heiderbirg and the rebuilt Tannenbirg.

The vampires' hold is tenuous. Their swift conquest has left them ruling a province more than seven times as populous as the Principalities. The nobles are afraid, but resistance grows among the peasants, the coast is still raided by [[Northlanders]], trolls, and [[Khazzaki]], and an alliance between the reaver dwarves and the exiled royals would be dangerous. Worse still if Queen Urzula wins over [[Baba Yaga]].

## Queen Urzula and the Court-in-Exile
King Eynryk's widow, Queen Urzula, fled with her eldest daughter Zosia Walerska, a respected priestess of [[Wotan]], and the king's brother Archduke Avgost, who commands great loyalty among the Slahta. Urzula has always been seen as a saintly figure who inspires women to excel as archers, priestesses, wives, and mothers; some credit her [[Perunalia|Perunalian]] finishing school for her support of female nobles and scholars. From [[Grand Duchy of Dornig|Dornig]], she bargains with the Wolfmark, offering to recognize its hold on the northern peninsula in exchange for military aid, while her mice supply advice and weapons to anyone in the province willing to rise. She had hoped to win the Imperatrix of Dornig to her cause, and the Imperatrix's mysterious coma broke her heart. As a last, desperate hope, she has sent agents to find Baba Yaga.
