When the vampires of Morgau invaded Krakova, Skuti the Whelp, youngest son of the king of Wolfheim in the [[Northlands]], saw a chance for glory. He landed on the northern coast with a few dozen longships, but instead of plundering and going home, his reaver dwarves stayed. They built a fortified town at Skogarholm and offered protection to Krakovans fleeing the undead, in exchange for tribute. The dwarves hate the vampires and are glad of any excuse to put an axe through a bony neck.

Within a few years Skuti held the whole northern peninsula. The fishing town of Jozht begged him for help, and he sent three thousand dwarves to take it and declared it a free city under his protection. Jozht now sends its salted herring to [[Grand Duchy of Dornig|Dornig]] and Courlandia instead of Morgau. The Wolfmark is a thorn in the side of Princess Hristina, who governs [[Krakovar]] for the [[Blood Kingdom]].

Before the Wolfmark declared itself a kingdom, the exiled Queen Urzula of Krakova was negotiating with Skuti, offering to recognize the Barony of Wolfmark if his dwarves would free more of her kingdom from the undead.

Since then, the Wolfmark has broken with [[Wolfheim]]. Its busy trade ships carried a scourge of vampirism, necromancy, and undead back across the Nieder Strait, and relations soured until the Wolfmark declared itself an independent kingdom and crowned its ruler King Skuti the First. In Wolfheim, some say they are well rid of the upstart, King Thulsamir still seethes over the revolt, and some reavers ready their longships to raid their former kin.

Its banner is a blue wolf's head facing left on a red field, and it trades in pine, amber, wool, salted fish, apples, and honey.

## Skogarholm and Jozht

| Settlement | Population | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Skogarholm | 4,300 (3,700 reaver dwarves, 600 humans) | The capital, a small fortified coastal town inside a perfectly circular wall on steep earthen ramparts. Four gates face the compass points, the northern one opening on the docks where the longships are moored, and the roads from them divide the town into quarters around Skuti's imposing longhall at the center. War songs, colorful insults, and splintering furniture pour from its mead halls from dusk until late at night. |
| Free City of Jozht | 18,000 (15,000 humans, 3,000 reaver dwarves) | Jozht, City of Fish, was an ordinary fishing town with a well-kept harbor, an earthen wall with brick towers, and a garrison used to Northern raids. When the undead invaded, its people asked Skogarholm for help and promised to rise in support. Being called a liberator as well as a conqueror appealed to Skuti's ego. Jozht is still a wealthy fishing town, exporting so much salted fish and herring that it must import salt from Salzbach, and keeping that salt route open is vital to it. |
