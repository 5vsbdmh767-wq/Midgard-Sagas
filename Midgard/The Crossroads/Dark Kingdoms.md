Northeast of [[Zobeck]], past the [[The Margreve|Margreve]], lie three kingdoms that seasoned travelers call the Triumvirate of Evil. No one goes there without a desperate need or a very large profit in mind, and the wise ones plan their escape before they arrive.

- [[Blood Kingdom]]: the Greater Duchy of Morgau, ruled by [[King Lucan]] and his vampire nobility, now including conquered [[Krakovar]]
- [[The Darakhul Imperium]]: the Ghoul Imperium, an empire of ghouls beneath Morgau that has enslaved the other races of the underworld
- [[Niemheim]]: the Nine Cities of the gnomes in the Wormwood Forest, who turned to devils to escape [[Baba Yaga]]

On Krakovar's northern coast, the reaver dwarves of [[The Wolfmark]] have carved out a barony of their own.

The [[Great Northern Road]] runs from Zobeck through the Margreve into Morgau, and Morgau's Ghost Knights collect tolls at the commanderies along it.

##### Recent Events
About ten years ago, Lucan sealed a closer alliance with the Ghoul Emperor and invaded the Electoral Kingdom of Krakova. A darakhul legion broke out of a tunnel under a border castle while the Ghost Knights stormed another, the Krakovan king was assassinated in his own throne room, and the city of Krakova surrendered to Princess Hristina. Lucan named himself king and made Krakovar the third province of his realm. Queen Urzula and the surviving royals fled to [[Grand Duchy of Dornig|Dornig]], where she is trying to raise resistance, and reaver dwarves from across the Nieder Straits have seized the northern city of Jozht for themselves.

Since then the vampires have grown bolder. They have raided as far as [[Perunalia]] to carry off its women, and a White Lion counterattack ended in disaster in the Cloudwall Mountains. The priestesses of [[Marena]], the Red Goddess, have spread their worship through Krakovar and, it's whispered, opened secret temples disguised as brothels in Zobeck and other cities. Morgau has also begun trading with Niemheim, which has not improved its relationship with Baba Yaga.
