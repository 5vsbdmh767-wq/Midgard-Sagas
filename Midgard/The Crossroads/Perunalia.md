Perunalia sits at the mouth of the River Argent, several days downriver from [[Zobeck]], and controls the river's trade with the [[Despotate of the Ruby Sea|Ruby Sea]]. It is ruled by the demigoddess Vasilka Soulay, daughter of [[Perun]], though she takes more after her human mother than her father. She is a scholar and a patron of the arts, and her people know her for courage and stern mercy. She did inherit her father's head for tactics, and it has saved the duchy more than once.

The duchy is ruled and defended by women. Its guards, soldiers, merchants, and scholars are women, and its most famous warriors are the paladins of the Order of the White Lions. Men keep households, raise children, and work trades like smithing, farming, and shipbuilding, but they can't own property or attend school. Women who flee the Blood Kingdom, the Ruby Despotate, or the [[Magdar]] lands are welcomed and put to work. Perunalian archery is famous across Midgard and said to rival the elves of the [[Arbonesse]]. Girls receive their first bow at 14, and an archer's rank is marked by the feathers she wears.

Perunalia keeps learning the way other lands keep armies. Sephaya was an elven capital before the Great Retreat, and its academies and the Royal Konytari library, which opens to the public one day a week, take daughters from all over the [[The Crossroads|Crossroads]]. Zobeck and Perunalia have been allies for about 90 years, and the duchy fights alongside the [[Magdar Kingdom]] against the [[The Mharoti Empire|Mharoti]], who besiege Sephaya often. [[Centaurs|Centaur]] raiders, the slavers of the Ruby Despotate, and lately the vampires of the [[Blood Kingdom]] all treat its women as prizes, and the duchy feels surrounded. Zobeck merchants will tell you Perunalians drive a hard bargain and sometimes close it at sword point.

The duchy's symbol is a red shield with blue lightning and a white rampant lion. It is small but well ruled, trading in fine cloth, divine magic, fish and oysters, furniture, honey, oranges, jewelry, and timber. None of it will raise the land to glory, but it keeps its people healthy and gives them time for trade, reflection, and art. Its learning is practical rather than arcane: mathematics, botany, agronomy, and architecture. Oddly, worship and theology are neglected. The duchess answers prayers and grants boons to a small priesthood, and most of her people also follow the Green Gods ([[Yarila & Porevit]]) and [[Lada]]. Old rivalry with the [[Magdar]] has faded with the Mharoti on both their doorsteps, and the duchy hires whole centaur clans against the dragon legions.

Visitors remark on the all-female city guards and the duchess's horse guards. The usual Perunalian reply is a wry "We have no idea why you trust men with swords and fire, given how they act with it." In war, the generals and marshals of the White Lions raise levies of men as well as women, and the men are said to fight harder for the amazons who lead them. Zobeck's Azeleanara Perunisis, a retired Perunalian emigrant, sponsors scholarships for girls to attend Sephaya's schools.

## Duchess Vasilka Soulay
Her mother was a human woman called Mother Illyena. Her Divine Transcendence spends much of her time in study and contemplation. She is a fair ruler and a patron of learning and the arts who collects pottery, sculpture, and paintings from all over Midgard, especially the [[Seven Cities]], and believes every student should see such works. She holds bards in high esteem and often invites female bards to Sephaya; some stay. She has not married or produced an heir, being too absorbed in her studies, though her baronesses and priestesses keep urging her to.

## Cities and Towns
Sephaya is the capital, famous for its gardens, where the duchess is said to speak with [[Lada]], Sif, and [[Yarila & Porevit]]. Clarsaya guards the north against the centaurs from its old elven tower, with the White Citadel, the training grounds of the White Lions, just outside. Orkasa is a river trade city whose barge polers, many of them [[Kariv]], feud with the locals. The duchess's Daughter's Feast is held at a winter palace on an island near Orkasa (see [[Customs of the Crossroads]]).

## Archery in Perunalia
The temples say [[Perun]] himself taught the amazons the bow, and his daughters have since added secrets learned from the Rothenian centaurs, the windrunner elves, and others they found worthy. To be a woman without archery skill in the duchy is to be an outcast. Girls receive small bows and arrows in a rite of passage at 14 and go to the best archery schools in the land, earning further rites and colored feathers at monthly tournaments. Outsiders think the feathers are decoration, but they mark an archer's rank; a red feather means mastery. At the Duchess's Fair, the famous summer fair and tournament, invited archers compete for land and titles, and the winner is named Grand Maiden. Countess Marquerra Zorin has held the title for over a decade.
