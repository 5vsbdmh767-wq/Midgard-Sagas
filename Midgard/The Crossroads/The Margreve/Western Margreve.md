The Western Margreve is everything west of the [[Great Northern Road]], and it is the oldest and darkest part of [[The Margreve]]. The canopy is so thick that the forest floor sits in permanent dusk, and you rarely see more than thirty to sixty feet in any direction. The ground is mostly flat, broken by the hill of [[Castle Shadowcrag]] near the middle and the rise of the Dwarven Cantons to the west. Streams run down from the high ground and pool in small, still lakes, and in places the undergrowth is so dense that a canoe is the only way through.

No human village is known to survive here. A day's walk in from the edge you start finding the ones that failed: cottages sagging into the moss, statues and stone arches swallowed by vines. The forest out here does not want to be lived in, and it shows. The head of the School of Star and Shadow Magic at [[Zobeck]]'s [[Arcane Collegium]] insists that shadow magic runs through these trees, pointing to Shadowcrag, the shadow fey who still come out of the west, and the hounds heard baying at midnight. His first lesson for anyone going in is to take the local superstitions seriously, especially the ones about shadows and death. Margrevians say never to sleep against the west side of a tree, or its morning shadow will swallow you.

Somewhere in this region lies the Western Heart, one of the [[The Margreve#Hearts of the Forest|hearts of the forest]], where the Old Margreve's power runs thick and tinged with shadow.

| Ashen Glades | Two long scars of burned and broken trees across the Western Heart, where legend says [[Perun]] once hurled a pair of lightning bolts. Nothing has grown there since. Storm spirits called ala nest in the dead trunks and drive off anyone who comes near. |
| Briarblack Hill | A hill of black buds and briars ringed by a field of baby's breath. The story goes that a great black dragon was brought down out of the sky here long ago and never quite rotted away, and that the hill is its grave. |
| [[Castle Shadowcrag]] | A ruined castle on a crag overlooking the whole western forest. Kobold miners, shadow fey, and the noble house of [[House Stross\|Stross]] all held it once, and later a dwarf clan sold starmetal and shadow-forged weapons out of it to buyers who didn't ask questions. |
| Genezek Kraj | The ruins of old temples, now just a scatter of stone arches rising out of the brush. Ravens and blackbirds crowd the arches and the treetops, and treasure hunters still talk about relics buried below. |
| Miners' Lament | A dry gulch on the southwest edge where gold, opal, and glowing bronze lie right in the walls. The first dwarves to swing a pick there were buried by a flood of red mud, and so has nearly everyone since. Locals say the gulch turns greed into the gold it uses as bait. |
