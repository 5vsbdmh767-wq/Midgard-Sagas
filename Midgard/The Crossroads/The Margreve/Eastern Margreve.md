The Eastern Margreve is the third of [[The Margreve]] that has crossed the Cloudwall Mountains and spilled down toward the [[Rothenian Plain]]. It is the youngest part of the forest, which means its trees are only a few thousand years old. Conifers cover the heights and give way to broadleaf trees as the land drops toward the steppe. The trunks stand far apart, like the pillars of a palace, and you can see a long way under them, but the canopy is just as thick as anywhere else, and the trees all look alike. People get lost here more easily than anywhere else in the forest.

Water is the real danger. A few small lakes hide among the trees, but finding them is hard, and travelers who don't bring their own often die of thirst. For the same reason there is little game, and many animals that drink from Grandfather's Tears have grown tumors, spines, and patches of shell. Undead and stranger things roam the east too, though not often.

Most visitors describe the Eastern Margreve as empty and dreamlike. There are long stretches where nothing moves and no bird sings, and the silence at night, with no insects or owls, is unnerving. Stories tell of people who walked in young and came out old, or the other way around, and ghosts are seen here more often than in the rest of the forest. Some scholars think the barrier to the Astral Plane is thin in the east. The forest has no heart here yet, but many believe one is about to form.

##### Places Travelers Speak Of

| Place | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Braidwood Village | A [[Centaurs\|centaur]] village where the [[The Old Ways\|Old Ways]] still rule. Every home is grown rather than built, shaped from living trees, with sculptures of battling ancestors braided into the grounds. The Braidwood centaurs are famous hunters and fight the alseid and the Rothenian centaurs over game and water. |
| Caves of Sorrow | A shallow cave high in the Cloudwalls, shaped from below like an eye with a tear. A sect of [[Kariv]] climbs to it to make offerings to a local forest godling, who lets them speak with their dead. |
| Djurdja Falls | The eastern Cloudwall streams gather here and pour into the mouth of a huge cave, falling a thousand feet into darkness. Strange plants hang their long tendrils over the lip to drink the spray. |
| Lost Heart's Copse | A stretch of forest where ghosts of stooped old men hunt through the brush for lost things and lost loved ones. The living who enter can no longer see the living. |
| Salt Springs | Salty, poisonous water seeps up among the rocks and leaves pillars of salt the size of a man. A kobold family quietly harvests these "salt men" and sells them to alchemists, and salt is worth a great deal to forest folk. |
| Sleepwalker's Hill | A hill in the far northeast where hanging plants drop pollen like snow. The aridni grind it into dust for their arrows, and they seem to be the only ones who can remember the place. People and beasts wander the hill with no memory of yesterday. |
| Edgewater Village | A village about a hundred miles upriver from the forest's edge, known for growing living canoes that can survive the waters of Grandfather's Tears. |
