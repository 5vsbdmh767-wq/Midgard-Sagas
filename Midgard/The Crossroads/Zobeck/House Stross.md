The Stross ruled the city and province of [[Zobeck]] for nearly 600 years, marrying well, fighting off invasion from the magocracies to the west, and holding their own against [[Blood Kingdom|Morgau and Doresh]] to the northeast. They did it the old-fashioned way, with fistfuls of silver and a ruthless cruelty that scattered their enemies. Their seat was Castle Stross, today the ruin of [[Castle Shadowcrag]].

The family's wealth flowed from river trade and deep silver mines worked by enslaved [[Kobolds|kobolds]]. Their rule rested on two pillars: a tight alliance with the Order of the Undying Sun, and a bargain with the [[Shadow Fey|shadow fey]]. In exchange for fostering their eldest son and daughter in the shadow fey courts, the Stross gained shadow magic and the right to rule. They offered some worship to [[Sarastra]] but never fully embraced her, and they taught their children the secrets of power over the fey.

##### Stross and Bemmea
More than 600 years ago, Abderus Stross became the first merchant of Zobeck to form close ties with the mages of [[Magocracy of Allain#Bemmea|Bemmea]], serving as apprentice to Exarch Salatis, an archmage of long years and deep mystic understanding. When Abderus came home with a shadow fey courtesan on his arm, the house changed forever. It split into loyal followers of the sun god Khors and those eager to dabble in the mystic arts offered by the shadow fey, demons, and worse. After the Great Mage Wars, that second branch became obsessed with recovering the lost lore of the West, above all orichalcum and vril technology, and sponsored expeditions into the [[Western Wastes|Wastes]] to find it.

==**The Great Revolt**==
Over time the costs of defense and the nobility's upkeep grew steep, and the knights and landowners took more and more. When Stross guards dragged a ten-year-old boy, Halsen Hrovitz, from the streets for denouncing them as leeches, the city rose. The silversmith Abelard and the journeyman wizard Marcenzo gave the mob direction. Citizens, artisans, and kobolds freed from the Stross silver mines broke the Stross forces at the Oros Bridge over two days of fighting. That night the mob stormed Strossheim and hanged the family from the battlements, youngest to eldest.

The Zobeck Revolt is now some ninety years past.
