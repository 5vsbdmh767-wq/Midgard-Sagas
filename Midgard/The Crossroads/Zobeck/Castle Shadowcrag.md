Once the home of [[House Stross]] (and called Castle Stross, or Strossheim, at that time), this black-stone ruin sits on a hill overlooking the Western [[The Margreve|Margreve]], a day's ride north of the Oros Bridge.

On the last night of the Great Revolt, the mob stormed the castle, freed prisoners from the dungeons, and carted off food and gold. By dawn, more than 40 bodies hung from the battlements. The village below burned the same night. Many looters sought the hoard of silver the Stross family vaults were said to contain, though they never found it. Some vanished in the search, and some were driven mad by what they saw below the hill.

The castle briefly served as the Free City's headquarters. Five years later, the army abandoned it as the number of missing or dead sentries mounted, and the rumors of hauntings date to that time. The dwarves of Clan Grimbold stayed on for a time. Wizards and cultists visited them to buy things best not discussed, and fighters came for weapons of starmetal or pure shadow not available anywhere else.

Most Zobeckers consider the ruins haunted by fey and by dark memories.
