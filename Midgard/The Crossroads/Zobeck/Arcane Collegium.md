The Arcane Collegium is [[Zobeck]]'s school of wizardry, held in high regard across Midgard and specializing in clockwork and shadow magic. By tradition, the Free City Council always includes the guildmaster of the Collegium.

The Collegium lies behind the Steam Gate, and the stairs to it are always slippery. Visitors who are admitted are most often hired help or someone offering great treasures in exchange for the Collegium's wisdom. Inside are two courtyards, a black tower, the large gray hall of the summoners, underground labs, and sturdy alchemical bunkers, all marked with tarnished silver runes along the eaves, gates, and windows. Gargoyles, clockwork traps, and undead under the masters' control guard the grounds.

The positions of master summoner and master of stars and shadows are vacant, last held by Linnea Thorn and Sariel of [[Blood Kingdom|Morgau]]. Mistress Thorn was recently murdered, and Master Sariel retired to the mountains. Roughly two dozen apprentices study under the masters, including kobolds, tieflings, dwarves, and humans, and a staff of about 40 keeps the place running.
