Every nation in the [[The Crossroads|Crossroads]] gathers its people at least once a year for a celebration, a tournament, or a day of judgment. Each one says a lot about the people who hold it.

##### Daughter's Feast
Held in [[Perunalia]] in Redleaf, after the harvest is in and the debts are paid, before the winter raids from the [[Rothenian Plain]]. The duchess holds it whenever she pleases, usually no more than once every three years or so, at a winter palace on an island near Orkasa. She always invites her father, [[Perun]], and sometimes he comes, arriving in a crack of thunder with some of his other children. When he brings one of his wives, the feast turns wild: thunder, great gifts, sudden deaths, visions, and a lot of drinking.

##### Dwarfmoot
The dwarves of [[The Ironcrags]] feud constantly over broken engagements, mining claims, and drunken insults. Every spring, within 30 days of the passes opening, the cantons meet at Bundhausen and weigh every feud. To keep a feud going, each side pays a feudgeld. If both pay, it goes on. If neither pays, it ends. If only one pays, the money goes to the side that wanted peace. Marriages, new chieftains, raids, airship launches, and mercenary contracts are all announced at the Dwarfmoot, and it sets the mood for the whole year.

##### Knight's Call
Two orders hold their gatherings in the Crossroads. The Order of the Storm meets at the White Citadel in Perunalia, and its call is the loud one. Valkyries, Perunalian women, Northland dwarves, [[Magdar]] riders, and a few elfmarked of [[Grand Duchy of Dornig|Dornig]] drink and swear huge oaths to raid the Blood Kingdom or kill trolls by the score. A few actually follow through. The Order of the Undying Sun meets at Smoltenberg in the [[Magdar Kingdom]], where its knights test their skill, shuffle their officers, and senior knights are required to attend.

##### Queensmeet
Every Low Summer, Widowed Queen Dorytta of the [[Magdar Kingdom]] holds a great tournament in the fields outside Cronepisht. Her vassals must attend, and she invites knights from Perunalia, [[Zobeck]], the [[Seven Cities]], and sometimes even Morgau. The champion wins the Queensmeet Crown, a helm set with garnets and a single ruby, and must defend it the next year. Courtships, marriages, and deals happen all around the lists, so it's worth going even if you've never held a lance.

##### Dwarven Thralls
"Ten years under the mountain" is a grudge many people in the Crossroads carry. The Ironcrag dwarves raid the lowlands, mostly the Seven Cities, the Magdar Kingdom, the [[Blood Kingdom]], and Zobeck, in spring and summer. They ransom captured dwarves for gold, but anyone else taken in war serves ten years in the mines and smelters, marked with a brand or tattoo on the shoulder. Thralls are fed and rested, not starved or beaten, but the work is brutal and some die. Each spring the dwarves turn hundreds of freed thralls loose at the top of a pass with a warning never to fight the dwarves again. Most listen. A few spend the rest of their lives raiding mule trains and bringing down airships.
