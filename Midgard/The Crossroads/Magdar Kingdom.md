The rolling hills and grasslands south of [[Zobeck]] are the provinces of the Magdar Kingdom, a place rich in traditions of chivalry and warfare, where the Widow Queen Dorytta holds tourneys every summer and hires mercenaries from [[The Ironcrags]] when war threatens. The Magdar Kingdom must fight often to defend its borders, both to the east against the wild tribes of the [[Rothenian Plain]] and to the south against the akinji skirmishers and dragonblooded sorcerers of [[The Mharoti Empire]].

Thanks to this constant conflict, one of the greatest weapons of the [[Magdar]] is the war wagon. These iron-reinforced wagons circle into a tall wall on the open plains, a laager almost as good as a palisade, with firing slits for crossbows and even ballistae. The rest of the kingdom's strength lies in the Black Army itself, a disciplined, professional standing army.

Zobeck, the Magdar Kingdom, and [[Perunalia]] recently renewed their century-old alliance of mutual aid. Duchess Baretta, daughter of the Widow Queen, signed on behalf of the Magdar.

The kingdom's symbol is a field quartered white and orange, with the red sun of Khors at the center.

## The Crown
The Magdar Kingdom is a monarchy with tight bonds between the throne, its two great knightly orders, and the peers of the realm. Every noble is a vassal of the crown, expected to raise troops, provide for the common welfare, and serve in the army when called. It has kept good alliances with its neighbors, absorbed smaller states over time, and held off the worst of the Mharoti assaults.

### Queen Dorytta
Her formal title is Her Illuminated Majesty, Queen Dorytta the Fair, 5th Horse Queen and 2nd of that Name, Commander of the Black Army, Beloved of Khors, Lady of Cronepisht, Marchioness of the [[The Margreve|Margreve]], and Lady of the Argent. Her subjects call her the Widow Queen. She lost her husband, King Stefanos, and her eldest son Zsigismond at the battle of Marroc's Stand, when the Magdar wagons rolled south to support [[Rumela|Illyria]] and were beaten by a far larger army under the flame dragon Glauvistus, a fire giant general, and a wormhearted warlock. Her grief was public and the realm was in real danger, since the Mharoti could have swung north to Cronepisht. They turned east to plunder Illyria instead, and the queen knows how close it came. Since then she has drawn closer to centaur mercenaries of the [[Rothenian Plain]], hired [[Perunalia|Perunalian]] archers and dwarven stonemasons to strengthen the southern border, and begun an aggressive campaign of castle building.

Her surviving daughter, Duchess Baretta, is heir to the throne and commands the Wands of Runkelstad, the realm's arcane military forces, though she is young for the post at 17.

### The Old Barony of Runkelsheim
Before Dorytta's marriage to Stefanos united the crowns 20 years ago, the Barony of Runkelsheim was a semi-independent vassal state ruling Runkelstad and the lands north of the River Argent. It was known for magical prowess, and constant feuds with centaur tribes gave it a strong light cavalry tradition. Its people still think of themselves as a little apart, and the heir to the Magdar throne holds the title of Duke or Duchess of Runkelsheim.

### Faith
Most of the land is devoted to Khors and [[Lada]]. After years of decline, the worship of Khors has found new strength since shadow fey merchants and envoys arrived in Zobeck and, more recently, Cronepisht. Their dark clothes and shadow magic unsettle the human population, and the priests of Khors offer bright protection: sun amulets, wards for doors and windows, and blessings that keep fey glamours from fooling merchants and buyers in the markets.

## Armies of the Magdar
The kingdom spends its wealth on its armies and its temples to Khors and Lada, and sometimes the two are the same thing, as castles become temples and temples become small fortresses.

### The Order of the Undying Sun
The order is strongest in the Magdar Kingdom and in [[The Ironcrags#Grisal, the Black Canton|Grisal]], with a lesser presence in Zobeck. Hundreds of knights serve in dozens of commanderies from Wagenstein to the Ruby Sea, and the queen would never leave Lord Clarikon out of her war councils. Its scarred, devoted warriors are the elite backbone of the army. It is famed for heavy cavalry but raises most other troops too, rarely archers: warrior-priests of Khors in war wagons, swift White Riders who scout and skirmish, and the "Sunset Regiments" of devout pikemen who serve a season for pay and honor under Provost Marshall Ulrichus Valotto, equipped by the order and better than most levies. Besides Wagenstein, the order holds Smoltenberg, several small keeps in Grisal, and a small commandery named Gelburg outside Zobeck. It lost the castle of Zamak Petros when Illyria fell.

Joining is simple: swear allegiance to the local commander, swear to obey the priests of Khors and uphold the sun god's creed, and prove yourself in mounted combat with lance and sword. Recruits get simple arms and armor (not warhorses) and serve as squires to a veteran knight for a month or a year, until they earn spurs, a sword, and the title of knight-novice.

The elite of the order, and most of its officers, are paladins of Khors, striking in white- and yellow-plumed helmets and blue cloaks on white or dappled gray horses. They are generous to friends and implacable against foes. Most are human, with some dwarves and centaurs. Zobeck's council gives them little respect, because the order backed the losing side in the Revolt, but everywhere else their white plumes mean righteous might.

### The Order of the Storm
The rougher, less disciplined outriders of [[Perun]], a much smaller faith among the Magdar, are the sun-knights' great rivals. [[Humans]], dwarves, and centaurs ride with them, led by Larina Perunal, daughter of Baroness Margit of Sveretska. They are excellent, fearless light cavalry, and in peacetime spend a lot of effort drinking and talking of pilgrimages to the Seat of [[Mavros]] or preaching to the Rothenian centaurs. Their castle at Valtros guards the Mharoti border, and the crown honors their service. Their detractors say, "Not everyone can be a servant of the sun."

### War Wagons
The copper-clad Magdar war wagons are built in Cronepisht with iron wheel rims from the Revskaya Foundry, leather yokes from the Ironcrags, and oxen from the best herds, painted in the land's traditional red, green, and yellow. Their oak or ash is enchanted and strengthened by alchemy, and the grounded copper sheathing turns lightning and fire, a vital shield when Mharoti elementalists unleash jinn of thunder, wind, and flame. Neighboring wagons have front and rear wheels of slightly different widths so they can be chained together into a tabor, or wagon camp, and the larger ones carry ballast stones underneath so they are almost impossible to overturn. Guards atop the wagons see farther and spot enemies sooner.

Many wagons are not the crown's. Zobeck's [[Zobeck#Gear District|Gear District]] outfits company wagons for Ironcrag pike and crossbow companies, [[Krakovar|Krakovan]] exile companies, and the amazon companies of Perunalia, traveling in groups of 2 to 10. Their strong axles can carry a great deal of treasure, and a successful company sometimes boasts, "We carried gold and silver ballast, so rich were we!"
