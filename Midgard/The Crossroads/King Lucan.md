King Lucan is the vampire ruler of the [[Blood Kingdom]], the Greater Duchy of Morgau, Doresh, and [[Krakovar]]. Since seizing power in Morgau, Lucan, now styling himself a king, has tripled the lands under his sway, spreading his reign north to the shores of the [[Northlands|Reaver Coast]] and the Bay of Ghed.

Lucan's edicts forbid the old folk charms that once protected the people of these lands against vampires. [[Zobeck]] and its allies in the Argentine Alliance stand against him.

## Rise to Power
Lucan, Lord of the Shroud-Eaters, was born in the [[Despotate of the Ruby Sea|Ruby Despotate]] and arrived in the Principality of Morgau some 300 years ago. He soon became the right-hand man (and possibly the lover) of its ruling prince. Shortly after, the priests of [[Lada]] sickened and died, the priests of the Green Gods fled into the forests and were hunted down, and the prince disappeared. Many of the court nobles became nocturnal servants of their new prince. Within a year Lucan held both Morgau and Doresh, and anyone who asked where the old prince had gone found themselves exiled to the poorest fiefs with the hardest tasks.

Under Lucan, worship of [[Marena]] spread through the land. Thirty years ago, warned by [[Baba Yaga]], he crushed the rebellious cleric Kjord and cemented his hold. After conquering [[Krakovar]] with the help of the [[The Darakhul Imperium|Ghoul Imperium]], he proclaimed himself king of the Greater Duchy.

He holds court at Bratis Castle in Bratislor at the Summer and Winter Solstices, where all the Elders and their spawn must appear. See [[Blood Kingdom]] for his realm, his nobles, and his court.
