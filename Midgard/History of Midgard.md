Midgard is a flat world, and every people in it tells a different story of how it was made. The dragons say the World Serpent Veles shaped it from his own coils, the giants say it was carved from the body of the first giant, and the [[Northlanders]] say it rose out of the void of [[Ginnungagap]]. Most folk in the [[The Crossroads|Crossroads]] don't worry much about which is true. What they do know is that the world is old, that it has fallen into ruin more than once, and that the elves left it less than five hundred years ago.

Scholars count dates many different ways (see [[Calendars]]), so most people just say how many years ago something happened.

##### The Great Retreat
Nobody outside the Summer Lands knows why the elves left. Their roads closed, their legions marched out of Thorn and Valera for the last time, and their cities stood empty within a week. Only the [[Arbonesse|River Court]] in the Arbonesse stayed behind, and it shut its borders. The elfmarked were left to fend for themselves, and the gnomes and halflings who had served the elves were suddenly on their own.

Dwarves and humans moved into the empty lands, and the wizards of the west, with no one left to restrain them, turned on each other. The summonings of the Mage Wars and the coming of the Isonade turned the western kingdoms into wasteland, and the fey roads became the shadow roads (see [[Ley Lines & Shadow Roads]]).
