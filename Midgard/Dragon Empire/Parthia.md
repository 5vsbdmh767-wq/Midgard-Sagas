Parthia is a human kingdom that pays tribute to [[The Mharoti Empire]] and worships the dragon gods as elemental powers. The dragons have thought about conquering it outright, but it already pays well and makes a useful buffer against Khandiria and the horse tribes of the Qaen and the [[Rothenian Plain]], so they haven't bothered.

Parthians are merchants and craftsmen who weave gold and silk cloth and breed horses tough enough, they say, to walk from Cathay to [[Nuria Natal|Nuria]]. They are quick to take offense and feud among themselves, but famously generous to travelers from far away. Isphahan is a city of orchards and blue and purple towers, Rhaga holds the Collegium Elemental and its fire stone of prophecy, and the horse fair at Mardis draws centaurs and [[Khazzaki]] riders every year.

South of Parthia lie the Red Wastes, a desert of red sand and old bones where the Mharoti and Khandiria fight their great battles. It is full of broken war wagons, ghosts, gnolls, and necromancers who find all the dead they could want.

Parthia's symbol is a red eagle on white, and it trades in gold, silk, horses, dates, pistachios, copper, iron, cotton, and sheep. Parthians are a bloodthirsty people, less prone to feuds than the [[Beldestan|Beldestani]] but quick to take offense, and yet extremely gracious to travelers: even their bandits can be talked into hospitality for a few silvers if a merchant proves he comes from farther away than the Mharoti lands. Neighbors and fellow Parthians get no such courtesy. Its temples are famous for their wealth.

## The Red Wastes
Dust goblins, dragonkin deserters, gnoll tribes, and ghuls roam the red sands between the empire and Khandiria. The ley lines are nowhere near as snarled as the [[Western Wastes]]', but water and shelter are scarce.
