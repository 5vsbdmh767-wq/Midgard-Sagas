The White Mountain Marches are the high wilderness between [[Rumela]], [[Triolo]], and the [[Magdar Kingdom]]. No lord has ever really ruled them. They were once rich in silver, marble, and iron, with goat-herding villages in the valleys, but now there's little left besides a few heavily guarded mines and the forts of bandits and petty warlords. It is a fine place to hide if you are an outlaw, an Illyrian holdout, or a monster.

The town of Parzsan was always a city of bandits and exiles. The [[The Mharoti Empire|Mharoti]] have claimed it, and the dragonborn Lord Khayr al-Agara holds it with thousands of troops to protect the mine shipments, but his gate still shuts at sundown and he pays bounties for raids on the [[Magdar]] border.

The real terror of the Marches is the ghost folk. Centuries of battles between Illyria and the dragons soaked the ground with dragon's blood and spell residue, and something came up out of it. The ghost folk are misshapen, chalk-faced humanoids who burrow through the earth at unnatural speed and burst out in silent waves to drag caravans and villages underground, as sacrifices to the [[White Goddess]]. They always come back for their dead. The ruins of Old Brezla, a city that fell to blood magic, are thought to be one of their strongholds, and the Lonely Spire, a wizards' tower that sealed itself when the dragons came, has swallowed everyone sent inside since.

Lord Khayr al-Agara's 8,000 troops guard the shipments from the Zlato Mine to Zaldiri and Harkesh, and a Mharoti flag flies from the Emerald Tower, Parzsan's six-sided keep overgrown with poisonous vines. Half his troops are always on the road south and the rest fight the ghost folk, and rocs, basilisks, and wolves roam the valley. Parzsan still grows little food, buying from Triolo, Droisha, Cronepisht, Salba, and even Zaldiri; it runs on gold and iron, and it takes a heavy hand to keep its self-styled mercenaries and hedge knights from raiding in every direction. Lord Khayr pays bounties for raids on Salba and the Magdar border, and 10 gp for the skull or ears of one of the ghost folk.

## The Ghost Folk
Previously thought legendary, the ghost folk are dedicated to the [[White Goddess]] and carry off sacrifices to power a great ritual decades in the making, which they believe will cleanse the surface of the "despoilers." The few bodies ever recovered suggest a people born of degenerate interbreeding between barbarian humans and deep orcs. South of Parzsan, the Bone Pit spews sulfurous fumes from an open wound in the earth lined with scaffolding made of the bones of their sacrifices; they believe it a blessed road between their deep realms and the surface.

## Old Brezla and the Lonely Spire

| Place | Description |
|---|---|
| Old Brezla, City of Ghosts | Once a free city of orchards, grain, hops, and vineyards devoted to Ceres and [[Rava]], until a trollkin necromancer named Sable and a warlock named Armanius made themselves tyrants and turned it into a charnel house of blood magic. Their rule collapsed, but the town square is stained with fresh blood each dark of the moon, foul fogs roll through after sunset, and the cobblestones moan. Some believe Armanius still lives in a gilded tomb. |
| The Lonely Spire | A wizards' chapter house in the high peaks where Archmage Kolos the Lame trained mages of uniformly excellent character and skill. When the dragons came, the duchess sent desperate pleas for help; the messengers went in, and no wizard ever came out. Glauvistus and three other dragons found only a sealed tower. A bored young white dragon, Serafeddin, now watches it with 150 dragonkin, and sometimes sends scouts or adventurers inside. None have returned. |
