Illyria was the Land of a Thousand Heroes, a proud old duchy that held the line against the dragons for centuries. Seven years ago the flame dragon Glauvistus led edjet troops, akinji cavalry, and kobold archers across the border. Help came too late or not at all, and within a month the nobles had fled, the armies were broken, and the Silver Duchess had surrendered in her own palace. Illyria is now Rumela, the newest province of [[The Mharoti Empire]].

The country is afraid and angry. Dragonkin enforcers hunt dissent, tens of thousands of kobold settlers have been brought in to work the land, and speaking Illyrian has been outlawed. A few noble families who make weapons have made their peace with the conquerors and throw parties as if nothing had happened. The old duchess is kept chained to a stone block before the gates of her palace as a warning to anyone else. Those who still pray to Perun or Mavros do it in hidden shrines.

The Illyrian cause isn't dead. Rebels, minotaur corsairs out of [[Triolo]], and the bandits of the [[White Mountain Marches]] all keep the province bleeding.

Glauvistus's titles are Conqueror of Rumela, Morza of the Fire Prophet, and Breaker of Legions. Appeals for help either came too late or, in the case of the Archmage of the Lonely Spire, were never answered (see [[White Mountain Marches]]). The Silver Duchess was captured in the palazzo of the Palasi Ardenu.

## Occupation
More than 400,000 humans, dwarves, and minotaurs live under roughly 30,000 draconic newcomers, but Illyria's standing army was only 7,000 or 8,000 strong, and with it dead or fled, untrained civilians stand no chance, especially with dragons flying over their towns every day. The Muhta, a brigade of dragonkin enforcers, holds the cities in fear, kobold drake riders patrol the countryside and oversee new pastures and farms, and even minor unrest brings mass reprisals. Most of the hundreds of noble families fled to Triolo, [[Trombei]], [[Kyprion]], or the [[Magdar Kingdom]]. The four that remain, Callensoe, Oreste, Valona, and Zenevisi, collaborate, making fine weapons under dragonkin overseers and throwing galas as if nothing were wrong. The commoners no longer dream of earning titles through heroism; they pray to any god who will listen for liberation.

## The Court of the Scourge
The sultan personally anointed Glauvistus as morza. She loves manipulation, destruction, and plots decades long, and traded relics from her hoard to other morza for troops and support, which arrive more slowly than she would like, so she has not yet moved against Raguza or Triolo. She is turning the Palasi Ardenu into a fortress palace, fortifying Droisha and the mountain border with Triolo, setting the remaining nobles against each other for amusement, and using harsh laws to goad would-be rebels into rising early so the Muhta can round them up. The Muhta is led by Zrandres, a young copper dragon who was long a prisoner of the Illyrian duchess and remembers every insult, curse, and bit of trash thrown at him. His informers leave no secret kept for long.

The old duchess, Veristi Terramaine, is chained day and night to a stone block before the gates of the Palasi Ardenu, bound by enchanted shackles [[Baba Yaga]] once made to hold a dragon, altered by the mages of Harkesh. Glauvistus keeps her physically well, fed by kobolds and healed by dragonkin clerics, to stretch out her anguish, and every morning Zrandres visits to remind her of all she has lost.

## Droisha, City of Eagles
The war-scarred capital is rebuilding. Its marble columns and causeways still stand proud over the wine-dark sea, overshadowed now by new watchtowers and the black walls and siege engines of the Palasi Ardenu, and ringed by the barracks, training yards, and forges of the Grand Western Legion. Dragonkin occupy the estates of slain or fled nobles. The great Temple of Victory has been rededicated to Azuran, and the other four great shrines honor Baal and Khespotan. New shipyards line the coast, and a dozen dragon turtles guard the harbor mouth.

The Grand Western Legion numbers more than 20,000, fed by vast herds south and east of the White Mountains and camel caravans from the empire. Half is stationed in Droisha under Glauvistus; the other half, at the new town of Kruja under the fire giant king General Stalek al-Grodoccor, builds massive fortifications along the passes into Triolo.
