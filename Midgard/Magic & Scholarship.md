Midgard has dozens of schools of magic, nearly all of them in human lands. Most choose students by birth, talent, and the ability to pay, or else take them on for a long apprenticeship of ten to twelve years. A handful have reputations that reach across the whole world, and a graduate of one of them is taken seriously wherever they go.

The [[Arcane Collegium]] in [[Zobeck]] is small, rarely more than a few dozen students, and the rest of the world thinks it's a strange place. It teaches clockwork magic, dwarven alchemy and divination, and illumination (star and shadow magic) under [[Orlando|Guildmaster Orlando]], a wizard of odd habits and very good instincts. Whatever outsiders say about its methods, it keeps turning out excellent wizards.
