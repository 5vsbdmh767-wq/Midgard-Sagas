Hrafnheim, the Kingdom of Leaf and Quill, is the North's newest realm. Its capital, Runaskr, the City of Thought, is perched on a new World Tree. Hrafnheim was founded long ago by a pact between [[Wotan]] and [[Ratatoskr]], and Runaskr once stood in the branches of [[Yggdrasil]] itself. Within the last year, the two gods grew the new World Tree and moved the city to the [[Northlands]].

Runaskr is home to [[Ravenfolk|ravenfolk]] Speakers and [[Ratatosk|ratatosk]] Seekers, who gather and study lore from across the planes. Its vine-draped bridges and glowing branchways bustle with scholars and couriers trading knowledge for secrets. Its two leaders struggle to keep the peace as prophecy, ambition, and stirring forces threaten its fragile harmony.

Since Hrafnheim's arrival, more ratatosk have ventured out into the mortal world, and ravenfolk live there in large numbers. Each autumn the realm keeps Hlijodfall, "Chimefall," a solemn festival honoring Wotan and Ratatoskr, when scholars and speakers share the year's omens and insights.
