South of where the great glaciers grind against the Reaching Mountains lie the fertile forests and fields of Trollheim, sheltered from the worst of the cold and wind. It is the largest of the reaver nations, a land of cunning hunters, fearless explorers, and fearsome raiders of every lineage. United, Trollheim might hold back [[Boreas (North Wind)|Boreas]], his glaciers, and the giants of the North. Instead its jarls quarrel, and old feuds threaten new wars. Its banner is Njord's trident between the twin trees of [[Yarila & Porevit|Freyr and Freyja]].

The settled heart of the kingdom is about 77,000 square miles of deep forest and valley south of the mountains, from [[Huldramose]] in the east to [[Vargrike]] in the west. To the southwest lies the frontier, a 25-mile-wide strip down the eastern side of the Huldramose peninsula to the Swive: rocky cliffs, crumbling ruins, and treacherous caves, where trolls, goblin clans, and kobold warrens have overrun several small settlements. Each setback only makes Trollheim more determined to settle it. Most people live on independent farmsteads and in villages that come together at regional althings when danger threatens, and otherwise have little patience for talk of kings.

## History

During the Vanir War, dwarven legions drove the fire giant thanes from their obsidian fortresses here and built Ishamagard, the dwarven empire's northernmost province, on the ruins. The Reaving shattered the Ice-Hammered Halls, and the surviving dwarves had to contend with elves, trolls, giants, and the humans just beginning to arrive. Dwarven ways were adopted by, or forced on, the humans and other peoples, but living together also taught the dwarves restraint, cooperation, and peaceful trade.

Then Boreas's glaciers broke through the Wall and threw down the [[Legendary Places of the North|Vanguard Kingdoms]], and the Swive flooded the green plains between Trollheim and [[Björnrike]]. The survivors of fallen Issedon rallied at Noatun, a far outpost of their city, and named what remained of their land Trollheim. Those calamities made its people hardy: they farm, brawl, raid one another, toast the gods, and consider themselves the best people in the world.

## A Kingdom Divided

Seven years ago, Jarl Asvaldr of the Havardr Clan led a great fleet of his own and many other clans' longships on a raid to the south, and it vanished somewhere in the Nieder Strait. So many jarls and warriors were lost that western Trollheim was left nearly leaderless. Many feuds ended, and trollkin and scattered frost goblin (fraughashar) tribes moved into the gap. Many people looked to old King Uffi Toothless to take charge, or to give way to Signy Fridrad, a younger and stronger ruler. Facing the first real threat to his reign, Uffi raised several young jarls, among them Veska Flamehair, the first trollkin jarl. The move gave the kingdom's growing trollkin population legitimacy and enraged hard-line dwarves and humans who see them as monsters.

A year ago, Asvaldr came home without his fleet, telling a harrowing tale of imprisonment by seafolk. He now leads the traditionalist jarls and calls for a return to the old ways, and he backs a claim that an obscure Ragnarok prophecy begins with a trollkin jarl. Others rally to Uffi and Veska. Cults and ancient powers see opportunity in the turmoil, and too few watch the north, where the glaciers keep grinding into settled land.

## Life in the Kingdom

Trade between the lowland human settlements and the mountain dwarves keeps wood, metal goods, and food flowing, and feeds the shipbuilding and arms-making behind each year's raids. All year round, settlements hold gaming festivals called leikmots, with prizes and status for the winners. Two favorites are knattleikr, a full-contact game with wooden bats and a leather ball, and a wrestling contest called grima. Deaths are rare, but they happen.

Ruins of the old dwarven empire and of later peoples dot the land, including forts, redoubts, and crumbling keeps left by the Vanguard Kingdoms. Below ground, miles of tunnels dug as shelter from the glaciers are now frozen labyrinths haunted by ice demons, elementals, and vaettir. The frontier holds the largest collection of unexplored dwarven ruins in the North, many of them home to trolls, goblins, monsters, or bandit reavers who prey on caravans and shipping. Ice mephits, hill giants, and elementals wander through gaps in the Wall to plague the west, werewolves raid the east, and selkies and sea elves trouble the coast.

## King Uffi Toothless

Uffi is nearly eighty: wrinkled and pale, with tufts of white hair on a bald, spotted scalp and a pronounced hunch. He wears fur-lined robes nearly all year and swaps his engraved iron crown for a warm wool cap. Once a mighty warrior, he is now frail and in constant pain from old wounds and swollen joints, but his green eyes and his mind are as sharp as ever, and his cunning has only grown. Long experience, natural guile, and (some whisper) a blessing from [[Loki]] keep him a step ahead of his ambitious jarls. He plays the kindly grandsire or the ruthless politician, whichever serves him better, and hides his frustration at his failing body behind a bawdy, wicked sense of humor.

He believes Trollheim's future can't be won by force alone, and he gambled on Veska Flamehair because he thinks the trollkin could be a great strength to the kingdom if they can be brought into it. He publicly welcomed Asvaldr home, but he doesn't trust that "miraculous return" and has agents looking into what really happened. Beyond his own borders, the glaciers creep into western Trollheim and the giants raid more every season. He fears most that Trollheim will tear itself apart when he dies.

## Places of Note

| Place | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Noatun, City of the Sea Wall | Trollheim's largest city, founded by the god Njord after he protected it from the Swive. Rare for a human city, it is built more of stone than wood, thanks to local dwarf clans. High walls face the land, and an enchanted sea wall that calms wave and weather encloses its docks and famous shipyards. It holds the Grand Temple of the [[Shield-Maidens of Sif\|Shield-Maidens]], the North's largest temple to [[Sif]]. Rich and welcoming, it hosts longships full of plunder and thirsty raiders, centaur caravans, now and then a cloud giant's flying island or a distant magician, and even trolls with gold to spend (closely watched). Uffi rules from Tannlaus Hall, hung with banners, shields, and trophies taken from raids, giants, and beasts as a pointed reminder to his jarls. Nine rival lords, each keeping a tower on the sea wall, form the core of his court. |
| Amberhall | A large coastal village with a lively trading post, named for the amber in its hills. Its sailors are known for skill and wily evasiveness, and have sailed to distant southern cities, though lately they keep closer to home. |
| Dead Skalds Tor | A bare rock pinnacle in the eastern hills, haunted by the ghosts of three dwarven skalds who charm passersby into listening to their songs until they die of hunger or cold. |
| Great Moss Hall | A revered trollkin feast hall hidden behind a moss-covered boulder on a fjord, where trollkin shamans of [[Yarila & Porevit\|Freyr and Freyja]] hold feasts and rites. On rare nights their workings let spirits mingle with the living. Runes of great power enforce peace in the hall. |
| [[Lodge of the Dancing Bear\|Gorsewood Lodge]] | A fortified lodge on Lake Dreken in the frontier, where senior guides of the Lodge of the Dancing Bear meet twice a year. |
| Isby | A fortress town straddling the River Tyskr at the mouth of a fjord on the eastern border, a trading center for furs and timber thanks to werekin hunters and dwarven lumber guilds. Raids from Vargrike strike it often. |
| Issvardr Tower | An ancient marble tower near Viezla, jutting nearly sideways from an advancing glacier. It is home to frozen vaettir and an ice maiden of Morrinn, the Snow Queen, trapped there since the fall of the Vanguard Kingdoms, who sends her servants to bring her living people to warm her cold heart. |
| Jarrgard | A squat, snow-covered dwarven fort on a low hill by Black Lake, guarding a rich silver mine. Its jarl keeps apart from the rest of the kingdom and told Uffi to stay out of his affairs; the king scoffed but hasn't challenged him. |
| Reaching Mountains | Snowcapped granite peaks looming over Trollheim like an army of weathered stone giants. High among them is the Jormulglatz Glacier, where skalds say the greatest heroes may speak with the Valkyries. |
| Skali | Walled seat of the Ranulfr Clan and a starting point for frontier expeditions. Its jarl trades protection to the ravenfolk of Wotan's Tree for prophetic blessings. |
| Stone Meadow | A rocky meadow of dolmens guarded by a vaettir called She Who Sleeps Lightly, in life a mighty troll shaman. |

| Varnerhall | Jarl Vngvarr Ironsides's seat, a prosperous, well-defended village on a cliff at the end of a forested valley. It keeps good relations with the nomads who pass through and trades with them for furs, ivory, and good cold-weather gear. |
| Viezla | A fortified fishing village of the Havardr Clan, healing from its jarl's absence now that he has returned. |
