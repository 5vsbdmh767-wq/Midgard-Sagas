Tanserhall, the Forge Hold, is the oldest and most sacred of all [[Dwarves|dwarven]] cities, beloved of [[Volund]] and [[Thor]]. It was the heart of the first dwarven empire, and it alone survived the Reaving intact. Far below its hallowed hearths and shrines lies the rune-carved Cradle Cave, where the two gods forged the first dwarves. There Volund showed them gleaming metals and glittering jewels, and Thor showed them courage and immortal glory, and said, "These things are yours, from now until the end of time." Its banner is the hammer of Thor and the anvil of Volund.

The clans of Tanserhall are pious and reclusive. The few who travel describe a vast underground fortress, more cathedral than city. On [[Wotan]]'s advice after the Reaving, they honor Volund and Thor in strict balance with elaborate rituals so that neither god is slighted, and they listen closely to the All-Father's counsel. They keep their birthplace pure by barring other peoples from most of its halls; even dwarves from elsewhere must undergo ritual purification first, and for anyone else, custom, bureaucracy, and dwarven stubbornness make entry nearly impossible. Uninvited guests face a lethal maze of trapped corridors, and few survive it. Rumor says only one intruder in a hundred is spared, and the rest are turned to stone statues that serve as warning and ward.

Tanserhall once stood among lofty peaks, but the land came crashing down under the blows of Thor's wrathful hammer, and the Most Hallowed Hall now sits among mere hills. On the surface, the dwarves farm terraced slopes with hired karls of other lineages, whom they prefer in the fields rather than underground in the mines.

## War in the Deep

Two years ago, the monstrous spawn of Chernobog rose from the depths of the Cradle Cave: upright, ink-black hulks crowned with elk horns, whose terrible bites can turn the wounded into more of their kind. For a time, Tanserhall's warriors held them back. Now the dwarves fight them in the lower streets of the city itself. Knowing the war is turning against them, they have sent out the call for Vigferth, a crusade, summoning dwarves from across the North to aid their kin.
