Thrumagard, the Thunder Mountain Hold, lies north of [[Stannasgard]] on the Trollbane Coast. It is the newest of the dwarven holds, though its halls are old: founded some 2,750 years ago, it was lost to an infestation of trolls and other monsters more than a thousand years ago. A hundred and fifty years ago, Alaric the Mithral-Dwarf and his adventuring company, the Band of the Thunder Hammer, retook it, settled down to rule, and reopened its mithral mines. His companions persuaded him to open the mountain to other peoples, and humans, winterfolk [[Halflings|halflings]], and huginn [[Ravenfolk|ravenfolk]] took up the offer as equals. Its banner is a red gauntlet holding a double hammer-staff on a white field.

The hold has prospered since, delving deep and building the Shrine of Golden Hammers to [[Thor]]. Its smiths, miners, and scholars work hard under the eye of Thor's clerics. The more conservative holds, such as [[Tanserhall]], find its open ways mildly indecent, but none dare challenge its strength, and its founders are powerful enough that even giants quail at the name. Nearby stands the Great Linnorm House, a college of rune magic roofed with the bones of an elder dragon (see [[Magic & Scholarship]]).

## The Order of the Thunderer

Thrumagard is the headquarters of the Order of the Thunderer, a militant dwarven order of Thor's priests, paladins, and warriors sworn to destroy the giants, trolls, and ogres that plague the region and to defend the hold against [[Jotunheim]]. Every new member receives a silvered hammer at initiation, and the order claims it was founded in the presence of the god. It is one of the few groups that truly understands the [[Cult of Ragnarok]], thanks to its spies. Two years ago it rooted out cult spies and attacked their safehouses, and the cult struck back by assassinating key figures, among them the archmage Delric and the order's best scout, Timesh Youngport. More recently, Thrumagard sent much of the order to defend Stannasgard after a dragon destroyed that city's army. With the order's attention elsewhere, giants and other monsters are flourishing in the nearby hills and mountains.
