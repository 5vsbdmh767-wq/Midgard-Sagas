Huldramose lies in a cold, low marshland between two wide rivers that often flood. It is ruled not by a king but by two [[Elfmarked|elfmarked]] queens, the old ways of the Vanir are strong here, and fey are common. Punts and rowboats take the place of roads and horses. Its banner is the Tree of Thorn.

The land is named for the Huldra, fierce [[Trollkin|trollkin]] women who think nothing of beating arrogant male warriors senseless with their bare hands. [[Wotan]] himself gathered the first Valkyries from among the Huldra, and they still train mortal women and girls in the fighting arts. Anyone who doubts a band of shield-maidens stops doubting after meeting the Huldra in battle. Trollkin make up most of the population.

Few dwarves come here, since the infamies of their old wars are well remembered. The capital stands on thick stilts, like giant wooden spiders crashing into one another. Witches and fey sorceresses are far more common than wizards or priests, and snake-handling is a common rite, along with visionary potions brewed from swamp-serpent venom and narcotic herbs. The oracles of the Huldra are sought out for powerful divinations.

## The Two Queens

Thorgerd and Dark Irpa claim descent from the vanished nobility of Thorn. They rule in turns, one at home while the other travels in search of adventure. Neither has a husband, though each takes heroes as lovers now and then, and both have borne strong daughters to carry on the line. Lately the two queens have been at odds.

## Neighbors

Huldramose trades with [[Wolfheim]], and the Wolfheim town of Rafrborg recently gave land inside its walls to a trollkin embassy from Huldramose. The frontier of [[Trollheim]] runs down the eastern side of the Huldramose peninsula.
