The windswept Isle of Berserkers takes the full force of the arctic winds off the Uttermost Sea. Everything on it serves one cause: glorious war. It is home to the Jomsvikings, berserker raiders and mercenaries famous as the fiercest of the fierce. Between contracts and through the winter, they train constantly in an austere, punishing regime meant to harden their bodies and perfect their rages. They fight for any lord who can afford their steep fees, roam far when the pay is right, and go raiding for battle or tribute. Their banner is Thor's hammer on a red field.

Despised along the [[Northlands|Reaver Coast]], the Jomsvikings still keep a strict code. A recruit must prove their valor and endure great tests of strength, including a ritual duel with a sworn Jomsviking. Members must defend one another in battle, may not quarrel or speak ill of their fellows (anything that can't be settled goes to holmganga), and must never flee a fight or show fear of hardship.

## A Split on the Isle

For generations the Jomsvikings took only humans. Then trollkin warriors came to claim a place in their ranks, and one of them, Solveig Rockhurler, challenged the Hersir ("battle lord"), Beoric Thorson, and won. As Hersir, she opened the isle to giantkin and other non-humans. That was too much for some. Beoric gathered those angry at the loss of the old ways, split from the Jomsvikings, and settled the town of Harmarstad on the far side of the island. Skirmishes between the two camps are growing more frequent, and the whole island may soon go to war.
