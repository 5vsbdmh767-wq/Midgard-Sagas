In Jotunheim, the land of the giants, you can taste magic in the air and feel it tingle in your bones. It is a land of towering mountains, treacherous glaciers, scalding geysers, and magical hot springs, where the northern lights can be seen clearly from every summit. Giants rule here, and even gods walk carefully. The giants have held it since the beginning of the world and call it the Bones of Aurgelmir, after their slain forefather. Every kind of giant lives here, much as their human neighbors do: crafting, hunting, farming, raiding the dwarven and human settlements below, and bowing only to whoever can make them.

The giants say this world is a poor replacement for the one Aurgelmir made. That first world was destroyed by the gods at the end of the First War, when the Aesir and Vanir joined forces to crush the giants' dreams of glory, and the giants took the far reaches of the North while the followers of the hated gods spread across the world. The jotun lords mourn how small and weak other giants have become, and blame the rise of the Aesir and the plots of [[Wotan]]. Some work tirelessly to restore their people's glory; others brood and see only to their own power and comfort. Giants roam the [[Northlands]] in growing numbers, and ruined farmsteads bear silent witness. Rumor says the [[Cult of Ragnarok]] has found refuge somewhere in Jotunheim's crags.

Apart from giant-sized geography and architecture, an understandable obsession with strength and size, and a hatred of the gods of Asgard, everyday life in the giants' realms is much like anywhere else in the North.

## The Giants

The common kinds of giant in the North, from smallest to tallest:

| Kind | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Thursir | The smallest, only 10 feet tall, crafters with a gift for metalworking and famously gluttonous appetites. |
| Hill giants | Industrious, dim, and 16 feet tall; blunt and direct, they enjoy the simple things in life. |
| Stone giants | Gray-skinned and about 18 feet tall, held in low regard by other giants for an ancient crime no one will speak of. The jotun lords exiled most of them from the North long ago, and most fled south. |
| Fire giants | Dark-skinned and flame-haired, about 19 feet tall. They love smelting and smithing but are usually too temperamental for fine artistry. |
| Frost giants | Ice-skinned and about 21 feet tall, living on the glaciers and hunting mammoths and other great beasts. |
| Cloud giants | Elegant, stately, 24-foot giants with sky-colored skin and blond hair, who live in floating towers and on high peaks. |
| Storm giants | Crackling with lightning, 26 feet tall, reclusive, but easily roused to fury. |
| Jotun | The oldest and mightiest, 60 feet tall and nearly immortal. Some have powers rivaling demigods. |

Ogres, trolls, and other "minor" giants also live in great numbers across the North, from sea to swamp to ice. Some clans live by hunting and raiding; others join larger giant communities or serve as heavy troops for warlords, mystics, and cults.
