South of the towering mountains of [[Sengajordensblod]], two peninsulas reach into the Nieder Strait on either side of the Munnrfjord. The Agnalan Headlands are no true kingdom but a sparse scatter of [[Giantkin|giantkin]] villages with no banner of their own. The giants' name for the region translates as "Land of the Bait," and it fits: reavers from elsewhere in the North regularly descend on Agnalan villages, only to find young true giants lying in wait. That dubious protection comes and goes, though, and more often the giantkin must defend themselves.

The headlands are too far south of the glacial heights for most giants and too close to [[Jotunheim]] for human or dwarven settlers. The villagers cling to what farmland the rocky ground allows, among trolls, ogres, and other monsters. The jotun fortress of Gifrvagga looms within striking distance.

## Villages and Sites

| Place | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Eikinstad | Perhaps the most fertile land in Agnalan, with hardy root crops and shaggy cattle, ruled by Jarl Thyra Anjadottir, famed as both druid and farmer. Rumor says something is tunneling under the village, the crops grow strangely sweet, and the night holds whispers. About 25 years ago, the vaettir Liv Iron-Eyes came out of the sea to save the village, and the grateful villagers have sheltered her and helped her recover her lost warriors ever since (see [[Legendary Places of the North#The Vaettir Mounds\|Vaettir Mounds]]). |
| Nyrdalr | Once a struggling farm village, now bristling with masts and sharpened axes. Jarl Asgaut Thornheel, a brutal, dwarf-hating giantkin, made it a haven for Northern pirates and leads raids in his own longboat. Loot flows freely, and the raids paint a large target on the village. |
| Orstagr | A cold, quiet fishing harbor veiled in near-constant fog. Jarl Ismar Cut-Tongue, a grizzled former reaver, struggles to protect his people since villagers and livestock began turning up drained of blood and scarred with sigils. |
| Svartnaer | A village of metalworkers on cliffs blackened by volcanic rock. Its dwarven jarl, Vorni Tiny-Giant, runs the bellows and the village with charm and grit. A recent cave-in uncovered ancient ruins no one has explored yet. |
| Vaettrey | An island village on a rocky spit above whale-rich waters, living by fishing and whale-rendering. Jarl Hrolf Greasebeard, a former harpooner, keeps the peace with brutal efficiency. Whaling has grown more dangerous since ghostly dwarven revenants began rising from the depths of the Nieder Strait. |
| Vetrbyr | Built from jorumungkyn bones and broken ships on a wave-battered coast. Its people harvest ambergris and herd goats in the hills. Jarl Kelta Three-Tooth, an acknowledged sea-witch, has kept the village safe with her hexes, but age has weakened her power. |
| Ymir's Pyre | A cyclopean pillar of obsidian and gneiss more than 100 feet tall on a lonely island, crowned by an enchanted fire that has burned since history began. Sailors use it as a beacon but keep their distance, because no one who has landed there has ever come back. |
