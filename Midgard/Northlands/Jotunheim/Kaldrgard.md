Where the great glaciers of [[The Bleak Expanse]] collide with the mountains of [[Jotunheim]] lie the scattered frost giant jarldoms of Kaldrgard, a frigid, barren realm of ice and snow around the Fjorhrim Glacier and its mountains. Few permanent settlements exist here, and the greatest are three towering, primordial ice castles glittering high on the mountain slopes, their crystal towers a symbol of their might. Its banner is a blue stone wall before a white glacier.

Frost giants believe Aurgelmir gave the coldest reaches of the North to their ancestors because they were the strongest giants and the only ones worthy of so harsh a land. That pride runs through their culture, and displays of strength decide a frost giant's standing. Fiercely competitive and even brutal, they are just as fiercely protective of their families and clans. Most live as nomadic families; a good part of the population lives in the three ice castles.

The giants hunt herds of mammoth, reindeer, and caribou in the valleys at the glaciers' edge, and train their young warriors against yeti tribes, saber-toothed tigers, and other arctic threats. They grow exotic cold-weather herbs and lichens and brew medicines from them. Kaldrgard is the main source of iced steel, a rare metal prized by other giant realms and beyond.

## A Divided Realm

A schism has opened in Kaldrgard. More and more young frost giants have turned away from the old veneration of [[Bergelmir]] and [[Angrboda]], drawn to the banner of [[Boreas (North Wind)|Boreas]] in the Bleak Expanse or to the war plans of the [[Blood Reckoning]]. The older jarls seem helpless to stop the exodus, torn between fear for the clans' future and their own deep individualism. A growing number of the young wear shards of a strange pale crystal said to "protect" them.

## The Frost Queen

Queen Atla Gunnlodottir is an imposing figure with smooth blue skin and snow-bright hair in thick braids set with carved bone, wearing a mantle of white mammoth fur and a crown of jagged white crystals. She won her throne by defeating her predecessor in single combat. The daughter of the last giant to drink the Mead of Poetry, she is uncommonly eloquent and fiercely wise, and wields metaphor the way others wield axes. She sees the old traditions fading and fears the realm is slipping from her grasp, and she means to keep Kaldrgard whole against everyone trying to break it.
