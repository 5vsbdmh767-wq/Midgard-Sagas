The proud cloud giants of the North live in high mountain holds or floating cloud steadings, but most of them live in Bjarmia, their flying island. It looks like a mote of land wrapped in white cloud, a rough oval three miles across, covered in longhouses and great halls of white marble with soaring golden roofs, linked by meandering cobbled paths between cold-weather gardens. Enchanted streams that never stop flowing feed terraced farms and animal pens before spilling off the island's edge. Eldritch power fills the place: magical orbs glow with flickering golden fire, and crystal pylons hold it at a steady subarctic climate whatever the season or height. Its banner is a golden tower on a gray cloud.

The Bjarmians say the goddess [[Gullveig]] taught them cloud weaving and how to raise their floating steadings, though she has not been worshiped in ages. Cloud giants still build small floating palaces, but the secret of raising whole islands was lost long ago.

Most of the time Bjarmia drifts above the clouds, to and from [[Jotunheim]], stopping regularly at the major giant settlements there and once a year near [[Stannasgard]] and [[Skaldholm]]. Now and then it touches down on the waves near a coastal port to trade. The giants deal in luxuries, rare magic, and precious things, but they are sharp traders, and their deals have unexpected consequences. They love fine cloth and wine, and they trade for them if they can and raid for them if they can't. Outsiders meet suspicion, but not outright hostility.

## The Sky Queen

Queen Azala Stormsinger is a strong-featured cloud giant with blue-white skin and golden hair in a single braid. She wears white robes and a wide belt set with golden disks, from which hangs a ceremonial scepter, and a diadem of silver and diamonds. She took the throne young, and her determination has won over most of her detractors. Like most cloud giants she thinks herself superior to everyone and doesn't hide it, but she truly cares for her people and worries about their place in the world. She is wary of the [[Cult of Ragnarok]]'s influence on the other giant clans and won't march to the orders of Jotunheim's warlords.

## Troubles

| Matter | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Power failures | Dunvak, king of the thursir, runs the eldritch machine beneath the High Halls that powers the island, and is blamed for a recent run of outages. He blames spire walkers in the lightning tunnels beneath the device. |
| Lagertha Giantblooded | A storm giantkin who recently arrived leading a delegation from the [[Thrumagard\|Order of the Thunderer]]. |
| Skyholm Light | A crumbling lighthouse at the island's edge whose purpose no one remembers. Lately a strange light flashes straight up from it every new moon. |
| Sverre Stone-Brand | A powerful cloud giant warlord who courts the queen, and who makes no secret of wanting to rule Bjarmia himself. He is an elitist and a snob who answers any slight to his people's glory with violence. |
| The White Wyrm | Dread Isandradr, a cunning white dragon, threatens to make Bjarmia her new home by killing everyone on it unless she is paid enough treasure to change her mind. If the queen can't pay the wergild, someone must find the dragon's lair and deal with her. |
