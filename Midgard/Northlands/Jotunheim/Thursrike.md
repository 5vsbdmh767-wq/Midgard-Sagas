Thursrike stretches along the western coast of the [[Northlands]], walled by glaciers on three sides: a land of hill giants, ogres, trolls, and others on the fringes of giant society. It wears the scars of the past like badges of honor. Some of the oldest structures in the North stand here, primordial fortress-towns whose walls withstood the great battles between the jotun and the gods. Strange Hyperborean ruins dot the tundra, and a wasteland of shattered hills where the laws of nature give way to twisting madness fills the middle of the realm. The advancing glaciers uncover ancient ruins and monsters as they grind across the land. Its banner is a shield with a red bend sinister.

The realm covers almost 475,000 square miles, most of it unsettled. In the mountainous south, the Haamu Hills press against [[Jotunheim]] before falling away into the dense Blaleir Forest; the rest is low arctic grassland and tundra dotted with fens and bogs. Most people live in or around four titanic fortress-settlements, Drekholl, Jarnskul, Kjothall, and Kupparsheim, whose surrounding pastures feed vast herds of sheep, shaggy northern cattle, mammoths, and aurochs, and whose meat feeds the giant kingdoms of Jotunheim. Mounted giantkin with dragonbone warhorns patrol the pastures. Beyond them, the land is wild and treacherous, home to remorhazes, white dragons, thuellai, liosalfar, and warlike bands of yeti.

The jarls of Thursrike are a cross-section of giantkind, which makes Thursrike more tolerant of other peoples than the rest of Jotunheim. Even non-giants can make a future here if they are strong enough to hold their own, a reputation that draws outlaws and exiles running from the consequences of their deeds. Most find the land accepting, but never kind. Guesting customs are fiercely kept, and visitors must drink the strong local mead as thanks to their host. Three years ago, the thursir and other giants of Thursrike and the western mountains marched into the human and dwarven lands of the North, and great numbers of trollkin rose to meet them.

## King Guthmundur

The jotun Guthmundur Raggnarson seized the fortress of Kupparsheim three years ago from the human and dwarven adventurers who had cleared it of monsters, killing the dwarf who had conquered it, and made it his capital. He is king in name, but his real authority ends at the four pasture fortresses. He dreams of bringing the whole region under his rule, and is training an elite force of hill giants as the core of a new army.

Even a good joke is risky, since his laughter shakes carts and riders off his belly. He hints that he waits there for some greater purpose, but won't say what. |
