At the heart of [[Jotunheim]], the spilled blood of the First Giant is said to have fed the mountains until they nearly pierced the sky. Among these peaks are the halls of the jotun, the near-immortal children of Aurgelmir, carved into mountains and glaciers. Some of them remember the war with the gods and the murder of their father, a crime that still defines their culture. Many know ancient secrets and scraps of arcane lore from before the world was remade, and the mightiest straddle the line between mortal and demigod. From their icy thrones, the jotun lords hold nominal rule over all Jotunheim, though in practice the lesser giants' kingdoms govern themselves. Its symbol is the Skull of Aurgelmir.

The jotun see themselves as the true heirs of creation, and most hate the gods eternally; only [[Bergelmir]] and [[Angrboda]] earn their reverence. They mourn the decline of giantkind and blame the rise of the Aesir. Some seek to restore their people; others hoard power. Long divided as they are, their hatred smolders, ready to forge Sengajordensblod into a force that could make the gods tremble.

All kinds of giants live here. The jotun are the most respected and feared; even the physically weaker thursir are valued for their craft; stone giants hold low status for an age-old crime few will discuss. The giants herd titanic beasts, including huge muskox, elk, axebeaks, and mastodons, and cave bears, dire wolves, and saber-toothed tigers prey on the herds and on travelers alike.

## The Blodting

When the omens call for it, the jotun lords summon the greatest giants to the Blodting, a council held in the Temple of the Skull at the center of Sengajordensblod, whose decisions can shape the North for generations. Each midwinter, jotun also gather at the temple to recount the murder of the First Giant, and each lord renews a vow to someday repay the hated gods. Now, following his seers' omens, Bolthorn has called a Blodting to decide whether Jotunheim should at last embrace the cause of the [[Blood Reckoning]] and launch its war of vengeance against [[Wotan]] and the gods.

## King Bolthorn

The ruler of the city of Sengajordensblod is Bolthorn the Eternal, the oldest surviving jotun and the grandfather of [[Wotan]]. If anyone can be said to rule Jotunheim, it is he. He is a towering figure of impossible age, his golden hair gone gray, his skin weathered like jagged stone, his beard hanging to his waist, and his eyes glowing with the weight of ages. He wears no crown, only a mantle of relics taken from kin slain by the Aesir. He remembers the shaping of the world and the murder of Aurgelmir, and has forgotten more magic than the greatest wizards ever knew. He rarely speaks, but his words carry great weight. To the young he is a relic; to the old, a reminder that the war is not over. Though bound by blood to the gods, he bears them no love, and he is said to be estranged from his son, Skymir.
