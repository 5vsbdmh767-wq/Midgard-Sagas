The spine of [[Jotunheim]] is the Blodejord, the "Crib of Earth's Blood" in the jotun tongue: a range of active volcanoes around the charred, desolate remains of once-fertile valleys. The peaks hurl fire and burning stone into the sky, and their ash, drawn from the primordial heart of the world, carries a dire enchantment: anyone who dies covered in it rises as twisted undead. Its banner is a crimson erupting volcano and a black sword.

Clans of fire giants live here, toiling in their mountain fortresses at war and metalwork. They are short-tempered and warlike, but also passionate and creative, and their society is more regimented than other giant cultures: though some choice is expected, most fire giants are strongly pushed toward callings that suit their abilities. Fire giant smiths call themselves the greatest crafters in the world, a boast the dwarves have long disputed, and relations between the two peoples swing between bonding over the craft and feuding over it.

Most of the population lives in obsidian fortresses built on and inside the volcanoes. Each is commanded by a svidari, "one who burns." King Isen Brekssen chose every current svidari, and all owe allegiance to Drottenbrand, the seat of the fire giant king.

The fire giants are usually the first to answer calls to war, and they train constantly. Raw ore from all over Jotunheim comes to their lava-powered smelters to be refined into iron, steel, mithral, and adamantine. They drink embermead, a spiced drink with flecks of gold that make it glitter like fire. They largely ignore the land outside their volcanoes and leave it to lindwyrms, ashwalkers, and elementals.

## The Flamelord

King Isen Brekssen is a powerfully built fire giant with the red-black skin and flaming hair of his people, armored in intricate adamantine and red dragon scale. He is young for his rank and won it by defeating the old king in single combat. Called "the Proud," he believes it is his sacred task to lead his people to greatness, and he struggles to temper pride and inexperience with wisdom. He is convinced the other giant kings are conspiring against him.

## Places of Note

| Place | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Drottenbrand, Seat of the Flamelord | One of the oldest structures in the [[Northlands]], an obsidian fortress from the First Age that rings its volcano's caldera like a crown. In its Golden Sky Hall of perfectly fitted cyclopean stone, the king issues decrees and appoints his jarls. At its heart burns the First Flame, a hearth fire said to have burned since the blood and bones of Aurgelmir made the world. Bathing in it tempers those who dare: sometimes strengthening the body, sometimes healing, sometimes turning a non-giant into a fire giant. |
| Galgvidr Forest | A great dark forest of towering oaks, twisted elms, and gnarled pines southeast of Drottenbrand, also called Idavoll, Jarnvidr, or the Ironwood. It has long been the refuge of the giant goddess [[Angrboda]], the "mother of monsters," and her children roam it, fiercely guarding their mother's domain. |
| Jattebrand | A fortress built almost entirely inside a volcano near the middle of the range. Near its heavily guarded core is a rift gate to the plane of fire, which its giants believe it is their sacred duty to guard. Svidari Droka Ragiirsson takes that duty seriously; his father, Ragiir Blisterbrand, commands Helspyre, the fortress on the gate's far side. |
