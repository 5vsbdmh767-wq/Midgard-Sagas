Ragnarok has begun, or so it would seem. Many details foretold in the ancient prophecies appear to be coming true, and the voices calling for the North to prepare for the Fimbulwinter and the deluge after it have many ears. The seers are less sure: the timing is wrong, the stars aren't right, and the fulfillments of prophecy are too neat. Behind much of the panic is the Cult of Ragnarok, whose members spread prophecies, report omens, and stage signs to convince [[Northlanders]] the end is at hand. Malevolent humans, dwarves, giants, and others join it, some hoping to bring about the end of the world and some to profit from the chaos. Lately the cult has gone beyond rumors to acts meant to hasten the end: dark rituals, attacks on [[Yggdrasil|World Trees]], tampering with the weather, and stirring up trouble between kingdoms.

The cult is not one body but dozens of sects and cells, each with its own leaders, rites, and creed. They may not agree on what robes to wear, what bloody sacrifices to make, or which villages to burn, but they all strive to end the world. The [[Blood Reckoning]] is said to be among their allies. The Order of the Thunderer of [[Thrumagard]], the [[Glorious Order of Giant Killers]], the [[Shield-Maidens of Sif]], the [[Lodge of the Dancing Bear]], and the [[Fellowship of Wyrm Slayers]] all oppose them, and the Order of the Thunderer has lost key members to cult assassins.

## The Sects

Three sects are behind the cult's most dangerous plots. They see themselves as independent but cooperate now and then; if they ever truly worked together, the end of days would be all but certain.

| Sect | Description |
| --- | --- |
| The Doomforgers' Creed | The largest and most notorious sect, said to be based high in the snowy peaks of [[Jotunheim]], and blamed for manufacturing many of the signs and omens of the end times. |
| The Apostles of Inexorable Ruin | The second most influential sect, led by Thyrin the Black and based at the ancient fortress of Birgkrona in [[Thursrike]], alongside the giants of the Blood Reckoning. They work openly to bring about Ragnarok. |
| The Coiled Ring | Named for the Great Serpent that encircles the world. Its members commit ritual murder, arson, and necromancy, believing that violence against the innocent and the righteous will bring the Fimbulwinter. It has been wreaking havoc from Stannasgard to [[Skaldholm]]. |
| The Children of the Blood Moon | A smaller sect of werewolves who hold howling ceremonies and mass sacrifices to [[Fenris]] in [[Vargrike]]. |
| The Nightbringers | A smaller sect of worshipers of Chernobog who believe they can summon the Fimbulwinter by snuffing out the sun. |

## Places

| Place | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Birgkrona | An ancient cyclopean fortress in the far northwest of [[Thursrike]], headquarters of the Apostles of Inexorable Ruin. Giants, ogres, trollkin, and human cultists garrison it under Thyrin the Black. Its circular stone walls are ringed by a palisade of huge tree trunks and warded by powerful magic laid centuries ago by stone giant sorcerers. A temple to the dark gods of Ragnarok is said to lie beneath it, its floor paved with the skulls of the cult's victims. |
| Forstol Cirkel | A circle of rune-carved megaliths deep in the forests north of [[Stannasgard]], the chief sanctuary of the Coiled Ring. Initiates who have committed the required act of violence are bitten on the neck by a giant venomous snake, the earthly image of the Great Serpent; those who survive are worthy to join. The runes on the stones drive off intruders: anyone not of the cult who enters the circle must succeed on a DC 17 CHA save or become frightened and flee. |

## Notable Members

| Name | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Thyrin the Black | Leader of the Apostles of Inexorable Ruin, an imposing human with a nest of thick black hair and an enormous bushy beard, dressed in black chainmail and a black fur cloak. The left side of his face is badly burned from an ill-judged attempt to ride a remorhaz to impress his elder brothers. He is a fanatical disciple of the dark gods, and the Blood Reckoning's leader Skymir Godkiller is his greatest ally. |
| Diar Mats Sandquist | A high-ranking priest of Chernobog who took unofficial leadership of the Coiled Ring after murdering its founder, Jorgen Coppenkoller.
