Renowned throughout the [[Northlands]], the Shield-Maidens of Sif, also called the sifjar, are a mostly female body of clerics, paladins, and warriors devoted to [[Sif]] the Sword Maiden, Mistress of the Valkyries and patron of warriors and archers. They fight the forces of evil and darkness wherever they find them, hoping for a valorous death and a ride to Valhalla with the Valkyries, where they will drink, feast, and fight until Ragnarok, when they will stand beside their goddess in Asgard's armies.

The Valkyries appear at the three Holy Musters of Remembrance, Fortitude, and Repentance, held in spring, autumn, and winter at Sif's new Grand Temple in Noatun, so many shield-maidens make the pilgrimage there to give offerings and receive the Valkyries' blessing.

## The Orders

Several orders belong to the Shield-Maidens, each tied to its own temple or stronghold.

## Joining

Clerics, fighters, paladins, and rangers are the most common members. Warriors of any gender or lineage may join, though women fill most of the ranks. You must show your fighting skill and swear an oath to uphold the order's values. Training never stops: the bow is Sif's favored weapon, so every shield-maiden learns archery, along with axe, spear, and, of course, shield. They test one another in single combat and in contests of archery, axe-throwing, and the hammer toss.

## Friends and Foes

The Shield-Maidens march wherever evil shows its face, and the undead are their most hated foes. They oppose the [[Cult of Ragnarok]], the [[Blood Reckoning]], the [[Storm Reavers]], and other villains. They are friendly with the [[Skaldic Brotherhood]], who spread tales of their deeds, and they admire the bravery of the [[Fellowship of Wyrm Slayers]], though many think the Wyrm Slayers would do better to stamp out evil than to chase glory.

## Strongholds

| Place | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Chapterhouse of Grajava the Shield-Maiden | Built into a mountainside just outside the city of [[Stannasgard]], headquarters of the Shield-Maidens of Grajava under the white-haired veteran high priest Britta Cragguard. It holds a splendid golden temple to Grajava, barracks, training grounds, and feast halls, and Grajava's priests are famous for their beer, Dragon Shield Ale. The order recently helped its allies in [[Wolfheim]] against the vampires and ghouls plaguing that land, and now works closely with the Order of the Thunderer of [[Thrumagard]] against the Cult of Ragnarok. |
| Queen's Hall | The longhouse rising over the wetlands of the town of [[Huldramose]], seat of the two queens and headquarters of the Huldra. The Huldra hold frequent contests of combat and boasting there, but are best known for their snake-handling rites: contestants drink a foul-smelling visionary brew made by trollkin witches, then handle venomous snakes to prove their bravery, composure, and faith in Sif. |
| Temple of the Shield-Maiden | The newly built grand temple of Sif in Noatun, in [[Trollheim]], its roof tiled in burnished bronze and its white banners snapping in the sea breeze. Built of carved granite and fine ash, it is both fortress and training hall, its vaulted halls ringing with chants and the clash of practice blades. Its matron is the legendary Hildr Ironbraid, who made her name fighting the giants of [[Jotunheim]]. For three Holy Musters in a row, the Valkyries have failed to appear, and some of the faithful whisper that Hildr has lost Sif's favor. |
