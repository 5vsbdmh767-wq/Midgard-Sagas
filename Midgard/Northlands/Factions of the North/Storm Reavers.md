[[Dwarves]], humans, and trollkin have long gone reaving in the [[Northlands]], but no raiders match the monstrous violence of the Storm Reavers, sea raiders and cultists of [[Boreas (North Wind)|Boreas]], the North Wind. From hidden coves and small islands along the western coast of the Swive, they attack merchant ships and other vessels crossing the Nieder Strait and pillage farms and fishing villages on both its northern and southern shores. Unlike other pirates and raiders, they can't be bought off with bribes or tribute. They are despicable outlaws with no code of honor and no mercy. Once everything of value is taken, survivors are murdered on the spot, carried off to be sacrificed to Boreas, or kept as thralls. Only corpses, bloody earth, and smoldering ruins are left behind.

## Who They Are

Most of the Storm Reavers and their leaders are [[Trollkin|trollkin]], but the promise of violence draws evil-hearted human mercenaries, cultists of Boreas, tundra gnolls, and hobgoblins as well. Because the dwarven reaver kingdoms are a deadly threat to them, dwarves are forbidden to join, and the Storm Reavers take great pleasure in murdering any dwarf foolish enough to ask.

They live in clans, though most aren't related by blood, scattered along the [[Trollheim]] frontier on the western shores of the Swive and hidden in secluded coves and sea caves. Each clan has a single longhouse, a shrine to Boreas, and one or two longships, and is usually led by a trollkin raider. The shrines are simple, a cairn on a windswept hilltop or a small cave with a wooden post or two, and sacrificial victims are stripped and chained to the posts to die of exposure. Clan leaders send messages by trained seagulls to coordinate raids. A big attack on a well-guarded merchant fleet or a large port such as Amberhall may bring a dozen or more longships together under a single fearsome trollkin berserker.

The overall leader can be challenged by any clan leader or ship's captain at the Great Mustering, a gathering of longships held every midwinter on a different remote island in the icy northern seas. The challenge is a fight to the death.

Kings and jarls across the North hate the Storm Reavers. The rulers of [[Stannasgard]], [[Trollheim]], [[Wolfheim]], and others are bent on stamping them out and pay their own reavers a hefty bounty for every Storm Reaver head. The [[Steadfast Company of Mile-Steppers]], who guard merchant ships against pirates, are their bitter foes, and the [[Shield-Maidens of Sif]] oppose them too.

## Places

| Wavesquall Caverns | The Storm Reavers' leadership is said to keep its headquarters in hidden sea caves on an island somewhere in the Swive, where their plunder is piled high. |
| Winterchill Hall | A longhouse overlooking a bay some fifty miles south of Wotan's Tree, home to one of the most feared clans. Its walls are covered in runes of winter magic, and it is rimed with frost even at the height of summer. Every member of the Winterchill clan is a cultist of Boreas, and many have survived a series of deadly trials to become Boreas's Chosen, transformed by their god's favor so that icy blood runs in their veins and their weapons are always coated in frost. They raid up and down the Swive, believing every soul they claim brings the endless winter a step closer. |

## Notable Members

| Drinks Dwarf-Blood | The overall leader, a wily trollkin berserker over 7 feet tall with moss-green, stony skin and a bulbous nose. His right ear is torn, a souvenir of his last challenger. He has won six challenges so convincingly that no one has challenged him in three years, but resentment is growing over how much loot ends up in his vault, and a challenge is likely soon. |

| Keelhaul Vott | Captain of the longship *Demon's Blood*, a ruthless tundra gnoll pirate who likes to kill his enemies as gruesomely as he can. His cutthroat crew adores him, and he has taken enough prizes this year to think about challenging Drinks Dwarf-Blood at midwinter. |
