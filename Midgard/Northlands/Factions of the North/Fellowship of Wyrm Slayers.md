The Fellowship of Wyrm Slayers is an association of thrill-seeking monster hunters and adventurers who travel to the remote corners of the [[Northlands]] to slay the biggest beasts they can find. Dragons are the ultimate prize, but members happily hunt lindwurms, nightgarms, remorhazes, saber-toothed tigers, and any other great monster. Many say that killing such creatures earns a seat in Valhalla and is perfect training for fighting beside [[Wotan]] when Ragnarok comes, and that in the meantime they are keeping the North safe. Jarls and other rulers often call on them when monsters plague their lands. Cynics say they do it for the bragging rights or for fun. Both are probably true.

Each local hall is led by a triad of hunters and acts mostly on its own. Once a year, at least one leader from every hall gathers at one of the halls to settle matters for the whole membership. At the most recent gathering, they voted to expel the disgraced warrior Solveg Runeflare for slaying the white dragon Verglastronth with poison and lying about it.

## Joining

Barbarians, fighters, and rangers make up most of the Fellowship, along with wizards and clerics of [[Thor]] who enjoy raining destruction on their foes. To join, you must slay a Large or bigger creature whose challenge rating is higher than your level, bring its head or another fitting trophy to your local hall, and tell the story of the fight at a rowdy gathering of the members. If they cheer and pound their tankards loudly enough, you're in, once you pay the 20 gp yearly dues.

## Friends and Foes

The Wyrm Slayers count on the [[Skaldic Brotherhood]] to spread their deeds, and some bring a skald along to chronicle a hunt firsthand. They hire guides from the [[Lodge of the Dancing Bear]] to find a dragon's lair, and use the [[Steadfast Company of Mile-Steppers]] for transport. They dislike the [[Glorious Order of Giant Killers]] for its cultlike devotion and rigid ranks, and they loathe the [[Cult of Ragnarok]] for wanting to hasten the end of the world. They know they need to kill a lot more dragons before they're ready to fight in Wotan's armies.

## Fellows' Halls

Each Fellows' Hall is a longhouse with a great feasting hall hung with stuffed monster heads and other trophies, where every kill is celebrated loudly, and a training ground outside where veterans teach dragon-slaying to newer members. Each hall is named for the biggest monster in its trophy collection, so a hall may change its name after an impressive new kill.

| Wyrmhaunt Hall | In [[Skaldholm]]. The skeleton of the powerful white dragon Izrilthandis hangs from its lofty ceiling. Two legendary dwarven brothers slew her decades ago and now feast and fight in Valhalla, but a trace of her spirit lingers in her bones, and now and then her voice booms from her skull. The last time, a month ago, she laughed and announced that Thorkel Half-Bear had died in the claws of an ancient red dragon. The hall is famous for its raucous contests of drinking, axe-throwing, and arm-wrestling, and above all for its weekly boasting competitions, which local skalds attend as guests. |
