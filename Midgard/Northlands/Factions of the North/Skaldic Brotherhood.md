The North is famous for its skalds, bards and poets who compose and perform verse in honor of kings and jarls, and who serve as teachers, historians, and advisors through the tales they tell. The Skaldic Brotherhood is their guild. Skalds learn their trade at skaldic schools, each taught by a maestro who insists their own style of verse is the greatest. Skaldholm's schools are the best known, but there are a handful of lesser colleges elsewhere in the [[Northlands]], and all of them belong to the Brotherhood, though each runs itself.

The Master of Thyles, ruler of Skaldholm, leads the Brotherhood. Every nine years the title goes to the greatest skald, decided by a long contest of poetry, storytelling, and flyting. As the Brotherhood's skalds travel the North performing, they gather rumors and secrets from informants in every town, and all of it flows back to the Master, where the Brotherhood's scholars record and study it. Publicly, the Brotherhood exists to celebrate great deeds in verse and song and to preserve those stories for future generations. The Master and many skalds are also busy with the Riddle of the Forgotten Thing (see [[Skaldholm]]), which the Master hopes to solve before his nine-year term ends.

## Joining

Bards make up most of the Brotherhood, with clerics of [[Wotan]], wizards, scholars, rogues, and spies among its members too. Anyone who studied at a Northern skaldic school is a member automatically. Bards from colleges in farther lands must first join a skaldic school by performing for its maestro to the required standard and paying a 50 gp fee. Prospective members who aren't skalds must bring three interesting secrets along with the fee.

## Friends and Foes

The Brotherhood is on good terms with the [[Lodge of the Dancing Bear]] and the [[Shield-Maidens of Sif]], sings the deeds of the [[Fellowship of Wyrm Slayers]], and trades stories of the road with the [[Steadfast Company of Mile-Steppers]]. It keeps tabs on villainous groups and cults across the North. Under the current Master it has opposed the followers of [[Boreas (North Wind)|Boreas]] who seek an endless winter and the werewolves of [[Vargrike]], and helped [[Wolfheim]] against its vampire scourge.

## Places

| Place | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Hall of the Great Sagas | The Brotherhood's headquarters in the center of Skaldholm town, also called the Hall of Sagas, a multistory stone building. Its roof, gables, grand entrance, and great hall are carved with legendary heroes and monsters, and the hall rings with poetry and song from dawn until the small hours. The Master is there most days with his herald Sylvoll, on a great wooden throne beside the raised stage, watching performances, presiding over gatherings, and hearing reports from traveling skalds. Its library on the upper floors, curated by Randulfr Shield-Wolf, holds many books and scrolls, including the Bard's Chronology, a history and timeline of the world, and many genealogies. Most of the Brotherhood's collection, though, isn't written down at all: it is held by its oral historians, scholars who memorize the sagas and poems so they can be passed on. |
| College of Feathers | A skaldic school in [[Hrafnheim]] that trains mostly huginn bards, with a few ratatosk and others. Its ramshackle, many-storied hall is built into the branches of Hrafnheim's [[Yggdrasil\|World Tree]] and is very noisy, full of croaking ravenfolk and chittering ratatosk competing in poetry and storytelling contests. A temple to Wotan at its heart offers relief from the racket. Its maestro is the ravenfolk Margit Gracekenning. |

## Notable Members

| Name | Description |
| --- | --- |
| The Master of Thyles | The master bard Eirik Ellefsen, who took the title seven and a half years ago. Until the Riddle of the Forgotten Thing is solved, he can think of little else. See [[Skaldholm]]. |
| Jenner Mirin | A charming tiefling skald and actor, a master of lies and subterfuge who wins the confidence, and the beds, of commoners and nobles alike. Jenner enjoys every perk of being a beloved skald, gifts of coin, ale, and adoration alike, and rarely does anything that doesn't serve Jenner. |
| Nalgar the Sailor Skald | A bold explorer, skilled sailor, and talented wordsmith, one of the Master's most trusted friends. He has sailed far into southern lands and the Uttermost Sea looking for answers to the Riddle. He walks with a swagger and strums his lyre with a winning smile, and he picks up new stories and songs wherever he goes, which makes him a fine source of obscure Northern lore. |
