Nearly all giants hate [[Wotan]] and the other gods for slaying Aurgelmir, the first giant, back in the mists of time, but most are content to raid the odd human or dwarf community, flatten its temples, and kill its priests. The Blood Reckoning, also called the Blodgjold, does all of that with enthusiasm, but this giant vengeance cult wants far more: to bring down the gods themselves as revenge for their forefather. Its leaders are jotun, the mightiest of giants and immortal foes of the gods. From Jotunheim they plot every path to vengeance against Wotan and the Aesir, including hastening Ragnarok. Once Asgard lies in ruins and the gods are dead, they say, the jotun will regain their rightful place and giants will rule all creation.

Giants of every kind join the Blood Reckoning, along with ogres, ettins, and trolls, and giantkin and trollkin cultists who make ideal spies in human and dwarven lands. Its cells are found wherever there are giants, including [[Thursrike]] and [[The Bleak Expanse]], and it has a hall or temple in every town of Jotunheim. Since the gods are fated to die at Ragnarok, the cult sees the end of days as its surest road to vengeance, and it is said to have allied with the [[Cult of Ragnarok]]. It hates the [[Glorious Order of Giant Killers]], who return the feeling.

The jotun Skymir founded the Blood Reckoning with his cold-hearted wife Geirhild, and the two took the name Godkiller and set out to make the gods pay. Now King Bolthorn of [[Sengajordensblod]] has called the greatest giants to a Blodting to decide whether Jotunheim will embrace the Blood Reckoning's war.

## Places

| Place | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Blood Eagle Hall | The cult's headquarters, an enormous stronghold carved into the mountainside north of Griotunagardar. The treacherous path up to it is lined with the mutilated, rotting corpses of human and dwarven priests the giants have ritually murdered. Inside is the great feast hall of Skymir and Geirhild, where the cult's leaders meet every few months, with tables made from whole redwood trunks running its length and twin granite thrones at its head, carved with a great battle of gods and giants that the giants are, of course, winning. Many powerful weapons and artifacts are said to lie in the vaults below, waiting for the day the giants march on Asgard, guarded by a menacing son of [[Fenris]]. |
| Nine Warriors Shrine | An outdoor shrine to Aurgelmir in the frozen wastes of [[Thursrike]] between Kupparsheim and Birgkrona: a circle of nine standing stones, each about twenty feet tall and marked with worn runes, around a bloodstained altar. The cultists believe the stones are the sleeping forms of primordial giants, children of Aurgelmir imprisoned by the gods, held there by the great ley line called Loki's Bridge that runs beneath the circle. They gather on each new moon to sacrifice captured priests of Wotan on the altar, hoping enough blood will turn the ley line aside and wake the sleepers. |

## Notable Members

| Name | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Skymir Godkiller | The cult's leader, a fearsome jotun over twenty feet tall, with blond hair, a thick blond beard, and bright blue eyes. Three fingers of his left hand are missing, lost when he tore apart a white dragon's jaws with his bare hands. In battle he wields a huge greataxe of gleaming blue steel and revels in the slaughter. He is cunning, two-faced, and proud, and finds it very hard to refuse a challenge or a wager. |
| Krusgr Ironcap | A fire giant warlord, one of Skymir's most trusted lieutenants and a fanatic of the cult's creed. He wears a huge helmet and heavy, dented iron armor over lava-red skin and wields an enormous flaming greatsword. He is quick to anger, and clever foes can goad him into mistakes. |
| Malfngur | Skymir's devoted bodyguard, a six-headed thrudgelmir giant, each head with a pair of glowing red eyes, who wields an adamantine axe named Shatterburn said to cut through anything. He speaks only when asked a direct question, tersely and only from his central head. |
