Crossing the frozen wastes and icy seas of the [[Northlands]] is a daunting task, so wily merchants and wise travelers hire professionals: the Steadfast Company of Mile-Steppers, dwarven transport experts who carry trade goods, small packages, and people safely and discreetly almost anywhere in the North.

The Company was founded ten years ago by Birgir Silverhorn, an unlucky dwarven merchant furious at losing yet another shipload to trollkin sea reavers. Trade could thrive in the North, he argued, if only there were a reliable way to move it. With a few retired dwarven reavers and grizzled ship captains, he gave up buying and selling and started a company selling safe passage instead. It has grown fast. Besides its Wolfheim headquarters, it keeps offices and warehouses in Bjeornheim, [[Trollheim|Noatun]], [[Skaldholm]], [[Stannasgard]], and a few major southern ports, and local agents in smaller towns across the North. It moves cargo by sea in knarrs and by land in wagons, dog sleds, and reindeer sleighs. Those who pay a premium for speed may be offered a flying sleigh or, rarely, a trip by planar pathways. The Company has an altruistic streak, but it is still out to enrich its members, and it takes contracts from almost anyone with very few questions asked.

## Joining

The Company draws adventurers and mercenaries who enjoy long treks through untamed wilds or across stormy seas and the thrill of fighting off monsters and pirates to see their charges safely home; druids, fighters, rangers, and rogues are the most common members. It is always looking for new members and often hires guards, sailors, and animal handlers for single jobs. Joining for good takes the sponsorship of a member in good standing. The founders were all dwarves, but anyone with the right skills and a strong work ethic is welcome.

## Friends and Foes

The Company hires guides from the [[Lodge of the Dancing Bear]] for jobs into unknown country and trades stories and news with the [[Skaldic Brotherhood]]. It sometimes works with the [[Glorious Order of Giant Killers]], whose stated goal of ridding the North of warlike giants fits the Company's own goal of safe trade routes, though Birgir thinks there is something off about the order's leadership. The [[Storm Reavers]] are its bitter foes.

## Places

| Place | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Frostwolf Hall | The Company's headquarters in the rowdy reaver town of [[Wolfheim]], an unlikely home chosen because that is where Birgir washed up; even Wolfheim, he declared, would benefit from strong trade. It is a well-fortified wooden compound on the harbor with its own docks, warehouse, kennels, and reindeer corral. Jobs are planned and assigned in the great longhouse at its center, whose walls are covered in detailed maps of the North. Winter wolves and devious traps guard the warehouse. |
| Sweet Honey Longhouse | The Company's base on the [[Björnrike]] coast, in Bjeornheim, with a drinking hall for members, docks for its knarrs, and a warehouse guarded by a hive of ferocious bees, a gift from Queen Yohana Honeyhair after the Company saved last year's salmon run from a hungry bukavac. Merchants buying wares from the south and the east here often hire the Company to carry them onward, so there is plenty of work for adventurers. |
| Blizzard Creek Waypost | One of the wayposts the Company builds along its routes, where couriers and their dogs or reindeer shelter for the night. This remote one on the edge of [[Jotunheim]] was attacked by undead from nearby vaettir barrows while a Mile-Stepper named Herman sheltered there. He survived a terrifying night, but his dogs were killed and he was left stranded, and days alone have driven him mad. Anyone who turns up there is likely to be attacked in a murderous rage. |

## Notable Members

| Name | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Birgir Silverhorn | The founder and leader, a wily, persuasive dwarf with an immaculately braided gray beard and a jeweled eyepatch, bought after a trollkin reaver took his eye on his last ill-fated voyage as a merchant. He is welcome in the halls of kings and jarls across the North. He insists on joining several expeditions each season to make sure the Company's meticulous standards are kept, which frustrates the other Mile-Steppers, not least because Birgir is spectacularly unlucky and things go wrong around him. |
| Radulfr Grousebeard | The courier for jobs that must go fast: a cantankerous, bald dwarven ringmage with a wild bushy beard, wrapped head to foot in furs, who prefers animals to people. He drives a magic sleigh whose six reindeer can fly, covering nearly 100 miles a day, alone or, on dangerous runs, with the Goreaxe Sisters, three trollkin reavers who never speak. |
| Shivertail | The courier for jobs that must go unseen: an extremely overconfident ravenfolk with bright blue eyes and pure white feathers that hide her against the snow, an expert at crossing the wilds undetected. She once sneaked into [[Vargrike]]'s Moon Palace to deliver a threatening message to Wargaz the Cruel from Uffi Toothless, and once crossed a gorge over [[Thursrike\|Kettil Buckbridge]]'s belly after making him laugh. She loves shiny things and insists on being paid in gems. |
