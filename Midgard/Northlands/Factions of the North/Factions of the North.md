Some groups in the [[Northlands]] reach across many realms: guilds, fellowships, mercenary orders, and cults. Their deeds turn up in rumors and skalds' songs even when no one from them is in the room, and adventurers can win their friendship, join them, or earn their enmity. Groups tied to a single realm are described on that realm's page.

## Standing

Your standing with each faction starts at 0 and rises or falls as your deeds help or hinder what it values. Higher standing earns favors, work, and access; lower standing brings snubs, trouble, and eventually violence. Standing with one faction is separate from your wider reputation, though a faction that is impressed or offended enough may talk you up or run you down.
