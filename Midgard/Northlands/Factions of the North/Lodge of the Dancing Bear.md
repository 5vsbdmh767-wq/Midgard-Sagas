The Lodge of the Dancing Bear is a loose, far-flung fellowship of guides and explorers, with members from [[Björnrike]] in the east to [[Thursrike]] and the [[Legendary Places of the North|Isle of Loki]] in the west. The Dancing Bear Guides are a rough-and-tumble lot, most at home in the wilds, who come into town for one reason: to show city folk the freedom and beauty of the wilderness. Guides believe that closed-in streets full of filth hide the truth the wider world has to offer, and that it's their duty to show townsfolk what they're missing. They work as wilderness guides, trappers, teamsters, and smugglers, taking any job that means dragging civilized folk through the brambles to broaden their horizons.

No one person leads the Lodge. The counselors meet at Gorsewood Lodge twice a year, on New Year's Dawn and at the summer solstice, debate long into the night, and send their decisions out to the guides, which can take weeks.

## Joining

The Lodge suits adventurers with a love of nature, who would rather sleep under the stars than in a flea-ridden inn bed, and who love finding new places to mark on their weather-stained maps; barbarians, druids, and rangers are the most common members. Candidates must pass an initiation test overseen by an experienced guide to prove their survival skills and knowledge of nature. Every test is different, but all involve several nights alone in the wild, often in foul weather or among dangerous beasts.

## Friends and Foes

Guides are on good terms with the [[Skaldic Brotherhood]] and lead the way for the [[Steadfast Company of Mile-Steppers]], though some refuse to, fearing that too many well-traveled trade routes will bring big settlements. They sometimes escort the [[Fellowship of Wyrm Slayers]], but only when convinced the quarry threatens other wildlife. They view the [[Glorious Order of Giant Killers]] with even more suspicion, oppose the [[Cult of Ragnarok]] for wanting to end the world, and won't knowingly work with villainous factions.

## Lodges

The Dancing Bears keep dozens of lodges, large and small, across the North.

| Lodge | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Gorsewood Lodge | A four-story lodge of wood and stone on the shores of Lake Dreken, where the counselors meet. A herd of giant elk led by an awakened bull named Silvertine lives in the surrounding woods. |
| Moonrise Lodge | A two-story wooden lodge hidden deep in the pine forests of [[Björnrike]]'s northern border, a haven for former werewolves who have fled [[Vargrike]] after the fey cured them. Asa Brokefang, a trollkin wandering druid, welcomes them and may invite worthy folk to join the guides. Anyone planning to brave the ruins of the Moon Palace or look for the Den of [[Fenris]] should come here for a guide. |

## Notable Members

| Name | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Gyda Nightpaws | A brown-furred bearfolk berserker who would rather defend the wild than show it off. She is waging a private war on the Coiled Ring, a Ragnarok cult that has been wreaking havoc from [[Stannasgard]] to [[Skaldholm]], and her body is covered in scars from the exploding bodies of cultists she has slain. He keeps a close eye on the giants of [[Jotunheim]] and worries that some have allied with the Cult of Ragnarok. |
| Tove Ulfdottir | The longest-serving and eldest of the counselors, gray-haired and nearly toothless, who has retired from guiding to live as a hermit on the windswept tundra of the [[The Bleak Expanse\|Bleak Expanse]] with only Snowfoot, a polar bear, for company. She speaks to others only on monthly supply trips to isolated settlements and at the counselors' meetings, where her wisdom is prized. Now and then she rescues travelers from remorhazes and lindwurms and guides them to safety. |
