The Glorious Order of Giant Killers is a military order dedicated to protecting the [[Northlands]] from the giants. It keeps chapterhouses in many towns, more of them in the kingdoms bordering Jotunheim, from which it answers giant attacks and raids into giant-held lands. Many chapterhouses are sponsored by local jarls and kings, who value the order as a deterrent to their giant neighbors and as a handy way to keep their rowdier warriors busy.

The Giant Killers are organized in a stricter hierarchy than most [[Northlanders]] are used to. Each chapter is led by a hersir, a commander, who answers to the order's founder, Jarl Folkvar Jotunslayer. Many members adore Folkvar for his near-legendary giant-slaying deeds, and his portrait hangs in every chapterhouse. Each evening, tales of the Glorious Leader's deeds are told with almost religious reverence, which some newcomers find a little odd.

## Joining

Clerics, fighters, and rogues are the most common members, and the order is always looking for recruits. Visit a chapterhouse and describe your fighting experience, especially against giants, ogres, or trolls. The hersir decides whether you have what it takes, and then you swear an oath of allegiance to Jarl Folkvar. Members supply their own weapons and armor and keep them in top condition. Pay is 1 gp a day in training or on duty, with room and board at the chapterhouse for those who need it. Recruits train to fight giants and other huge foes before their first missions; other duties include scouting, carrying messages, and guarding caravans.

## Friends and Foes

The order's most hated enemy is the [[Blood Reckoning]], and the two clash often and bloodily. It also skirmishes regularly with the [[Cult of Ragnarok]]. The [[Steadfast Company of Mile-Steppers]] sometimes works with it. Most other orders are a little wary of it: the [[Fellowship of Wyrm Slayers]] dislikes its cultlike devotion and rigid ranks, and the [[Lodge of the Dancing Bear]] keeps its distance.

## Strongholds

| Place | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Jokulsborg | The order's headquarters, a mighty circular fortress on the river northeast of [[Thrumagard]], about 100 miles from [[Legendary Places of the North\|Reykurbrand]]. Its stone walls are tall and thick enough to keep out giants and bristle with siege engines. A great eyrie atop the central keep houses snow-white giant eagles that carry the order's scouts and messengers, and in the foundry beneath the castle, dwarven ringmages enchant the order's weapons with giant-slaying magic. |
| White Wolf Chapterhouse | In the town of [[Wolfheim]], a longhouse whose entrance is shaped like a wolf's jaws and roars at anyone who isn't a Giant Killer who touches the doors. Its hersir, Aevar Splintershard, a one-eyed dwarven priest of [[Wotan]], gave up reaving to fight the jotun. He won King Thulsamir's ear after the order dealt with giants and cultists of Chernobog slaughtering farmers in the north of the kingdom, and he urges the king to let the order march north and claim the lands of the fallen reaver kingdoms. |

## Notable Members

| Name | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Jarl Folkvar Jotunslayer | The order's founder and Glorious Leader, commander of Jokulsborg: a handsome, charismatic dwarf with an elegantly braided beard, deep blue eyes, and a winning smile. He has long believed the North could be a power in the world if only its squabbling kings and jarls worked together. A cunning commander, he beat back several giant raids on his lands before marching into Jotunheim himself and slaying the jotun warlord Gorm Foehammer in his own castle, which ended the raids for good. More victories followed, recruits flocked to his banner, and jarls and kings from [[Stannasgard]] to [[Tanserhall]] now support the order. Some folk already talk of him as a future king. |
| Osk Silverpaws | Folkvar's lieutenant and chief diplomat, an exuberant bearfolk skald who swoops into town on her giant eagle, Snowwing, whenever the order needs to win over a jarl or noble. She tells stirring tales of the Giant Killers standing firm against jotun aggression, makes a convincing case for money and warriors, and rarely leaves without new recruits. |
