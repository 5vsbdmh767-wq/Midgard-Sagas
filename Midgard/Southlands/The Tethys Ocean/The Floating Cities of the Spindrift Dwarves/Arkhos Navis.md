The largest and most prestigious of the [[Driftholm]] cities, Arkhos Navis consists of over fifty vessels bound together in a circular pattern. At its center stands the Grand Forge, where master crafters work with both traditional metals and seafaring materials.

- The Tidekeepers' Council: Elite navigators who chart courses through treacherous waters
- The Floating Markets: Multiple levels of shops and trading posts connected by rope bridges
- The Shell Archives: A library carved into massive pearl-lined chambers below deck
