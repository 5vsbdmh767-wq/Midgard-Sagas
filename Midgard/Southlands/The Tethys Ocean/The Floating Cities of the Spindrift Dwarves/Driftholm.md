The [[Spindrift]] [[Dwarves]] have developed a unique architectural marvel - mobile cities built upon massive interconnected vessels called "Driftholms." These floating settlements combine dwarven engineering with maritime innovation, creating sustainable communities that can navigate the waters of the [[Southlands]].
### Engineering Innovations
- Hull-binding runes that allow vessels to be safely connected and disconnected.
- Specialized forges that can operate at sea.
- Water purification systems using coral and enchanted filters.
- Storm-prediction devices combining traditional dwarven runecraft with maritime wisdom.
