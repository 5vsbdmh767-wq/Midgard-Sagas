The [[City Godlings of Nuria]] Natal are minor gods, saints, demigods, and obsolete powers—deities with limited  abilities and small portfolios, and supported by few  shrines or worshipers. Their temples are dusty, and  their influence sometimes restricted to strange places,  though a minor godling in [[Nuria Natal|Nuria]] might be more widely venerated in the tosculi hives, or vice versa.

The godlings are most prevalent in Nuria Natal, a place that has spawned a great number of gods over time; most of the gods of the [[Southlands]] originated in Nuria, and many others are the offspring of Nurian gods. However, despite prevalence in Nuria Natal, you will find the worship of at least one of these minor deities in most, if not all, cities of the Southlands. Their followers are sometimes wandering pantheist priests, individuals who serve multiple gods.
