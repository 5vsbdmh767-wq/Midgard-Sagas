Residence of [[Lord Saein Zadimus Seskel Silwyvern Tyrnoir]] and the rest of the Tyrnoir family, in theory. 

They operate heavily out of [[Eudalis]] and [[Sar Shaba]] and many don't know the true home of the family is on this heavily disputed island. 

It is Lord Saein's influence that keeps it in [[Aksaba]]n hands and from falling entirely into the hands of the merchants of [[Shibai]].
