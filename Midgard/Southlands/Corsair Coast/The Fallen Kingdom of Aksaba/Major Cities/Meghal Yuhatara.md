This ancient fortress city guards the main trade way, especially the northwestern routes from [[Mhalmet]], [[Free Cities of the Desert|Siwal]], and [[Nuria Natal]]. Its location enables foreigners to engage in commerce without needing to come near the dangerous area around [[Sar Shaba]], but they pay higher prices for the privilege. The fortress is ruled by Commander [[Fakhiri Rezghi]] of the [[Queen’s Guard]] who dispatches regular patrols to keep the roads safe. Its inhabitants are predominantly [[Makudai]]
