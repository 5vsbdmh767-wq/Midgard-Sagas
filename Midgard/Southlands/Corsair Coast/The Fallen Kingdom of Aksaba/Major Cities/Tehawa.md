This small city is ruled by [[Shadmehr Khoshkam]], a former commander in the [[Queen’s Guard]], which maintains a training facility here for young Aksabans known as the [[Captain’s Guardhouse]]. The city also hosts temples to Astarte ([[Isis]]) and Mithras ([[Horus]]), and shrines to their various saints known collectively as the [[Holy Queens]].
