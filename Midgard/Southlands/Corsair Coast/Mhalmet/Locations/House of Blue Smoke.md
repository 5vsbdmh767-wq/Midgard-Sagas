[[Mhalmet]] has several “dens” where patrons can go to inhale dream smoke, a highly addictive drug that causes euphoria, which is grown in parts of the east. These high-end establishments offer their customers luxurious surroundings, silk divans, and attractive young attendants. At the other end of the spectrum, the House of Blue Smoke is scruffy and ill-kept, and only the poorest, most desperate addicts come here. Thefts and violence are commonplace occurrences.
