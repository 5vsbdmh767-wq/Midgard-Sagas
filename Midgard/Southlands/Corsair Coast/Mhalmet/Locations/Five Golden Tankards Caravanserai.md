Located just inside the city gates, this walled three-story compound offers accommodations for newly arrived caravans, stabling for their camels, and somewhere to store goods before they are brought to market. In the shady central courtyard, palm trees grow around a fountain that depicts a jovial [[Ninkash]] pouring water from her oversized tankard. Lodging here is convenient but pricey, and merchants must pay protection to [[the Black Table]] on arrival or risk their goods going missing.
