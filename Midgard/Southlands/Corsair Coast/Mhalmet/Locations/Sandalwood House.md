Adventurers in [[Mhalmet]] are often drawn to the infamous Sandalwood House, a social club and guild hall where like-minded thrill-seekers can join expeditions into the [[Southlands]] to hunt big game, discover lost civilizations, or plunder ancient tombs. The Dashing Scoundrels, a discerning thieves’ guild that carries out daring heists with considerable panache, can be contacted here.
