[[Shibai]]’s navy is based at Talut, the island’s third largest city. Grand Mirali Harim Habir commands the fleet that protects merchant vessels from the pirates of [[Mhalmet]] and the [[Free Islands of Tethys]]. Hyfad was the amir of Talut before becoming Sultan of Shibai, and was replaced by his lugubrious qadi, Kurosh
