This estuary city is famous for its spice market. Here, exotic spices and herbs from all over Midgard—and 
sometimes farther afield—are bought and sold in a huge, covered bazaar topped with a domed roof decorated with tiled mosaics depicting ships and the sea. Ormzhad, a water jinnborn who abandoned her siraati path long ago to join [[Shibai]]’s merchant houses, serves as amir of Alhijr.
