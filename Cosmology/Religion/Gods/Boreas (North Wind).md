*The Devouring Wind, God of the North*
*Wind, Bringer of Storms and Killing Cold,*
*Patron of the Giants*

**Domains:** Tempest, Travel
**Alignment:** Chaotic Evil
**Favored Weapons:** Pick

Boreas is the autumn storm and the winter gale, the wind that carries sleet and hail down from the north. He is the son of [[Marena]] in her guise as the Winter Maiden, and his work is to carry her killing cold out into the world. He means to bury all of Midgard under an endless winter, and the prophecies say he will manage it someday. From the highest mountain in the north he brews his storms and sends giants, yeti, and worse south. Some worship him only as a herald of Marena, which he hates.

He is usually pictured as a winged old man with wild hair and a wilder beard, a cackling madman full of lust, suspicion, and rage. He likes to change shape, and has appeared as a living storm, a white stallion, a dragon, or a hawk. In stallion form he sired the Winterborn horses and the hardy ponies of the [[Khazzaki]], and he claims to be grandfather to the winter wolves. [[Northlanders]] curse him as the father of every evil the cold brings.

==**Worshipers**==
Giants, trolls, and ogres follow Boreas more than anyone, along with evil dwarves, winter wolves, and some goblin tribes. Nightgarms and fraughashar sometimes become his priests. His symbol is a circling line of wind, and he has no holy books.

==**Shrines and Priests**==
Boreas has few priests and fewer shrines, usually a pile of stones or a cave near the top of a windy mountain.

==**Masks**==
Some think Boreas is an evil mask of Azuran or of Chernobog.

==**Other Faiths**==
Boreas gets along reasonably well with Chernobog, [[Loki]], and his mother [[Marena]]. [[Thor]], [[Wotan]], [[Sif]], [[Lada]], and Khors are all his enemies, as are the three Wind Lords of the [[Southlands]].

==**In the North**==
Northerners tell Boreas's origin differently. In their telling, he was once the primordial spirit of the North Wind, born at the dawn of creation. Exiled by his sibling wind spirits over a quarrel about mortals, he fled into the [[Northlands]] and shed his spirit form to be reborn as a god. His favored weapon there is the war pick. See [[Gods of the North]].

He believes the prophesied Fimbulwinter before Ragnarok proves his endless winter will come, and he would like it sooner rather than later; meanwhile he works tirelessly to visit evil and strife on the world, knowing his giants can endure the icy death he brews. His usual guise is an eagle-winged frost giant in furs with a wild, windswept beard, and all his forms are shaggy and unkempt.

Besides giants, ogres, and trolls, his followers include goblins, hobgoblins, and bugbears, Northern dwarf clans that have turned from community to bitterness and greed, and many winter wolves. His worshipers sacrifice captives by leaving them naked to the winter night, sabotage hearth fires and storehouses, and help yeti, ice maidens, and other creatures do his will. Theft is no crime to them, and burning the homes, hearths, and altars of his enemies is a sacred duty. He demands they work to break the power of the other gods and bury the North in ice and snow, and they believe their faith will spare them when he wins. His symbol is a circling, knotted line of wind. Some of his followers paint crude scenes of his power in their victims' blood under rocky overhangs and on cave walls, always showing smaller folk beneath the boots and talons of his monsters. The paintings are so alike that some explorers believe a single hand made them all, perhaps the god's own. He has no organized priesthood and needs no holy sites, feeding on the blood his followers spill. He grants power to depraved clerics, druids, and rangers who kill in his name, and some warlocks hear his whispers and do his bloody bidding for power and protection from the cold.
