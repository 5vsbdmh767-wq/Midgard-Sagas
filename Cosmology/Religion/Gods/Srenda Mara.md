*Goddess of Portals and Forgotten Secrets,*
*Mistress of the Great Maze, Voice of the Void,*
*Sacred Guide to the Lost*

**Domains:** Knowledge
**Favored Weapon:** Staff-mace

Srenda Mara is a dark goddess who comes dressed as a friend to the weak, the lost, and the dying. She wears a pale blue cloak, has pale skin and red hair, and carries a staff topped with a blue crystal mace. Many of her followers are people she rescued: explorers starving in a dead end of the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]], or freezing on a dead world in the Near Void. From every one of them she takes a price. She was once the wife of [[Hune]], the god of portals, before she turned to darkness.

==**Worshipers**==
Cultists, blood mages, rogue portal mages, disgraced [[Keepers of the Keys|Keepers]], and fallen minotaurs. Many of her faithful have been changed by her bargains into derro, shades, ghosts, vampires, wraiths, or minor demons, and they go looking for desperate travelers to offer the same deal.

==**Symbols and Books**==
A lantern burning with green light. Her only book, *The True Path*, mixes healing poultices with blood rituals and soul-trapping spells, and the [[Concord of Stars]] burns every copy it finds.

==**Shrines and Priests**==
Her shrines are always hidden deep in the Labyrinth behind a secret door or a turning stair, lit by green tapers and covered in maps of the maze. At the heart of each is a dusty altar where the living are remade into undead servants.

==**Other Faiths**==
She is on good terms with [[Vardesain]], [[Mot]], and the archdevil Parzelon, King of Secrets.
