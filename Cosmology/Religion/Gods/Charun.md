*God of Death, Master of the Rivers [[The Ever River|Styx]]*
*and Lethe, Guardian of Souls, Watcher at*
*the Door, Patron of Sailors and Gravediggers*

**Domains:** Death, Nature
**Alignment:** Neutral
**Favored Weapon:** Staff

Charun watches over the passage of souls: into the world at birth, out of it at death, and along the roads beyond. He ferries the dead on the rivers Styx and Lethe and knows every branch of every river between the planes. He is not a dark god, only a guide from life into death, and he protects mortal travelers on the planes from demons and angels alike. He sees most undead as broken shells of the souls he should have carried, though he has more respect for darakhul, vampires, and liches, who keep their minds. Fey and other immortals annoy him, because they trade in souls and upset their proper passage.

He is always hooded, and no one agrees on his face. Some see snaky hair and boar's tusks, some cold blue eyes or a long black beard. Everyone agrees about the snake tattoos on his forearms.

==**Worshipers**==
Everyone honors him a little, since everyone dies, but soldiers, executioners, mourners, necromancers, and the grieving are his devoted. The river elves of [[Arbonesse]] love Charun the Boatman, and the shadow fey respect his hold over the planes and the shadow roads. In the [[The Darakhul Imperium|Ghoul Imperium]] he is a slaves' god who offers the hope of escape.

==**Symbols and Books**==
A boat or an oar. His holy book is the *River Passage*, a collection of dirges and hymns.

==**Shrines and Priests**==
His holy places are graveyards and rivers, and many of them touch other planes. His temples deliver babies, bury the dead, and drive out hauntings, and for a good price his priests will guide travelers beyond the mortal world. The faith is centered in [[Valera]], where the pale figure named Vanth is high priest. Undead are rare within church grounds but common as guardians in the graveyards beside them.

==**Masks**==
[[Anu-Akma]], [[Marena]], [[Seggotan]], and [[Thoth-Hermes]] are all rumored to be his masks, and a few even claim [[Chernobog]], which his faithful call rank heresy. In a few places in the West he is linked with [[Lada]]. In the North he is Hod the Blind, a son of [[Wotan]].

==**Other Faiths**==
He argues with Anu-Akma over who owns the fate of souls, and with [[Sarastra]]. He consults with [[Rava]] when fates get tangled, and some say he is Ceres's husband. [[Nethus]] is an old friend, and some say a brother.

==**What Charun Demands**==
Bury the dead by their rites and leave no body to rot, not even an enemy's. Learn to walk in the dark. Protect those coming into the world and those leaving it. Honor the remains of the dead, and destroy rampaging undead and thieving immortals. Visit a grave every week to tend it and meditate on the end of all things.

==**Beyond Midgard**==
In the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]], Charun is patron of the [[Minor Wayfaring Groups|Boatwrights]] who ply the River Styx, and his followers are often trusted as go-betweens because he takes no side in worldly quarrels.

==**In the Shadow Realm**==
Charun is one of the five great gods of the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]], where the Styx and the Lethe run through the land itself. The sable elves and river elves love him as a sheltering, welcoming figure at the end of a long life, and the shadow fey court him for his control of the planes and the shadow roads. He is one of the few gods worshiped by creatures of the planes, thanks to his hold on the planar rivers. His clergy's duties are set out in *Chiron's Coin*, and every priest must learn healing, the undead, or the planes. He has thriving shrines in [[Soriglass]], a pilgrimage town devoted to him at Vysanth on the Styx, and a high priestess in [[Merrymead]], the boatwoman Linne Mallemele, and he is the patron of [[Nightbrook]]. In the [[Moonlit Glades]] he is Hod the Blind, god of caves and darkness, a minor son of Wotan or perhaps a diminished Wotan himself. [[Loki|Laughing Loki]] is rumored there to be another of his masks, and a few even claim the dark god Chernobog, which his faithful call rank heresy. [[Alquam]] is his greatest friend among the dark gods, and the followers of [[Kupkoresh]] often aid his. Ceres, sometimes called his lover or wife, is a minor goddess at best in the Shadow Realm.
