*First Dragon and Chewer of the World Tree,*
*Lord of the Corpse Shore, Eater of the Dead,*
*Render of the Void, Friend to Fenris*

**Domains:** Knowledge, War
**Favored Weapon:** Longsword

Nidhogg is the dragon who lives among the roots of [[Yggdrasil]] and gnaws at them, meaning to bring down the World Tree and every world with it. He claims to be the first of all dragons and above every other. Half mad with prophecy and with things learned from the Void, he eats anyone who visits him, and the dead who wash up on the Corpse Shores from the Astral Sea. He wants supremacy, chaos, and the end of everything.

His golden eyes pull viewers into despair. His scales are green and midnight black, his breath rots the living, and his claws go through steel plate like leaves. Some of his spawn look like giant centipedes. Strangely, the ratatosk get along with him. By old custom they send a messenger down to his roots every week with news from the great eagle at the top of the Tree, and on some days he holds his breath and listens.

==**Worshipers**==
Mostly the mad: Void cultists, the insect-like satarre, derro, and berserkers of the frozen worlds, along with his own spawn and the void dragonborn. Some void dragons honor him as an ancestor.

==**Symbols and Books**==
A withered dragon's foot with long talons, sometimes marked with the silver rune of rebirth. The *Ending Saga*, kept by the Black Scribes of the [[Great Chantry of the Voidmother|Great Chantry]], records his ravings and prophecies of Ragnarok.

==**Shrines and Priests**==
His shrines are usually built by dragons or dragonborn: runes clawed into a cliff, a pile of arranged bones, or a pale violet glow. The rare temples hold a wooden statue of the god, always riddled with wormholes and gnaw marks.

==**Other Faiths**==
He is a friend of [[Fenris]] and an enemy of every god who wants the worlds to go on existing.

==**In the North**==
In the [[Northlands]], Nidhogg is said to lie coiled on a hoard of grave goods and sacrifices hidden among [[Yggdrasil]]'s roots, and those who have dared to look into the Ur-dragon's golden eyes claim all the secrets of the Void can be glimpsed in their maddening depths. The god [[Ratatoskr]] trades news with him and plays tricks on him. See [[Gods of the North]].
