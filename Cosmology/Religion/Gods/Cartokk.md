*God of Dreams, Forest Lord, Summoner of Seeds,*
*Lord of Eternal Life, King in the Buried Kingdom,*
*the Slumbering Titan*

**Domains:** Knowledge, Life, Nature
**Favored Weapon:** Club

Cartokk is the Dreaming God of the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]], a god of visions and mysteries, of seekers trying to understand the rhythm of the seasons and the secrets of the stars. He is also a harvest god, lord of crops, winter sleep, spring rebirth, and summer feasting. His faithful believe his dreams made the [[Worlds of the Labyrinth|Ten Thousand Worlds]]. Unbelievers think his followers mostly waste their time chasing visions and getting drunk. He is shown as a handsome bearded man crowned with holly or barley, dressed in a bearskin or lion's pelt, sometimes with up to eight arms and mist curling around his legs. His statues have closed eyes. His holy symbol has open ones.

==**Worshipers**==
Shepherds, farmers, hedge witches, druids, hermits, and mystics. The devoted ones call themselves the [[Servants of the Dreaming God|Dreamers]], or Servants of the Dreaming God, and travel the Labyrinth to learn his mysteries.

==**Symbols and Books**==
An open eye or a third eye, wisps of purple smoke, peacock feathers, and a particular turquoise. Every Dreamer consults *The Holy Eye*, a mix of mysticism, vision-potion recipes, and sensible advice on treating people kindly.

==**Shrines and Priests**==
His greatest shrine, the Temple of the Golden Portal on the island of Bamahi, is built around a dead portal said to lead to the room where he sleeps. Priests try to wake it every day. Villages welcome his priests at planting and harvest. The rest of the year they get mistaken for alchemists, beggars, or drunks.

==**Other Faiths**==
[[Ninkash]] and [[Milikamar]] are his companions, and [[Zenthar]] protects his Dreamers.
