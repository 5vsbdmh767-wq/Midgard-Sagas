*God of the Earth and Waters, Creator of Midgard, Father of Serpents, Lord of Oceans, Emperor of Earthquakes, Patron of Giants and Dragons, Voice of Distant Stars*

**Domains:** Apocalypse, Dragon, Hunger, Nature, Ocean, Prophecy
**Alignment:** Neutral
**Favored Weapons:** Bite, spear, battleaxe

Midgard is encircled by a god made flesh: the great World Serpent, a monstrous beast that marks the edge of the world and that holds, and some say created, the whole of it. Biting his own tail, he makes the tides with his breathing and the storms with his snorts and sneezes. He is called Veles in the East and South, Jörmungandr in the North, Djyy in the [[Southlands]], and Ouroboros in the [[The Crossroads|Crossroads]] and the West. The [[Northlanders]] and the [[The Mharoti Empire|Mharoti]] know him well, and other lands less so.

The Serpent embodies worldly things: strength and rage, wisdom and water, earth, blood, and death. He cares nothing for any afterlife and everything for the natural world and his many children, scaly and otherwise. He is hugely powerful but indolent, even lazy, and rarely speaks to his worshipers. Prophecy says he will one day devour the earth entirely. Some claim he is one of [[Loki]]'s children, but that is likely another of Loki's tall tales. Sailors who say they have seen his flanks describe a wall of mossy, scaly flesh encircling the oceans, and beyond it the Void, full of stars and darkness.

It was Veles who ended the open slaughter of the gods. When jealous Aten had killed a dozen of the southern gods and even [[Thoth-Hermes]] and [[Wotan]] grew afraid, Veles spoke the Words of Peace, which compelled the gods to stop warring openly and to wear masks so that they might not know one another. See [[The Nature of Gods & Religion in Midgard]].

==**Worshipers**==
Giants and dragons are his chief followers, though some dwarves, humans, and kobolds also honor him. Ouroboros reminds mortals how short and small their lives are, so few care to worship him, and most who do are dour stoics or frenzied believers who live as fully as they can, since the afterlife is uncertain at best. Certain druid cults follow the Serpent of Wisdom, and cults of the Void and the outer darkness claim a dark, apocalyptic Serpent as their patron.

==**Symbols and Books**==
His symbol is a snake biting its own tail. Some believe Veles taught the Northern Tongue to the giants and the Southern Tongue to the dragons. Any books of his teaching are rare and known to few. Whispers tell of one called the *Forked-Tongue Prophecies*, or the *Circled Tongue*, said to grant power over serpents, drakes, and dragons and to foretell the end of all things.

==**Shrines and Priests**==
Veles has few great shrines or famous priests, though many caves serve as his holy places, especially those with streams or springs. Some dragons and giants call themselves his high priest, and he does not seem to care. The Portal of the Void in Harkesh claims to be his greatest shrine, and its high priest, Yavuz al-Prezhan, is one of the few who performs rites to Veles himself, rites dragons are keen on, involving sacrifices of kobolds and dragonkin. A lesser but important shrine stands in the [[Grand Duchy of Dornig]], at Bad Solitz, in the private sanctuary of the vann Rottsten family at the Tourmaline Palace. Its cleric, Hudricus Orovan, is called the Keeper of the Keys, after the Keys of Veles that are said to open all roads and portals.

==**Masks**==
His priests claim every other god is one of his masks. More plausibly, the eastern dragon gods may all be his faces or avatars. A few Northlanders believe Veles is only a mask of Loki, part of a tremendous plot with the giants.

==**Other Faiths**==
Ouroboros ignores other faiths, and they mostly ignore him. The great exceptions are [[Thor]] and his mask or brother [[Perun]], both mortal enemies of the Great Serpent. The dragon gods acknowledge Veles but do little to help or hinder him. Some Mharoti prophecies invoke "the Five Dragon Gods," while others speak only of "the Four Great Patron Gods of the Empire."

==**What Ouroboros Demands**==
Your time is short, and all things end. Make your mark on the world, show mercy or cruelty, but prepare yourself for the end of all things. Keep the ley lines running smooth and magic strong, and the world will live longer. Corrupt the ley lines and walk with dark gods, and you hasten the hour of the apocalypse.
