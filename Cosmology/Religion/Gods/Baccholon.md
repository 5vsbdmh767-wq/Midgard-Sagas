*God of Poetry, Lord of Wine, Master of Revels, Golden Words,*
*and Charming Courtesies, King of Lust and Chaos, Warden of*
*Shadow Roads, Lion Lord of Shapeshifters, Keeper of Prophecies,*
*God of the Third Eye, Patron of the Nymphs and Satyrs*

**Domains:** Beer (called Wine), Death, Lust, Prophecy, Travel
**Alignment:** Chaotic
**Favored Weapon:** Shortbow

Baccholon, also called Baccho, was once the Prince of Courtesies and Master of the [[Elves]], their patron and paragon. When the elves left the mortal world for the [[Silendora|Summer Lands]], he led the procession himself, flowers in his hair and a song on his lips. His human and elfmarked followers fell into a long despair, full of madness, drunkenness, and bitter wrath, and his temples fell out of use or were rededicated to [[Ninkash]], [[Charun]], or other gods. His echo still lingers in the poetic hymns and joyous images carved in old elven halls, but his priests are vanishingly few and often hard to tell from beggars and starving poets. A few of his order claim to remember the heights of elven ritual magic, though that may be wishful dreaming, something his followers have always been prone to. Once thought entirely dead, in recent years he has grown back a little, like leaves on the grapevine in spring.

## Worshipers

His few but vivid worshipers include debauched circles among certain noble houses, young poets, vintners, seers, and shapeshifters. Oddly, he also has a small following among dwarves, ravenfolk, [[Satyrs|satyrs]], and [[Bearfolk|bearfolk]], especially bear-shifters, wolf reavers, and doom croakers, who honor him as a martyred patron of shifters and seers.

## Symbols and Books

His holy books are many and contradictory, and charges of heresy and false prophecy split his followers into rival groups. His symbol is an amphora of blue-green faience, and sometimes a roaring lion.

## Shrines and Priests

His shrines were once everywhere, the greatest being the now-ruined Shrine of Purple Revels in Hirschberg. Wine shops and vintners keep small statues of him, as do some courtiers in the [[Arbonesse]] and poets in Salzbach, [[Perunalia]], and [[Valera]]. The Golden Temple of Baccho in Valera is now the city's wool and cloth market, though its crypts still hold generations of high priests of the Laughing Prince.

## Masks

He is sometimes linked with [[Loki]], [[Bastet]] (or Ailuros), Azuran, or [[Bengta]], though the connections are tenuous at best. Others believe he has been corrupted and is a mask of a dark goddess, the [[Black Goat|Goat of the Woods]], who was once Bacchana, his human and female form.

## Other Faiths

He finds the followers of Ninkash crude and unworthy. He dislikes "Mother Holda," whom he finds suffocating, and "Father Valeresh," whom he finds too pushy, though he was always Valeresh's fool and the only god who could tell the archer god when he was badly wrong. His charm is great, but so is his disdain for anyone who doesn't acknowledge his glory.

## What Baccholon Demands

Make art and celebrate life to the fullest. Leave no stone unturned and unpainted. Rage, love, and make your mark on everything. Embrace the dragon and the lamb, for both are your children.

## In the Shadow Realm

His shrines were once as common among the [[Shadow Fey|shadow fey]] as among the elves, gnomes, and alseid, but the Laughing Prince's followers there have slowly fallen into decay and neglect. His tavern-shrines now survive mainly in [[Dalliance]], where he is patron and the Shrine of Silver Laughter stands, and in [[Langmire's Court]], with its Sign of the Twin Cups. Prayers, offerings, and blessings are served there alongside gambling, dancing, and drink, so they double as favorite meeting places for the lesser nobility. He is close to [[Gytellisor]] in the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]]: the sable elves often name the two in the same breath, and bawdy tales say they were lovers in the Summer Lands or in [[Valera]] long ago.
