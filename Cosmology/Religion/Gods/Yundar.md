*God and Protector of the Ancients*

**Domains:** Crafting, Light
**Favored Weapon:** Spear

Huge, worn marble statues of Yundar stand all through the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]], every one of them headless. He wears a heavy collar of jewelry in a sunburst pattern and holds a strange spear with a point like a ray of light. The Ancients who carved him are long gone, but the minotaurs have adopted him as a symbol of lost greatness and are sure his missing head looked like theirs. To them he is the builder of the Labyrinth. [[Rinpash]] is his wife or sister.
