*Lord of the Sun, Bright Master of the Chariot, Son of Svarog, the Perfect Knight, Lord of Light and Destroyer of the Darkness, Patron of the [[Magdar]], Friend to Magus and Warrior*

**Domains:** Justice, Light
**Alignment:** Lawful Good
**Favored Weapons:** Lance, longsword

Khors was once king of the [[The Crossroads|Crossroads]] pantheon. He is fading now, as [[Rava]] takes his place in many lands, but he has by no means vanished. The Sun God still stands supreme in the [[Magdar Kingdom]] and Grisal, where his followers value his power against the terrors of the night. His many statues show him in a feathered cloak, heavy armor, and a wild plumed helmet, striking down dragons, knighting new heroes, and defending common folk against darkness.

==**Worshipers**==
The knights of the Order of the Undying Sun are his largest remaining stronghold. He is still widely worshiped in the Magdar Kingdom, in Grisal, and, in secret, in [[Rumela]] and [[Krakovar]]. Elsewhere he is largely forgotten.

==**Symbols and Books**==
His symbol is a radiant sun. His sacred text is the *Book of the Sun*, divided into the First Sun and the New Sun.

==**Shrines and Priests**==
Because they serve a declining god, his priests know how to slip into other temples and priesthoods, and many are pantheist priests. His worship once reached far to the east, but most places there now honor other gods. The temple in [[Zobeck]] is now devoted more to his father, Svarog ([[Volund]]), than to Khors himself. Some of his priests have gone to [[Triolo]] seeking converts, and it may be fertile ground, since the city is home to the head of his church, High Sunpriest Salomonn Csabos, a refugee from conquered Illyria.

==**Masks**==
Many think Khors is a gentler mask of Aten. Others link him to Baldur in the North, or to [[Lada]]. In Marea and Achillon he is called Apollon, part of a minor pantheon the humans of [[The Mharoti Empire]] keep, and in [[Ishadia]] he is honored as Quorzu.

==**Other Faiths**==
Khors is the unrelenting enemy of [[Sarastra]], the [[White Goddess]], [[Marena]], [[Vardesain]], and all the dark gods.

==**What Khors Demands**==
Rise and pray at dawn and at noon. Bring light to the darkness. Never approach a foe by stealth, only bravely and openly. Cast down demons, devils, and the dark gods without quarter. Stand fast in battle, for courage is the warrior's greatest virtue. If you must retreat, make it clear you mean to return and win the day. Let no shadow harm the innocent.
