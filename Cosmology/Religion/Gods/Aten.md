*Sun God, Jealous Lord of Light and Good, Master of the Law, Father of Khors and Lord of the Horizons, Patron of the Khemti*

**Domains:** Justice, Light
**Alignment:** Lawful Good
**Favored Weapon:** Mace

Aten is a rarity among the gods of Midgard: a jealous god who forbids his followers to worship any other. His priests may never be pantheists, and his followers are known for their unyielding devotion, including driving "unbelievers" out of villages devoted to him. The old tales say jealous Aten once killed a dozen of the southern gods, Ra first of all, then Isis, Nut, and [[Ptah]], and grew stronger with each death, until [[Veles]] spoke the Words of Peace.

He appears as a bronzed giant with a braided beard, piercing black eyes, and a powerful chest. His statues, honored by humans and dwarves alike, are often rather dwarvish in proportion, bald and bearded. He is not a maker or forger but the shining light that defines the day, protector of the weak and the old, friend to heroes and foe of the dark gods. His radiant face is hard to look upon, for his glory outshines mortal understanding and every other god. His followers are so sure of this that they call all other gods mere "reflections" or "pale shadows." The only ones they debate are [[Lada]] and [[Khors]], his half-divine children, whom they treat as saints, prophets, or great priests rather than gods, and small shrines to the two are common.

==**Worshipers**==
[[Humans]], dwarves, and gnolls of [[Nuria Natal]] and the rest of the South. His most devoted followers are in Per-Xor and [[Free Cities of the Desert|Saph-Saph]], where a temple school trains new generations of his priests and paladins.

==**Symbols and Books**==
His symbols are the mace and the staff, signs of rule and divine authority, sometimes combined with sacred texts in ceremonial pieces gilded over a core of iron. His sacred texts are the *Word of the Invincible Sun* and the *Prophecies of Ra-Amon-Ra*.

==**Shrines and Priests**==
His greatest shrine is the Tomb of the Prophet in Per-Xor, though the healing waters of his temple in Siwal are also famous. His priests are many and take new names at their ordination. The greatest now is Ikhnaton of Siwal, a "simple preacher" who wanders Nuria Natal and the [[The Mharoti Empire|Mharoti Empire]] preaching Aten's love to the crowds. Most of his priests have several spouses, a custom not unusual in the [[Southlands]], and the most powerful are the high priest and high priestess of Per-Xor. His military orders are numerous and make up much of the officer corps of Nuria Natal, the largest being the Order of the Phoenix, the Brotherhood of the Temple, the Sisterhood of the Solar Law, and the Order of Radiant Justice.

==**The Prophet Ra-Amon-Ra**==
The preacher Ra-Amon-Ra foretold a dozen calamities and was burned by the dragonkin in a public bonfire during their attack on Per-Xor fifty years ago. His prophecies are well recorded by his followers, among them dwarves, whose halls one of his prophecies spared from a Mharoti attack, and many women. Nine human women and one dwarf woman made up half his twenty known disciples, and women are still most of his followers. His prophecies are kept in the *Eternal Tablet of Ra-Amon-Ra* and his wife's *Gospel of Aiysha*, in an archaic Southern Tongue and often in heretical Draconic versions. Some versions call him divine, others only a speaker of divine truth. His followers call him Aten's Prophet.

His cult is in some ways as strong as Aten's own, and the two are not fully reconciled. His teaching offers forgiveness without effort and salvation to all who proclaim belief and die in Aten's name. The rulers of both Nuria Natal and the Mharoti Empire find the cult distasteful and have banned its open worship, which only drives its followers to further acts of holy murder and mayhem. For all its bloody name, the women of the Prophet feed and clothe the poor and sick, take in orphans, and care for widows and the old, and many of those they help become followers too.

==**The True Aten**==
Aten's greatest danger lies within. Some "secret initiates" worship him as the Father of Fire, the True Aten, an evil cult as jealous as the mainstream faith but far readier to use fire, assassination, and demonic pacts to clear his path. Moderate followers believe the True Aten is a mask of [[Loki]] or [[Baal-Hotep|Baal]]. The faithful reserve their greatest wrath for it: discovered members are always singled out for torture and inquisition.

==**Masks**==
His masks confuse outsiders, since his followers do not want their true god compared with others. Priests of Khors and Lada believe Aten may be a mask of their own gods, and some imagine [[Sif]] or even the [[White Goddess]] are his masks. Radical priests say all other gods are masks of Aten. Some bold theologians claim Aten is a mask of Horus.

==**Other Faiths**==
There are no other true faiths. Priests and paladins of other gods are to be ignored or slaughtered as unbelievers, as circumstances dictate, and the temples of other gods plundered for their vile heresy. Followers of Khors and Lada are in error but must be shown the way, for Khors and Lada are Aten's children, worthy of respect but not of worship. Horus is his great rival, and their faithful are openly hostile.

==**What Aten Demands**==
Aten wants worship and sacrifice. Spend two hours in prayer every day, ideally at sunrise, noon, and sunset, and gaze on the life-giving sun daily, or risk your soul and your afterlife. He wants gold, gems, incense, and chanted prayers through the hours of the day, and candles, mirrors, and magical light to glorify his temples by night. Every worshiper must try to convert infidels before slaughtering them, though the effort is sometimes perfunctory at best.
