*God of Sleep, Awakenings, and Dreams*

**Domains:** Crafting, Nature
**Favored Weapon:** Warhammer

Milikamar is a minor bearfolk god of sleep and waking. Followers of [[Cartokk]] know him as the Dreaming God's friend, the shaggy Bear Brother who pulls dreamers back before they sleep forever. He may be more popular with humans than with [[Bearfolk|bearfolk]], who pray to the Waking Brother only on mornings after a long, peaceful sleep.
