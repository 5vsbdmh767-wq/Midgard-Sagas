*Kobold God of Traps and Mechanisms*

**Domains:** Crafting, Knowledge
**Favored Weapon:** Shortsword

The copper-scaled Tillitakka is the kobold god of traps and machines, and, according to the kobolds, the inventor of the [[Gearforged|gearforged]]. Where other gods deal in prophecies and miracles, he asks for hard work, clever claws, and ever more devious traps and ways to outsmart big, clumsy folk. His priests are trapsmiths with astonishing memories, and even dwarves grudgingly admire the spinning, blade-covered altars in his offering-caves.
