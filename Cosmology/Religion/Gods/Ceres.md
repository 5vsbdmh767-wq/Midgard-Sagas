*Goddess of the Harvest and Civilization, Mother of Grain, Lady of Merchants, Matron of Prosperity, the Traveler's Friend, Wearer of the Golden Sleeves, Patron of Trombei*

**Domains:** Life, Nature, Travel
**Alignment:** Good
**Favored Weapons:** Sickle, scythe

Ceres is the goddess of civilization. Her domain is the worked field, the settled land, the trading house, and the road between them. Everyone feels her presence when crowds gather, and her favor brings prosperity. Her followers are builders and growers who tame animals, raise crops, build roads for trade and travel, and make communities. She favors unity, but her unity does not always mean peace, and her city and country followers are often in conflict, openly or quietly. In the [[Seven Cities]], where religion is close to war, only her civilizing hand keeps the region from even more frequent bloodbaths.

Her harvest rites are the Spring Rite of Sowing and the Fall Rite of Harvest. The weeklong sowing rite ends with the crowning of the Harvest Maiden, whose health is bound to the village's crops, and the harvest rite brings dances, long parades, and a joyful celebration of thanks. In the cities every market day is hers, when farmers come to trade, and market days right after the harvest rites are great holidays.

==**Worshipers**==
Most of the Seven Cities honor Ceres, and her largest following is in [[Trombei]]. [[Humans]] are her most devoted worshipers, though she welcomes all. Most of her followers are advocates of peace and trade, and city guards pay her respect. Several groups worship her as Ceres the Reaper, above all the serpent cults of Trombei's marshes, who keep bloody rites, including sacrificing an unhealthy Harvest Maiden to choose a healthier one, and who sometimes try to slaughter their enemies wholesale in their Rite of Reaping. Even in peacetime the Reapers love excess.

==**Symbols and Books**==
She is shown as a young woman with a sickle and a sheaf of wheat, flanked by a dog and a serpent. Many images of Ceres the Reaper show her with golden snake bracers and scaled servants. The Commune of Trombei seeks a lost holy book of secret knowledge, *The First Seeds*.

==**Shrines and Priests**==
Her temples are built of wood near market districts, and every fifty years they are torn down and rebuilt in a festival, reflecting the seasons of all things. Her altars are of fine polished wood. Her largest temple, the Temple of Endless Sheaves, stands on a small hill inside the walls of Trombei and serves her followers as both market and bank. For twenty years the high harvester has been Jana Waltin of Trombei, known for her shrewdness, who tries, with limited success, to ease the hostility between her country and city followers.

==**Masks**==
In the country she is worshiped as Ceres-of-the-Harvest, and in the cities as Ceres of the Golden Sleeves, honored for her role in commerce. Merchants and bankers call her Ceres-in-Gold. Rare cults worship her dark aspect, Ceres the Reaper or Ceres Vindicator, who brings famine and misfortune to enemies. She is sometimes linked with [[Ninkash]] or with [[Yarila & Porevit]], and some associate [[Sif]] with her.

==**Other Faiths**==
Ceres is friend and rival to [[Rava]]. She and [[Mavros]] are not enemies, but his actions and those of his followers threaten her domains. Her followers view [[Hecate]]'s with some mistrust. Her enemies include [[Baal-Hotep|Baal]] and the [[White Goddess]]. She and [[Charun]] are sometimes said to be lovers or married.

==**What Ceres Demands**==
Ceres values teamwork and cleverness. Help in the harvest rites and honor farmers. She favors those who make a yearly pilgrimage to her temple in Trombei, and expects every worshiper to help raise a temple at least once in their life. Food may never be stolen. Protect farmers, herders, cooks, and growers, and all who provide life and sustenance.
