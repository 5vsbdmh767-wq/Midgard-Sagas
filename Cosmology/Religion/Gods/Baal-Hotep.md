*Lord of Fire and Master of Noble Sacrifice,*
*King of All Dragons, Guardian of the Sultan,*
*the Supreme Fire Dragon, Patron of Dragonkin*

**Domains:** Crafting, Tempest
**Alignment:** Lawful
**Favored Weapon:** Longsword

In Midgard he is simply Baal, the fire god of the [[The Mharoti Empire|Mharoti Empire]]. Wrapped in smoke, flame, and incense, fond of gold, blood, and jewels, he is a god of raucous festivals and of noble sacrifice, up to and including martyrdom for the glory of the dragons' conquests. His jaws are enormous and full of fire, and his scriptures repeat one line over and over: the fires must be fed. Every worshipper gives him gold and jewels, the sultan most of all, since the ruler is under his special protection. He looks like a red-orange dragon with golden teeth, claws, and horns, and black wings streaked with green and gold, and his form is male or female to match whoever sits on the throne.

==**Worshipers**==
The Mharoti, above all the dragonkin, though many humans and some gnolls like his confidence. Every hearth, child, and lamp in the empire is his, and every true dragon and most drakes call him patron. Professional mourners and funeral attendants follow him, as do all the titled nobles of the empire, for he protects authority and the divine right of the draconic ruling class. The Mharoti think it an honor for a son or daughter to join his priesthood. He is sometimes male and sometimes female, matching the ruling sultan or sultana. His current face is male, though many still use the female form out of recent habit. Thieves, merchants, dwarves, necromancers, and scribes are barred from his temples.

==**Symbols and Books**==
A horned dragon head or a leaping flame. His priests wear red, gold, black, and orange. Nothing of his faith is written down. His learned priests, the Baal-Shek, memorize all 444 of his sacred stories, and the last 44 are secrets of the priesthood.

==**Shrines and Priests**==
The Sultan's Fire Shrine, with its jeweled towers and ceiling of hammered gold, is his greatest temple. His priests are also the empire's tax collectors and bankers, since the Counting House of Baal is the sultan's treasury. His chief priest today is Hasibe al-Harkeshi, who goes everywhere with elementalists and tophet guards and is said to sacrifice bars of pure gold to him every week. The Shimmering Temple in Kalpostan, heartland of the empire, is also justly famous. Thirty or forty orders of cavaliers, paladins, wizards, and priestly warriors serve him, from the Golden Lanterns of Harkesh, an order of fire wizards, to the zealous paladins of the Humble Knights of Searing Truth.

==**Other Faiths**==
He finds Seggotan's tides tedious and despises most human gods. [[Bastet]]'s followers hate him. Of the dragon gods he is the most active, finding [[Seggotan]]'s slow tides tedious. He despises most human gods, above all the ancient, weak southern gods and the vile gods of the [[The Crossroads|Crossroads]], schemers and tricksters such as [[Volund]] and [[Rava]]. His greatest rivalry is with [[Khespotan]] over the treatment of the dead: Baal favors cremation and Khespotan burial, and their priests fight street battles over the right to perform the funerals of great officials. Baal's priests are quick to point out that all drakes and dragons insist on cremation. [[Horus]] holds him a demon-dragon and would-be conqueror of [[Nuria Natal]], and [[Nethus]] calls him a foul fire god.

==**Masks**==
Some believe Baal is a mask of [[Aten]], [[Chernobog]], [[Khors]], [[Loki]], or even Volund, but within the Dragon Empire that is blasphemy. In practice the fire god has many children, as the Mharoti tell it, and dozens of local fire saints, fire demigods, and even flame dragons are called Sons and Daughters of Baal.

==**What Baal Demands**==
Sacrifice gold, blood, and treasure to the dragon masters. Pray before a fire every day, and burn fat, meat, paper, and incense in his name. Avoid water, and never swim or sail if you can walk or fly. Rage is righteous and proper worship, but protect the innocent, unless their hour of sacrifice has come. Never write down the lessons of Baal, but keep them pure in your heart.

==**Beyond Midgard**==
In the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]] he is Baal-Hotep, who rides the clouds and brings rain and dew to the fields as well as fire. Outside the dragon worlds he is more often shown as a bronze-skinned minotaur with a mouth full of flame, and his Fire Shrines and Torch Shrines are covered in gold leaf. [[Dragonborn]] and kobolds worship him openly, and many humans in secret.
