*Master of War, God of Blood and Honor,*
*Lord of Strife and Rebirth, Patron of*
*Valera and the Seven Cities, the Thunderbolt*

**Domains:** Life, Tempest, War
**Alignment:** Neutral
**Favored Weapons:** Longsword, spear

Mavros is the war god of the south and the patron of most of the [[Seven Cities]], and it is his will that they stay at war with each other as a holy mission. His church is almost a nation of its own. Some mercenaries, adventurers, and priests swear loyalty to his priest-commander and the House of Swords before any city. Soldiers, officers, and most of the citizens of [[Valera]] worship him, and women and children see him as a protector as well as a war god. Unusually for a god of battle, his mysteries also deal with healing, resurrection, and the cleansing of the soul.

In the south his character is bloody and hard. In the North, where he is better known as [[Perun]] or Thor, people remember him more as the Thunderer who guards the world against the Serpent.

==**Symbols and Books**==
His prayer book is the *Call to Glory*. The *Annals of Mavros*, a difficult work of history and mysticism, exists in only a few copies, can't be copied by any means, and has a habit of being stolen or carried off by the god's heralds.

==**Shrines and Priests**==
The greatest temple is the Golden Pantheon in Valera, where the High Pontifex Miter Konya Mellas presides over the faith and its relics. The militant Order of Mavros, the Red Knights, keeps fortified temples across the Septime lands, trains soldiers in their courtyards, and blesses the troops of every nation. Every temple-fortress is a sanctuary, even for criminals, and a criminal who takes holy orders is forgiven, as long as they lead a first assault on a fortified wall. The order's inquisitors, the Hounds of Mavros, collect tithes and punish the wicked. The great pilgrimage runs from the House of Swords south of Valera more than nine hundred miles to the Seat of Mavros in the goblin wastes.

==**The Order of Mavros**==
The militant Order of Mavros makes up much of the church's clergy, and its priest-commander, the blessed Armengol de Aspa, is a military and spiritual power throughout the Septime lands whom rulers compete to please. It includes clerics, fighters, cavaliers, oracles, paladins, and inquisitors, but no druids or rangers. Those who swear their weapons to Mavros win wide respect and follow a clear ladder:

| Rank | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Squires and priest-pages | Little more than servants to the priesthood. |
| Sword-brothers and sword-sisters | Sworn veteran fighters or mercenaries. |
| Temple knights | Sworn to defend the church and wage unceasing war on the enemies of the Septime cities and the faith. They answer to the priests and paladins. |
| Priests and paladins | The ordained clergy. |
| Holy bladesaints | The high priests, oracles, and powerful paladins who rule the church, the priest-commander among them. |

Apart from all these stand the hard-bitten Order of Blood, the Hounds of Mavros, the god's inquisitors, who answer to the priests and bladesaints but work on their own, collecting tithes and punishing the wicked. Their leader, Bladesaint Ulderico Vensali, is a gentle, well-bred man who is utterly ruthless in defense of the order, the pontifex, the relics of the saints, and the faithful, and who keeps the House of Blood Mercy running smoothly.

Those who join the clergy to escape their crimes must show their contrition by leading at least one first banner in an attack on a strong position such as a castle wall. Many find their courage fails them, and they are dealt with especially harshly within the church, so taking holy orders is not seen as an easy way out.

==**The House of Swords**==
The chief chapterhouse and temple of Mavros in the south is the House of Swords, in the central hills south of [[Valera]], commanding a valuable pass. It is the seat of the priest-commander, the place where most of the priesthood is ordained, and home to a rare nunnery for female acolytes. More than a thousand people live there, half of them clergy and half servants and petitioners. Within sight of it stands the House of Blood Mercy, home of the Order of Blood. Its prisoners never escape, and the inquisitors execute cowards, traitors, blasphemers, pacifists, and other criminals against the faith.

==**The Pilgrimage to the Seat**==
The road from the House of Swords to the [[Western Wastes#The Seat of Mavros|Seat of Mavros]] runs more than nine hundred miles over hills and fields, out of civilized lands, and through the goblin wastes, two months on foot each way with several river crossings and the risk of goblins and bandits. Even hardened warriors sometimes die on the way, a fate said to bring special virtue. Riding is allowed but rare, since most think it less worshipful. A member of the Order leads each party of twelve to a hundred armed pilgrims. At the Seat all make offerings, some fight a sacred bout with a fellow pilgrim, usually to first blood, and then they turn for home.

==**Masks**==
Perun in the east and Thor in the North. Some say he is brother to Horus and Takhar in the [[Southlands]].

==**Other Faiths**==
He was once married to [[Hecate]], but now he is devoted to [[Marena]] the Red. [[Lada]], whose priests preach peace, is his enemy, and he makes war on the [[White Goddess]] wherever her followers appear.

==**What Mavros Demands**==
Victory. Seek battle, keep your skills sharp, and never refuse a fight or a duel. Never leave a comrade's body on the field. Cowardice is a disgrace, and dying of old age is a sad end. Attend his mysteries before any great battle or long journey, and make the pilgrimage to his Seat once in your life.

==**Beyond Midgard**==
On other worlds of the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]] he is Herak-Mavros, god of war and thunder, husband to the White Goddess, whose priests raise fallen heroes in cavern-temples with the help of angelic spear-maidens.
