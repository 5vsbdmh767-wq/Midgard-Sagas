*God of Copper, Lord of Light, Master of the Arcane,*
*Patron of [[Humans]] and Alchemists*

**Favored Weapon:** Mace

Kupkoresh is a godling of the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]], shown as a copper-skinned man with black hair and a strong, narrow beard in the Nurian style. He is widely thought to have come from the bright lands when [[Corremel]] fell into shadow. He has a strong following among humans, who believe his smoky green incense burns away corruption and brings friendship, alliance, and insight.

## Worshipers

His followers are mostly a small community of humans in and around Corremel, the City of Lanterns, and along the Lethe and the [[The Ever River|Styx]]. Fey worshipers can be found in the shadow goblin city of [[Fandeval]] and in the [[Court of Golden Oaks]], and now and then gnomes or halflings follow him as the Candle God or the Lord of Light.

## Symbols and Books

His symbol is an orange or reddish sun, sometimes paired with a temple sword or dagger. Orange candles burn at his shrines, and his services are made bright with many torches, lanterns, mirrors, and gilding. His only holy text is the *Scroll of Amber*, with the prayers and offerings for moonrise and moonset, charms for lighting candles, instructions for using wax and light to cleanse a corrupted spirit, and a short treatise on the proper use of judges and punishments.

## Shrines and Priests

The only shrine known to keep a permanent priesthood is the Evening Star Temple in the hills west of Corremel. It tends an eternal reddish flame and is part of a copper mine and smelting works that sends a steady flow of copper ingots to the umbral humans of the City of Lanterns. The shrines in Fandeval and elsewhere draw occasional priests but are too small to support weekly services and offerings. Throughout the Shadow Realm, servants of [[Alquam]] attack his shrines, and his priests are often hurt or killed.

## Masks

[[Lada]], [[Bengta]], and Kupkoresh are often seen as different faces of one godhead, and a few even claim Kupkoresh is Lada's or Bengta's divine father. Those who know the gods of [[Nuria Natal|Nuria]] say he is a son of Aten. A few say he was a priest of Aten or Lada who grew so holy he became a saint and then a demigod.

## Other Faiths

He has a long rivalry with the followers of [[Hecate]], who call his faith a cult that spreads false hope and bravado instead of a realistic view of danger. The followers of [[Charun]] and of Kupkoresh often help each other, since both seek protective guardianship and a good death. Kupkoresh calls Alquam the vilest of demons, and his followers always root out and execute followers of the Lord of Night. They are suspicious of the poetry and frivolity of [[Gytellisor]].

## What Kupkoresh Demands

Burn incense, follow the light, and always tell the truth. Help every stranger in need, and never let a debt lie forgotten. Wear your copper proudly, and in times of despair remember that light burns within you. Hold your head high if doom comes, for an honest death is no shame, and fire consumes darkness in the end. Be as honest with yourself, and hold yourself to account as strictly, as you do others.
