*Demon Lord of Night, God of Stealth, Patron of the Darklings,*
*Master of Nightmares, the Silent God, Slayer of the Sun,*
*Keeper of the Black Gate, Prince of Nocturnes,*
*King of the Whispering Wastes*

**Favored Weapons:** Mace, shortsword

Alquam is the demon lord of darkness and the night, and shadows are his natural home. He is devoted to stealth and to the silent killing loved by assassins, raptors, and nocturnal animals. He is called the Silent God, yet when his followers strike they shout his name, and many of his most devoted are loud, brash people unafraid to show their power. Aggression and bullying are common among the warriors, alchemists, and bandits who serve the Master of Nightmares. He holds court in Qes-al-Marit, the Hell of Darkness and Bone Dunes, one of the Eleven Hells.

## Worshipers

Most of his followers in the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]] are umbral humans seeking secret strength, darkvision, or other gifts, though darklings, gnomes, and shadow goblins number among them too, as do night watchmen, hunters, bandits, and everyone whose daily work involves death and burial. Darakhul are often fond of him, though never in place of [[Vardesain]] or [[Anu-Akma]]. Some [[Gnolls|gnolls]] of the [[Southlands]] revere him as their patron.

## Symbols and Books

His symbols include a black owl, usually with blue eyes and sometimes amber ones like a real owl, his sign of watchfulness and keen sight. Small statues of the owl are kept as watchers in forest shrines, and his priests often keep owls as sacred animals. A five-rayed dark sun is also his sign, and a few sects use dried raptor claws as holy symbols. His faith has only one holy text, the *Book of the Black Gate*, with his hymns, chants, sacrifices, and laws, and a series of hunting parables on following spoor, choosing targets, and dressing and butchering game. Its chapters on the black gate itself are mystical, describing a transforming portal that strengthens those who pass through it. Certain herbs, rituals, and unguents are said to prepare a devotee to enter, and those who undergo the rite are sure it makes them crueler, swifter, and keener-eyed.

## Shrines and Priests

Most of his shrines are caverns carved with ornate runes, wards, blessings, and sigils. Most have a small pool for night blessings and ritual cleansing, and the larger ones keep a rookery or roost for bats, owls, and other night creatures.

## Masks

Alquam is often believed to be a mask of [[Hecate]] or [[Vardesain]], though his own followers more often call [[The Hunter|the Hunter]] one of his aspects, and some say [[Charun]] is his mask on the water and in the afterlife. Mostly, though, he is counted a quiet, silent god, or even the shadow of greater gods.

## Other Faiths

Alquam despises [[Lada]], [[Kupkoresh]], [[Bengta]], [[Umbeserno]], and all the gods of the sunlit lands, and they try to wipe out his followers wherever they find them. Umbeserno takes particular offense at the foul dreams Alquam sends to disturb the bearfolk's long sleep. He is on good terms with the Hunter and with Hecate, who is sometimes called "Alquam's grandmother" and sometimes a friend and fellow creature of the dark. He and Vardesain respect each other as allies of convenience after the same ends, and [[Mot]], little known in the Shadow Realm, is said to have long favored him as a good, silent slayer who swells the ranks of the undead. His greatest friend is Charun the Boatman. The two are old companions who cross from shadow into darkness, both at home on the [[The Ever River|Styx]] at midnight, and their faiths trust each other: "as close as cards with Charun," his followers say, of embracing a friend or stalking an enemy.

## What Alquam Demands

Never stand in the sun when shade beckons. Pray at midnight, in deep darkness, and praise the darkness for its soft concealment. Learn to listen and to see when all others are deaf and blind. Your senses are keen at night, and your heart knows where your enemies hide. Destroy light and cherish silence.

## In the Shadow Realm

For all his enemies, Alquam is counted a powerful god in the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]], with command over the seasons when no moon shines and no star twinkles, and his followers say his hand shelters all the creatures of the dark from the cruel and the rapacious. He is the patron of [[Oshragora]] and among the great gods of [[Nightbrook]] and several fey courts, and his servants attack the shrines of Kupkoresh wherever they find them. His assassins and spies, the Wings of Alquam, are known in the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]] for hunting down Void cultists.
