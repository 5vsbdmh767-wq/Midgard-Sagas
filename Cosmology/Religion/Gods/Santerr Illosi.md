*Our Lady of Blood, Goddess of Blood and Strength, Lady of Shadows,*
*Santerr of the Shroud, the First Necromancer, Patron of Ghouls*

**Favored Weapon:** Scythe

Santerr Illosi is a darakhul goddess of blood and mystery whose three-layered cult takes in most of the darakhul and lesser ghouls of the [[Twilight Empire]]. *Santerr* is an Umbral title, something like "saint," given to holy figures first tied to a particular place; Illosi is a common name among the ghouls, so the title is always used. She is usually shown as a necromancer in a green robe, holding a set of bones or a scythe, with a ferocious grin the darakhul call a "feeding smile," and now and then with a sandaled foot resting on a human or shadow fey skull.

The outer ranks of her cult are the Reapers, the middle ranks the Sowers, and the highest the Harvesters. Each rank is given more knowledge, and the highest ranks are believed to be all elder darakhul, forming a network of necromancers and blood mages within the Twilight Empire.

## Worshipers

Most darakhul worship her, though the common rabble and beggar ghouls do so more openly than officers and nobles. Some umbral humans follow her too, rarely, and mostly as a goddess of healing and of speaking with the dead. A few rogues and dealmakers among the shadow goblins also pay her homage, usually in secret.

## Symbols and Books

Her symbols include a stylized sickle and eye, and a small pile of finger bones is said to serve as one of her makeshift altars. Her holy books include the *Harvest of Souls* for the Reapers, which covers the proper rites of burial and cremation and simple necromancy such as speaking with the dead and raising skeletons and zombies. The *Rites of Santerr Illosi* are for the Sowers and are believed to cover blood magic and rituals that fortify a worshiper with necromancy. The *Rites of the Blood Queen* is said to be shared only among the Harvesters, and no outsider knows what it holds.

## Shrines and Priests

Small shrines to her usually stand inside the temples of [[Vardesain]] in the Twilight Empire and in Midgard's [[The Darakhul Imperium|Ghoul Empire]], their bone piles and green murals of reaping distinct from Vardesain's altars. Her most famous priests are Her Luminance Ingalla Vert in [[Soriglass]] and Her Witchlight Resplendence Tiberessa Vert in the Twilight Empire.

## Masks

Some darakhul believe she is simply a mask of [[Marena]], the Blood Goddess, made fitting for ghouls, while others believe she is the feminine side of Vardesain. A few believe she is the true power behind Marena, or even, for the truly devout, the force behind [[Hecate]].

## Other Faiths

Her followers are on excellent terms with Vardesain's and on good terms with those of [[Alquam]] and [[Charun]]. Hecate counts her a minor rival, and [[Kupkoresh]], [[Bengta]], and [[Anu-Akma]] are unfriendly if not hostile. Most gods of Midgard consider her faith a minor but dangerous branch of Vardesain's church and stamp it out just as eagerly.

## What Santerr Illosi Demands

Take blood and bone from the weak to fuel your rise. The strong deserve to rule, and you must grow strong. Draw power from the living and from the land, and never forget that your people are the true rulers of the shadows.
