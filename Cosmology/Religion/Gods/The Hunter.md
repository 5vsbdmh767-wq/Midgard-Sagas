*Master of the Hunt, the Horned Huntsman, the Blood God,*
*the Horseman, the Many-Skinned Walker, the God-Wolf,*
*Patron of the Bandit [[Centaurs]], Patron of Per-Kush*

**Favored Weapons:** Bow (any), boar spear

The Hunter goes by many names, Cherne, Kirme, Huern, Ebern, Herion, and every tribe calls him its own. He walks in countless forms: dire wolf, human huntsman, fell hound, centaur archer, pale king, fey assassin, blood demon, goblin trapper. He embodies the ancient and deadly animals of the wild fringes and those who chase them down and conquer them. The Blood God stirs the dark instincts that drive beasts to fury and the kill, and he revels in the chase, in power, violence, and blood. He wears a crown of horns or claws, a rich fur cloak, and deerskin leggings, and carries a black-tipped spear, and his eyes flash green and gold, especially in the thick of a hard chase.

He is not the same being as the Lord of the Hunt, the fey lord who leads the Wild Hunt.

## Worshipers

The Hunter's roots are ancient, and his priests lead or sway many of the most violent centaur clans, bandit gangs, and raiders. To the fey, the hunt is an undeniable, timeless rite: when the Hunter rides out, everyone who hears his call must answer it, and even fey gods have been caught up in it. He has a large following among human and catfolk hunters, dwarven reavers, elves, lycanthropes, vampires, ogres, shadow fey, gnolls, worgs, and winter wolves, and among many hobgoblin, trollkin, orc, and goblin tribes. The minotaurs see him as the champion of murderous urges and the delight of tracking and killing prey, and his worship among them may be wider than anyone likes to admit. His church has even grown among the aristocrats of civilized nations, who form Blood Lodges to follow his teachings for sport, and for the assassination of personal enemies.

## Symbols and Books

Every predatory beast, monstrous or mundane, can stand for the many-skinned Hunter, but his chief symbol is a bloodied carcass. Others include stylized spearheads and arrowheads, especially where the Horned One's nature must stay hidden, and observant prey can read such signs as omens or warnings. His faith spreads by word of mouth, in stories told around hunters' campfires. Written texts are rare, though sacred lore hides in manuals such as *The Slaying and Field Dressing of Northern Beasts*. Books are feeble guides to the Horned God anyway; a believer must feel real bloodlust before understanding his call.

## Shrines and Priests

Every bloodied hunting ground is his shrine. Full temples are rare, but formal shrines are consecrated in remote woods and hidden valleys, piled with the bones and skins of deer or boar and painted red with their gore. Less grisly altars belong to the Blood Lodges, exclusive hunting fraternities in gentler lands such as the [[Grand Duchy of Dornig]] and [[Krakovar]], where his shrines now stand in the open air instead of in dark groves. Elite huntsmen called herls lead the lodges, and their chief is Red Marcken, who survived a great mother bear and still bears her bloody scars.

## Masks

His priests say he wears no masks, though others claim [[Vardesain]], [[Anu-Akma]], [[Mavros]], the [[Black Goat|Goat of the Woods]], or the Red Goddess [[Marena]] is one of his aspects, and some of his names are said to be taken from gods who became his prey. Among the giants he is called Sugaar. Mortals who ride too long with the Wild Hunt, such as St. Hubertus, gain a measure of immortality and divine power, and some of them preach a tamer hunter's creed. The Horned One tolerates them because they still promote the hunt. Bastet is linked with him in the [[The Crossroads|Crossroads]].

## Other Faiths

To the Hunter there are only two kinds of gods. The worthy ones are fellow hunters who ride with him on his great chases, and their followers are their yeomen and attendants. All other gods are meat, and so are their clergy and their followers, all the sweeter for their mortal tenderness. Mighty [[Wotan]] and [[Perun]] once rode at the head of the pack beside him, in ages past.

## What the Hunter Demands

Hunt or be hunted. Join the Master's Hunt when it rides by, and hunt boar, deer, elk, pheasant, rabbit, whatever crosses your path. Learn to track, learn to kill without hesitation, and eat what you kill. Know that your own life may someday end in violence, and live it with gusto before that day comes.

## In the Shadow Realm

In the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]] the Hunter is one of the five great dark gods, worshiped openly by many [[Shadow Fey|shadow fey]] and some darakhul, who both love the traditions of the chase. Hunting fraternities keep altars to him in [[Fandeval]], the [[Court of Night and Magic]], and the [[Court of Winter's Love]], and he is among the great gods of [[Corremel]], the [[Tower of Horn and Gold]], and [[Oshragora]], where his high priest is the lich Cilestros Margevan. The followers of [[Alquam]] often call the Hunter one of their god's aspects, and [[Bastet|Ailuros]], [[Loki|Laughing Loki]], and Vardesain all find common cause with him. [[Umbeserno]] is his enemy, for the needless slaughter he stands for.
