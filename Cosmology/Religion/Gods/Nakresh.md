*The Many-Handed, Patron of Thieves and Tomb*
*Robbers, Archmagus Among Wizards, King of Revelry*
*and Japes, Protector of the Poor, Bane of the Rich*

**Domains:** Knowledge, Trickery
**Favored Weapons:** Shortsword, wand

Nakresh the Many-Handed works through tricks, magic, and corruption. He is enormously greedy, steals anything that's supposed to be perfectly safe just to prove he can, and is always underestimated. He is shown as a mandrill, or half man and half mandrill, with little black horns, blazing eyes, and six or eight arms holding a wand, a dagger, a bag of coins, a jewel, a scroll, and other loot. One hand is always empty, for the wealth he hasn't grabbed yet.

==**Worshipers**==
Thieves, wizards, diabolists, tomb robbers, and the desperately poor, who call him Uncle Whispers, the Duke of Air and Steel, or the Peach Collector. His priests share his rough sense of humor.

==**Symbols and Books**==
An open hand with a star in the palm. His one holy book, the *True Gospel of Prosperity and Wealth*, is a collection of cons and swindles.

==**Shrines and Priests**==
His temples are underground or behind back rooms, and his cult is as often led by thieves and cutthroats as by priests. He is mostly known on the darker worlds of the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]].
