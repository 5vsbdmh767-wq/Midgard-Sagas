*God of Portals, Inventor of Keys*

**Domains:** Crafting, Knowledge
**Favored Weapon:** Staff

Hune is an ancient human god who invented portals and bottled cities. He taught the [[Keepers of the Keys]] how to forge the first keys and how to open and close the ways of the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]]. He is shown in a gray cloak with a staff, and travelers often believe they've met him at some roadside shrine deep in the maze or in the ruins of a desert world. He was once the husband of [[Srenda Mara]], before she turned to darkness. Travelers, builders, and the lost pray to him.
