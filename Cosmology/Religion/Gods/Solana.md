*Lady of Light, Goddess of the Sun, Marshal of*
*Angels, Matron of Steel and Silver, Queen of Pure*
*Radiance, Forge Mother*

**Domains:** Crafting, Life, Light
**Favored Weapons:** Mace, warhammer

Solana is the sun goddess of the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]] and the champion of everyone who fights the Void. She appears as an elf wreathed in gold light and crowned with rays or stars, and she gives her faithful healing, strength, and protection. Many peoples worship her as a goddess of creation, and Void cultists hate her name. [[Dwarves]] know her as the Forge Mother, a golden-skinned dwarf smith with a hammer and a crown of light.

==**Worshipers**==
Healers, smiths, and soldiers, the paladins of the [[Minor Wayfaring Groups|Radiant Order]], and the marshals of the [[Concord of Stars]]. [[Lada]] is called her younger sister, and some followers of [[Sabateus]] think she is his daytime face.

==**Symbols and Books**==
Suns, crowns of seven rays or of stars, and a glowing orange anvil, the *sun forge*, which her faithful believe is hidden somewhere in the Labyrinth. Her books are the *Rules of Radiance* for priests and paladins and the *Word of Light* for everyone else.

==**Shrines and Priests**==
The Concord of Stars worships her at the First Forge Temple in the [[The Smithy|Smithy]], and her shrines are common wherever elves live, especially in the Summerlands. Her priests are forge priests, healers, or zealous warriors against the Void.
