*The Sword Maiden, Goddess of Family and*
*Marriage, Mistress of Valkyries, Wife to [[Thor]],*
*Patron of Women Warriors and Archers*

**Domains:** Nature, War
**Alignment:** Good
**Favored Weapon:** Bow

Sif is Thor's golden-haired wife: she keeps the gold, shoots the bow, brews the ale, and is the equal of any man and better than most. She leads the valkyries and the shield maidens, and she is a good mother and a loyal friend to her faithful.

==**Worshipers**==
Women, married and unmarried, make up most of her faithful, along with archers, farmers, and gold-hungry dwarves. Every woman warrior gives her due. In [[Perunalia]] she is honored as the duchess's sister.

==**Symbols and Books**==
A brace of arrows. Her priests keep no books.

==**Shrines and Priests**==
Her greatest shrine is the wooden church of Jarlshallen, on a mountaintop in [[Vidim]], where her shield maidens gather every spring and fall for blessings before the raiding season and the coming of the giants. Her priestesses include prophets and oracles. Saint Adelind, a shield maiden of hers who died defending [[Krakovar|Krakova]] against the undead of [[Blood Kingdom|Morgau]], answers the prayers of women who swear vengeance on the darakhul and the vampires. Her sign is the snowdrop.


==**In the North**==
In the [[Northlands]] she is also called Golden-Haired and the Patron of [[Huldramose]], and her favored weapons there are the bow and longsword. See [[Gods of the North]].

Sif is the goddess of battle, the battle-maiden of the frozen worlds, and the enemy of giants and trolls, who leads the Valkyries on [[Wotan]]'s behalf to collect the honored dead. She calls Freyja her "ale-sister" and Freyr her "hunting brother." Where her husband [[Thor]]'s followers value strength, hers value skill, and above all she despises cowardice and treachery. She appears as a sharp-featured woman, beautiful and strong, in silver clothes or armor, with long braided hair of real gold.

Her faithful, called *sifjar*, include archers, women warriors, and farmers, and anyone who fights for honor or to protect others can earn her favor. They gather at her temples for three great musters each year: Remembrance in spring, Fortitude in autumn, and Repentance in winter. She teaches that might alone is not enough; skill and honor matter. On rare occasions she joins a battle herself and leaves a magical gift for truly exceptional warriors. Two of her best-known followers are Rhiam Skyddaboggon, a human shield-maiden raising a warband in Huldramose, and the dwarven dragon-hunting scout Olympia Ferrymantle of [[Trollheim]].

She is linked with the protective *Gebo* rune. Instead of holy books, her faithful memorize the tales of worthy warriors, such as *Regina of the Bow*, the paragon of archers. Many braid yellow ribbons into their hair or dye a lock gold, and every morning they practice in non-lethal duels. Offerings to her include arrows and broken weapons burned in sacred fires, especially at the musters.

Her temples are fortified halls that double as training grounds, ringing with the clash of steel. Her clergy must prove their prowess by dueling the head of the local order at least three times a year, and visitors are encouraged to train too. Her priests are skilled warriors chosen for quick wit, physical aptitude, and strength of personality. Many believe the Valkyries watch over them, not to protect them but to make sure they are worthy. The largest temple to her in the North is the Grand Temple of the [[Shield-Maidens of Sif|Shield-Maidens]] in Noatun.
==**Masks**==
Some call her the northern face of [[Lada]], or link her to Ceres or [[Ninkash]]. The dwarves call her Grajava the Shield Maiden, and the giants Skadi.

==**Other Faiths**==
She is close to Thor, Lada, and [[Volund|Svarog]], and the sworn enemy of [[Boreas (North Wind)|Boreas]], Chernobog, and [[Marena]].

==**What Sif Demands**==
Be fierce and faithful and fight for what you believe in. Learn the bow, axe, and spear, or raise strong and honest children, or both, and do it with your whole heart. Stand strong in the summer and learn to bend in the winter storm. Strike down evil, and practice your own excellence without ever insulting another's skill.

==**Beyond Midgard**==
In the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]] she calls Ninkash her ale-sister and [[Knodor]] her hunting brother.
