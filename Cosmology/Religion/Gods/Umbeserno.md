*God of Hibernation and Twilight, Lord of Winter Dreams,*
*Bringer of Spring, Lord of Sleep, Holy Father of the Snows,*
*Patron of the [[Bearfolk]]*

**Favored Weapons:** Axe, spear

Umbeserno is a drowsy, nocturnal god of the bearfolk, honored for his part in hibernation and prophetic dreams. Where [[Bengta]] watches over the bearfolk by day and urges them to good works, Umbeserno watches over them by night and through the cold months when they lie dreaming and snoring. The bearfolk think of dreaming as a time of plans and hopes, an optimistic season for gathering strength. He is always shown as a large black bearfolk with a crescent mark on his forehead, usually in a shaman's robes, often holding an enormous salmon or a rainbow-colored dreamstone. Another popular image is Umbeserno Dreamlord, asleep among signs of spring and life: flowers, fish, lightning bolts, comets, and rabbits, his signs and portents.

## Worshipers

He is worshiped mainly by bearfolk, though a few humans and shadow goblins call on him for help with bad dreams, the mysteries of spring and rebirth, and even marriage and healing. Those troubled by nightmares ask him to come and take their dreams away, and many matchmakers seek his guidance before settling a match between two bearfolk (or two humans; goblins make their own, more complicated arrangements).

## Symbols and Books

His symbols are the rainbow dreamstone, the leaping salmon, and antlers ringing a bird's nest (no two priests tell quite the same story about that last one, but it seems to be a bearfolk sign of spring). He has many holy books, most called the *Book of Dreams*, but there is no standard collection: some dreams appear in every *Book of Dreams* (falling, losing one's fur, the scent of honey, summer flies), while others appear only in some.

## Shrines and Priests

His largest shrine is the Snoring Temple in Solvheim in the [[Moonlit Glades]], where the caverns run deep into the hills; many wealthier bearfolk spend their winter sleep there or visit with questions of fortune and the like. His smaller temples are usually well-built round lodges with walls of clay and whole logs and grass roofs with a single smoke hole. His priests are often mocked as drowsy and slow, forever gazing at stars or clouds, but most are generous, extremely patient, hardworking members of their communities, always ready to help the faithful. His best-known priest is Snuffling Tilda, Keeper of the Hearth, in [[Merrymead]].

## Masks

He is often assumed to be a mask of [[Gytellisor]], and sometimes compared to [[Bastet|Ailuros]] when his dreaming side is strongest. Others believe he is a mask of [[Wotan]], a rambling, gentler font of wisdom and persistence.

## Other Faiths

The followers of Bengta get along very well with his, as do those of Ailuros and even [[Charun]]. Umbeserno opposes the needless slaughter of [[The Hunter|the Hunter]] and the fears and terrors of [[Alquam]], who disturbs the bearfolk's long sleep with foul dreams. Surprisingly, he and [[Loki|Laughing Loki]] are often seen as friends.

## What Umbeserno Demands

Dream big and sleep well. Make your plans carefully, and see them through relentlessly. Watch the world with a sharp eye and a keen nose. Patience is often rewarded with wisdom. Ponder your choices, and always keep your word.
