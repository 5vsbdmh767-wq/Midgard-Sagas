*God of Eternal Death, Lord of the Undead,*
*the Death of Fire, Black Emperor of the Silent*
*Land of Mirey, Prince of Necromancers*

**Domains:** Death, War
**Alignment:** Evil
**Favored Weapon:** Greataxe

Mot is the god of endings: the field that never grows, the noble line that dies out, the nation wiped off the map. Slaughter on a grand scale, necromancy, and obliteration are all his. He is shown as an almost skeletal king with jeweled eyes and a crown, his greataxe resting at his side and one arm raised in command. His green robes are marked with fire and wards, and whatever they cover is said to vanish into the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]]. He rules the afterlife from a black throne in the mists beside the [[The Ever River|River Styx]], and keeps the plane of Mirey as a place of filth and torment for the unworthy. "Rich as Mot's vault" means unimaginably wealthy, since his treasury holds the riches of all his dead.

==**Worshipers**==
All intelligent undead pray to him, ghouls, darakhul, vampires, ghosts, and mummies alike, especially when priests of light come for them. In the [[Southlands]], humans worship him openly only in the jungles of Kush, but he also has forest tribesmen, some of the fire legions of Narumbeki, and certain cold-blooded nobles among his faithful.

==**Symbols and Books**==
He has three scriptures: *The Hand of Death*, a grim guide to acting rightly in the face of nothingness; *Revelations of Black Fire*, a book of prophecy; and the *Fuligin Book of the Void*, so evil that anyone who reads it becomes a darakhul, a vampire, or a ghost.

==**Shrines and Priests**==
He has many small shrines for a dark god, most of them in the Southlands and meant for a handful of undead or one necromancer and their followers. His most famous priest, the necromancer Jabir Al-Seki, became a lich and wandered off between the worlds.

==**Beyond Midgard**==
Mot's power is greatest on Mirey and on the dead world of [[Evermaw]], and he is worshipped on many shadowed worlds of the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]].
