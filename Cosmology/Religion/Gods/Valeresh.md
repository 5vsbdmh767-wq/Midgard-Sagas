*Supreme Archer, Lord of the Battlefield, Master of Sword and Arrow, Swift Destroyer and God of Battle, Patron of the [[Elves]]*

**Domains:** Death, Justice, Speed, Tempest, War
**Alignment:** Neutral Good
**Favored Weapons:** Longsword, longbow

Swift and sure in battle and devoted to justice in peace, Valeresh is the elven king of the gods, his wife long dead and mourned. That wound explains his stern face and his ruthless destruction of evil, darkness, madness, and foul magic. When the elves ruled a great realm his following was vast, but his priesthood led the Great Retreat, and almost nothing remains of his liturgy, temples, and rites except a handful of extremely powerful enchanted weapons.

==**Worshipers**==
Long ago, elven soldiers, officers, nobles, and merchants worshiped Valeresh as the embodiment of their empire and the keeper of its peace. Now only a handful of the elfmarked in the [[Grand Duchy of Dornig|Grand Duchy]] and a few humans in the [[Seven Cities]] remember him and keep his feast days. The god essentially left Midgard with the Great Retreat.

==**Symbols and Books**==
He had two sacred books. The *Book of the Silent Sword* was a treatise on a stoic, honorable life. The *Arrow of Truth* taught in parables how to rule the elven empire of [[Valera]], with fair attention to raising archer levies, keeping a battlefield supplied with arrows, taxes, granaries, and justice. Few copies of either survive, for their gem-studded covers and rich illumination made them among the first treasures plundered when the empire fell. His symbol is a fine elven bow tipped with red and gold.

==**Shrines and Priests**==
His greatest temple is the Cathedral of Bright Honor on a hill just outside Valera, the city named for his reign in the mortal world. It is stark and empty, and only the emperor and his closest advisers gather there to seek guidance from the ancient power. Now and then a golden light appears on its altar and speaks in Elvish to those present.

All the weapons of his priesthood were enchanted, but two stand above the rest: Illethandril, the Silent Sword of Valeresh, and Korren-Gadresh, the True Bow or Farseeing Bow, said to shoot over hills and through walls. They lie as relics in the echoing temple of Valeresh in Hirschberg, and it is said they will be lifted again only when the elves return to reclaim their empire, or when Valeresh comes to slay some great evil. No one dares touch them, for they destroy any unworthy creature who lifts them without being bound to the service of the elves.

==**Masks**==
Valeresh is widely thought to be the elven mask of [[Thor]], [[Perun]], and [[Mavros]]. A few claim he is a male, elven form of [[Sif]]. The elves of the [[Arbonesse]] sometimes call [[Volund]] his armorer.

==**Other Faiths**==
He was always on good terms with [[Holda]] and [[Yarila & Porevit|Yarila]], though the two goddesses were each jealous of their time with the Archer God. [[Baccholon]] was his fool, and the only god who could tell him when he was badly wrong.

==**What Valeresh Demands**==
Fight evil and defend the elves. Protect the innocent, and strike down a foe swiftly and without cruelty.
