*Lord of Fire, Master of Fire and Anvil,*
*God of Horses, Smiths, and Marriage,*
*Patron of the Cantons, Patron of the [[Kariv]],*
*Master Smith of the Gods, the Wanderer,*
*the Rider*

**Domains:** Forge, Travel, Mountain
**Alignment:** Neutral
**Favored Weapons:** Hammer (humans) and battleaxe (dwarves)

Called Svarog among humans and Volund among dwarves, the god of smiths and fire is a friend and rival to the Gear Goddess [[Rava]]. Bearded and often a traveler, the work of the smith-god is part of many things, from tools to weapons, from nails to hearths. Every dwarven anvil is sacred to him and bears his mark.

Among humans, Svarog is also the god of marriage and horsemen, though [[Dwarves|dwarves]] consider this a corruption of the true faith.

Marriage is sacred to his faith, since it forges a bond between two people, and so are horses. The [[Kariv]] say Volund stole the first steeds from [[Boreas (North Wind)|Boreas]] and showed humankind how to tame them. Dwarves scoff, but the [[Khazzaki]] of the [[Rothenian Plain]] praise this side of him above all, revering him as the Rider, master of the open steppe and guarantor of their freedom. In his youth he was a great traveler, some even dare say a mortal, and in his wanderings taught many peoples to forge not only swords and shields but laws and communities. Angered, he was merciless, crafting cursed items that still cause trouble in Midgard.

==**Worshipers**==
Reaver dwarves, cantonal dwarves, the Kariv and Khazzaki, and the people of Zobeck are especially fond of him. Most nations count him at least a lesser god, and smiths everywhere venerate him. Among the Khazzaki his battleaxe is called the shashka.

==**Shrines and Priests**==
In [[Zobeck]], Svarog's son, the Sun God Khors, is worshiped in his father's temple. The old temple was associated with the nobility and burned to the ground during the Great Revolt.

The great festival of Volund is the autumnal equinox, when the Fire Blessing is laid on weapons, armor, and metal tools at a fiery nighttime service that ends in the Anvil Prayer. Nearly every priest, smith, geargrinder, and steamworker in the city brings an anvil or a metal pan to hammer on during the chorus.

Volund is one of the most widely worshiped gods, with major temples in the Free Cantons, [[Grand Duchy of Dornig|Dornig]], the North, the [[The Crossroads|Crossroads]], and the [[Seven Cities]]. The greatest of his forge-shrines is the Great Temple of the Sacred Hammer in Templeforge, a canton of [[The Ironcrags]], where iron is smelted to light the services and the anvils rarely rest. Its master, Toveli Rogest, is his most famous priest. Others include the young master smith Hydrig Vallesulm of Salzbach and the more aggressive Øpir Skapti, priest-captain of the young dwarven company of the Hall of Spears in [[Stannasgard]].

==**Other Faiths**==
Volund and [[Rava]] have always been friendly rivals, and he is on excellent terms with [[Thor]] and [[Perun]]. Depending on who you believe, he is the lover of [[Sif]] or the husband of [[Lada]]. His greatest enemies include [[Seggotan]] and the water gods, [[Loki]] the trickster, and Boreas. Some claim he made [[Chernobog]]'s hammer.

==**What Volund Demands**==
Craft great works in his name, and bend stone, metal, and fire to your will. [[Humans]] add mastery of riding to his demands. His priests and many of his worshipers marry young, a sacred act that builds a family and forges links with the community. They make pilgrimages to his shrines far and wide, above all his hammer shrine in the Free Cantons and his anvil shrine in the North, bringing raw materials or tools to donate, and they must add something, however small, that they made themselves to every temple they visit.

==**In the North**==
In the [[Northlands]] he is also the Spear-Shaker and Mage-Smith, Patron of Reavers and Smiths, and his favored weapons are the hammer (any kind) and, among dwarves, the battleaxe. See [[Gods of the North]].

Volund is the god of earth and fire, of the hammer's ring on the anvil and the hiss of the quenched blade. He is a divine patron of the reaver dwarves, and many others revere him as the paragon artisan and lord of every jewel and precious metal in the earth. Among humans he is also the god of family and hospitality. As a hammer has two heads, he has inspired potent magic items and ancient cursed relics alike. He appears as a bald, powerfully built dwarf with a long red beard braided with metal clasps and rings, in practical clothes, his wide belt hung with every kind of smithing and crafting tool.

Smiths, craftspeople, artists, and reavers follow him, and anyone who feels the fierce joy of making. His faithful often wear an anvil-shaped pin or amulet. He demands that they craft great works in his name and bend stone, metal, and fire to their will, and he encourages bonds within a community such as blood-oaths and marriages. His pilgrims add something they made with their own hands to every shrine they visit.

His symbols are his anvil, marked with a horse's head, and his weapons, and he is linked with the *Kaunen* rune. His holy writings are the *Book of the Anvil* and the *Saga of Volund's Wanderings*, both concerned with tempering steel and souls. At his temples the liturgy is a crafting session, with circles of priest-smiths singing hymns as they hammer. The anvils of the great forge-shrines are tuned to different notes, and their chords are said to turn items into Volund's songsteel. The greatest forge-shrine is the Shrine of a Thousand Anvils in [[Stannasgard]], where pilgrims bring ingots for new anvils. On his holy days his priests work an everburning forge, hammers striking in unison like a chant.
==**Masks**==
Some dwarves associate Volund with [[Ptah]] of the south. The elves of the [[Arbonesse]] called him Veyland and honored him as fletcher and bowyer as well as smith, sometimes calling him the armorer of [[Valeresh]]. Humans call him Svarog south and east of the Nieder Straits. The [[Spindrift]] Dwarves reject this.
