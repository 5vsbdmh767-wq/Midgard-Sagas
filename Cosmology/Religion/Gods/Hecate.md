*Bringer of Magic, Lady of Darkness, Lady of Sighs,*
*Lady of Tears, Opener of Doors, the Queen of Night,*
*Patron of Kammae Straboli, Wife of the Ocean*

**Domains:** Knowledge, Light
**Alignment:** Evil
**Favored Weapons:** Dagger, garrote

Hecate is the goddess of the moon, magic, and change, and her ways baffle even the other gods. Her followers say she uses her foresight to make the world better. Her critics say her visions drove her mad. She once helped chain the sea god Nethus and later freed him, and the two are now married, which has done a great deal to calm the feud between their churches. For years Nethus's followers attacked her church, and some of his priests still refuse to forgive her.

She takes three forms with the moon. At the new moon she is the Lady of Darkness, a deadly young woman beloved of poisoners and shadow-walkers. At the half moon she is the Lady of Sighs, holding a key and a book, who whispers secrets to wizards and opens every door, even the one between life and death. At the full moon she is the Lady of Tears, a kind old woman with a lamp, who shows her servants the future and grieves for the world.

==**Worshipers**==
Her worship is strongest in [[Kammae Straboli]], where she is the greatest of gods. She is also honored across the [[Seven Cities]], on the minotaur island of [[Kyprion]], in [[Dominion of the Wind Lords#Roshgazi|Roshgazi]] and Cindass, the ancient minotaur homeland, in [[Verrayne]], and in the [[Magocracy of Allain]]. Most of her faithful are human, but many kobolds, minotaurs, and lycanthropes follow her.

==**Symbols and Books**==
The moon, dagger, key, book, and lamp. Her priests use a triangle inside a circle. Her followers wear small amulets or pins of these. Her holy book is the *Book of Mysteries*, with rites and rules for every time of year and detailed instructions for daily life. Her greatest celebrations are Midsummer, the Harvest Moon, and the Hunter's Moon.

==**Shrines and Priests**==
Her grandest temple is the Great Gold and Silver Temple of the Three Goddesses in Kammae Straboli, which she shares with [[Lada]] and [[Rava]]. Her temples always have rooms open to the moon. Her most famous priestess is the Oracle of Kammae Straboli, Yeneva Podella. The Bemmean mage Erramun the Sleepless is also a devoted follower.

==**Masks**==
To most, Hecate is her masks, and each of her faces deserves worship. It does not matter if some call her by another name, such as [[Azuran]], [[Marena]], Thoth, or [[Seggotan]].

==**Other Faiths**==
She keeps herself apart from other gods, and since the chaining of Nethus other faiths have grown ever more distrustful of her, even to the point of violence. Her enemies reach well beyond the Seven Cities and include the followers of [[Khors]] the sun god. [[Bastet]], who also claims the moon, is her rival, and the followers of [[Mavros]] say she was his wife until her affair with Nethus. Nethus, [[Sarastra]], Lada, and [[Ninkash]] are on good terms with her.

==**What Hecate Demands**==
Seek and acquire knowledge, and learn magic or shapechanging if you can. Follow the Mysteries and obey her chosen without question. Attend her services at least once a week. Speak many languages and revere the spirits of the dead. All items of magic and transformation belong to the goddess. Keep no secrets from her priests.

==**Beyond Midgard**==
In the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]] she is called the goddess who made the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]], the first to spin a world out of shadow, and some call her the sister of [[Sabateus]].

==**In the Shadow Realm**==
In the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]] Hecate is one of the five great gods, Patron of the Sable [[Elves]] and [[Shadow Fey]] and Protector of the Shadow Realm, said to have raised its land from formless shadow. Her three forms there follow the aligned phases of the twin moons. In shadow she is a twin goddess and the source of all light, seen as less dangerous and more civilizing than her reputation in the mortal world. She is widely venerated in the shadow fey courts and among goblins, trollkin, and even bearfolk, and she is patron of several courts, among them the [[Court of Midnight Teeth]], the [[Court of Pale Roses]], the [[Court of Winter's Love]], and the [[Witch Queen's Court]]. Her largest followings are among the humans of [[Corremel]], the shadow goblins of [[Fandeval]], and a growing congregation of darakhul in [[Soriglass]] and the [[Twilight Empire]]. After a long neglect her temples are growing in importance, and her followers hope to spread the faith among the bearfolk. Her most famous temples there are soaring, curving buildings of glittering black and luminous white stone, with rooms open to the moon: in the [[Court of Golden Oaks]], in Corremel, the Cathedral of the Dark Moons in Fandeval, and the decaying Cathedral of the Luminous Moon in Soriglass, where two of her relics are shown. Her most famous priestess is Salarra Mortre of Fandeval. Her great festivals are Midsummer, the Harvest Moon, and the Hunter's Moon.

Many elves and shadow fey believe Hecate is a mask of [[Sarastra]], though few of her human followers agree, and a few of her enemies call her chaos and darkness a friendlier face of the [[Black Goat|Goat of the Woods]]. In shadow her enemies include Bastet (there called Ailuros), [[Kupkoresh]], and [[Anu-Akma]], who calls her mysteries corrupting, and her allies include Sarastra, [[Bengta]], and [[Santerr Marossa]]. The [[The Old Roads|Old Roads]] are said to be her servants and her eyes, and [[Alquam]]'s followers sometimes call her his grandmother.
