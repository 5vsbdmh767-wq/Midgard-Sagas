*Portal Goddess of the Ancients, Ocean Mother*

**Domains:** Nature, Tempest
**Favored Weapon:** Mace

Rinpash was the goddess of portals, waters, and oceans for the Ancients, the long-vanished people of the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]], revered as the wellspring of life and health. Her statues are always found headless, sometimes with a scroll or mace in her belt. Some minotaurs and a few giants still pray to her as the Ocean Mother or the Giver of Waters, and once in a while she answers, faintly, from far away. Her ancient temples are ruined or abandoned, and her husband or brother [[Yundar]] often stands beside her.
