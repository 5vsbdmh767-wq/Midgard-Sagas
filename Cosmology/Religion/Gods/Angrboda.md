*Mother of Monsters, Grief-Bringer, Goddess of Change, Nature's Fury,*
*Witch-Queen of the [[Blodejord|Ironwood]], Mother of Fenris, Hel, and Jormungandr*

**Favored Weapons:** Claws, quarterstaff

The firstborn child of Aurgelmir, the wild and sorrowful Angrboda is a shapechanging jotun goddess. She is the untamed face of nature, the force of change that destroys the old to make way for the new. She believes the gods perverted the path of nature when they built this world from her father's body, and that only its destruction can set things right (see [[Gods of the North#The First Gods|The First Gods]]). Her influence is felt in the darkened sky, the howling wind, and the roars of monsters in the dark.

When she chooses to be seen, Angrboda appears as a fierce giant with wild, red-streaked black hair, a beautiful raven-haired maiden, or a gnarled hag with ebony claws. She gives little thought to her garb or manner, preferring to act in the moment. Her few icons show her in ragged robes of fur and hide, carrying a skull-tipped staff.

## Worshipers

Giants, trolls, ogres, and giantkin follow Angrboda, and many werekin venerate her as the mother of [[Fenris]] and the World Serpent. All across the [[Northlands]], people pray and sacrifice to her during dangerous weather or monster attacks, if only to talk her into venting her wrath somewhere else.

Some druid circles and witch covens revere her not as a destroyer but as a force of change. In [[Vargrike]], a cult of werekin and lycanthrope druids called the Ironhowl Circle holds rites at ancient standing stones and trains monstrous beasts to guard wild places against encroaching civilization.

## Symbols and Books

Her symbols are a black tree and a coiled serpent. Before [[Wotan]] stole them, the *Eiwaz* and *Thurisaz* runes were hers. Outside [[Jotunheim]], cults caught displaying her symbols are rooted out, and their members exiled or killed.

Most of her lore passes from believer to believer as sung or spoken liturgy, but she does have a sacred text, *Blotmodr, the Sacrificial Womb*, an account of her affair with [[Loki]] and the birth of their monstrous children. Between its salacious and horrible passages lie fell curses, ancient magic, and world-changing prophecies.

## Shrines and Priests

South of Jotunheim she is worshiped only in carefully hidden shrines or wild places touched by storm and grief. Most giant settlements keep a public shrine to her, and there she is openly worshiped.

Her priests never stay in one place for long, preferring to wander the wilds communing with their goddess and her children. They are a two-edged blade: they offer healing and aid, especially to those in childbirth or hurt by monsters, but they rail against anyone who despoils the wilderness and are quick to call down their goddess's wrath.

## Other Faiths

Angrboda and [[Bergelmir]] are the only children of Aurgelmir to survive his murder, and the only gods the jotun truly revere.
