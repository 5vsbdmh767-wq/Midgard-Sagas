*Goddess of Tomb Robbers and [[Gnolls]]*

**Domains:** Death, Trickery
**Favored Weapon:** Spear

Dalcris is an old goddess of grave robbers, shown with bandoliers full of potions and daggers and a shovel or crowbar for breaking into tombs. Thieves and bandits love her. She is always a step ahead of the law and smarter than the gate guard, and because she knows hardship she is generous to beggars and the hungry. Her offerings are grave goods or pickpocketed coins. Her symbol is a shovel or a tomb's arch, and her priests work as gravediggers, mourners, and embalmers, which is a good way to learn which tombs are worth robbing. She is known mostly in the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]].
