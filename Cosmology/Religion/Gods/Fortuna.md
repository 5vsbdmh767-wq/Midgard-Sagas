*Lady Luck, Goddess of Gamblers, Fate,*
*and the Astral Sea*

**Domains:** Life, Trickery
**Favored Weapon:** Mace

Fortuna hands out good luck or bad as the mood takes her. She guides sailors home, helps the unlucky, and is the god people pray to when nothing else has worked. She is shown blindfolded with her eyes shut, though in some pictures she is peeking. Her symbols are a ship's rudder, a ball of string, and a horn of plenty. Gamblers, travelers, and the [[Servants of the Dreaming God|Dreamers]] of the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]] all honor her, and her power seems to rise and fall over time.
