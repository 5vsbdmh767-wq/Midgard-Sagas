*God of the Seas, Gale Master, Wake-Warden, Bringer of Bounty, Wavecaster,*
*Patron of Sailors and Ships, Patron of Noatun*

**Favored Weapons:** Trident, wand

Njord rules the sea with wisdom rather than chaos. He commands the winds, blesses the sails of the worthy, and watches over coastal peoples and fisherfolk. He is calm and generous but not to be taken lightly: storms gather at his call, and wizards whisper that he hoards all the ancient knowledge lost beneath the waves. He founded Noatun in [[Trollheim]], holding back the Swive to save it.

He appears as a stout, sunburned man in his late fifties with silver streaks in his brown hair and beard. His garments ripple as though underwater, and his piercing blue eyes hold the stillness of deep water. He is usually shown in robes of blue and sea-green with gold coin jewelry, holding a trident, standing atop the waves, or casting a net over a storm.

## Worshipers

Sailors, shipwrights, fishers, traders, sea-wizards, and anyone who works the sea honor Njord. Coastal towns hold feasts in his name before the longships leave on raids and when the fishing fleet comes home. His clerics bless sails and use spells to calm storms or call the wind, and merchants and reavers alike spill a little wine or mead overboard for him at the start of a voyage.

The *Oarbound*, a ship of his sea-priests, sails between ports offering healing, divination, and weather magic, and throw half of whatever they are paid into the sea in thanks.

## Symbols and Books

His symbols are a blue longship, a golden trident, and a gold coin marked with waves, and he is linked with the *Laukaz* rune. His priests keep many sacred books, together called the *Tide-Songs*, full of sea-lore, tidal observations, and navigational charts.

His oldest known text survives only as fragments, the *Scrolls of Foam and Wave*, thought by many to be pages of Njord's own spellbook from before he became a god. They are said to hold forgotten storm-binding spells and even the ritual he used to hold back the Swive, but too few fragments have been found to reconstruct it.

## Shrines and Priests

His shrines stand in harbor towns, cliffside grottos, and aboard ships, their altars built of driftwood, coral, or stone and decorated with shells, fish-bone charms, and pieces of wrecks. His priests wear sea-blue robes often hung with silver fish-hooks. They preside over sea burials, bless voyages, and call or calm the winds with ritual chants.

His greatest temple is the Tideward Hall in Noatun, whose altar holds a barnacle-crusted staff Njord left as a sign of his patronage. Its high priest, the trollkin skald Vetra Saltvoice, is famous across the North for singing storms to sleep and calling whales to her aid.

## Masks

Some clerics believe Njord is one of the sea gods absorbed by Seggotan, the Great Sea Dragon, and is now one of Seggotan's names.

## Other Faiths

[[Yarila & Porevit|Freyr and Freyja]] sponsored Njord for divinity. He married the giant [[Sif|Skadi]] as wergild for her father's death, though the marriage failed and they now live apart (see [[Gods of the North#Other Deities|Other Deities]]).
