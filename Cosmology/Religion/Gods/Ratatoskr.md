*Lord of Secrets, Nut Bringer, Messenger to Eagles, Tree Runner,*
*Keeper of Gossip, Chitterer, Patron of Travelers and Ratatosk*

**Favored Weapon:** Dagger

Ratatoskr is the clever runner, mischievous trickster, and laughing messenger of [[Yggdrasil]]. Duties, rules, and orders are forgotten in the joy of new sights, gathered secrets, and spread rumors. Many think him more nuisance than god, but he knows a great deal, trading news with the eagles of the high branches and with the surly dragon [[Nidhogg]] among the roots. When other gods ask his advice before a journey or trial, their task seems easier and their success more certain.

Few mortals worship him, but sayings, tales, songs, and curses inspired by him run all through the culture of the [[Northlands]]. Whenever a curse is uttered or a story told of someone small beating a much larger foe, Ratatoskr hears it wherever he is, and smiles. He appears as a human-sized [[Ratatosk|ratatosk]] with tiny silver tusks and a golden horn on his forehead, and speaks telepathically in a whisper brimming with suppressed laughter.

## Worshipers

His own ratatosk worship him most, and his faith in the North is centered in [[Hrafnheim]]. Elsewhere messengers and tricksters revere him, as do those facing impossible-seeming tasks or traveling between the planes. Such wayfarers leave him cracked hazelnuts for luck. His followers treat secrets as a challenge to uncover and share. He teaches them to gather choice rumors like the finest acorns, share them freely, guide the lost and wandering, and always respect mighty Yggdrasil.

## Symbols and Books

His symbol is a golden acorn. It is widely thought he has no holy book, but he does: the *Bok of Vel*, or *Book of Wiles*, written on preserved leaves fallen from Yggdrasil and bound with a twig from one of its branches. The only known copy is in Runaskr. Its leaves record his many meetings with the Lord of Eagles in the canopy and his many tricks on Nidhogg.

## Shrines and Priests

His only formal temple in the North is in Runaskr, but shrines to him stand on many of Yggdrasil's branches and at the feet of other World Trees across the planes, piled with acorns and nuts left in return for safe, swift travel. His priests include ratatosk, ravenfolk, and a scattering of werekin.
