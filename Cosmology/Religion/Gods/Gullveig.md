*The Matron of Coin, the Ice-Blinded, the Silver Witch,*
*the Burned Matron, the Invisible Coin*

**Favored Weapons:** Quarterstaff, sickle

Gullveig is a vanished goddess of wealth and magic, known above all for her skill with illusion and transmutation. Weakened in the ages since she disappeared, she was once widely worshiped by mortals and elves alike. Fragments of lore suggest the Silver Witch started the war between the Aesir and Vanir that ended with [[Yarila & Porevit|Freyr and Freyja]] traded to the Aesir as hostages.

As punishment, the Aesir pierced her three times with spears and burned her body to ash. The first two times she came back with a sly smile and a chunk of pure silver in each hand. The third time the Aesir waited, but her ashes blew away on the North Wind and she was never seen again, though [[Sif]] and Freyja both swear they heard her laughter on that breeze.

Legend says she once lived among an isolated community of dwarves and taught them to bind magic runes into precious metals, the gift still carried by the [[Lineages#Dwarf Lineage Variant: Baugsmidr Dwarf|baugsmidr dwarves]].

## Worshipers

At her height, the Silver Witch was worshiped by landowners, community leaders, and merchants. Reavers threw hacksilver into the water as they left home to go raiding, hoping for easy wealth and little struggle. As a patron of magic, she was a favorite of enchanters, illusionists, and transmuters, and many of her devout lived as druids in the forests, as often on four legs as on two.

## Symbols and Books

Her symbol is a silver coin. Her holy book is *The Book of Smiling Silver*. Few copies exist now that she has long been silent, but some of her most devout claim she still sends them messages, pressed into the pages in the blocky runes of the giants.

## Shrines and Priests

Most of her shrines have fallen into ruin or vanished. A single temple remains, in a hidden valley in the mountains east of [[Huldramose]], where her last high priest, an ancient elf, refuses to travel to the Summerlands until her mistress returns to her place among the gods.

## Masks

Some say Heid or Gullveig is the Northern name of [[Sarastra]].
