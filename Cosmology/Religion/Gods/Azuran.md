*Lord of the Four Winds, Arbitrator of Victory, Lord of the Skies Beyond the Sky, the Four-Faced Compass, the Gambling God, the First and Final Breath, Patron God of Sikkim and the Ravenfolk*

**Domains:** Dragon, Knowledge, Speed, War
**Alignment:** Chaotic
**Favored Weapons:** Shortsword, shortbow

The dragon gods of [[The Mharoti Empire]] are semi-draconic, semi-human, faceless, masked, and jealous elemental gods, comforting and familiar to dragonkin but looming and strange to everyone else. Even among them, Azuran is especially strange: a unity of four conflicting forces, four great strands of a faith in wind and sky. The four are one god and also the Gathering of Winds, each a separate reading of Azuran. They disagree on finer points but agree on the founding truth: breath is life, and wind and sky are the highest expression of the divine.

Azuran dwells at the roof of heaven, soaring above all heights, a calm fury of storm. Everything that breathes is his creation, and every last breath is a prayer to him. He sees all fates and knows everything spoken on the breath. His eye is everywhere and yet invisible. To understand his mysteries is to pierce the veil of illusion and rise to where wisdom dwells. He is shown as a circle or a glass sphere, an emptiness, or by the more literal-minded as a four-faced figure with no eyes, nose, or features, only four blank ovals.

==**Worshipers**==
[[Humans]], dragonkin, [[Ravenfolk|ravenfolk]], and [[Kobolds|kobolds]] follow him, split into four rival sects: the Gamblers of the Eastern Wind, who rule fate and fortune and are honored by merchants and gamblers; the Savants of the Southern Wind, sought by wizards, oracles, and scholars for wisdom beyond the mortal sphere; the Warriors of the Western Wind, who aid soldiers in battle; and the Wanderers of the Northern Wind, who give sailors and travelers good roads. He is the foremost god of distant Sikkim, whose artificers, priests, and wizards are his most devoted followers. Other famous priests include Basha General Azladdan ir-Rahullah, war-cleric and commander of the Mharoti Windrider Legion, and Windspeaker Dariosh of [[Parthia]]. Anyone seeking luck or a helpful wind calls on Azuran, and every call makes him stronger. The mages of [[Magocracy of Allain|Bemmea]] and the sages of [[Friula]]'s Great Library find his teachings intriguing.

==**Symbols and Books**==
His faith has a remarkable number of symbols, four chief ones and a dozen more for its factions. The most common are a compass rose and wind signs of waving lines, spirals, clouds, an arrow, or concentric circles, sometimes with wings or fangs. His core teachings are gathered in *The Azuran*, a disputed collection with divergent sections and apocrypha. Well known but poorly understood are the *Prophecies of the Wind*, the *Ephemeral Whispers of Aahuz*, and the *Invisible Tome of the Unseers*.

==**Shrines and Priests**==
His temples stand throughout the Dragon Empire and beyond, usually more than one per city so each sect has its own, and they stay austere despite huge donations. The grandest is the Open Temple on a peak near Harkesh, fifty colossal pillars holding up a great roof over an ivory statue of the four-faced god, otherwise bare. The second is the Ringing Temple of Qiresh, a cliffside temple known for sacrifices hurled into the [[Despotate of the Ruby Sea|Ruby Sea]]. The most famous holy site is the Singing Chantry of Sikkim, where the wind flowing through the building chimes and whistles the music of the winds. Each Wind is said to keep a sacred shrine at one of the four corners of the world, each hidden and the goal of lifelong pilgrimages.

==**Masks**==
Masks come easily to his followers, some of whom believe the four faces were once separate godlings. To them every foreign god is one of the Four Winds, and only the dragon gods are Azuran's peers. They claim the storm god [[Perun]] is the Northern Wind and see a strong link between Horus and the Southern Wind. Others connect him to Nuberu the Cloud Master, a god of the giants. [[Bastet]] is widely associated with him, especially with the Eastern Wind.

==**Other Faiths**==
Azuran is cordial with [[Baal-Hotep|Baal]] and [[Seggotan]], for air feeds flames and carries rain clouds across the earth. He pities [[Khespotan]], forever earthbound and bound by rigid fate.

==**What Azuran Demands**==
Trust the Winds and they will shower you with fortune and victory. Breathe deep, travel far and wide, and tell your tales to all who will listen. Silence is no virtue. Walk the world as the Winds walk above it, proud, bold, and swift. Your voice belongs to Azuran, and you must return it after your final breath, well used. Store up wonders and stories worth telling. Speak well when you must speak, and sing at dawn and dusk. Gamble, preach, and tell others of the strength of the True Wind.
