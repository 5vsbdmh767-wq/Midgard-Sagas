*Voice of the Titans, Bellowing Storm, God of Glory, Revenge, and Domination,*
*Son of Aurgelmir, First Warlord, Patron of the Jotun*

**Favored Weapon:** Maul

Bergelmir is the last son of Aurgelmir and, with [[Angrboda]], the only child of the First Giant to survive his father's death (see [[Gods of the North#The First Gods|The First Gods]]). He embodies all the rage the giants still nurse over Aurgelmir's murder. As First Warlord, he will lead the jotun and giants against the hated gods when the time for revenge comes, and every giant calls on him when charging into battle.

He appears as a towering, deathly pale giant cloaked in the hides of monstrous beasts. It is said a nimbus of killing frost surrounds him, his voice can shatter mountains, and his long hair and beard are jagged spikes of ice. Statues show him in rune-carved armor holding a two-handed maul.

## Worshipers

All giants, ogres, and trolls worship Bergelmir. He is the patron of the jotun and plainly favors them, but every descendant of Aurgelmir fears slighting him and losing his blessing in a time of need. Some berserkers, reavers, and warlords also swear oaths to him in desperate times, begging for the strength to dominate or destroy. His rites involve roaring chants, blood rituals, and oaths of vengeance.

The Bellowing Storm demands that his followers always prove their strength over weaker creatures, conquer in his name, punish cowardice and timidity, and glorify combat.

## Symbols and Books

His symbols are a spiral of ice and a screaming face. Before [[Wotan]] stole them, the *Berkanan*, *Sowilo*, and *Tiwaz* runes were his. His followers carve his sigil on weapons, bones, and shields, believing it strengthens them in battle.

For a revenge-obsessed war god he has many sacred texts. The most important are *The Bellowing*, a hymnal of jotun chant-cycles from the First Age, and the *Oathbound Litany*, a chronicle of leadership studied by giant warlords and would-be cult leaders.

## Shrines and Priests

His temples are built of blackened ice, colossal bones, or the ribcages of titanic beasts, deep in mountain caverns or atop howling cliffs where the wind carries his name. One well-known temple is the Hall of Resounding Glory in [[Agnalan]], where his priests train giantkin warriors for [[Jotunheim]]'s armies.

His priests are war leaders, raiders, tyrants, and warlords, always at the front of the charge with howling prayers on their lips. The clergy has no formal structure, and its members challenge each other to ritual duels to settle rank and keep their authority. His most feared priest is Kolrjot Icehowl, a jotun skald who once froze an avalanche with a single bellow. He wields a great axe called *Rifkljufr*, "Rib-Splitter," and leads the Bloodhail raiders from a hidden redoubt north of the Alvavann Forest in [[Wolfheim]].
