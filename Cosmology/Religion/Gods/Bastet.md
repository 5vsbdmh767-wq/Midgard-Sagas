*Goddess of Cats and Hunters, Queen of Perfumes,*
*Bastard Child of Aten, Mother of Alchemy, Patron of the [[Gnolls]],*
*Wife of [[Anu-Akma]]*

**Domains:** Nature, Trickery
**Alignment:** Chaotic
**Favored Weapon:** Temple sword

Bastet is the goddess of sunny afternoons and wild abandon, of desire, and of the hunt. She is patron of perfumers, alchemists, and everyone whose trade is beauty. As a lioness she smells of the open desert, and as the house cat she is the farmer's friend, killing the rats and snakes in the granary. She is shown with a cat's head or a lion's, golden fur, a sun or moon disk above her head, and bracelets shaped like cobras.

==**Worshipers**==
An odd crowd: alchemists, dancers, farmers, temple courtesans, common soldiers (never officers), and the gnolls of the south, who adopted her when she married the jackal-headed Anu-Akma. The gnolls of Kesh and of Per-Bastet are devoted to her, and her standing as supreme huntress endears her to them.

==**Symbols and Books**==
A cat's head beneath a sun disk, and sometimes the lion or the cobra. Everyone knows her book of sacred love poems, *Divine Lives and Desires*. Few know the darker *Words of the Huntress*.

==**Shrines and Priests**==
She has a whole city of her own, Per-Bastet in [[Nuria Natal]], where she often appears in person and her temples stand on every street. Only women can be her priestesses. Male worshipers are welcome. Some of her priestesses are weretigers, werelions, or, in scandalous stories more than fact, rakshasas, and many are also alchemists, hunters, rangers, or temple courtesans. Per-Bastet is a center of alchemy, perfume, and fine jewelry, with a surprisingly civilized Gnoll Quarter. Its greatest temple is the Dome of the Divine Face of Bastet, a huge soaring dome over a dozen fragrant altars, and the Black House of Anu-Akma beside it seems an afterthought. As Ailuros she is also popular in the Grand Duchy of [[Bourgund]].

==**Masks**==
She is Ailuros in [[Bourgund]] and Artemis in the [[Seven Cities]]. In the [[The Crossroads|Crossroads]] she is linked to St. Hubertus the Hunter and to the dark god [[The Hunter|the Hunter]]. Oddly, she is one of the few human goddesses said to wear a dragon god's mask: she is widely linked with [[Azuran]], especially the Eastern Wind.

==**Other Faiths**==
Her temples and priestesses are on the best of terms with those of [[Anu-Akma]], and her priestesses and his priests marry in a "mirror marriage" reflecting the divine wedding. She hates [[Marena]], who perverts both lust and death, and [[Hecate]], who intrudes on her moon, and the jealous faith of [[Aten]] calls her a demonic abomination. She hates serpent gods even more, especially the resurrected Set, [[Baal-Hotep|Baal]], and [[Veles]].

==**What Bastet Demands**==
Enjoy life and live for the day. Hunt your food when you can and kill serpents gladly. Visit the perfumers often and bring gifts of scent and spice to her altars. To divine the future, read the entrails of a huge, unblemished animal, and sacrifice such a creature before any great endeavor.

==**Beyond Midgard**==
In the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]], Bastet shows her favorites the catslide alleys, city streets that lead between worlds. Travelers say that following a cat is a fair way to get through the Labyrinth alive. It may not take you where you meant to go, but it will find food and water.

==**In the Shadow Realm**==
In the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]] she is Ailuros, Goddess of Deer and Hunters, one of its five great gods, and the deer is her sacred animal. There she is the wild, free goddess of moonlit nights and joyous hunting, patron of the [[Alseid|alseid]], so often seen with her hounds and glowing moonlit arrows that some think her half-fey, a member of the Great Hunt. She is shown with golden hair, dressed for the hunt, with a solar or lunar disk above her head and bracelets shaped like deer or serpents. Only women may be her priestesses; some are alseid, werewolves, or bearfolk, and many are also alchemists, hunters, rangers, or trackers. Her greatest shadow shrine is the Scented Church of [[Merrymead]], where the Fragrant Servant Sayzanette Belleflor leads a mostly bearfolk congregation. The High Temple of Perfumed Night in [[Bourgund]] is the start of a shadow road to Merrymead, a route the bearfolk know but rarely mention. Her holy books there are *The Path of the Huntress*, a volume of hymns, parables, hunting lore, and poetry, and *The Grove of Sirens*, a collection of prophecies, perfumer's lore, and guidance for blessing incense and hunters. In [[Corremel]] she is called Bastet, and she is often linked with [[The Hunter|the Hunter]], who finds common cause with her. [[Anu-Akma]]'s priests respect hers, who supply the perfumes and incense of the death god's rites, and [[Baccholon]] treats her with fondness. Besides Marena and Hecate, her enemies there include the jealous faith of [[Kupkoresh]], whose followers call Bastet a demonic abomination. Before any great undertaking, her faithful read the entrails of a huge, unblemished animal and sacrifice it.
