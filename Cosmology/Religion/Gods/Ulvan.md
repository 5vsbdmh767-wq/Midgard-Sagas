*God of Ghosts and Spirits*

**Domains:** Crafting, Death
**Favored Weapon:** Mace

Ulvan is the god of the ghosts and spirits his brother [[Charun]] didn't carry into the afterlife. He is a maker and a crafter, wise with the wisdom of all the souls he has looked after, and a friend to demons, angels, and undead alike. He knows whenever someone reaches beyond the veil to speak with the dead. He is shown as a mask with three holes hanging above tattered cloth. His priests talk with the dead and are followed by phantoms, and killing one brings terrible hauntings. In the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]], they seal the doors to dead worlds after laying their ghosts to rest.
