*Goddess of Night and Magic, Her Celestial*
*Majesty Sarastra Aestruum, [[Hecate|Queen of Night]]*
*and Magic, Duchess of the Heavens,*
*Countess of Thorn, Mistress of Air and*
*Darkness, Lady of the Winter Palace and*
*Bride of Shadow, Patron of the Shadow Fey*

**Domains:** Darkness, Knowledge, Labyrinth, Trickery
**Alignment:** Neutral
**Favored Weapons:** Rapier and dagger

Said to be a goddess among the fey and certainly popular in [[Triolo]], [[Nuria Natal|Corremel]], and the south, Sarastra's priests claim she is the source of all darkness and raw arcane energies. She is also the patroness of the [[Shadow Fey|shadow fey]], whom she rules from the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]] as the Queen of Night and Magic.

Her goals are oblique and mysterious, often cruel and heartless, and so are her favorite followers, the shadow fey. She plots out of boredom and malice, and her mastery of the arcane and of deception and misdirection make her dangerous. She has a perfect memory for the slightest insult, and some of her revenges take centuries. She appears as an impossibly beautiful shadow fey woman in an indigo gown covered in diamonds, with a diadem of mithral and glowing starstones and a cloak that seems part of the night. In the Shadow Realm her avatar presides over the Courts of the Shadow Fey. Her attention is dangerous but can bring great rewards to those who please her, no easy task given her moods. She makes complex rules and protocols for her court and changes them on a whim, and she recruits many pawns to use against her enemies.

==**Worshipers**==
Sarastra is not native to [[Zobeck]] and has no temple or shrine within the city. Most of its people have no love for her. Only a handful of Sarastrans live in the city, most of them shadow fey, whom the other citizens of Zobeck have yet to accept.

Beyond Zobeck, her chief followers are shadow fey, elves, and the elfmarked, along with human and tiefling arcanists. Pockets of her worship exist in [[Friula]] and [[Triolo]]. Her worship is banned in many places, especially where rival gods of magic such as [[Khors]], [[Hecate]], or [[Thoth-Hermes]] are favored.

==**Symbols and Books**==
Her symbol is a stylized star inside a triangle above three whorls of air. Her holiest book is *The Mistress of Air and Darkness*, a book of confusing tales her worshipers use to try to decipher her desires.

==**Shrines and Priests**==
Her shrines are common in elven lands, where they are shared with other gods of shadow: dark buildings lit by glinting silver offerings, each with an entry room partly open to the sky, the moon window or moon gate. Her greatest temple in Midgard is at the River Court of the [[Arbonesse]], led by an elven pair, the cleric Laurin Marcen and the bard Genessa, who have spread her worship in the Arbonesse and the [[Grand Duchy of Dornig]], somewhat to the discomfort of its rulers.

==**Masks**==
She takes many forms in the mortal world. Besides her favored shadow fey form, she may appear as wind and shadow, a white deer, ravens, or owls to advise the lost or teach magic, or as a pale maiden who tempts chivalrous knights to abandon their orders and waste away in pursuit of her. She is called Heid or [[Gullveig]] in the North. Hecate and even [[Charun]] have been suggested as her masks in the south. Sarastra and Hecate, despite their similar domains and the hostility between their followers, never acknowledge each other in any way, and some believe they are the same deity, which their followers deny. Some say she is the elven mask of [[Chernobog]].

==**Other Faiths**==
[[House Stross]] offered Sarastra some worship during their long rule of Zobeck, though they never fully embraced her, and remained loyal to the Sun God Khors for many decades. The shadow fey worked for generations to turn the family further into shadow.
Beyond Zobeck she counts many gods as rivals or enemies. She has clashed with Charun and [[Wotan]], her relationship with Thoth-Hermes is complicated but mostly rivalry, and she competes with other fey gods, such as [[Yarila & Porevit]], for the elves' attention. Her followers and [[Holda]]'s are almost always at odds. Her chief ally is [[The Hunter]], in his aspect as the [[Court of the Moonlit King|Moonlit King]]. They are lovers, passionate though often estranged.

==**What Sarastra Demands**==
Walk the world and understand its magic. Celebrate beauty, mystery, and magic. Enchantment, illusion, and deceit are worthy weapons; learn them. Let nothing beautiful perish thoughtlessly. Follow your passions, whatever others think. Ignore the laws of men. Sacrifice blood and magic to the goddess by night, and reap your rewards each day.
