*King of the Sea, Master of Waves, Patron of Kammae and Ankeshel, Lord of Fish and Whales, God of Broken Chains, Keeper of Storms, Protector of Ships*

**Domains:** Ocean, Tempest, Travel
**Alignment:** Chaotic
**Favored Weapons:** Trident, net

Once loved throughout Midgard, Nethus fell far when the Oracle of [[Kammae Straboli|Kammae]] chained him and held him prisoner. Eight years ago he was freed, but many of his old temples had been abandoned and others taken over by [[Seggotan]], the dragon god of the sea. His priesthood is rebuilding, and he has been restored as Kammae's patron. He has even married Kammae's great goddess, [[Hecate]], in the Marriage of Moon and Ocean, and some believe he swore a vow of service in exchange for his freedom.

==**Worshipers**==
Nethus is widely worshiped in Kammae, [[Capleon]], and [[Friula]], and as far north as [[Magocracy of Allain|Bemmea]], mostly by humans and, to a lesser degree, by minotaurs and elves. In ancient days his faith was widespread in Ankeshel before it sank. His priesthood is growing, and his followers are rebuilding his neglected temples and regilding his statues. Fishers and pearl divers are among his most devoted followers, along with merchant captains, shipwrights, netmakers, seafaring soldiers, and the explorers of [[Barsella]].

==**Symbols and Books**==
His most common symbol is a fish or whale, then the trident or hook. In his years of captivity a chain stood for his suffering, but that symbol has now been cast aside. All his teachings are in one book, the *Parables of the Sailing God*, divided into practical advice on seafaring and fishing, advice on living a good life, and a mystical section called the *Fisherman's Dream*, sometimes bound as a separate volume.

==**Shrines and Priests**==
His two most famous shrines are the Temple of the Ocean Moon in Kammae, once the place of his imprisonment and now a seat of shared power where Hecate and Nethus appear in visions as allies, and the Steps of the Sea in Capleon. Smaller shrines are common in every harbor, including those of [[Nuria Natal]], the islands of [[Triolo]], and the coasts of the [[Seven Cities]]. His high priest is the Ocean Seer Qorette Mardefon, who until recently lived in [[Valera]] as an adviser to Emperor Loki V and now leads the rebuilding of the Steps of the Sea.

==**Masks**==
Dragonkin believe Nethus is only the human mask of Seggotan. [[Humans]] say the reverse, and that Seggotan and even [[Wotan]] are masks of Nethus. A few heretics, mostly druids who have indulged in too many ecstatic trances, claim moon and sea are one god in two forms, their divine marriage proving it.

==**Other Faiths**==
Nethus is a new friend to Hecate and a foe to the usurper Seggotan and the foul fire god [[Baal-Hotep|Baal]]. He also sets his followers against [[Aten]] and [[Bastet]]. His allies include the elven gods [[Yarila & Porevit]] and the [[Black Goat|Goat of the Woods]], whose followers believe the sea is a friend to the forces of chaos. He and [[Charun]] are old friends, and some say brothers. Some priests of Nethus still refuse to forgive Hecate for his captivity.

==**What Nethus Demands**==
Honor the tides and the moon. Learn to sail, fish, and swim. Explore new lands to the west and east, and respect the seas. Make a sacrifice before any voyage over water. Never forget that all waters return to the sea, and that a dam can never hold a river for long.
