*All Father, Rune Master, God of the Tree and*
*Raven, God of Strife, King of Asgard and Lord*
*of Valhalla, Patron of Kings and Wizards*

**Domains:** Knowledge, War
**Alignment:** Lawful
**Favored Weapon:** Spear

[[Thor]] and [[Lada|Baldur]] may be better loved, but Wotan is the mightiest of the Northern gods. He made the world from the carved-up body of the first giant, Aurgelmir, and earned the hatred of every giant since. He gave an eye for wisdom, and when that wasn't enough he hung himself on the [[Yggdrasil|World Tree]] and stole runes and charms from the Void beyond existence. His ravens, Thought and Memory, fly the world bringing him news, and from his throne he can watch all creation. He is everything a strong king should be: wise, far-sighted, ruthless to enemies, generous to friends, a master of magic and war.

==**Worshipers**==
Fewer than you'd expect. He is dour, and his gifts come at a price, so his followers are mostly jarls, wizards, and graybeards. Among dwarves, turning to Wotan marks the step from youth into middle age. The ravenfolk adore him.

==**Symbols and Books**==
The World Tree, the single eye, runes, ravens, and wolves. His holy writings are all in the Northern tongue, including the *Well of Mimir* and the *Secret Runes*, and he expects his followers to read.

==**Shrines and Priests**==
His greatest shrine is in the dwarven hall of [[Stannasgard]], where the rune chorus never stops. The kings and captains of the dwarven reavers are his priests or devoted followers.

==**In the North**==
The [[Northlands]] also call him Shaman of the Gods. See [[Gods of the North]].

Wotan led the gods against Aurgelmir at the dawn of time, and his cunning lets him triumph again and again, until Ragnarok swallows the world. He appears as a dour, powerfully built old man with long gray hair and a flowing beard, in patched blue, gray, and brown clothes, often with a raven on each shoulder and a pair of wolves at his side. Every depiction shows his missing right eye. His ravens are Huginn (Thought) and Muninn (Memory), and his wolves Freki (Greedy) and Geri (Ravenous).

He demands that his followers be wise, canny in judgment, skilled in poetry, and quick to lead in honorable battle; cowards and fools get no sympathy from him. Warriors pray to him for luck and victory, or, if defeat is fated, for the notice of his Valkyries and a place in Valhalla. Some women revere him too, for the swift news his ravens bring or for rune magic. Sacrifices to him are hung and pierced by spears.

Besides the books above, his texts include the *Tree of Knowledge*. Fiercely guarded in the heart of Runaskr in [[Hrafnheim]] is the only known copy of *Aldrunir*, "Giant Magics of the First Age," which holds enchantments he seized from the jotun before the world was made. The Hall of the Watching Eye in [[Wolfheim]] will be his largest temple in the North once it is finished. Perhaps his greatest cleric is Brokk Kolisen of the Shrine of the Gallows Tree in [[Trollheim]], a black-bearded human raised among dwarves and respected for his understanding of their ways, who leads many expeditions against the giants of [[Jotunheim]].
==**Masks**==
The eastern horse tribes and elves call him Ellel, the angelic folk of [[Ishadia]] call him Az, and the haunted giants call him Gades the Titan. Some link him with [[Aten]], a dubious claim at best, and in the North [[Horus]] is sometimes thought his mask. Mostly he wears few masks and walks under his own name.

==**Other Faiths**==
He is rarely worshipped outside the [[Northlands]] and the [[The Ironcrags|Ironcrags]], though Bemmean mages like him for his lore. [[Loki]] is his blood-brother, and [[Charun]] is called Hod, his son, in the North. Loki is also his chief antagonist in the North. His priests are enemies of [[Sarastra]], goddess of night and magic, and keep watch against the dark gods, among them [[Mammon]], the [[Black Goat|Goat of the Woods]], the [[White Goddess]], and the derro goddess [[Addrikah]].

==**Beyond Midgard**==
On the worlds of the [[Old Ring]] in the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]], Wotan has turned some of the giants into allies against the Void. Some Void cults claim he tainted the silver rune of making with the Void to save the cosmos.
