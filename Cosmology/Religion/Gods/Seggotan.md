*Lord of the Ocean and Eternity, Keeper of Time, Oathbinder, Counter of the Stars, the Great Water Dragon, Patron of the Sea Drakes, Maritime Overlord, Commander of the Tides*

**Domains:** Dragon, Life, Ocean, Tempest
**Alignment:** Neutral
**Favored Weapon:** Trident

Seggotan, a dragon god of [[The Mharoti Empire]], is an ancient power who claims to have seen the beginning and the end of time. Every oath sworn to him must be kept, for he never forgets. Mortals may dare to bargain with him and his servants, though he fulfills his bargains in mysterious ways or with unintended results. Enigmatic and unpredictable, he holds the sea and all in it as his forever. If you have eaten one fish from the sea or taken one step in the surf, Seggotan has claimed you.

He speaks through his servants, the Kyree, capricious shapeshifting sea drakes who can speak with his voice. When he appears, he is an enormous shadow beneath the waves, or a gigantic two-headed dragon, one head green and one blue, speaking in perfect unison.

==**Worshipers**==
For centuries most coasts favored his rival, [[Nethus]], and Seggotan was worshiped mainly in the Mharoti Empire, where he greatly favored the dragonkin. After Nethus was chained in [[Kammae Straboli]], Seggotan became master of the sea, and his attention turned to grander or stranger things. His Kyree fell silent. After a string of unusual defeats against the navies of [[Capleon]] and [[Triolo]], Mharoti sailors coined the curse "Spit on Seggotan." He is still the navy's official patron, though, and its officers give him full respect, fearing to spurn the ancient god and hoping to win back his favor.

==**Symbols and Books**==
He is shown as a two-headed serpent, a trident, a loop, or a mix of these. His wisdom is recorded in verse on sheets of silver foil, the *Song Eternal*. Sects fight over the sheets, and paper drakes and rivals steal some, so no one knows how many there are or in what order they go.

==**Shrines and Priests**==
The Ageless Spire towers over the Mharoti city of Prezhan and the seat of Mharoti naval power. A great sacrificial pier runs half a mile from the temple into the sea, where locals make their offerings, and where the navy executes recruits who fail the empire. Outside the empire, coastal towns have shrines to him and sailors make perfunctory appeals, but only two other places have real priesthoods. The Great Library of [[Friula]] holds several sheets of the *Song Eternal*, which have given rise to the Order of Renewal, stressing rebirth and eternity over the god's warlike side. In [[Magocracy of Allain|Bemmea]], a small cult of mages bargains with him for elemental boons. His small priesthood in Capleon was driven out and its temple destroyed. The faith seems unable to take root in human lands.

==**Masks**==
Seggotan is. He wears no masks in organized faiths, though he answers animists who worship the sea and druids devoted to the ocean. The sea whispers his name to those who do not yet know it. Many clerics believe the lesser sea gods who were once his rivals, Aegir, [[Njord]], Nodens, Lir, and Tefnut, are now his heralds or servants, and that their names are his. Some believe Nethus, now free, is the last sea god he has not yet absorbed.

==**Other Faiths**==
He pays little mind to other faiths, though it irks him that [[Mavros]] and [[Perun]] claim the title of storm lord. Storms come from the sea, and the title is rightfully his. Mavros and his followers must drown. [[Baal-Hotep|Baal]]'s arrogance must sometimes be humbled too.

==**What Seggotan Demands**==
Sacrifice gold, jewels, and blood to the sea. Keep every oath sworn to him. Drop a coin into the sea, or prick your finger for a drop of blood, each time you enter it. Light no fires, and watch the stars. Never fail to bathe when water is near. Seggotan rewards the victorious, those who send their enemies' ships and treasure to a watery grave, and he is most pleased by death by drowning.
