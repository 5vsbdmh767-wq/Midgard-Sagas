*The Black God, Lord of the Night, Master of Men, Architect of Disaster, God of Fears and Fires, Patron of Betrayers and Murderers*

**Domains:** Darkness, Death
**Alignment:** Evil
**Favored Weapon:** Warhammer

Chernobog is one of the dark gods, who prey on their followers and trade power for blood and souls. He is the embodiment of every mortal fear, and his shape is changeable and horrifying. Most often he is an enormous black bear with horns and eyes like coals, his fur matted with blood and ichor. He is master of the dead, not through respect but by compulsion, and whole graveyards rise at his call. He is feared for his ravenous hungers and raging strength, and even [[Mavros]] fears to fight him, or so his priests say.

When Chernobog walks the earth in the dark of the moon and during eclipses, winds rise and howl, animals grow skittish and dogs bite, and ghosts rise from every grave. Every foul deed is laid at his door.

==**Worshipers**==
The corrupt and the vile follow him, especially witches, sorcerers, and wizards, but also thieves, murderers, bandits, and anyone seeking power, revenge, or wealth. Hags, trolls, ogres, dragons, and goblins follow him too and commit murders in his name. Some say [[Baba Yaga]] is secretly Chernobog's greatest follower. Others claim the two are rivals, or former lovers.

==**Symbols and Books**==
His symbols are a black square and a bear's claw. He has no sacred texts, only oral histories and parables of destruction. The books found among his priests are practical works of ghost binding, torture, necromancy, and demon summoning.

==**Shrines and Priests**==
The Black Spire stands tall in the hidden gnome city of Königsheim, and a small shrine to him, the Hidden Shrine of the Black Hand, rests in the king's palace in [[Blood Kingdom|Morgau]], where Xanthus the Flenser keeps its altar stained with blood and offers spies, assassins, and thieves to those who need them. Most of his temples are hidden, and those who find them discover that the priests are all too happy to hammer in visitors' heads on the altar. Lo'dain the Lithe, a son of the Master of Demon Mountain, is counted a great follower, most often found in [[Vidim]] and along the Nieder Straits in a longboat rowed by a zombie crew, and so is Black Mother Yeva among the [[Khazzaki]]. By far his greatest priest is Sablehorns, the Metropolitan of Chernobog among the gnomes of [[Niemheim]].

==**Masks**==
Some say every dark god is his mask, since all evil and corruption are his. Others, less bombastic, say [[Sarastra]] is his elven mask, the [[White Goddess]] his mask among the white raiders and goblins, and [[Loki]] his mask in the [[Northlands]]. Some even say he is a sinister brother of [[Thor]] and [[Perun]], whose hammer strikes down the just and the righteous.

==**Other Faiths**==
Chernobog has few divine allies and few friends. [[Khors]], Perun, Thor, and [[Lada]] are his unremitting enemies, but some claim [[Volund]] made his hammer and [[Hecate]] taught him to take the forms of bear and wolf, raven and vulture. He is on reasonably good terms with [[Boreas (North Wind)|Boreas]].

==**What Chernobog Demands**==
Be strong, fight to take what you want, and never show mercy, pity, or forgiveness. The weak are less than you and should serve you, and as you are less than Chernobog, you must submit and serve him. Make blood sacrifices as often as you can. Human sacrifices are best, followed by black animals.
