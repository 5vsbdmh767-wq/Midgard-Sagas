*Mother of Madness, Lady of the White Bat,*
*Maker of the Black Road, Voice of the Void,*
*Patron of the [[Derro]]*

**Domains:** Death, Knowledge, Light (reversed)
**Alignment:** Chaotic Evil
**Favored Weapon:** Battleaxe

Addrikah looks like a tiny, ancient derro woman who never stops babbling. Her voice is said to spill wisdom, but her hymns are nonsense and she never answers a question straight. The derro worship her as the one who freed them from slavery by giving them madness, and she is their go-between with stranger, more alien powers. To everyone else she simply seems insane. For all her age, she can crush a skull in her little hands, drink what's inside, and turn the shriveled brain into gems and *ioun stones*.

==**Worshipers**==
Derro and duergar, and dwarves and humans who have lost their minds. Some death knights and liches find her taste for blood magic agreeable.

==**Symbols and Books**==
A white bat. She has no holy books.

==**Shrines and Priests**==
She has no shrines on the surface. Below ground, her shrines are crusted with old blood, and the big ones are deep in bat guano, home to huge colonies of bats.

==**Masks**==
Some say she is a mask of the [[Black Goat|Goat of the Woods]]. Most think she is a fallen demon lord.

==**Other Faiths**==
She gets along with [[Mammon]], the Goat of the Woods, and the [[White Goddess]], and is rumored to have had a long affair with [[Loki]]. Everyone else is her enemy, especially [[Yarila & Porevit|Freyr and Freyja]], Khors, and [[Thoth-Hermes]]. [[Rava]] finds her works abominable.

==**What Addrikah Demands**==
Endless madness and chaos. Her followers baptize their children into her cult, kidnap and sacrifice in her name, and nothing pleases her more than driving a dwarf mad.

==**Beyond Midgard**==
In the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]], derro outposts always have a shrine to her, and she has more on Martyr Mountain in the Deep Void and scattered across the Astral Sea. Void angels and the [[Great Chantry of the Voidmother]] pay her honor.
