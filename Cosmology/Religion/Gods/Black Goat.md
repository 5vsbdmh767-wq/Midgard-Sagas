*Queen of Decadence, Whisperer in the Woods,*
*the Wicked One, Patron of the [[Goblins]], Piper*
*of the Midnight Sun, Father of Mayhem*

**Domains:** Life, War
**Alignment:** Chaotic
**Favored Weapons:** Dagger, whip

The Goat of the Woods was once Bacchana, the great goddess of the old magocracies of the West, a goddess of night, wealth, fertility, and power who wore a mask you could see straight through. Her priests gave her people license for every kind of excess. When the mages of those lands began summoning things from beyond the Void, her church cheered them on and blessed the wars that followed. All that spending of magic, wealth, and prayer delighted her, but the atrocities of the Mage Wars poisoned her as surely as they poisoned the land. She looked toward the stars where the Ancient Ones came from, and her mind and body broke.

Now she appears either as a galloping, tentacled crab-thing covered in growths, or, when she doesn't want to drive her worshippers mad on the spot, as a black-skinned satyr with great curving horns, dressed in a tunic of stars. She is called "she," but she is as much male as female.

==**Worshipers**==
The hungry for power, the broken, and the frightened, and anyone about to try something dangerous. The goblins and giants of the [[Western Wastes]] turn to her in desperation, and outcasts hear her voice at night. Lawful mages everywhere have banned her worship and burned her groves, but she is a great patron of witches and oracles.

==**Symbols and Books**==
Five stalks of golden wheat in a pentagram, and star-shaped things like starfish and star-shaped knives. Her followers know each other by a hand sign: middle fingers folded over the thumb, pinky and forefinger raised. Fragments of her litany survive in the banned *Viridian Codex*.

==**Shrines and Priests**==
She was worshipped in groves of rowan and white oak, and only the most remote and dangerous survived the burnings, the greatest being the Mother's Grove in the Ghostlight Forest. Many cities still keep a small hidden shrine. Her priests, the speakers, rave to anyone who will listen, break laws and taboos as a duty, and speak up for the downtrodden and the exiled, and many count dressing as another gender, the more flamboyantly the better, an act of devotion. The current grand speaker is the goblin Tivishta Trikinta, who wanders the Wastes and the Ghostlight Forest and sometimes strays into the Grand Duchy or the [[Seven Cities]]. Her lost holy teachings were once stamped on rings worn by her clergy, and now the faithful listen for her whispers on the wind.

==**Masks**==
In the old magocracies she was sister to [[Marena]] and [[Sarastra]], or a mask of them, the three Dark Sisters who opposed [[Ceres]], [[Hecate]], and [[Lada]]. Among the giants she is called Emari. Now [[Loki]], [[Ninkash]], and [[Vardesain]] are whispered to be her masks.

==**Other Faiths**==
She is ally, lover, and betrayer to any god who comes near her. Khors, Aten, Sarastra, and [[Hecate]] are her enemies, though she hardly notices them. The only things she truly hates are the Ancient Ones of the Wastes.

==**What the Goat Demands**==
Gain power and feed your appetites. Stretch your mind by any means, and magic above all. Madness is a blessing. Break the law, tear down hierarchies, and welcome change.

==**Beyond Midgard**==
Across the worlds of the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]] she is called the Black Goat, and most of her speakers there are goblin women.

==**In the Shadow Realm**==
In the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]] the Goat of the Woods is one of the five great dark gods, and both goblins and giants turn to her in desperation. The selang are often her heralds and most zealous priests. Her grand speaker there is the shadow goblin Indoo Langomall, who wanders the wastes and forests with a band of reveling, mud-stained goblins and humans and somehow always finds enough to eat and shelter for the winter; some say the cult has a huge cavern shrine somewhere in the mountains near [[Soriglass]]. She is among the great gods of [[Oshragora]]. In shadow she is said to be the sister or a mask of [[Gytellisor]], and a few call her the evil face of [[Santerr Marossa]], a vile heresy to Marossa's faithful. The faiths of [[Kupkoresh]], [[Anu-Akma]], [[Sarastra]], and [[Hecate]] are her committed enemies, and [[Bengta]] counts her and [[Alquam]] her worst foes. She alone despises the [[The Old Roads|Old Roads]], and her goblins and chaos cultists steal and deface their milestones. Of all things, she seeks only to hear the song of the Void.
