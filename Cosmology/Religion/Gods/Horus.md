*Sky Lord, Master of the Sun, the Moon, and the Heavens, the Desert Falcon, Prince of Princes, the Majestic One, the Chieftain, the Vigilant, the True King, Patron of Nuria Natal*

**Domains:** Justice, Light, Tempest
**Alignment:** Lawful
**Favored Weapon:** Khopesh

For long ages, as long as the sun and moon crossed the heavens, Horus was lord of the sky and the righteous god-emperor of the South. In time he grew old and tired, and the upstart [[Aten]] seized his mantle and cast him down. The people mourned and turned their eyes to the blinding glory of the sun. But Aten was soon lost in his vanities, and dark things stirred in that time of neglect. Dragons rose in the east. A weakened Horus wandered the desert, the realm of his old rival Set, who had been defeated by the demon serpent Apophis. Horus rescued Set's corpse from defilement and performed the proper funeral rites, and was rewarded with rule over Set's kingdom. Engulfed by the fiery desert, he rose renewed from his ashes.

Reborn as a young warrior chieftain, hawk-headed or with the face of a handsome prince, Horus works to set the world back in order. Demons must be slain and usurpers put in their place. By bold deeds he will reclaim his rightful place, reunite the fractured cities of [[Nuria Natal]], and drive the dragon gods back into the East they came from.

==**Worshipers**==
The [[Nurians]], their Sarklah cousins, and the [[Tamasheq]] nomads of the Crescent all follow Horus. They are people of action who lead their tribes through the hardships of desert life. In the old delta cities his popularity has hardly waned, though other gods draw louder crowds. His most fanatical believers are the heruti, the avian folk known elsewhere as huginn or [[Ravenfolk|ravenfolk]], wandering desert mystics, sword masters, and bearers of his fury and justice.

==**Symbols and Books**==
Falcons are his sacred animals, representatives, and messengers. The Crown of Nuria Natal is also his symbol, but his greatest is the Eye of Horus, a potent ward against evil in righteous hands. The *Code of Horus*, set down by King Hawk in the elder Nurian script, holds his laws and precepts. Nuria Natal was founded on it millennia ago, and it still governs the River Kingdom. The *Chronicles of Kings and Wanderers* holds popular tales of heroes he favored, which his priests use to teach children and converts.

==**Shrines and Priests**==
Besides the many ancient temples of the delta cities, his best-known shrines are the Traveling Temples, mobile shrines that go with caravans of traders, pilgrims, or performers, whose priests serve as guards, healers, and guides. The Soaring Shrines stand on high peaks, desert outcrops, and great pyramids, marking the borders of old Nuria Natal. His temples are large and airy, with tall columns and huge open spaces, built into cliffs or on mountains under the open sky. The most famous, Khepri Khnum, stands on a peak of the Chelamite range in the [[Dominion of the Wind Lords]], home to his army of ten thousand birds and served by beautiful harpy priests with tremendous plumage. In the [[Southlands]], the priest and storyteller Old Khenses travels from town to town telling the *Chronicles of Kings and Wanderers*, and the Wingless One leads holy slayers of the falcon god's foes.

==**Masks**==
Khors may be his avatar in lands north of Nuria. Bolder theologians claim Aten is a mask of Horus, devised to test his faithful and root out hidden evils. Nomads of the steppes call him the Skyfather and hunt with his hawks on the plains. In [[Grand Duchy of Dornig|Dornig]] and the [[Seven Cities]], especially [[Valera]], he is equated with the Imperial Eagle and sometimes worshiped under his own name as an exotic cult. In the [[Northlands]] he is thought a mask of [[Wotan]], or else called [[Tyr]]. Azuran's followers see a strong link between him and the Southern Wind. Some say [[Mavros]] is his brother.

==**Other Faiths**==
Horus is the kindly chief of the southern pantheon, with deep ties to each of its gods. Aten the False is another matter: their rivalry stokes open hostility among their faithful, and try as he might, Aten cannot absorb Horus in the cities, and chasing his wanderers into the desert is suicide. As king, Horus welcomes foreign gods who come in peace, among them [[Charun]], Ceres, and [[Rava]]. Strange gods who come to invade are met with holy steel and his divine eye. His greatest enemies are the demon Apophis and his followers, and the demon-dragon [[Baal-Hotep|Baal]], would-be conqueror of Nuria Natal.

==**What Horus Demands**==
The *Code of Horus* separates the noble nomad from the uncouth barbarian. Obey it always. Battle and slay ancient abominations and their cults. Carry yourself upright, whether great king or lowly peasant. Treat others with hospitality and honor. Kill the enemies who threaten your homeland and enslave their kin. Be princes among men and Horus will watch over you.
