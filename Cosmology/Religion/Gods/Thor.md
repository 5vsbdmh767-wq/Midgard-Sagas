*The Thunderer, God of Lightning, War, and Strength, Slayer of Monsters and Protector of Humankind, Husband of Sif, Patron of the [[Northlands]] [[Dwarves]] and Reavers*

**Domains:** Tempest, War
**Alignment:** Chaotic
**Favored Weapon:** Warhammer

Wotan's eldest and boldest son, Thor is the champion of humans and dwarves. Riding out in search of glory in a chariot drawn by tireless celestial goats, hammer at his side, he is the adventurer of Asgard and the eternal foe of giants, trolls, and other monsters. He is usually shown as a big man with a wild red mane and beard, full of daring courage and none too bright. Countless godlings and ancient spirits fell beneath his hammer, and Northern monsters, giants above all, still fear and hate him.

The dwarves are wary of Thor, for though he made them alongside [[Volund]], he also smote their halls when they dared favor the smith god over him. Reaver dwarves call on him both as their maker and for vengeance and retribution.

For the Northlands view of Thor, with his hall, hammer, rune, and northern temples, see the "In the North" section of [[Perun]].

==**Worshipers**==
Reaver dwarves and [[Northlanders]] worship Thor as the god of war and raiding, thunder and glory. As a great defender of the North and a good husband to his divine wife [[Sif]], he is also widely revered by elders, wives, and villagers, not only by those who go a-viking.

==**Symbols and Books**==
His hammer is the sign of strength and thunder, fertility, and protection from the supernatural. Everyone from raiders to farmers makes offerings to Thor. Warriors look to him for strength and courage, and peaceful folk hang his hammer to ward off trolls and keep danger from their doors. Its touch blesses newborns with strength, seals marriage vows, and drives back the undead. He has no sacred books, but his songs and chants are widely known.

==**Shrines and Priests**==
The greatest shrines of Thor are in [[The Ironcrags]] and the North, especially in Wintersheim, [[Tanserhall]], and the Shrine of Golden Hammers in [[Thrumagard|Thunder Mountain]]. His greatest priest is Ulfrich Thorson, a dwarf of the Order of the Thunderer in Thunder Mountain.

==**Masks**==
Thor, [[Perun]], and [[Mavros]] are all related, as brothers or as masks. Their worshipers are on friendly terms, though they argue over which is the god's true face. In [[Grand Duchy of Dornig|Dornig]] he is called Donar. Some say Valeresh is his elven mask.

==**Other Faiths**==
Thor is the eternal enemy of [[Chernobog]], [[Vardesain]], [[Boreas (North Wind)|Boreas]], and all the dark gods of the giants and the south. His allies are Sif, [[Wotan]], and [[Lada|Baldur]]. [[Loki]] is his half-brother, and Thor finds him vexing as often as not. He and Perun are mortal enemies of the World Serpent, [[Veles]].

==**What Thor Demands**==
Thor's worshipers invoke him with deeds, not words. They shout his name in battles and duels and as a challenge to monsters in the wild. His temples are battlefields old and new, where worshipers seal oaths of brotherhood with runes of painted blood and sacrifices of bears and oxen. Followers must match his deeds of strength and bravery, never fearing death or dodging an honorable challenge. To fall bravely in battle is the perfect end. The Thunderer has little love for those who die a "straw death," of sickness or old age on a straw mattress instead of in the iron and blood of battle.
