*God of Comets, Omens, and the Heavens*

**Domains:** Light, Trickery
**Favored Weapon:** Dagger

Lithe, red-haired, and cheerful, Zenthar is the quick younger brother of [[Sabateus]], fast to think and fast to act. He advises his followers through signs in the sky: comets, flights of birds, and the shapes of clouds. The ratatosk call him the cloud messenger. On the Dreamer's Islands he protects the [[Servants of the Dreaming God|Dreamers]] and helps them make sense of other gods' visions. He is a friend to [[Rava]] and [[Mavros|Herak-Mavros]], but he has so few followers that scribes often list him as a saint rather than a god.
