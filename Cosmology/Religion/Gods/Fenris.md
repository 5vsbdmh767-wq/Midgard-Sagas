*The Wolf of the Apocalypse, Herald of Ragnarok*

**Domains:** War
**Favored Weapon:** Scimitar

Fenris is the giant wolf, son of [[Loki]], who will break free at the end of the world and devour the gods. To most people he is a monster, not a god, but he is a dark god still growing, a hungry thing of the Void. Giants, ogres, orcs, and trolls see him differently: strong, fast, a clever tracker, deadly to his enemies and loyal to his friends, and the promise of a final victory over the valkyries, dwarves, and humans who keep them down. [[Goblins]], trolls, and trollkin wear amulets of his wolf's head.

==**Worshipers**==
Hunters, giants, trolls, and void cultists.

==**Other Faiths**==
[[Nidhogg]] calls him friend. Some name him, as Fenris-Hati, among the masks of [[Vardesain]].

==**In the North**==
In the [[Northlands]] he is the Father of Wolves, Devourer of the Sun and Moon, and the Hand-Snatcher. Bound by the Aesir, he is anchored to his prison by a primeval earth elemental called Thviti. For biting off [[Tyr]]'s hand while straining at his fetters, the gods put him into a supernatural sleep in a hidden cavern. He is destined to devour [[Wotan]] at Ragnarok. See [[Gods of the North]].
