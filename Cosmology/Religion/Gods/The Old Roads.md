*Gods of the Roads and Bindings, Guardians of Hecate,*
*Holy Keepers of the Realm, Lords and Ladies of the Gates and Ways*

**Favored Weapon:** Staff

The Old Roads are gods of shadow, born in shadow and binding its land together. They are believed to be the ascended spirits of ancient road wardens and arcanists such as Hargen the Builder, and they are usually shown in pairs or in groups of three. They guide travelers, help the lost, and protect merchants, pilgrims, and shepherds. See [[Shadow Roads]].

## Worshipers

Every traveler in the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]] reveres the Old Roads and offers them at least a few words, a twist of woven grass, or some other small token before setting out. Merchants, soldiers, and pilgrims all know that ignoring them is foolhardy.

## Symbols and Books

Their most common symbols are milestones carved with runes or signs, though the details vary from place to place: some Old Roads are marked with an oak leaf, others with a ram's head or a green ring. They have no known holy books.

## Shrines and Priests

Every waymark and roadside shrine along the highways, byways, and shadow roads matters to the Old Roads. They are gods of path and journey who can't stay in one place for long, and their priests are the same, always wandering. They tend to be elderly men and women, or sometimes ancient shadow fey or goblins, who beg alms from other travelers, keep the roads in repair, help fellow travelers, round up strays, heal the sick, and report to the road wardens when something is wrong. Most go by use-names such as Old Bridge, Old Stone, or Old Fence, though their worshipers often seek out the true names of the gods and their famous priests, the better to understand them, or to control them.

## Masks

No other gods seem to be masks of the Old Roads, though some believe these godlings are tiny avatars of [[Umbeserno]] or [[Hecate]].

## Other Faiths

Hecate and the Old Roads seem well acquainted. Some say they are her servants and her eyes, spreading the land she raised from formless shadow long ago. Even some dark gods, such as [[Vardesain]], respect the Old Roads and extend them with new paths and destinations. Only the [[Black Goat|Black Goat of the Woods]] is known to despise them, and her goblins and chaos cultists often steal or deface their milestones.

## What the Old Roads Demand

Travel swiftly and keep the roads clear of brush and timber. Offer something to the roads at the start of a journey and drink to their health at the end. Never steal or deface a milestone. Hunt down those who cut or harm the roads. Always help the lost, the injured, and the starving.
