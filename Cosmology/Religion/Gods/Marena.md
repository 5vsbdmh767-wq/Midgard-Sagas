*The Red Goddess of Winter, Lust, Sickness,*
*and Death, the Blood Maiden, the Winter Maiden,*
*Patron of the Greater Duchy of Morgau*

**Domains:** Death, Tempest
**Alignment:** Evil
**Favored Weapons:** Spear, whip

Marena is everything people fear about the flesh: that it fails, that lust can wreck a good life, and that plague can empty a village in a season. She is feared all across the [[The Crossroads|Crossroads]], and in the North she is called Mara. Her cults do best in secret, in small villages when times are bad, and in the cellars and back rooms of the poorer quarters of the great cities. Her face is beautiful and cold at once, and anyone who sees it and lives is marked by it, with white hair, a wine-red birthmark, or haunted silver eyes. Her faithful believe strict worship buys real power, and in Morgau her orders include hermits, flagellants, and orgiasts.

North of the River Argent she is worshipped openly. She is patron of the [[Blood Kingdom]], of vampires and ghouls, of prostitutes, and of the Ghost Knights, and the vampires are raising new temples to her across conquered [[Krakovar]]. On the [[Rothenian Plain]] she is honored more quietly as the Winter Maiden, and hidden sects of hers turn up all over the Crossroads and the [[Seven Cities]]. Lately it is whispered that her priestesses have opened secret temples in [[Zobeck]] itself.

==**Worshipers**==
Vampires, ghouls, the vengeful, and the lustful, along with the desperate and anyone who wants to rise in the Blood Kingdom. Some kobolds worship her out of fear. Her symbols are red garments and skulls stained with ochre or rust.

==**Symbols and Books**==
Her books are banned nearly everywhere as manuals of corruption and disease. The best known are the *Book of Holy Lust*, the *Song of Blood & Winter*, and the *Scarlet Commandments*.

==**Shrines and Priests**==
Her greatest public shrine is the Aprostala temple in the Barony of Doresh, a place of pilgrimage and daily sacrifice. Every village of any size in Morgau, Krakovar, and Doresh has at least a small bloodstained altar stone, and her name is spoken at every birth, funeral, and battle. Her priesthood, the Red Sisterhood, handles justice, law, and punishment in the Blood Kingdom, and its sentences are harsh and public: floggings, quarterings, brandings, and beheadings. Her orders in Morgau include anchorites, flagellants, and orgiasts, and her faith is one of the few ways for the living to rise in the Blood Kingdom. Her high priestess, Lileshka of the Chalice, rarely executes followers or visitors, but seduces pilgrims when the goddess demands it. Those who accept are granted an audience to ask whatever they wish, and those who refuse are handed to harsher priests for chastisement. She keeps the respect of many of the realm's elders, gives generous offerings, always demands favors in return, and chooses her conquests carefully. Those who think her weak or easily led soon learn otherwise. Everywhere else, her worship is hidden.

==**Masks**==
Some call [[Hecate]], [[Sarastra]], and the [[Black Goat|Goat of the Woods]] her dark sisters, or her masks. Others think [[Vardesain]] is closer to her than any of them.

==**Other Faiths**==
Marena is wife to [[Mavros]] and on good terms with his priests, and [[Boreas (North Wind)|Boreas]] is called her son in the North. The Goat of the Woods is her ally, and some suspect a secret pact with Vardesain. Her enemies include [[Rava]], Aten, Khors, [[Lada]], [[Sif]], [[Ninkash]], and [[Wotan]], and Lada most of all.

==**What Marena Demands**==
Kill her enemies, especially the followers of Lada. Keep her rites of seduction, blood sacrifice, and the scourge. Her worship is bitter, but she hands out strength and magic more readily than most gods, even to those outside her priesthood. Her faithful who reach the age of fifty must make a pilgrimage to Morgau.

==**Beyond Midgard**==
Travelers of the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]] know Marena on many worlds. She is popular on shadowed worlds and among Void cultists, and she is patron of the [[Brothers of the Last Cathedral]].
