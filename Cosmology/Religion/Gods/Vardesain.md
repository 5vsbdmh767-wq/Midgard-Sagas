*Ghoul-God of the Bottomless Maw, Lord of*
*Unending Thirst, the Hunger God, the Unsated*
*God, Devourer of Souls, Patron of the Darakhul*

**Domains:** Death, Life, Light (reversed)
**Alignment:** Neutral
**Favored Weapon:** Handaxe

Everything that lives must eat, and Vardesain built a faith on that one fact, from simple gluttony all the way to cannibal feasts. To his faithful, food is a sacrament that builds health and righteous strength, which makes them both great gourmets and the least picky eaters in the world. His sacrifices are always edible, and sometimes still alive. He takes the forms of scavengers and predators, white wolves, ghouls, darakhul, and huge undead, and in every vision of him he is eating something.

==**Worshipers**==
The hungry and the ambitious: power-hungry humans and monsters, ghouls, vampires, darakhul, and other undead. He is the great god of the [[The Darakhul Imperium|Ghoul Imperium]].

==**Symbols and Books**==
His holy beast is the purple worm, which eats the offerings in his temples, shown on vestments as a purple snake swallowing its tail or as open jaws. His priests keep bats, snakes, and oozes as living symbols of hunger. His holiest text is *the Feast*, a cycle of cave paintings in his shrine in Darakhan, the White City. The empire runs on the *Feeding Laws*, written on scrolls of skin, which are broken constantly.

==**Shrines and Priests**==
His red and white shrine in Darakhan is his most famous temple. His current high priest is the darakhul Cimbrai, one of the rare few whose hunger Vardesain has consumed as a blessing, who works to topple Anu-Akma's church in the Ghoul Empire. Ancient statues of him as the Lord of Unending Thirst, holding an upturned cup, stand at oases in the [[Southlands]]. His priests are mostly ghouls, gnolls, and wights, and they work to bring down the gods of light and healing, sometimes literally, by setting the dead to dig under their temples.

==**Masks**==
The [[Black Goat|Goat of the Woods]], [[The Hunter|the Hunter]], Hel, [[Fenris|Fenris-Hati]], and [[Mavros]] have all been suggested, none very likely. [[Chernobog]] and [[Charun]] are more associated with his underworld and his power over the undead, and a few think the Hunger God is a mask of [[Veles]]. His priests say he devoured an older god called Mordiggian, whose name still sometimes appears in their liturgy. Because the two gods seem indifferent to their priests' rivalry, some suspect Vardesain is the darker side of [[Anu-Akma]].

==**Other Faiths**==
Chernobog, the Goat of the Woods, and the Hunter are loose allies, and some suspect a pact with [[Marena]]. [[Lada]], Khors, and [[Perun]] despise him. His priests are bitter rivals of [[Anu-Akma]]'s, though the gods themselves seem not to care.

==**What Vardesain Demands**==
Show your strength. Hunt what you eat, though scavenging is no shame. Fast when you need clarity, and eat your enemies' hearts.

==**In the Shadow Realm**==
Vardesain's rule in the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]] is fairly recent, brought by the darakhul and spread with fervor. He is the great god of the [[Twilight Empire]], where *the Feast* has been copied in Zhurakh with new emphasis on the righteous rule of the emperor. His high priest there is His Carmine Eminence and High Patriarch of Zhurakh, Devros Perghallen, one of the rare few whose hunger Vardesain has consumed as a blessing, who preaches the power of devouring others and denounces [[Bengta]] and [[Kupkoresh]]. His other great shadow temples are the cathedral of Evernight and the Great Vault in [[Soriglass]], home of the Bone Cloak of Vardesain. Shrines to [[Santerr Illosi]] stand inside his temples, and her followers are his close allies. In shadow, [[Charun]], the [[Black Goat|Goat of the Woods]], and [[The Hunter|the Hunter]] are his loose allies, [[Alquam]] respects him, and he even respects and extends the [[The Old Roads|Old Roads]]. Lada, Kupkoresh, and Bengta despise him and destroy his temples whenever they can.
