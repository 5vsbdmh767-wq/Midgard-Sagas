*[[Minotaurs|Minotaur]] God of the Hunt, Raids, and Explorers*

**Domains:** Knowledge, War
**Favored Weapon:** Mace

Knodor is an old minotaur god whose power has faded over the years, though he still has followers in the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]] and the [[Old Ring]]. These days he is pictured as often as a drunk or a spirit of revelry as a bold scout or tough soldier. Those who travel the maze know his wisdom can save them. He is a guide to the lost and the stranger who turns up at your campfire. Minotaurs and soldiers worship him, and [[Sif]] calls him her hunting brother.
