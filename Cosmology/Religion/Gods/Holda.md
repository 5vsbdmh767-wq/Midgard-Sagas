*Goddess of Hearth and Seasons, Queen of the [[Arbonesse]], Mother of Yarila and Porevit, Keeper of Summer and Winter, Goddess of Reaping and Spinning, Keeper of the True Springs, Harvest Goddess*

**Domains:** Life, Light, Tempest
**Alignment:** Neutral Good
**Favored Weapon:** Staff

The gods of the elves are scattered and failing, their temples quarried for stone, their priests few and ignored, though their remaining followers are often very powerful. In recent years their shrines seem to have grown a little stronger, and [[Yarila & Porevit]] and Holda remain strong in [[Grand Duchy of Dornig|Dornig]].

Holda, the mother goddess of the elves, rules the seasons, the hearth, and the harvest. All the world was once hers, and her children, Yarila and Porevit, watched over its beasts and growing things. As goddess of home and seasons, her followers made the elven cloth and cloaks. That tradition faded when her priesthood became human and elfmarked, but her followers still weave astonishingly colorful, silky garments, often shot with threads of gold and mithral. Wind, wave, and thunder are hers to command, and so are light and dark. Her manner is always gentle, but her standards for craft, creation, and children are very high, and her followers see great sin in sloth and idleness.

==**Worshipers**==
Her worshipers are quiet but powerful, the opposite of [[Baccholon]]'s revels and Valeresh's boasting and honor. Most are older men and women in positions of quiet power: guild leaders, mayors, great landholders, ley line wielders, and scholars of steady temper. They rarely make a fuss, but when they do, their words carry great weight.

==**Symbols and Books**==
Her only holy book is the *Book of Seasons*, which may exist in many versions, since no two copies are exactly alike. Many trace ley lines and describe both everyday life and great magic, ley wells and roads long since turned to dust. Her symbol is a tree bare on one side and in full leaf and flower on the other.

==**Shrines and Priests**==
Her temples are built on or beside ley wells and springs, the places where trickles of ley line energy first appear.

==**Masks**==
Holda is often thought an ancient form of [[Lada]] or [[Khors]], and sometimes not the mother of Yarila and Porevit but the same goddess, made one.

==**Other Faiths**==
Her followers and [[Sarastra]]'s are almost always at odds, except when the elves and their people are threatened. Followers of Yarila and Porevit generally defer to Mother Holda. Baccholon finds her suffocating. She and [[Valeresh]] were always on good terms.

==**What Holda Demands**==
Defend your home, and comfort the weak and the desperate. Shelter children, watch the seasons, and know when the time has come to join the ancestors. Respect your elders, and always keep your word, above all to those smaller and younger than you.
