*Goddess of Bright Pain, the Sun's Queen,*
*the Pale Matriarch of Blood and Strength,*
*Patron Goddess of the [[Orcs]] and Ogres*

**Domains:** Death, Light (reversed), War
**Alignment:** Evil
**Favored Weapons:** Greatclub, temple sword

The White Goddess was born pale-skinned and red-eyed. When she climbed out of her people's first home under the skin of the world, the sun god Khors blazed at her to drive her back. She stared him down and chased him until he hid beyond the horizon, crushing his sun-kissed peoples as she went and armoring herself in their bones. Every morning he sneaks back, and every night she and her faithful chase him off again. She is a war goddess of harsh life and cruel sunlight, of the will to keep going and the rest that comes to those who've earned it. She appears as a huge albino orc with carved tusks and ember-red eyes, wearing bone armor, with a club in one hand and a sword in the other.

==**Worshipers**==
Orcs above all, now few and scattered in the deep places and far wilds. [[Goblins]], derro, ogres, and sometimes even kobolds and centaurs follow her too.

==**Symbols and Books**==
A splash of blood like a sun on a white field, a red skeleton, a tusked skull, or a black sun. Her only holy text is the *Sun's Eye*, a black ball carved with tortures, which her priests say was torn from the sun. A solar eclipse is the sun turning its empty socket on the world, and her people's holiest day for raiding.

==**Shrines and Priests**==
Her only altars are piles of bones left where her followers killed. Rumor tells of a Great Fane of Skulls somewhere in the Green Abyss of the [[Southlands]]. Her high priests are war chiefs. The current one is the goblin Jagger Ungligger of the [[White Mountain Marches]], though she has many priests in the Southlands as well.

==**Masks**==
Several Lords of Hell are said to be her masks, and the [[Black Goat|Goat of the Woods]] is sometimes rumored to be one. Enemies of the heretical church of [[Aten]] accuse the True Aten of being one of her masks too.

==**Other Faiths**==
She has no allies but the strange gods of the orcs. [[Mavros]] makes war on her followers wherever they appear, and Khors and Horus are her sworn enemies.

==**What She Demands**==
Kill your enemies. Don't flinch from pain or light. Take what you want from the weak, and pile high the bones of the fallen.
