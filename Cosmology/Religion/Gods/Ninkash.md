*Mother of Beer, Goddess of Merriment,*
*Patron of Brewers and Tavern [[Keepers of the Keys|Keepers]],*
*Matron Goddess of the Cantonal [[Dwarves]]*

**Domains:** Life, Nature
**Alignment:** Good
**Favored Weapon:** Mace

Ninkash is the goddess of ale, good company, and the joy of a full table. The [[Kariv]] brought her to the dwarves of the cantons long ago, and the dwarves took her to heart. She turned their stern old rites of brewing and drinking into something warmer and more frequent, though still serious. When a dwarf drinks until they fall asleep, the dwarves say they've "gone to visit Ninkash."

She usually shows herself as a glowing golden tankard floating in the air, always full. To her faithful she appears as a cheerful, barefoot dwarf woman in loose robes that shift from nut-brown to gold. She is always smiling. When she's displeased the smile only gets smaller.

==**Worshipers**==
Dwarves of the Free Cantons and the south, brewers, innkeepers, the common folk of [[Nuria Natal]], and some in [[Trombei]], Salzbach, [[The Mharoti Empire]], and places as far off as Khandiria and Sikkim. The Kariv call her Mother Ale. She stands for small, daily kindnesses: shared meals, hospitality, family, and questioning rules that keep people apart.

==**Symbols and Books**==
Her symbol is a rough golden Y called the baerra, meaning munificence. Her smile shows as a golden, rippling glow above altars and above dwarves singing her praises over their drinks, and even unseen she sends directions and visions of guidance, warning, or instruction. She has few texts and speaks mostly through dreams and drunken visions of golden tankards, rivers of ale, and noisy taverns.

==**Shrines and Priests**==
Her priests, the vaer, wear plain brown robes and carry two tankards, a tiny taster and a big belt-tankard. They brew holy ale, and some of their brews heal or let a drinker breathe underwater. Those who heard her voice from a tankard or altar and dared to answer are called kalath. Her clergy advise their communities, calming and soothing when needed but also warning against other influences, even the clergy of other gods. Within the vaer, men and women are equal and either may lead any rite or hold any office, though the devout say only dwarves can be true vaer, while respecting and working with the rare "Holy Ones," non-dwarves who have spoken with Ninkash. Her best-known priests today are Herma Heid, High Vaer of Kubourg in the Free Cantons; Ekibe of the Barley, her foremost voice in Nuria Natal; and Mother Hapesh of the Barrels, her best-known human priestess, who travels with the Kariv.

==**Masks**==
Some say [[Ceres]] is her human face and Ninkash the dwarven mask of the same goddess. In the North a few link her to [[Sif]] or even [[Perun]]. The oldest dwarves remember when elves and dwarves argued whether Ninkash or Baccho was greater. Clearly the dwarven goddess endured and triumphed, while [[Baccholon|Baccho]] proved a thin and watery god, a jape of Ninkash's bottomless mirth.

==**Other Faiths**==
[[Wotan]]'s priests look down on her, [[Loki]]'s think her a fool and sometimes a useful weapon, Baccho's few followers call hers a "gutter faith" of commoners who can't write proper poetry, and [[Marena]]'s followers call her faith a pack of drunks who can't organize anything. Few others have a bad word for her. Most gods find her faith useful or at least harmless, and [[Lada]], [[Volund|Volund-Svarog]], and even the strange priests of [[Seggotan]] and [[Hecate]] embrace her. Her closest ally is Ceres of the Harvest, and southern legend calls them sisters.

==**What Ninkash Demands**==
Get or make your own tankard and drink ale from it every day. Learn to brew before you marry. Ale offerings are welcome at her altars, and her clergy must learn brewing and alchemy too. A dwarf is not a true dwarf until they face all their secret fears, wants, and delights. Her ales help worshipers set aside the armor of civility, reserve, and secrecy for a while and see more clearly. Ordinary ale is a road to truth, and her holy ale is the road to the mysteries of reality.

==**Beyond Midgard**==
In the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]] she is beloved of the [[Servants of the Dreaming God|Dreamers]], who call her a handmaiden of [[Cartokk]], the Dreaming God.
