Covering a tenth of the plane of gears is a vast desert, an inhospitable wasteland of oxidized metal fragments, dust, and grit. At its heart, imprisoned titans labor to hollow out a continent-sized plateau of oxidized steel as punishment for losing the Vanir War. A clan of reaver dwarves guard the prisoners and battle the rust drakes and rust monsters who inhabit the desert. Above them all, an infinitely long crane lifts defective or worn out parts from the rest of [[Ravatet]], dropping them onto the desert without warning, sending shockwaves across the wasteland.
