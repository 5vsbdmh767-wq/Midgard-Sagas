The wondrous crystal pyramids and palaces of [[Anu-Akma]]’s court rise like a shining oasis in the blood desert. However, looks can be deceiving. The crystal necropolis is not a place for the living. Within the guarded walls is a demi-plane within [[Evermaw]] where the honored servants of Anu-Akma find their final reward. The mummies and liches of the Assembly of the Worthy manage the day to-day operation of the god’s court while the undead gnolls of Akma’s Fangs guard against any intrusions. At the heart of the necropolis, a book written on pages of gold holds the secret to eternal life.
