A honeycomb of niches pierces this miles-wide, 500-foot-tall cliff, each small chamber gathering memories from the undead of [[Evermaw]]. While the orange-and white stone is soothing to the undead, any living creature who enters must succeed at a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw or suffer 2d12 psychic damage and become immobile for 1 hour. The millions of voices echoing from the gathered memories whisper forgotten and forbidden knowledge.
