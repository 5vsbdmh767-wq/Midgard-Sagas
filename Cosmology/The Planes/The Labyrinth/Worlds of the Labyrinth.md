[[The Labyrinth]] touches the Ten Thousand Worlds, but most travel centers on about a dozen core worlds and the hundred or so closest to them. Beyond those, the worlds get stranger the farther out you go.

##### Armillary
The mechadrons lost their first world to the Void long ago and built a new one from a single living gear they saved. Armillary is four huge metal rings turning around an arcane sun, with no night, brass trains that run exactly on time, and the Great Forge where mechadrons are built and repaired. They are hungry for metal and coal to build a Fifth Ring, part of a grand Plan only their four leaders, the Quadrum Conclave, fully understand. The [[Keepers of the Keys|Keepers]] think the Plan is meant to seal away the Void for good. The Old Ring mostly sees mechadrons cutting down its forests.

##### Celestia
The heavens: somewhere between seven and seventy-seven worlds of law and mercy, guarded by angels and untouched by the Void. Everyone enters through the Celestial Arch at the end of a sunlit crystal highway, and the Arch burns away void taint, painfully, from anyone who passes under it. Beyond are [[Ravatet]], the clockwork heaven of [[Rava]]; the Shining Heaven of [[Solana]], a city of gold domes on floating clouds; and [[Taivas]], lowest of the Seven Heavens (see [[The Infinite Halls]]). From Midgard, the Portal of the Heavens in [[Ishadia]] leads there, but only in dire need.

##### Coldforge
A world of ice and empty cities on the edge of falling to the Void. Its stone dwarves, the Dwellers, dug too deep and mined a reddish-gold ore that turned out to be the body of a sleeping earth primordial. It woke, the mines collapsed, and the ore drove the surviving dwarves into a murderous rage. Undead frost knights now guard the ruins so nothing can live there again, and the Concord and the Keepers both have small, embattled camps there, digging for the Dwellers' lost weapons and lore. The cold freezes skin in minutes and makes metal brittle.

##### Dreamer's Islands
![[Map - Dreamer's Islands.webp]]
*Map from the Labyrinth Worldbook.*

Warm jungle islands on the shore of the Astral Sea, full of beaches, ruined pyramids, and unsettling dreams. New worlds are born in the Astral Sea nearby, and the [[Servants of the Dreaming God]] race there in fast ships to be the first to set foot on each one. Devils from Brimstone Tower race them. Revelport is the Dreamers' rowdy harbor town, and Delinos holds [[Cartokk]]'s greatest temple.

##### Greater Hell of Tyver-Sarok
Part core world and part Hell, Tyver-Sarok sits just outside [[The Eleven Hells]] and is where the factions come to parley with devils. Invited guests ride in carriages to gilded palaces and are wined and dined by goat-headed diplomats. Everyone else faces a hedge maze of poison thorns, bribe-hungry devils, and a queue that never seems to get shorter. The real ruler is Countess Levosu, a red-winged fallen angel and charming ambassador. Nobody trusts her, and everybody deals with her.

##### Lands of Parsantium
A world named for its great free city, which straddles a strait between two continents. It is a melting pot of chariot races, criminal gangs, ancient dungeons, and schemers, and its many cats know the catslide alleys into the Labyrinth.

##### Midgard
To the rest of the Labyrinth, Midgard is a flat world ringed by the serpent Veles, full of ley lines, shadow roads, masked gods, and fallen empires. It has more ways into the Labyrinth than most: a dozen World Trees, the minotaur mazes of Cindass, [[Capleon]], and ruined [[Roshgazi]], the Red Portals of [[Nuria Natal]], catslide alleys, and the Portal of the Heavens. Its magocracies, and others since, have opened doors to the Void more than once. Travelers know of [[Zobeck]] and its [[Arcane Collegium]], and of [[The Margreve|the Old Margreve]], a forest as old and as alive as any in the Old Ring.

##### The Old Ring
![[Map - Oakroot.webp]]
*Map from the Labyrinth Worldbook.*

Eleven ancient forest worlds, each named for the grove where its World Tree first grew: Ancient Alder, Autumn Ash, Maple Leaf, Oakroot, Redwood Haven, Rowanheart, Seven Pines, Summer Birch, Twin Hawthorns, Twisted Cedar, and Willow Shore. Oakroot's Darach Forest is as aware as the Margreve, and people who live there for generations grow root-like feet and bark-like skin. Seven Pines is cold, giant country, split between two quarrelling jotun kingdoms. Twin Hawthorns holds the druid college where the Great [[Druid]] teaches. These are the homes of the Elders (see [[Factions of the Labyrinth]]).

##### The Shadow Realm
The realm of the shadow fey, made by the goddess of moon and magic and ruled by [[Sarastra]], the Queen of Night and Magic, whose estranged husband, the [[Court of the Moonlit King|Moonlit King]], has long been absent from his court. Its great city is [[Corremel]], the City of Lanterns, linked by shadow road to [[Zobeck]]. The bearfolk of the [[Moonlit Glades]] hold back the shadows with moonlit cairns, a darakhul exile has founded a [[Twilight Empire]] in the north, and the shadow goblin king rules [[Fandeval]] where the Lethe meets the [[The Ever River|Styx]]. See [[The Shadow Realm]].

##### The Summerlands
The sunlit, forested world of the bright fey, full of slender towers and palaces under the hills. Dozens of fey courts squabble under the High King and High Queen in the silver city of Palsarian, and the Holly King and the Oak King fight over their borders every year. Grandmother Berchta walks there, and woe to anyone who threatens a child near her.

##### Sunhome
The Floating World, made entirely of discs and spheres drifting in a radiant sky, linked by sky ships. The Concord of Stars keeps its legions at the fortress of Hopefire. Not long ago death knights struck while the legions were away and killed the king and queen, and the young Golden Prince Jassin, eleven years old, rules now with revenge on his mind.
