Beyond the worlds and the Astral Sea lies the Void, which Midgard calls [[Ginnungagap]]. It devours worlds and gives power to anyone willing to serve it, but everything it touches is eventually corrupted or destroyed. A world that draws on it too long loses its light and life until only a husk is left. The Void itself isn't alive or thinking, but cultists swear it speaks to them.

The part nearest [[The Labyrinth]] is the Near Void, also called the Abyss: dead worlds, empty seas, badlands, and blasted mountains, where suns have gone black and broken magic bleeds from the ground. The air is thin and bitterly cold, and anyone who lingers risks void taint. Farther out is the Deep Void, where air and gravity can't be counted on and even demons and undead get eaten if they aren't careful. Hundreds of dead worlds are known. These are the ones travelers talk about.

##### Dead Worlds of the Near Void

| Place | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Arch of the Sky | A heavenly world the Void devoured. Its cities stand perfectly preserved under dust, and its people have turned to marble and chalk. Anything taken from it crumbles within days. |
| Black Velvet | A sunless world of black soil lit by glowing fungus around boiling springs that sometimes erupt. |
| Corpse Shore | In the frozen depths of Niflheim, where one of the [[Yggdrasil\|World Tree]]'s roots drinks from the spring Hvergelmir, [[Nidhogg]] gnaws at the root and eats the bodies of murderers and oathbreakers. |
| Dry Lands | The realm of [[Mot]] under a blood-red sky: deserts of bone sand, skull ziggurats, and forests of splintered bone that only the undead find beautiful. |
| Sea of Bones | Where the Astral Sea runs into the Void and becomes a milky sea of bone and black ooze, haunted by wailing mists. Its island of Kelmar Abbey holds a library no living scribe could have written. |
| Whispering Dunes | Endless white salt dunes and buried ruins, where devils stranded by the army that killed this world beg visitors to help them get home. |

##### Places of the Deep Void

| Place | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Black Sun | A freezing black orb ringed with red fire. It appears in the sky of a world about to fall to the Void. |
| Citadel of Lost Hope | Once the last stand of a world's star elf mages, now the tower of the void saint Nargoth. |
| Cracked Sky Bowl | A great scar in the sky over a mesa, where Void armies mass before tearing a rift into a living world. |
| Last Cathedral | A ruined temple of a forgotten goddess of life and love, now the throne room of the undead [[Brothers of the Last Cathedral]]. |
| Martyr Mountain | A lone mountain honeycombed with the tombs of void saints, with a temple near the summit. |
| Unblinking Stars | Six snowy mountains topped with prismatic beacons that cultists climb to bathe in. Most freeze on the way. The rest are changed. |
