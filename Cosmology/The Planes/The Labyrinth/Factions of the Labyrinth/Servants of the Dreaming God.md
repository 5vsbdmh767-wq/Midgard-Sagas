The youngest of the great factions, the Dreamers follow the visions [[Cartokk]] the Dreaming God sends them in sleep, wandering the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]] in search of truth and beauty and preaching what they've seen. Plenty of people think they're lazy drunks. But Dreamers somehow always know where the Void is breaking into a world, or which trusted leader has just quietly gone bad, because their god tells them. Every Dreamer has had at least one great vision, and that is how they recognize each other.

They believe that if enough people dream of worlds without the Void, the Void can be beaten, and if enough people laugh at death, death loses its grip. They work surprisingly hard at spreading joy, freedom, and a good party. They are also easy to mislead with false prophecies, and their passions run hot. Dreamers often travel to places they've seen in dreams, build shrines there, and a few can step bodily into the dream and come out somewhere else.

They split into three loose clans. The Great Believers fight the Void with light, chaos, and fists. The Seekers of Truth explore new worlds and meditate on their wonders. The Embraced turn inward and spend most of their lives asleep in visions.
