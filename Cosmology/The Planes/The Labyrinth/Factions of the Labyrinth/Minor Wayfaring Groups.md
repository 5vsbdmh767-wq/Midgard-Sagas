Not everyone on the roads of [[The Labyrinth]] belongs to one of the great [[Factions of the Labyrinth|factions]]. These smaller groups make good friends, or bad enemies.

##### Boatwrights
[[Humans]] and fiendish sydereans changed by ritual and the waters of the Styx, often with orange, brown, or blue skin, who ferry travelers up and down the river better than anyone. They follow [[Charun]], collect his fares in Styx coins, build their boats at the Silver Arsenal near the Hells, and keep a busy port at Old Boatwright's Harbor inside the Labyrinth. See [[The Ever River]].

##### Forge Hammer of Truth (the Armorers)
A dwarven brotherhood obsessed with making blades that can cut the Void and armor that can stand against it. Their great forge, the Ancient Anvil, is deep in the Labyrinth under a statue of [[Rinpash]], and it holds a sealed portal to the City of Brass. They'll work for anyone who pays or who'll pick up a hammer, and they hire adventurers to fetch rare metals and monster parts.

##### Lords and Ladies of the Bright and Shining Lands (the Fey Knights)
Bright fey and elves of the Summerlands who go on Grand Tours through the Labyrinth as knights errant, challenging everyone they meet at Styx crossings to feats of arms. They are cheerful, helpful, deadly against void creatures, and terrible show-offs who always bring a bard to record their deeds.

##### The Radiant Order (the Radiants)
Human paladins of [[Mavros|Herak-Mavros]] who serve angelic masters from Celestia. They turn up unannounced in full armor, destroy demons and corrupted portals, and leave without explaining themselves or worrying about the damage. They can't be hired, but they hear petitions from anyone and specialize in hopeless causes.

##### The Ancients
Not a group anyone can join, but everyone hunts for their ruins. The Ancients were giants, at least twenty-five feet tall, who built the first bridges and strongholds of the Labyrinth before their world was lost. Every image of them has had its head chiseled off. Their wheel-shaped cities have portals at the end of every spoke, mostly to dead worlds now. The minotaurs still honor their gods, [[Rinpash]] and [[Yundar]].
