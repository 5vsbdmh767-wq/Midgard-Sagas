The Keepers of the Keys are wizards, mechanists, clockworkers, and smiths who see themselves as the scholars and caretakers of the Labyrinth. Unlike the other heroic factions they live inside it, in the Smithy, where they forge the keys and build the portals that tie the worlds together. Their mission is to connect every world worth saving, so the heroes of one can come to the rescue of another, and to arm those heroes. [[Concord of Stars|Concordans]], Elders, and [[Servants of the Dreaming God|Dreamers]] all depend on Keeper weapons.

What Keepers really love is knowledge, the more obscure the better. Whenever a new world, road, or spell turns up, a Keeper probably found it. They are less good at everyday things like fixing roofs, growing food, or noticing that the ancient lever they're about to pull might wake something horrible. Cultists find them very useful idiots. They are close friends of the mechadrons and the gearforged.

##### Tools of the Trade
The Keepers can seal a portal, and all other ways into the same world, with a Great Portal Seal, usually to quarantine a world from the Void for good. Floating Invisible Eyes spy for them along the roads. And for every new world they open, they quietly forge a world key that can switch off every portal into it, just in case.
