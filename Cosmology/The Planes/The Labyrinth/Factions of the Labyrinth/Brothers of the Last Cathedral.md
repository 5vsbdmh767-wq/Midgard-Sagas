Two brothers who suffered a childhood of unspeakable cruelty took their revenge, and then kept going until they wanted every living thing dead. As the Lich King Domitos and Visimar, King of the Death Knights, they sit on twin thrones in a ruined cathedral in the Deep Void, the last surviving temple of a goddess of life and love whose name they wiped out on a dozen worlds. They hate [[Solana]] and her faithful above all, and burn her temples wherever they find them.

Their Eternal Legions of skeletons, zombies, and death knights march through void rifts to kill worlds, then garrison them so that nothing ever grows back. [[Worlds of the Labyrinth#Coldforge|Coldforge]] is their work. The legions are commanded by the Prince of the Death Knights, a title won in single combat every five hundred years. The new prince, Gundhold, is far cleverer than the last, and his undead have started taking prisoners. The Concord suspects he is hunting for the *Compass of the Ancients*, which would let his armies go almost anywhere. The Brothers are also behind the death knight raid on Sunhome that killed its king and queen.
