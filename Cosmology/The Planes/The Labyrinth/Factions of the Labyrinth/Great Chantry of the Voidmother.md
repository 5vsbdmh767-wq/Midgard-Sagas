The Great Chantry is a priesthood of the Void itself, which worships the end of all things and treats dark gods like [[Black Goat|the Black Goat]], [[Vardesain]], and the [[White Goddess]] as its lesser prophets. It has two halves. The Scribes of the Black Star, a small sect of satarre and humans marked by a black star tattoo on the writing hand, sit at [[Nidhogg]]'s side on the Corpse Shore and write down the dragon's ravings. The Sisterhood of the Outer Darkness, all women, studies those writings and sends the worst of them out to dark priests and cults everywhere. Their goal is the Irrevocable Apocalypse, when the true Void swallows every world.

Their chapels hide behind the faces of harmless local gods. Their open strongholds are the Cathedral of Silence on the dead world of Ashbloom, where the Scribes work in total silence, and the Ebon Bastion, a derro fortress in the Astral Sea whose ships pose as ordinary pirates.
