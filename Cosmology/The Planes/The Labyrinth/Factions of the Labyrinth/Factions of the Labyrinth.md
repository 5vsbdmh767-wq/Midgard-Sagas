Travelers in [[The Labyrinth]] sort themselves into factions for safety, friendship, and shared purpose. Four heroic factions have made themselves the guardians of the maze against the Void. They don't always agree, but when something big threatens the worlds they work together, and they recruit heroes from every world they touch, Midgard included. Membership brings a bed in a faction house, useful gear, and contacts at first, and potions, libraries, and hirelings later on. Four villainous powers want to tear it all down or rule what's left.

Not everyone joins. Independent travelers are called Wanderers, or less kindly, Wayfarers Without Maps, and the factions are always trying to recruit them.

##### Heroic Factions
- [[Concord of Stars]], the Concordans: crusaders of the heavens who fight the Void head-on
- [[Keepers of the Keys]], the Keepers: arcanists and smiths who build portals and keys in [[The Smithy]]
- [[Old Ring]], the Elders: druids and wardens of the [[Yggdrasil|World Tree]]'s forest worlds
- [[Servants of the Dreaming God]], the Dreamers: wandering visionaries, revelers, and pilgrims of [[Cartokk]]

##### Villainous Factions
- [[Great Chantry of the Voidmother]]: a priesthood of the Void and the dark gods
- [[Infernals]]: the devils of the Hells and their mortal servants
- [[Brothers of the Last Cathedral]]: two undead kings and their legions, out to end all life
- [[Servants of Nidhogg]]: a doomsday cult of the World Tree's devourer

##### Lesser Groups
- [[Minor Wayfaring Groups]]: the Boatwrights, the Fey Knights, the Radiant Order, the Armorers, and others, friendly and otherwise
