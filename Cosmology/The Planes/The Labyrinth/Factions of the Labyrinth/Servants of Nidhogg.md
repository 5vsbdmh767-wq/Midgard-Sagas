A doomsday cult devoted to [[Nidhogg]], who gnaws at the roots of the [[Yggdrasil|World Tree]]. Its founder Nargoth was a wizard who talked with the dragon until he went completely mad, was entombed alive by his own followers, and came back as a lich and void saint. His sermon is simple: only the end of everything can bring true peace.

The cult works in cells. Its preachers arrive in a town and warn of the end times, while other cultists quietly turn neighbors and families against each other with lies and enchantment. When the town tears itself apart, they move in to finish it, then open a door to the Void and let something worse in. Their sign is a sideways Y crossing two upright lines, the wyrm gnawing the Tree, and they wear gray, green, or purple robes by rank. Their greatest temple, Blighted Root, lies among the World Tree's roots near the Corpse Shore, and void giants who worship both Nidhogg and [[Fenris]] answer their call.
