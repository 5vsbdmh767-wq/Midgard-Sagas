The Concord of Stars is the mortal army of the heavens, or so they say, and few can prove otherwise. Its scholars, astronomers, and priests are fascinated by the great harmony of energies they call the concord of existence, and its soldiers go wherever the Void breaks through. They don't try to use or bend the Void. They study it from their high towers and astral castles, side with every good power against it, and burn it out with radiant magic, holy weapons, and plain bright light wherever they find it. They argue among themselves about whether the Void can think, or only be contained.

Their legions spend years at a time in the field. The Azure Lions have wandered the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]] for decades without coming home, and the Blood Legion takes anyone who swears to its code and to fight the Void, orcs, blood mages, ex-cultists, and even devils included. The Concord is brave and well-meaning, but it can be hasty and overconfident. It has been known to slam a rift shut with people still on the other side, and it holds that nothing should ever be powered by void energy, whatever the reason.
