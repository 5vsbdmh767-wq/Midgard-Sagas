The infernals are less a faction than a culture: the devils, demons, and mortals of the Hells, from archdukes down to the lowliest imp. They are greedy, cruel, and hungry for power, and they claim all of it is necessary, that their order, their blood magic, and their bargains are what keep the Void from eating everything. They use the Void themselves, carefully, and fiercely defend their own worlds from anyone else doing so.

They are usually enemies. But they love laws and contracts, and they hate the Void, so heroes sometimes find themselves in uneasy alliance with them. Their Hell Caravans travel the roads for months, and their Wandering Tower, a fortress of floatstone and demon skulls, roams the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]] hunting specific victims. Their spymaster, the goat-headed devil Master Barrosh, can turn up anywhere.
