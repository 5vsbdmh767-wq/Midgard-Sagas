The first people to walk the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]] came from the forest worlds of the Old Ring: treants, minotaurs, and clever smallfolk. The faction they founded, the Elders, are druids, hunters, gardeners, and actual plant folk who grow new worlds from the seeds of the World Tree and tend them against the blight of the Void. To them the Labyrinth is a handy tool, but [[Yggdrasil]] and the Ring are what really matter.

Elders are homebodies who love their green worlds, but once they leave home they are superb travelers, scouts, and hunters of cultists, and they can smell Void corruption on a person or a place. They are slow to act and utterly ruthless when they do. When a real war comes, the Elders bring the biggest armies: allied dragons, giants, elemental lords, and ratatosk skirmishers. They also run their own messengers along the World Tree, and pay bounties on anyone spreading blights that threaten the oak roads.
