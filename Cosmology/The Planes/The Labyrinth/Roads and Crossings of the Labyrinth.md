The roads of [[The Labyrinth]] change as you walk them, shaped by whoever made them and whatever worlds lie near. Close to [[The Smithy]] they are paved roads and flagstoned halls, with glowing arches and rune-carved doors marking the [[Keepers of the Keys|Keepers]]' portals. Near the [[Old Ring]] they turn into lonely trails through twilit forest, and on one of them, the [[Great Northern Road]], anyone who strays from the path eventually wanders back onto it, somewhat the worse for wear. The shadow roads of the shadow elves cross the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]] and touch Midgard and the Old Ring. Roads to Celestia are starry stairways and elven bridges, roads to the Hells are tunnels whose walls moan with trapped souls, and the roads to the dead worlds are rotten and crumbling. Nobody sensible walks those without protection against the Void.

##### Tunnels and Junctions

| Name | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Fire Wheel | A round chamber of black stone near the Hells. Six one-way entrances lead in, and twelve exits carved like fiendish mouths lead out: eleven to the Hells, one to the Corpse Shore where [[Nidhogg]] feeds. |
| Lunar Beamway | Tunnels of glowing white stone that appear near newborn worlds, where lazy lunar devils bask. Flattery gets you through. |
| Narric Trace | Fool's Passage, which twists, dead-ends, and moves around on the maps. The Keepers insist it is not the gut of a giant worm. Some travelers think it's alive, or haunted. |
| Quicksilver Canal | A canal of pure elemental water linking several water worlds, its locks and bridges kept by river spirits who may wave you through or demand a duel. |
| Spindle | A turntable platform where six passages meet. The sigils marking where each one goes are not always up to date. |
| Street of Frozen Tears | A tunnel of blue glacier ice from Seven Pines to the fey realm of Everfrost and the [[Northlands]] of Midgard, with warriors frozen in its walls by the Holly King. |

##### Waystations and Strange Places

| Place | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Bubbler's Well | A derro-built well whose water is perfectly fine to drink. Toss in a coin and a huge three-eyed toad rises, and [[Servants of the Dreaming God\|Dreamers]] lick its skin for visions of the roads. Too much toad sweat is poison. |
| Clockwork Kingdom | A marching army of mechadrons that never stops, stripping the wood and metal from every world it passes through. |
| Florez Belltower | A white marble tower of the Dreamers whose seven bronze bells, rung in different orders, open the way to different worlds. Annoying visitors tend to vanish. |
| Giant-King's Falling Mountain | A mountain tumbling forever through a sky of elemental air, hollowed out into a fortress by a giant king and his court. |
| Howler's Corner | A junction where the catslide alleys of many worlds meet and [[Bastet]] appears in cat form. You'll smell it before you see it. |
| Maze of Ariadne | A stone maze atop a pillar in a vast web over the Astral Sea, with a portal at its center to [[Rava]]'s temple in the Smithy. |
| Moonlit Kingdom of Tes-Qamar | The court of the minotaurs, moved into the heart of their Great Maze after dragons destroyed their cities in Midgard. The maze shifts constantly and lost minotaurs roam it. |
| Peddleton Cross | A goblin market where the [[Yggdrasil\|World Tree]]'s branches meet. No money, only barter, and an ogre sheriff who keeps order by knocking heads together. Things people thought lost forever often turn up here. |
| Pindelsburg | A kobold town near the Smithy, good for supplies and for charms "guaranteed to keep you from getting lost." |
| Silber Stad | A town of fireforge dwarf silversmiths who keep notes on everyone who passes their gates. |
| Sphere of Chaos | A knot of passages that spin, join, and come apart at random. Crossing it takes perfect timing or wings. |
| Tomb of the Sun Witch | The radiant tomb of an ancient celestial, guarded by light dragons. Those who sleep there have marvelous dreams. |
| Tower of Solana | A beacon of the [[Concord of Stars]] whose light shines through stone and across worlds, though only celestials and those blessed by [[Solana]] can see it. |
