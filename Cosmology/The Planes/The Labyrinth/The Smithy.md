The Smithy, also called the Great Hub, is the city at the heart of [[The Labyrinth]], and from it a traveler can reach any of the core worlds and plenty beyond them. It was founded where explorers first linked the [[Old Ring]] world of Seven Pines to the minotaurs' Great Maze, and it is home to the Keepers of the Keys, the arcanists who open and close portals, stretch or shorten the roads between worlds, and forge keys to places no one has been yet. When a portal key goes missing or a road between worlds goes wrong, people come to the Smithy. When they need weapons against the Void, they come here too.

The city grew around the Keepers' citadel on a hill in a bend of the Everrun River, inside a cavern where several Labyrinth roads met. [[Humans]] built tall houses on the cavern floor, dwarves and kobolds dug homes into the hill, and the fens across the river became farms. At some point, the legends say, the cavern roof became a sky. Today the Smithy is a crowded tangle of narrow streets and confusing alleys. Gear-covered wizards' towers crowd the top of the hill and the workshops pour steam and colored smoke into the air, while the old streets down by the docks crumble, because the Keepers are too busy with their studies to fix the roofs. Beyond a few miles of farmland, the smoke and the riverbanks fade into the roads of the Labyrinth.

The market outside the citadel gates is one of the great bazaars of the worlds, where mule trains and clockwork caravans jostle with celestial merchants and laughing fey who drive very careful bargains. The city's forges burn charcoal from the Old Ring's forests, which the Elders don't love.

##### The Citadel of the Keys
The Keepers' headquarters fills the top half of the hill behind high walls patrolled by clockwork watchmen. It holds the Library of Worlds, the Hall of the Grand Orrery with its thirty-foot clockwork model of the Labyrinth's worlds, and the Clockwork Temple of [[Rava]], where the Queen of the Labyrinth blesses each new portal key. Archmage Alana's villa has a landing platform for her dragon visitors. Kontak Tekar lives in a tiny flat over an enormous workshop that runs hundreds of feet into the hill, and mostly sleeps on a cot in his office.

##### Hub & Hounds
A famous tavern that never stays in one place. It turns up somewhere different in the Smithy, and sometimes farther off, according to the moons and tides of some fey realm. The wine and rabbit pies are wonderful, but people who drink too much sometimes wake up somewhere unexpected.
