General of the Azure Thunderbolts. The fierce and temperamental leader of the [[Citadel of Bright Wind]]’s famed defenders, Shabaan (djinni) commands the djinni and elemental soldiers of the Thunder Legion. Though from all appearances loyal, Shabaan chafes under The [[Calipha Astallah of Bright Wind]]'s peaceful reforms and longs for the days of the old Caliph’s territorial expansions.
