Pirate Queen of the Hungry Tempest. Feared across the plane, even by the powerful djinn, Gray Sulka is a doppelganger buccaneer that shares a telepathic bond with her carnivorous ship, the Hungry Tempest. Sulka and her pirate crew operate out of a concealed redoubt deep within the storms of [[Storoval]]. Provoked by her raids on djinni holdings and other merchant vessels, she boasts a hefty bounty for her capture or elimination.
