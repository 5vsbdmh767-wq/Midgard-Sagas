The Steel City of Shining Ice. This large city is the capital of the djinn and rests on a massive white cloud that encircles 
the [[Citadel of Bright Wind]], the palace of the Grand Calipha. The cloud beneath the city is infused with enough magic to render it solid. The spires and domes of Qasfi Alasam are fashioned from magically stable ice and polished steel, giving the city a cold, stark feel. However, the population—djinn, jinnborn, humans, and a scattering of other planar races—is welcoming and joyful for the most part. Some notable locations in Qasfi Alasam are the Jade Mosque of Endless Contemplations, the Flowing Gardens of Delight, and the Endless Souk, an enclosed marketplace that’s larger on the inside.
