Floating through the worst storms of [[Storoval]] are a few relatively calm fogbanks of swirling clouds and eddying, nutrient-rich mist. Stellar rorqual come to breed and give birth. The gigantic cetaceans generally ignore other creatures in the brumes, unless they pose a threat to the mothers or young. Rorqual brumes offer a rare safe zone in the otherwise perilous [[Storoval]]. Creatures that finish a long rest in a brume gain 10 temporary hit points that last for 8 hours
