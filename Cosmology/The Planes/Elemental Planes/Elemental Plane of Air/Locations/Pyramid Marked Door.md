In the [[The Mharoti Empire|Mharoti]] city of [[Harkesh]], north of where the Sumarra River flows beneath the great aqueduct, is an establishment called Flights of Fancy. Specializing in flying creatures and flying magic, the shop’s owner, Zingara, guards a door emblazoned with the alchemical symbol for air. A creature stepping through this doorway emerges in the city of [[Qasfi Alasam]] in the [[Elemental Plane of Air]].
