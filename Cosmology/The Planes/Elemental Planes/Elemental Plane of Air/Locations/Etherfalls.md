Visible from across the plane, a massive cascade of water erupts from a perpetual cloud in the highest reaches of
[[Endalaus]] to fall in a sparkling torrent into [[Storoval]] where it enters a lightning-wreathed tempest before exiting the plane. Ships sailing upon the [[The Ever River|Ever River]] can find a tributary leading to the Etherfalls, though the passage down the cascade, not to mention traversing the tempest, poses significant risks.
