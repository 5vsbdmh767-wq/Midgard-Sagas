The great Yggdrasil connects Midgard to all of the other planes, which nestle in its branches and roots like glittering ornaments. Where it manifests on Midgard, it appears as massive trees with roots as large as hills and trunks reaching miles into the sky. These individual trees however are but tiny tendrils and twigs springing from Yggdrasil itself.

Traversing Yggdrasil’s twisting paths 
requires the traveler to first climb a world 
tree and deal with any guardians along the 
way. During the two-mile climb, the traveler 
slowly transitions from Midgard to the planar 
highway of Yggdrasil’s branches. For every 
half-mile climbed, the traveler (or the majority 
of the group if they climb together) must 
succeed at a DC 10 Wisdom saving throw or 
be forcefully returned to Midgard. Characters 
or groups who fail the check immediately 
attract the attention of one of the World Tree
guardians (or child of yggdrasil from Creature 
Codex) and lose that half-mile of progress. 
After two miles, the transition is complete.
Once upon Yggdrasil itself, the traveler can 
traverse a twisting network of branches, each 
as wide as an imperial road, to any plane, 
making this a popular transit route. Reaching 
a destination can take many days of travel 
and is made worse by the confusing and 
constantly changing branches. Some paths 
have their own gravity or feature treacherous 
footing. Falling from a branch inflicts 5 (2d4) 
damage per 10 feet of height before impacting 
another branch. Portals marked with the 
Eiwaz rune and disguised as holes, wooden 
doors, or arches of entwined branches offer 
shortcuts for those with the knowledge to find 
them. The mischievous ratatosks often hire 
themselves out as guides for choice gossip, 
tasty tidbits of food, or shiny treasure. 
##### People of the Tree
The ratatosk are the most numerous folk on [[Yggdrasil]], and they will tell you they aren't beastkin at all but messengers of the gods, who knew about the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]] long before the minotaurs got the credit. Their great city is Grenstad, the City of the Five Branchings, ruled by Queen Clarahekkarina from the Acorn Palace. Three of its five branches lead to Midgard (to Alvatrad, the [[Witch]] Tree, and [[Trollheim|Wotan's Tree]]), one to the [[Court of Golden Oaks|Court of the Golden Oak]] in the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]], and one to Revelport in the Dreamer's Islands. Smaller ratatosk villages called drays hide up and down the tree. [[Ravenfolk]] live in ramshackle rookeries of nests and walkways, and many of them are [[Servants of the Dreaming God|Dreamers]]. Human treehouse villages belong to the Elders of the [[Old Ring]], and elves live on the branches nearest Celestia.

Near the top of the tree sits the Eyrie, where an eagle the size of a roc watches the worlds, with a smaller eagle named Vedfolnir perched on his head so nothing is missed. The ratatosk visit morning and evening to swap news, and they can't resist embellishing it. High in the branches is Wotan's Gallows, where [[Wotan]] hung for nine days to win the runes, and the ravenfolk village of Hrafnby has grown up beside it.

The roots are another matter. [[Nidhogg]] and his spawn gnaw there, goat-legged kallikantzaros try to saw through the trunk, and void dragonborn have settlements among the roots. A secret cult of void-tainted ratatosk, the Madbranch, is said to send messengers down to Nidhogg. Living on a road between worlds suits the ratatosk fine, though. Each week, by old custom, they send a scout down to the dragon to hear what he has to say.
