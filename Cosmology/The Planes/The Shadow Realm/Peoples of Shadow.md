The [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]] is home to strange creatures and ordinary folk alike, all shaped by the land one way or another. Five peoples have risen as its main powers, each struggling to master the ebon tides and hold its lands: the bearfolk, the darakhul, the shadow fey, the shadow goblins, and the umbral humans. Each rules its own corner of the realm, governing settlements, building roads, and living in enough numbers to keep its towns and cities going.

Others had a foothold from the beginning as servitors of the shadow fey. The quicksteps, erina, and wyrd gnomes still often fill that role, but they have carved out modest holdings of their own too. The [[Ratatosk|ratatosk]] have a small presence everywhere the [[Yggdrasil|World Tree]]'s branches reach, and the Stygian shades are the realm's own kind of the [[Shades|shades]] that appear wherever mortals live. A few peoples have come to shadow only recently, drawn by its mystery and power, among them the ravenfolk and the satarre.

## The Five Powers

### Bearfolk

All [[Bearfolk|bearfolk]] share an iron resolve, and none more than those who make the Shadow Realm their home. The bearfolk of the [[Moonlit Glades]] are hardened by a life of constant peril and an unending battle against shadow corruption, and only by banding together in close communities devoted to their purpose have they survived. Since [[Björnrike|Mesikämmen]] the Bear King issued his fateful decree, they have fought as a source of light in the realm. Most of it is deadly even to skilled travelers, but the bearfolk have carved out pockets of safety guarded by druidic magic, and hard-won wisdom has taught them how to resist and purify shadow corruption.

Generations of bearfolk have now been born in shadow, and the longer they stay, the more each generation shows an innate power to shape the darkness around them. The shadowborn are cubs born in the Shadow Realm with a leaning toward the dark. They have gray coats from infancy, sometimes with strange arcane black markings, and they not only resist darkness more easily than other bearfolk but are often drawn to it.

See also: [[Bearfolk Lineage#Natural Adaptation: Shadowborn|shadowborn bearfolk rules]].

### Darakhul

The darakhul are no strangers to darkness, and over the years the [[Twilight Empire]] has adapted well and built a thriving civilization in shadow. Living flesh is scarce in the Shadow Realm. The darakhul still hunt the living and keep elaborate trade networks to bring in living cargo from the mortal lands, but they have found that the crackling trees, an invasive growth from [[Evermaw]], can ease much of their hunger, and they have learned to live on the bloodlike sap drawn from them. The habit marks them: the corruption in the trees blackens their teeth, pulls back their gums, and shrivels the flesh around their mouths. Others say the black teeth come from the shadow itself, drawn in through the flesh of the realm's living creatures; either way, every ghoul of the Twilight Empire bears the mark. As a result, the darakhul of the Shadow Realm worry far less about hiding what they are. They are far from the worst things in the dark.

Darakhul keep the first names they had in life. Their surnames are granted or chosen after the Crossing, their passage into undeath, and are often the name of a patron or lord, of a legion, or of a faith. Priesthood names include Al-Mot, Al-Qaan, Akma-aldan, Bloodtide, and Vardeson; legion names include Bonecracker, Ivory, Maggot, Mortus, Reaver, and Secondus; and older names run from Blackwater and Bloodborn to Styxdrinker, Unsated, and Wraithcaller. Ghouls made outside the Twilight Empire may take names tied to their goals, keep their old surnames to hold on to their past lives, or go without one.

See also [[The Darakhul Imperium]].

### Shadow Fey

The [[Shadow Fey|shadow fey]] are the most numerous and best established of the realm's peoples. Long ago they were stolen away from the Bright Lands with promises of power, though to outsiders their origins remain a mystery. They rule from courts thick with intrigue and maneuvering, and they are immune to the shadow corruption that threatens visitors from the mortal world. That protection has a price. More than anything, the shadow fey want to feel the emotions and passions that don't come easily to them, and a great deal of courtly wealth goes into growing and trading those emotions, made solid by fey magic as memory philters.

Outside the courts, the shadow fey have built magnificent lantern-lit cities and fortresses at crossroads across the realm. They know the land deeply, and have a long history with its powers, places, and secrets. A shadow fey might be a minor noble of the [[Fey Courts of Shadow|courts]], a citizen of one of the realm's few cities, or a scout or hunter from a remote outpost where those unfit for politics are sent to train. They look much like elves, but their skin runs to alabaster white, ebon black, or subtle grays, and many have horns of every size and shape. They are raised from youth to be quick with words and with the blade, and the endless drama of the courts gives their long lives purpose, though their hunger for novelty often sends them off on long journeys.

Shadow fey names are much like elven ones, but darker and more guarded, favoring gloom and seclusion over sunshine and tinkling brooks.

See also: [[Shadow Fey Lineage|shadow fey lineage rules]].

#### Lunar Elves

Lunar elves are a rare offshoot of the shadow fey, and a lunar birth is usually taken as an auspicious omen. They are always born under the light of the full moons and seem soaked in it: they look like other shadow fey except that their skin glows in the dark and their eyes are luminous silver. They can learn to hide the glow, but strong emotion tends to reveal them, and their horns are usually long, spiraled, and pearly. Their origin is even more mysterious than that of the shadow fey, and many feel a pull toward the absent [[Court of the Moonlit King|Moonlit King]]. They are not cast out of the courts, but many prefer work that keeps them out from under the watchful eyes of the nobles: serving as emissaries to the Moonlit Glades, living at the [[Court of One Million Stars|Starry Court]], or hunting the beasts of the realm, all of which beat the suspicious gaze of the [[Sarastra|Queen of Night and Magic]]. Lunar elves who want to stay in high society take ordinary shadow fey names; those who embrace their difference name themselves after the stars, so rarely seen through the shadow sky, as a sign of independence.

See also: [[Shadow Fey Lineage#Lunar Elf|lunar elf lineage rules]].

#### Sable Elves

When the elves began to decline, some stood their ground and others made a pact with the shadows. The sable elves simply ran. That made them enemies quickly, and the elves who took the shadow pact scorned them, so now the sable elves are scattered across the realms in lasting disgrace, belonging nowhere. They have one stronghold of their own, the [[Court of Golden Oaks]]. The few who remain hold the last traces of the powerful elven magic that helped shape the early world, and they guard those secrets in isolation. With so few left and only one community, the sable elf line is dying out. They are dark-eyed, usually red- or raven-haired, and silver-skinned, tall and willowy, and most carry an air of deep world-weariness. They keep the ancient elven birth name and common name, but not the third, lineage name, in token of their disgrace.

See also: [[Elf Lineage#Sable Elf|sable elf lineage rules]].

### Shadow Goblins

Shadow goblins grew into their place in the realm as servitors and allies of the shadow fey, and in time founded a city of their own, [[Fandeval]], and cut their own path. They are closer to the fey than to their monstrous cousins in the mortal lands, and their cunning lets them fit right into the shadow courts. They are often blue- or purple-skinned with bright orange or yellow eyes, and they have a knack for blending into shadow. They make good rogues and better assassins, and they have a dark reputation, but they also love low humor, drink, roughhousing and wrestling, and contests of skill (which they often lose). They learn from infancy to take advantage of anyone who underestimates them. They share the shadow fey's resistance to the realm's corruption and have a surprising gift for shadow weaving and moon magic, which most of them keep as a useful secret.

Shadow goblin given names lean toward darkness or whimsy, or ideally both, and many consider it a personal failing to share a name with anyone else, so they work hard to give their children names no one else has. Family names are practical and descriptive: Bellringer, Candlesmith, Moonshine, Netmender, Potlicker, Skullsniffer, Wargbender, Weaselwise.

See also [[Goblins]], and [[Goblin Lineage#Shadow Goblin|shadow goblin lineage rules]].

### Umbral Humans

The Shadow Realm twists beings with its magic, and for umbral humans the effect can be dramatic. They are not merely humans corrupted by shadow but humans slowly changed by it over generations until they are inured to it. Beyond shadow's natural influence, the shadow fey have long experimented with what shadow does to mortals, to punish fools, to make better servants, or just for fun, and some umbral humans are the survivors of those rituals. Umbral humans age slowly, much like elves or shadow fey.

Changelings are the natural result of humans adapting to life in shadow over many generations. Some descend from humans stolen to serve the shadow fey, or taken on a whim, and many more from humans who got lost on a shadow road or were abandoned and stranded. They resemble their mortal cousins, but with the marks of shadow, much as the shadow fey differ from other elves: skin of alabaster white, ebon black, or subtle grays, and sometimes even small horns. Their looks may change further in generations to come.

The gifted are a warning about dealing with the fey. They are umbral humans who drank too deeply and too quickly of the shadows, usually through the shadow fey, though there are many dark powers in shadow. The change might be forced on an unlucky soul, or be the price or the prize of a fey pact. The gifted vary widely: some look like ordinary umbral humans, some like shadow fey, and some bear small or dramatic changes such as tails, hooves, or vestigial wings, along with changes of mind and heart. Every one of them bears some sign, visible at least to themselves, and sometimes the change takes their memories. Others call them "the gifted"; they rarely think of themselves that way. Many reinvent themselves completely, name and all.

Where umbral humans gather, newcomers often keep the names of the mortal lands they came from, if they remember them, while long-settled families use a blend of customs from every land that has fed the realm.

See also: [[Human Lineage#Umbral Human|umbral human lineage rules]] and the [[Heritages#Umbral Heritage|Umbral Heritage]].

## The Lantern Bearers

Even in the Shadow Realm the light persists. Here and there, in darkened glades and deep caverns, the gloom thins and a place reaches a kind of balance where travelers can rest and recover. Such havens are usually claimed by natives who have not given in to shadow: exiles at odds with their rulers, peoples who have not lost their way, or inscrutable beings who keep their reasons to themselves. Each keeps at least some contact with another, which keeps contact with others in turn, and together they form a loose network from one wavering border of the realm to the other, known to a few as the Lantern Bearers. Travelers looking for safe passage out of shadow, or working against its darker powers, do well to find them and earn their trust. The bearfolk of the Moonlit Glades stand at the network's center. Spies of the shadow fey courts, ravenous ghouls, the mad umbral vampires, and stranger things threaten it constantly.

## Other Peoples

The Shadow Realm draws in the strange, the dispossessed, the curious, and the lost, and some of them have built small communities. None are great in number, but they are often influential in a particular court, city, or region.

### Quicksteps

Quicksteps were among the servitors the shadow fey brought with them, and like the shadow fey they are naturally resistant to the realm's corruption. They love finery and are entirely at home amid the riches of court. As their name says, they are fast, which suits them perfectly to two favorite shadow fey pastimes: sabotage and dueling. They rarely live outside the courts but have thriving communities in many of them, above all the [[Court of Midnight Teeth]] and the [[Court of Winter's Love]]. They are short-lived for fey, living about fifty years, and they share the fey's wanderlust, which makes them able adventurers welcome in any company crossing the realm. They like flashy, memorable names that sound good announced before a duel: Bolt, Crimson, Dandy, Double Time, Hotspur, Starburst, Zephyr.

See also: [[Quickstep Lineage|quickstep lineage rules]].

### Ratatosk

In recent decades the [[Ratatosk|ratatosk]] have grown bored with simply guarding the World Tree and have decided to take the fight to the corruption of the Shadow Realm. Exuberant sentinels against everything evil, with personalities to match, they are often at odds with the realm's natives.

### Spiritfarer Erina

The shadow fey brought [[Erina|erina]] from the Bright Lands as servitors, and the hedgehog folk adapted quickly, thriving in the dark forests and underground warrens. Their love of exploration and their unrivaled burrowing have taken them to many strange places, including the mortal realms. Most have settled around the plateau of the Queen's Wood and the Silver Hills, but bands of erina explorers, naturalists, and guides keep underground camps across the realm. The shadow fey are not pleased with the erina's wanderlust, which makes them less useful servants, nor with their open habit of helping travelers in and out of the Shadow Realm through their underground shadow roads, but for now it's easier to ignore them. The spiritfarers have a gift for speaking with the spirit world. Outside their own lands, erina like names full of long, open vowels, which are rare in their own language and pleasant to their ears, and they stretch the vowels out when they introduce themselves: May-nay, Wayee, Leemay, Ur-win.

See also: [[Erina Lineage#Spiritfarer Erina|spiritfarer erina lineage rules]].

### Stygian Shades

The Shadow Realm's own [[Shades|shades]] rise from the [[The Ever River|River Styx]] with almost no memory of who they were. See [[Shades]] for more, including the [[Soriglass|City of Shades]], Soriglass.

See also: [[Shade Lineage#Stygian Shade|Stygian shade lineage rules]].

### Sublime Ravenfolk

A growing number of [[Ravenfolk|ravenfolk]] have joined a mystery cult in the Shadow Realm. Some say the cult is new; others say it is ancient and only now reviving. Its members call themselves Seekers of the Sublime, and they are said to be drawn by a mysterious psychic song. There are still few of them, but more arrive along the shadow roads from the mortal lands all the time, waiting for the unknown singer to reveal what it wants. They are very secretive about their purpose, but so far they have not turned on the other peoples of shadow.

See also: [[Ravenfolk Lineage#Sublime Ravenfolk|sublime ravenfolk lineage rules]].

### Unbound Satarre

The satarre are famous planar travelers. As the kingdoms of shadow grow, so does the web of gateways feeding their shadow roads, and none know it better than the unbound satarre, a small faction zealously devoted to unraveling the barriers between the worlds. Even other satarre find them curious, for they have an instinctive sense for places of transition, and an unbound satarre feels compelled to find such gateways and widen them by any means.

See also: [[Unbound Satarre Lineage|unbound satarre lineage rules]].

### Wyrd Gnomes

Wyrd gnomes are accomplished arcanists who came to the Shadow Realm with the shadow fey and hold a favored place among their servitors. They look like whatever kind of gnome they are born to, except that their eyes never match: the right is always blue and the left always green. They are born with an innate prescience that grows stronger near other wyrd gnomes. Some gnome communities see this as a blessing; others find it disturbing. Over generations, wyrd gnomes have gathered into small communities of their own, though wyrd gnomes don't always give birth to wyrd gnomes, so their families include other kinds of gnome too. Their shared foresight makes those communities fascinating to visit: a traveler might walk into an inn to find the meal they meant to order already waiting, and conversations often begin with the answer before the question. Anyone who moves into a wyrd gnome community takes the clan name of the household that adopts them.

See also [[Gnomes]], and [[Smallfolk Lineage#Wyrd Gnome|wyrd gnome lineage rules]].
