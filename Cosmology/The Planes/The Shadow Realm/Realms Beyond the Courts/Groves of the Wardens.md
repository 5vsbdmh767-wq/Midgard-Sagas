The bearfolk groves of the wardens are druidic sites and training grounds for bearfolk shadow gnawers. Several have grown into villages, but their first purpose is still training bearfolk wardens and guardians against shadow.

## Eagle Grove

A single stone temple stands in the hills by the headwaters of a stream, a pilgrimage site for [[Umbeserno]], the bearfolk god of dreams. His priests keep the grove, teach shadow gnawers and others to protect themselves from shadow corruption, and sell amulets and holy draughts of mead to ease the winter sleep. The sales and pilgrimages stop in late fall, when the priests go to their own slumber until spring.

## Hawthorne Grove

Favored by birch sirens and river giants as well as treants, ogres, unicorns, and alseid, Hawthorne Grove is the oldest school and seminary for young druids of every kind in the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]]. As many as two dozen initiates study here at a time under Lady Rowan, a bearfolk druid.

## Leaping Salmon Grove

Once only a druid circle, this small settlement by a rickety wooden bridge over a narrow point of the [[The Ever River|Styx]] has grown into a town of almost 1,000 bearfolk, wyrd gnomes, and erina, most of them fisherfolk, who smoke and preserve much of their catch for trade.

## Lost Grove

This grove once stood near the Whispering Plain, far from the larger bearfolk lands. A ritual gone wrong during a time of great flux in the ebon tides broke it loose, and over a few months it drifted across the Whispering Plain until it came to rest near the Silver Hills. It is still a potent stone circle, defended by the fierce Lord Istler, a bearfolk knight of shadows, with his company of shadow gnawers and two lunar elf archers nicknamed Folly and Flight.

## Moonstone Grove

Known for its druid school, its enormous standing stones, its tall trees, and its moon tower, Moonstone Grove is too quiet to interest most fey and too peaceful for most goblins. Its chief druid and village elder is Franelle Elderberry, a human woman who has lived her whole life among the bearfolk. She left the fey courts long ago and still dislikes the shadow fey and shadow goblins in general.

## Sleeper's Grove

This deeply scented pine grove high in the mountains can't be reached in winter. Its druids are skilled in winter magic and know many bird forms for shapeshifting, which has earned them the joking name "owlbearfolk." It holds dozens of tombs of honored bearfolk, especially royalty and archdruids, kept by a few year-round guardians and caretakers under Chief Tomb Warden Tromble Dorran. Small bands of ravenfolk visit now and then, perhaps to speak with the dead; the guardians frown on it but don't stop them.

## Three Hives Grove

A center of honey making, as its name says, this sleepy timber town is a ramshackle collection of bearfolk cabins inside a wooden palisade. It also has a radiant well, a source of arcane power that somehow gives off moonlight, which the local druids turn into moonstone, moonsteel, and other useful forms of solid radiance. The Beekeeper in Chief is Lord Aparombo, a bearfolk thunderstomper.

## Veiled Grove

Joined to the capital at [[Moonlit Glades|Solvheim]] only by a narrow track, the Veiled Grove is a sanctuary and holy ground where any outlaw or exile can find shelter, whether bearfolk, shadow fey, or a shade from [[Soriglass]]. Its trees are enormous pines of a kind seldom seen, with reddish trunks and foliage tending to dark plum. Its druid is Rumbella Nuuneva, a grandmotherly bearfolk who seems able to see through illusions and sense the truth of corrupted hearts. A small spring in the grove is used to cleanse shadow corruption, drawing the occasional pilgrim and wayward adventurer.
