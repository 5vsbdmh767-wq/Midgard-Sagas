Built by the sable elves on the banks of the [[The Ever River|Styx]] in ages past, Soriglass is an enormous mausoleum, a ruin more famous for its crypts, ghosts, and [[Shades|shades]] than for any power, art, or wealth. Its people collect tomb dust to make requiem, a highly addictive drug that lets those who smoke it speak with the dead, and visitors often come to explore one of the region's two great dungeon complexes. The Cloisters of the Outer Void are held mostly by ghouls, satarre, and undead (see also [[Great Chantry of the Voidmother]]). The Pit of the Squamous Fiend is said to connect directly to the lower layers of the Eleven Hells, and it holds enough fiends and aberrations to make that seem likely. Both are death traps full of fiends and undead, tied to deadly sorcerers, frothing cultists, and satarre doomsayers, and both have been ruled by a succession of lich lords, minor demon lords, and evil high priests. They are too strong for the local king of the shades to root out, but fortunately they spend most of their strength fighting each other rather than honest travelers or the city's people.

## Bright Barge Dock

Soriglass has no bridge across the Styx, unlike the [[Court of Golden Oaks]], but it does have a small community of halflings, river-loving satarre, and umbral humans who build, load, and work barges upriver to Vysanth, a pilgrimage town devoted to [[Charun]], and downriver as far as [[Fandeval]]. The mistress of the Bargeworkers' Guild, Happy Fillbec, is a strong, no-nonsense leader of the barge captains and crews who tolerates no foolishness about cargo or payment. Visitors usually come to Soriglass by boat rather than by road, and her work keeping the barge traffic safe is one reason why.

## Castle of Frozen Tears

King Tomarrich the Howler rules a small court of shades, lesser phantoms, and necromancers who use the city's wealth of tombs and ghosts as raw material for dark magic. He dreams of gathering the darakhul to his side, though in practice he is very much a minor vassal of Emperor Vilmos Marquering of the [[Twilight Empire]], which is always nearby and always on his mind. The castle is built of finely worked blue-white stone that glows over the plains and can be seen from across the Styx.

## Low Temple of the Ghouls, High Temple of Hecate

[[Charun]] has several thriving shrines and priests in Soriglass, but the busiest temple is the Great Vault of Vardesain and Santerr Illosi, where ghoul and darakhul pilgrims come to honor a relic: the Bone Cloak of Vardesain, said to be made from the finger bones of more than 400 darakhul and one bone from an avatar of [[Vardesain]] himself. The priests bring it out on holy days, and darakhul wrapped in it are said to find relief from the endless hunger and to be cured of various ailments of the undead. Her Luminance Ingalla Vert keeps the cloak, and the high priests of Vardesain from Vysanth and Ossean often make the trip to see it, though neither cares to stay long at the Great Vault.

The Cathedral of the Luminous Moon was once the largest temple to [[Hecate]] in the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]], but with the long decay of Soriglass its stained-glass windows are empty sockets in a weathered shell. High Priestess Vert leads other darakhul priestesses and several human adepts and initiates in the rites of the moon, and everyone is welcome in the black pews and at the full-moon blessings of weapons, armor, livestock, and wands. Rumor says the chancel is enchanted against evildoers, guarding the altar and its platinum dagger and chalice. The cathedral's real treasures are kept underground: a lock of Hecate's silvery hair and a bottled piece of her sacred shadow, both relics of the faith, shown to pilgrims at each full and new moon.

## Sixty Cyclopean Tombs

Outside the city, but always following in its wake when the ground shifts and the land ebbs and flows, stand the Sixty Tombs, a cluster of mausoleums, pillared shrines, and the occasional obelisk. Their script is not related to modern Umbral but to an ancient dialect, and the bones in them are more like an ogre's or a trollkin's than a human's or a fey's: heavy, tall, and imposing. Hidden passages, buried tombs, and powerful undead are always rumored among them, though few know where to look. Necromancers and priests of Charun often come to study their mysteries.

## Ghosts of Soriglass

The city is famous for its ghosts. A few of the best known:
