Many parts of the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]] resemble the mortal world, but some regions adrift in shadow are much stranger. The Pits of the Roachlings are an utterly bizarre terrain made by congealing, spinning, and reshaping raw shadow into something like demonic termite mounds, or hollow dunes and curving pits. A roachling pit usually lies out on the plains as a gentle hollow in the land with an inviting pool at the bottom, ringed by reeds or trees. The water really is pure rainwater, cold and drinkable, but the gentle slope around it is often very slippery, and climbing out takes a successful DC 15 STR (Athletics) check. Other pits look like a ring of stones or a small mesa, again usually with a pure spring, that sinks into the shadowy plain as someone approaches. Creatures that camp by the water overnight or take a long rest there slowly sink along with the stones and the pool, and may wake to find themselves 30 or 40 feet below the surrounding land. The walls of a pit are usually pocked with niches, tunnels, and small dwellings, often occupied.

Either way, the ground nearby is riddled with roachling tunnels, and lesser fiends such as rattok, quasits, imps, and lemures may live there too. True to their nature, roachlings can survive almost anywhere, including the Shadow Realm. Their communities live by trapping game, which roachlings can eat without fear of corruption, and by a little trade with more civilized peoples. Their great enemies are the darakhul, who find roachling flesh perfectly palatable, and the [[Twilight Empire]] organizes frequent "roach hunts" on the Whispering Plains and elsewhere to feed its growing population.
