The umbral vampires were men and women once. Now they are twisted fiends sustained by the power of shadow and hungry for the life they lost. They are not undead but creatures wholly of the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]]: pale, gaunt humanoids with long pointed ears and sharp features, darkness weeping like smoke from their eyes, mouths, and noses, whose touch drains the life and vitality from their victims. More shade than substance, they can slip through solid matter to reach their prey or to flee the light. They drift through Oshragora, the City Fallen into Shadow, a crumbling ruin twisted through space and time.

## Sundered History

The umbral vampires are the Shadow Realm's greatest cautionary tale. They were the people of a thriving city whose rulers were spellcasters mightier than the greatest sorcerers of this age, wise and just, who used their power to preserve the glory of their people. Their decline began subtly. To stop the march of time, the archmages twisted it and turned its flow aside, and the whole city was freed from age and death. For generations they lived that way, making ever greater magic, art, and architecture to rival the palaces of the gods. For all their wisdom, they were blind to the danger. As time flowed on in the world around them, it wore away the spells that held it back, and when the spells failed, the city drowned in all the years it had denied. In a rush of uncontrolled magic and wildly shifting time, the whole city vanished from the mortal world, pulled into the darkness between worlds, and only the barest traces remain. Its people suffered the ravages of shadow corruption trapped in chaotic temporal storms, spending an eternity in that closed loop of time, devoured by shadow and reborn again and again, until at last the timestorm died away.

When the winds of history settled, Oshragora was born. Great towering ruins appeared in the Shadow Realm. Cobbled streets cracked and twisted along new paths set by shadow's distortions. Buildings that once touched the sky stand frozen mid-collapse, held in unstable pockets of temporal stasis that fail, unwind, and reassert themselves at random, so that pieces of the city crumble or rebuild themselves before an observer's eyes. Even by the standards of the Shadow Realm the city defies geography: where the rest of the realm shifts over months or years, Oshragora changes with no pattern or schedule at all.

## Inscrutable and Uncountable

Oshragora isn't organized enough to be called a city-state, let alone a nation. No one knows how many umbral vampires lurk in its ruins and shadow-choked alleys; despite repeated attempts, no scouting party or explorer has ever counted them, because they slip between the cracks of reality as if still shifting through time and space. One half-mad shadow fey hunter raved that she fought and killed the same umbral vampire for weeks while lost in a forest that kept coiling back on itself.

The vampires' motives are obscure, or at least prone to sudden shifts. Now and then one will talk with visitors instead of devouring them, but these conversations are always frustrating: the vampire mentions events out of order and refers to people and places no sage can identify, while giving the impression it understands far more than it can say. Shadow fey emissaries are sure a ruling council still exists somewhere in the city, but its instability has made it impossible to find, and the [[Sarastra|Queen of Night and Magic]] has promised an emperor's ransom in treasure and secrets for a reliable connection to the vampires' dark council, or at least to Lord Nalgevar.

Other creatures live there too. Undead shadows thrive in and around the city and its splinters, drifting in the vampires' wake, and they never attack the vampires, which suggests either some mastery over them or a sense of kinship. Ghouls of the [[Twilight Empire]] visit often, and through covert means and interrogation the shadow fey have come to believe the Twilight Emperor is searching the city for a lost artifact, probably one with power over time, and perhaps even part of the magic that doomed the city. Stranger visitors appear as well, such as shadow mages and [[Eonics|eonic]] drifters, enigmatic travelers of the aeons who step between past and future as easily as walking into the next room. Given the city's troubled relationship with time, that is hardly surprising, and travelers from all realms and times find themselves drawn to it.

## Ever Reaching

The other peoples of the Shadow Realm shun the umbral vampires and drive them back with blade and spell wherever they meet, so the vampires can't easily use the shadow roads or the places where other planes touch the realm. Diminished as they are, they remain powerful and devilishly cunning. Their most disturbing talent is finding weak spots in the barrier between worlds. The places where despair, blood, and darkness tear rifts between the realms draw them, and their presence seems to speed the opening of doorways into shadow. Thankfully, they seem compelled to stay near the ruins of Oshragora, so they keep to a limited area.

Just as the city warps the land around it, or at least moves in some fashion, it sometimes sheds pieces of itself. These splinters, sometimes a single ruined building and sometimes a whole neighborhood or district, simply appear somewhere else, perhaps miles away, and stay for days or weeks, dragging umbral vampires and other denizens with them. A single building might hold one vampire and a host of shadows; a district might unleash a dozen vampires or more on the land around it. The bearfolk of the [[Moonlit Glades]] have kept a close watch on Oshragora since a few disturbing splinters appeared in their lands. So far they have contained the vampires in a dozen splinters until those splinters vanished, but they lack the arcane knowledge to do more than react, and they are eager to learn how the splinters work and how to keep them away.

The latest to take an interest in the city are the fey of the [[Court of One Million Stars]], who drift through the ruins wrapped in starlight that pains the shadows and the vampires. They study the city but carefully avoid its areas of distorted time. Only one has ever spoken while about this work. After destroying a pack of umbral vampires with a blazing wave of starfire, the luminous creature whispered to the only surviving adventurer, "The Void calls to the Void. It must not be allowed to answer." The adventurer told his story to Revich, the blind angel of the [[Court of Night and Magic]], and then threw himself from the top of the angel's tower.

## Places in the City

### Albent Stadium

Athletic contests and prisoners' blood sports are held here to amuse the vampire overlords.

### Burning Towers

At least two towers in the city burn with endless flames, usually deep orange but sometimes purple, and there may seem to be three or four when visual or temporal distortions double them. They were built over radiant wells that someone tampered with, and lines of arcane power feed them a constant stream of phlogiston that needs no other fuel. The vampires avoid them, but fire elementals sometimes dance in the flames, and some believe a shadow road to the City of Brass once began in one of these towers.

### Castle Darros

The castle and its nearby barracks are home to Lady Elenya and her handmaidens, a group of wraiths, along with her court of shadow goblins and human thralls.

### City Hall

This fine half-timbered building is one of the few in good repair, and it is the hub of commerce, industry, and plotting for those who dare visit the city. Its basement is the Ratskellar, the only tavern in town, whose food is notoriously poor; the specialty is fried purpure, a lilac-colored fish from the city's river. Lord Nalgevar keeps an audience chamber somewhere in the dungeons below.

### Dusk Guildhall

Vampires visiting their umbral cousins founded this guild to spread the city's people and power. It works as a strange kind of explorers' society, trading information and pursuing targets among the darakhul, humans, and shadow fey. Most members are dhampirs, vampires, vampire spawn, or shades, and some of them want a close alliance with [[Soriglass]].

### Storm Tower

This tall Gothic tower, with its many gargoyles, bloodthirsty vines, elaborate balconies, and eight great bronze doors at its base, is home to the city's umbral vampires. As the home of many of the city's commoners and the most livable of its buildings, it is a useful crossroads between all the city's splinters, however they twist in space, and it always stands at the city's center, often visible from anywhere in it. Lightning and flickering witchfire often light it, and azza gremlins and spire walkers are often seen on it. The city's rulers rarely come here, preferring Castle Darros and City Hall.

### Violet River

This tributary of the [[The Ever River|Styx]] runs through Oshragora silently and mostly unhindered. Sometimes it seems to flow into a portal or a void, and sometimes it appears out of nowhere in the middle of a street.
