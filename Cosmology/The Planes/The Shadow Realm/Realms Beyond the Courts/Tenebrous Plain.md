Downstream from the Forest of the Firebirds, a plain of tall, lush grass stretches around a tributary of the Lethe. Gnarled trees and skeletal, leafless shrubs stand out sharply against the serene green, and the shadows of the plants, living and dead, fall in strange directions, some stretching farther than others. The fast tributary winds among clumps of growth, and it is fickle: sometimes it runs with the inky black of most [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]] waters, and sometimes as clear and cool as the freshest stream in elven lands, at the whim of Oma Rattenfanger, the Keeper of the Plain. It is hard to say what moves her to such generosity, but tales tell of countless mortals making offerings on the plain in hope of clean water, and sometimes the black vanishes from the riverbed and leaves perfectly drinkable water behind. Other tales tell of ungrateful young adventurers gulping down the clear water only to have it swirl with black motes as they drank. Some say lost souls who were mistreated by those they revered are likely to find help from Oma Rattenfanger; others say such people are never seen again, taken into her eternal service.

## Hidden Dangers

Most creatures of the Shadow Realm avoid Oma Rattenfanger's domain, but the plain still holds plenty of danger. She keeps not only the plain but its inhabitants, among them the orphans of the black, feral, childlike creatures who were mistreated as children and now take revenge on anyone in authority. Orphans of the black are rarely loyal, but those of the Tenebrous Plain are fiercely devoted to Oma, and anyone who tries to harm her must deal with them. She has given sanctuary to other creatures fleeing the mortal world or other parts of the realm as well: shadow-touched plants such as shadow blights and razorleafs thrive here, and a grove of weeping treants has watched over the keeper and the plain for as long as she has been there. A single feyward tree roams the plain, guarding it against trespassing fey. It never harms Oma or those in her care, a mystery its victims never get the chance to solve.
