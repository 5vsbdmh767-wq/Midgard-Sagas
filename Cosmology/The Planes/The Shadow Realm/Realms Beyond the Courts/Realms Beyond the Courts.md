The fey courts are the best-known settlements of the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]], but other peoples have their own strongholds, cities, and even nations there. The most important are the goblin city of Fandeval, the ghost city of Soriglass, and the bearfolk halls of Merrymead and the Moonlit Glades. The darker lands include the Twilight Empire of the darakhul, the reeking roachling pits of the Whispering Plains, and Oshragora, the City Fallen into Shadow. See [[Peoples of Shadow]] for the peoples themselves and [[Fey Courts of Shadow]] for the courts.

## The Realms

- [[Fandeval]], the City of Goblins: a free shadow goblin city where the Lethe meets the [[The Ever River|Styx]], famed for its moon towers
- [[Groves of the Wardens]]: the bearfolk druid groves where shadow gnawers train against shadow
- [[Merrymead]], the City of Revels: a bearfolk crossroads city of honey, mead, and the summer Honey Fair
- [[Moonlit Glades]]: the bearfolk nation of the far north, the brightest refuge in the realm
- [[Oshragora]], the City Fallen into Shadow: the drifting, time-torn ruin of the umbral vampires
- [[Soriglass]], the City of Shades: a vast mausoleum city of ghosts, shades, and requiem on the Styx
- [[Tenebrous Plain]]: the grassland of Oma Rattenfanger, the Keeper of the Plain
- [[Pits of the Roachlings]]: sinking pits and tunnels of the roachlings on the Whispering Plains
- [[Twilight Empire]]: the exiled darakhul empire of the Black Iron Depths
