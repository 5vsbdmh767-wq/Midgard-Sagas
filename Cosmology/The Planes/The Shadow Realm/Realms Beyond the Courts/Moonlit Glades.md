The capital is Solvheim (population 12,000), and other settlements include Eagle Grove, Hawthorne Grove, Leaping Salmon Grove, Sleeper's Grove, Three Hives Grove, and Veiled Grove (see [[Groves of the Wardens]]). The Glades' symbol is a crowned bear rampant with sword and claws.

The Moonlit Glades are the brightest ray of light and hope in the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]]. Here the [[Bearfolk|bearfolk]] live and tend their small patch of home against the dark, and their numbers grow steadily year by year. Though the bearfolk are natives of the realm now, they are as vulnerable to its corruption as any visitor from the mortal world. That generations have lived and died here without falling to its temptations is a minor miracle, and a sign that the bearfolk understand shadow's temptations better than most and that their druids and gods protect them.

## The Moonswept North

The Moonlit Glades lie deep in a black pine forest in the Shadow Realm's far north, corresponding roughly to [[Björnrike]] and the lands of ice and snow in the mortal world, and the moon shines on them as if blessing the bearfolk in their struggle. Shadow roads to [[Soriglass]] and the fey courts are the most reliable way in, and the bearfolk have wrested control of those roads from the shadow fey. Cairns of stone and silver mark the borders of the Glades, always bathed in moonlight, and bearfolk druids hold rituals at them to renew the magic that keeps shadow's touch at bay. Every bearfolk settlement has a standing stone or cairn at its center as a second layer of protection.

## The Bear Kingdom of Midgard

The bearfolk came from Björnrike, the Bear Kingdom in the [[Northlands]] of Midgard. Its ruler, Mesikämmen the Bear King, foresaw a terrible doom growing in the Shadow Realm that threatened all his people. He gathered his most trusted warriors and druids and charged them with an impossible task: to enter the Shadow Realm and fight the corruption at its source. They set out on a forbidden shadow road without hesitation and never looked back.

## A Land Kept Pure

Fairly safe from the realm's more powerful denizens, the bearfolk have made the dark land their own. The Moonlit Glades hold more pure resources than anywhere else in the realm, and the bearfolk fight furiously to keep them. Their streams run clear, glittering like rivers of molten silver under the moon, and the spirits that haunt the rest of the realm's waters grow quiet within their borders, so travelers can drink unharmed. The bearfolk tribes keep a strong druidic tradition and worship their own gods, [[Bengta]] and [[Umbeserno]], as well as the Green Gods in their masks of Freyr and Freyja. Their faithful spend most of their time tending crops, fruits and vegetables free of shadow corruption, and even the beasts here are more like their mortal counterparts, their meat wholesome and nourishing. Few things anger the bearfolk faster or more violently than poachers.

Settlements in the Glades are small and scattered, but each stays in close touch with the others. Runners, hunters, druids, and priests make regular rounds between the villages and the capital at Solvheim, carrying news and requests for aid and keeping the tribes close.

## The Peoples of the Glades

The Glades were once all bearfolk, but over the years they have taken in humans, elves, and others. Folk of other races from the Kingdom of the Bear came first to keep the colony in touch with its kin in the mortal world, and many stayed and joined the bearfolk communities. [[Humans]] are the most numerous newcomers, but a company of reaver dwarves who honor bear spirit totems also found their way here and settled. They are invaluable, since they are among the few sailors willing to brave the fog-shrouded black waters of the Frost Gulf. Even they don't go far from land, but their boats give the bearfolk and their allies fast access to other parts of the realm when the roads are too dangerous or don't go where they need. [[The Ever River|The Styx]] and the Lethe are far safer for these moonlit reavers, who now and then raid darakhul, goblin, and shadow fey villages along the rivers.

Some renegade shadow fey call the Glades home, though they have to go to extraordinary lengths to prove their good intentions and win the bearfolk's trust. [[Elfmarked|Half-elves]] and a fair number of alseid and werebears live among the population too. The Glades have a calming effect on werebears: even at the full moon their more destructive urges are curbed, and werebears often seek the way here to live out their lives in righteous service with less fear of losing themselves to the curse.

## Rulers of the Glades

Despite their differences, the peoples of the Glades think of one another as family. They are ruled by a diarchy of the chieftain and the hierophant. The chieftain oversees defense and civil government and is the final authority in disputes between tribal leaders. The hierophant is the spiritual center of the Glades and the most trusted counselor on magical matters: raising new druids and clerics, shadow corruption, the flow of the ebon tides, the care of the boundary stones, and the like.

The chieftain, Gulfwyr Moonrage, is a massive grizzlehide bearfolk werebear whose pitch-black fur is heavily frosted with silver. Terrifying in battle, he has earned his deed name hundreds of times over, turning his rage on his foes in defense of the Glades. His restraint with the werebear curse is the stuff of legend, and he has never infected anyone. Outside battle he is wise and fair, and many already consider him a living ancestor. He tolerates no foolishness in deciding disputes and is quick to punish anyone who brings him frivolous ones; in the depths of the Shadow Realm, there is always more at stake than spite or selfishness.

The hierophant, Ernalda Berlasdottir, is the spiritual and magical counterpart to Gulfwyr's strength and rule. A slender but tough purifier bearfolk, she decorates her long red-gold fur with braids, beads, feathers, and silver charms. She is the most powerful druid in the Glades and watches closely for any shadow incursion into her home. She is the greatest source of healing and restorative lore in the whole realm; there is no affliction she can't identify, even if she can't always cure it. She spends her time maintaining the moonlit wards and pursuing cases of shadow corruption. She wavers between soft-spoken and sharply decisive, as if not quite in step with what goes on around her, but it would be a mistake to think her ignorant: her distraction comes from feeling the struggle of light against shadow and keeping constant watch for any weakness in the light.

## Elder Whitepaw

A strange sight in the Glades is Elder Whitepaw, a venerable bearfolk shaman with snow-white fur who calls no settlement home and wanders from town to town. His manner can be odd and off-putting: he talks at random, answers questions no one asked, and shifts without warning from talking with the people in front of him to talking with figments, or with beings no one else can perceive. For all that, he is the only bearfolk besides the chieftain with a direct line to Hierophant Ernalda, and his wandering is not as random as it looks. He is more in tune with the healing and awakening spirits of nature in the cleansed Glades than anyone, and he knows a great deal about the spread of shadow corruption and the influence of dark powers such as the umbral vampires, the cultists of the [[Black Goat|Black Goat of the Woods]], and the undead, though getting that knowledge out of him is often hard. He often stumbles onto agents of the Lantern Bearers or visiting adventurers and gives them a task of great importance. Those who do well, or who show kindness to people in need, earn his favor and the goodwill of the bearfolk.

## Against the Darkness

When the bearfolk first walked the shadow roads, they had only the faintest idea of what awaited them or even what enemy they had come to fight. At first the danger seemed small: the occasional shadow or unquiet spirit, predators hungry for a first taste of uncorrupted flesh, all easily handled, and their remote home kept them away from the realm's worst creatures. Too soon they learned that the true danger of the Shadow Realm is one's own imperfect nature. Corruption crept into their ranks and divided them. Melancholy became paranoia, and paranoia became conflict. Those who resisted tried in vain to help the rest, which only fed the fire, and before long the corrupted bearfolk turned on their own kin. The blood they spilled drew the umbral vampires, who one day were simply there, rising from dark ruins the bearfolk had never seen before. The warriors fought bravely, but with their numbers thinned by corruption they knew they were doomed.

Then the druids' last desperate plan succeeded. With the last drops of pure water brought from home and silver mined in the Shadow Realm, they drove back the darkness that empowered the invaders. In that victory the bearfolk learned how shadow corruption, together with betrayal, murder, and the despair that follows, bores holes in the veil between the Shadow Realm and the mortal world, and how the umbral vampires slip through those widening cracks, drawn by their timeless nature to places where shadow bleeds into the world. In the light that shone down from the heavens, the bearfolk saw the truth that had brought them here and resolved to fight the spread of shadow from within. They rallied and drove the vampires back into their ruins, and those strange slivers of the [[Oshragora|City Fallen into Shadow]] melted into the dark as the moonlight overtook them.

What they know sets the bearfolk against two great powers of the realm, the darakhul of the [[Twilight Empire]] and the umbral vampires of Oshragora. Both have a terrible knack for finding rifts and cracks to exploit, and the bearfolk know they threaten to push shadow corruption on a large scale into their home here and into the mother lands in the mortal world.

## Allies in the Light

The Moonlit Glades are a solid bastion of hope, protected from shadow corruption and from the worst of the realm's creatures by their moonlit wards. The bearfolk know how pervasive shadow is, and they and their allies are sometimes corrupted, but they hold the key to long-term survival: a ritual handed down from the first settlers that scours away shadow corruption with cleansing moonlight. The druids regularly renew the moonlit wards around the borders and at the heart of every settlement. Because they can remove shadow's touch, the Glades became the center of the network called the [[Peoples of Shadow#The Lantern Bearers|Lantern Bearers]]. The bearfolk never teach their purification rites to outsiders, for fear the powers of shadow would find a way to corrupt or get around them, but they are willing to use them to help outsiders fighting shadow corruption. The safety and purity of the Glades are known across the realm, and the hidden allies of light know they may be their only hope in desperate times.

A few bearfolk settlements have been founded far from the Glades, each built around a standing stone inlaid with silver or a cairn of stone and silver that holds back the shadow. They usually lie away from well-traveled places and always have a purpose, most often watching a spot where the barrier between the Shadow Realm and the mortal world has worn thin, to make sure nothing pushes through. They also serve as sanctuaries, and last-ditch salvation, for friendly travelers lost in the dark.

Guides from the Glades are fascinated by the witchlights, the strange glowing crystals that float through the realm. The bearfolk aren't sure what to make of them. They have seen witchlights lead travelers out of danger again and again, and sometimes to exactly what lost souls needed to survive. But witchlights look too much like will-o'-wisps for the bearfolk to trust them fully, and though no one has confirmed a witchlight harming or misleading a traveler, the bearfolk keep a healthy caution.
