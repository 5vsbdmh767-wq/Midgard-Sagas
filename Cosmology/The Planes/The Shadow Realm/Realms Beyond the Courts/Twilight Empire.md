The capital is Zhurakh, the City of Bone and Iron (population 15,000), and the other enclaves are Chaingard, Gallwheor, Blackstone, Evernight, Ossean, and Bargol's Watch. The empire's symbol is a white skull crowned with green fire. Its slaves are bearfolk, elves, umbral humans, shadow goblins, and shadow fey.

The [[The Darakhul Imperium|Empire of Ghouls]] tunnels beneath the [[The Crossroads|Crossroads]] of Midgard and beyond, but the darakhul are active in the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]] too, and their empire there is, if anything, even more dangerous. It is allied with shadow dragons and a cave dragon lich called Dreadwing, and its ability to walk shadow roads and to reach [[Evermaw]], the planar realm of the undead, makes its ghouls especially dangerous. Two things set them apart from the ghouls of the mortal world: they can feed on the strange trees of the Crackling Forests, so they hunt and devour humanoids somewhat less often, and they are fewer here than in other undead realms, or even in many human lands.

North and west of the Blackwood where the shadow fey live, a great pit gouges the Shadow Realm. A series of deep depressions dives into thick shadow and dark tunnels, and a scholar of Midgard's geography would find its location sinister, for it lies in the shadow of the [[The Ironcrags|Ironcrags]], the dwarven heartland. In these Black Iron Depths thrives an exiled arm of the Empire of Ghouls known as the Twilight Empire. Its darkness-touched ghouls absorb the essence of shadow through the flesh of their meals, the shadow fey and other living creatures of the realm, and every ghoul of the Twilight Empire bears its mark: teeth gone pitch black.

## Emperor in Exile

Vilmos Marquering, called the Black Fang, once commanded the Iron Legion in the White City. His coup was well planned and brilliantly executed: he turned key conspirators to his cause, several of them close to the true emperor, and killed those he couldn't turn and replaced them with loyal cat's-paws. When he struck, he learned how deep the emperor's secrets ran and how badly his careful preparations fell short. He barely escaped, and the mightiest warriors of his legion were turned to dust by the emperor's magic. With what must have been [[Vardesain]]'s help, he and his surviving allies fled into the Underworld. Starving and broken, they found a shadow road in a lightless cavern full of twisted abominations; the bloodshed of the battle there woke the road just long enough for the traitor legion to march into the Shadow Realm. The ghouls collapsed the tunnel behind them, sealing the road against pursuit, and safe from reprisal, set about building a new empire.

The Twilight Emperor now rules from his Black Iron Throne in the heart of the Black Iron Depths. He reforged the battered remnants of his coup into a fighting force and named his five most trusted lieutenants dukes, each commanding a city of the Depths and raising a twilight legion to defend it in his name. He remembers that betrayal comes from those closest to power, and he is wary even of them. He has not forgotten his humiliating defeat, either, and he still dreams of returning to the mortal world one day, striking at the White City, and taking the throne of Emperor Nicoforus.

## Dealings with Darkness

The Twilight Empire has grown quickly. Its ghouls raid the living regularly, fey communities, the [[Moonlit Glades]], goblin settlements, and any other breathing creatures they can find, and they manage their food carefully, rarely tolerating a ravenous beggar ghoul among them. Living flesh is harder to come by in the Shadow Realm, so the twilight ghouls have made peaceful contact with the shadow fey courts. The shadow fey control most of the shadow roads, so they can arrange passage for ghoul raiding parties into the mortal world and make sure they come back safely with their cargo of "food." In return, the emperor lends legionnaires to help maintain roads through especially dangerous parts of the realm and roads that tend to draw unwanted trespassers.

The Queen of Night and Magic found the sudden appearance of a ghoul empire intriguing, and she amuses herself by keeping track of it. Lately she has grown concerned about the rising ghoul activity in and around [[Oshragora]], which somehow escapes her strongest magic and her cleverest spies. For now she contents herself with the strange magical relics the ghouls dig up and trade for favor, but that won't last; Her Majesty is not known for patience once her curiosity is roused.

Many of the empire's loyal ghouls know that just on the other side of a nearly impenetrable barrier lies a nation of flesh and blood unaware of the danger walking so close to it. They hunger for dwarf flesh and search for paths through the veil, hoping to open the way to slaves, food, and death in the [[The Ironcrags|Ironcrag Cantons]] and the Crossroads of the mortal world.

## The Road of Bones

Lately the twilight ghouls have worked to open paths to [[Evermaw]], the planar realm of the undead ruled by [[Mot]], god of the undead, and [[Vardesain]], god of hunger. Their black road, the Road of Bones, runs from the palace in Zhurakh straight to Evermaw and takes blood magic to open. Darakhul, satarre, vampires, fiends, and others find it a very useful, direct way between the planes, and the emperor collects a toll for its use. No one knows how the necrophages and priests of Vardesain built it, and the shades of [[Soriglass]] are wild with curiosity to find out. The road brings stability, trade, and income to the empire from both sides.

## Crackling Forests

These alien trees first grew in the Dry Lands of [[Evermaw]]. They are not green or woody but cartilaginous masses of splintered bone with leathery leaves, they have an air of menace, and they move rather more than ordinary trees do (though not enough to fight off a ghoul or a woodsman). Only a few grow in the Shadow Realm, imported from Evermaw, and raising more is very hard, because the ghouls devour the saplings too quickly for a real forest to take hold. Cut or broken, the trees bleed a black milk that undead find nourishing and pleasant, though the living call it rancid, foul, or rotten. Ghouls, vampires, and other corporeal undead love to crack open the hairy bark and gorge on the thick syrup, and for them a pint of black milk is as restoring as devouring a living creature.

## Places in the Empire

### Bargol's Watch

This small fortified manor and its outbuildings protect a village of tanners and bonecutters, and serve as the unofficial embassy between the darakhul and the fey, and now and then the umbral vampires, when Oshragora drifts close. Its steward, Captain Ingelmarr Rothand, is a hard case, a former leader of the legions' shock troops retired here after he lost his right hand to fey magic. He despises the shadow fey, gnomes, and anything light and cheerful. He keeps the holding in fine order, drills his small garrison often, and tolerates no nonsense and no laughter, carousing, or joy of any kind among his troops. He spends most of his time in the Great Tower, 70 feet tall, with a fine view of the nearby chasm. His troops mostly hate him, except his lieutenant, Valmira Brandorf, a warlock who spends most of her time conjuring spirits, imps, and animal spies to gather information on the manor's visitors. [[Bearfolk]] merchants, fey road wardens, and even gnomish distillers come by now and then to trade with the darakhul, or to trade news of where Oshragora is or where the elder shadow drakes are moving.

### Blackstone

Home to the empire's shock troops, the Ravenous Legion, Blackstone broods over one of the few roads into the Black Iron Depths, guarded by the gate fortress of Gloomhold. Its warden, Duke Wierdunn Bonehand, scours the population for ghouls strong and cruel enough to join the Ravenous Guard, a band of a hundred imperial ghouls. Blackstone is also home to the Temple of the Faceless Warden, the seat of [[Anu-Akma]]'s faith among the darakhul, led by the Protector of Souls Sen-Nefer.

### Chaingard

The largest settlement on the surface, at the edge of the Black Iron Depths, Chaingard is the forbidding main gateway into the empire and the receiving point for all its slave traffic. Duchess Angvyr Ssetha, the Lady of Chains, runs the slave supply with meticulous efficiency and enjoys greeting high-ranking envoys in a haughty, sneering manner. The Shackled Legion guards the walls and gates and herds the slaves with brutal efficiency, though for all its name it is a motley band of hunters, slavers, and guards more used to cruelty than to battle.

### Evernight

Hewn from the black stone and iron ore of the depths, Evernight sits on the shore of a dark lake. It is ruled by Duke Eloghar Vorghesht, high priest of [[Vardesain]], and it is the center of the empire's faith. A great cathedral to the ghoul god dominates the city, its black altar stained darker by the blood and flesh of countless sacrifices. The Midnight Company of 100 elite darakhul, warrior clerics and dark reflections of paladins among them, defends the city.

### Gallwheor

Where Blackstone produces terrifying shock troops, Gallwheor produces the empire's finest tactical minds. Duke Borag the Executioner, Warlord of Gallwheor, never lost a battle in the Empire of Ghouls or since the exile. His Headsmen are assassins and stealthy killers known for deadly strikes at the heart of an enemy force, and rumor says they have killed bearfolk nobles, fey ladies, and gnomish princes who spoke against the emperor.

### Kaerim's Deep

This deep canyon is widely believed to be the work of the ghouls, but no darakhul, bearfolk, or anyone else is known to live there. Pale giants and cave dragons are now and then seen on its narrow paths and tunnels, and most people stay away.

### Ossean

The first stop at the bottom of the steep and dangerous Clanking Descent from Chaingard down the sheer cliffs of the Black Iron Depths, Ossean gets first pick of every new shipment of slaves. Among its bone-clad buildings, the empire's necrophages labor and study under Duchess Mikalea Soulreaper, Lorekeeper of Ossean, and her apprentices, prying secrets from the flesh and souls of captives, guarded by the arcane and martial might of the Bone Company. Its ranks hold not only stout ghouls but dark wizards, darakhul necrophages, and eldritch knights who blend arcane magic with steel. Ossean is also home to a powerful priestess of Santerr Illosi, Her Witchlight Resplendence Tiberessa Vert, who has spent decades building a library of scrolls, tomes, and grimoires chronicling the ghouls' expansion and the unique fusion of shadow magic and necromancy. She has befriended nearly 100 witchlights, who seem to enjoy her company and sometimes carry her messages in a kind of luminous code; a dozen or more always surround her like a veil of lights.

### Zhurakh, City of Bone and Iron

The deepest city of the Black Iron Depths, Zhurakh is the seat of the Twilight Emperor and the unbeating heart of the empire. The emperor learned from his failed coup, and his secret police fill every level of the city, always watching for dissent, so would-be conspirators avoid it entirely. Nearby, the Endless Falls plunge into the black depths of the Shadow Realm, and this is the emperor's favorite place to deal with traitors and criminals, casting them over personally. The empire's most elite soldiers, the Blackmaw Legion, stand ready to enforce his will. Zhurakh is also the seat of the Carmine Temple of Vardesain and of the red-handed master of that faith, His Carmine Eminence and High Patriarch of Zhurakh, Devros Perghallen. The temple is carved from an unsettling red stone that seems to ripple and move in torchlight, so that its walls look like a bloody abattoir. It is a source of power for the whole priesthood, carved with sacred writings and a new testament of corruption that praises the lord of hunger and confirms his blessing on Emperor Vilmos. It is also the seat and first house of the inquisitors of Vardesain, who enforce the feeding laws and keep a watchful eye on darakhul and servants alike.
