The Shadow Realm is an echo of the worlds of mortals, made of elemental light and elemental darkness rather than earth and fire. Its lands are malleable. Places that are old, much-visited, or tied to names and legends hold their shape against the tides of shadow; the natives call them natural anchors. Other places wash away or sink silently beneath new terrain.

The realm is best known as the home of the [[Shadow Fey|shadow fey]], who rule it through their fey courts. Many lords and ladies claim to rule some part of it, but most such claims apply only to a fragment of the whole or to a particular season. Ultimate power rests with [[Sarastra]], the Queen of Night and Magic, who rules the Winter Court. The [[Court of the Moonlit King|Summer Court]] belongs to her estranged husband, the Moonlit King, who has not attended it in a very long time.

## Etiquette and Status

Shadow fey customs change by the week or the season, but a few things hold. The courts are impenetrable mazes or empty shells to anyone who lacks the status to deal with them. What the fey say is not necessarily what they mean. And harming a servant is considered dishonorable.

## The Shadow Realm and Zobeck

The shadow fey were once fey of the [[The Margreve|Margreve]]. Over 1,400 years ago, a ritual called the Heartwood Pact cursed them to the Shadow Realm and bound them to Sarastra's service, and it linked the hill where [[Castle Shadowcrag]] now stands to the realm. Centuries later, [[House Stross]] struck a bargain with the shadow fey for shadow magic and the right to rule [[Zobeck]]. Today the shadow fey keep an embassy in the city, Winter's Kiss.
