A minor court sworn to the [[Court of Mice and Ravens]], the Court of Sparrows is a tan stone stronghold that has taken a recent turn. It began as a whimsical, or at least polite, court founded by courtfolk halflings who bred sparrows, ducks, geese, and chickens for royal banquets, a place of jesters and jugglers where visitors came for the food and the fun. Its castellan was Traverty Peaseblossom, a halfling bard loved by his people and his poultry alike. A few years ago, King Marrow, a [[Trollkin|trollkin]] warlord, stormed it with a ruthless company of trollkin, bearfolk, and umbral humans and made it his base. He kept the halflings, and his company's arrows are now fletched with very fine goose feathers. Prince Peaseblossom escaped by a secret passage and may now be a wandering minstrel at some other court.

Tradition says a conquered court should change its name, to something like the Court of Trolls or the Court of Hammers, but Marrow wasn't quite bright enough to do it, and that has worked out well: so long as the eggs and ducks keep flowing to the great courts nearby, nobody seems to mind who is in charge. Marrow raids the shadow roads and hunts in the Queen's Forest, eating a great many eggs all the while. Some of the halflings slip away to serve elsewhere, but many find that having a big trollkin warlord "in charge" spares them a lot of tedious diplomacy and bootlicking. Real power is shifting to Marrow's son and heir, Mardovac the Foul, the Royal Keeper of Ducks, who has more knack for wringing coin out of the flocks than for banditry. King Reynard finds the whole thing hilarious, though if a shipment of fresh duck ever arrives late, he will surely pay Marrow a visit "as one ruler to another," and perhaps install a more trustworthy vassal, or just stay a while, until the eggs run out.
