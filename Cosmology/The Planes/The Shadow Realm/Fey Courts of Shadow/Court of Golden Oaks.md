The Court of Golden Oaks is the strongest holding of the sable elves in the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]], ruled by the golden-haired Queen Valda, in whom joy and sternness are fused into careful planning, shrewd insight, and arcane power. As a young bride, most of the court thought her a charming, well-read ornament rather than a ruler. Then her husband, the Crow Prince, left the court to seek wisdom from [[Yggdrasil]], and she had to put down her books, calligraphy, and arcane musings and take command to keep the sable elves' fortress from falling. She has outwitted and bewitched suitors, foes, and assassins since, and won her people's hearts. She has spent little time wondering where the Crow Prince is, because she has enemies enough among the shadow fey, the satarre, and the gnomes. The court sits on a planar nexus, which both strengthens it, when outsiders come to trade and pay well for goods from afar, and endangers it, when they come to take its position for themselves. Its patron is Valeros, god of the elves, and its people value martial strength, arcane power, and personal courage.

## The Golden Branch of Yggdrasil

The court's strength is bound up with the Golden Oak, a World Tree, with its paths and its mystical acorns. The portal into its branches is treated like any other gate or road out of the court, watched and respected, since the Golden Oak connects easily to Grenstad, the city of the [[Ratatosk|ratatosk]] on Yggdrasil, to the Uppermarket of a pocket plane devoted to trade, and to [[Wotan]]'s Tree in the snowy northlands of the mortal world. Ratatosk are a small but loudly chittering minority at court, and many of the boldest serve as guides and heralds to other realms. The merchants and travelers who come through the Golden Oak include celestials, humans, ratatosk, tieflings, satarre, and even fiends and fey lords, and nearly all are let in so long as they swear to keep the peace within the court and its lands. Those who refuse are turned back, with wand and sword if need be.

## Tournaments of Oak and Ash

[[Humans]], shadow fey, and others love the court for the contests and feats of strength Queen Valda sponsors. As in many elven courts, she declares the Tournaments of Oak and Ash when the season seems right and invites all comers to prove themselves as archers, knights, or wizards, in jousts, melees, target shooting and shooting on the wing, battle magic, counterspell duels, horsemanship, drawing shadow power, or tilting at a golden ring. Sometimes she adds poetry or dance, to show that a knight's worth isn't all in their arms. Winners take prizes and renown among elves, shadow fey, and gnomes alike. Archery is a favorite pastime, and the court bowyer, Vilhopelli, makes astonishing bows, arrows, and fletching, some of them enchanted, from the Golden Oak's own wood, though always in exchange for great favors or magic; his nickname, "the Generous," is a joke at the expense of a notoriously tight fist.

## College of the Acorn

Outsiders come for the tourneys, but the queen and her advisors care more about study and about training new battle mages for the Company of Golden Wands, the court's arcane soldiers. They are trained by Arcane Proctor Justee Arnosh, a shadow goblin wizard of great power and, unusually for a shadow goblin, great humility, who delights in serving the queen and teaching young humans, sable elves, and shadow goblins battlefield magic. Ratatosk rune wizards and bearfolk druids sometimes add to the curriculum, and Court Sorcerer Lumi-Livia and the golden-furred bearfolk librarian Drossilda both teach. The college asks every visiting druid, wizard, and sorcerer to address the students on a topic of interest.
