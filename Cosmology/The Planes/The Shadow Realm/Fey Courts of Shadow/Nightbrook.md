A minor court sworn to the [[Court of Night and Magic]], Nightbrook occupies a hunched building of stone and black wood in the Sable Forest, more temple than noble seat, its walls and domed roof covered in strange glyphs. It stands at the headwaters of the Nightbrook, a small black stream that eventually joins the River Lethe. The shadow fey of Nightbrook seem strangely at home on the realm's waters. They live and work along the brook, ferrying people across or downriver on small boats and rafts, for a price, and the vengeful spirits that swarm in the black rivers seem to ignore them, though no one outside the court knows why. Three sisters rule it, sour-faced shadow fey with back-curving horns and long black hair streaked with white. For all their glowering, the Ladies of Nightbrook welcome guests, especially mortal ones, with surprisingly warm hospitality, and they are always interested in buying memories.
