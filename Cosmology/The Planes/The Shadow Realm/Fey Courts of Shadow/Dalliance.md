Where the [[Nightbrook]] spills into the River Lethe, Dalliance sits like a fat but happy leech. Its people make their living trading with forest folk and trappers and working as woodsmen and hunters for the [[Court of Night and Magic]] and the Court of Roses. Water fey from Nightbrook visit with dark philters, and a goblin troupe of actors or jugglers is often in town. But the town's real wealth comes from pleasure, gambling, and debauchery for anyone who can pay. Three pleasure houses, the River House, the Red Door, and the Cat's Paw, are fine stone-walled manors that cater to everyone from the hunter wanting wine and friendly company to dissolute gnome princes demanding exotic entertainments. Any intoxication and any company is available to whoever asks the right house with a fat purse. It's a well-known secret, but Dalliance keeps its secrets very well: what happens behind its doors doesn't leave town.

The Master of Shades, Lord Correto Anders, keeps a string of ghosts, haunts, and shades bringing the addictive, soul-bending requiem from [[Soriglass]], and it is offered, with other pleasures, at the small River House. The Master of Coin, Tristoffos, pays very well for gossip, news, and well-founded speculation, and is most often found questioning the staff of the Red Door, which makes many think his information is first for the baroness's ear. The river near Dalliance is busy with water fey, river spirits, shadow river lords, and rusalka, led by a lorelei who falls in love with various fey at various times. She sings in the brightest moonlight and welcomes gifts from suitors and songs and poems from bards and travelers, and in return for song and elegance she grants safe passage along the river as far as [[Corremel]].
