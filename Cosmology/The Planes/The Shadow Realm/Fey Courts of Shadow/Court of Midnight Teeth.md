Gnomes are peculiar creatures, all pointed teeth, vanity, and blue hair, or in the case of the Countess of Midnight Teeth, all three. Countess Phylomara Gladrienne, an influential and loyal vassal of the [[Court of Night and Magic]] (and sometimes of the [[Court of the Moonlit King|Moonlit King]]), is a refined and fashionable gnome in fine silks and gleaming jewels that set off her azure hair, which she styles elaborately and never the same way twice, setting the fashion for her courtiers. Beneath the civility and style, there is blood and there are secrets. Manicured lawns, eerily lifelike statues, and fountains bubbling with inky midnight water surround her home, and string music drifts through the gloom, drawing travelers to this lantern-dappled oasis. The Mage's Road runs from this court to [[Magocracy of Allain|Bemmea]], and the road to the gnomish city of Hexen in [[Niemheim]] ends in one of its mirrors.

## The Blue Barbers

The countess's gnome courtiers also serve as her agents abroad. They call themselves the Blue Barbers, after their blue hair and their wish to bring style to everyone they meet, and they work as valets, barbers, art dealers, decorators, and stylists of every kind. They are polite, sociable, and knowledgeable, and happy to share what they know while they work. Their guildmaster, Garrolo Goldenlock, and his associates are bards, bandit captains, peddlers, and spymasters of various stripes, and their work keeps the gnomes well informed and their enemies nervous. Sabotage, blackmail, and assassination are all part of their very broad and very ruthless portfolio.

Their halls are a white marble tower and a maze of manor-like wings, tunnels, and two-story banquet halls thrown together in a style that can charitably be called improvised. There is so little logic to it that guides are needed to reach even the guildmaster's audience chamber, the purser's office, the scriptorium, or the mess hall, while dozens of Blue Barbers flit about knowing exactly where they're going. It's all meant to confound visitors and leave them worn out before they reach anyone important: dead-end stairs, doors that lead nowhere (though a Blue Barber may have just come out of one), secret passages, rotating fireplaces, chimneys with hidden stairs. The places visitors most want to go are usually locked and marked "Closed for Cleaning," or just "Closed." Breaking in is grounds for expulsion or prison, though often only the main door is closed and a side, hidden, or servants' entrance works fine. Strangest of all is a shrine to Alquam, Demon Lord of Night, whose azure dome can be seen from outside, where a group called the Alabar Chorus, including half a dozen famous gnomish bards and jesters, sings praise to the darkness. They insist it's just a friendly gathering for wine, song, and macabre stories.

## Mirrors and the Keening Tower

The countess makes great use of mirror portals, scrying, and divination, which has made an enemy of the Duchess Shelesorra, Demon of Lies and Cruelty, who considers the mirror realms her own and takes the countess's mirrors as an insult. Phylomara guards her mirrors closely and employs a stubborn, sharp-eyed erina named Ingo to watch, polish, and report anything odd in them. Even so, demonic visits and even attacks out of Sooleleed, the Mirror Hell of Lies, are not unknown.

The Tower of the Keening Winds, a gray-black prison for rebels, nobles, traitors, and unfaithful royal spouses, is heavily warded so that invisible or ethereal intruders show as glittering gold or silver outlines. Its prisoners range from the serious to the ridiculous, among them a ghost who swindled the treasury, a pretender to the throne of the Golden Oaks, a heretical seer of [[Sarastra]], a shadow goblin warlord, and assorted arcane frauds. The most famous is Marina Nikla, Mistress of Pearls, the countess's longtime companion, who left her for a lover among the gnomes of the [[Court of Pale Roses]]; when Nikla came back on a diplomatic visit, the heartbroken countess seized her and locked her up, and relations with the Court of Pale Roses have been icy ever since.
