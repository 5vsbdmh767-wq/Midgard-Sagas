The stars rarely shine over the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]], but above its clouds rests the most enigmatic of dark fey gatherings: the Court of One Million Stars. Delicate spires of silver and pearl rise from great discs spun of dreamstuff and starlight, clustered together and joined by graceful bridges of moonbeams woven with silver. Prince Valendan, a noble [[Satyrs|selang]] wizard, rules with aloof, quiet wisdom. He makes a point of meeting every visitor at least once, though few get a second audience. His strange looks repel some, but he is far less bound to chaos and evil than most selang.

## The Starry Court

The heart of the court is the Starry Court, a magnificent spire of pearl and silver spiraling up like a unicorn's horn from the central audience chamber, a seven-sided hall of white stone with a gate on each side. Courtiers gather in its wings, the higher their station the nearer the center, where the prince sits on a tripod seat of silver and silk. The spire holds his chambers and those of his consorts and the other royals, with balconies looking up at the stars and down on the realm below. Gardens of plants from shadow and the mortal world surround it, laced with walkways of crushed pearl lit by floating pastel lanterns, and each of seven outer gardens anchors a moonlight bridge to another part of the court. One bridge is blackened and shattered, ending a few yards out, destroyed long ago by terrible magic. Fey of every kind come to study illumination magic, the stars, and their movements; dark pixie scholars may spend decades tracking a single star, recording every hint of prophecy as they wait for the momentous event they call the starfall.

The largest community of starry courtiers lives in the Spiral-Downs, a chain of platforms hanging in the sky, joined to the Starry Court by a moon bridge and to each other by staircases of moonlight, rising in a spiral of status: the lowest and largest holds the humblest courtiers, and each higher platform is smaller, finer, and more exclusive, up to the loftiest, with its lush gardens, ethereal music, and spires of starlight and dreams.

## When the Stars Walk

The stars are not just points of light in an uncaring sky. They are living things of power and unfathomable knowledge, and when great events shake the world, a star may leave its bower of black velvet and fall to the mortal world or the Shadow Realm. There it becomes a cloaked and hooded being of luminous beauty and impenetrable mystery, wrapped in starlight. Star emissaries are aloof, and when they do speak, being understood isn't guaranteed; they reuse words with wildly different meanings from one part of a sentence to the next, and even magic like *comprehend languages* never makes them perfectly clear. More unsettling still, the courtiers believe that when a star's visit ends, it never leaves alone, though who or what goes with it varies and isn't always obvious. Starfalls are rare, but one star emissary lives at the court permanently: First Consort Shemani.

The heavens have taken a particular interest in the court. Cylentha the Silver Mistress, a deva, came down to the Shadow Realm nearly two hundred years ago and has never gone home; many believe she is searching for something. She advises the prince, who keeps her counsel very private, and certain courtiers would give a trove of stardust diamonds to know why a celestial spends so long at a court full of fey decadence and Void followers. She spends most of her time in her bare observatory, a round building under a dome of iridescent pearl circled by seven blue-white lights like tiny stars, from which any star, nebula, or the farthest reaches of the utterdark can be seen, perhaps even a glimpse of the Void.

## Whispers from the Void

Reaching toward the stars means drawing close to the cold emptiness between them, where ancient things stir and whisper. The careless or foolish can listen, and learn, at a price. Prince Valendan hasn't forbidden the study of Void magic, though he takes an interest in anyone who seeks it. One of the court's most momentous recent arrivals is Phaerliggath, an ancient Void dragon from the cold dark beyond the stars, whose mind has badly frayed from all he has seen. He claimed a whole spire of the court, once magnificent and now crumbling, called Voidreach, and rarely leaves it. He entertains visitors now and then, and seems disturbingly willing to share the Void's forbidden secrets, but only those truly captivated by the Void ever visit twice. Lady Cylentha visits the dragon's tower from time to time and politely questions anyone who has spoken with him.

## Reaching the Court

Getting there is no easy thing. Unseen paths lead up from the surface that are nearly impossible to follow without an invitation or a guide. Sometimes a witchlight leads a pilgrim up to the stars, leaving a glittering trail that stays solid underfoot. Shadow fey wizards speak quietly of star bridges that appear only in certain places and at certain alignments of the stars, and a rare glimpse of the sky can give a sharp enough mind the insight to work out where and when the next will open. Every path ends at a gatehouse guarded against hostile incursions, and the most formidable, Vigil, a hovering disc of close-fitted tiles with horn-like spires around its edge and a bronze dome at its center, stands over the only path from the surface to the Starry Court. Its silver gates look delicate and are nearly indestructible, and its wardens, the fiercest warriors of the court, question every newcomer closely.
