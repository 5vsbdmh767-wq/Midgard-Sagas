The coldest and most desolate of the fey courts is ruled by Kalaslurr, the Lady of Ash and Sorrow, a ghost whose touch and voice destroy most mortals who meet her. Ice bogies, snow maidens, ice demons, and a thriving community of quicksteps live there, all seemingly immune to her song and terrible presence. Most of the court keeps well away from her, except her ghostly court of devoted shades, all immune to her. The head chamberlain, Porthan, a shade, is also the chief priest of [[Charun]] and one of the few voices the queen listens to when deciding where to send her demons next.

The court's tall white tower is unmistakable, and at most hours a slow, low howling comes from it: the wails of the queen, whose voice draws in and slowly devours any visitor she finds entertaining, old lovers, foes, rivals, or simply those whose looks she likes. An audience with her is easy to get and hard to survive, because the distant moan becomes a song of terror inside the tower. Her wyrd gnome servants are often deafened by it, or are said to destroy their own eardrums to serve her better. The only servant unafraid of her singing is the bearfolk bard Umberell, who long ago learned the knack of a countersong, and who will teach it to any trustworthy and honest bard or singer.

In the Demon's Hall, a black stone binds seven ice demons who serve the queen without hesitation, tearing apart her foes, fetching gentians from alpine meadows, and threatening travelers for tolls or news: First Hand, Second Hand, All Thumbs, Wicked Frost, Silver Eye, Little Cook, and Wanderer. In the Frost Bogie Workshop, the ice bogies, small snow fey who obey anyone but worship the demons and the court's officers, carve wooden trinkets, card and spin wool, and clean and tan the furs of fox, rabbit, ermine, and beaver when they aren't fetching ale or gathering pine nuts.
