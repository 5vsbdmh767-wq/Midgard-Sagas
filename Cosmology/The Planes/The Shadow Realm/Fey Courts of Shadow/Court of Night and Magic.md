Ruled by [[Sarastra]], the Queen of Night and Magic, this is one of the largest and best-organized shadow fey courts, sitting astride several shadow roads with towers and wards to control them. It has long dealt with mortals, and with powerful demons and devils too, including Akyishigal, Lord of Cockroaches, and [[Alquam]], Lord of Night.

The Court of Night and Magic is the seat of the Winter Court, and the true rulers of the shadow fey live in its upper reaches, in the queen's Winter Palace and the Royal Halls. Its counterpart, the [[Court of the Moonlit King|Summer Court]], belongs to the queen's estranged husband, the Moonlit King, who has not attended it in a very long time, and the courts whisper that the queen would like to replace her absent king. The court itself runs on consorts, courtesans, servants, and the usual petitioners crowding the entrance to the Royal Halls. The Gray Ladies live there too: three ancient shadow fey crones with a fondness for spiders and weaving, who spend their days spinning silk and whispering to one another, looking for people they can use to bend fate to their whims.

Ultimate power over shadow fey society rests with Her Transcendent Majesty Sarastra Aestruum. Only the most respected supplicants are invited into the Royal Halls for an audience, and most get only a sliver of her attention: they make their bow, ask their favor, and are handed off to court functionaries. A lucky few catch her interest. Whatever it looks like, that always serves the queen's designs or a passing whim, and alliances and rivalries spring up instantly around anyone so "blessed." The Royal Halls also house honored guests, usually envoys from other courts. Somewhere in the court is a strange pit called the Black Well of Night, where the Demon Lord of Roaches and his roachlings are kept in the queen's service.

## Visiting the Court

First-time visitors find the courts deserted. Graceful bridges of black stone span deep chasms and marble colonnades lead through fantastic gardens to the inner halls, but the only ones there to greet a supplicant are constructed footmen, empty suits of graceful plate armor, and room after room stands empty but for crows, rooks, owls, and the odd scurrying mouse. It is an illusion, and the courts' first defense against uninvited guests. A visitor with an invitation from a shadow fey of enough standing, or who can convince the unseen fey that they are worth talking to, sees the illusion fade and the courts revealed in all their glory. Magic weaker than *true seeing* can't pierce it.

## The Lower Court

Below the Winter Palace and the Royal Halls, the Lower Court houses the shadow fey guards and soldiers and the Goblin Court, ruled as a tyranny by Moggo the Chamberlain. The goblins won't betray their shadow fey masters outright, but they love to gossip and gripe, which makes them excellent sources of information. Low-status visitors who break the rules of the courts are sent to Moggo for judgment, and the Goblin Court runs on betrayal, flattery, bribes, and blood.

## Trade with Midgard

The shadow fey keep formal embassies or at least nominal contact with every thinking people of the realm, and in recent years they have reopened dealings with the mortals of Midgard. Shadow fey blades, jewelry, curios, and marvelously potent wines now sell in [[Zobeck]] and beyond the [[The Crossroads|Crossroads]]. The fey care little for gold in return. What they want is memories, painful ones above all, which they take from willing mortals through arcane rites and distill into strange spirits, each with its own flavor. Asked why, they always give the same answer: "It warms us." Merchants swear by the profits, since a painful memory seems a paltry price for a chest of fey-forged moonsteel, though a few wonder about giving up their lives piece by piece, and some have begun to think about finding other donors.

The courts also broker secrets and power. Many shadow fey remember ancient history as if it were yesterday, and lost tombs, forgotten artifacts, and old knowledge have all been recovered with their help. They sell illumination magic and shadow magic too, asking only that the student come to the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]] as their guest for tutelage, whatever the risk of shadow corruption. Whispers abound that by dark means the shadow fey can turn a sufficiently corrupted mortal into one of their own, and that the fiends called heralds of darkness, who gather cults that worship them, can do the same for any human or goblin who accepts the gift.

## Lands of the Court

Besides [[Corremel]], the court's main holdings include these:

### Hunt's Retreat

Population 7,700. A stone lodge halfway along a hunting path between the road through the forest and the cliffs of the courts, a haven for the hunters and trappers of the Blackwood and the Forest of the Firebirds and a shelter for lost travelers, though it has its own dangers. The Black Prince lodges there when he hunts, and the Lord of the Hunt camps outside with his retinue before sounding the horn and charging into the forest.

### Dalliance

Population 5,300. See [[Dalliance]].

### Wormwood

Population 3,500. The gate town in the northernmost quarter of the shadow fey lands, guarding the road south through the dark woods to the courts and on to Corremel, and holding back the haunts and ghouls of the plains and the umbral vampires of [[Oshragora]]. A strong wall with shadow oak gates surrounds it, and everyone who lives there is a battle-tested soldier, with a few spies and assassins mixed in. They treat travelers with heavy suspicion, and anyone carrying relics out of Oshragora without leave from the queen or another high noble with something worse.

### The Tower of Horn and Gold

Population 600. See [[Tower of Horn and Gold]].

### Nightbrook

Population 100. See [[Nightbrook]].

### The Sable Court

Population 100. A small forest court in the branches of great shadow oaks high above the floor of the Sable Forest. It looks like a high elven forest home, but it reminds the locals, the Ladies of Nightbrook in particular, that they are the queen's vassals. Baroness Yleira the Swift, appointed by the queen, keeps its shadow fey and hounds. It can be reached only by flying, by a dangerous climb within sight and bowshot of the sentries, or by lifts guarded at all times. It is the home and training ground of the shadow fey forest hunters; shadow fey unfit for court life often try to join them, and deadly assassins, forest guides, and iron-tough trailblazers come out of their ranks.
