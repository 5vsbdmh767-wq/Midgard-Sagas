The Summer Court belongs to Ludomir Imbrium XVI, the Moonlit King, [[Sarastra]]'s estranged husband and once her co-ruler. He has not attended it in a very long time. Most of his followers drifted away long ago, and what remains of his court is a set of names, spells, philters, and titles and a handful of servants, among them lunar devils and quicksteps. Most who have any stake in the matter believe he still holds his court and simply chooses not to attend it.
