The gods of the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]] are a strange mix of the gods of the fey and the gods of the dark, with a few borrowed from the bearfolk, the [[Nurians]], and the darker divine realms. The gods seldom found elsewhere are those of the shadow goblins and the bearfolk, whose worship is less common in the mortal world and whose mysteries are stronger and more useful in the twilight forests of shadow.

## Gods and Masks

As in Midgard, the gods of the Shadow Realm are not individuals so much as archetypes, incarnations of universal forces, and not people in the sense mortals are. The same god can look and act entirely differently from one region to the next, and the beliefs of the faithful vary from place to place even among worshipers of the same deity. Many shadow gods go by several names and change gender and appearance when it suits them, so the gods are unknowable and mysterious and their faiths are shifting channels of power.

Savants believe only five or six gods can truly thrive in a city, perhaps three in a town, and one in a village. Shrines to more than a few gods rarely prosper; the priests of these extra gods find their power to heal or intercede limited, their prayers unheard, and their sacrifices of no account. But the heart has room for many gods. Faith in the Shadow Realm is not a matter of choosing one god but of choosing the right god for the need or the occasion, so pantheist priests are found there now and then.

The gods seem determined to hide their true identities from their worshipers, and sometimes pretend to be other gods entirely: Ailuros might be [[Bastet]], and [[Bengta]] might be Isis. However changeable they are, each keeps a core identity the faithful recognize, even when the details shift. The true god, they say, casts different shadows for different worshipers.

Two shadow gods carry the title *santerr*, an Umbral word something like "saint," given to holy figures first tied to a particular place. It is always used with their names, since Marossa and Illosi are common names among the shadow goblins and the ghouls.

## The Great Gods

These five are the most widely worshiped and recognized gods of the realm. Even where they aren't worshiped, they are known by name, and their temples, groves, and worshipers outnumber those of both the lesser godlings and the dark gods.

- [[Bastet|Ailuros]], Goddess of Deer and Hunters, the moonlit huntress and patron of the alseid
- [[Anu-Akma]], God of the Underworld, Judge of the Dead, and patron of the ghouls
- [[Bengta]] the Bear Maiden, Goddess of Dawn and Honey and patron of the bearfolk
- [[Charun]], God of Death and Master of the [[Rivers Styx and Lethe]]
- [[Hecate]], Protector of the Shadow Realm and patron of the sable elves and shadow fey

## Godlings of the Shadow Realm

These seven demigods and saints of shadow are little known outside the realm, and their followers are few compared to the great gods.

- [[Baccholon]], the Laughing Prince, god of poetry and wine, his shadow shrines now faded to a few taverns
- [[Gytellisor]], God of Poetry and Wisdom, beloved of the sable elves and shadow fey
- [[Kupkoresh]], God of Copper and Lord of Light, said to have come from the bright lands when [[Corremel]] fell into shadow
- [[The Old Roads]], gods of the roads and bindings, guardians of every traveler
- [[Santerr Illosi]], Our Lady of Blood, the first necromancer and patron of the ghouls
- [[Santerr Marossa]], Our Lady of Roses, trickster mother goddess of the shadow goblins
- [[Umbeserno]], Lord of Sleep and Winter Dreams, the bearfolk's god of hibernation

## Dark Gods of the Shadow Realm

The Shadow Realm has more than its share of night terrors, demon lords, and dark gods, as well as godlings exiled or banished from brighter realms. A few are familiar from Midgard, and a few are similar but not quite the same when seen in shadow. These five are the most important.

- [[Alquam]], Demon Lord of Night and the Silent God
- [[Black Goat|The Black Goat of the Woods]], the Wicked One, patron of the goblins
- [[The Hunter]], Master of the Hunt and the Blood God
- [[Loki|Laughing Loki]], God of Cunning, Mischief, and Malice
- [[Vardesain]], the Hunger God and patron of the darakhul

## The True Dark Gods

Dangerous as the dark gods of the umbral peoples are, other forces swim in the shadow stuff and hide beneath the hills. The two best known are thought to be gods of the Ancients: Nammerkah the Devourer, a kind of tunneling horror thought to be a god of war or the patron of the vampires of [[Oshragora]], and Friselka, the Seal Maiden, whose cold touch is both the hand of death and the bringer of new life, sometimes linked with [[Charun]] or [[Lada]]. Their statues and images are common in the tombs of the Ancients. No one knows their prayers or rites, and time has softened even their shapes.

## Riddles of the Ancients

A few shrines and buildings of the Ancients survive on their driftwood islands and in their ruined haunts, many inscribed with prayers and poetry in an antique form of Umbral that sounds quaint and stilted to modern ears. The writing is usually fragmentary, though some figures appear again and again: the Moon Goddess, various Mage Princes, and the Keepers of the Tides, presumably mages with power over oak, road, and stone (see [[The Ebon Tides]]). Two fragments:

> And yea, the Moon bestrode the clouds and turned her face from us, wrapping us in mists, leaving us wandering. We called to the Mage Prince, but he was lost in mire and returned years . . .

> I pray to you, Keepers of the Tides, for protection against the Dark Hours, for shelter in the storms, for form and shape that do not . . .
