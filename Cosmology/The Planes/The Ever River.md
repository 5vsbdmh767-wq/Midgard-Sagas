The River Styx, also called the Black River or the Ever River, winds between the worlds, linking the [[Old Ring]], the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]], [[The Eleven Hells|the Hells]], and the elemental planes for anyone willing to pay the ferryman. It loops and meanders in ways that make no sense. Sometimes you have to row upriver to go down, or take a tiny side stream to reach somewhere far away, and a trip can take hours or weeks with no relation to the distance. It flows only where it flows, and most worlds don't touch it at all.

[[Charun]] the Ferryman, Guardian of Souls, is Lord of the Rivers, and his black-robed followers pole flat-bottomed skiffs up and down the Styx for gold. The Boatwrights, a folk of the river changed by its waters, ferry travelers too and know its currents better than anyone (see [[Minor Wayfaring Groups]]). In the Shadow Realm you can hire a shadow goblin barge, and near the Hells there are devils with boats, if you like that sort of deal.

From [[The Smithy]], boats reach the Styx through the central arch of the Five Ways Bridge. Elsewhere, people get there by dyeing themselves blue and dancing at a lakeside henge in the Old Ring, by drinking one of the [[Servants of the Dreaming God|Dreamers]]' stranger brews, or by bargaining with the shadow fey. River giants, shadow goblins, and demons are among the things met along the way. Its sister river, the Lethe, runs through the Shadow Realm city of [[Corremel]].
