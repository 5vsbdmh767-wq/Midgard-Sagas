Addakala is an Ishadian Knight and a son of the moon god [[Sabateus]]. This makes him a demigod.

He is the Bey (ruler, not too dissimilar to a sultan.) of Zinj Iram.

He works, with the counsel of his court wizard, [[Zuberi Mchawi]], to unite the [[Spice Coast]] city-states once again under the banner of [[Ishadia]] and return the glory days of the ancient kingdom. He does this with the hopes of pushing back [[The Mharoti Empire]], who are currently devastating [[Ishadia]] in a drawn out war. 

He believes [[The Esoteric Order of Dagon]] to be a much larger threat to The [[Spice Coast]] and [[Ishadia]] than most others who are up-to-date on the matter.
