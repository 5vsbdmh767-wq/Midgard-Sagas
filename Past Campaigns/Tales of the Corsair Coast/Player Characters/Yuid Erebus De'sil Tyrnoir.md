### Past, Present & Future

How and by whom was your character raised? 

- Yuid was raised by his mother, Lady Charlotte Tyrnoir, and his older brothers, Ayelnod, Godryk, Sayewulf, and Ulryc Tyrnoir. His father, Saein Tyrnoir, rarely spent time with Yuid as he was busy building an empire. If ever, Lord Saein spoke to Yuid, it was usually in some sort of scolding or disciplinary manner. 

- Yuid enjoyed his time with his mother. He loved reading poetry with her and learning to read and write from her. Lady Charlotte also taught Yuid about etiquette and being courtly. 

- Yuid’s older brothers, especially Godryk, taught him how to hunt and how to use a bow. Ayelnod was always away with Saein. This caused the siblings to fear or resent Ayelnod—they did not want him to take over Saein’s empire. Yuid, however, still thought highly of Ayelnod and respected him.

  

Family history: 

- Born in Sar Shaba, Yuid Erebus Tyrnoir was destined for a life of pride and struggle. His familial legacy bore an even darker undercurrent. His father, Lord Saein, once a prosperous merchant, fell prey to the allure of vampirism, using his newfound abilities to ascend even higher within the echelons of society. With ruthless ambition and cunning, Saein orchestrated the demise of Lord Gallius Leoufward, seizing control of his house and marrying into its remnants with Lady Charlotte Leoufward (a woman he had met back when she was a teenager with whom he had fallen in love with), a union that bore six children, Yuid being the youngest son among five brothers, all dhampirs.

- The Tyrnoir household harbored a venomous rift, fueled by Saein's machinations. Each brother, groomed for dominance, found himself pitted against his kin in a deadly game for supremacy over the house. For more than half a century, the siblings danced upon the edge of treachery, their familial bonds eroded by the insidious influence of their father's legacy. Amidst the turmoil, Silyette, the youngest and only daughter, yearned for an end to the bloodshed and violence, clinging to the memory of her mother, who embodied compassion amidst the darkness that enveloped their lineage. Yuid's journey took a harrowing turn when he was ambushed by one of his brothers, left to the mercy of the sea with chains binding him. He was poisoned with a rare and potent holy water and the pain and trauma caused him to lose his memories, drifting towards an uncertain fate on the unforgiving tides off the coast of [[Aksaba]].
  
Were there any major events that shaped who they are now?

- Yuid was a typical middle child. He was picked on and bullied by a lot of his brothers. Being the youngest, Ayelnod would often look out for Yuid—despite the heavy pressure from their father to show no mercy.

List at least two important figures or characters in your character’s life:

- Yuid loved and cherished his mother, Charlotte. He had a close relationship with his younger sister Silyette. Other than that, Yuid had books. He was a pretty lonely child. After his mother passed, he grew even more detached and turned inward.

Why did your character become an adventurer? 

- Yuid woke with amnesia floating on a clump of driftwood and was pulled aboard the Black Bird by the party. He only has a signet ring with his family’s crest from his past life. He’d like to find out where he comes from and why he lost his memories. 

What is/are the main goal(s) your character wishes to accomplish?

- Once Yuid regains his memories, he starts to realize that he was a very skilled and deadly assassin. Growing up as the youngest brother he learned to be sneaky and quiet. He also learned to far exceed his brothers’ skills with a bow. Now he’d like to find which brother ambushed him and tried to kill him. He wants revenge.
