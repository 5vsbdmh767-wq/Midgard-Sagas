The list below provides clockwork extensions for gearforged (see *Labyrinth Worldbook*) or other contruct‑based characters that can replace limbs with weapons or utility gadgets. For certain campaigns, a GM might also allow flesh-and-blood PCs to take these in place of lost body parts.

A clockwork extension replaces the existing part of the gearforged equipping it. To replace the current part, the gearforged character must spend at least 1 hour (which can include a short rest) making the alterations with tinker tools. All gearforged have five available body slots (head, left arm, right arm, torso, and legs) for clockwork extensions. As with regular armor and weapons, clockwork extensions can be enchanted as magic items. See the **Clockwork Extensions** table below.

**Clockwork Extensions**

***Arachnid Legs.*** These six or eight radially spaced legs give you a walking speed of 40 feet and a climbing speed of 20 feet. You must stay in motion while climbing. If you stop moving, you lose your grip and fall. You can’t climb upsidedown with arachnid legs.

***Fall Bracer Legs.*** With this extension, you can fall up to 60 feet and land on your feet without injury. You have advantage on DEX (Acrobatics) ability checks made to land on your feet. If you fall farther than 60 feet, ignore the first 60 feet of the fall for purposes of calculating damage.

***Fist of Wrath.*** This extension is an oversized, threeclawed arm. Unarmed strikes with this extension deal 1d4 + your STR modifier bludgeoning damage. If you already have improved unarmed damage, your damage goes up one die step (for example, your fist of wrath deals 1d6 bludgeoning damage if you already use a d4 for unarmed strikes). When you are hit by a melee attack but before damage is rolled, you can use your reaction to make a contested melee attack roll. If you win the contested roll, you take no damage from the attack and you trap your opponent’s weapon until the end of your next turn or until you release it, whichever comes first. When a creature’s weapon is trapped in this way, it has disadvantage on attack rolls with that weapon, and you have disadvantage on attacks using your fist of wrath.

***Hidden Compartment.*** The storage compartment concealed in this extension can hide two objects of Tiny size or smaller. You can access the compartment with a Use an Object action. Anyone searching for a hidden compartment can find it with a successful DC 15 WIS (Perception) check.

***Horned Helm.*** Modeled after the horns of a minotaur, this extension allow you to perform a gore action. After moving at least half your speed in a straight line toward a target, you can make a melee weapon attack using the horned helm as a natural weapon. On a hit, the target takes 2d6 piercing damage and must succeed on a STR save with a DC equal to 8 + your PB + your STR modifier or be knocked prone.

***Leaper Legs.*** You have advantage on all STR (Athletics) checks made for running or jumping. While adjacent to a wall, you can take a bonus action to jump up to 15 feet from the wall and sail over enemies that are up to one size larger than you without provoking opportunity attacks.

***Propeller Jet Diver.*** You gain a swimming speed of 40 feet. You can take the Dash action as a bonus action while underwater. While using the Dash action underwater, if you break the surface of the water, you can use any remaining movement to propel yourself up and out of the water.

***Sage Sight.*** These replacement eyes allow you to read three languages of your choice. Once implanted, you can read the languages, but you can’t speak them and you can’t understand it when spoken. The languages can be altered with one week of adjustment using tinker tools.

***Utility Hands.*** You can activate one of the following utilities as a bonus action. Except for Snatch and Grab, while one of the utilities is activated, the hand on the arm containing this extension can’t be used to hold an item or wield a weapon or shield.

- **Thieves’ Tools.** The fingers of this extension unfurl into a variety of delicate instruments. The hand on this arm now functions as thieves’ tools.

- **Assassin Dagger.** A poisoned dagger appears from the palm of this hand. You can use it as a dagger that deals an additional 1d4 poison damage on a successful attack.

- **Snatch and Grab.** The hand of this extension is connected to a 60-foot retractable cable in the arm.

While the hand is extended, you can still manipulate the hand’s digits. As a bonus action, you can shoot your hand up to 60 feet away and recoil it. If you can interact with an object this round, you can grab an item of up to 50 pounds and bring it to you.

***Vampiric Spikes.*** As a bonus action, small, sharpened metal spikes burst out of small holes that cover the front and back of the torso of this extension. While the spikes are active, creatures you grapple take 1d4 piercing damage and you gain temporary hit points equal to the amount of damage dealt by the spikes.

***Vision Lock.*** You can lock on with your vision for short periods, allowing for greater precision. This extension has 2 charges. As part of an Attack action, you can expend 1 charge to have advantage on the attack. In addition, you can expend 1 charge to have advantage on a single WIS (Perception) check. You regain all used charges after finishing a long rest.

***Wings of Flight.*** As an action, you can spread clockwork wings (which appear either angelic or batlike), gaining a flying speed equal to your walking speed until the end of your next turn. In addition, while flying, you can use the wings to make a melee weapon attack. On a hit, they deal 1d6 + DEX modifier slashing damage. These wings are considered monk weapons.

## Clockwork Arcanepunk Devices

Clockwork items can fail spectacularly. The combination of unstable energies and experimental technology (often hastily repaired) creates the perfect formula for explosive results. When such devices fail, consider rolling on the **Clockwork Malfunctions** table for a consequence.
