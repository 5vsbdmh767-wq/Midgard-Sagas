## Berserker’s Mead

%% source: Northlands Worldbook %%

*Potion, Rare 650 gp*

For 1 minute after you consume this potion, you are immune to the first 40 points of damage you take.

## Defender Honey

%% source: Northlands Worldbook %%

*Potion, Uncommon 800 gp*

The honey is light brown and has a smooth sweetness. For 10 minutes after you consume this honey, you can use your reaction to put yourself between an attack that would hit an ally within 5 feet of you. The attack targets you instead with the same attack roll.

## Draught of Clear Thought

%% source: Night Hunters %%

*Potion, Rare 50 gp*

When you drink this potion, you have advantage on saves against being charmed for 1 hour.

The liquid is cloudy, smelling strongly of garlic, rose, and hawthorn.

## Draught of False Death

%% source: Night Hunters %%

*Potion, Rare 50 gp*

When you drink this potion, you fall into a deathlike, catatonic state for 1 hour. For the duration, you are blinded, deafened, and incapacitated, and your speed becomes 0. You appear dead to anyone inspecting you or using magic to determine your current state. In addition, you are resistant to all damage. The effect ends early if you are subject to a *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Restoration|restoration]]* spell or similar magic.

The liquid smells strongly of mud and earth.

## Draught of Imperception

%% source: Night Hunters %%

*Potion, Very Rare 50 gp*

When you drink this potion, for 1 hour you cannot be detected by creatures using keensense. This effect ends if you attack or cast a spell.

The liquid is viscous and contains granules of grave dirt.

## Draught of Mind Shielding

%% source: Night Hunters %%

*Potion, Very Rare 150 gp*

When you drink this potion, for 1 hour you are immune to magic that allows other creatures to read your thoughts. In addition, for the duration, creatures can communicate telepathically with you only if you choose to allow it.

The liquid is a deep purple and contains flecks of ash.

## Elixir of Fury

%% source: Trade Secrets %%

*Potion, Uncommon 500 gp*

This potion’s dark red liquid boils and bubbles while held. When you drink the potion while not wearing heavy armor, you gain the following benefits for 1 minute:

- *You have advantage on STR* checks and saves. When you make a melee weapon attack using STR, you gain a +2 bonus to the damage roll. The damage type matches that of the weapon used to deal it.

- You have resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage. If you can cast spells, you can’t cast them or concentrate on them while raging.

- If you have the Rage class feature and drink this potion while raging, the potion duration adds to your Rage duration and prevents your rage from ending early unless you choose to end it.

When the potion effect ends, you suffer one level of exhaustion. If you use your Rage feature and choose to end it early, you suffer no exhaustion from this potion.

## Far Traveler’s Potion

%% source: Labyrinth Worldbook %%

*Potion, Rare 350 gp*

For 1 hour after you drink this potion, you are resistant to cold damage and necrotic damage, and you don’t require air if you are a breathing creature (but you are still affected by gases or other air-based effects when in an area that has an atmosphere).

## Honey of Robust Health

%% source: Northlands Worldbook %%

*Potion [Honey], Common 50 gp*

When you consume this honey as part of a short rest, you can reroll any result of 1 or 2 on hit dice spent to regain hit points during that rest. You must use the new roll. The honey has a deep red tinge and a rich flavor, and it warms the stomach.

## Honey of the Hearth Fire

%% source: Northlands Worldbook %%

*Potion [Honey], Uncommon 200 gp*

When you consume this honey, you end the blinded, deafened, frightened, and poisoned conditions on yourself.

## Honey of the Hive Warden

%% source: Northlands Worldbook %%

*Potion [Honey], Very Rare 1,500 gp*

When you consume this honey, you have resistance to poison for 1 hour. Once during the duration, you may cast *[[5th-Circle Spells#Insect Plague|insect plague]]* (spell save DC 15). Instead of locusts dealing piercing damage, the spell creates bees that deal poison damage.

## Honey of the Norn-Blessed

%% source: Northlands Worldbook %%

*Potion [Honey], Rare 650 gp*

When you consume this honey, you have advantage on DEX saves for 1 hour. Once during the duration, you may cast *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Augury|augury]]*.

## Honey of the Sacred Bear

%% source: Northlands Worldbook %%

*Potion [Honey], Rare 650 gp*

When you consume this honey, you have advantage on STR checks for 1 hour. Once during the duration, you may cast *[[1st-Circle Spells#Heroism|heroism]]* on yourself, requiring no concentration.

## Honey of the Skald’s Tongue

%% source: Northlands Worldbook %%

*Potion [Honey], Rare 350 gp*

When you consume this honey, you have advantage on CHA (Performance) and CHA (Persuasion) checks for 1 hour. Once during the duration, you may cast *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Suggestion|suggestion]]* (spell save DC 15).

## Oil of Etherealness

*Potion, Rare 2,500 gp* Beads of this cloudy gray oil form on the outside of its container and quickly evaporate. The oil can cover a Medium or smaller creature, along with the equipment it’s wearing and carrying (one additional vial is required for each size category above Medium). Applying the oil takes 10 minutes. The affected creature then gains the effect of the *[[7th-Circle Spells#Etherealness|etherealness]]* spell for 1 hour.

## Oil of Sharpness

*Potion, Very Rare 1,500 gp* This clear, gelatinous oil sparkles with tiny, ultrathin silver shards. The oil can coat one slashing or piercing weapon or up to 5 pieces of slashing or piercing ammunition. Applying the oil takes 1 minute. For 1 hour, the coated item is magical and has a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls.

## Oil of Slipperiness

*Potion, Uncommon 350 gp* This sticky black unguent is thick and heavy in the container, but it flows quickly when poured. The oil can cover a Medium or smaller creature, along with the equipment it wears and carries (one additional vial is required for each size category above Medium). Applying the oil takes 10 minutes. The affected creature then gains the effect of a *[[4th-Circle Spells#Freedom of Movement|freedom of movement]]* spell for 8 hours.

Alternatively, the oil can be poured on the ground as an action, where it covers a 10-foot square, duplicating the effect of the *[[1st-Circle Spells#Grease|grease]]* spell in that area for 8 hours.

## Potion of Animal Friendship

*Potion, Uncommon 50 gp* When you drink this potion, animals tend to be friendly to you for 1 hour. For the duration, you can use an action to cast the *[[1st-Circle Spells#Animal Friendship|animal friendship]]* spell (save DC 13) at any time. Agitating this muddy liquid brings little bits into view: a fish scale, a hummingbird tongue, a cat claw, or a squirrel hair.

## Potion of Clairvoyance

*Potion, Rare 200 gp* When you drink this potion, you gain the effect of the *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Clairvoyance|clairvoyance]]* spell. An eyeball bobs in this yellowish liquid but vanishes when the potion is opened.

## Potion of Climbing

*Potion, Uncommon 100 gp* When you drink this potion, you gain a climbing speed equal to your walking speed for 1 hour. During this time, you have advantage on STR (Athletics) checks you make to climb. The potion is separated into brown, silver, and gray layers resembling bands of stone.

## Potion of Courage

%% source: Player's Guide 2 %%

*Potion, Rare 100 gp*

When you drink this potion, you cannot be frightened for 1 hour. The liquid initially appears copper in color. Periodically, dark whorls appear, but they eventually recombine back into the coppery liquid.

## Potion of Diminution

*Potion, Rare 200 gp* When you drink this potion, you gain the “reduce” effect of the *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Enlarge/Reduce|enlarge/reduce]]* spell for 1d4 hours (no concentration required). The red in the potion’s liquid continuously contracts to a tiny bead and then expands to color the clear liquid around it.

## Potion of Due Notice

*Potion, Common 50 gp* When you drink this potion, double your PB for the purpose of calculating your passive Insight, Investigation, and Perception scores for 1 hour. If you don't have proficiency in any of these skills, you gain proficiency in them for the duration of the potion. This potion’s liquid has a bright green effervescence.

## Potion of Flying

*Potion, Very Rare 200 gp* When you drink this potion, you gain a flying speed equal to your walking speed for 1 hour and can hover. If you’re in the air when the potion wears off, you fall unless you have some other means of staying aloft. This potion’s clear liquid floats at the top of its container and cloudy white impurities drift in it.

## Potion of Gaseous Form

*Potion, Rare 200 gp* When you drink this potion, you gain the effect of the *gaseous* *form* spell for 1 hour (no concentration required) or until you end the effect as a bonus action. This potion’s container seems to hold fog that moves and pours like water.

## Potion of Giant Strength

*Potion, Rarity Varies Price Varies*

When you drink this potion, your STR score changes for 1 hour. The type of Giant determines the score, as shown on the **Potion of Giant Strength Type** table. The potion has no effect if your STR score equals or exceeds that score.

This potion’s transparent liquid has floating in it a sliver of fingernail from a giant of the appropriate type. The *potion* *of frost giant strength* and the *potion of stone giant strength* have the same effect.

## Potion of Growth

*Potion, Uncommon 200 gp* When you drink this potion, you gain the “enlarge” effect of the *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Enlarge/Reduce|enlarge/reduce]]* spell for 1d4 hours (no concentration required). The red in the potion’s liquid continuously expands from a tiny bead to color the clear liquid around it and then contracts. Shaking the bottle fails to interrupt this process.

%% source: Player's Guide 2 %%

*Potion, Uncommon 650 gp*

When you drink this potion, you remove one level of exhaustion. The liquid is thick and viscous with unmixing swirls of rich brown and dark green, plus occasional flecks of gold.

## Potion of Heroism

*Potion, Rare 350 gp* For 1 hour after drinking it, you gain 10 temporary HP that last for 1 hour. For the same duration, you are under the effect of the *[[1st-Circle Spells#Bless|bless]]* spell (no concentration required). This blue potion bubbles and steams as if boiling.

## Potion of Invisibility

*Potion, Very Rare 100 gp* When you drink this potion, you become invisible for 1 hour. Anything you wear or carry is invisible with you. The effect ends early if you attack or cast a spell. This potion’s container looks empty but feels as though it holds liquid.

## Potion of Knowledge

%% source: Player's Guide 2 %%

*Potion, Uncommon 100 gp*

When you drink this potion, you have advantage on all INT checks for 1 hour. This liquid is translucent with an iridescent shimmer at its surface. Motes of soft light seem to dance within it.

## Potion of Locomotion

%% source: Player's Guide 2 %%

*Potion, Rare 650 gp*

When you drink this potion, you gain a climbing, flying, and swimming speed equal to your walking speed and a burrowing speed equal to half your walking speed for 1 hour. The liquid doesn’t coat the inside of the vial and continues swirling with only minor agitation. Its high volatility is apparent from the pop of escaping vapor and sharp aroma at uncorking.

## Potion of Mind Reading

*Potion, Rare 100 gp* When you drink this potion, you gain the effect of the *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Detect Thoughts|detect thoughts]]* spell (save DC 13). The potion’s dense, purple liquid has an ovoid cloud of pink floating in it.

## Potion of Near-Death Portent

%% source: Northlands Worldbook %%

*Potion, Very Rare 650 gp*

This potion’s cloudy grey fluid has a golden thread suspended in it. Drinking this potion gives you a vague vision of your potential death. The next time within 1 hour that you would be reduced to 0 hit points, you can use a reaction and roll 1d20. If the roll is 10 or higher, you take no damage. If the roll is less than 10, you are reduced to 1 Hit Point instead.

## Potion of Puissance

%% source: Player's Guide 2 %%

*Potion, Rare 200 gp*

When you drink this potion, if you are a spellcaster, you can add your spellcasting ability modifier to your spell attack damage for 1 minute. The liquid is a vibrant pink that leaves shimmery, pearlescent trails on swirling.

## Potion of Readiness

%% source: Player's Guide 2 %%

*Potion, Rare 100 gp*

When you drink this potion, you have advantage on initiative rolls and cannot be surprised for 1 hour. The liquid is thick yet also seemingly ethereal, and it beads readily, like quicksilver.

## Potion of Replication

%% source: Player's Guide 2 %%

*Potion, Rare 200 gp*

A minute after you drink this potion, you violently vomit its contents into the closest unoccupied space. The regurgitated liquid then proceeds to grow, requiring 1 additional minute, into an exact copy of you. The copy is unanimated but otherwise physically appears like you in every way. It can be manipulated easily and retains whatever pose you give it. If a creature uses an action to examine it and succeeds on a DC 13 WIS (Perception) check, it is clear that the copy is neither breathing nor responding to stimulus. The check has advantage if a minute or more is spent examining the copy. The copy remains for 1 hour and then dissolves.

## Quicksilver Draught

%% source: Night Hunters %%

*Potion, Rare 150 gp*

When you drink this potion, for 1 hour you are immune to magic that would alter your form. If you have an effect that would allow you to change your shape, you’re unable to use it for the duration. If you are not in your true form when you drink this potion, you must succeed on a DC 15 CON save or immediately revert to your true form (though you can choose to fail this save).

The liquid is silver and shimmers eerily in the moonlight.

## Sunfire Pitch

%% source: Labyrinth Worldbook %%

*Potion, Very Rare 1,500 gp*

Made from leftover slag and ash from the forging of *sunfire* armaments, these thick glass flasks hold a dark, viscous fluid with threads of fiery-golden sheen.

Each of the following applications of *sunfire pitch* consumes one flask; the effects end after 8 hours:

***Light.*** *Sunfire pitch* is considered a magical light source, able to penetrate even Void-tainted darkness. ***Enchant Weapons***. It takes 1 minute to apply *sunfire pitch* as an oil to any one weapon or 20 pieces of ammunition. This grants the weapon the *sunfire* weapon trait.

***Hallow.*** If burned in a brazier, firepit, or other large receptacle, *sunfire pitch* creates a *hallowed* area for 60 feet around it. The area has the following effects:

- Fiends or Undead entering the area become vulnerable to radiant damage.

- Creatures allied with either the [[Concord of Stars]] or the [[Keepers of the Keys]] gain resistance to necrotic damage.

- It also sheds bright light for 60 feet and dim light for an additional 60 feet. This illumination remains unaffected by magical darkness.

## Trollkin Unguent

%% source: Northlands Worldbook %%

*Potion, Rare 50 gp*

One vial of this oil can cover one medium or smaller creature, along with the equipment it’s wearing and carrying (one additional vial is required for each size category above medium). Applying the oil takes 10 minutes. The affected creature is resistant to fire damage for 1 hour.

If the creature takes fire damage, it becomes wreathed in ghostly green flames that shed dim light in a 15-foot radius. While ablaze, the creature’s melee weapon attacks deal an extra 1d6 necrotic damage. After 10 minutes, the flames die out and the oil’s effects expire.

This oil is transparent with a rainbow sheen.

## Warrior Honey

%% source: Northlands Worldbook %%

*Potion [Honey], Common 50 gp*

When you consume this honey, you have advantage on the first initiative check you make during the next hour. You also have advantage on the first melee attack roll you make. The honey has small bubbles throughout and tingles in your stomach.
