Fabled items grow in power along with their owner. This section lists some fabled magic items. Each entry provides key information about the item, including its type, any attunement factors, and a description of its properties.

## Blood Spike Armor

*Armor (Plate), Fabled Priceless (5th-Level and Higher Properties Require Attunement)*

This ancient plate armor is crafted from a strange, black metal. Vicious spikes protrude from the shoulders and breastplate. Hideous, demonic faces are etched into the metal. While wearing this armor, you can use a bonus action to make one melee weapon attack with the armor’s spikes against a target within 5 feet of you. If the attack hits, the spikes deal 1d4 piercing damage. You are proficient with this attack and use your STR modifier for the attack and damage rolls.

***Frightening Armor (Requires Attunement).*** As your level increases, you gain the following benefits while wearing this armor.

**5th Level.** Once per turn, when you hit a creature with your armor spikes, you cause unnatural bleeding from the resulting wounds. After you hit a creature with the armor’s spikes, it takes 1d4 necrotic damage at the start of each of its turns from a bleeding wound. Each time you hit that creature with the armor’s spikes, the damage dealt by the wound increases by 1d4. At the end of the creature’s turn, it can make a DC 15 CON save, ending the effect on itself on a success. Alternatively, any creature can take an action to stanch the wound with a successful DC 15 WIS (Medicine) check. The wound also closes if the target receives magical healing.

**9th Level.** You gain a +1 bonus to AC and to attack and damage rolls with the armor’s spikes.

**13th Level.** You now gain a +2 bonus to AC and to attack and damage rolls with the armor’s spikes, instead of +1. In addition, you can use an action to frighten a creature you can see within 30 feet of you. If the creature can see you, it must succeed on a WIS save (DC = 8 + your PB + your CHA modifier) or be frightened until the end of your next turn. On the following turn, you can use an action to extend the duration of this effect until the end of your next turn. You can continue extending the duration in this way until the creature is no longer frightened, it ends its turn out of line of sight, or it is more than 60 feet away from you. If a creature succeeds on its save, you can’t use this property on that creature again for 1 day.

**17th Level.** You now gain a +3 bonus to AC and to attack and damage rolls with the armor’s spikes, instead of +2.

## Book of Names

*Wondrous Item, Fabled Priceless*

*(5th-Level and Higher Properties Require Attunement)*

This leather-bound tome smells faintly of blood and brimstone and is filled with names from throughout the ages, past and present, both great and small. When you hold this book, you have advantage on INT (History) checks involving specific creatures.

***Intimate Details (Requires Attunement).*** As your level increases, you gain the following benefits while holding this book.

**5th Level.** If a specific creature’s name is recorded in the book, you are considered familiar with the creature for purposes of *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Sending|sending]]* and similar magic. There is a 30 percent chance a specific creature’s name is already recorded in the book when you obtain it. You can spend 10 minutes and 25 gp worth of magical ink to scribe a specific creature’s name into the book. In addition, you can spend 10 minutes meditating with the book, focusing on a specific creature whose name is in the book. The creature must make a DC 18 CHA save. On a failure, you gain insight into the creature’s emotional state and know the topic that is at the forefront of their mind. If the creature is on a different plane of existence than you, there is a 10 percent chance this property fails. You can't use the book in this way again until the next dawn.

**9th Level.** The book has 10 charges. You can use an action to expend 1 or more of its charges to cast one of the following spells from it, using CHA as the spellcasting ability and only targeting a creature whose name is in the book: *[[Rituals 4th–6th Circle#Dream|dream]]* (5 charges), *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Locate|locate]]* (4 charges), *[[Rituals 4th–6th Circle#Scrying|scrying]]* (5 charges), and *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Sending|sending]]* (3 charges).

**13th Level.** You can spend 10 minutes meditating with the book, focusing on a specific creature whose name is in the book. The creature must make a DC 18 CHA save. On a failure, for the next 24 hours, if the creature is on the same plane of existence as you, you know the distance and direction to the creature. If the creature is moving, you know the speed and direction of their movement. If the creature is on a different plane of existence, you learn the plane but not their specific location. You can't use the book in this way again until the next dawn.

**17th Level.** You can spend 10 minutes meditating with the book, focusing on a specific creature whose name is in the book. The creature must succeed on a DC 20 CHA save. A creature can choose to fail this save. On a failure, you can apply a Boon or a Bane to the creature, as described below. Once you successfully use this property, it can't be used again for 7 days.

- **Boon.** The creature is under magical protection for the next 7 days. For the duration, it can't be targeted by any divination magic or perceived through magical scrying sensors except by you, it gains a +2 bonus to AC, and it has advantage on all saves.

- **Bane.** The creature is cursed for the next 7 days. For the duration, the target is vulnerable to all damage types. If it has any damage resistances, this negates those resistances. If it has any damage immunities, it instead has resistance to those damage types. In addition, each dawn for the duration, the target gains one level of exhaustion.

## Ring of the Flamekeeper

*Wondrous Item, Fabled Priceless*

*(5th-Level and Higher Properties Require Attunement)*

This ring is made of a reddish metal and set with a brilliant star ruby. While wearing this ring, you can cast the *produce* *flame* cantrip.

***One with the Fire (Requires Attunement).*** As your level increases, you gain the following benefits while wearing this ring. For any spells that allow a choice of element type, you must choose fire.

**5th Level.** You have resistance to fire damage. In addition, the ring has 5 charges. You can cast the following spells from the ring, expending the necessary number of charges: *[[1st-Circle Spells#Burning Hands|burning hands]]* (1 charge), *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Elemental Blade|elemental blade]]* (2 charges), or *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Fireball|fireball]]* (3 charges). The ring regains all expended charges at the next dawn.

**9th Level.** In addition to the previously listed spells, you can cast the following spells from the ring, expending the necessary number of charges: *[[4th-Circle Spells#Elemental Shield|elemental shield]]* (4 charges); *[[5th-Circle Spells#Flame Strike|flame strike]]* (5 charges).

**13th Level.** You can use an action to speak the ring’s command word and summon a **fire elemental**. The creature appears in an unoccupied space that you can see within 30 feet of you. For the next 24 hours, the elemental is friendly to you and your companions. It obeys any commands you give it, no matter what language you use. If you fail to command it, the elemental defends itself against attackers but takes no other actions. The elemental disappears after 24 hours, if it is reduced to 0 HP, or if you dismiss it as a bonus action. The ring can’t be used again in this way until 5 days have passed.

**17th Level.** You have immunity to fire damage. In addition, once per day, you can use a bonus action to speak the ring’s command word and polymorph into a fire elemental. While in this form, your statistics are replaced by the statistics of the fire elemental, but you retain your personality, and INT, WIS, and CHA scores. This change lasts for 4 hours, until you use a bonus action to revert to your normal form, or until you drop to 0 HP or die.

## Quickfinger Gloves

*Wondrous Item, Fabled Priceless*

*(5th-Level and Higher Properties Require Attunement)*

These gloves are made of a soft, supple leather. While wearing these gloves, you have proficiency in the Sleight of Hand skill. In addition, you know the *[[Cantrips#Guidance|guidance]]* cantrip.

***Legerdemain (Requires Attunement).*** As your level increases, you gain the following benefits while wearing these gloves.

**5th Level.** Your DEX score increases by 1, to a maximum of 20. When making a DEX check that involves the use of fine motor skills, such as using thieves’ tools or the Sleight of Hand skill, you have advantage on the check.

**9th Level.** When you take the Attack action with a Light or ranged weapon on your turn, you gain an extra attack with that weapon. In addition, you can use a bonus action to take the Use an Object action during combat.

**13th Level.** Your DEX score increases by 2, to a maximum of 20. In addition, the gloves have 3 charges. As an action, you can expend 1 charge to cast *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Haste|haste]]* on yourself.

**17th Level.** If you fail a DEX (Sleight of Hand) or DEX check using tools, you can choose to succeed instead. Once used, the gloves can’t be used in this way again until the next dawn.

## More Fabled Items (Player's Guide 2)

*Optional, from Player's Guide 2.*

A fabled magic item grows in power alongside the PC who wields it. Rules for fabled items are summarized here and described in further detail in the *Player’s Guide*.

## Properties and Attunement

A fabled magic item begins as a common magic item and does not require attunement to use, granting a useful minor trait to its owner. To access its higher-level traits, a PC must attune to it, using standard attunement rules (see *Player’s Guide*). Once attuned, that PC can use the item’s increasingly powerful properties as they themselves gain experience. If a PC higher than 1st level attunes to the item, its level-dependent properties, up to the PC’s current level, are immediately available. Unless otherwise noted, the item’s properties remain in effect as long as the PC is wearing, carrying, or touching it. If a PC chooses not to attune to it, the item remains a common magic item, granting only its minor property, regardless of the PC’s level.

At the GM’s discretion, a fabled magic item’s more powerful properties might become available only after the PC completes a particular quest or event related to the item rather than simply reaching each required level.

You can only attempt to reattune to it once per dawn.

*Optional, from Player's Guide 2.*

*Wondrous Item, Fabled Priceless* *(5th-Level and Higher Properties Require Attunement* *by a [[Cleric]] or [[Paladin]] of a Relevant God)* This magical iron medallion features a bas-relief engraving of the likeness of a god whose domains include brewery, carousing, mirth, and singing. When you wear it, you have advantage on any ability check or save associated with the creation, identification, or consumption of alcohol.

In addition, if you reach out your empty hand, you can conjure a chilled, empty tankard in it, which fills with beer as if from a divine tap (no action required). The tankard always fills with what *looks* like beer, but the gods of mirth want everyone to have a good time, so it actually tastes and behaves like whatever beverage you prefer (alcoholic or not). The tankard is a physical object only for the purpose of holding your beverage, and it vanishes into the ether after you dismiss it (no action required) or finish drinking.

Or perhaps these two specific items can change their forms upon attunement to something better, embodying their wielders’ needs.

***Chalice Divinity (Requires Attunement).*** As your level increases, you gain the following benefits while wearing this medallion.

**5th Level.** You can use the medallion as your holy symbol. You gain a +1 bonus to your spell save DC and to spell attack rolls. Once per dawn, you can also use your Channel Divinity class feature (see the cleric or paladin in *Player’s* *Guide*), without expending one of the feature’s uses.

**9th Level.** As an action, you can summon tankards for you and your companions, up to a number of creatures equal to your WIS modifier. A creature can use its action to imbibe this “battlebrew” and gain temporary hit points equal to your total number of cleric and paladin levels + either your WIS or CHA modifier (your choice). Once used, this property can’t be used again until the next dawn.

**13th Level.** You gain a new effect for your Channel Divinity class feature: Intoxicate. At the end of each of the creature’s turns, it can repeat the save, ending the effect on a success. This effect lasts for 1 minute or until you are incapacitated.

**17th Level.** You can use the medallion to cast the *heroes’* *feast* spell (see *Player’s Guide*). The feast you conjure can be made entirely of drink instead of food if you wish, and creatures consuming it will get a pleasant buzz if desired but suffer no ill effects. Once used, this property can’t be used again until 7 days have passed.

## Bracers of Holy Fury

*Optional, from Player's Guide 2.*

*Wondrous Item, Fabled Priceless*

*(5th-Level and Higher Properties Require Attunement)*

These anointed wrappings are inked with delicate holy scripts. While wearing these magic bracers, when you score a critical hit with a melee weapon attack, including unarmed strikes, add 1d4 necrotic or radiant damage (your choice) to your extra damage. In addition, any melee attacks you make while wearing the bracers count as magical for the purposes of overcoming resistance to nonmagical damage.

***Divine Wrath (Requires Attunement).*** As your level increases, you gain the following benefits while wearing these bracers.

**5th Level.** Any melee weapon you wield, or your unarmed strikes, functions as a magic weapon with a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls, unless the weapon already has a higher bonus. The extra necrotic or radiant damage you deal on a critical hit increases to 1d6.

**9th Level.** The extra necrotic or radiant damage you deal is now applied every time you hit a creature with a melee weapon or unarmed strike (not just critical hits). In addition, when fighting Celestials, Fey, Fiends, or Undead, you score a critical hit on a d20 roll of 19 or 20.

**13th Level.** Any melee weapon you wield, or your unarmed strikes, functions as a *+2 weapon*, unless the weapon already has a higher bonus to its attack and damage rolls. In addition, when fighting Celestials, Fey, Fiends, or Undead, add 1d6 to the amount of extra necrotic or radiant damage you deal on a hit.

**17th Level.** When you hit a Celestial, Fey, Fiend, or Undead with a melee weapon attack, including unarmed strikes, the target must succeed on a DC 17 WIS save or be turned for 1 minute or until it takes any damage. A creature turned by this feature must try to move as far away from you as it can, though it can’t first get closer to you to do it. On its turn, it can use only the Dash action or otherwise try to escape from an effect that prevents it from moving. If there’s nowhere to move, it can use the Dodge action. It can’t take any reactions. Once per turn, if you hit a creature turned by this feature with a melee weapon attack, you can damage it without ending the turned effect.

## Dragonblood Vial

*Optional, from Player's Guide 2.*

*Wondrous Item, Fabled Priceless*

*(5th-Level and Higher Properties Require Attunement)*

This incredibly resilient crystal vial absorbs dragon blood, and holding it is enough to channel some of the power of the dragon whose blood it absorbed. Roll on the **Dragon Type** table to determine the type of dragon the blood came from (or GM’s discretion).

If touched to a sufficient amount (GM’s discretion) of another dragon’s blood, the vial will absorb it, replacing any blood already within and altering the relevant effects with the new dragon type.

While wearing the vial, as indicated in the **Dragon Type** table, you gain a corresponding breath weapon of the same damage type listed in the Damage column with the area of effect listed in the Breath Weapon column. As an action, you can make a breath weapon attack, and targets in the area must make a DC 13 DEX save, taking 1d10 damage of the specified damage type on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. You can use this breath weapon a number of times equal to your PB, regaining all expended uses at the next dawn.

***Draconic Boon (Requires Attunement).*** As your level increases, you gain the following benefits while wearing this vial.

**5th Level.** You are resistant to the damage listed in the Damage column of the **Dragon Type** table.

**9th Level.** The damage dealt by your breath weapon attack increases to 3d10 on a failed save, and the save DC increases to 17. In addition, as a bonus action, you gain a flying speed of 60 feet for 1 hour. Once used, this property can’t be used again until you finish a long rest.

**13th Level.** You are immune to the damage listed in the Damage column of the **Dragon Type** table.

**17th Level.** As an action, you manifest a dragon’s frightful presence. Each creature of your choice within 30 feet of you and aware of you must succeed on a DC 19 WIS save or be frightened for 1 minute. A creature can repeat the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. Once used, this property can’t be used again until the next dawn.

## Dreadnought Plate

*Optional, from Player's Guide 2.*

*Armor (Plate), Fabled Priceless (5th-Level and Higher Properties Require Attunement)*

This especially thick plate armor is crafted from ancient metal, heavily scarred and weathered. Curved plates cover the joints at the shoulders, elbows, and knees, each inscribed with arcane runes. While wearing this magic armor, you can make an unarmed strike against a creature you can see within 5 feet of you, using the armor’s gauntlets, in place of one of your normal attacks. If the attack hits, it deals bludgeoning damage equal to 1d8 + your STR modifier. You are proficient with this attack and use your STR modifier for the attack roll.

***Armored Strength (Requires Attunement).*** As your level increases, you gain the following benefits while wearing this armor.

**5th Level.** Your carrying capacity increases to twice its normal value, and you can push, drag, or lift twice what you normally can. In addition, you have advantage on STR checks and saves.

**9th Level.** You gain a +1 bonus to AC. In addition, your gauntlet attacks now deal 1d12 bludgeoning damage, instead of 1d8. If you move at least 20 feet and then hit a creature or object with a gauntlet attack on the same turn, the target takes an extra 2d8 bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 15 STR save or be pushed up to 10 feet and knocked prone.

**13th Level.** You now gain a +2 bonus to AC, instead of +1. In addition, you are resistant to either bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage (your choice). You can change the damage type after a long rest.

**17th Level.** You gain a +2 bonus to STR. This bonus can’t raise your STR above 20.

## Helm of the Medusa

*Optional, from Player's Guide 2.*

*Wondrous Item, Fabled Priceless*

*(5th-Level and Higher Properties Require Attunement)*

This tarnished bronze helm covers the wearer’s eyes and is embossed with snakes. While wearing it, you have darkvision to a range of 30 feet. If you already have darkvision, wearing the helm increases its range by 30 feet.

***Aspect of the Medusa (Requires Attunement).*** As your level increases, you gain the following benefits while wearing this helm.

**5th Level.** You are resistant to poison damage and to the petrified and poisoned conditions. In addition, you can communicate with snakes as if you shared a language. As an action, you can cast the *[[1st-Circle Spells#Animal Friendship|animal friendship]]* spell (see *Player’s Guide*), but the target must be a snake. Once used, this property can’t be used again until the next dawn.

**9th Level.** As a bonus action, you can animate the snakes on the helm to attack a creature you can see within 10 feet. The target must succeed on a DC 15 DEX save or take 1d6 piercing damage plus 2d6 poison damage. In addition, as a reaction when a creature you can see targets you with a melee weapon attack, the snakes animate to intervene, and the attacker has disadvantage on the attack. You can use each of these properties a number of times equal to your PB, regaining all expended uses at the next dawn.

**13th Level.** You are immune to poison damage and to the petrified and poisoned conditions.

**17th Level.** As an action, you can turn your magical gaze toward a creature you can see within 30 feet that can also see you. That creature must make a DC 17 CON save. If the save fails by 5 or more, the creature is instantly petrified. Otherwise, a creature that fails the save begins to turn to stone and is restrained. The restrained creature must repeat the save at the end of its next turn, becoming petrified on a failure or ending the effect on itself on a success. The petrification lasts for 1 minute, or until the creature is freed by the *[[5th-Circle Spells#Greater Restoration|greater restoration]]* spell (see *Player’s* *Guide*) or other magic.

## Mayhem

*Optional, from Player's Guide 2.*

*Weapon (Greataxe), Fabled Priceless (5th-Level and Higher Properties Require Attunement)*

This greataxe has a dulled, brushed texture and shows many scars, but it occasionally seems to pulse as its impatient power bubbles out. While wielding this magic weapon, you can’t cast or concentrate on spells, but you are resistant to the charmed and frightened conditions and are immune to the effects of the *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Calm Emotions|calm emotions]]* spell (see *Player’s Guide*).

***Sentience.*** This is a sentient weapon with the following statistics: INT 11, WIS 9, CHA 17. It has hearing and darkvision out to a range of 120 feet and can communicate telepathically with its wielder by transmitting intense, chaotic emotions that unsettle the mind.

***Personality.*** The mind of *[[#Mayhem|Mayhem]]* is chaos incarnate. Whenever you find yourself in a position to make a decision, it floods your mind with thoughts urging you toward chaos.

***Chaos Reigns (Requires Attunement).*** As your level increases, you gain the following benefits while wielding this weapon. While attuned to *[[#Mayhem|Mayhem,]]* if you consistently choose order over chaos (GM’s discretion), you must succeed on a DC 13 CHA save or your attunement to it ends. You can attempt to reattune to it only once per dawn.

**5th Level.** When you hit with an attack using this weapon, the target takes an extra 1d8 psychic damage.

**9th Level.** You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon. In addition, it has 3 charges and regains all expended charges daily at dawn. As a reaction when you damage a creature with it, you can expend 1 charge to force the target to succeed on a DC 15 WIS save or suffer the effects of the *[[4th-Circle Spells#Confusion|confusion]]* spell (see *Player’s Guide*) for up to 1 minute, requiring no concentration.

**13th Level.** You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon, instead of +1. You are immune to the charmed and frightened conditions.

**17th Level.** You gain a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon, instead of +2. While wielding this weapon, as a reaction when you are hit by a weapon attack, you can cause another creature within range of the attack (which can include the attacker) to take an amount of psychic damage equal to the amount of damage you took from the attack. You can use this property a number of times per day equal to your PB, regaining all expended uses at the next dawn.

## Staff of the Toad King

*Optional, from Player's Guide 2.*

*Staff, Fabled Priceless*

*(5th-Level and Higher Properties Require Attunement)*

Warts dot the length of this green staff, an emerald frog sitting at its top. While holding this magic staff, you can speak with frogs and toads as if you shared a language.

***Toad Dominion (Requires Attunement).*** As your level increases, you gain the following benefits while holding the staff.

**5th Level.** You are resistant to poison damage and to the poisoned condition. In addition, you have a swimming speed equal to your walking speed and can breathe air and water. The staff has 5 charges and regains all expended charges at the next dawn. While holding the staff, as an action, you can expend 1 or more of its charges to cast one of the following spells from it, using CHA as the spellcasting ability: *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Conjure Animals|conjure animals]]* (frogs and toads only, 3 charges), *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Enlarge/Reduce|enlarge/reduce]]* (2 charges), *[[1st-Circle Spells#Longstrider|longstrider]]* (1 charge), and *[[1st-Circle Spells#Thunderwave|thunderwave]]* (1 charge). (See *Player’s Guide* for spells.)

**9th Level.** The staff now has 10 charges. In addition to the previously listed spells, you can cast the following additional spells from the staff, expending the necessary number of charges: *[[5th-Circle Spells#Contagion|contagion]]* (5 charges) and *[[4th-Circle Spells#Polymorph|polymorph]]* (frog or toad only, 4 charges). In addition, as a bonus action, you can expend 1 charge to make your skin secrete a mild toxin for 1 minute. For the duration, a creature that touches you or hits you with a melee attack while within 5 feet of you must succeed on a DC 15 CON save or be poisoned until the end of its next turn.

**13th Level.** You can make a long jump equal to your walking speed and a high jump equal to half your walking speed, with or without a running start. In addition, as an action, you can expend 3 charges to call forth a rain of hundreds of frogs in a 10-foot radius centered on a point you can see within 60 feet. Each creature in the area must make a DC 17 DEX save, taking 8d6 bludgeoning damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. The area then becomes difficult terrain for 1 minute.

**17th Level.** You are immune to poison damage and to the poisoned condition. The staff now has 20 charges. In addition to the previously listed spells, you can cast the following additional spells from the staff, expending the necessary number of charges: *[[8th-Circle Spells#Animal Shapes|animal shapes]]* (8 charges) and *[[7th-Circle Spells#Transmogrification|transmogrification]]* (7 charges).

## Bright Fey Weapons (Labyrinth Worldbook)

*Optional, from Labyrinth Worldbook.*

The bright fey of the Summerlands have always stood sentinel against the creeping malignance of the Void, but recent years have seen the fey fall farther out of touch with the constant turbulence in the space between worlds. Some centuries ago, in an effort to better arm those most directly affected by the spread of the Void, several fey lords and ladies of the Bright Lands created an array of weapons to aid mortalkind in their continuing efforts.

Three such fabled weapons have been documented: the *springtide bow*, the *summerborn spear*, and the *frostfallow* *blade*. However, many more of these weapons likely exist somewhere in the [[Worlds of the Labyrinth|Ten Thousand Worlds]], waiting to be claimed by heroes worthy of their might.

See the start of this page for how fabled items work.

### Frostfallow Blade

*Optional, from Labyrinth Worldbook.*

*Weapon (Greatsword), Fabled Priceless*

Born of the dangerous and cunning archfey who preside over winter, the *frostfallow blade* is the ultimate embodiment of nature’s cruel finality. The hilt and cross guard of this greatsword is made from a living branch of pure gold, with a black steel blade marbled with cracks of electric blue magical light. A thread of blue magical light is wrapped around the hilt like a braided cord, and part of this cord hangs off the pommel of the hilt, weighted by a diamond charm shaped like a snowflake.

While you hold this sword, you can’t be disarmed of it by any means unless you allow it.

***Winter’s Bite (Requires Attunement).*** As your level increases, you gain the following benefits while wielding the sword.

**5th Level.** While holding this weapon, you can stand on and move across any liquid surface as if it were solid ground. You also gain the ability to breathe underwater.

**9th Level.** You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this blade. If you are holding the sword at the start of your turn, you can spend 1 hit die to recover hit points, as you would on a short rest. While within 30 feet of the sword, you have advantage on death saves, and spells or spell‑like abilities that would kill you outright instead reduce you to 0 HP.

**13th Level.** You now gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this blade, instead of +1. You are covered in a layer of permafrost which allows you to ignore 10 points of all instances of nonmagical damage.

**17th Level.** You now gain a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this blade, instead of +2. Once per turn when you successfully hit a creature with the blade, it must succeed on a DC 15 CON save or be paralyzed until the end of its next turn as it is frozen in place. Once you successfully paralyze a creature, you can’t use this feature again until you finish a short or long rest.

### Springtide Bow

*Optional, from Labyrinth Worldbook.*

*Weapon (Longbow), Fabled Priceless*

Lovingly crafted from a shoot of [[Yggdrasil]] itself, the *springtide bow* is the ultimate creation of the archfey who preside over the flora and fauna of the fey courts. This longbow is made from a living branch of gold, strung with a cord of vibrant green light. Vines dotted with colorful wildflowers grow and twine around the weapon’s body.

You don’t need ammunition to fire this bow. When you prepare to make a ranged attack with it, the bow magically conjures a loaded arrow of green light.

***Vitality’s Kiss (Requires Attunement).*** As your level increases, you gain the following benefits while wielding this bow.

**5th Level.** While you hold this bow, your walking speed can’t be reduced below 30 feet by any means, even if you are encumbered or wearing heavy armor. Your steps also make no sound, and you always have advantage on DEX (Stealth) checks made to move silently.

**9th Level.** You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this bow. As an action, you can use the nectar of the bow’s flowers to soothe the wounds of one creature you touch. The target regains hit points equal to its maximum number of hit dice. A creature can only be healed in this way once per short rest, and this healing has no effect on Undead or Constructs. Once used three times, this property can’t be used again until next dawn.

**13th Level.** You now gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this bow, instead of +1. While you hold the bow, any creature that targets you with an attack while within 30 feet of you must first attempt a DC 15 CHA save. On a failed save, the creature must choose a new target or lose the triggering attack or spell. On a success, the attack works as normal and this property is suppressed for 1 minute. Creatures immune to the charmed condition are immune to this effect.

**17th Level.** You now gain a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this bow, instead of +2. While holding the bow, you regain 10 HP at the start of each of your turns. If you take fire damage, this trait is suppressed until the following dawn.

### Summerborn Spear

*Optional, from Labyrinth Worldbook.*

*Weapon (Spear), Fabled Priceless*

The *summerborn spear* is a treasure forged by the archfey who preside over the blazing radiance of the Summerlands. The shaft of this spear is a living branch of pure gold, topped by a red-hued steel head shaped like a flame. The “cords” wrapped around the point where the spearhead meets the shaft are ribbons of scarlet red, vibrant orange, and rich yellow magical energy. Some of these ribbons hang freely from the spear, weighted down by decorative ruby beads.

While you hold the spear, you can use an action to cause it to emit bright light out to a radius of 20 feet and dim light for an additional 20 feet. You can reduce the radius of this light by 5 feet, expand the radius of this light by 5 feet (to a maximum radius of 20 feet), or extinguish the light entirely with a bonus action.

***Nature’s Brilliance (Requires***

***Attunement).*** As your level increases, you gain the following benefits while wielding the spear.

**5th Level.** While holding the spear you ignore the effects of extreme cold and extreme heat. In addition, as long as you are conscious, you recover hit points equal to your PB at the start of each of your turns.

**9th Level.** You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this spear. Hostile creatures that start their turns within 5 feet of you must make a DC 15 CON save. On a failure, a creature takes a number of d6s fire damage equal to your PB. On a success, it takes half as much damage.

**13th Level.** You now gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this spear, instead of +1. You are immune to fire damage and have resistance to radiant damage.

**17th Level.** You now gain a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this spear, instead of +2. When you score a critical hit with this weapon against a creature, the target takes an additional 50 points of magical piercing damage. If this damage kills a creature, it bursts into a flare of brilliant light and is destroyed. Undead within 100 feet of the target take 2d6 radiant damage, and plants in the affected area become vibrant and overgrown as with the *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Plant Growth|plant growth]]* spell.

## The Grave Blade

*Optional, from Northlands Worldbook.*

*Weapon (Longsword), Fabled Priceless (5th-level and higher properties require attunement)*

The reavers of [[Wolfheim]] brought home many strange treasures as souvenirs and mementos from their campaigns abroad. A dwarven warrior named Essalla claimed a strange sword as her share of the spoils on one such raiding journey. Unusual in design and unadorned by dwarvish standards, it seemed destined to be a souvenir brought out only when tales of the campaign were to be told.

This sword has a dull, lifeless grey blade, and the hilt is carved from aged bone. The guard is made from ribs, and the pommel is sourced from the rounded end of a femur, both cast in bronze.

The Grave Blade became known in different tales, for the threads of fate of each bearer seemed to be cut short soon after taking possession of it. It has changed hands many times over the next nine years, seemingly taken from a corpse as often as it was exchanged for gold or favors.

The blade is known for the song it hums in the presence of undead, and with the death of its carriers, the blade’s song grows ever more insistent. Some say the song is a warning, while others theorize that it wants to be found and interred with the body of its first bearer.

The souvenir of so much death was lost among a merchant’s wares that never reached their destination in Wolfheim, and the mystery of its song persists in tales told on dark nights when the fire burns low.

The pommel of this sword hums a wordless song that can be heard within a 5-foot radius when Undead are within 60 ft. of it. As your level increases, it has the following additional properties if you are wielding it or holding it while attuned to it:

***5th level.*** When you are reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, you can drop to 1 hit point instead. If the damage came from a creature, you have advantage on attack rolls against it for 1 minute. Once used, the sword can’t be used in this way again until the next dawn.

***9th level.*** You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this sword. In addition, the sword deals an additional 1d6 necrotic damage on a hit.

***13th level.*** You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls (instead of +1) with this sword. In addition, when the sword deals damage to a living creature, the target must succeed on a DC 15 CHA save or become frightened until the end of its next turn.

***17th level.*** You gain a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls (instead of +2) with this sword. If you make a successful attack roll against a creature that has 50 hit points or fewer, you can compel it to die. Once used, the sword can’t be used in this way again until the next dawn.

## Skinchanger Arm Ring

*Optional, from Northlands Worldbook.*

*Wondrous Item, Fabled Priceless (5th-level and higher properties require attunement)*

This arm ring is made from copper and is covered with a patina that cannot be removed.

There was once a sect of Northern warriors dedicated to learning from the animals in their region. Each member of the order sought to exemplify the capabilities and spirit of their chosen animal. The raven warrior was keen to observe and could travel far and fast. The follower of the wolf fought well in conjunction with others. Others had strength and endurance like bears.

These ideals were forged into an enchanted arm ring worn by the leader of the sect. Whether this magic was achieved with the aid of dwarves, druids, or sorcerers, none can say. With experience, the wearer of the arm ring could shapeshift into one of the three animals exemplified by the warriors.

Though the order won many victories on the battlefield, they couldn’t be held in the employ of any one jarl. The wilds called to them, and they visited the settled lands of the North less and less as the years went by, until they were never seen again.

While wearing this arm ring, you can comprehend and verbally communicate with ravens, wolves, and bears of all varieties so long as they are beasts, and you can use any skills to try to change their attitudes, not just animal handling.

As your level increases, you gain the following benefits while wearing this arm ring.

***5th level.*** You can use an action to grow black-feathered wings and gain a flying speed of 50 feet for 1 hour. While you have these wings, your size decreases by one category— from medium to small, for example. You also have disadvantage on STR checks and STR saves. Your attacks with weapons or unarmed strikes deal 1d4 less damage on a hit (this can’t reduce damage below 1). You can end the effect early using a bonus action. Once used, the arm ring can’t be used in this way again until the next dawn.

***9th level.*** You have advantage on attack rolls with melee weapons and unarmed strikes against a creature if at least one of your allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn’t incapacitated.

***13th level.*** You can use an action to give yourself temporary hit points equal to twice your level. You also are resistant to cold damage until the temporary hit points are depleted or you finish a long rest. Once used, the arm ring can’t be used in this way again until the next dawn.

***17th level.*** The arm ring has 3 charges. You can use an action and spend 1 charge to cast the *[[4th-Circle Spells#Polymorph|polymorph]]* spell on yourself. The forms you can take are limited to a raven; wolf or dire wolf; or black, brown, or polar bear. When you cast *[[4th-Circle Spells#Polymorph|polymorph]]* in this way, you retain your INT, WIS, and CHA scores. The arm ring regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn.

## Storrbani

*Optional, from Northlands Worldbook.*

*Weapon, Fabled Priceless (5th-level and higher properties require attunement)*

Thogar Giant-Hewer earned great renown through unwavering bravery but not intellectual brilliance. His unmatched courage often forged far ahead of his reason. As a youth, he could not prevent marauding giants and trolls from destroying his village and murdering his family. Left alone on the blood-soaked snow, a fire kindled deep in Thogar’s heart. From that day, he unwaveringly sought vengeance against giantfolk, not for glory or renown but in the hope his daring might earn him friends or even a new family of his own.

The Giant-Hewer became a terror to trolls and giants alike. Though not the strongest warrior and certainly never the wisest, he braved troll dens and giant camps, throwing himself into battle where others paused to strategize. Yet time and again, the people he saved turned cold. His deeds, though brave, were rarely welcome. His plans resulted in the death of everyone but himself. His traps lured giants to their doom at the expense of entire villages. Those he rescued offered no feasts of thanks, only wary glances and closed doors. The more he gave, the more alone he became, yet still he fought on believing if he was only brave enough, he could win through to find a new family.

Thogar met his end in his own typical fashion. After running across the trail of a band of storm giants heading toward a village, he foolishly attacked them in the settlement square. His blade, Storrbani, slew many giants before the others fled, but unfortunately, the fires ignited by the battle burned down half the village. Thogar’s shattered body lay in the remains of the wedding feast (to which the giants had been invited), and no one came to his aid or to witness his last breath. Skalds say he died utterly alone, but when the Valkyries came to collect him, he did not wish to enter Valhalla alone. Taking pity on the brave warrior, they cast his spirit into his blade, binding it there until the day he falls again, fighting beside a true friend, so together they might enter the Hall of Golden Shields side by side.

This longsword is supernaturally light and easy to use. Its weight is reduced to 1 pound, and it gains the Finesse weapon property. Sparks trail from Storrbani’s blade as though it were a burning branch. When you hit a target with Storrbani, the target takes an extra 1d4 fire damage as the blade burns upon impact.

As your level increases, you gain the following benefits while wielding or holding this weapon.

***5th level.*** The rune *Gebo* appears on the blade. As a bonus action, you can touch the rune and cast *[[1st-Circle Spells#Shield of Faith|shield of faith]]* on a creature (besides yourself) you can see within 60 feet. You don’t need to concentrate on the spell to maintain it. Once used in this way, you must finish a short rest before you can use it again.

In addition, the spirit of Thogar Giant-Hewer awakens within the longsword. It becomes a sentient, Lawful Neutral weapon with INT 8, WIS 13, and CHA 10. It has hearing and vision out to a range of 60 feet. It communicates telepathically with its wielder, and it can speak in a gravelly voice much like an angry growl. It can read and understand Common and Giant. Thogar’s spirit is passionate but not particularly bright. It wants the wielder to confront giants and trolls, and (secretly) to fall in glorious battle against the forces of [[Jotunheim]] so that wielder and blade may feast and fight together in eternal Valhalla. It urges its wielder to seek out and destroy evil trolls and giants, and to never back down from a fight. If its wielder displays cowardice or refuses an opportunity to battle an evil troll or other giant, the spirit sulks, suppressing the magical properties of the weapon until the following dawn.

***9th level.*** You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this weapon, and the amount of fire damage it deals increases to 2d6. In addition, the rune *Tiwaz* appears on the blade. As a bonus action, you can touch this rune and give the extra fire damage from this blade to the weapon of a creature you can see within 60 feet. At the end of your next turn, the fire damage property returns to Storrbani.

***13th level.*** You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this weapon instead of a +1 bonus. In addition, the rune *Thurisaz* appears on the blade. As a bonus action, you can touch this rune. When you do so, your attack rolls with this magic weapon score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20 until the end of your next turn. If the target is a creature of the giant type, Storrbani deals an extra 1d8 damage on a critical hit.

***17th level.*** You gain a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this weapon, instead of a +2 bonus. In addition, the rune *Ansuz* appears on the blade. As a bonus action, you can touch the rune and be immune to all damage until the end of your next turn. Once used, the sword can’t be used again in this way until the next dawn.

## Tordalfr’s Rebuttal

*Optional, from Northlands Worldbook.*

*Javelin, Fabled Priceless (5th-level and higher properties require attunement)*

Tordalfr was a storm giantkin born to a human mother in a small village. After years of jibes and taunts from the children of local warriors, he snapped.

Brael, son of the village headman, and a pompous, aspiring warrior, called Tordalfr out in public, proclaiming to his assembled cronies that there was irrefutable evidence that Tordalfr was the unwanted child of forbidden lovers, and defying anyone present to offer proof to the contrary.

Tordalfr’s fury reached such a pitch that the cloudless sky opened up and a bolt of lightning struck his outstretched hand, coalescing into the form of a throwing spear, which he hurled at his tormentor. The javelin struck Brael in the chest, hurling him backward and leaving a blackened, smoking crater where his heart had been. Tordalfr marched furiously from the village and never returned.

When you hit with a ranged weapon attack using this Snaerispear, you may push the target up to 10 feet directly away from you.

As your level increases, you gain the following benefits while wielding or holding this weapon.

***5th level.*** Immediately after hitting or missing an enemy, this javelin reappears to your hand.

***9th level.*** You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this weapon. In addition, when thrown, the javelin is wreathed in electricity, dealing an extra 1d6 points of lightning damage to any target it hits.

***13th level.*** You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this weapon instead of +1. In addition, you can use a bonus action to charge your next attack with the weapon with electricity. The next time you hit with this javelin before the end of your turn, the target must succeed on a CON save (DC equals 8 + your DEX modifier + your PB ) or be stunned until the start of your next turn. Once you use this ability, you cannot use it again until you complete a short rest.

***17th level.*** You gain a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this weapon, instead of +2. In addition, when you hit with this weapon, you can call down a bolt of lightning from the heavens to strike the target. This bolt deals 8d8 lightning damage to the target and all creatures within 15 feet of it. Creatures within the blast radius are pushed 15 feet away from the target. Once used, this ability can’t be used again until the next dawn.

## Monster Hunter’s Crossbow

*Weapon (Heavy Crossbow), Fabled Priceless (5th-level and higher properties require attunement)*

This crossbow is made of polished wood and iron, inlaid with silver runes. Its attacks count as magical for the purpose of overcoming resistance and immunity to nonmagical attacks and damage.

In addition, when you hit an Aberration, Dragon, Fiend, Monstrosity, or Undead (or a creature with the Lycanthrope tag) with this weapon, the target takes an extra 2d6 piercing damage.

***Slayer’s Prowess (Requires Attunement).*** As your level increases, you gain the following benefits while wielding this weapon.

**9th Level.** You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon.

## Shroud of Shadows

*Optional, from Night Hunters.*

*Wondrous Item, Fabled Priceless (5th-level and higher properties require attunement)*

This cape is woven from a light, gauzy material that blurs at the edges, as if made of mist or smoke. While wearing this cloak, you have darkvision to a range of 60 feet and advantage on DEX (Stealth) checks.

In addition, as an action while you are in dim light or darkness, you can become invisible as long as you remain motionless—or until you attack or cast a spell.

***Gloaming Gifts (Requires Attunement).*** As your level increases, you gain the following benefits while wearing this cloak.

**5th Level.** The cloak has 5 charges and regains all expended charges daily at dawn. As an action while wearing this cape, you can expend 1 or more of its charges to cast one of the following spells from it (spell save DC 17): *[[4th-Circle Spells#Black Tentacles|black tentacles]]* (3 charges), *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Darkness|darkness]]* (1 charge), *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Fear|fear]]* (2 charges), and *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Misty Step|misty step]]* (1 charge).

**9th Level.** As an action, you can transform yourself, along with anything you’re wearing or carrying, into a Medium cloud of mist. This functions as the *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Gaseous Form|gaseous form]]* spell, except that you have a flying speed of 20 feet, and it does not require concentration. Once used, this property can’t be used again until the next dawn.

**13th Level.** As a bonus action, you can cause the cloak to swirl in a disorienting fashion. You gain 30 temporary hit points. While you have any of these temporary hit points, creatures have disadvantage on attack rolls against you. This property is suppressed while you are incapacitated, restrained, or otherwise unable to move. When the temporary hit points are expended, the effect ends. Once used, this property can’t be used again until the next dawn.

**17th Level.** When you expend a charge to cast the *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Darkness|darkness]]* spell from this cloak, you can choose to add the following effect: a creature that enters the area for the first time on its turn or starts its turn there must make a DC 17 CON save. On a failure, it takes 6d6 necrotic damage, and its speed is halved until the beginning of its next turn. On a success, it takes half as much damage, and its speed is unaffected.

## Witch Light

*Optional, from Night Hunters.*

*Wondrous Item, Fabled Priceless (5th-level and higher properties require attunement)*

This lantern is crafted from elaborate wrought iron and detailed stained glass, depicting blue and green motes of light that float within skeletal branches. As an action while holding the lantern, you can cause it to emit bright light in a 15-foot radius and dim light for an additional 15 feet. The light can only be seen by specified creatures (your choice). You can expand or reduce this radius (no action required) by 5 feet each round, to a maximum of 30 feet and a minimum of 5 feet—or no light at all. It stops emitting light if not held.

The lantern can be used as a spellcasting focus to channel spells of any source.

***Eldritch Illumination (Requires Attunement).*** As your level increases, you gain the following benefits while holding this lantern.

**5th Level.** As an action, you can focus the lantern’s light into a 15-foot cone of eldritch light for 1 minute. The area illuminated allows you to see in magical darkness as though it were dim light, and it also reveals secret doors. Once used, this property can’t be used again until the next dawn.

**9th Level.** You gain a +1 bonus to spell attack rolls while holding the lantern, and the cone of eldritch light can now be used twice before the next dawn.

In addition, as an action, you can cast the *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Moonbeam|moonbeam]]* spell (5th circle, spell save DC 18). Creatures within the area of the cone of eldritch light have disadvantage on saves against this spell. Once used, this property can’t be used again until the next dawn.

**13th Level.** You now gain a +2 bonus to spell attack rolls while holding the lantern, instead of +1, and the cone of eldritch light is now 30 feet, instead of 15 feet. The eldritch light now also reveals invisible creatures and items—in their true forms, if applicable—and visual illusions appear transparent, allowing you and your allies to automatically succeed on saves against them.

In addition, as an action, you can summon three **will-o’-wisps** that remain for 1 hour. The creatures are friendly to you and your companions. They act on your initiative, taking their turn immediately after yours, and obey any verbal commands you give them (no action required by you). If you don’t give any commands, they defend themselves from hostile creatures but otherwise take no actions. They disappear when reduced to 0 HP or when the duration ends.

**17th Level.** You now gain a +3 bonus to spell attack rolls while holding the lantern, instead of +2, and the cone of eldritch light can now be used three times before the next dawn.
