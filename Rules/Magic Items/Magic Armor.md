## Animated Shield

*Armor (Shield), Very Rare 30,000 gp (Requires Attunement)*

While wielding this shield, you can speak its command word as a bonus action to cause it to animate. The shield leaps into the air and hovers in your space to protect you as if you were wielding it, leaving your hands free. The shield remains animated for 1 minute, until you use a bonus action to end this effect, or until you are incapacitated or die. Then the shield falls to the ground or flies into your hand if you have one free.

## Armor of Resistance

*Armor (Light, Medium, or Heavy), Rare 5,000 gp + armor base cost (Requires Attunement)*

You are resistant to one type of damage while you wear this armor. The GM chooses the type or determines it randomly from the options below.

| 1 | Acid | 6 | Necrotic |
| 2 | Cold | 7 | Poison |
| 3 | Fire | 8 | Psychic |
| 4 | Force | 9 | Radiant |
| 5 | Lightning | 10 | Thunder |

## Armor of Thorns

*Optional, from Player's Guide 2.*

*Armor (Light), Very Rare 45,000 gp (Requires Attunement)*

While wearing this armor, you gain a +2 bonus to AC. In addition, as a bonus action, you can cause sharp thorns to emerge from the surface of the armor, providing you the following:

- A creature that you grapple, or that grapples you, takes 1d6 piercing damage after each successful grapple check.

- A creature within 5 feet of you that attacks you with a melee weapon attack takes 1d4 piercing damage.

- You gain a climbing speed of 30 feet. The thorns remain in place for 1 minute or until you use a bonus action to dismiss them.

## Arrow-Catching Shield

*Armor (Shield), Rare 8,000 gp (Requires Attunement)*

You gain a +2 bonus to AC against ranged attacks while you wield this shield. This bonus is in addition to the shield’s normal bonus to AC. In addition, when an attacker makes a ranged attack against a target within 5 feet of you, you can use your reaction to become the target of the attack instead.

## Demon Armor

*Armor (Plate), Very Rare 60,000 gp (Requires Attunement)*

While wearing this armor, you gain a +1 bonus to AC, and you can understand and speak Abyssal. In addition, the armor’s clawed gauntlets turn unarmed strikes with your hands into magic weapons that deal slashing damage, with a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls and a damage die of 1d8.

***Curse.*** Once you don this cursed armor, you can’t doff it unless you are targeted by a *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Remove Curse|remove curse]]* spell or similar magic. While wearing the armor, you have disadvantage on attack rolls against demons and on saves against their spells and special abilities.

## Dragon Scale Mail

*Armor (Scale Mail), Very Rare 60,000 gp (Requires Attunement)*

Dragon scale mail is made of the scales of one kind of dragon. Sometimes dragons collect their cast-off scales and gift them. Other times, hunters skin and preserve the hide of a dead dragon. In either case, dragon scale mail is highly valued.

While wearing this armor, you gain a +1 bonus to AC, you have advantage on saves against the Frightful Presence and breath weapons of dragons, and you are resistant to one type of damage. The damage type depends on the dragon scales used to make the armor, as shown on the **Dragon Scale Mail Type** table.

In addition, you can focus your senses as an action to magically discern the distance and direction to the closest dragon within 30 miles of you that is of the same type as the armor. This special action can’t be used again until the next dawn.

**Dragon Scale Mail Type**

| Dragon | Damage | Dragon | Damage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Black | Acid | Gold | Fire |
| Blue | Lightning | Green | Poison |
| Brass | Fire | Red | Fire |
| Bronze | Lightning | Silver | Cold |
| Copper | Acid | White | Cold |

## Dwarven Plate

*Armor (Plate), Very Rare 10,000 gp*

While wearing this armor, you gain a +2 bonus to AC. In addition, if an effect moves you against your will along the ground, you can use your reaction to reduce the distance you are moved by up to 10 feet.

## Featherlight Armor

*Armor (Light, Medium, or Heavy), Common 25 gp*

*+ armor base cost* These suits of armor are etched with cloud and feather motifs. When you would take falling damage while wearing this armor, roll a d12 and subtract the result from the damage taken.

## Goblin Shield

*Optional, from Tales of the Old Margreve.*

*Armor (Shield), Rare (Requires Attunement) 4,000 gp*

This shield resembles a snarling goblin's head. It has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. While wielding this shield, you can use a bonus action to expend 1 charge and command the goblin's head to bite a creature within 5 feet of you. Make a melee weapon attack with the shield. You have proficiency with this attack if you are proficient with shields. On a hit, the target takes 2d4 piercing damage.

## Iced Steel Armor

*Optional, from Northlands Worldbook.*

*Armor (Any medium or heavy armor made primarily of metal), Uncommon 1,000 gp + armor base cost*

This suit of armor is made of iced steel, which legends hold originates in the frozen underworld of Niflheim. Objects made of iced steel always feel cold to the touch. While you are wearing it, you automatically succeed on saves against extreme heat, and you have advantage on saves against spells involving fire and heat.

## Laekning Shield

*Optional, from Northlands Worldbook.*

*Round shield, Uncommon 1,000 + shield base cost*

While holding this shield, you have a +1 bonus to AC, in addition to the Shield’s normal bonus to AC. This round shield magically repairs itself and is not destroyed at the end of an encounter where you are struck.

## Prismatic Steel Armor

*Optional, from Northlands Worldbook.*

*Armor (medium or heavy, metal), Uncommon 2,000 gp (Requires attunement)*

This suit of armor is made of prismatic steel infused with the power of the Bifrost. While you are wearing it, you automatically succeed on saving throws against effects that would push, pull, teleport, or otherwise move you against your will.

While attuned to the armor, you can use an action to teleport the armor onto or off of your body, even if it is on another plane of existence from you. When you teleport the armor off of your body, it moves to an adjacent square. You can't use this effect again until the next dawn.

## Quickbuckle Armor

*Armor (Any), Common 250 gp + base armor cost*

While carrying or wearing this armor, you can use your action to speak a command word to instantly don or doff it.

## Quillback Armor

*Armor (Leather or Hide), Common 100 gp + armor base cost*

This armor has sharp porcupine quills sewn throughout the body. While you are grappling a creature, the creature takes 1d4 damage at the start of each of your turns.

## Reciprocating Armor

*Optional, from Player's Guide 2.*

*Armor (Any Medium or Heavy), Rare 5,000 gp + base armor cost*

While wearing this armor, as a reaction to being hit by a melee attack, the attacker must succeed on a DC 14 CON save or take bludgeoning damage equal to half the damage dealt to you.

## Rostungr Armor

*Optional, from Northlands Worldbook.*

*Armor (light or hide), Very Rare 30,000 gp*

You have a +1 bonus to AC while wearing this armor. You can take a bonus action to cause the armor to envelop you for 1 minute. You grow flippers and a thick layer of blubber, which remain for the duration. While in this state, your speed is halved, you have a swimming speed of 30 ft., and you are resistant to bludgeoning damage. Once this action is used, it can’t be used again until the next dawn.

## Sealskin Armor

*Optional, from Northlands Worldbook.*

*Armor (Any light), Uncommon 2,000 gp (Requires attunement)*

You have a swimming speed equal to your normal speed when wearing this armor. Also, you have advantage on any checks and saves to avoid or end being grappled.

## Shield of Annihilation

*Optional, from Labyrinth Worldbook.*

*Armor (Shield), Very Rare 30,000 gp*

This entirely black shield looks like a hole in reality. No light that touches it seems to escape. When a creature attacks you with a weapon while you are wielding this shield, you can use a reaction to intercept the attack. The attacker must succeed on a DC 15 DEX save or its weapon hits the shield and is subjected to the shield’s magic. If the affected weapon is a manufactured weapon and is nonmagical, it’s destroyed by the shield. If the affected weapon is a manufactured weapon and is magical, it loses any magical properties it has for 1 hour. If the affected weapon is an unarmed strike or the natural weapon of a creature, the attacker takes 2d10 force damage.

As an action, you can deliberately touch the shield to a nonmagical object or other nonmagical material to destroy it. The shield can destroy up to a 2-foot cube of nonmagical material with an action.

Once the shield destroys 24 cubic feet of nonmagical objects or material, it can’t destroy more. A newly found *shield of annihilation* has already destroyed 1d6 cubic feet of nonmagical material. If the shield can’t destroy any more nonmagical material and it’s used to do so, its enchantment overloads, causing the shield to crumble to dust.

## Shield of Clamor

*Armor (Shield), Common 25 gp + base shield cost*

The surface of this shield vibrates unpredictably when struck. When a weapon attack misses you while you are wielding this shield, it makes one of several sounds that are audible to 60 feet. Choose which sound it makes from the following options: a beaten drum, a duck’s quack, a gopher’s screech, an elephant’s trumpet, a foghorn, a bleating goat, or a ringing bell.

## Shield of Dislocation

*Optional, from Labyrinth Worldbook.*

*Armor (Shield), Rare (Requires Attunement) 30,000 gp*

The shield has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. While you are wielding the shield, you can use a bonus action to expend 1 charge and swing the shield at a creature you can see within 5 feet of you. The target must succeed on a DC 15 DEX save or be briefly sucked into the shield then teleported to an unoccupied space you can see within 60 feet of you.

## Shield of Missile Attraction

*Armor (Shield), Rare 8,000 gp (Requires Attunement)*

While holding this shield, you have resistance to damage from ranged weapon attacks.

***Curse.*** This shield is cursed. Attuning to it curses you until you are targeted by the *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Remove Curse|remove curse]]* spell or similar magic. Removing the shield fails to end the curse on you. When a ranged weapon attack is made against a target within 10 feet of you, the curse causes you to become the target instead.

## Slipforged Armor

*Armor (Any), Common 100 gp + armor base cost*

Every surface of this armor is slippery and hard to hold on to. While you wear this armor, creatures have disadvantage on checks made to grapple or restrain you and you have advantage on checks made to escape a grapple.

## Songsteel Armor

*Optional, from Northlands Worldbook.*

*Armor (Any metal), Uncommon 1,000 gp*

Metal armor made of songsteel rings like a bell or chime when it clangs against an object. When wearing the armor, you can call out to [[Volund]] as a bonus action. Until the end of your next turn, you take 5 less damage from all attacks that hit you. Once you use this feature, you must finish a long rest before you can use it again.

## Songsteel Shield

*Optional, from Northlands Worldbook.*

*Armor (shield), Uncommon 1,000 gp*

Metal shields made of songsteel rings like a bell or chime when they clang against an object. When holding the shield, you can call out to Volund as a bonus action. Until the end of your next turn, you have a +5 bonus to your AC. Once you use this feature, you must finish a long rest before you can use it again.

## Spellguard Shield

*Armor (Shield), Very Rare (Requires Attunement) 60,000 gp*

While wielding this shield, you have advantage on saves against spells and other magical effects, and spell attacks have disadvantage against you.

## Starry Half Plate

*Optional, from Labyrinth Worldbook.*

*Armor (Half Plate), Legendary (Requires Attunement) 100,000 gp*

Crafted from the hide of a void dragon, this black armor seems to contain stars, comets, and wispy, Void-like veils of purple, red, and blue. While wearing this armor, you gain a +1 bonus to AC.

When a creature hits you with a weapon attack while you are wearing this armor, it must make a DC 17 CON save. On a failure, the void energy in the armor lashes out after the weapon deals its damage. If the weapon is a manufactured weapon and is nonmagical, it’s destroyed by this energy. If the weapon is a manufactured weapon and is magical, it loses any magical properties it has for 1 hour because of this energy. If the weapon is an unarmed strike or the natural weapon of a creature, the attacker takes 1d12 necrotic damage from the energy.

## Sunfire Armor

*Optional, from Labyrinth Worldbook.*

*Armor (Any), Uncommon 1,500 gp + armor base cost*

You prove resistant to necrotic damage while wearing this armor. You also gain advantage on saves against Void terrain effects and hazards (see **Chapter 7**), spells from the void magic style spell list (see **Chapter 5**), and dread effects. The armor’s brilliant golden sheen emits flickering bright light in a 5-foot radius and dim light for an additional 5 feet. This illumination remains unaffected by magical darkness.

## Sunfire Plate

*Optional, from Labyrinth Worldbook.*

*Armor (Half Plate or Plate), Rare 2,500 gp + base armor cost (Requires Attunement)*

The crystalline surface of this armor is transparent, allowing you to see the veins of molten fire that run through its core. While wearing this armor, you gain a +1 bonus to AC and can comfortably survive in temperatures as low as –100 degrees Fahrenheit.

In addition, you can use a bonus action to cause the armor to emit sunlight in a 15-foot radius and dim light for an additional 15 feet. While activated, you can use a bonus action to extinguish the light entirely, or to expand or reduce the radius of sunlight and dim light by 5 feet each, to a maximum of 30 feet each or a minimum of 10 feet each.

## Swarm Suit

*Optional, from Player's Guide 2.*

*Armor (Leather or Padded), Rare 8,800 gp (Requires Attunement)*

This armor has stitching all over it, giving the impression that it could fall to pieces at any time.

The armor has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. While wearing it, as an action, you can expend 1 charge to discorporate your body into hundreds of discrete chunks, becoming a swarm for 1 hour. As a swarm, you gain the following abilities:

- You are immune to the grappled, paralyzed, petrified, prone, restrained, and stunned conditions.

- You are resistant to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage.

- You can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny version of you.

- You can occupy another creature’s space, and vice versa. While a swarm, you can no longer deal weapon damage, but you can target a creature in your space with an attack, dealing your unarmed strike damage on a hit, or half that damage if you have half your HP or fewer. You can still cast spells, but only those with verbal or somatic components. As a bonus action, you can return to your normal form.

## Tireless Armor

*Optional, from Northlands Worldbook.*

*Armor (Any), Rare (Requires attunement) 2,000 gp*

This armor has 3 charges and regains 1d3 charges daily at dawn. Each time you would suffer a level of exhaustion, you may spend a charge to negate that exhaustion.

As long as the armor has at least 1 charge remaining, you cannot take penalties to your speed.

## Valhallan Armor

*Optional, from Northlands Worldbook.*

*Armor (Any medium or heavy), Rare 4,000 gp (Requires attunement)*

While wearing this armor, you aren’t unconscious when you drop to 0 HP. You still make death saves normally. You can’t use the Help action to make a Medicine check to stabilize yourself.

## Verdant Armor

*Optional, from Player's Guide 2.*

*Armor (Hide, Leather, or Padded), Rare (Requires Attunement) 5,000 gp + base armor cost*

While wearing this armor, you gain a +1 bonus to AC. In addition, you have advantage on DEX (Stealth) checks when near suitably obscuring plant life (such as trees or hedges, large cactuses, or scattered patches of tall grasses), and you ignore difficult terrain due to nonmagical plant life.

Each day at dawn, the armor grows three pieces of magical fruit, which you can pick as a bonus action and eat as an action. A creature eating one of these fruits regains 1d8 + 4 hit points and has sufficient nourishment to sustain them for 24 hours.
