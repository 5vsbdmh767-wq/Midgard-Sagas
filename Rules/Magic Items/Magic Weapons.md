## Ammunition, +1, +2, or +3

*Weapon (Any Ammunition), Rarity Varies Price Varies*

You have a bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this piece of magic ammunition. The bonus is determined by the rarity of the ammunition. Once it successfully hits a target, the ammunition becomes a nonmagical piece of ammunition.

**Magic Ammunition Rarity**

| Bonus | Rarity | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| +1 | Uncommon | 50 gp (each) |
| +2 | Rare | 100 gp (each) |
| +3 | Very Rare | 350 gp (each) |

## Ammunition of True Death

*Optional, from Night Hunters.*

*Weapon (Any Ammunition), Very Rare 100 gp + base ammunition cost*

Damage dealt by this ammunition counts as magical. When you reduce a creature to 0 HP with an attack that uses this ammunition, the target can’t become Undead through any means and is immune to effects that would cause it to later rise as Undead (such as from a vampire’s Draining Bite or a wight’s Life Drain). Also, if an Undead is reduced to 0 HP by this ammunition, it can’t gain the benefits of the Undead Fortitude trait, and if a creature with the Outsider tag is reduced to 0 HP by this ammunition, it is permanently destroyed, even if it is slain on a plane other than its home plane.

## Arrow of Accuracy

*Optional, from Player's Guide 2.*

*Weapon (Arrow), Uncommon 100 gp + ammunition base cost*

When this arrow is used to make an attack, it grants advantage to your attack roll. Once it successfully hits a target, the arrow becomes nonmagical.

## Arrow of Ice-Trapping

*Optional, from Northlands Worldbook.*

*Weapon (Arrow), Rare 350 gp*

This arrow deals an additional 1d6 cold damage on a successful hit. If a creature takes damage from the ammunition, it must make a DC 15 STR save. On a failed save, the target becomes restrained in bands of ice. It can repeat its save at the end of each of its turns to end this effect on itself. While it is restrained, the target takes 1d6 cold damage at the start of each of its turns. The target or a creature within 5 ft. of it can take an action to make a DC 15 STR (Athletics) check, freeing the target on a success.

## Arrow of Multiplication

*Optional, from Player's Guide 2.*

*Weapon (Ammunition), Rare 500 gp + ammunition base cost*

When this ammunition is used to make an attack, it magically creates 1d6 duplicates of itself. Make an additional attack roll for each duplicate arrow and deal normal damage on each successful hit. Once it successfully hits a target, the ammunition becomes nonmagical.

## Arrow of Slaying

*Weapon (Arrow), Very Rare 650 gp*

An *[[#Arrow of Slaying|arrow of slaying]]* is a piece of magic ammunition effective against a particular kind of creature. If a creature belonging to the type associated with an *[[#Arrow of Slaying|arrow of slaying]]* takes damage from the arrow, the creature must make a DC 17 CON save, taking an extra 6d10 piercing damage on a failed save, or half as much extra damage on a successful one.

Once an *[[#Arrow of Slaying|arrow of slaying]]* deals its extra damage to a creature, it becomes a nonmagical arrow.

Ammunition of this type exists for other weapons, such as *bolts of slaying* meant for a crossbow, though arrows are most common.

## Ashbraid

*Optional, from Northlands Worldbook.*

*Weapon (battleaxe, greataxe), Very Rare (Requires attunement) 15,000 gp + weapon base cost*

This axe has a fire-blackened blade, and its haft is wrapped with strands of unbreakable golden hair. You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon. The weapon deals an extra 1d6 fire damage on a hit.

When you hit a creature with this magic weapon, the target has vulnerability to fire damage until the beginning of your next turn. If the target has resistance to fire damage, it loses its resistance until the beginning of your next turn, though it does not have vulnerability to fire damage. This has no effect on creatures with immunity to fire damage.

## Axe of Shredding

*Optional, from Player's Guide 2.*

*Weapon (Battleaxe), Varies Price Varies (Requires Attunement)*

You have a bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. The bonus is determined by the item’s rarity. Attuning to this weapon grants you proficiency with it and grants it the Finesse property. You can use the axe as a spellcasting focus for your spells.

This axe doubles as a magical lute, and you can simply will the axe’s strings into existence (no action required). Songs played on the axe are amplified and distorted, though still pleasing to hear. When you make a CHA (Performance) check while playing the axe, treat any d20 roll of 9 or lower as a 10.

If you score a critical hit with the axe, your music fills the target’s head, forcing it to make a CHA save against your spell save DC. On a failed save, the creature is so enthralled by the music that it drops anything it’s holding and becomes incapacitated until the start of your next turn. While incapacitated in this way, the only thing the creature can do is dance to the music.

## Balefire Bow

*Optional, from Night Hunters.*

*Weapon (Any Bow or Crossbow), Rare 4,400 gp + base weapon cost*

This bow is made from black wood that gives off a faint green glow in the moonlight. The weapon holds three charges, and when you hit with an attack using it, you can expend one of these charges, causing your strike to explode with dark fire and dealing an extra 2d6 fire damage plus 2d6 necrotic damage to the target. Each creature within 5 feet of that target must also succeed on a DC 15 DEX save or take 1d6 fire damage plus 1d6 necrotic damage.

The weapon regains all expended charges daily at dawn.

## Bell-Ringing Greatclub

*Optional, from Northlands Worldbook.*

*Weapon (greatclub), Uncommon 1,000 gp + 2 sp*

This greatclub is fashioned from the clapper of a giant’s bell. When you score a critical hit with this greatclub, you can choose for the target to be stunned until the start of your next turn instead of dealing the extra damage from the critical hit.

## Berserker Axe

*Weapon (Any Axe), Rare 5,000 gp (Requires Attunement)*

You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. In addition, while you are attuned to this weapon, your hit point maximum increases by 1 for each level you have attained.

***Curse.*** This axe is cursed, and becoming attuned to it extends the curse to you. As long as you remain cursed, you are unwilling to part with the axe, keeping it within reach at all times. You also have disadvantage on attack rolls with weapons other than this one, unless no foe is within 60 feet of you that you can see or hear.

When a hostile creature damages you while the axe is in your possession, you must succeed on a DC 15 WIS save or go berserk. While berserk, you must use your action each round to attack the creature nearest to you with the axe. If you can make extra attacks as part of the Attack action, you use those extra attacks, moving to attack the next nearest creature after you fell your current target. If you have multiple possible targets, you attack one at random. You are berserk until you start your turn with no creatures within 60 feet of you that you can see or hear.

## Blade of Insults

*Optional, from Northlands Worldbook.*

*Weapon (Any simple weapon that deals piercing or slashing damage), Rare 1,500 gp + weapon base cost (Requires attunement by a bard)*

A bard wielding this weapon is empowered to mix swordplay with witty repartee. If you deal damage with this weapon on your turn, you can use your bonus action to cast *[[Cantrips#Vicious Mockery|vicious mockery]]* on the same creature you damaged. If you wish, as part of that bonus action, you can also expend a use of your bardic inspiration, which otherwise functions as it normally would.

## Bloodlink Blade

*Optional, from Player's Guide 2.*

*Weapon (Any Sword), Rare 8,000 gp (Requires Attunement)*

You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. In addition, when you reduce a creature to 0 hit points with this weapon, you can use a bonus action to create a blood link with the target, and for the next hour, you deal an extra 2d6 slashing damage to other creatures of the same creature type. For example, if you reduce a troll to 0 HP and create a blood link with it, you would deal the extra damage to all creatures with the creature type Giant.

## Bow of Arrow Splitting

*Optional, from Player's Guide 2.*

*Weapon (Longbow or Shortbow), Very Rare 16,525 gp (Shortbow) or 24,250 gp (Longbow)*

*(Requires Attunement)*

When you use this weapon to make a ranged attack, you can use the command word to split your arrow. This allows you to target two separate creatures within 30 feet of each other with one shot. Make the attack roll with disadvantage and apply the result to both targets.

The bow has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. As an action, you can expend 1 charge along with an arrow to create a hail of arrows that rain down on a 30-foot radius, centered on a point within the bow’s normal range. (You can instead expend 2 charges to increase this to the bow’s long range.) Each creature in the area must make a DC 16 DEX save, taking 8d6 piercing damage (shortbow) or 8d8 piercing damage (longbow) on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. If this reduces one or more creatures to 0 HP, the bow regains 1 charge.

## Dagger of Darkness

*Optional, from Labyrinth Worldbook.*

*Weapon (Dagger), Uncommon 1,000 gp*

While holding this dagger, you can use a bonus action to speak the command word, gathering writhing darkness around your hand and sending a bolt of darkness at a creature you can see within 30 feet of you. Make a ranged spell attack using your INT modifier. If the target is in dim light or darkness, you have advantage on the attack roll. On a hit, the target takes 2d8 necrotic damage and is frightened until the end of its next turn. The dagger can’t be used this way again until the next dawn.

## Dagger of Venom

*Weapon (Dagger), Rare 8,000 gp*

You can use an action to cause thick, black poison to coat the blade. The poison remains for 1 minute or until an attack using this weapon hits a creature. ## Dancing Sword

*Weapon (Any Sword), Very Rare 30,000 gp (Requires Attunement)*

You can use a bonus action to toss this magic sword into the air and speak the command word. When you do so, the sword begins to hover, flies up to 30 feet, and attacks one creature of your choice within 5 feet of it. The sword uses your attack roll and ability score modifier to damage rolls.

While the sword hovers, you can use a bonus action to cause it to fly up to 30 feet to another spot within 30 feet of you. As part of the same bonus action, you can cause the sword to attack one creature within 5 feet of it.

After the hovering sword attacks for the fourth time, it flies up to 30 feet and tries to return to your hand. If you have no hand free, it falls to the ground at your feet. If the sword has no unobstructed path to you, it moves as close to you as it can and then falls to the ground. It also ceases to hover if you grasp it or move more than 30 feet away from it.

## Dart of Silence

*Optional, from Player's Guide 2.*

*Weapon (Dart), Uncommon 200 gp*

When this dart is used to make an attack, the target takes normal damage on a hit and is affected by the *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Silence|silence]]* spell (see *Player’s Guide*). The effect is limited to a 5-foot-radius sphere centered on (and moving with) the target. Maintaining the effect does not require concentration, and it lasts for 1 minute. If the attack misses, the dart’s magic is not expended.

Barbs make the dart difficult to remove after a hit. The target, or another creature within 5 feet of it, can use its action to attempt to remove the dart with a successful DC 15 STR (Athletics) check. The target of the original attack takes 1d4 slashing damage if it is removed. This doesn’t change the target or duration of the *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Silence|silence]]* spell (see *Player’s Guide*).

Once its magic is expended, the dart can be treated as a nonmagical dart, which can be left embedded in a target’s body as part of a successful attack.

## Defender

*Weapon (Any Sword), Legendary 100,000 gp (Requires Attunement)*

You gain a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

The first time you attack with this sword on each of your turns, you can transfer some or all of the sword’s bonus to your AC, instead of using the bonus on attacks that turn. For example, you could reduce the bonus to your attack and damage rolls to +1 and gain a +2 bonus to AC. The adjusted bonuses remain in effect until the start of your next turn, although you must hold the sword to gain a bonus to AC from it.

## Doombringer’s Blade

*Optional, from Labyrinth Worldbook.*

*Weapon (Any Axe, Halberd, Sword, or Dagger), Rare 16,000 gp (Requires Attunement)*

This magic weapon resembles a slice of pure black energy surrounded by a purple or blue nimbus. You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with it. When you deal damage with the weapon, you can choose for the damage to be necrotic instead of its normal damage type.

As an action, you can slice the weapon across the air or water in a space within 5 feet of you, creating a rift in the planar fabric of reality. The area within 20 feet of the rift is difficult terrain, as the rift pulls at those nearby. Each creature other than you that starts its turn within 10 feet of the rift must succeed on a DC 15 STR save or be pulled up to 10 feet toward the rift, touching it. A creature that touches the rift takes 2d12 necrotic damage. The rift remains until it has dealt 30 damage to creatures or until you dismiss it as a bonus action. The weapon can’t be used to create a rift again until the next dawn.

## Groundbound Ammunition

*Optional, from Player's Guide 2.*

*Weapon (Any Ammunition), Rarity Varies Price Varies*

When a flying creature is hit by an attack using this ammunition, it takes normal damage and must succeed on a WIS save (DC determined by rarity) or be forced to the ground. On a failed save, the creature must immediately use its reaction to move up to its full speed toward the ground. On its next turn (and on subsequent turns if necessary), if it is not already on the ground, it must use its movement to get as close to the ground as possible, though it can take other actions as normal while it descends. After it lands, the magic of the ammunition keeps the creature grounded until the end of your next turn.

Once the creature is on the ground, you have advantage on attack rolls against it when using this ammunition. If you hit the grounded creature with additional *groundbound* *ammunition*, the creature makes any subsequent saves against its effect with disadvantage.

## Oathbow

*Weapon (Longbow), Very Rare 16,000 gp (Requires Attunement)*

When you nock an arrow on this bow, it whispers in Elvish, “Swift defeat to my enemies.” When you use this weapon to make a ranged attack, you can, as a command phrase, say, “Swift death to you who have wronged me.” The target of your attack becomes your sworn enemy until it dies or until dawn seven days later. ## Sickle of Thorns

*Optional, from Tales of the Old Margreve.*

*Weapon (Sickle), Uncommon (Requires Attunement) 1,501 gp*

You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon. As an action, you can swing the sickle to cut nonmagical vegetation up to 60 feet away from you. Each cut is a separate action with one action equaling one swing of your arm. Thus, you can lead a party through a jungle or briar thicket at a normal pace, simply swinging the sickle back and forth ahead of you to clear the path. It can't be used to cut trunks of saplings larger than 1 inch in diameter. It also can't cut through unliving wood (such as a door or wall). When you hit a plant creature with a melee attack with this weapon, that target takes an extra 1d6 slashing damage.

This weapon can make very precise cuts, such as to cut fruit or flowers high up in a tree without damaging the tree.

## Spellbinder

*Optional, from Labyrinth Worldbook.*

*Weapon (Longsword), Artifact Priceless (Requires Attunement)*

This *+3 longsword* has a blade of mirror-polished steel, inlaid with geometric patterns filled with black gold. Its cross guard is stylized after of curled horns of a bull.

***Maze Master.*** When you attack a creature and roll a 20 on the attack roll, you can choose for the creature to be immediately affected by the *[[8th-Circle Spells#Maze|maze]]* spell. If you don’t want to use the *[[8th-Circle Spells#Maze|maze]]* spell or the creature couldn’t normally be affected by the spell, it instead takes 6d8 force damage in addition to *Spellbinder*’s standard weapon damage.

***Spells.*** The sword has 12 charges and regains 1d8 + 4 expended charges each dawn. You can use an action and expend 1 or more charges to cast one of the following spells from it (save DC 18): *[[4th-Circle Spells#Banishment|banishment]]* (4 charges), *[[4th-Circle Spells#Dimension Door|dimension door]]* (4 charges), *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Golden Bull|golden bull]]* (3 charges), *[[8th-Circle Spells#Maze|maze]]* (8 charges), or *[[5th-Circle Spells#Passwall|passwall]]* (5 charges).

***Sentience.*** *Spellbinder* is a sentient longsword with an INT of 14, a WIS of 12, and a CHA of 16. It has hearing and darkvision out to a range of 120 feet. The sword can speak, read, and understand all languages, and it can communicate with its wielder telepathically. Its voice is bright and clear.

***Personality.*** *Spellbinder* is continually troubled by a feeling that it has been made to forget something critically important. While the sword can’t remember the details of its creation or purpose, it’s certain that its history is somehow tied to the creation of the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]], and it believes the answers it seeks are hidden somewhere within the Great Maze. The sword encourages its wielder to learn all it can about the Labyrinth, preserve its passageways, and punish those who threaten its existence (such as the forces of the Void). It also has a deep love of minotaurs and any attempt a wielder makes to attack a minotaur results in automatic conflict.

***Destruction.*** *Spellbinder* can only be destroyed if it’s cast into the heart of the Great Maze, which reportedly lies at the so-far-undiscovered center of the Labyrinth.
