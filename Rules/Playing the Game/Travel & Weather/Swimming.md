Under normal circumstances, a character must succeed on a DC 10 CON save at the end of each hour they spend swimming, or they gain one level of exhaustion. A creature that has a swimming speed can swim all day without penalty and uses the forced march rules (see [[Adventuring]]). Due to its pressure and temperature, deep water is treated as difficult terrain for the purposes of tactical movement. A creature without a swimming speed treats each hour they spend swimming at a depth greater than 100 feet as 2 hours when determining exhaustion. A creature that spends 1 hour swimming at a depth greater than 200 feet is treated as though they had been swimming for 4 hours.
