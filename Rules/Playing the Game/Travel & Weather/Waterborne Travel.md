PCs can crew a boat or other waterborne vessel for the same amount of time they can travel overland without penalties. The tasks required to crew a water vessel include rowing, handling sails, rope work, cleaning decks, steering, making repairs, and keeping watch. If the crew of a sailing vessel works in shifts, a ship can sail all day and through the night.

While a waterborne vessel is in sight of land, there is no danger of getting lost. Once a ship is no longer in sight of the shore, its navigator must use the position of the sun and stars to determine relative latitude and must use dead reckoning to estimate longitude. Use the Ship Navigation table to determine whether the ship veers off course.
