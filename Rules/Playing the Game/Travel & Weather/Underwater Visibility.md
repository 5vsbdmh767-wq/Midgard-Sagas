The distance a creature can see underwater depends upon the clarity of the water and on the available light, which is also dependent on the depth of the water. Use the **Underwater Encounter Distance Table** to determine the distance at which swimming PCs become aware of potential encounters. For every 500 feet the PCs are below the water’s surface, decrease the light level by one step. 

The table assumes the PCs are encountering a creature of Medium size, so increase the encounter distance by 30 feet for each size category a creature is above Medium.

###### **Underwater Encounter Distance Table**

| Water Clarity | Light Level  | Encounter Distance (Ft) |
| ------------- | ------------ | ----------------------- |
| Clear Water   | Bright Light | 60                      |
| Clear Water   | Dim Light    | 30                      |
| Clear Water   | Darkness     | 10                      |
| Murky Water   | Bright Light | 30                      |
| Murky Water   | Dim Light    | 10                      |
| Murky Water   | Darkness     | 5                       |
