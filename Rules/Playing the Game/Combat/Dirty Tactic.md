Using the Attack action, you can make a special melee attack to in some way debilitate a target you can see within 5 feet of you, such as throwing sand in its face, knocking the wind from its lungs, tossing a coil of rope around its feet, or similar. Make a DEX (Sleight of Hand) check. The target must make a DEX save against a DC equal to the result of that check. On a failure, the target has disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn. At the GM’s discretion, this option can apply different hindering effects that last until the end of the target’s next turn, such as reducing the target’s walking speed by 10 feet or preventing the target from casting spells with verbal components.

##### **Enduring Effect.** 
If the target fails the save by 5 or more, the effect lasts for 2 rounds. On its turn, the target can take its action to correct the debilitating effect (clearing sand out of its eyes, detangling the rope around its legs, or similar), immediately ending the effect.

##### **Overplayed Hand.** 
If the target succeeds on the save by 5 or more, the target can use its reaction to make an opportunity attack against you, catching you off guard as you focus on debilitating it.
