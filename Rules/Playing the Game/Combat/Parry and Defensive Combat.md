PCs and their opponents sometimes find themselves in situations where they need to go on the defensive while waiting for healing or reinforcements. This optional rule explores methods creatures can use to defend themselves more effectively.

A PC acting defensively can use any one of the following options. A creature that isn’t a PC and that is acting defensively can use only the Focused Defense option.

##### **Parry.** 
If a creature wields a melee weapon with which it is proficient, it can use its reaction to interpose the weapon between itself and a melee attack aimed at it, gaining a +2 bonus to its AC against that attack. Moving its weapon in this way throws off its momentum and causes it to have disadvantage on the next attack roll it makes before the end of its next turn.

##### **Defensive Posture.** 
The creature positions itself in a way to allow it to block or dodge blows at the cost of its fighting prowess. It must decide to take this stance at the start of its turn. It gains a bonus to its AC equal to half its PB until the start of its next turn. In exchange, it doesn’t add its PB to its attacks (or it takes a penalty equal to half its PB if it isn’t proficient with the weapon). 

##### **Focused Defense**. 
The creature takes its action to make itself as hard to hit as possible. When it uses this action, attacks against it have disadvantage, and it takes half damage from weapon attacks until the start of its next turn. A creature can’t use reactions while using focused defense.
