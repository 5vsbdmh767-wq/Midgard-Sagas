At the GM’s discretion, you can include the following incremental successes and failures when shoving a creature.

##### **Mighty Shove.** 
If you win the contest by 5 or more, you can choose to push the target up to 10 feet away from you, or you can choose to push it 5 feet away from you and knock it prone.

##### **Off-Balance.** 
If you fail the contest by 5 or more, the target can choose to push you 5 feet away from it or knock you prone.
