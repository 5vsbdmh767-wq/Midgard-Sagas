##### 1. Captain Phase. 
Captains chooses [[shipboard action]]'s for the round, and they may roll to Push the Crew.

> [!info] Push the Crew
> During a time of need the Captain may decide to push the crew to their limits. If the Captain makes a successful Intimidation or Persuasion check, the ship gains one extra action point this turn but loses one action point next turn. A Captain cannot Push the Crew if they succeeded in doing so the previous round.

##### 2. Initiative Phase.
Characters and unique foes not concerned with running their ship should roll initiative as normal at the beginning of the encounter. Ships move last in initiative, and their station crews act on their ship’s turn in any order. However, determine initiative between competing vessels each round. Each round, to decide which ship acts first, determine which ship has the advantage of position by having each helmsman make a WIS (Water Vehicles) check. The winner chooses whether to act first or delay. If the helm is not controlled, that ship automatically fails the check.

##### 3. Player & NPC Phase. 
All player characters and NPC's, allies and enemies alike, whom are not tied up in a shipboard action commanded by their captain, can act on their turn normally in initiative.

##### 4. Ship's Phase
A ship may move a distance up to it's speed (must move at least one hex) and all shipboard actions declared by the captain during the captains phase are carried out. These actions can be taken before, after, or at any point along the ships movement. Characters may also ready actions (i.e wait for a target to be in range.) Any actions not carried out by other characters will be rolled by The Captain.
