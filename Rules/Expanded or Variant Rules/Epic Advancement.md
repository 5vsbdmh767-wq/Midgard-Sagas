%% source: Player's Guide 2 %%

Typical*ly*, PCs can’t advance beyond 20th level. However, if your party reaches 20th level and wants to continue game play, you can use the following optional rules to advance beyond this cap. With this system, you can gain up to 10 additional epic levels. These epic levels don’t progress individual character classes, but they still provide significant increases to power.

## Advancement After 20th Level

If your table chooses to use the optional epic advancement rules, PCs continue to gain XP and gain epic levels at the advancement rate listed in the **Epic Advancement** table.

### Elements of an Epic Level

This section breaks down the various components of an epic level and the special rules governing these levels.

#### Level

PCs start at 20th level and can gain epic levels up to 30th. GMs can continue using either of the standard methods for tracking level advancement: awarding XP or tracking milestones.

#### Proficiency Bonus

Your proficiency bonus (PB) continues to increase as you advance in epic levels, as shown in the **Epic Advancement** table. Remember, PB is still always based on your total character level, not your level in a class.

#### Epic-Level Features

When you gain a new epic level, you gain the feature listed for that level—either an Epic Improvement or an Epic- Level Boon.

##### Epic Improvement

The Epic Improvement feature works similarly to the standard Improvement class feature. However, if you choose an ability score increase, you can now raise a single ability score to a maximum of 30 (instead of 20). If you choose instead to gain a talent, you still select a new talent only from the lists you have access to via class features or abilities (as normal).

##### Epic-Level Boon

When you gain an Epic-Level Boon, choose one of the following benefits:

- Gain one epic-level boon from the choices granted by the 20th-level Epic Boon feature of your character class. If you have multiple classes, you can only select boons from the class in which you have the most levels (and if it’s an even split, you can choose).

- Gain one of the special epic-level boons presented in the **Epic-Level Boons** section of this chapter. These boons aren’t associated with any specific character class.

- Increase a single ability score by 1 (to a maximum of 30) and gain one heroic boon from the choices granted by the 10th-level Heroic Boon feature of your character class. If you have multiple classes, you can choose from the options provided by any of your classes.

## Epic-Level Boons

The following epic-level boons are available to any PC with at least one epic level, regardless of character class:

- **Epic Aim.** When you miss with an attack roll, you can choose to hit instead. You can use this feature a number of times equal to the number of epic levels you have, and you regain uses when you finish a long rest.

- **Epic Casting.** Choose one of the following magic sources: Arcane, Divine, Primordial, or Wyrd. When you cast a spell of 1st circle or higher using a spell slot of your chosen source, you can choose to roll a d10. If the number rolled is higher than the circle of the expended spell slot, you automatically recover that slot. Once you successfully recover a number of spell slots in this way, equal to the number of epic levels you have, you can’t use this feature again until you finish a long rest. You can select this boon multiple times, choosing a different source each time.

- **Epic Health.** Your hit point maximum increases by an amount equal to your PB. Recalculate this bonus whenever a new level increases your PB. Additionally, when you are reduced to 0 HP, you instead automatically regain a number of HP equal to the bonus HP granted by this feature. You can use this feature a number of times equal to the number of epic levels you have, and you regain uses when you finish a long rest.

- **Epic Luck.** The maximum Luck you can have at any one time increases by an amount equal to the number of epic levels you have. For example, at 22nd level, you can have a maximum of 7 Luck. This maximum continues to increase each time you gain an epic level after selecting this boon. Additionally, whenever you gain Luck, you gain an additional 1 Luck. This bonus stacks with any other features that grant additional Luck.

- **Epic Magic.** Whenever a creature succeeds on a save against a spell you cast with a spell slot of 1st circle or higher, you can force the target to reroll the save with disadvantage (potentially turning success into failure). If the target still succeeds, you recover the spell slot used to cast the triggering spell. You can use this feature a number of times equal to the number of epic levels you have, but you can only use this feature to affect the same save once. You regain all uses of this feature when you finish a long rest.

- **Epic Power.** Whenever you cast a spell that deals damage, you can roll a number of additional damage dice equal to the total number of epic levels you have. The type of this additional damage and size of the damage die matches the type and size used by the spell.

- **Epic Saves.** Choose one type of save: STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, or CHA. You gain proficiency in that save. Additionally, you now double your PB when calculating the result of saves you are proficient in. You can select this boon multiple times, choosing a different save each time.

- **Epic Senses.** You gain keensense out to a range of 30 feet and truesight out to a range of 60 feet. Your truesight functions out to a range of 30 feet even if you are blinded or otherwise unable to perceive via sight (as long as your keensense is still active).

- **Epic Skills.** You gain proficiency in all skills. Additionally, choose a number of skills equal to the number of epic levels you have, and double your PB for any ability check you make that uses any of those chosen skills. Whenever you gain a new epic level after selecting this boon, choose one additional skill to receive this benefit.

- **Epic Strikes.** When you make an attack with advantage on your turn and successfully hit, you automatically deal maximum damage with that attack. You can use this feature a number of times equal to the number of epic levels you have, and you regain uses when you finish a long rest.

- **Epic Talent.** Choose one type of talent list: Magic, Martial, or Technical. Whenever you gain a new talent, you can choose a talent from either the chosen list or the talent lists you can access via your class features. Immediately, gain a talent of your choice from the chosen list when you gain this Epic-Level Boon. You can select this boon multiple times, choosing a different talent list each time.
