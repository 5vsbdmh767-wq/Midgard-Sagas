A retreating creature might want to take a final snipe at enemies as it retreats from the battlefield. You can use this optional rule to allow PCs (and their enemies) to take parting shots as forces separate.

A creature taking the Disengage action can use a bonus action to make one ranged attack at a creature it can see. Because of this attack’shaphazardness, the weapon’s normal range is reduced by 20 feet, and its long range is reduced by 50 feet. A creature with the Ranged Weapon Mastery martial talent ignores these range reductions. At the GM’s discretion, similar talents or features that enhance a creature’s aptitude with ranged weapons, such as the robot drone’s Point-Blank Shots trait, also allow the creature to ignore these range reductions. 

A creature that is the target of a parting shot can use its reaction to make one ranged attack against the creature taking the parting shot.
