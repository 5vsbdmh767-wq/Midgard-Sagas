Using the Attack action, you can make a special melee attack to push through the space of a hostile creature you can see within 5 feet of you. To use this option, you must have at least enough movement remaining to fully move through the target’s space, and there must be at least one unoccupied space that will fit you on the other side of the target. Make a STR (Athletics) check contested by the target’s STR (Athletics) or DEX (Acrobatics) check (the target’s choice). You have advantage on this check if you are larger than the target; you have disadvantage if you are smaller than the target. If you win the contest, you move through the target’s space to an unoccupied space of your choice on the other side of it and within 5 feet of it, expending the appropriate movement in the process. 

##### **Out of the Way.** 
If you win the contest by 5 or more, you can choose to push the target 5 feet in the direction of your choice.

##### **Brick Wall.** 
If you fail the contest by 5 or more, the target can choose to knock you prone.
