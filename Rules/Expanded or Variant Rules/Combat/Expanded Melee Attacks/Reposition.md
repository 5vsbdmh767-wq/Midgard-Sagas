Using the Attack action, you can make a special melee attack to force a creature you can see within 5 feet of you to move to another location. Make a STR (Athletics) check contested by the target’s STR (Athletics) or DEX (Acrobatics) check (the target’s choice). If you win the contest, you move the target to a different, unoccupied space within your reach. The new space can’t be hazardous for the target, such as a space containing a pit or lava for a creature not immune to fire.

##### **Dangerous Reposition.** 
If you win the contest by 5 or more, you can move the target into a space that is hazardous for it.

##### **Momentum Shift.** 
If you fail the contest by 5 or more, the target can force you to move 5 feet to a different space within its reach. This space can be hazardous to you.
