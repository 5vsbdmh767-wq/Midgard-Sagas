Using the Attack action, you can make a special melee attack to wrest an item from a creature you can see within 5 feet of you. The target item must be an object that can be held in one hand, weighs no more than 10 pounds, and isn’t wrapped around or firmly attached to the target, such as a belt, shirt, or armor. The item can’t be a weapon wielded by the creature, but it can be a weapon hanging in a sheath from the creature’s belt or similar. For example, you can attempt to steal a creature’s belt pouch or quiver of arrows but not its boots or the bow it wields. Make a DEX (Sleight of Hand) check. The creature in possession of the item must make a DEX save against a DC equal to the result of that check. The creature has advantage on the save if you are attempting to take an item that is fastened to the creature, such as a cloak with clasps or a belt pouch. If the creature fails the save, you wrest the target item from it, and the creature is aware of the theft.

##### **Undetected**. 
If the creature in possession of the item fails the save by 5 or more, it doesn’t notice the item is missing.

##### **Tunnel Vision.** 
If the creature in possession of the item succeeds on the save by 5 or more, it can use its reaction to make an opportunity attack against you, taking advantage of your focus on the item.
