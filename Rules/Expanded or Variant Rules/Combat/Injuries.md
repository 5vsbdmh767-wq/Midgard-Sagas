Damage typically has no other effect than a reduction in hit points. This optional rule adds the possibility for damage to also cause unique injuries. A creature might sustain an injury for one of the following reasons:

• Failing a death save by 5 or more.

• Failing a save against a damaging effect by 5 or more.

• Hit by a called shot (see [[Called Shot]]).

• Reduced to 0 HP but not killed from the damage.

• Suffering a critical hit.

• Taking massive damage.

When a creature sustains an injury, roll 1d20 and consult the Injury table or select the most appropriate injury. The table covers many common physiological aspects of living creatures, but it might require some adjustment to be suitable for creatures with atypical physiologies. 

|  d20  | Injury                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| :---: | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  1-2  | **Internal Organ.** An injury to a vital organ makes it difficult to fully engage in combat. Whenever the injured creature attempts an action in combat, it must succeed on a DC 15 CON save or lose its action, and it can’t use reactions until the start of its next turn. Magical healing or spending 10 days doing nothing but resting removes this injury.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
|  3-5  | **Broken Ribs.** This injury works exactly like an internal organ injury, except the save DC is 10 and the recovery time is only five days of activity that isn’t strenuous, such as reading, sewing, minor gardening, or similar                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
|   6   | **Throat.** The injured creature can’t speak, making it impossible to cast spells with verbal components or use magic items requiring command words. Magical healing or spending 10 days without attempting to speak removes this injury.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
|   7   | **Eye*.** The injured creature has disadvantage on WIS (Perception) checks that rely on sight and on ranged attack rolls. A creature with all its eyes injured becomes blinded. Magic such as the regenerate spell restores the lost eye(s).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
|   8   | **Destroy Arm/Hand*.** The injured creature has one fewer hand available to hold objects (most humanoids can no longer hold anything requiring two hands). A creature reduced to no hands can no longer hold anything. Magic such as the regenerate spell restores the lost appendage(s).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 9-10  | **Contused Arm Bone.** When making attacks requiring two hands that involve the contused arm, the injured creature has disadvantage. A creature with all its arms contused has disadvantage on all weapon attack rolls. Magical healing removes this injury                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
|  11   | **Destroy Leg/Foot*.** The injured creature has one fewer foot available, and its walking speed is halved (if it has two feet) or reduced by 5 feet (if it has more than half its total walking appendages remaining). If the creature takes the Dash action, it must succeed on a DC 15 DEX save or fall prone. It also has disadvantage on DEX checks made to maintain balance. A creature with no legs/feet and no means to offset the missing appendages has a walking speed of 0. Magic such as the regenerate spell restores the lost appendage(s).                                                                                 |
| 12-13 | **Contused Leg Bone.** The injured creature’s walking speed is reduced by 5 feet. If the creature takes the Dash action, it must succeed on a DC 10 DEX save or fall prone. Multiple contusions increase the DEX save DC by 1 (to a maximum of 15). Magical healing removes this injury.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| 14-15 | **Infection.** The wound becomes septic, and until this injury is removed, the creature can’t regain hit points except by magical means. The injured creature’s hit point maximum is reduced by 1 for every 24 hours the wound persists. If its hit point maximum is reduced to 0, it dies. The infection is removed if the injured creature receives magical healing. Without magical healing, a creature can tend to the wound and make a DC 15 WIS (Medicine) check every 24 hours, preventing the hit point maximum reduction for that day on a success. After five successes, the wound is no longer infected, removing this injury. |
| 16-17 | **Disfigurement**. The injury has created a large wound that the creature can’t easily hide. The injured creature has disadvantage on CHA (Persuasion) checks, but it has advantage on CHA (Intimidation) checks. Magic such as the regenerate spell removes this injury                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| 18-20 | **Scar.** This minor cosmetic wound has little effect on the creature. At the GM’s discretion, the injured creature gains a bonus of up to +2 on CHA (Intimidation) checks or suffers a penalty of up to –2 on CHA (Persuasion) checks when interacting with other creatures, depending on such a creature’s reaction to scars and other obvious battle wounds. Magic such as the regenerate spell removes this injury.                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
