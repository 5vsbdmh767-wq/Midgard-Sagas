*Optional, from Night Hunters.*

Horror adventures differ in many ways from those of fantasy, especially from the high fantasy that so many RPGs are based in. The often-defining emotions of fear and helplessness in horror gaming can easily clash in more heroic adventures, potentially causing confusion at the table with players accustomed to saving the day.

One way to encourage players to lean into the elements of horror is to use the shadow tokens system, which rewards them for making those choices that align with horror storytelling and that are potentially detrimental to their PCs. Players can release themselves from the pressure of making the “right” choice for optimal heroic gameplay and instead focus on narratively interesting choices that provide opportunities to delve into the more compelling aspects of horror and drive the story forward.

If your gaming table decides to use shadow tokens, the GM should share these rules with everyone.

## What Are Shadow Tokens?

Shadow tokens are a currency players can earn and spend throughout the course of each gaming session. At the start of a session, the GM places a pool of tokens in the center of the table, which serves as a reminder that they are available. When a player then earns shadow tokens (see **Earning Shadow Tokens**), they draw from the pool to hold until ready. After spending 1 or more tokens, they then return the spent tokens to the pile. Each player can only ever have a total of 3 tokens at a time, and they must return unspent tokens to the pile at the end of each session.

## Earning Shadow Tokens

A PC can earn shadow tokens in a number of ways:

- Choosing to fail a roll when confronted with an established source of fear (specific to PC). PC earns 1 token.
- Giving themself disadvantage on a roll due to the corrupting influence of evil. PC earns 1 token.
- Indulging in impulsive, regressive, or self-destructive behavior when presented with triggers of past trauma. PC earns 1 token.

The GM can and should also award tokens when players facilitate the threads of horror running through the narrative in ways not listed here.

## Spending Shadow Tokens

A PC can spend shadow tokens in a number of ways:

- Deal an additional die of damage (same type as triggering attack) after successful attack roll. PC spends 1 token.
- Force the GM to reroll any one ability check, attack roll, or save, keeping the new result. PC spends 3 tokens.
- Reroll a hit die spent at the end of a short rest, keeping the higher result. PC spends 1 token.
- Reroll any one ability check, attack roll, or save, keeping the new result. PC spends 1 token.

## Dread Villains and Shadow Tokens

As archetypes of horror, the dread villains of *Night Hunters* each have their own shadow token mechanics specific to their narrative and associated tropes. These provide additional ways for players to earn and spend tokens. Your GM will tell you about them when they come into play.
