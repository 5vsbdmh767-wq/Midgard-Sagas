%% source: Night Hunters %%

After witnessing terrors beyond mortal comprehension, a PC might experience dread. This mechanic goes beyond standard fear, instead blasting and scarring the mind—and typically requiring magical or medical intervention to remove. Heroic PCs aren’t generally burdened by these additional mental challenges, despite the relentless horrors they face or the carnage they participate in day after day, but sometimes, the accumulated anguish and stress of adventuring can weigh heavily. In a campaign with strong horror themes, incorporating dread can help emphasize a PC’s dangerous reality. (See [[Life and Death#Dread|Dread]] for the core rule, and the *Game Master’s Guide* for more on dread.)

## Accruing Dread

Various diseases, magical effects, and supernatural hazards can create dread within an otherwise regulated mind. Some powerful magic items can also wreck the psyche of creatures who use or become attuned to them. Resisting dread typically requires a WIS or CHA save, but a given effect will provide any necessary details for avoiding or overcoming it.

### Optional Rule: Dread Exposure

Certain creatures, hazards, and similar supernatural effects within the game specifically prompt interactions with the dread rules. However, horror adventures and campaigns often warrant additional opportunities to introduce dread. If your gaming table agrees, you can use the following suggestions to expand the use of dread in your game.

The **Dread Exposure** table presents common sources of dread beyond those specifically prompted elsewhere in the game. When exposed to a source of dread, a target must make a WIS or CHA save (determined by GM) to avoid it. Recommended DC thresholds and effect severities for failed saves are detailed in the table.

**Dread Exposure**

| Target . . . | Save DC | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| Attunes to or uses an item favored by a dread villain (GM’s discretion). | 15 | Long-Term Dread |
| Encounters a dread villain or a high-ranking cohort for the first time (GM’s discretion, but generally not repeated for different creatures of the same type). | 10 + Creature’s CHA Modifier | Short-Term Dread |
| Fails a save against a curse, disease, or hazard (see [[Curses, Diseases, and Hazards]]). | Same as Triggering Save | Short-Term Dread |
| Finishes a short or long rest within a dread villain’s lair (without magical protection). | 20 | Indefinite Dread |
| Finishes a short or long rest within a dread villain’s regional effect radius (without magical protection). | 10 | Long-Term Dread |
| Uses a *spell scroll* or similar item to cast a Divine or Wyrd spell beyond the circle of their highest available spell slot. | 10 + Spell Circle | Short-Term Dread |
| Witnesses a cult ritual, reads from a tome penned by creatures aligned with the dark, or otherwise learns forbidden or profane lore. | 12 | Long-Term Dread |

## Curing Dread

A *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Calm Emotions|calm emotions]]* spell or similar magic suppresses the effects of dread for the duration, and a *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Restoration|restoration]]* spell or similar magic removes a short-term or long-term dread. Depending on the source of the dread, a *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Remove Curse|remove curse]]* spell or similar magic might also prove effective. A *[[5th-Circle Spells#Greater Restoration|greater restoration]]* spell or similar magic is required to remove an indefinite dread.

## Effects of Dread

Dread can be short-term, long-term, or indefinite. Simple horrors typically impose short-term dread, which last for just a few minutes. More horrific encounters or cumulative exposure can result in long-term or indefinite dread:

- A PC with a short-term dread is subjected to an effect from the **Short-Term Dread** table for 1d10 minutes.
- A PC with a long-term dread is subjected to an effect from the **Long-Term Dread** table for 1d10 × 10 hours.
- A PC with an indefinite dread gains a roleplaying quirk from the **Indefinite Dread** table that lasts until removed.

**Short-Term Dread**

| d10 | Effect on Target (Lasts 1d10 Minutes) |
|---|---|
| 1 | *Distracting Thirst.* Until you use one or more actions to consume at least 1 gallon of liquid (approximately two waterskins’ worth), you can’t make attacks or cast spells. |
| 2 | *Drained.* You gain one level of exhaustion. At the end of your turn, if you are within 30 feet of the source of your dread, you must succeed on a CON save or gain an additional level of exhaustion (to a maximum of three total levels from this dread): the DC is 13, or GM’s discretion, based on effect severity for the situation. |
| 3 | *Floored.* You immediately fall prone and can’t stand up, but you can crawl and take other actions as normal. |
| 4 | *Hallucinations.* You must use your action each turn to attack an imaginary enemy that’s always within 5 feet. |
| 5 | *Overwhelmed Senses.* Beyond a 10-foot radius, you are effectively blinded and deafened. |
| 6 | *Seeing Red.* You must use your action each turn to make a spell/weapon attack against a hostile creature. In the absence of an eligible target, you spend each turn attacking nearby objects and structures. |
| 7 | *Seizing Up.* You have disadvantage on STR and DEX checks. |
| 8 | *Shaky Grip.* You drop any held items and are unable to pick anything up or effectively manipulate anything with your hands. |
| 9 | *Spiraling Dread.* You have been deeply affected and are not reacting well. You immediately gain a long-term dread: roll on the **Long-Term Dread** table or choose something appropriate. |
| 10 | *Uncontrolled Screaming.* Your constant screaming attracts the attention of all creatures within 300 feet that can hear it. |

**Long-Term Dread**

| d10 | Effect on Target (Lasts 1d10 × 10 Hours) |
|---|---|
| 1 | *Aversion.* If a spellcaster, you lose the ability to cast one spell you know or have prepared (GM’s choice or random), and a prepared spell lost in this fashion can’t be replaced by another spell for the duration. If not a spellcaster, you lose proficiency with either your preferred weapon or the last weapon you wielded (GM’s choice). |
| 2 | *Disconnected.* You are unable to attune to magic items and immediately lose any existing attunement. |
| 3 | *Feeling Trapped.* You feel strangled by armor or heavy clothing and have disadvantage on all checks while wearing any form of armor (except shields). This disadvantage is also imposed by any spells or magic items modifying your AC, such as *[[1st-Circle Spells#Mage Armor\|mage armor]]* or a *ring of protection*. |
| 4 | *Fixated.* You become obsessed with the source of your dread, either unwilling to move away from it or ceaselessly seeking it out. If the source is a creature, you are charmed by it (for the duration or until it damages you). If the source is an object, you spend your movement and actions attempting to possess it. |
| 5 | *Lasting Scar.* Roll for a different long-term dread: you gain that dread plus one indefinite dread. (Roll on the **Indefinite Dread** table or choose something appropriate.) |
| 6 | *Nervous Noise.* You hum, whistle, or otherwise make similar repetitive noises when under pressure, making you automatically fail any check made to hide, sneak, or otherwise escape notice. Creatures within 60 feet of you can’t be surprised. |
| 7 | *On Edge.* You can’t be surprised while conscious and have disadvantage on any save made to avoid being frightened or gaining dread. |
| 8 | *Restless.* You are unable to spend hit dice or recover hit points via short or long rests. |
| 9 | *Suggestible.* You have advantage on saves to avoid being frightened, and you have disadvantage on saves to avoid being charmed or gaining dread. |
| 10 | *Unfocused.* You have disadvantage on any CON saves to maintain concentration. In addition, if a spell requiring your concentration is active at the end of your turn (including the turn you cast it), you must make a DC 10 CON save to maintain your concentration. If you also take damage that round, make just one save but use the higher DC. (Roll for a different long-term dread if not a spellcaster or choose something appropriate.) |

**Indefinite Dread**

| d12 | Effect on Target (Lasts Until Removed) |
|---|---|
| 1 | I am haunted by someone I once wronged. |
| 2 | I am the chosen emissary of a being from beyond. |
| 3 | I am the weakest link and must become more powerful, or my companions will leave me behind. |
| 4 | I cannot be killed. |
| 5 | I grow weaker each day as my vitality is siphoned away by an incurable magical affliction. |
| 6 | I must determine which of my allies has been replaced by a monster. |
| 7 | I must regularly consume the blood of other creatures, or I’ll die. |
| 8 | I’m hunted by something that will devour me if I’m ever in complete darkness. |
| 9 | My reflection is trying to kill me. |
| 10 | Something terrible will happen if I enter a church, temple, or other holy place. |
| 11 | When I look at people, I see their true faces—the monstrous ones they try to hide. |
| 12 | When I touch someone, I experience horrific visions of the ways they might die. |
