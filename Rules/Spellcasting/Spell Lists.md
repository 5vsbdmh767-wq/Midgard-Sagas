## Wyrd Rituals

### 1st-Circle
- *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Dreamspan|Dreamspan]]* (Evocation): You form a shimmering bridge or ladder from the ephemeral whisps of dreams.
- *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Find Familiar|Find Familiar]]* (Conjuration): You bind a supernatural creature to serve you.
- *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Illusory Script|Illusory Script]]* (Illusion): You scribe an illusion of false, hidden, or indiscernible script.

- *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Unseen Servant|Unseen Servant]]* (Conjuration): You conjure an invisible servant to perform simple tasks for you.
- *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Warding Charm|Warding Charm]]* (Abjuration): You imbue an item with defensive magic.

### 2nd-Circle
- *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Alter Room|Alter Room]]* (Illusion): You make a room appear like a different environment.
- *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Augury|Augury]]* (Divination): You receive an omen about the potential outcome of your plans.
- *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Beneficent Effigy|Beneficent Effigy]]* (Necromancy): You create a model of a creature, which suffers harm in the creature’s stead.
- *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Enhance Familiar|Enhance Familiar]]* (Transmutation): You make your familiar more powerful.
- *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Font of Mucus|Font of Mucus]]* (Necromancy): You curse a humanoid with a gross, annoying cold.
- *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Locate|Locate]]* (Divination): You discern the direction and distance of desired objects or creatures.
- *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Magic Mouth|Magic Mouth]]* (Illusion): You enchant an object to deliver a short message when the time is right.
- *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Maleficent Effigy|Maleficent Effigy]]* (Necromancy): You create a model of a creature, using it to inflict discomfort and pain.
- *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Portal Jaunt|Portal Jaunt]]* (Conjuration): You create a magical portal between two doors.
- *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Read Object|Read Object]]* (Divination): You gain information from handling an object.
- *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Shadow Board|Shadow Board]]* (Divination): Local spirits are compelled to spell out answers to your questions.
- *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Spirit Sensing|Spirit Sensing]]* (Divination): You attune to the spirit world.
- *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Veteran Spirit|Veteran Spirit]]* (Necromancy): You conjure the spirit of an expert to help you.

### 3rd-Circle
- *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Clairvoyance|Clairvoyance]]* (Divination): You conjure a small, invisible sphere through which you can spy and sense nearby locations.
- *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Curse Shuffle|Curse Shuffle]]* (Transmutation): You channel the magical energies of the fey to alter a cursed object.
- *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Dream Step|Dream Step]]* (Illusion): You become an invisible observer in another creature’s dream.
- *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Dreamward|Dreamward]]* (Abjuration): You craft a ward, shielding everyone within it from harmful magics targeting their dreams.
- *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Magic Circle|Magic Circle]]* (Abjuration): You circle a small location with warding magic and arcane runes, barring access for certain foes.
- *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Phantom Steed|Phantom Steed]]* (Illusion): You conjure a ghostly mount that can run at incredible speed for a short time.
- *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Speak with Dead|Speak with Dead]]* (Necromancy): You bestow sentience on a corpse long enough to ask it five questions.
- *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Spirit Lure|Spirit Lure]]* (Conjuration): A lure draws in creatures of a specific type.

- *[[Rituals 4th–6th Circle#Ancestral Whispers|Ancestral Whispers]]* (Divination): You draw upon the collective dreams of your ancestors to temporarily gain a pair of proficiencies.
- *[[Rituals 4th–6th Circle#Dread Echoes|Dread Echoes]]* (Abjuration): You pull a target’s dread into yourself.
- *[[Rituals 4th–6th Circle#Fog of Sleep|Fog of Sleep]]* (Conjuration): A fog spreads across the land, which puts creatures to sleep.

- *[[Rituals 4th–6th Circle#Personal Time Fold|Personal Time Fold]]* (Transmutation): You create a pocket of space and time to pass an hour, while outsiders see you disappear for mere moments.
- *[[Rituals 4th–6th Circle#Phantom Chariot|Phantom Chariot]]* (Illusion): You summon a spectral chariot that can transport a small group.
- *[[Rituals 4th–6th Circle#Secret Chest|Secret Chest]]* (Abjuration): You conceal a chest on the Ethereal Plane, which you can access and send back as needed.

- *[[Rituals 4th–6th Circle#Contact Other Plane|Contact Other Plane]]* (Divination): You contact a being from another plane to answer questions.
