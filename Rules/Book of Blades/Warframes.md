*Optional, from Book of Blades.*

Warframes are magically infused sets of armor that a creature can strap itself into or arcanepunk, gem-powered vehicles that allow a creature to wield a higher level of arcane firepower while enjoying the protection of the warframe’s bulk. Warframes are considered vehicles, but some of them are further classified as “suits.”

A warframe suit is activated by the vital life force of the wearer within them. When a suit pilot has fewer than half of their maximum hit points, the warframe powers down and ejects the pilot into an open space 5 feet away.

More traditional vehicle warframes are arcanepunk devices, powered by gems that collect and focus magical energy. These devices often have weak points due to their larger frame that can be exploited by clever foes.

## Using Warframes

Generally, warframes are designed to be crewed by Small or Medium Humanoids. Warframe suits are significantly more cumbersome to get into than heavy armor, requiring 20 minutes to don. Once inside, however, the user can eject themselves from the armor as an action. Warframe vehicles require 20 minutes and a full complement of crew to prepare them for use.

Like vehicles, all warframes have their own set count for when they act in initiative, and they lose ties to non‑warframes. Similarly, warframes can’t move independent of crew. Unlike vehicles, a warframe suit can move as nimbly as the creature who pilots it. A warframe suit does not move automatically on its turn and can change direction or stop freely during its turn, according to its crew’s wishes. Fine motor actions such as making a DEX (Sleight of Hand) check or DEX (Stealth) checks might be limited or have an increased DC in a warframe suit, at the GM’s discretion. However, any action available to the pilot outside the suit is generally available while crewing a warframe suit, including casting spells and using class features.

## Warframe Repairs

Damaged warframes can be field repaired in the same way that normal vehicles are repaired (see **Vehicle Repairs** in **Chapter 5** of *Player’s Guide*). However, the gems that power a warframe vehicle are a special exception. If a warframe vehicle is operable, but has taken damage and has fewer than half its hit points remaining, at least one of its powering gems is cracked. Every 30 HP the warframe vehicle takes beyond half cracks an additional gem. The cost of each replacement gem is 100 gp × the number of crew, in addition to the necessary access to spare parts worth a gold-piece amount equal to 10 percent of the warframe’s base cost.

## Warframe Costs

A warframe’s cost is highly dependent on how unique it is to a setting. In some settings, warframes are unique creations made by a singular mind, and in this case, each one is priceless. In a more industrialized setting, they might serve as siege engines: rare but replaceable to a well-funded state or faction. The **Warframe Base Cost** table assumes warframes are very expensive siege engines in commonality and akin to magic items in cost. A GM might increase the price for any number of reasons.

## Warframe Proficiency?

Arcanepunk is a polarizing concept for heroic fantasy campaigns. Warframes don’t belong in many settings, and settings in which they do appear often revolve around them. In the latter case, the GM might consider adding a vehicle proficiency type to the standard air, land, and water trio. A warframe proficiency separates those who are trained to crew a warframe and represents those who crew or pilot one as having superior skill compared to untrained crew.

A wise GM treads carefully when introducing new proficiencies. The game is already tuned to a specific, limited set of proficiencies for skills, tools, and vehicles. Instead of going through every lineage, heritage, and background and thinking about which ones merit the addition of a Warframe Vehicle proficiency, consider adding it to the list of vehicle types for any character option that already offers a vehicle proficiency, and then adding it to the list of proficiency options for explicitly martial characters such as fighters and paladins. A PC who doesn’t have access to the proficiency can learn it during downtime from a martial mentor (see **Martial Mentors** in **Chapter 2**).

## Elements Of A Warframe

Warframes come in various sizes, capabilities, and power levels. They use the standard elements of vehicles in their stat blocks:

***Size.*** Warframe suits range from Medium to Large and act as a suit of powered armor the character wears. Warframe vehicles range from Large to Gargantuan and act like a mix of siege weaponry and transport.

***Type.*** Warframes use the same terrain types that vehicles use: air, land, and water. Burrowing is a land type. Submersibles are water types. However, a warframe might have multiple types, depending on its construction and intent.

***Armor Class and Damage Threshold.*** Warframes have an armor class and a damage threshold. If a warframe is hit by a single instance of damage that fails to meet its damage threshold, it instead takes no damage.

***Hit Points.*** A warframe is destroyed when it reaches 0 HP and is beyond nonmagical repair.

***Speed.*** This covers the various speeds and travel capabilities of the warframe, in feet and miles per hour, and miles per day for its primary mode of travel. Warframe suits often have additional speeds beyond walking, but these are not useable over long distances (at the GM’s discretion).

***Vulnerabilities, Resistances, and Immunities.*** Warframes have resistances, immunities, and vulnerabilities to certain damage types or conditions. These do not automatically convey to the crew. Like most items, warframes are immune to poison and psychic damage, and the following conditions: blinded, charmed, deafened, exhaustion, frightened, incapacitated, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, prone, stunned, and unconscious. Instead of writing all those immunities out in each stat block, a warframe that fits this standard has Vehicle Resilience in the Immune line of its stat block.

***Initiative.*** A warframe has a set count for when it acts in initiative and loses ties with non-vehicles or non‑warframes. In case of ties between warframes, the GM determines their initiative order. For more on initiative rules, see the **Initiative** section in **Chapter 6** of *Player’s Guide*.

***Crew.*** The crew represents the number of creatures a warframe requires to operate. If the warframe has fewer than this number, it is inoperable.

***Passengers.*** Since warframes are made for combat, they often have little or no capacity to safely or comfortably transport passengers.

***Cargo Capacity.*** Cargo capacity refers to the carrying capacity of non-essential goods and materials. This capacity is measured in pounds or tons; however, a GM can rule the nature of a vehicle prevents it from carrying certain kinds of cargo. As with passengers, warframes are purpose‑built for combat, so they seldom have much capacity for non‑essential cargo.

***Actions.*** Warframes often offer their crew powerful actions to take. Unlike vehicles, warframe suits allow a pilot to use its normal actions freely and they do not move automatically on their turns.

> [!tip] Balancing Warframes
> Not only do warframes bring elements of arcanepunk into your game, they fulfill the specific power fantasy of becoming an unstoppable juggernaut. The fantasy of suiting up in an armored exoskeleton or climbing into the hatch of a magical tank feeds a delightful feeling of invulnerability while cruising over any opposition. However, this power fantasy can clash with the goal of delivering tense challenge in combat. How does a GM challenge players with this kind of power? Consider the following options for dealing with the power level of warframes in your game:
>
> - ***Powerful, But Expensive.*** Make the warframe expensive to power, requiring either a wealth of arcane gems or unique magic items to fuel for a short time. This turns the warframe into a potent but limited resource at the PCs' disposal. They must plan when and how roughly to use it.
> - ***Temperamental Machines.*** Rather than present warframes as sleek, perfect technology, lean into the steampunk flavor and have the warframe frequently malfunctioning. Each time a creature takes an action while in a warframe armored suit, or each time a creature takes an action using a warframe's trait, roll a d20. On a result of 5 or less, the device suffers a random malfunction. See the **Clockwork Malfunctions** table in **Clockwork Arcanepunk Devices** in **Chapter 3** for potential problems.
> - ***Limited Time Only.*** As with other large-scale resources like airships or legendary artifacts, the GM must decide if the warframe is making life too easy for the PCs. Perhaps the technique of crafting warframe power cores has been lost to time, and when the core runs out of charges, the warframe becomes unusable. Or perhaps a villain steals the warframe with the plan of mass-producing their own version of it.

Bandits and warlords use this warframe vehicle for sieges and raids. The vehicle’s creation starts with a simple large wagon, then heavy modifications add armor plating, weaponry, and a powerful, spinning saw blade. Traditionally, horses would pull such a vehicle, but these wagons are outfitted with arcane gems on their wheels for propulsion.
