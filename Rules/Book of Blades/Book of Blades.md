> [!note] Optional Content
> Everything from *Book of Blades* is optional. Ask your GM before taking any of it for your character. Throughout the rules pages, material from this book is marked: *Optional, from Book of Blades.*

*Book of Blades* is Kobold Press's book for warriors: people who fight with weapons, fists, armor, and tactics, along with the gear, mounts, and machines they use.

## Character Options
- **Subclasses:**
  - [[Barbarian#Frostborn Raider|Frostborn Raider]] and [[Barbarian#Undying Warrior|Undying Warrior]] barbarians
  - [[Fighter#Iron Bastion|Iron Bastion]] and [[Fighter#Skirmisher|Skirmisher]] fighters
  - [[Mechanist#Clockmaker|Clockmaker]] and [[Mechanist#Fleshsmith|Fleshsmith]] mechanists
  - [[Monk#Equine Soul|Equine Soul]] and [[Monk#Righteous Fist|Righteous Fist]] monks
  - [[Paladin#Ashen Base|Ashen Base]] and [[Paladin#Anamnesis|Anamnesis]] paladins
  - [[Ranger#Peerless Scout|Peerless Scout]] and [[Ranger#Windsworn|Windsworn]] rangers

- **Heritages:** [[Heritages#Horse Lord Heritage|horse lord]], [[Heritages#Martial Law Heritage|martial law]], [[Heritages#Privileged Heritage|privileged]]
- **Backgrounds:** [[Backgrounds#Ascetic|Ascetic]], [[Backgrounds#Avatar|Avatar]], [[Backgrounds#Battle Academician|Battle Academician]], [[Backgrounds#Conscript|Conscript]], [[Backgrounds#Grave Keeper|Grave Keeper]], [[Backgrounds#Judicial Champion|Judicial Champion]], [[Backgrounds#Prodigal|Prodigal]]
