In a fight, your gear can be what saves you in a bad moment.

## Rogue Gear

When time for skullduggery arrives, a set of thieves’ tools might not be enough. Use this additional gear to get the drop on your foes.

**Rogue Gear**

| Gear | Cost | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Ether | 25 gp | 1/4 lb. |
| Garrote | 5 sp | 1/2 lb. |
| Smokeball | 10 gp | 1 lb. |

***Ether.*** Ether and similar alchemical substances are designed to knock out a target. Industrious souls proficient with alchemist tools can craft bottles of ether with a successful DC 13 INT (Alchemist Tools) check.

To use ether, you must take an action to douse a rag in the contents of a vial. The fumes fade quickly and lose effectiveness after 1 minute. During this time, you must succeed in grappling a creature that breathes, who then must make a DC 13 CON save over a number of turns. As long as you maintain the grapple, the creature must make a new CON save at the start of each of your turns. Creatures who are surprised during the grapple attempt have disadvantage on the save.

Once a creature accumulates 3 failures, it falls unconscious for 1 hour or until it takes damage. If a save fails by 5 or more, treat it as two failures toward the total. After three successes, the ether has no effect.

***Garrote.*** A garrote is a wire or thin rope about a foot or two in length, often attached to handles for grip. Acquiring one is rarely legal. The purpose of this grim tool is to strangle a creature with maximum efficiency. Anyone proficient with thieves’ tools is proficient with a garrote.

You must successfully grapple a creature to effectively use a garrote. Instead of making a STR (Athletics) check for the grapple, you can make a DEX (Thieves’ Tools) check. You have disadvantage on the check if you are sensed by your target. While attempting to garrote a target, attackers have advantage to hit you and you have disadvantage on DEX saves.

Grappled creatures begin suffocating immediately, having no time to draw breath. See [[Life and Death#Suffocating|Suffocating]].

***Smokeball.*** This pellet or pouch filled with alchemical substances releases sudden, powerful smoke after a strong impact, such as striking the ground. A character proficient with alchemist tools can craft smokeballs with a successful DC 15 INT (Alchemist Tools) check.

As an action, you can throw a smokeball at a point up to 30 feet away from you. This heavily obscures a 10-footradius area around that point until the start of your next turn. A favorite tactic of many rogues is to throw a smokeball, move to where the line of sight is broken, and then hide using the Cunning Action feature.

## Mount Gear

The **Mount Gear** table lists gear for sale specifically for use with mounts. Items that have special rules or require further explanation are covered here.

**Mount Gear**

| Gear | Cost | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Aerial compass | 100 gp | — |
| Barding, aerial | ×6 (base armor cost) | ×1.5 (base armor weight) |
| Cold weather tack | 20 gp | 10 lb. |
| Courier's bag | 15 gp | 5 lb. |
| Hobbles (set) | 5 sp | 1 lb. |
| Howdah (Large) | 30 gp | 100 lb. |
| Howdah (Huge) | 75 gp | 250 lb. |
| Parachute | 75 gp | 25 lb. |
| Quick-release mechanism | +30 gp | +10 lb. |
| Saddle, aerial | 20 gp | 30 lb. |
| Saddle, bubble | 150 gp | 70 lb. |
| Signal flags | 1 gp | 3 lb. |
| Smoke bomb | 50 gp | 1 lb. |
| Warm weather tack | 25 gp | 3 lb. |

***Aerial Compass.*** An aerial compass is a specialized clockwork device that accounts for altitude and heading to aid in navigating from the air where landmarks might be less visible.

***Barding, Aerial.*** Made of expensive lightweight materials, this armor protects your flying mount’s head, neck, chest, and body while accommodating it wings. The cost to bard an aerial mount is six times the equivalent armor made for Humanoids and it weighs one and a half times as much.

***Cold Weather Tack.*** Exposure to frigid temperatures can come from cold-weather climes or high-altitude flights. This tack includes a fur-lined body blanket and hood and is specially designed to keep your mount warm and mobile. A mount wearing this has advantage on CON saves due to cold weather.

***Courier’s Bag.*** This waxed leather saddle bag is water‑resistant and comes with a lock that requires a successful DC 15 DEX (Thieves’ Tools) check to pick. A copy of the key is usually held by the recipient and not carried by the courier. It has space for a cubic foot of volume and can hold 30 pounds.

***Hobbles.*** These restraints are placed around your mount’s legs to prevent it from straying while grazing during short or long rests when your attention might be elsewhere. While hobbled, your mount’s movement is limited to 10 feet.

***Howdah.*** These canopy-covered platforms accommodate multiple riders, usually carried on the backs of camels or elephants. Large mounts can carry two riders in a howdah, while Huge mounts can carry four riders. Riders in a howdah have half cover from attacks from a lower elevation.

***Parachute.*** This small backpack parachute is used for emergency landings if the rider’s mount becomes incapacitated. As a reaction, the wearer can deploy the parachute and make a DEX (Acrobatics) check. The wearer reduces bludgeoning damage taken from falling by an amount equal to twice the result of the check. If the roll is a natural 1, the parachute fails to properly deploy and the wearer takes falling damage as normal.

***Quick-Release Mechanism.*** This mechanism is added to an aerial saddle that allows the rider to quickly release themselves from a mount that is injured or in danger. Adding this mechanism to a saddle allows a rider to dismount as a reaction. Repacking the mechanism so it can be used again requires 1 minute.

***Saddle, Aerial.*** An aerial saddle has additional straps to keep a rider firmly mounted in aerial battles. It requires an action to mount or dismount, but the rider automatically succeeds on DEX ability checks or saves to remain mounted.

***Saddle, Bubble.*** A bubble saddle is an enclosed saddle meant for riders with burrowing or underwater mounts. Bubbles are usually constructed from alchemically treated leather or chitin, increasing their durability and transparency. A bubble saddle contains four hours of breathable air. The bubble has 15 AC and 20 hit points. If reduced to 0 HP, the bubble ruptures.

***Signal Flags.*** These colorful flags are used to communicate between distant riders or by ground forces to signal formations or give orders mid-flight. One of these flags can be seen clearly from 1 mile away.

***Smoke Bomb.*** Designed for aerial combat, this alchemical sachet disperses thick smoke creating a 30-foot-radius sphere of heavy obscurement that lasts for 4 rounds in still air, but even a moderate wind disperses it in 1 round.

***Warm Weather Tack.*** This tack includes lightweight, breathable mesh coverings that reflect heat and protect the mount from biting insects. A mount wearing this has advantage on CON saves due to warm weather and diseases spread by biting insects.

## Magic Items For Mounts

Below is a list of magic items for use by or with mounts. Items that require attunement must be attuned to by the mount’s rider, not the mount itself.

## Armor of the Paired Heart

*Armor, Rare Price Varies*

These matched sets of barding and armor are bedecked with scrollwork depicting a flaming heart. This armor only functions while you and your mount wear matching sets of armor. The various types of armor and their costs are on the **Armor and Barding Types** table.

**Armor and Barding Types**

| 2 | Studded Leather | 8,180 gp |
| 3 | Brigandine | 8,200 gp |
| 4 | Hide | 8,040 gp |
| 5 | Scale mail | 8,200 gp |
| 6 | Breastplate | 9,600 gp |
| 7 | Half plate | 11,000 gp |
| 8 | Chain mail | 12,000 gp |
| 9 | Splint | 14,000 gp |

The armor allows you to take some of your mount’s injuries onto yourself. When your mount takes damage, you can use your reaction to halve the damage it takes and take the other half yourself (rounded up). In addition, the armor makes you an effortless burden for your mount. Your weight and the equipment you carry do not count against your mount’s carrying capacity.

## Banner of the Horse Lord

*Wondrous Item, Rare Price 16,000 gp*

This three-foot-long tapered banner affixes to the saddle of a mount. When activated, the banner unfurls and allied mounts within 300 feet are filled with vigor and excitement for battle. They become immune to fear, increase their speed by 10 feet, and gain a +2 bonus to melee attacks and saves. Mounts with the Trampling Charge trait increase the save DC of that ability by 2.

These benefits last for 10 minutes or until the banner is struck, either as an action taken by the mount’s rider or by slaying or disabling the mount carrying the banner. Once you use this ability, you can’t use it again until the next dawn.

## Blanket of the War Steed

*Wondrous Item, Rare Price 4,000 gp*

These blankets are decorated with heroic heraldry and magically adjust their size and cut to your mount. While your mount is wearing this blanket and you are riding it, your mount gains a +1 bonus to all saves and is immune to fear effects. In addition, when you roll initiative, your mount gains temporary hit points equal to 1d8 + your PB.

## Bridle of Calling

*Wondrous Item, Uncommon Price 2,000 gp*

While your mount wears this bell-covered bridle, you can mentally call your mount to you as long as it is within 10 miles. Your mount instantly knows your direction and distance even if you continue to move, and it does everything it can to come to you. It doesn’t take actions or travel through dangerous terrain that causes obvious harm, such as swimming through lava, but it instead seeks the shortest path around dangerous terrain. Your mount takes rests as needed, but otherwise does not stop until it finds you. Once you use the bridle, you can’t use it again until the next dawn.

## Bridle of Handling

*Wondrous Item, Common Price 50 gp*

Gold tassels hang from this finely made bridle. While using this bridle to control your mount, you gain proficiency in Animal Handling. If you already have proficiency, double your PB.

## Chain of Hexed Souls

*Weapon (Flail), Rare*

*Price 4,010 gp (Requires Attunement)*

This magical length of gray chain is warm to the touch and wraps around your arm when you attune to it. You can make melee attacks with the chain as if it were a flail with the Reach property, and you have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with it. Once per turn, when you hit a creature with an attack using this magic chain, you can hex it. While the creature is hexed, you can use a bonus action to deal 1d8 force damage to you and the creature. At the start of each of the hexed creature’s turns, it can make a DC 15 WIS save, ending the hex on a success.

## Hallowed Feedbag

*Wondrous Item, Common Price 25 gp*

Created by faiths devoted to war, animals, or healing, these one-use items are prized by owners of valuable mounts. Food placed within the feedbag becomes blessed when the feedbag is attached to your mount’s muzzle. The mount consuming the food is cleansed of all poison and disease. The food becomes nonmagical if the feedbag is removed from the mount. Once the feedbag is used, its magic is expended and it becomes nonmagical.

## Infernal Bit

*Wondrous Item, Uncommon Price 2,000 gp*

This bit is worked from iron tainted with fiendish blood. While in your mount’s mouth or similar orifice, as an action, you can command it to exhale a 20-foot cone of fire. Each creature in the area must make a DC 13 DEX save. Creatures take 4d6 fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a success. Once used, you cannot use the bit again until you finish a short or long rest.

## Ironhide Barding

*Armor (Half-Plate), Rare Price 30,000 gp*

This magical half-plate barding is composed of thick, angular plates with Dwarven runes carved into intricate scrollwork. Your mount has a +1 bonus to AC while wearing this barding. While riding it, you can use an action to make your mount resistant to nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage for 10 minutes. Once you use this effect, you can’t use it again until you finish a long rest.

## Horseshoes of Traveling

*Wondrous Item, Rare Price 8,000 gp*

These set of four horseshoes are covered in arcane runes of travel. While all four shoes are affixed to the hooves of a horse or similar creature, they allow the creature a second mode of travel with a speed equal to the creature’s walking speed. The horseshoes do not provide any special adaptation to terrain.

## Right-Sizing Saddle

*Wondrous Item, Rare 4,000 gp (Requires Attunement)*

Created by a dungeon-delving wizard who never wanted to be separated from her mount, this plain leather saddle always seems to be a little too small for the mount wearing it, but it still manages to fit. When you saddle your mount while attuned to this saddle, your mount shrinks one size category—from Large to Medium, for example. This decrease in size doesn’t change its speed, carrying capacity, or ability to carry you. While affected by the magic of the saddle, your mount’s attacks deal 1d4 less damage on a hit (this can’t reduce the damage below 1).

The size reduction lasts until you use an action to end it or until 8 hours have passed. Once used, the saddle can’t be used again until the next dawn.

## Saddle of Flying

*Wondrous Item, Uncommon 8,000 gp (Requires Attunement)*

This leather saddle is inlaid with a pegasus with its wings unfurled. While you are attuned to this saddle and riding a creature saddled with this saddle, your mount has a flying speed equal to its walking speed as spectral wings extend from the saddle, lifting you both into the air.

You can use the saddle to allow your mount to fly for up to 4 hours, all at once or in several shorter flights, each one using a minimum of 1 minute from the duration. If your mount is flying when the duration expires or if you are dismounted while the wings are active, the saddle’s wings deactivate and the mount descends at a rate of 30 feet per round until it lands or until you are seated in the saddle again and choose to reactivate the wings (no action required).

The saddle regains 2 hours of flying capability for every 12 hours it isn’t in use.

## Thundering Horseshoes

*Wondrous Item, Rare 17,600 gp*

The surface of these heavy horseshoes roils with dark storm clouds, leaving whispers of fog as the shod creature runs. While all four shoes are affixed to the hooves of a horse or similar creature, and that creature hits with a Hoof or similar attack, the target takes an extra 1d6 thunder damage.

The horseshoes have 3 charges and regain 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. While riding a creature shod with these horseshoes, you can use an action to expend one charge to amplify the sound of your mount’s hooves into the thunderous pounding of a cavalry charge until the start of your next turn. If your mount moves at least 10 feet, each creature within 60 feet of your mount when it stops moving must succeed on a DC 15 WIS save or be frightened of you and your mount for 1 minute. While frightened, a creature must take the Dash action and move away from you by the safest available route on each of its turns, unless there is nowhere to move. An affected creature can repeat the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. This thunderous charge can be heard for 1 mile.

## Vehicular Charm Bracelet

*Wondrous Item, Uncommon 2,200 gp*

This gold bracelet has three small charms, each shaped like a miniature vehicle. The bracelet has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn.

While wearing the bracelet, you can use an action to expend one charge and activate one charm. A full-size, functional version of the vehicle depicted by the charm appears in an unoccupied space of your choice within 10 feet of you. If there is not enough space for the vehicle, it doesn’t appear, and the charge is wasted. The vehicle remains for 8 hours and during that time, the same charm can’t be used again. If you expend the last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the bracelet blackens, crumbles into ash, and is destroyed.

Eight magic charms exist. The GM decides the types of charms on the bracelet or determines them by rolling a d20 and consulting the **Vehicle Charms** table. See [[Vehicles]] for a vehicle’s statistics. Some of the vehicles listed in the chart are basic vehicles pulled by mounts and similar creatures, and they don’t have statistics. At the GM’s discretion, bracelets featuring charms for larger or more powerful vehicles might exist.
