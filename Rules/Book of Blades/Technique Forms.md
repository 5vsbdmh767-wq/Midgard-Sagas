*Optional, from Book of Blades.*

Monks master the magical energy that flows through living bodies. They can manipulate energy in others and themselves to achieve amazing feats or debilitate their foes. In addition to their normal class and subclass features, they can master technique forms.

To use these forms, choose a specialization when you gain access to the Techniques class feature at 2nd level. You gain one form immediately in addition to the other techniques you normally receive. If a form has a level requirement, you must be that level in the monk class to learn it.

## Acrobatic Forms

Some monks harness their technique to aid in acrobatic maneuvers, such as jumping, flipping, and balancing. When you specialize in acrobatic forms, learn Perfect Balance immediately.

***Perfect Balance.*** As a bonus action, you can spend 1 technique point to gain advantage on DEX (Acrobatics) checks for 1 minute.

You can choose an additional form at 5th, 10th, and 16th level.

***Flip Kick.*** When you make an unarmed strike against a creature, you can spend 2 technique points to move into an unoccupied space within 5 feet of you. This movement does not provoke opportunity attacks.

***Keen Reflexes.*** If you fail a DEX save, you can spend 4 technique points to reroll the save, using the new result.

***Rolling Dodge.*** When a creature you can see hits you with an attack, you can use your reaction to spend 2 technique points to attempt a rolling dodge. Make a DEX (Acrobatics) check contested by the creature’s attack roll. If you succeed, the attack misses and you move into an unoccupied space within 5 feet of you. This movement does not provoke opportunity attacks.

## Pressure Point Forms

Monks who specialize in pressure points use sharp jabs to exploit weak spots in a creature’s anatomy and turn a target’s body against it. When you specialize in pressure point forms, learn Hobbling Strike immediately.

***Hobbling Strike.*** When you hit with an unarmed strike, you can spend 1 technique point to injure one of your opponent’s legs. Its movement speed is reduced to half until the end of your next turn.

You can choose an additional form at 5th, 10th, and 16th level.

***Disorienting Strike.*** When you hit with an unarmed strike, you can spend 4 technique points to attempt to disorient your target. The creature must make a WIS save. On a failure, it has disadvantage on DEX and INT checks and saves until the end of your next turn.

***Heart Strike.*** When you hit with an unarmed strike, you can spend 4 technique points to attempt to hinder your target’s ability to heal. The creature must make a CON save. On a failure, it can’t regain hit points until the end of your next turn.

***Numbing Strike.*** When you hit with an unarmed strike, you can spend 2 technique points to attempt to inflict numbness on your target’s limbs. The creature must make a CON save. On a failure, the target has disadvantage on all attack rolls until the end of your next turn. If you spend 4 technique points instead of 2 when you execute this form, on a failure your target also has disadvantage on DEX checks and saves until the end of your next turn.

## Self-Defense Forms

Some martial arts forms focus primarily on self-defense rather than offense. Monks who focus on such styles are often difficult to hit and know how to exploit their attacker’s movements and weak points. When you choose a specialization, learn Artful Dodge immediately.

***Artful Dodge***. When you take the Dodge action, and a creature hits you with an attack before the beginning of your next turn, you may spend 1 technique point to add your DEX modifier to your AC again. If your new AC is higher than the attack roll, it misses you.

You can choose an additional form at 5th, 10th, and 16th level.

***Bear to the Ground (10th Level Required).*** When a creature moves more than 5 feet and then makes an attack against you, you can use your reaction to spend 2 technique points to attempt to interrupt its attack and use its momentum against it. The creature must make a DEX save. On a failure, its attack misses, and it lands prone in an unoccupied space of your choice within 5 feet of you.

***Quick Step.*** When a creature moves within 5 feet of you, you can use your reaction to spent 1 technique point to move 10 feet in the direction of your choice. This does not provoke opportunity attacks.

***Twist Escape.*** You can spend 2 technique points to gain advantage on a DEX (Acrobatics) check made to resist or escape a grapple.
