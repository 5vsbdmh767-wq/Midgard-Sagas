The bearfolk are broad-shouldered, bipedal humanoids that strongly resemble upright bears. Though they lack a bear's sharp claws, their hands are as dexterous as any human craftsman. They are covered in black or brown shaggy fur that often have light frosting on the chest, shoulders, and upper back. The color of their fur sometimes gives clues as to a particular bearfolk's ancestry and what tribe they hail from. As they get older, the fur around their muzzle begins to silver, giving them a distinguished look.

## Bearfolk in Midgard

[[Bearfolk]] in Midgard are wide-ranging in their wandering and settling. While most hail from the cold climates of the [[Northlands]], standing against both the challenging environment and fearsome neighbors, many bearfolk families inhabit the Margreve Forest and the cities of Midgard. Several families of bearfolk have even carved out a small nation of light in the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]], bringing the healing power of moonlight to that twilit realm.

## Bearfolk Lineage Traits

Your bearfolk character has the following hereditary traits.

***Age.*** Bearfolk reach adulthood in their late teens, living up to 80 years.

***Size.*** Your size is Medium. Bearfolk are typically large and muscular, reaching a height of over 7 feet and weighing anywhere between 250 and 350 pounds.

***Speed.*** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

***Apex Predator.*** When you make a CHA check, you can add your STR or CON modifier to it (choose which during character creation). You can use this trait a number of times equal to your PB, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

***Wild Heart.*** You and allied creatures within 5 feet of you have advantage on saves against being frightened while you are conscious and not incapacitated.

***Thick Coat.*** You are resistant to cold damage and immune to the effects of extreme cold.

## Northlands Bearfolk

%% source: Northlands Worldbook %%

Hailing from the cold forests and mountains of the Northlands, bearfolk are massive people of iron resolve and deep nobility who resemble bears that walk upright rather than on all fours. They put their trust in family and tribe to stand against the world and the challenges of their homeland. Many outsiders assume they are some form of beastkin, but bearfolk claim the fey lord [[Björnrike|Mesikämmen]] elevated them from their bear cousins.

In contrast with their brutish appearance, bearfolk are deeply spiritual and artistic. They lack a bear's sharp claws, and their hands are as dexterous as any human craftsman. They are covered in fur that often has light frosting on the chest, shoulders, and upper back. The color of their fur sometimes gives clues as to a particular bearfolk's ancestry and what tribe they hail from. As they get older, the fur around their muzzle begins to silver, giving them a distinguished look.

Much as their natural cousins, the natural environment causes minor differences between bearfolk if they inhabit it for generations. Andari bearfolk are slighter of build than their cousins, with reddish or pale fur. Also known as purifiers, Andari tend to have a strong affinity for nature. Andari often serve as spiritual anchors in their communities. They are a recent branch of the bearfolk race. The majority of Andari originate in the settled communities, such as Bjeornheim or in other settlements across the North. The other bearfolk, Garhamr, are thicker and hardier than their cousins. Also known as grizzlehides, Garhamr have fur ranging from black to golden. Tough and ferocious grizzlehides are the original bearfolk stock native to the colder climes of the Northlands forests.

### Bearfolk Names

Bearfolk are given cub names by their parents, similar to names found throughout the Northlands. When a bearfolk comes of age, they earn a new name, either bestowed upon them by an elder or one they choose themselves. This name befits a deed undertaken during their rite of passage into adulthood, describes some honorable or prominent trait of the bearfolk in question, or honors an ancestor.

**Cub Names:** Anga, Beorst, Burli, Cauldvyr, Cirdhan, Daegna, Dorngal, Durtta, Erlundt, Farla, Gurralt, Hrosvir, Ingyrd, Johanna, Lhod, Nodhr, Padhra, Sivaer, Tuli, Yrn.

**Deed Names:** Fireheart, Ironhide, Lightblade, Moondancer, Razorclaws, Shadowbane, Stonebones, Valgerdsdottir, Yellowfang, Yrnsson.

### Bearfolk Lineage Traits

Your bearfolk character has the following hereditary traits.

***Age.*** Bearfolk have similar lifespans to humans, reaching adulthood as older teenagers and typically living no more than 100 years old.

***Size.*** Your size is Medium. Bearfolk are typically large and muscular, reaching a height of over 7 feet and weighing anywhere between 250 and 350 pounds.

***Speed.*** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

***Apex Predator.*** When you make a CHA check, you can add your STR or CON modifier to it (choose which during character creation). You can use this trait a number of times equal to your PB, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

***Wild Heart.*** You and allied creatures within 5 feet of you have resistance to the frightened condition while you are conscious and are not incapacitated.

***Thick Coat.*** You have resistance to cold damage and immunity to the effects of extreme cold.

***Natural Adaptation.*** You inherited one of the following unique traits, determined by whether your characteristics are Andari or Garhamr:

- ***Andari.*** Your affinity for nature allows you to learn one Primordial cantrip of your choice. Choose whether INT, WIS, or CHA is your spellcasting ability for this spell during character creation.

- ***Garhamr.*** Your rough-and-tumble attitude grants you a bear hug. When you hit a target with an attack roll and deal damage to it, you can make an unarmed strike as a bonus action. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your CON modifier, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

## Natural Adaptation: Shadowborn

%% source: Book of Ebon Tides %%

The longer the bearfolk stay in the Shadow Realm, the more of each new generation shows an innate power to shape the darkness around them. The shadowborn are cubs born in shadow, gray-coated from birth and sometimes marked with strange arcane black patterns. They resist the darkness more easily than other bearfolk, and are often drawn to it.

If you play the [[#Northlands Bearfolk|Northlands bearfolk]], you can choose this Natural Adaptation instead of Andari or Garhamr:

- ***Shadowborn.*** You have darkvision to a range of 60 feet, and while you are in dim light or darkness, you can take the Hide action as a bonus action.

Suggested heritages: Moonlit, Umbral.
