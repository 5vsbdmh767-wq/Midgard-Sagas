Fecund and adaptable, vast populations of goblins are found everywhere creatures can survive. Their inborn opportunistic optimism means goblins stubbornly prosper in even the worst conditions and excel at finding the best in any situation.

Despite their numbers, goblins aren’t known for building great cities, and their settlements tend to be small and purpose-built to incorporate the natural environment. A region is likely to have several settlements with dozens of active alliances and rivalries between them. [[Goblins]] are also comfortable living in large cities where they form unruly communities, often in undesirable neighborhoods.

Goblins delight in the discovery of hidden things, and many view petty theft as an enjoyable challenge, a kind of helpful service meant to test the worthiness of the wealthy. Their innate stealth, cunning, and adaptability means they excel in spycraft, thievery, and scouting. They happily work at occupations that others shy away from, such as tending to middens or rat catching.

Goblins are short with ropey musculature and slightly oversized heads. They have skin tones ranging widely from gray through yellow, depending on their native environment, but most tend to shades of green. Most have red eyes, but yellow or brown is not uncommon.

For who they are in Midgard, see [[Goblins]]. Common heritages: [[Heritages#Aerobat Heritage|Aerobat]], [[Heritages#Conflicted Heritage|Conflicted]], [[Heritages#Salvager Heritage|Salvager]], [[Heritages#Shadowlands Heritage|Shadowlands]], [[Heritages#Wastelander Heritage|Wastelander]].

## Goblin Lineage Traits

Your goblin character has the following hereditary traits.

***Age.*** Goblins reach adulthood between the ages of 14 to 16. They rarely live longer than 100 years.

***Size.*** Your size is Small. Goblins range between 3 and 4 feet in height. They are heavier than they appear, owing to their dense tissue, and weigh 75 pounds on average.

***Speed.*** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

***Darkvision.*** You have darkvision to a range of 60 feet.

***Nimble Escape.*** You can use a bonus action to take the Disengage or Hide action.

***Strength in Numbers.*** When you deal damage to a creature with a weapon attack or spell attack while one of your allies is within 5 feet of that creature, your attack deals additional damage of the same damage type equal to your PB.

## Midgard Goblin

%% source: Lineages & Heritages %%

Fecund and adaptable, vast populations of goblins are found everywhere creatures can survive. Despite their numbers, goblins aren’t known for building great cities. Their settlements tend to be small and purpose-built to incorporate the natural environment. A region is likely to have several settlements with dozens of active alliances and rivalries between them.

Goblins are also comfortable living in large cities where they form unruly communities, often in undesirable neighborhoods. They happily work at occupations that others shy away from, such as tending middens or rat catching.

In prior eras, goblins warred with elves and dwarves, often as shock troops for orc or hobgoblin warlords. This history causes them to be viewed with suspicion, if not hostility, though this view is decreasing as goblins prove to be industrious and conscientious neighbors.

Goblins are short and tend to be slim with ropey musculature and slightly oversized heads. They have skin tones ranging widely from gray through yellow, depending on their native environment, but most tend to varying shades of green.

### Goblin Lineage Traits

Your goblin character has the following hereditary traits.

***Age.*** Goblins reach adulthood between the ages of 14 to 16. They rarely live longer than 100 years.

***Size.*** Your size is Small. Goblins range between 3 and 4 feet in height. They are heavier than they appear, owing to their dense tissue, and weigh 75 pounds on average.

***Speed.*** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

***Darkvision.*** You have darkvision to a range of 60 feet.

***Adaptive Physiology.*** You have advantage on checks or saves made to resist debilitating weather effects, such as those caused by extreme heat or cold. In addition, you can hold your breath for a number of minutes equal to double your CON modifier (minimum 1 minute).

***Goblin Hardiness.*** When you finish a long rest, you gain a number of temporary hit points equal to your PB. These temporary hit points last until expended or until you finish a long rest.

## Shadow Goblin

%% source: Book of Ebon Tides (a 5th Edition book; converted for this wiki following Converting 5e Material) %%

Shadow goblins grew up in the Shadow Realm as servitors and allies of the shadow fey, and in time founded their own city, [[Fandeval]], and cut their own path. They are closer to the fey than to their monstrous cousins in the mortal world, share the shadow fey's natural resistance to shadow's corruption, and have a surprising, usually secret, knack for shadow weaving and lunar magic. They are often blue- or purple-skinned with bright orange or yellow eyes, and they have a gift for blending into shadow. Good rogues and better assassins, they have a dark reputation but love low humor, drink, roughhousing, wrestling, and contests of skill (which they often lose). Each shadow goblin's given name should be entirely their own, and family names are practical: Bellringer, Candlesmith, Goldbiter, Moonshine, Netmender, Potlicker, Weaselwise.

### Shadow Goblin Lineage Traits

Your shadow goblin character has the following hereditary traits.

***Age.*** Shadow goblins reach adulthood at age 8 and can live up to 70 years.

***Size.*** Your size is Small. Shadow goblins stand between 3 and 4 feet tall and weigh between 40 and 80 pounds.

***Speed.*** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

***Darkvision.*** You have darkvision to a range of 60 feet.

***Nimble Escape.*** You can use a bonus action to take the Disengage or Hide action.

***Stink Eye.*** As an action, you can make a combination of rude gestures and sounds to distract one creature you can see within 30 feet. If the target can see and hear you, it must make a CHA save (DC 8 + your PB + your CHA modifier). On a failure, it has disadvantage on the next ability check, attack roll, or save it makes before the start of your next turn. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your PB, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

***Unseelie Blessing.*** You have advantage on saves against being charmed, and magic can't put you to sleep.

Suggested heritages: Umbral, Cosmopolitan, Joymonger.
