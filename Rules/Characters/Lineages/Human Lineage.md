[[Humans]] are the youngest people of the world, and their tenacity and flexibility has allowed them to quickly spread. Humans are diverse in appearance and culture, so it’s not surprising that their creation myths are similarly plentiful.

Despite, or perhaps as a consequence of their tangled history, humans leave their stamp wherever they go. A natural drive fills their lives with dreams of long-lasting achievements. More than just a few reach those dreams.

Humans are found in nearly every environment, in nearly every form of community. Humans crop up even in surprising places with an adaptability and a burning desire to succeed.

For who they are in Midgard, see [[Humans]]. Common heritages: [[Heritages#Cosmopolitan Heritage|Cosmopolitan]], [[Heritages#Nomadic Heritage|Nomadic]].

## Human Lineage Traits

Your human character has the following hereditary traits.

***Age.*** Humans reach adulthood in their late teenage years and rarely live to be more than 100 years old.

***Size.*** Your size is Medium or Small. Humans vary in height and weight, but most adults range between 5 feet and 6 feet tall and average around 150 pounds. If you choose to play a human shorter than 4 feet however, you can choose your size to be Small.

***Speed.*** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

***Ambitious.*** You gain proficiency in one skill of your choice, and you gain one talent of your choice. This talent can be from any of the talent lists, but you must meet the talent’s prerequisites if any are required (see **Talents** in **Chapter 4**).

## Umbral Human

%% source: Book of Ebon Tides (a 5th Edition book; converted for this wiki following Converting 5e Material) %%

The Shadow Realm twists those who live in it. Umbral humans are not simply humans corrupted by shadow but humans slowly changed by it over generations, until they are inured to it. Some descend from humans stolen to serve the shadow fey, others from humans who strayed onto a shadow road and never found their way home. Others still are the survivors of shadow fey experiments with shadow's effects on mortals, done to punish, to make better servants, or just for fun. Umbral humans take the names of the communities they grow up in, often a blend of mortal and shadow customs (Abradal, Analinda, Breck, Cloria, Dalid, Haggar, Helgamet, Korim, Linnet, Masoosh, Nuriam, Obrax, Soffy, Woldemar).

### Umbral Human Lineage Traits

Your umbral human character has the following hereditary traits.

***Age.*** Your natural aging slows to match that of an elf or shadow fey.

***Size.*** Your size is Medium or Small. Humans vary in height and weight, but most adults range between 5 feet and 6 feet tall and average around 150 pounds. If you choose to play a human shorter than 4 feet however, you can choose your size to be Small.

***Speed.*** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

***Darkvision.*** You have darkvision to a range of 60 feet.

***Dark Infusion.*** You are resistant to cold damage.

***Fade Away.*** While standing perfectly still, you can use an action to become invisible. You become visible again if you move or take an action. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your PB, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

***Shadow Infusion.*** Shadow has changed you in one of two ways. Choose one:

- ***Changeling.*** You are a human adapted to the shadows over many generations, much as the shadow fey differ from other elves. Your skin tends toward alabaster white, ebony black, or subtle grays, and you may have rudimentary horns. You can cast the *[[1st-Circle Spells#Disguise Self|disguise self]]* spell at will, without a spell slot.
- ***Gifted.*** You drank too deeply of the shadows, too quickly, most likely through a bargain with a shadow fey, whether willing or not, as boon or bane. You may look like an ordinary umbral human, like a shadow fey, or carry anything from small marks to tails, hooves, or vestigial wings, and the change may have taken some of your memories; whatever it is, you can always see its sign. You are resistant to necrotic damage, and you have one of the following Shadow Gifts, each a benefit with a price (or work out another with your GM):
  - You have proficiency in one skill of your choice and always make checks with it at advantage. Choose a second skill; you always make checks with it at disadvantage.
  - You no longer need to eat or breathe, and you need only 4 hours for a long rest, but you gain one level of exhaustion that can't be removed by any means.
  - Your walking speed is halved, and you gain a fly, swim, or climb speed equal to your new walking speed.
  - Each day at dawn, you gain temporary HP equal to your CON score, but you can no longer spend Hit Dice to heal during short rests.

Suggested heritages: Umbral, Changeling, Cosmopolitan.
