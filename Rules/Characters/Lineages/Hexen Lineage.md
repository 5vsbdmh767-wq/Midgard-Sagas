Hexens are mortal beings born of hags. Some are conceived from the direct union of a hag and a Humanoid, while others are the result of ritual magic, transforming offspring of other lineages. The reasons a hag might create a hexen are varied. They might just want to raise a child, caring for them and preparing them to one day embark on the transformation required to become a full-fledged hag. Or a coven of hags might combine their power to create a hexen servant. A hag might instead transform a child into a hexen to incite chaos within a community or to enact some personal vengeance. Regardless of their origins, hexens aren’t bound to the wills of their creators and are free to decide whether they embrace or reject the hags that shaped them.

Few societies are open-minded enough to fully accept a hexen living among them. Even cultures otherwise open to other creatures tend to regard hags and their kin with fear and suspicion. Consequently, hexens living among nonhags often learn to disguise their true natures from an early age, using both mundane and magical means. Many also gravitate toward solitary lives, perhaps a life on the road.

> [!note] Hag Transformation
> Hags are created in myriad ways, but a common method is the transformation of a willing hexen into a full-fledged Fey. The process requires the hexen to perform a series of lengthy, grueling rites and to locate a coven of hags willing (and able) to perform the necessary dark rituals. To complicate matters further, the entire process is a carefully guarded secret known only to the most ancient and powerful of hags. Due to the danger and difficulty of the process, most hexen forgo it entirely. However, a hexen PC who wants to embark on this path can work with the GM to do so. Upon completing the process, the PC is irreversibly transformed into a hag type of their choice, becoming an NPC controlled by the GM (GM’s discretion).

## Hexen Lineage Traits

Your hexen PC has the following traits.

***Age.*** Hexens physically mature more quickly than humans, reaching adulthood in their early teenage years. They live longer than many Humanoids, often reaching 300 years or more.

***Size.*** Your size is Medium. Hexens vary widely in size and weight, ranging anywhere from 4 to 6 feet in height and averaging around 150 pounds.

***Speed.*** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

***Hag Magic.*** You learn one Wyrd cantrip of your choice, and you can cast the *[[1st-Circle Spells#Disguise Self|disguise self]]* spell without needing to spend a spell slot or use components. Your spell attack bonus and spell save DC for these are determined by your INT, WIS, or CHA modifier (your choice during character creation).

***Hybrid Humanoid.*** You are a Humanoid, but your hag ancestry gives you traits in common with Fey. You have advantage on saves against being charmed and against effects that would cause you to fall unconscious.

***Natural Adaptation.*** You have inherited one set of the following unique traits, determined by the type of hag that shaped you:

- ***Ambush Hag.*** You have advantage on saves against the poisoned condition. You also gain a climbing speed equal to your walking speed and can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.

- ***Green Hag.*** As an action, you can turn invisible for up to 1 minute, along with any equipment you are wearing or wielding. This invisibility ends early if you attack or cast a spell or if your concentration ends (as if concentrating on a spell). You can use this ability a number of times equal to your PB, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

- ***Night Hag.*** You learn one additional Wyrd cantrip of your choice as part of your Hag Magic feature. You also have advantage on saves against spells and other magical effects.

- ***Sea Hag.*** You have advantage on saves against the frightened condition. You also have a swim speed equal to your walking speed and can breathe air and water.
