Dhampirs are the improbable children of vampires and humans. People who interact with dhampirs often have strong subconscious feelings about them. They either find them compelling and seek to be near them, or they are repulsed and find the earliest opportunity to leave their presence. It’s rare for a living person to meet a dhampir and feel nothing about their new acquaintance.

For this reason, lone dhampirs without a community of peers to support them favor large, cosmopolitan settlements where they can maintain many acquaintances without causing too much of a disturbance. Such dhampirs who don’t live adventurous lives gravitate toward work that they can do in the evening or at night and that allows regular casual contact with the public.

As the children of humans, dhampirs run the range of human height, though they tend to be lean, often appearing thin or undernourished. Their hair and skin tones similarly run the human range, though their hair tends to be vibrant in color while their skin is paler than that of a typical human. Dhampirs are prone to sunburn. They favor hats and head coverings, and they wear clothing that leaves little flesh exposed. Many even take to wearing gloves during the day. Even if not ostentatious, dhampirs generally prefer to wear finely made clothing that is kept in good repair.

## Dhampir Lineage Traits

Your dhampir character has the following hereditary traits.

***Age.*** Dhampirs mature in their late teenage years, but their aging slows upon adulthood. A dhampir can live up to 750 years.

***Size.*** Your size is Medium or Small. Dhampirs vary in height and weight, but they usually grow to between 5 feet and 6 feet tall and weigh an average of 140 pounds. If you choose to play a dhampir shorter than 4 feet however, you can choose your size to be Small.

***Speed.*** Your base walking speed is 30 feet. You also gain a 30-foot climbing speed, which you can use to move up vertical surfaces, across horizontal surface, and upside down along ceilings, while leaving your hands free.

***Dark Thirst.*** You have short fangs that serve as a natural weapon. Your fangs counts as a simple melee weapon with which you are proficient. You choose whether you add your STR or DEX modifier to attack rolls made with your fangs. On a hit, your bite deals 1d4 piercing damage. If your target isn’t a Construct or Undead, you can feed from it as part of your attack. If you choose to do so, your target takes an additional 1d4 necrotic damage, you gain temporary hit points equal to the necrotic damage dealt, and you have advantage on the next attack roll you make before the end of your next turn.

***Darkvision.*** You have darkvision to a range of 60 feet. While in sunlight, you have disadvantage on WIS (Perception) checks when perceiving by sight.

***Hybrid Humanoid.*** You are a Humanoid, but your vampiric blood gives you traits in common with Undead. You have advantage on saves against being poisoned and advantage on checks made to resist suffering levels of exhaustion.

## Midgard Dhampir

%% source: Lineages & Heritages %%

Dhampirs are the improbable children of vampires and human mothers. The world itself seems to reject the half-living, and strange occurrences abound around them, especially if they are in a relaxed atmosphere. Small items fall from tables, light furniture seems to move of its own accord, shutters and doors open or close without a hand upon them. In settlements where inhabitants are suspicious of unexplainable phenomena, dhampirs are thought to attract ghosts and are either avoided or attacked outright if the people are particularly superstitious.

People who interact with dhampirs have strong subconscious feelings about them. They either find them compelling and seek to be near them, or they are repulsed and find the earliest opportunity to leave their presence. It’s rare for a living person to meet a dhampir and feel nothing about their new acquaintance.

For this reason, lone dhampirs without a community of peers to support them favor large, cosmopolitan settlements where they can maintain many acquaintances without causing too much of a disturbance. Such dhampirs who do not live adventurous lives gravitate toward work they can do in the evening or at night and that allows regular casual contact with the public.

As the children of humans, dhampirs run the range of human height, though they tend to be lean. Females are markedly slighter than males. Their hair and skin tones similarly run the human range, though their hair tends to be vibrant in color while their skin is paler than a human of the same lineage. Dhampirs are prone to sunburn. They favor hats and head coverings, and they wear clothing that leaves little flesh exposed. Many even take to wearing gloves during the day. Even if not ostentatious, dhampirs generally prefer to wear finely made clothing that is kept in good repair.

### Dhampir Lineage Traits

Your dhampir character has the following hereditary traits.

***Age.*** Dhampirs mature in their late teenage years, but their aging slows upon adulthood. A dhampir can live up to 750 years.

***Size.*** Your size is Medium or Small. As the children of humans, dhampirs vary in height and weight, but they usually grow to between 5 feet and 6 feet tall and have a slight build, weighing an average of 120 pounds.

***Speed.*** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

***Darkvision.*** You have darkvision to a range of 60 feet.

***Dark Thirst.*** You have short fangs that serve as a natural weapon. You can bite with your fangs as an unarmed strike, but you can do so only against an incapacitated creature or a creature you have grappled. Your bite deals 1 piercing damage, and if your target has blood and isn’t a Construct or Undead, you can feed from it as part of your attack. Your feeding deals necrotic damage equal to your CHA modifier (minimum of 1), and you gain temporary hit points equal to the necrotic damage dealt. Once you gain temporary hit points with this trait, you can’t do so again until you finish a short or long rest.

***Vampiric Resilience.*** You have advantage on saves against disease, and you have resistance to necrotic damage.
