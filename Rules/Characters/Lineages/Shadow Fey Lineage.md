Shadow fey stalk the Shadow Realm and encroach on the mortal world where the barriers between the planes are thin. While they resemble elves physically, shadow fey have horns that range from subtle nubs to large protrusions. They are physically beautiful, but their mentalities combine temperaments common to both elves and goblins, shifting between benign and cruel with little notice.

Despite their fickle whims and darker proclivities, the shadow fey are not inherently good or evil. Instead, much like their fey cousins, they are content pursuing their own desires for seemingly alien reasons. While they never make their goals known, they are endlessly patient, preferring slow and subtle plans that overtake their enemies without anyone noticing.

Shadow fey in the mortal world of Midgard often hail from places that mingle with the borders of the Shadow Realm, such as [[Castle Shadowcrag]] or the Margreve. Courts from the Shadow Realm sometimes send delegations to the material plane. The most notable of these is the Winter’s Kiss Embassy in Zobeck.

For who they are in Midgard, see [[Shadow Fey]]. Common heritages: [[Heritages#Shadow Court Heritage|Shadow Court]], [[Heritages#Supplicant Heritage|Supplicant]].

## Shadow Fey Lineage Traits

Your shadow fey character has the following hereditary traits.

***Age.*** While Shadow Fey reach physical maturity around the age of 20, their mental and emotional faculties reach full maturity around the age of 100. Shadow Fey can live to be 750 years old.

***Size.*** Your size is Medium. Shadow fey range from under 5 feet to over 6 feet tall, and weigh around 110 pounds.

***Speed.*** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

***Darkvision.*** You have darkvision to a range of 60 feet.

***Shadow Step.*** When in darkness, dim light, or a shadow large enough to cover your body, as an action you can cast the *misty step* spell without using a spell slot. Your spellcasting ability for this spell is WIS, INT, or CHA (choose which during character creation). You can use this trait a number of times equal to your PB and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

***Fey Whim.*** You have proficiency in Persuasion. When you finish a short or long rest, you can exchange this proficiency for Deception, and vice versa.

## Lunar Elf

%% source: Book of Ebon Tides (a 5th Edition book; converted for this wiki following Converting 5e Material) %%

Lunar elves are a rare offshoot of the [[Shadow Fey|shadow fey]], always born under the light of the full moons, and their births are usually taken as an auspicious omen. Their origin is even more mysterious than that of the shadow fey, and many have lately been drawn toward the absent [[Court of the Moonlit King|Moonlit King]]. They look like other shadow fey except that their skin glows in the dark and their eyes are luminous silver, and their horns tend to be long, spiraled, and pearlescent. They can learn to hide their glow, but strong emotion tends to reveal it. They aren't cast out of the courts, but many prefer work that keeps them out from under the nobles' eyes: emissaries to the [[Moonlit Glades]], residents of the [[Court of One Million Stars|Starry Court]], or hunters of the realm's beasts. Those who stay in high society take ordinary shadow fey names; those who embrace their difference name themselves after the stars (Aldebaar, Belline, Caropa, Celene, Lestra, Mintella, Solaris, Virgesh).

### Lunar Elf Lineage Traits

Your lunar elf character has the following hereditary traits.

***Age.*** While lunar elves reach physical maturity around the age of 20, their mental and emotional faculties reach full maturity around the age of 100. Lunar elves can live to be 750 years old.

***Size.*** Your size is Medium. Lunar elves range from under 5 feet to over 6 feet tall, and weigh around 110 pounds.

***Speed.*** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

***Darkvision.*** You have darkvision to a range of 60 feet.

***Fey Whim.*** You have proficiency in Persuasion. When you finish a short or long rest, you can exchange this proficiency for Deception, and vice versa.

***Luminous.*** You shed bright light in a 15-foot radius and dim light for an additional 15 feet. At any time, without using an action, you can dim this to dim light in a 15-foot radius, suppress it completely, or restore it. This light counts as a spell of a circle equal to your PB; if any of its area overlaps an area of darkness created by a spell of that circle or lower, the spell that created the darkness is dispelled.

***Moon Child.*** When in darkness, dim light, or a shadow large enough to cover your body, you can cast the *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Moonbeam|moonbeam]]* spell without using a spell slot. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your PB, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest. WIS is your spellcasting ability for this spell.

Suggested heritages: Umbral, Moonlit, Feysworn.
