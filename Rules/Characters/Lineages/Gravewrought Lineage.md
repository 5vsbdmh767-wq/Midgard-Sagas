Gravewroughts are an artificial lineage of mortal souls bound into bodies crafted from the flesh of various once-living creatures. Mystery surrounds the mystical circumstances needed to create such beings though, and most attempts produce soulless golems or mindless undead. The few spellcasters capable of creating gravewroughts rarely produce more than one in their lifetime, as their minds tend to break under the strain of failure to replicate their previous results.

Despite the challenges in their creation, repeat experiments have proven several key truths. Gravewroughts can only be created from Humanoid remains, and only the soul of a Humanoid who has died (even briefly) can be born again as one. Once the binding rituals are complete, most awaken with memories of their former lives, but the traumatic process of rebinding the soul always leaves a mark: some awaken with scattered fragments and confused impressions of who they once were, and some violently reject traces of their former selves, unable to psychologically align their past with their new grotesque form. Almost all have marked changes in personality due to shrouded memories of what their souls endured in death.

The lives of gravewroughts are inevitably marked by tragedy. Because so few exist, their nature is misunderstood, and they are frequently mistaken for undead, which often leads to immediate violence. Perhaps worse still are the reactions of those who knew a gravewrought in their former life, for those friends and family rarely learn to accept the full magnitude of the physical and psychological changes endured by their loved one.

## Gravewrought Lineage Traits

Your gravewrought PC has the following traits.

***Age.*** The soul bound within a gravewrought can be any age, but the binding process typically rejects the souls of those who died at the extremes of their natural lifespans—the very young and the very old. A gravewrought’s body doesn’t age or decay, so there is no known limit to how long it can function. However, due to lives so marked by misunderstanding, fear, and tragedy, there are no recorded instances of a gravewrought living beyond a century.

***Size.*** Your size is Medium or Small. Gravewroughts vary in height and weight, depending on the form used to construct their bodies. Most range between 5 and 6 feet tall and average around 150 pounds. If you choose to play a gravewrought shorter than 4 feet, your size is Small.

***Speed.*** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

***Hybrid Humanoid.*** Your soul inhabits a body crafted from the flesh of once-living creatures, making you primarily a Humanoid. However, your constructed body also gives you traits in common with Constructs. You are immune to disease and resistant to poison damage, and you have advantage on saves against being paralyzed, petrified, and poisoned.

***Soulbound.*** If you start your turn with 0 HP, you immediately revive with a number of hit points equal to your CON modifier (minimum of 1). If you take necrotic damage, this trait doesn’t function at the start of your next turn, and you instead roll death saves as normal. You still die if your body is destroyed entirely or if you fail enough death saves, as normal.

***Stitched Flesh.*** As a bonus action, you can remove or reattach your head or one of your limbs without injury. You can also use this ability to reattach limbs that have been severed. While detached from your body, you can feel the body part as if it were still attached, and you can manipulate it to a limited degree. For example, a detached hand can grab a coin, an arm could grasp a flagpole, or a head could scream from inside a box. Your GM has final say on what you can accomplish with a severed part, but you can’t actively manipulate more than one at a time, and a severed part is incapable of complex actions, like making attacks. While your head is severed, you are blinded, deafened, and can’t speak intelligibly or cast spells—but you otherwise function as normal.

If a body part remains detached for 24 consecutive hours, it is permanently destroyed and can only be restored via magical means, such as the *[[Rituals 7th–9th Circle#Regenerate|regenerate]]* spell. If that severed part is your head, you die.
