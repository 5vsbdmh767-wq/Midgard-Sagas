Satarre are renowned planar travelers, and the unbound satarre are a small faction of them zealously devoted to unraveling the barriers between worlds. They are a curiosity even among their own kind, with an instinct for places of transition, and an unbound satarre is driven to find such gateways and widen them by mundane or magical means. As the kingdoms of shadow grow, so does the web of gateways feeding their shadow roads, and none know it better than the unbound.

Satarre often take names from the cultures that raise them, though names in Void Speech are common (Aggorret, Atlessa, Binulett, Denahrek, Frizel, Ixa, Medloss, Miskra, Mörkothr, Narkot, Unterra, Vessar). Every satarre is also given a cult name at its naming day, marking the dark god its life was dedicated to.

## Unbound Satarre Lineage Traits

Your unbound satarre character has the following hereditary traits.

***Age.*** Satarre grow quickly, walking soon after hatching and reaching the development of a 15-year-old human by age 6. They keep the same appearance until about 50, then decline rapidly; few live past 65.

***Size.*** Your size is Medium. Satarre are tall and thin, from 6 to 7 feet tall, with peculiar segmented limbs.

***Speed.*** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

***Darkvision.*** You have darkvision to a range of 60 feet.

***Portal Sense.*** While in a confined space, you instinctively know the direction and distance to the nearest nonmagical way into or out of the area you are in. You also have advantage on INT (Investigation) and WIS (Perception) checks to find secret or invisible doors and magical portals.

***Unraveling Touch.*** As an action, you tear at the reality that holds a creature together. Choose a creature you can see within 30 feet. It must make a CON save (DC 8 + your PB + your CON modifier). On a failure, the creature must use its action on each of its turns to repeat the save as it struggles to hold itself together, and the effect ends when it succeeds. Once you use this trait, you can't do so again until you finish a long rest.

Suggested heritages: Umbral, Void-Touched, Diaspora.
