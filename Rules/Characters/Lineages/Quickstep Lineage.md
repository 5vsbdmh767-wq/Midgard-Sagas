Quicksteps came to the Shadow Realm with the shadow fey as their servitors, and like the shadow fey they are naturally resilient to its corruption. They love finery and feel at home amid the riches of court, and their gift for speed suits two favorite shadow fey pastimes, sabotage and dueling. They are rarely found outside the fey courts but thrive in many of them, above all the [[Court of Midnight Teeth]] and the [[Court of Winter's Love]]. Like most fey, though, they have a streak of wanderlust, and they make skilled, efficient adventurers.

Quicksteps choose flashy, memorable names that sound good announced before a duel, such as Birch, Bolt, Crimson, Dandy, Double Time, Echo, Glory, Hotspur, Indigo, Starburst, or Zephyr.

## Quickstep Lineage Traits

Your quickstep character has the following hereditary traits.

***Age.*** Quicksteps have remarkably short lives for fey. They mature around 10 years and live up to 50 years.

***Size.*** Your size is Small. Quicksteps stand between 3 and 4 feet tall and weigh between 25 and 40 pounds.

***Speed.*** Your base walking speed is 50 feet.

***Darkvision.*** You have darkvision to a range of 60 feet.

***Fey Ancestry.*** You have advantage on saves against being charmed, and magic can't put you to sleep.

***Startling Speed.*** When you move at least 10 feet on your turn, you can choose to give attack rolls against you disadvantage until the start of your next turn. You can't do so while incapacitated or restrained. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your PB, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

Suggested heritages: Umbral, Feysworn, Cosmopolitan.
