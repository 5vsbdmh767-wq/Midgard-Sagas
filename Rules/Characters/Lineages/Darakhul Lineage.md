Darakhul are a rare form of undead creature that can arise from Humanoid corpses infected with the ghoul hunger disease (see *Game Master’s Guide*). Unlike ordinary ghouls, darakhul retain their minds and memories, and they can control their feral instincts. While they function mentally much as they did in life—and even physically resemble their previous form—they rapidly take on traits that mark them as living dead. Any hair, fur, or feathers rot, becoming patchy or falling out entirely. Their teeth elongate into fangs, and their flesh fades, becoming gaunt and gray, stretching taut over bone and accentuating a more skeletal appearance.

Darakhul can rarely exist openly in societies of the living, where their nature is almost universally misunderstood and feared. So to survive, they must be clever and thorough, using mundane and magical means to hide their true nature. Under normal circumstances, a darakhul with a careful disguise can move among the living without rousing suspicion.

However, darakhul rarely choose to become what they are, so the true challenge is finding how to live with their transformation and the endless hunger that comes with it. Whether a darakhul chooses to accept their new powers and hungers and whether they fight their daily hungers to retain a semblance of their old selves are the questions that truly determine their survival.

For the darakhul of Midgard, see [[The Darakhul Imperium]], and for those of the Shadow Realm, see [[Peoples of Shadow#Darakhul|Peoples of Shadow]].

## Darakhul Lineage Traits

Your darakhul PC has the following traits.

***Age.*** Darakhul appear as the age they were at their time of death—though their features become more corpse-like and less-distinguished as they age. The upper limit of darakhul age has never been discovered since most meet violent ends before whatever unnatural lifespan they have runs out. Despite their potentially limitless lifespan, the oldest recorded darakhul begin to lose their faculties after a few centuries, becoming plagued by insatiable hunger and feral tendencies around the 500-year mark.

***Size.*** Your size is Medium or Small. When you create your darakhul PC, choose a different lineage option (such as human or kobold) to represent the lineage you were in life. This choice informs your overall appearance, for your size, weight, and height are determined by this lineage. If you choose a lineage with multiple size options, you can choose between them. For example, if you were a kobold in life, you can choose whether your size is Medium or Small.

***Speed.*** Your speed is determined by the lineage you were in life.

***Dark Hunger.*** You have a heavy jaw, which serves as a natural weapon powerful enough to crush bones to powder. Your bite counts as a simple melee weapon with which you are proficient. You choose whether you add your STR or DEX modifier to attack rolls made with your bite. On a hit, your bite deals 1d4 piercing damage. If your target isn’t a Construct or Undead, you can feed from it as part of your attack. If you choose to do so, your target takes an additional 1d4 necrotic damage, you gain temporary hit points equal to the necrotic damage dealt, and you have advantage on the next attack roll you make before the end of your next turn. You must consume 1 pound of raw meat each day or suffer the effects of starvation. If you go 24 hours without such a meal, you automatically gain one level of exhaustion (which your Hybrid Humanoid benefits don’t allow you to avoid or resist).

***Darkvision.*** You have darkvision to a range of 60 feet. While in sunlight, you have disadvantage on WIS (Perception) checks when perceiving by sight.

***Hybrid Humanoid.*** You are a Humanoid, but your partial transition into undeath gives you traits in common with Undead. You are resistant to poison damage, and you have advantage on saves against being poisoned and advantage on checks made to resist gaining levels of exhaustion.

Game effects that raise a creature from the dead work on you as normal, but they return you to life as a darakhul. A *[[Rituals 7th–9th Circle#True Resurrection|true resurrection]]* or *[[9th-Circle Spells#Wish|wish]]* spell can restore you to life as a living member of your original lineage.
