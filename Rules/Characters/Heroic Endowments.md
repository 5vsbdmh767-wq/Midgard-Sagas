%% source: Book of Blades %%

Legends tell of the few who stood against many, of warriors blessed with strength beyond all others. Perhaps, in a time of strife and war, your PCs rise to become these legends, facing a thousand foes in combat. If so, consider the following optional rule: heroic endowments. A heroic endowment is a power granted to characters by gods, powerful beings, fate, or their own martial prowess. A character of 3rd level or higher can claim an endowment, so long as they meet its prerequisites. Heroic endowments are selected only when a character enters a battlefield to wage a righteous or terrible war. These endowments are extremely powerful, last only as long as the battle, and vanish the moment combat ends. They are best used to quickly power up a PC who is about to face an opponent of significantly greater number or unusually high CR.

The GM determines which heroic endowments are available. A PC can hold a maximum number of endowments based on character level:

**Number Of Endowments Based On Character Level**

| Level | Number of Endowments |
|---|---|
| 3rd | 1 |
| 5th | 2 |
| 10th | 3 |
| 15th | 4 |

## General Heroic Endowments

The following endowments are available to any class so long as they meet any listed prerequisite.

## Deft Movement

While wearing light or no armor, you gain a +1 bonus to AC. Once per turn, you can reduce the damage of a single attack that hits you by an amount equal to your PB.

## Fearsome Name

*Prerequisite: 10th Level*

Your reputation precedes you, driving fear into the heart of lesser enemies. Creatures and Units with a CR of 5 or lower that target you with an attack or harmful spell must first succeed on a DC 16 WIS save or lose the attack or spell. A creature that fails its save by 5 or more is frightened of you for 1 minute.

## Fell Rider

*Prerequisite: Proficiency in Animal Handling*

Atop your mount, you are unstoppable. While you are mounted and aren’t incapacitated, you gain the following benefits:

- You can ride your mount through the space of any creature smaller than your mount. That creature must succeed on a DC 15 STR save or be knocked prone.

- When you hit a creature with a weapon attack, each creature within 5 feet of the target takes damage equal to your STR modifier (minimum 1).

- As a bonus action, your mount can move up to half its speed.

## Hammer Of Falling Stars

Once per turn, when you attack a creature with a melee weapon attack that deals bludgeoning damage, the target’s AC becomes 10 for that attack. On a hit, the attack deals additional bludgeoning damage equal to your Strength modifier.

## Porcupine Armor

While wearing armor, you have resistance to piercing damage. Each time you take piercing damage from a ranged or thrown weapon, you gain a +1 bonus to AC. This bonus ends the next time you take damage from a melee weapon attack.

## Relentless Advance

If you move at least 10 feet toward an enemy in a straight line, your movement does not provoke opportunity attacks, and each creature within 5 feet of you while you are moving must succeed on a DC 14 STR save or be knocked prone.

## Sanguine Tithe

*Prerequisite: 5th Level*

Blood begets blood. When you hit one or more creatures with a melee weapon attack and deal 5 or more damage with the attack, you gain a number of temporary hit points equal to your character level.

## Storm Of Blades

*Prerequisite: 15th Level*

Once per turn, when you reduce a creature to 0 HP, you can move up to 5 feet without provoking an opportunity attack and make one additional melee weapon attack.

## Sweeping Blows

Your weapons carve through nearby foes. Each melee weapon attack you make targets a number of creatures in range equal to your PB.

## Unbreakable

Attacks break upon you like water on stone. While wearing heavy armor, you gain a +2 bonus to armor class and reduce bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage you take by an amount equal to your PB.

## Unceasing Vigor

You gain a number of temporary hit points equal to twice your hit point maximum. These temporary hit points last for 1 hour.

## Walking Bulwark

*Prerequisite: 15th Level*

While wearing medium or heavy armor and wielding a shield, you have a damage threshold of 10. Attacks that would deal 9 or less damage to you instead deal no damage.

## Class-Specific Heroic Endowments

The following endowments are available to specific classes.

## Unyielding Rage

*Prerequisite: [[Barbarian]]*

While raging, you gain the following benefits:

- As a bonus action, you can expend 1 hit die to regain a number of hit points equal to the number rolled plus your CON modifier plus your barbarian level.

- Once per turn, when you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack, you can expend 1 hit die to deal an additional 1d12 damage on the attack of the same damage type as your weapon.

## Crimson Advantage

*Prerequisite: [[Fighter]]*

In the same round you use your Last Stand class feature, you gain the following benefits:

- Each creature of your choice within 20 feet of you gains a number of temporary hit points equal to the amount healed by your Last Stand.

- You have advantage on attack rolls against any creature that has taken damage until the end of your turn.

## Peaceful Body, Hostile Mind

*Prerequisite: [[Monk]]*

At the start of each of your turns, you can expend 1 technique point to do one of the following:

- You gain resistance to all damage until the start of your next turn.

- You have advantage on attack rolls with unarmed strikes until the end of your turn.

## Divine Wrath

*Prerequisite: [[Paladin]]*

When you score a critical hit with a weapon attack, maximize the damage of all weapon and Divine Smite damage dice you roll for that attack.

## Favored Target

*Prerequisite: [[Ranger]]*

This endowment offers you the following benefits when attacking a creature marked with your Mystic Mark feature:

- You score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20.

- In addition to the normal extra damage from Mystic Mark, your attack deals damage equal to your PB.

- When you hit your marked enemy while it has fewer than half its total hit points, it must succeed on a CON save against your spell save DC or die. Creatures with Legendary Actions automatically succeed on this save.
