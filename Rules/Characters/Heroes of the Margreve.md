[[The Margreve]] Forest is home to a variety of peoples, many of whom have been touched by the forest's magic in some way. Whether the creatures live in harmony with the ancient forest or barely scrape a living together, the forest changes its inhabitants over time. These options suit characters born, raised, or residing within the forest.

## Where to Find It
- **Lineages:** [[Alseid Lineage|alseid]], [[Bearfolk Lineage|bearfolk]], [[Erina Lineage|erina]], and [[Piney Lineage|piney]]
- **Heritages:** [[Heritages#Delver Heritage|delver]], [[Heritages#Forest Bastion Heritage|forest bastion]], [[Heritages#Hiveheim Heritage|hiveheim]], [[Heritages#Moonlit Heritage|moonlit]], and [[Heritages#Old Ways Heritage|Old Ways]], plus the [[Erina]] and [[Piney]] languages
- **Names:** [[Random Names#Margrevian Names|Margrevian names]]
- **Background:** [[Backgrounds#Forest Dweller|Forest Dweller]]
- **Talents:** [[Magic Talents#Teleporting Harrier|Teleporting Harrier]], [[Martial Talents#Stunning Sniper|Stunning Sniper]], [[Technical Talents#Forest Denizen|Forest Denizen]], [[Technical Talents#Friend of the Fey|Friend of the Fey]], [[Technical Talents#Friend of the Forest|Friend of the Forest]], and [[Technical Talents#Relentless Hunter|Relentless Hunter]]
- **Subclasses:**
  - [[Barbarian#Hive Tender|Hive Tender]] and [[Barbarian#Shadow Chewer|Shadow Chewer]] barbarians
  - [[Bard#Wildlife|Wildlife]] bards
  - [[Cleric#Hunt|Hunt]] clerics
  - [[Druid#Bees|Bees]], [[Druid#Owls|Owls]], [[Druid#Roses|Roses]], and [[Druid#Verdant|Verdant]] druids
  - [[Ranger#Griffon Scout|Griffon Scout]], [[Ranger#Grove Warden|Grove Warden]], and [[Ranger#Spear of the Weald|Spear of the Weald]] rangers
  - [[Rogue#Underfoot|Underfoot]] rogues

  - [[Warlock#Elder Wood|Elder Wood]] and [[Warlock#Hunter in Darkness|Hunter in Darkness]] warlocks, plus new [[Warlock#Eldritch Invocations|eldritch invocations]]

- **Spells and rituals:** in the [[Spells]] and [[Rituals]] lists with all the others
- **Magic items:** in the [[Magic Items]] lists

Some options mention creatures (such as bramble creatures) whose stat blocks are in the GM's books rather than on this wiki.
