> [!note] Optional Content
> Everything from *Trade Secrets* is optional. Ask your GM before using any of it. Throughout the rules pages, material from this book is marked: *Optional, from Trade Secrets.*

*Trade Secrets* is Kobold Press's book about commerce: shopping, markets, running a business, trade goods, and the gear and magic items that go with them.

## Shopping and Business
- [[Shopping & Markets]]: the Shopping downtime activity, and how markets, auctions, the black market, fairs, and festivals work
- [[Creating a Business]]: choosing a location, a trade, inventory, a name, and security for a business of your own
- [[Running a Business]]: investing, securing supply, hiring staff, and the Running a Business downtime activity
- [[Setting Up Shop]]: financing, shop components, location, mobile and permanent shops, workshops, factories, farms, and renting

## Money and Trade
- [[Trade Goods & Money]]: how economies work, currency, stores of value, the trade goods price list, market prices, and merchant organizations
- [[Batch Crafting]]: fourteen downtime activities for brewing, farming, mining, smithing, and other trades

## Gear
- [[Adventuring Gear#More Adventuring Gear (Trade Secrets)|New adventuring gear]]: provisions, alchemical items, herbal concoctions, mechanical devices, and equipment packs
- [[Tools#More Tool Uses (Trade Secrets)|New tool uses]]: big projects, and new goods and tasks for every tool set
- [[Weapons#More Weapons (Trade Secrets)|New weapons]] and [[Armor#More Armor (Trade Secrets)|new armor]]
