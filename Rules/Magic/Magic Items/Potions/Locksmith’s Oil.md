*Potion, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Common]]* %% price pending %%

This shimmering oil can be applied to a lock. Applying the oil takes 1 minute. For 1 hour, any creature that makes a DEX check to pick the lock using thieves’ tools rolls a d4 and adds the number rolled to the check.

Part of [[Potions]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
