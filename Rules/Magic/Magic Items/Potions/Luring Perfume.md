*Potion, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 350 gp*

This pungent perfume has a woodsy and slightly musky scent. As an action, you can splash or spray the contents of this vial on yourself or another creature within 5 feet of you. For 1 minute, the perfumed creature attracts nearby humanoids and beasts.

Each humanoid and beast within 60 feet of the perfumed creature and that can smell the perfume must succeed on a DC 15 WIS save or be [[Conditions#Charmed|charmed]] by the perfumed creature until the perfume fades or is washed off with at least 1 gallon of water. While [[Conditions#Charmed|charmed]], a creature is [[Conditions#Incapacitated|incapacitated]], and, if the creature is more than 5 feet away from the perfumed creature, it must move on its turn toward the perfumed creature by the most direct route, trying to get within 5 feet. It doesn’t avoid opportunity attacks, but before moving into damaging terrain, such as lava or a pit, and whenever it takes damage, the target can repeat the save. A charmed target can also repeat the save at the end of each of its turns. If the save is successful, the effect ends on it.

Part of [[Potions]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
