*Potion, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 200 gp*

This clear liquid glitters with miniscule particles of light. A bottle of this potion contains 6 doses, and its lid comes with a built-in dropper. You can use an action to apply 1 dose to the eyes of a [[Conditions#Blinded|blinded]] creature. The [[Conditions#Blinded|blinded]] condition is suppressed for 2d4 rounds. If the blinded condition has a duration, subtract those rounds from the total duration; if doing so reduces the overall duration to 0 rounds or less, then the condition is removed rather than suppressed. This eyewash doesn’t work on creatures that are naturally blind, such as grimlocks, or creatures blinded by severe damage or removal of their eyes.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Illuminating Essence
