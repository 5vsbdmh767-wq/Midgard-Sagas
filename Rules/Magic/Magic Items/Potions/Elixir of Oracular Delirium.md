*Potion, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Common]] 100 gp*

This pearlescent fluid perpetually swirls inside its container with a slow kaleidoscopic churn. When you drink this potion, you can cast the *[[Cantrips#Guidance|guidance]]* spell for 1 hour at will. You can end this effect early as an action and gain the effects of the *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Augury|augury]]* spell. If you do, you are afflicted with short-term madness after learning the spell’s results.

Part of [[Potions]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
