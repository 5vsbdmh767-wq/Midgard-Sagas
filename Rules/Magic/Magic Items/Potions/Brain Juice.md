*Potion, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] 1,500 gp*

This foul-smelling, murky, purple-gray liquid is created from the liquefied brains of spellcasting creatures, such as aboleths. Anyone consuming this repulsive mixture must make a DC 15 INT save. On a successful save, the drinker is infused with magical power and regains 1d6 +4 expended spell slots. On a failed save, the drinker is afflicted with short-term madness for 1 day. If a creature consumes multiple doses of *brain juice* and fails three consecutive INT saves, it is afflicted with long-term madness permanently and automatically fails all further saves brought about by drinking *brain juice*.
