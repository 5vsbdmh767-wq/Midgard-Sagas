*Potion, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 100 gp*

When you drink this vibrant green, effervescent potion, you gain a finite amount of good fortune. Roll a d3 to determine where your fortune falls: ability checks (1), saves (2), or attack rolls (3). When you make a roll associated with your fortune, you can choose to tap into your good fortune and reroll the d20. This effect ends after you tap into your good fortune or when 1 hour has passed.

Part of [[Potions]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
