*Potion, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]]* %% price pending %%

This rusty-red gelatinous liquid glistens with tiny sparkling crystal flecks. The oil can coat one weapon or pieces of ammunition. Applying the oil takes 1 minute. For 1 hour, if a ghoul, ghast, or darakhul takes damage from the coated item, it takes an extra 2d6 damage of the weapon’s type.

Part of [[Potions]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
