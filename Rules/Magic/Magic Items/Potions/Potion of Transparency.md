*Potion, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Common]]* %% price pending %%

The liquid in this vial is clear like water, and it gives off a slight iridescent sheen when shaken or swirled. When you drink this potion, you and everything you are wearing and carrying turn transparent, but not completely [[Conditions#Invisible|invisible]], for 10 minutes. During this time, you have advantage on DEX (Stealth) checks, and ranged attacks against you have disadvantage.

Part of [[Potions]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
