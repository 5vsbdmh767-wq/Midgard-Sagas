*Potion, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 100 gp*

This odorless, colorless oil can cover a Medium or smaller object or creature, along with the equipment the creature is wearing or carrying (one additional vial is required for each size category above Medium). Applying the oil takes 10 minutes. For 1 hour, the affected target gives off no scent, can’t be tracked by scent, and can’t be detected with WIS (Perception) checks that rely on smell.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: scent gland

Part of [[Potions]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
