*Potion, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 100 gp*

This perfume has a sweet, floral scent and captivates those with high social standing. The perfume can cover one Medium or smaller creature, and applying it takes 1 minute. For 1 hour, the affected creature gains a +5 bonus to CHA checks made to socially interact with or influence nobles, politicians, or other individuals with high social standing.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Fragrance Gland

Part of [[Potions]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
