*Potion, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]]* %% price pending %%

The interior of this bottle is pitch black, and it feels empty. When opened, it releases a black vapor. When you inhale this vapor, your eyes go completely black. For I minute, you have darkvision out to a range of 60 feet, and you have resistance to necrotic damage. In addition, you gain a +1 bonus to damage rolls made with a weapon.

Part of [[Potions]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
