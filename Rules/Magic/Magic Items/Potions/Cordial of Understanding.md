*Potion, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Common]] 50 gp*

When you drink this tangy, violet liquid, your mind opens to new forms of communication. For 1 hour, if you spend 1 minute listening to creatures speaking a particular language, you gain the ability to communicate in that language for the duration.

This potion’s magic can also apply to non-verbal languages, such as a hand signal-based or dance-based language, so long as you spend 1 minute watching it being used and have the appropriate anatomy and limbs to communicate in the language.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Fey Tongue
