*Potion, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 200 gp*

When you drink this potion, you gain the “enlarge” effect of the *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Enlarge/Reduce|enlarge/reduce]]* spell for 1d4 hours (no concentration required). The red in the potion’s liquid continuously expands from a tiny bead to color the clear liquid around it and then contracts. Shaking the bottle fails to interrupt this process.

Part of [[Potions]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
