*Potion, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Common]] 50 gp*

When you drink this potion, your skin glows with radiance, and you are filled with joy and bliss for 1 minute. You shed bright light in a 30-foot radius and dim light for an additional 30 feet. This light is sunlight. While glowing, you are [[Conditions#Blinded|blinded]] and have disadvantage on DEX (Stealth) checks to hide. If a creature with the Sunlight Sensitivity trait starts its turn in the bright light you shed, it takes 2d4 radiant damage. This potion’s golden liquid sparkles with motes of sunlight.

Part of [[Potions]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
