*Potion, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]]* %% price pending %%

Small flecks of brown hair are suspended in this clear, syrupy liquid. When you drink this potion, you transform into a worg for 1 hour. This works like the *[[4th-Circle Spells#Polymorph|polymorph]]* spell, but you retain your INT, WIS, and CHA scores. While in worg form, you can speak normally, and you can cast spells that have only verbal components. This transformation doesn’t give you knowledge of the [[Goblins|Goblin]] or Worg languages, and you are able to speak and understand those languages only if you knew them before the transformation.

Part of [[Potions]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
