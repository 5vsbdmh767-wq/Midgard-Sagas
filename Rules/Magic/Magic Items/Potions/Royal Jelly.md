*Potion, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 100 gp*

This oil is distilled from the pheromones of queen bees and smells faintly of bananas. The oil can cover a Medium or smaller creature, along with the equipment it’s wearing and carrying. For larger creatures, one additional vial is required for each size category above Medium. Applying the oil takes 10 minutes. The affected creature then has advantage on CHA (Persuasion) checks for 1 hour.

Part of [[Potions]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
