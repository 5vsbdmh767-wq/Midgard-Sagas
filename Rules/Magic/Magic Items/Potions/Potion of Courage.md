*Potion, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 100 gp*

When you drink this potion, you cannot be [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]] for 1 hour. The liquid initially appears copper in color. Periodically, dark whorls appear, but they eventually recombine back into the coppery liquid.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Frenzying Oil

Part of [[Potions]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
