*Potion, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Common]] 10 gp*

When you drink this clear, effervescent liquid, your body becomes unnaturally buoyant for 1 hour. When you are immersed in water or other liquids, you rise to the surface (at a rate of up to 30 feet per round) to float and bob there. You have advantage on STR (Athletics) checks made to swim or stay afloat in rough water, and you automatically succeed on such checks in calm waters.

Part of [[Potions]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
