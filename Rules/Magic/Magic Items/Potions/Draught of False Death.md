*Potion, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 50 gp*

When you drink this potion, you fall into a deathlike, catatonic state for 1 hour. For the duration, you are [[Conditions#Blinded|blinded]], [[Conditions#Deafened|deafened]], and [[Conditions#Incapacitated|incapacitated]], and your speed becomes 0. You appear dead to anyone inspecting you or using magic to determine your current state. In addition, you are resistant to all damage. The effect ends early if you are subject to a *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Restoration|restoration]]* spell or similar magic.

The liquid smells strongly of mud and earth.

Part of [[Potions]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
