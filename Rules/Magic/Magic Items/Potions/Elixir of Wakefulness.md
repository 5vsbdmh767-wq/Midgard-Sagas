*Potion, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 100 gp*

This effervescent, crimson liquid is commonly held in a thin, glass vial capped in green wax. When you drink this elixir, its effects last for 8 hours. While the elixir is in effect, you can’t fall asleep by normal means. You have advantage on saves against effects that would put you to sleep. If you are affected by the *[[1st-Circle Spells#Sleep|sleep]]* spell, your current hit points are considered 10 higher when determining the effects of the spell.

Part of [[Potions]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
