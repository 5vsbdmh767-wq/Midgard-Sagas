*Potion, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Common]] 10 gp*

This magical cordial is deep red and smells strongly of fennel. You have advantage on the next save against being [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]]. The effect ends after you make such a save or when 1 hour has passed.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Idolatrous Heart

Part of [[Potions]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
