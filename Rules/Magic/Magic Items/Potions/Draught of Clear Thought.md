*Potion, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 50 gp*

When you drink this potion, you have advantage on saves against being [[Conditions#Charmed|charmed]] for 1 hour.

The liquid is cloudy, smelling strongly of garlic, rose, and hawthorn.

Part of [[Potions]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
