*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]]* %% price pending %%

When not bound to a book, this red leather book cover is embossed with images of eyes on every inch of its surface. When you wrap this cover around a tome, it shifts the book’s appearance to a plain red cover with a title of your choosing and blank pages on which you can write.

When viewing the wrapped book, other creatures see the plain red version with any contents you’ve written. A creature succeeding on a DC 15 WIS (Perception) check sees the real book and can remove the shroud.

Part of [[Books & Writing]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
