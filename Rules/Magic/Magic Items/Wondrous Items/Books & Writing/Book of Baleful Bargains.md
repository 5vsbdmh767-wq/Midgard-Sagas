*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] 25,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]] by a [[Warlock]])*

This stained tome is inscribed with hundreds of names. As an action, you can read one of these names aloud to summon an Undead—a former warlock, bound to the book by Wyrd magic. It appears within 10 feet of you and obeys your commands. It lasts until it is destroyed, until it is more than 30 feet away from you for 1 minute or more, until you become [[Conditions#Unconscious|unconscious]], or until the book closes. You can summon only one Undead at a time using this book.

Summoning a creature from the book requires expending a spell slot of a circle corresponding to the Undead you wish to summon.
