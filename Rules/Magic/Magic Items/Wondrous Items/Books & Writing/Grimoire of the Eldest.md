*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]]) 55,000 gp*

This volume details the foundations of a cult of the void and is bound in leather made from human skin. It holds the following spells: *[[5th-Circle Spells#Conjure Minor Voidborn|conjure minor voidborn]]*, *[[7th-Circle Spells#Conjure Voidborn|conjure voidborn]]*, *[[Cantrips#Corrosion|corrosion]]*, *[[Cantrips#Crushing Curse|crushing curse]]*, *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Destructive Resonance|destructive resonance]]*, *maddening* *whispers, nether weapon*, *[[1st-Circle Spells#Truth of the Void|truth of the void]]*, and *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Void Strike|void strike]]* (these spells are detailed in this chapter). If you are attuned to this book and are a wizard, you can use it as a spellbook and as an Arcane focus.

***Void Knowledge.*** The book functions as a source of alien wisdom that answers questions and prophesizes the future with frightening accuracy. As an action, you can pose one question to the book and make a DC 17 CHA save. On a success, you know the answer as if you used the *contact other* *plane* or *[[5th-Circle Spells#Legend Lore|legend lore]]* spells. On a failure, you suffer one level of void taint (see **Chapter 7**). Once used, this property of the book can’t be used again until the next dawn.

Part of [[Books & Writing]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
