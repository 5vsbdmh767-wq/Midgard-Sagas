*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 1,000 gp*

This glass vessel looks like an ordinary inkpot. A quill fashioned from an ostrich feather accompanies the inkpot. You can use an action to speak the inkpot’s command word, targeting a bookkeeper that you can see within 10 feet of you. An unwilling bookkeeper must succeed on a DC 13 CHA save or be transferred to the inkpot, making you the bookkeeper’s new “creator.” While the bookkeeper is contained within the inkpot, it suffers no harm due to being away from its bound book, but it can’t use any of its actions or traits that apply to it being in a bound book. Dipping the quill in the inkpot and writing in a book binds the bookkeeper to the new book.

If the inkpot is found as treasure, there is a 50 percent chance it contains a bookkeeper. An *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Identify|identify]]* spell reveals if a bookkeeper is inside the inkpot before using the inkpot’s ink.

Part of [[Books & Writing]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
