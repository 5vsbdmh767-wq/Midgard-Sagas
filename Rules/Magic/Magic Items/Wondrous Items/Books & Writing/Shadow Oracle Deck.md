*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Artifact]] Priceless ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

When found, this oracle deck contains 2d4 + 4 magical cards. The depiction on a card varies by the deck found, for cards often adapt their artwork to suit the preferences of their users. Only one of each type can exist in a deck. This artifact can be used as a standard oracle deck for fortune telling, but it possesses additional properties.

***Sacrifice Card.*** As an action, you can sacrifice a card for a specific effect (see **Shadow Oracle Deck** table). A sacrificed card remains in the deck, but the image on it appears lifeless and burned afterward. Any spells or effects created in this way have a DC 19, and you are their origin point.

***Shadow Whispers.*** The deck whispers to you, providing occasional insights. While attuned to the deck, you gain one additional language (GM’s discretion) and proficiency in all INT skills. If you’re already proficient in a particular INT skill, double your PB for that skill. In addition, while holding the deck, you have advantage on DEX saves.

***Spellcasting.*** The deck has a number of charges determined by the number of unsacrificed cards remaining: 1–2 cards (1 charge), 3–5 cards (2 charges), 6–8 cards (3 charges), 9–11 cards (4 charges), or 12 cards (5 charges). While holding the item, you can expend 1 or more charges to cast one of the following spells from it (spell save DC 19): *[[Rituals 7th–9th Circle#Astral Projection|astral projection]]* (4 charges), *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Augury|augury]]* (1 charge), *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Clairvoyance|clairvoyance]]* (2 charges), *[[Rituals 4th–6th Circle#Contact Other Plane|contact other plane]]* (3 charges), or *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Locate|locate]]* (1 charge). The deck regains all expended charges daily at dawn.

***Cursed.*** The whispers of the cards continue, even when you sleep. When you finish a long rest, you must succeed on a DC 19 WIS save or gain an indefinite dread effect that remains until you finish your next long rest (see [[Dread Expanded]]). Once you experience an indefinite dread effect from the deck, the same one recurs each time you fail the WIS save.
