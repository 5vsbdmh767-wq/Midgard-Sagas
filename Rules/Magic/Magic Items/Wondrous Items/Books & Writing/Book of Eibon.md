*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Legendary]] 200,090 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

This fragmentary black book is reputed to descend from the realms of [[Legendary Places of the North|Hyperborea]]. It contains puzzling guidelines for frightful necromantic rituals and maddening interdimensional travel.

The book holds the following spells: *semblance of dread*, *ectoplasm*, *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Animate Dead|animate dead]]*, *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Speak with Dead|speak with dead]]*, *emanation of Yoth*, *green decay*, *yellow sign*, *eldritch communion*, *[[Rituals 4th–6th Circle#Create Undead|create undead]]*, *[[9th-Circle Spells#Gate|gate]]*, *[[6th-Circle Spells#Harm|harm]]*, *[[Rituals 7th–9th Circle#Astral Projection|astral projection]]*, and *[[6th-Circle Spells#Void Rift|Void rift]]*. At the GM’s discretion, the book can contain other spells similarly related to necromancy, madness, or interdimensional travel.

If you are attuned to this book, you can use it as a spellbook and as an arcane focus. In addition, while holding the book, you can use a bonus action to cast a necromancy spell that is written in this tome without expending a spell slot or using any verbal or somatic components. Once used, this property of the book can’t be used again until the next dawn.

Part of [[Books & Writing]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
