*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 4,000 gp*

This book contains mnemonics and other tips to better perform a specific mental task, and its words are charged with magic. If you spend 24 hours over a period of 3 days or fewer studying the tome and practicing its instructions, you gain proficiency in the INT, WIS, or CHA-based skill (such as History, Insight, or Intimidation) associated with the book. The tome then loses its magic, but regains it in ten years.

Part of [[Books & Writing]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
