*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] 60,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

When this book is discovered, it contains 1d4 + 2 curses (GM’s discretion). The curses are garbled and unreadable: deciphering a curse requires 14 total hours of study time, spread out over no more than 30 days. Once you have deciphered a curse, you gain access to it and can use an action to attempt to inflict the curse upon another creature.

***Cursed.*** Each time you decipher a curse, you gain an indefinite dread effect. If this dread effect is removed, you lose access to the associated curse and must decipher it again to regain access. In addition, when you attempt to inflict a deciphered curse upon another creature, you must succeed on a DC 15 WIS save or gain a long-term dread effect. (See [[Dread Expanded]].)

Part of [[Books & Writing]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
