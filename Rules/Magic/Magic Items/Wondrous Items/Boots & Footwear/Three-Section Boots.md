*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 4,060 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

These boots are often decorated with eyes, flames, or other bold patterns such as lightning bolts or wheels. When you step onto water, air, or stone, you can use a reaction to speak the boots’ command word. For 1 hour, you gain the effects of the *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Meld into Stone|meld into stone]]*, water walk, or *[[6th-Circle Spells#Wind Walk|wind walk]]* spell, depending on the type of surface where you stepped. The boots can’t be used this way again until the next dawn.

Part of [[Boots & Footwear]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
