*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 4,000 gp*

*(Requires Attunement)*

While you wear these boots, you can use an action to cast the *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Levitate|levitate]]* spell from them on yourself.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Celestial Feather · Elemental Gemstone (air)

Part of [[Boots & Footwear]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
