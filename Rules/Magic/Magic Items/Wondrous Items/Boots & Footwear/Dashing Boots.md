*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 2,000 gp*

*(Requires Attunement)*

When wearing these boots, as a bonus action, you can take the Dash action.

In addition, you can make a CHA (Deception or Persuasion) check with advantage if the target can both see and hear you. Once used, this property can’t be used again until the next dawn.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Trampling Hoof

Part of [[Boots & Footwear]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
