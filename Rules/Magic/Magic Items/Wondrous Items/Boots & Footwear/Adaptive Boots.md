*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 3,300 gp*

While wearing these boots, you can ignore any natural difficult terrain as they adapt for optimal movement.

The boots have 3 charges and regain 1d3 charges daily at dawn. As a bonus action, you can expend 1 charge to be able to walk across liquid surfaces and ignore magical difficult terrain for 1 minute.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Trampling Hoof

Part of [[Boots & Footwear]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
