*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] 27,000 gp*

The furry uppers of these boots have ice and gravel matted into them that never melts. These boots have 3 charges and regain 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. While wearing the boots, you can take an action to expend 1 charge and activate them for 1 hour. While the boots are active, you can move in a straight line through the space of any creature smaller than huge. You do not provoke opportunity attacks due to this movement. The first time you enter a creature’s space during this move, it must succeed on a DC 15 STR save or be pushed ahead of you for the rest of your move.

Part of [[Boots & Footwear]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
