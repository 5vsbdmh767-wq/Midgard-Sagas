*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 2,500 gp*

These fluffy boots have wide soles and sharp spikes along the front that act as crampons. While wearing the boots, you can move through difficult terrain caused by snow or ice without expending additional movement, automatically succeed on DEX (Acrobatics) checks to avoid becoming [[Conditions#Prone|prone]], and you have advantage on STR (Athletics) checks to climb. While wearing the boots your footprints resemble those of a large cat.

Part of [[Boots & Footwear]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
