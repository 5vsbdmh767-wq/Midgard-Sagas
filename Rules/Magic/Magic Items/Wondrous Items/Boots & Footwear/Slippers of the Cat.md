*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 1,000 gp*

While you wear these fine, black cloth slippers, you have advantage on DEX (Acrobatics) checks to keep your balance. When you fall while wearing these slippers, you land on your feet, and if you succeed on a DC 13 DEX save, you take only half the falling damage.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: paw pads of a great cat

Part of [[Boots & Footwear]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
