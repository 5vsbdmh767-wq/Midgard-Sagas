*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 4,000 gp*

While wearing these rugged, hobnail boots and standing on solid ground, you can use a bonus action to root yourself to that spot. Your speed is 0 feet, and you can’t be forced to move or knocked [[Conditions#Prone|prone]] by any means. You remain firmly in place until you choose to end the effect (no action required).

Part of [[Boots & Footwear]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
