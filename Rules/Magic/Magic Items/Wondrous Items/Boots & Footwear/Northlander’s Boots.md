*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Common]] 40 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires attunement]])*

The wearer of these furred boots can use a bonus action to transform them into snowshoes, ice skates, or skis, and back again.

Part of [[Boots & Footwear]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
