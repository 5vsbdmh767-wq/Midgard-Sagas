*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 2,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

While wearing this rough, blue-gray leather cloak, you have a swimming speed of 40 feet.

When you are hit with a melee weapon attack while wearing this cloak, you can use your reaction to generate a powerful electric charge. The attacker must succeed on a DC 13 DEX save or take 2d6 lightning damage. The attacker has disadvantage on the save if it hits you with a metal weapon. The cloak can’t be used this way again until the next dawn.

Part of [[Cloaks, Robes & Clothing]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
