*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Legendary]] 100,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires attunement]])*

You are resistant to cold, lightning, and thunder damage while wearing this cloak. You also ignore any obscurement created by magical or non-magical clouds, fog, or mist. Lastly, whenever you cast a spell of 1st circle or above that deals cold, lightning, or thunder damage, you may treat the spell as if you had cast it with a spell slot 1 circle higher than the slot used.

Part of [[Cloaks, Robes & Clothing]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
