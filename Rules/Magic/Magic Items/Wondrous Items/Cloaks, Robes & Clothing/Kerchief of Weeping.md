*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 2,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

This black-lace handkerchief smells slightly of frankincense and myrrh. If you make a CHA (Persuasion) check while holding it, you can roll 1d4 and add the result to the check.

***Cursed.*** A Humanoid who holds this handkerchief must make a DC 18 WIS save. On a failure, they are overcome with sorrow and begin weeping uncontrollably. A weeping creature can repeat the save after each hour, ending the effect on a success. On a success, they are immune to this curse for 24 hours.

Part of [[Cloaks, Robes & Clothing]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
