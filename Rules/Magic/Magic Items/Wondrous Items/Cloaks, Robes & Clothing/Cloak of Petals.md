*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 2,200 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

This delicate cloak is covered in an array of pink, purple, and yellow flowers. While wearing this cloak, you have advantage on DEX (Stealth) checks made to hide in areas containing flowering plants.

The cloak has 3 charges. When a creature you can see targets you with an attack, you can use your reaction to expend 1 of its charges to release a shower of petals from the cloak. If you do so, the attacker has disadvantage on the attack roll. The cloak regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: dancing foliage petals

Part of [[Cloaks, Robes & Clothing]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
