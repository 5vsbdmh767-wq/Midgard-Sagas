*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 8,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

While wearing this midnight-blue mantle covered in writhing runes, you gain a +1 bonus to saves, and if you succeed on a save against a spell that allows you to make a save to take only half the damage or suffer partial effects, you instead take no damage and suffer none of the spell’s effects.

***Void-Powered Strike.*** You can use a bonus action to infuse your next spell or attack with power from the Void. Choose one creature that failed its save against your spell or that was hit by your attack. That creature is [[Conditions#Stunned|stunned]] until the end of its next turn. Once used, you can’t use this property again until the next dawn.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: void giant rune-covered flesh · Void Essence

Part of [[Cloaks, Robes & Clothing]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
