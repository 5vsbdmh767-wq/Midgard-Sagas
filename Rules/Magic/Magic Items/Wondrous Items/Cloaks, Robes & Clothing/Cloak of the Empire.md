*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 2,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]] by a Creature of Non-good Alignment)*

This voluminous grey cloak has bright red trim and the sigil from an unknown empire on its back. The cloak is stiff and doesn’t fold as easily as normal cloth. Whenever you are struck by a ranged weapon attack, you can use a reaction to reduce the damage from that attack by your CHA modifier (minimum of 1).

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Abnormal Leather

Part of [[Cloaks, Robes & Clothing]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
