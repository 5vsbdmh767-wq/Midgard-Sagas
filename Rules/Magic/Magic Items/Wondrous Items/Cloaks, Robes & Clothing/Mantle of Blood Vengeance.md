*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 1,100 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

This red silk cloak has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. While wearing it, you can visit retribution on any creature that dares spill your blood. When you take piercing, slashing, or necrotic damage from a creature, you can use a reaction to expend 1 charge to turn your blood into a punishing spray. The creature that damaged you must make a DC 13 DEX save, taking 2d10 acid damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

Part of [[Cloaks, Robes & Clothing]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
