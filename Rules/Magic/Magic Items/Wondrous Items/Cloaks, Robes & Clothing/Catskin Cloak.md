*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 8,000 gp*

*(Requires Attunement)*

These cloaks are made from the pelts of various large cats. While wearing one, you gain darkvision to a range of 60 feet and advantage on WIS (Perception) checks made using smell. If you already have darkvision, wearing the cloak increases its range by 30 feet. You also gain the following properties unique to the type of cloak.

***Cheetah.*** Your walking speed increases by 10 feet.

***Lion.*** As an action, you can make a thunderous roar in a 15-foot cone. Each creature in the area must succeed on a DC 15 CON save or have disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn.

***Panther.*** You gain a climbing speed of 30 feet.

***Snow Leopard.*** You have resistance to cold damage. In addition, you and your equipment are unharmed by temperatures as low as −50 degrees Fahrenheit.

***Tiger.*** You have advantage on DEX (Stealth) checks.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: large cat pelt · Sensitive Whisker

Part of [[Cloaks, Robes & Clothing]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
