*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Legendary]] 200,040 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

This black cloak appears to be made of pure shadow and shrouds you in darkness. While wearing it, you gain the following benefits:

- You have advantage on DEX (Stealth) checks.

- You have resistance to necrotic damage.

- You can cast the darkness and *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Misty Step|misty step]]* spells from it at will. Casting either spell from the cloak requires an action. Instead of a silvery mist when you cast *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Misty Step|misty step]]*, you are engulfed in the darkness of the cloak and emerge from the cloak’s darkness at your destination.

- You can use an action to cast the *[[4th-Circle Spells#Black Tentacles|black tentacles]]* or living shadows spell from it. The cloak can’t be used this way again until the following dusk.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Devil’s Eyes · Shadowporting Blood · Eternal’s Humor

Part of [[Cloaks, Robes & Clothing]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
