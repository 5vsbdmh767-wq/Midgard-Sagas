*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])* %% price pending %%

While wearing this gray garment, you have a +5 bonus to your passive WIS (Perception) score.

***Rat Form.*** While wearing this cloak, you can use an action to cast *[[4th-Circle Spells#Polymorph|polymorph]]* on yourself, transforming into a rat. While you are in the form of the rat, you retain your INT, WIS, and CHA scores. In addition, you don’t need to maintain concentration on the spell, and the transformation lasts for 1 hour, anti you use a bonus action to revert to your normal form, or until you drop to 0 hit points or die. Once used, this property of the cloak can’t be used again until the next dawn.

***Shrink.*** While wearing this cloak, you can use a bonus action to shrink in size as if you gained the “reduce” effect of the *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Enlarge/Reduce|enlarge/reduce]]* spell until the end of your next turn. Once used, this property of the cloak can’t be used again until the next dawn.

Part of [[Cloaks, Robes & Clothing]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
