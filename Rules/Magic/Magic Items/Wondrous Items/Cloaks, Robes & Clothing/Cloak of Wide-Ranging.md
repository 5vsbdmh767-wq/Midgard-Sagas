*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Common]] 75 gp*

While wearing this wolf-skin cloak, you have advantage on CON saves to avoid levels of [[Conditions#Exhaustion|exhaustion]] after traveling for more than 8 hours in a day.

Part of [[Cloaks, Robes & Clothing]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
