*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]]) 2,000 gp*

This cloak imparts on you the heightened senses and awareness of Beasts, and it can be used to camouflage yourself in grass and trees. While wearing this cloak, you have advantage on WIS (Perception) checks that rely on smell, and you have advantage on initiative rolls.

You can use an action while in terrain with ample plant life, such as a grassland, forest, or fungal forest, to become [[Conditions#Invisible|invisible]] for 1 minute. The effect ends early if you attack or cast a spell. Once you have used the cloak to become [[Conditions#Invisible|invisible]], you can't do so again until the next dawn.

Part of [[Cloaks, Robes & Clothing]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
