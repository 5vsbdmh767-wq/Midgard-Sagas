*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] 60,000 gp*

*(Requires Attunement)*

While you wear this cloak, it projects an illusion that makes you appear to be standing in a place near your actual location, causing any creature to have disadvantage on attack rolls against you. If you take damage, the illusion ceases to function until the start of your next turn. This effect is suppressed while you are [[Conditions#Incapacitated|incapacitated]], [[Conditions#Restrained|restrained]], or otherwise unable to move.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Photoadaptive Hide · Scintillating Hide · Shadowporting Blood

Part of [[Cloaks, Robes & Clothing]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
