*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])* %% price pending %%

This wool brocade cloak features a repeating pattern of squirrel heads and tree branches. It has 3 charges and regains all expended charges daily at dawn.

While wearing this cloak, you can use an action to expend 1 charge to cast the *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Legion of Rabid Squirrels|legion of rabid squirrels]]* spell from it. You don’t need to be in a forest to cast the spell from this cloak, as the squirrels come from within the cloak. When the spell ends, the swarm vanishes back inside the cloak.

Part of [[Cloaks, Robes & Clothing]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
