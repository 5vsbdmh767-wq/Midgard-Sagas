*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 1,000 gp*

This dun-colored, well-worn silk wrap is long enough to cover the face and head of a Medium or smaller humanoid, barely revealing the eyes.

While wearing this headscarf over your mouth and nose, you have advantage on ability checks and saves against being [[Conditions#Blinded|blinded]] and against extended exposure to hot weather and hot environments. Pulling the headscarf on or off your mouth and nose requires an action.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Shrouding Hide (desert)

Part of [[Cloaks, Robes & Clothing]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
