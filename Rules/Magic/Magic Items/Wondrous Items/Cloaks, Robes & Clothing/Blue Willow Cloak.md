*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 4,000 gp*

This light cloak of fey silk is waterproof. While wearing this cloak in the rain, you can use your action to pull up the hood and become [[Conditions#Invisible|invisible]] for up to 1 hour. The effect ends early if you attack or cast a spell, if you use an action to pull down the hood, or if the rain stops. The cloak can't be used this way again until the next dawn.

Part of [[Cloaks, Robes & Clothing]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
