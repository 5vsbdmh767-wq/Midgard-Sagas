*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 16,010 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

This elaborate headpiece is adorned with small gemstones and thin strips of gold that frame your head like a radiant halo. While you wear this headdress, you have advantage on CHA (Intimidation) and CHA (Persuasion) checks. The headdress has 5 charges for the following properties. It regains all expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the headdress becomes a nonmagical, tawdry ornament of cheap metals and paste gems.

***Command.*** While wearing the headdress, you can use an action to expend 1 or more charges to cast the *[[1st-Circle Spells#Command|command]]* spell (save DC 15) from it. For 1 charge, you cast the 1st-circle version of the spell. You can increase the spell’s circle by one for each additional charge you expend.

***Enchanting Sovereign.*** When you cast an enchantment spell while wearing the headdress, you can use a bonus action to expend 1 of its charges to strengthen the spell. While the spell is strengthened, each target has disadvantage on the save. If you are also attuned to and holding a *[[Scepter of Majesty|scepter of majesty]]*, the save DC of the strengthened spell increases by 1.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Aberrant Brain · Celestial Feather

Part of [[Cloaks, Robes & Clothing]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
