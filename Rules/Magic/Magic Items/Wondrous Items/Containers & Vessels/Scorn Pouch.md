*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]]) 4,000 gp*

As the heart of a lover scorned turns black and potent, so too does this pouch when enemies approach. While wearing or carrying this small, leather pouch, you can't be [[Conditions#Surprised|surprised]] by a creature within 30 feet of you, as the pouch darkens from brown to black and turns cold to the touch when a creature hostile to you moves to a space within 30 feet of you.

Part of [[Containers & Vessels]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
