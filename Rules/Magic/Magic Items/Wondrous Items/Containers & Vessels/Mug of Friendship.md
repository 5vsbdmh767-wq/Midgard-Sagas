*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Common]] 90 gp*

You can use an action to enchant any potable liquid within this flagon. A creature that drinks the enchanted liquid is friendly to you, and you have advantage on CHA (Persuasion) checks against it for 1 hour. After the hour is over, the creature is still friendly, though its attitude can change based on your behavior.

After it has been used to enchant liquid once, it cannot be used again until the next dawn.

Part of [[Containers & Vessels]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
