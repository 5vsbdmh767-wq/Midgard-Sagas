*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] 17,500 gp*

This unassuming leather flask is as powerful as it is convenient and random. As a bonus action, you can drink from the flask, imbibing a single dose of a random potion that is determined by a d100 roll on the **Random Potions** table. The flask can’t be used again until the next dawn.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Eruptive Sac · Laughing Fruit · Bezoar

Part of [[Containers & Vessels]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
