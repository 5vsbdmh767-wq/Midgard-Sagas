*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Common]] 25 gp*

This large, steel stock pot has arcane symbols etched into its bottom. No matter how wet, windy, cold, or high in elevation you are, you can use this pot to cook food with no campfire, stove, or other heat source required.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Fire Heart

Part of [[Containers & Vessels]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
