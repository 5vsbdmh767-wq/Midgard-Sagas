*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 2,200 gp*

*(Requires Attunement)*

This nondescript wooden goblet can hold up to a quart of liquid. While you are attuned to the item, it will not spill any liquid within it unless you tip it upside down.

The goblet has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. As a bonus action, you can expend 1 charge to determine if the liquid inside the goblet is [[Conditions#Poisoned|poisoned]] or diseased (indicated by the goblet subtly warming at the stem. As an action, you can expend 2 charges to have the goblet remove any poisons and diseases polluting liquid inside it.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Bezoar · Poison Coil

Part of [[Containers & Vessels]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
