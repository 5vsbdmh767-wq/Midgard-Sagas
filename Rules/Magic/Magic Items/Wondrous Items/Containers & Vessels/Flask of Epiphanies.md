*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Legendary]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])* %% price pending %%

This flask is made of silver and cherry wood, and it holds finely cut garnets on its faces. This ornate flask contains ounces of powerful alcoholic spirits. As an action, you can drink up to 5 ounces of the flask’s contents. You can drink 1 ounce without risk of intoxication. When you drink more than 1 ounce of the spirits as part of the same action, you must make a DC 12 CON save (this DC increases by 1 for each ounce you imbibe after the second, to a maximum of DC 15). On a failed save, you are [[Conditions#Incapacitated|incapacitated]] for 1d4 hours and gain no benefits from consuming the alcohol. On a success, your INT or WIS (your choice) increases by 1 for each ounce you consumed. Whether you fail or succeed, your DEX is reduced by 1 for each ounce you consumed. The effect lasts for 1 hour. During this time, you have advantage on all INT (Arcana) and INT (Religion) checks. The flask replenishes 1d3 ounces of spirits daily at dawn.

Part of [[Containers & Vessels]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
