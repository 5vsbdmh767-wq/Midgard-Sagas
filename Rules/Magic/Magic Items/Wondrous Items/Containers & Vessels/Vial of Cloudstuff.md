*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 1,000 gp*

This transparent vial contains two miniature, white, fluffy clouds. As a reaction when you or a creature within 30 feet of you falls, you can target a point within 30 feet and release one of the clouds, which then spreads out into a 20-foot cube centered on that point. Any creatures falling through the cloud treat their total falling distance as 60 feet shorter. The area is also heavily obscured. It lasts for 1 minute or until a wind of moderate or greater speed (at least 10 miles per hour) disperses it, after which it reappears in the vial as a gray cloud. Once used, a cloud can’t be used again until the next dawn.

Part of [[Containers & Vessels]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
