*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Common]] 55 gp*

This item appears a seemingly ordinary small wooden box with a brass latch. It has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. As an action, you can expend 1 charge to open the box and designate a 5-foot area within 500 feet of you, where a shrill screaming emanates. This effect lasts for 1 minute or until you use an action to close the box.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Imitative Vocal Cords

Part of [[Containers & Vessels]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
