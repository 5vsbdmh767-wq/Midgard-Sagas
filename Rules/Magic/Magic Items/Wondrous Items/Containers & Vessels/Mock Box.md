*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])* %% price pending %%

While you hold this small, square contraption, you can use an action to target a creature within 60 feet of you that can hear you. The target must succeed on a DC 13 CHA save or attack rolls against it have advantage until the start of its next turn.

Part of [[Containers & Vessels]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
