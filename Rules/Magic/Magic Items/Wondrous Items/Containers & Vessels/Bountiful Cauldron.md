*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Common]]* %% price pending %%

If this small, copper cauldron is filled with water anda half pound of meat, vegetables, or other foodstuffs then placed over a fire, it produces a simple, but hearty stew that provides one creature with enough nourishment to sustain it for one day. As long as the food is kept within the cauldron with the lid on, the food remains fresh and edible for up to 24 hours, though it grows cold unless reheated.

Part of [[Containers & Vessels]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
