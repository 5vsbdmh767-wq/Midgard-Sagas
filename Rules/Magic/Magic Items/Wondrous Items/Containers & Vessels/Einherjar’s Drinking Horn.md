*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 3,400 gp*

This ornately carved drinking horn has 6 charges and regains 1d6 charges each day at dawn. It appears empty, but you can take an action and expend a charge while putting it to your lips to drink a draught of delicious mead. You are invigorated, gaining enough nourishment to sustain yourself for a day. Alternatively, you can take an action and expend 6 charges to drink from the horn and gain the same benefits, and the horn removes 1 level of exhaustion from you. You cannot benefit from this reduction in exhaustion more than two days in a row.

Part of [[Containers & Vessels]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
