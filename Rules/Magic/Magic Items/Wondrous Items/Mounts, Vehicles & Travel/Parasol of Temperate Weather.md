*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]]* %% price pending %%

This fine, cloth-wrapped 2-foot-long pole unfolds into a parasol with a diameter of 3 feet, which is large enough to cover one Medium or smaller creature. While traveling under the parasol, you ignore the drawbacks of traveling in hot weather or a hot environment. Though it protects you from the sun’s heat in the desert or geothermal heat in deep caverns, the parasol doesn’t protect you from damage caused by super-heated environments or creatures, such as lava or an azer’s Heated Body trait, or magic that deals fire damage, such as the *[[Cantrips#Fire Bolt|fire bolt]]* spell.

Part of [[Mounts, Vehicles & Travel]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
