*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 4,000 gp*

You can affix this bright-blue sail to any vehicle to improve it. A vehicle fitted with this sail increases its speed by 10 feet in combat, or 1 mph (24 miles) out of combat, and its damage threshold by 5.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Elemental Gemstone (air)

Part of [[Mounts, Vehicles & Travel]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
