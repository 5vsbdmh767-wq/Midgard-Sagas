*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])* %% price pending %%

This worked leather collar has stitching in the shapes of various animals. While a beast wears this collar, its base AC becomes 13 + its DEX modifier. It has no effect if the beast’s base AC is already 13 or high This collar affects only beasts, which can includea creature affected by the *[[4th-Circle Spells#Polymorph|polymorph]]* spell or a druid assuming a beast form using Wild Shape.

Part of [[Mounts, Vehicles & Travel]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
