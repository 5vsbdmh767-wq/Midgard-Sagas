*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 17,600 gp*

The surface of these heavy horseshoes roils with dark storm clouds, leaving whispers of fog as the shod creature runs. While all four shoes are affixed to the hooves of a horse or similar creature, and that creature hits with a Hoof or similar attack, the target takes an extra 1d6 thunder damage.

The horseshoes have 3 charges and regain 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. While riding a creature shod with these horseshoes, you can use an action to expend one charge to amplify the sound of your mount’s hooves into the thunderous pounding of a cavalry charge until the start of your next turn. If your mount moves at least 10 feet, each creature within 60 feet of your mount when it stops moving must succeed on a DC 15 WIS save or be [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]] of you and your mount for 1 minute. While [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]], a creature must take the Dash action and move away from you by the safest available route on each of its turns, unless there is nowhere to move. An affected creature can repeat the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. This thunderous charge can be heard for 1 mile.

Part of [[Mounts, Vehicles & Travel]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
