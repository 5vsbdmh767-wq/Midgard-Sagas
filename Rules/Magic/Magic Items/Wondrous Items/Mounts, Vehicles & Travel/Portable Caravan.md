*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Legendary]] 125,000 gp*

This set of festively painted brass figurines includes horses, mules, camels, and a dozen wagons. If you spend an hour arranging them on the ground as you speak the command words, the figurines transform into normal-sized versions of the things they represent. In the case of the animals, they are standard live specimens, though effectively tireless. Each of the wagons is a Large object with AC 18, 100 HP, and a cargo capacity of 500 pounds. The caravan moves at a pace of 96 miles per day, provided a creature is on hand to direct the animals.

The caravan reverts to brass figurines 2 weeks after it is activated, but you can spend a minute speaking a series of command words to retain an existing caravan for another 2 weeks. Once the caravan becomes inert, you must wait a week before using its magic again.

Part of [[Mounts, Vehicles & Travel]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
