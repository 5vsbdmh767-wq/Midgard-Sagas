*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 2,000 gp*

*(Requires Attunement)*

When you make a ranged attack with a weapon with the Thrown property, the weapon flies back to your hand immediately after the attack. In addition, when you throw any weapon or item that deals damage, such as a handaxe or alchemist’s fire, increase that damage by 1d4.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Magnetizing Agent

Part of [[Belts, Gloves & Bracers]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
