*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Common]] 100 gp*

This practical three-slot bandolier has a sewn-in bottle opener, an always-warm wax melter, and a wine key on a chain. Over the course of 1 minute, which can be done as part of a short or long rest, you can load up to three potions into the bandolier’s slots. Drinking or administering a potion loaded into the bandolier takes a bonus action instead of an action.

Part of [[Belts, Gloves & Bracers]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
