*Wondrous Item, Rarity Varies ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])* %% price pending %%

While wearing this bear head-shaped belt buckle, you can use an action to cast *[[4th-Circle Spells#Polymorph|polymorph]]* on yourself, transforming intoa type of bear determined by the type of belt buckle you are wearing. While you are in the form of a bear, you retain your INT, WIS, and CHA scores. In addition, you don’t need to maintain concentration on the spell, and the transformation lasts for 1 hour, until you use a bonus action to revert to your normal form, or until you drop to 0 hit points or die. The belt buckle can’t be used this way again until the next dawn.

***Black Honey Buckle (Uncommon).*** When you use this belt buckle to cast *[[4th-Circle Spells#Polymorph|polymorph]]* on yourself, you transform into a black bear.

***Brown Honey Buckle (Rare).*** When you use this belt buckle to cast *[[4th-Circle Spells#Polymorph|polymorph]]* on yourself, you transform into a brown bear.

***White Honey Buckle (Very Rare).*** When you use this belt buckle to cast *[[4th-Circle Spells#Polymorph|polymorph]]* on yourself, you transform into a polar bear.

Part of [[Belts, Gloves & Bracers]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
