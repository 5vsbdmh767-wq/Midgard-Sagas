*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] 28,000 gp*

This arm ring has the rough appearance of hammered iron. You can take an action to swing the arm ring at a Huge or smaller creature within 5 feet.

Make a melee attack roll with an attack bonus equal to your STR or DEX modifier plus your PB. On a hit, the arm ring coils around the target’s arm, and the magical properties of magic items held or worn by the target cease to function until you take a bonus action to issue a command that releases the arm ring.

A creature that can touch the arm ring, including the one wearing it, can take an action to make a DC 20 STR (Athletics) check to remove the arm ring. On a success, the item is removed, and the creature is freed from its effect. On a failure, any further attempts made by that creature automatically fail until 24 hours have elapsed. The arm ring can also be removed if the limb is severed or if the ring is destroyed.

Once the arm ring is used, it can’t be used again until the next dawn.

Part of [[Belts, Gloves & Bracers]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
