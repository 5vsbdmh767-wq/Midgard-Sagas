*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] 30,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

This wide leather girdle has many sewn-in pouches and holsters that hold an assortment of empty beakers and vials. Once you have attuned to the girdle, these containers magically fill with the following liquids:

- 2 flasks of alchemist’s fire
- 2 flasks of alchemist’s ice
- 2 vials of acid
- 2 jars of swarm repellent
- 1 vial of assassin’s blood poison
- 1 *[[Potion of Climbing|potion of climbing]]*
- 1 *[[Potion of Healing|potion of healing]]*

Each container magically replenishes each day at dawn, if you are wearing the girdle. All the potions and alchemical substances produced by the girdle lose their properties if they’re transferred to another container before being used. %% alchemist’s ice and swarm repellent come from another Kobold Press book and aren’t on the wiki yet %%

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Eruptive Sac · Acrid Slag · Astralsupial Pouch

Part of [[Belts, Gloves & Bracers]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
