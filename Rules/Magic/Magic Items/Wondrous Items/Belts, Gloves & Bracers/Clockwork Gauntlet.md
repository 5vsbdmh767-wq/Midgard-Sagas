*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 1,100 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

This metal gauntlet has a steam-powered ram built into the greaves. It has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. While wearing the gauntlet, you can expend 1 charge as a bonus action to force the ram in the gauntlets to slam a creature within 5 feet of you. The ram thrusts out from the gauntlet and makes its attack with a +5 bonus. On a hit, the target takes 2d8 bludgeoning damage, and it must succeed on a DC 13 CON save or be [[Conditions#Stunned|stunned]] until the end of its next turn.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Whirling Gizmo

Part of [[Belts, Gloves & Bracers]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
