*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])* %% price pending %%

These hand wraps are made of flexible beeswax that ooze sticky honey. While wearing these gloves, you have advantage on grapple checks. In addition, creatures [[Conditions#Grappled|grappled]] by you have disadvantage on any checks made to escape your grapple. >

Part of [[Belts, Gloves & Bracers]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
