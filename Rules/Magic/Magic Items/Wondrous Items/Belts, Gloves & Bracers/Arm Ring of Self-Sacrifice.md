*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 8,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires attunement]])*

This arm ring is mildly painful to wear. It has 5 charges and regains 1d4 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn. While you are wearing it, you can use its charges as follows:

***Aggressive Strike.*** When you hit a creature with an attack, you can expend 2 charges to deal maximum damage with the attack. However, you also take force damage equal to the minimum possible damage of the attack, and you cannot reduce or mitigate this damage in any way.

***Redirection.*** When a creature you can see makes an attack roll against an ally within 5 ft. of you, you can use your reaction and expend 1 charge to become the target of the attack instead.

Part of [[Belts, Gloves & Bracers]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
