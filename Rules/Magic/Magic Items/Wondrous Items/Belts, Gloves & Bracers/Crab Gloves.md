*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])* %% price pending %%

These gloves are shaped like crab claws but fit easily over your hands. While wearing these gloves, you can take the Attack action to make two melee weapon attacks with the claws. You are proficient with the claws. Each claw has a reach of 5 feet and deals bludgeoning damage equal to 1d6 + your STR modifier on a hit. If you hit a creature of your size or smaller using a claw, you automatically grapple the creature with the claw. You can have no more than two creatures [[Conditions#Grappled|grappled]] in this way at a time. While grappling a target with a claw, you can’t attack other creatures with that claw.

While wearing the gloves, you have disadvantage on CHA and DEX checks, but you have advantage on checks while operating an *[[Apparatus of the Crab|apparatus of the crab]]* and on attack rolls with the apparatus’ claws. In addition, you can’t wield ‘ weapons or a shield, and you can’t cast a spell that has a somatic component.

A creature with an INT of 8 or higher that has two claws can wear these gloves. If it does so, it has two appropriately sized humanoid hands instead of claws. The creature can wield weapons with the hands and has advantage on DEX checks that require fine manipulation.

Pulling the gloves on or off requires an action.

Part of [[Belts, Gloves & Bracers]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
