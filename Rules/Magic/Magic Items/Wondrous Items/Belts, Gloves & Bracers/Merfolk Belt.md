*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 2,000 gp*

*(Requires Attunement)*

While wearing this belt, as a bonus action, you can transform the lower half of your body into a fish’s tail (or to end the transformation). While transformed in this way, you can breathe underwater, you have a swimming speed of 40 feet, your walking speed is 10 feet, and you can use a bonus action—including the bonus action you use to activate the belt—to take the Dash or Disengage action. You can use the belt in this way for up to 1 hour, all at once or in shorter durations, each one using a minimum of 1 minute from the duration.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Flippers

Part of [[Belts, Gloves & Bracers]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
