*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Common]] 25 gp*

This wide belt has short, soft grey fur on both its inner and outer faces, with a long strap bearing many more punch holes than an ordinary belt. While wearing this belt, you don’t have to consume as much food or water. You can go an additional number of days equal to your CON modifier before you suffer the effects of starvation or dehydration.

Part of [[Belts, Gloves & Bracers]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
