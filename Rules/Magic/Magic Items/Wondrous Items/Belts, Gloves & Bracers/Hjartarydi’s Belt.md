*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 4,400 gp*

Touching this blood-red leather belt fills you with a sense of comfort. While you have the belt equipped, you have advantage on saves against spells or effects that make you [[Conditions#Charmed|charmed]] or [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]].

Part of [[Belts, Gloves & Bracers]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
