*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 8,000 gp*

*(Requires Attunement)*

While wearing these bracers, you gain a +2 bonus to AC if you are wearing no armor and using no shield.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Insectoid Carapace · Thick Hide

Part of [[Belts, Gloves & Bracers]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
