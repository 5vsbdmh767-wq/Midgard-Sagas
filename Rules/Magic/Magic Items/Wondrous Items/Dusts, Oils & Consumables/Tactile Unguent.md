*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Common]]* %% price pending %%

Cat burglars, gearworkers, locksmiths, and even street performers use this gooey substance to increase the sensitivity of their hands. When found, a container contains 1d4 + I doses. As an action, one dose can be applied to a creature’s hands. For 1 hour, that creature has advantage on DEX (Sleight of Hand) checks and on tactile WIS (Perception) checks.

Part of [[Dusts, Oils & Consumables]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
