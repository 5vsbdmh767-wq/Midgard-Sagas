*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] 300 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

When found, this crystal vial of perfume contains 1d6 + 2 uses. As an action, you can apply 1 use to yourself, and for 8 hours, you have advantage on all Charisma checks. The duration ends early if you fully submerge yourself in water or otherwise wash the perfume off.

***Cursed.*** For the duration, enemies within 15 feet of you prioritize targeting you until you are reduced to 0 HP. If you regain hit points before the duration expires, this trait remains active.

Part of [[Dusts, Oils & Consumables]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
