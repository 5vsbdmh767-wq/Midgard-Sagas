*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Common]] 25 gp*

You can use an action to spread this salt on 5-foot square area within 10 feet of you. Ice up to 1-foot thick in that area instantly melts.

Part of [[Dusts, Oils & Consumables]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
