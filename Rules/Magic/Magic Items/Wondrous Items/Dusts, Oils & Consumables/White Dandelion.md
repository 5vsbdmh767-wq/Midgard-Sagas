*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Common]] 10 gp*

When you are attacked or are the target of a spell while holding this magically enhanced flower, you can use a reaction to blow on the flower. It explodes in a flurry of seeds that distracts your attacker, and you add 1 to your AC against the attack or to your save against the spell. Afterwards, the flower wilts and becomes nonmagical.

Part of [[Dusts, Oils & Consumables]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
