*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 200 gp*

Normally found in a small glass jar containing 1d3 doses, this foul-smelling, greasy yellow substance is made by hags in accordance with an age-old secret recipe. You can use an action to rub one dose of the wax onto an ordinary broom or wooden stick and transform it into a *[[Broom of Flying|broom of flying]]* for 1 hour.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Hag’s Blood

Part of [[Dusts, Oils & Consumables]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
