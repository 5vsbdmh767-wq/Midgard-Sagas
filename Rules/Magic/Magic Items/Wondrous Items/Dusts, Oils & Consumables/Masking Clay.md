*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 150 gp*

This unremarkable gray clay is typically found in a small glass jar. You can apply the clay to your face over the course of 1 minute. Once applied, you can use an action to alter your appearance as per the *[[1st-Circle Spells#Disguise Self|disguise self]]* spell, except the duration lasts for 24 hours or until you finish a long rest (whichever comes first). If you don’t activate the clay’s properties within 1 hour of application, the magic of the clay fades and it becomes useless.

A single jar of *masking clay* contains enough material for one use.

Part of [[Dusts, Oils & Consumables]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
