*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 350 gp*

This small packet contains soot-like dust. There is enough of it for one use. When you use an action to blow the choking dust from your palm, each creature in a 30-foot cone must make a DC 15 DEX save, taking 3d10 necrotic damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. A creature that fails this save can’t speak until the end of its next turn as it chokes on the dust.

Alternatively, you can use an action to throw the dust into the air, affecting yourself and each creature within 30 feet of you with the dust.

Part of [[Dusts, Oils & Consumables]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
