*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]]* %% price pending %%

Fashioned from the stomach lining of a devil shark, this rubbery pellet is cold to the touch. When you consume the pellet, you feel bloated, and you are immune to cold damage for 1 hour. Once before the duration ends, you can expel a 30-foot cone of cold water. Each creature in the cone must make a DC 15 CON save. On a failure, the creature takes 6d8 cold damage and is pushed 10 feet away from you. On a success, the creature takes half the damage and isn’t pushed away.

Part of [[Dusts, Oils & Consumables]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
