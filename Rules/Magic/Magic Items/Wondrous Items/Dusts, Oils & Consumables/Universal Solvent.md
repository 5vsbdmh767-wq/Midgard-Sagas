*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Legendary]] 10,500 gp*

This tube holds milky liquid with a strong alcohol smell. You can use an action to pour its contents onto a surface within reach. The liquid instantly dissolves up to 1 square foot of adhesive it touches, including *[[Sovereign Glue|sovereign glue]]*.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Stone-Eating Acid · Slippery Slime

Part of [[Dusts, Oils & Consumables]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
