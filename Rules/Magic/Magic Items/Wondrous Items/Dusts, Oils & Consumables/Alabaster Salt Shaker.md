*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 650 gp*

This shaker is carved from purest alabaster in the shape of an owl. It is 7 inches tall and contains enough salt to flavor 25 meals. When the shaker is empty, it can’t be refilled, and it becomes nonmagical. When you or another creature eat a meal salted by this shaker, you don’t need to eat again for 48 hours, at which point the magic wears off. If you don’t eat within 1 hour of the magic wearing off, you gain one level of [[Conditions#Exhaustion|exhaustion]]. You continue gaining one level of exhaustion for each additional hour you don’t eat.

Part of [[Dusts, Oils & Consumables]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
