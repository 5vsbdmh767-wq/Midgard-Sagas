*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 350 gp*

This small packet contains black pollen from a gloomflower. Hazy images swirl around the pollen when observed outside the packet. There is enough of it for one use. When you use an action to blow the dust from your palm, each creature in a 30-foot cone must make a DC 15 WIS save. On a failure, a creature sees terrible visions, manifesting its fears and anxieties for 1 minute. While affected, it takes 2d6 psychic damage at the start of each of its turns and must spend its action to make one melee attack against a creature within 5 feet of it, other than you or itself. If the creature can’t make a melee attack, it takes the Dodge action. The creature can repeat the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. On a success, a creature becomes [[Conditions#Incapacitated|incapacitated]] until the end of its next turn as the visions fill its mind then quickly fade.

A creature reduced to 0 hit points by the dust’s psychic damage falls [[Conditions#Unconscious|unconscious]] and is stable. When the creature regains consciousness, it is permanently plagued by hallucinations and has disadvantage on ability checks until cured by a *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Remove Curse|remove curse]]* spell or similar magic.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Gloompetal

Part of [[Dusts, Oils & Consumables]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
