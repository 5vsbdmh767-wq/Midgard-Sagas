*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]]* %% price pending %%

This jar is made of beaten metal and engraved with honeybees. It has 7 charges, and it regains 1d6 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the jar’s last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the jar shatters and loses all its magical properties.

While holding the jar, you can use an action to expend 1 charge to hurla glob of honey at a target within 30 feet of you. Make a ranged attack roll with an attack bonus equal to your DEX modifier plus your PB. On a hit, the glob expands, and the creature is [[Conditions#Restrained|restrained]].

A creature restrained by the honey can use an action to make a DC 15 STR (Athletics) or DEX (Acrobatics) check (target’s choice). On a success, the creature is no longer restrained by the honey.

Part of [[Dusts, Oils & Consumables]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
