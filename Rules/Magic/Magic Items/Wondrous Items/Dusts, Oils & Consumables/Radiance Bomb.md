*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]]* %% price pending %%

This small apple-sized globule is made from a highly reflective silver material and has a single, golden rune etched on it. Typically, 1d4 + 4 radiance bombs are found together.

You can use an action to throw the globule up to 60 feet. The globule explodes on impact and is destroyed. Each creature within a 10-foot radius of where the globule landed must make a DC 13 DEX save. On a failure, a creature takes 3d6 radiant damage and is [[Conditions#Blinded|blinded]] for 1 minute. On a success, a creature takes half the damage and isn’t blinded. A blinded creature can make a DC 13 CON save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

Part of [[Dusts, Oils & Consumables]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
