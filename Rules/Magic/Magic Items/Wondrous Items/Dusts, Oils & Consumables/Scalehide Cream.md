*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]]* %% price pending %%

As an action, you can rub this dull green cream over your skin. When you do, you sprout thick, olive-green scales like those of a giant lizard or green dragon that last for 1 hour. These scales give you a natural AC of 15 + your CON modifier. This natural AC doesn’t combine with any worn armor or with a DEX bonus to AC.

A jar of scalehide cream contains 1d6 + 1 doses.

Part of [[Dusts, Oils & Consumables]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
