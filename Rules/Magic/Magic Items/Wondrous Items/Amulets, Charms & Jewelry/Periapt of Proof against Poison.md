*Wondrous Item, Rare 500 gp* This delicate silver chain has a brilliant-cut black and green gem pendant. While you wear it, poisons have no effect on you. You are immune to the poisoned condition and to poison damage.

Part of [[Amulets, Charms & Jewelry]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
