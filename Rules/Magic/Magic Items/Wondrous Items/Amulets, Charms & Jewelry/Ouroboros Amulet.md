*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 2,020 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

Carved in the likeness of a serpent swallowing its own tail, this circular jade amulet is frequently worn by serpentfolk mystics and the worshippers of dark and forgotten gods. While wearing this amulet, you have advantage on saves against being [[Conditions#Charmed|charmed]]. In addition, you can use an action to cast the *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Suggestion|suggestion]]* spell (save DC 13). The amulet can’t be used this way again until the next dawn.

Part of [[Amulets, Charms & Jewelry]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
