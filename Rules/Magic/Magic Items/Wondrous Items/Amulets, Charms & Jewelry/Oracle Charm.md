*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Common]] 100 gp*

This small charm resembles a human finger bone engraved with runes and complicated knotwork patterns. As you contemplate a specific course of action that you plan to take within the next 30 minutes, you can use an action to snap the charm in half to gain the benefit of an *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Augury|augury]]* spell. Once used, the charm is destroyed.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: a human finger bone

Part of [[Amulets, Charms & Jewelry]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
