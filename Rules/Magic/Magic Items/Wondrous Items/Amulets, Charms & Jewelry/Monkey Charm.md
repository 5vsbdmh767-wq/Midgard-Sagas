*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 550 gp*

*(Requires Attunement)*

This charm has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. When you make an ability check related to a skill you aren’t proficient in, as a bonus action, you can expend 1 charge to add your PB to the roll.

Part of [[Amulets, Charms & Jewelry]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
