*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Common]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]]) 50 gp*

This deep-blue sapphire is carved with a single eye surrounded by tentacles or wavy rays, and the amulet is always cool to the touch. While wearing this amulet, you can understand and speak Labyrinthian. In addition, you don’t need to breathe when in an airless space (but you are still affected by gases or other air-based effects when in an area that has an atmosphere).

Part of [[Amulets, Charms & Jewelry]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
