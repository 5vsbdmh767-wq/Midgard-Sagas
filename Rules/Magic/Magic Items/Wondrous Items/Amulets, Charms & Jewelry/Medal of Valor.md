*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 2,000 gp*

You are immune to the [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]] condition while you wear this medal. If you are already frightened, the effect ends immediately when you put on the medal.

Part of [[Amulets, Charms & Jewelry]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
