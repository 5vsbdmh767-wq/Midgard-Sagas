*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 14,500 gp*

This silver chain has 1d8 + 2 Humanoid teeth hanging from it. As an action, you can detach a tooth to cast one of the following spells (spell save DC 16), destroying the tooth: *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Bestow Curse|bestow curse]]*, *[[4th-Circle Spells#Banishment|banishment]]*, *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Darkness|darkness]]*, or *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Speak with Dead|speak with dead]]*.

In addition, as an action, you can detach a tooth and throw it at an unoccupied space up to 30 feet away. When you do, the tooth bursts, and a **ghost** appears in the space, friendly to you and your companions. As a bonus action on each of your turns, if the ghost is within 120 feet, you can mentally command it. You decide what action it takes and where it moves on its next turn, or you can issue a general command, such as to guard a particular corridor. If you issue no commands, it only defends itself against hostile creatures. The ghost disappears after 1 hour or when it is reduced to 0 HP. You can only have one ghost under your control in this way at a time, and if you use the necklace to summon a second ghost, the first becomes hostile toward you and your allies.

Part of [[Amulets, Charms & Jewelry]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
