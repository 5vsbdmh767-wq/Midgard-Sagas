*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 1,000 gp*

*(Requires Attunement)*

This gold coin bears a representation of comedy on one face and tragedy on the other. It is weighted such that it has an equal chance of landing on either face as well as on its edge. When attuned to the coin, as a reaction when you must make a d20 roll, instead roll three d20s and a d6:

- If the d6 results in a 1 or 2, use the lowest d20 result.

- If the d6 results in a 3 or 4, use the middle d20 result.

- If the d6 results in a 5 or 6, use the highest d20 result. You can spend Luck to change the value of the d6, rather than any of the d20s. Once used, the coin can’t be used again for 1 hour.

Part of [[Amulets, Charms & Jewelry]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
