*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 1,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

While wearing this amulet, you need to eat and drink only once every 7 days. In addition, you have advantage on saves against effects that would cause you to suffer a level of [[Conditions#Exhaustion|exhaustion]].

Part of [[Amulets, Charms & Jewelry]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
