*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]]* %% price pending %%

If you use an action to flick this fly whisk, you have advantage on CHA (Intimidation) and CHA (Persuasion) checks for 10 minutes. You can’t use the fly whisk this way again until the next dawn.

Part of [[Amulets, Charms & Jewelry]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
