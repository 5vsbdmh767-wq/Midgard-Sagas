*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 1,100 gp*

This charm has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. As a reaction when you or another creature you can see within 30 feet are [[Conditions#Charmed|charmed]], you can expend 1 charge to allow the save to be rerolled with advantage.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Magic-Absorbing Organ

Part of [[Amulets, Charms & Jewelry]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
