*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 2,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires attunement]])*

This necklace is made of carved runes strung with giant’s hair. The runes glow faintly blue. While wearing this necklace, you can use an action to learn and influence the future of one individual that you are familiar with.

You choose a type of situation from battle, diplomacy, trade, trickery, or other situation. The GM tells you if that individual is likely to encounter such a situation today. For the remainder of the day, you know when that individual is in that type of situation, and as a reaction, you may add or subtract 1d10 from a die roll they just made during that event, though you have no additional knowledge of what transpired where the individual is.

You may not use this action again until the next dawn.

Part of [[Amulets, Charms & Jewelry]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
