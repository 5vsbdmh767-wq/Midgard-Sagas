*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] 60,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])* %% cursed: listed at half price %%

The coldly beautiful and deadly [[Legendary Places of the North|Snow Queen]] grants these delicate-looking snowflake-shaped mithril talismans to her most trusted spies and servants. Each talisman is imbued with a measure of her power and is magically tied the queen. It can be affixed to any piece of clothing or worn as an amulet. While wearing the talisman, you gain the following benefits:

- You have resistance to cold damage.

- You have advantage on CHA checks when interacting socially with creatures that live in cold environments, such as frost giants, winter wolves, and fraughashar.

- You can use an action to cast the *[[Cantrips#Ray of Frost|ray of frost]]* cantrip from it at will, using your level and using

INT as your spellcasting ability.

***Blinding Snow.*** While wearing the talisman, you can use an action to create a swirl of snow that spreads out from you and into the eyes of nearby creatures. Each creature within 15 feet of you must succeed on a DC 17 CON save or be [[Conditions#Blinded|blinded]] until the end of its next turn. Once used, this property can’t be used again until the next dawn.

***Eyes of the Queen.*** While you are wearing the talisman, the Snow Queen can use a bonus action to see through your eyes if both of you are on the same plane of existence. This effect lasts until she ends it as a bonus action or until you die. You can’t make a save to prevent the Snow Queen from seeing through your eyes. However, being more than 5 feet away from the talisman ends the effect, and becoming [[Conditions#Blinded|blinded]] prevents her from seeing anything further than 10 feet away from you. When the Snow Queen is looking through your eyes, the talisman sheds an almost imperceptible pale blue glow, which you or any creature within 10 feet of you notice with a successful DC 20 WIS (Perception) check. An *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Identify|identify]]* spell fails to reveal this property of the talisman, and this property can’t be removed from the talisman except by the Snow Queen herself.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: can't be crafted; only the Snow Queen grants them.
