*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 1,000 gp*

*(Requires Attunement)*

As an action, you can affix this charm to an object weighing up to 10 pounds with its longest dimension no more than 3 feet. While the charm is affixed to an object, you can use a bonus action to teleport the object to you as long as the object is within 1 mile of you and isn’t being worn or carried by another creature. Your hand(s) must be empty to teleport the object to you.

Part of [[Amulets, Charms & Jewelry]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
