## Magical Contracts
%% source: Vault of Magic %%

Some scrolls are contracts, such as the [[Aberrant Agreement|aberrant agreement]], [[Celestial Charter|celestial charter]], [[Feysworn Contract|feysworn contract]] and [[Fiendish Charter|fiendish charter]]. Elder beings, archangels, demon lords, archdukes of Hell, fey lords and ladies, and even gods write them, and they can bind a dangerous creature into a mortal's service. Whoever wrote the contract had their own reasons. Some are only selfish, and others are evil or too alien to tell the difference. None were written with mortals' interests at heart, and even a celestial charter is risky, since catching a god's attention rarely ends well in the old stories.
