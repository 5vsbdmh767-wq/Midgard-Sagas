*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 500 gp*

This feather sheds bright light in a 20-foot radius and dim light for an additional 20 feet, but it creates no heat and doesn't use oxygen. While holding the feather, you can tolerate temperatures as low as –50 degrees Fahrenheit.

Spellcasters with access to Primordial spells and clerics and paladins who worship nature deities can use the feather as a spellcasting focus. If you are a cleric and use the feather in place of a holy symbol when using your Turn the Profane feature, Undead in the area have a –1 penalty on the save.

Part of [[Gems, Stones & Orbs]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
