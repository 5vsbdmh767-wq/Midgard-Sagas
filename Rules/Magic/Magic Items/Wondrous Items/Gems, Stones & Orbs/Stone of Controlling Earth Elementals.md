*Wondrous Item, Rare 14,500 gp* This carved stone weighs 5 pounds. If the stone is touching the ground, you can use an action to speak its command word and summon an **earth elemental**, as if you had cast the *[[5th-Circle Spells#Conjure Elemental|conjure elemental]]* spell. The stone can’t be used this way again until the next dawn.

Part of [[Gems, Stones & Orbs]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
