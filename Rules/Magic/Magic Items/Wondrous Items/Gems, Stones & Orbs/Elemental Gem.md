*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 650 gp*

This gem contains a mote of elemental energy. When you use an action to break the gem, an elemental is summoned as if you had cast the *[[5th-Circle Spells#Conjure Elemental|conjure elemental]]* spell, and the gem’s magic is lost. The type of gem determines the elemental summoned by the spell.

**Elemental Gem Type**

| Gem | Summoned Elemental |
|---|---|
| Blue sapphire | Air elemental |
| Yellow diamond | Earth elemental |
| Red corundum | Fire elemental |
| Emerald | Water elemental |

Part of [[Gems, Stones & Orbs]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
