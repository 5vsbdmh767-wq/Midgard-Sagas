*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] 5,000 gp*

This magic item is a specially treated, unfertilized cockatrice egg. As an action, you can throw the egg at a point within 60 feet, shattering it and splattering its interior over a 10-foot radius centered on that point. Each creature in the area must succeed on a DC 15 CON save or be [[Conditions#Restrained|restrained]] as it begins to turn to stone. On a failed save, the creature must repeat the save at the end of its next turn. On a successful save, the [[Conditions#Restrained|restrained]] effect ends. On a failed save, the creature is [[Conditions#Petrified|petrified]] for 24 hours. The egg is expended in the process.

Part of [[Gems, Stones & Orbs]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
