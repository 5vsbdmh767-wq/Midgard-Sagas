*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 4,000 gp*

*(Requires Attunement)*

These gems come in a variety of shapes and colors but are usually precious stones. As an action, you can target a 5-foot square you can see within 60 feet and capture images of the space for up to 1 minute. If a creature occupies this space, it must make a DC 14 CHA save. On a successful save, the image capture fails.

While the gem has images of any length stored within it, as an action, you can project those images into a 5-foot-square space you can see within 60 feet. The images captured within the gem fill the space, overlaying the space’s natural appearance and concealing any objects or creatures there. No sound, smells, temperatures, or textures are created.

Physical interaction with the image reveals it to be an illusion, for things can pass through it. A creature that uses its action to examine the image can determine that it is an illusion with a successful DC 15 INT (Investigation) check. If a creature discerns the illusion for what it is, it can see through the image to the true appearance beneath, the illusory qualities becoming only faint afterimages.
