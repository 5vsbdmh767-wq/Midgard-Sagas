*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 22,500 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]] by a [[Wizard]] or [[Cleric]])*

The bones of a void saint are dangerous tools, especially when retrieved intact. The bones are often a dark-purplish color with ends or joints of pale blue, imbued with the unholy power of the Void. While wearing or carrying an intact bone from a void saint, you have resistance to radiant damage.

In addition, you can use an action to cast the *conjure* *minor voidborn* spell (detailed in this chapter) from it. If your concentration on the spell is broken, the creatures don’t become hostile to you as long as you are wearing or carrying the *void saint bone*, but they are no longer under your control. Once used to cast this spell, you can’t use the bone to do so again until the next dusk.

Part of [[Gems, Stones & Orbs]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
