*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 2,000 gp*

This golden bracelet is set with ten glistening crystal bangles that tinkle when they strike one another. When you must make a save against being [[Conditions#Charmed|charmed]] or [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]], the crystals vibrate, creating an eerie melody, and you have advantage on the save. If you fail the save, you can choose to succeed instead by forcing one of the crystals to shatter. Once all ten crystals have shattered, the bracelet loses its magic and crumbles to powder.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Resonant Trunk

Part of [[Gems, Stones & Orbs]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
