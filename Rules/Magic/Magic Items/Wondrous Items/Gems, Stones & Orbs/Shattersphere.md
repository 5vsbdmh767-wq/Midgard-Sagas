*Wondrous Item, Varies Price Varies*

As an action, you can throw this small crystalline sphere at a point up to 60 feet away. The sphere explodes on impact and is destroyed, triggering a *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Shatter|shatter]]* spell. Unlike the normal spell, however, the item creates an interference that ensures the sound doesn’t reach past the effect’s radius. Creatures failing their save are also [[Conditions#Deafened|deafened]] for 1 minute.

The circle of the *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Shatter|shatter]]* spell determines the spell’s save DC, as well as the item’s rarity, as shown in the **Shattersphere Rarity** table.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Eruptive Sac · Ringing Claw. Use as many as the version's rarity calls for.

Part of [[Gems, Stones & Orbs]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
