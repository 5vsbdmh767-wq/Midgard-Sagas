*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] 50,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires attunement]])*

Two black bird wings sweep back from the sides of this unusual silver helm, which has an unbroken smooth plate where the right eye hole would be, though the magical properties of the helm allow you to see through it. While wearing the helm, you can use an action to gain a flying speed of 60 ft. for 1 hour. If you are flying when the duration expires, you descend at a rate of 30 feet per round until you land. While this effect is active, you may maintain concentration on two spells at once, and the plate where the right eye hole would be glows a ghostly blue. This ability cannot be used again until the next dawn.

Part of [[Headwear & Eyewear]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
