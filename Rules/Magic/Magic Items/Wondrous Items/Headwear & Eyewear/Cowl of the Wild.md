*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 500 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]] by a [[Druid]])*

If you use the Beast Form druid feature while wearing this leather cowl, you don’t automatically revert to your normal form when you become [[Conditions#Unconscious|unconscious]] or drop to 0 HP, though you do still revert if you die.

Part of [[Headwear & Eyewear]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
