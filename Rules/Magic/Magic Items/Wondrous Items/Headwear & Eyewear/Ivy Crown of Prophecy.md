*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 5,500 gp*

While wearing this ivy, filigreed crown, you can cast the divination ritual from it. The crown can't be used this way again until the next dawn.

Part of [[Headwear & Eyewear]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
