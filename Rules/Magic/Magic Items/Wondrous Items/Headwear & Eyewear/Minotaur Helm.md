*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 4,000 gp*

*(Requires Attunement)*

This leather helm is reinforced with bronze and mounted with two large horns, like those of a minotaur. While wearing this helm, if you move at least 10 feet first, you can make one melee weapon attack with the horns against a target within 5 feet of you. If the attack hits, the horns deal 2d6 piercing damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 14 STR save or be pushed up to 10 feet away from you and knocked [[Conditions#Prone|prone]]. You are proficient with this attack and use your STR modifier for the attack and damage rolls.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Trampling Hoof · Minotaur Brain

Part of [[Headwear & Eyewear]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
