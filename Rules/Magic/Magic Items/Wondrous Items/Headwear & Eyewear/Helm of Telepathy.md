*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 3,000 gp*

*(Requires Attunement)*

While wearing this helm, you can use an action to cast the *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Detect Thoughts|detect thoughts]]* spell (save DC 13) from it. As long as you maintain concentration on the spell, you can use a bonus action to send a telepathic message to a creature you are focused on. It can reply—using a bonus action to do so— while your focus on it continues.

While focusing on a creature with *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Detect Thoughts|detect thoughts]]*, you can use an action to cast the *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Suggestion|suggestion]]* spell (save DC 13) from the helm on that creature. You don’t need to concentrate to maintain *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Suggestion|suggestion]]* for the duration of the spell, but it ends if the *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Detect Thoughts|detect thoughts]]* spell ends. Once used, the *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Suggestion|suggestion]]* property can’t be used again until the next dawn.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Aberrant Brain

Part of [[Headwear & Eyewear]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
