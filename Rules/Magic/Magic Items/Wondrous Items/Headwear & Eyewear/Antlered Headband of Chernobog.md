*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Legendary]] 120,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires attunement]] by a bard, paladin, sorcerer, or warlock)*

Bone-white antlers, the tips stained with dried blood, are mounted on a tanned leather headband stained with the black ichor that oozes from the bases of the antlers.

While wearing this headband, you gain the following benefits.

***Devil’s Sight.*** You can see normally in dim light and darkness—both magical and nonmagical—within 120 ft.

***Antler Charge.*** You can make a melee weapon attack with the antlers. Add your PB and your STR modifier to the attack roll. The antlers deal 1d10 piercing damage plus your STR modifier. The antlers also have the Push weapon option.

If you move at least 15 ft. in a straight line before dealing damage to a creature with the antlers, that creature must succeed on a DC 15 CHA save or become [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]] for 1 minute. At the end of each of its turns, the [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]] target can repeat the save, ending the effect on itself on a success.

***Spells.*** The headband has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. You can take an action to expend 1 or more of its charges to cast one of the following spells from it using your spell save DC: *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Darkness|darkness]]* (1 charge), *[[8th-Circle Spells#Greater Dominate|greater dominate]]* (3 charges). You can target Undead creatures with this use of *[[8th-Circle Spells#Greater Dominate|greater dominate]]*, even if they have immunity to the [[Conditions#Charmed|charmed]] condition.

Part of [[Headwear & Eyewear]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
