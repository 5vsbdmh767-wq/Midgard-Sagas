*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 1,500 gp*

These crystal lenses fit over the eyes. They have 3 charges and regain all expended charges daily at dawn. While wearing them, you can expend 1 charge as an action to cast the *[[1st-Circle Spells#Charm|charm]]* spell (save DC 13) on a Humanoid within 30 feet of you, provided that you and the target can see each other.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Fey Tongue

Part of [[Headwear & Eyewear]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
