*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] 72,500 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

This red-lacquered wooden mask resembles a skeletal face, embellished with black and gold accents. While wearing it, you are immune to poison and disease. In addition, it has 6 charges and regains all expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the mask crumbles to dust and is destroyed.

***Plague Spreader.*** As an action while wearing the mask, you can expend 1 or more charges to cast one of the following spells (spell save DC 17): *[[5th-Circle Spells#Contagion|contagion]]* (3 charges) or *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Necrotic Pustules|necrotic pustules]]* (2 charges).

***Virulent Vapor.*** As an action, you can expend 6 charges from the mask to cause a fog of diseased vapor to spread from you in a 30-foot radius. Creatures of your choice in the area must make a DC 17 CON save. On a failure, a target takes 10d6 necrotic damage, falls [[Conditions#Prone|prone]], and is [[Conditions#Stunned|stunned]] for 1 minute as it seizes and foams at the mouth. On a success, a target takes half as much damage, doesn’t fall [[Conditions#Prone|prone]], and isn’t [[Conditions#Stunned|stunned]]. At the start of each of its turns, a stunned creature must repeat the save, taking an additional 3d6 necrotic damage on a failed save or ending the stunned effect on themselves on a success. The fog has no effect on Constructs or Undead.

Part of [[Headwear & Eyewear]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
