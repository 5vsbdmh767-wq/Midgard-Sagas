*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] 40,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]] by a [[Witch]])*

This delicate silver circlet holds a flawed moonstone. When worn, it resembles a gray eye in the middle of the wearer’s forehead. The circlet has 3 charges that can be used for the following effects. Each effect uses 1 charge. The circlet regains all expended charges at dusk.

***Illusory Appearance.*** You can cover yourself and anything you are wearing or carrying with an illusion, making you look like another creature of your general size and Humanoid shape for 8 hours. The illusion doesn’t hold up to physical inspection, similar to the *disguise self* spell. Without physical contact, a creature can take an action to make a DC 20 INT (Investigation) check. On a success, it learns that your appearance is illusory.

***Death Glare.*** As an action, select one creature you can see within 30 feet of you. That creature must succeed on a DC 15 WIS save or be [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]] until the end of its next turn. If the target is already frightened and fails the save, it takes 6d6 psychic damage instead. If reduced to 0 HP in this way, the creature is stable.

***Truesight.*** As an action, you gain truesight to a range of 60 feet, for a number of minutes equal to your PB. When this duration expires, you are [[Conditions#Blinded|blinded]] for an equal number of hours. You can end your truesight early to minimize this condition. You can also remove the blinded condition through *restoration* or similar means.

Part of [[Headwear & Eyewear]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
