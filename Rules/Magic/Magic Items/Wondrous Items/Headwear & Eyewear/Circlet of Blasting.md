*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 3,000 gp*

While wearing this circlet, you can use an action to cast the *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Scorching Ray|scorching ray]]* spell with it. When you make the spell’s attacks, you do so with an attack bonus of +5. The circlet can’t be used this way again until the next dawn.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Elemental Gemstone (fire)

Part of [[Headwear & Eyewear]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
