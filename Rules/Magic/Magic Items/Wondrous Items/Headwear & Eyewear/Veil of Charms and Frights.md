*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 4,400 gp*

*(Requires Attunement)*

When you wear this magic veil, it becomes [[Conditions#Invisible|invisible]], leaving your appearance unchanged. While attuned to the veil, it modifies your appearance when interacting with others. Choose one of the following:

- You can appear friendlier, gaining advantage on CHA (Persuasion) checks.

- You can appear more trusting, gaining advantage on CHA (Deception) checks.

- You can appear more frightening, gaining advantage on CHA (Intimidation) checks. As a bonus action, you can choose a different appearance from the list. The veil has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. As a bonus action, you can expend 1 charge to intensify the expression presented by the veil. Each creature of your choice within 30 feet that can see you must make a DC 13 WIS save:

- If you appear friendlier when you invoke this feature, on a failed save, the creature is [[Conditions#Charmed|charmed]] by you for 1 minute or until you or your companions do anything harmful to it.

- If you appear more trusting when you invoke this feature, on a failed save, you have advantage on your next ability check or attack roll targeting the creature.

- If you appear more frightening when you invoke this feature, on a failed save, the creature is [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]] of you for 1 minute. A frightened creature can repeat its save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

Part of [[Headwear & Eyewear]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
