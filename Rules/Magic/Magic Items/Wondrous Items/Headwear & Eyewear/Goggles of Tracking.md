*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 2,800 gp*

These goggles have multiple lenses that can be swiveled. The goggles have 3 charges. While wearing the goggles, you can take an action to expend a charge. For 1 hour, you can see any tracks that were obscured by natural phenomena such as rain, snow, or blowing sand in the last week. In addition, you have advantage on ability checks to track creatures. The goggles regain 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn.

Part of [[Headwear & Eyewear]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
