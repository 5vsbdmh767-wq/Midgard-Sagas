*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 1,500 gp*

*(Requires Attunement)*

While wearing this hat, you can use an action to cast the *[[1st-Circle Spells#Disguise Self|disguise self]]* spell from it. The spell ends if the hat is removed.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Scintillating Hide

Part of [[Headwear & Eyewear]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
