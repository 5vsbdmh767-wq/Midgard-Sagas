*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 2,200 gp*

This helm has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. While wearing it, as an action, you can expend 1 charge to cause the patterned disk above its faceplate to spin. Each creature of your choice within 60 feet of you, and that can see you, must make a DC 14 WIS save. On a failed save, the target becomes dizzy and unable to orient itself properly for 1 minute. If it moves more than half its walking speed in a turn, it must succeed on a DC 14 CON save or fall [[Conditions#Prone|prone]]. If the creature is flying, it has a 50 percent chance of flying in the opposite direction as intended. If this causes it to fly into the ground, it takes bludgeoning damage as if it had fallen and falls [[Conditions#Prone|prone]].

An affected creature can repeat its WIS save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

Part of [[Headwear & Eyewear]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
