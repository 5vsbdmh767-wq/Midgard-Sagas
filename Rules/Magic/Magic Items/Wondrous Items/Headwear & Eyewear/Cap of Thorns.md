*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])* %% price pending %%

Donning this thorny wooden circlet causes it to meld with your scalp. It can be removed only upon your death or by a *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Remove Curse|remove curse]]* spell. The cap ingests some of your blood, dealing 2d4 piercing damage. After this first feeding, the thorns feed once per day for 1d4 piercing damage.

Once per day, you can sacrifice 1 hit point per level you possess to cast a special *[[1st-Circle Spells#Entangle|entangle]]* spell made of thorny vines. CHA is your spellcasting ability for this effect. Restrained creatures must make a successful CHA save or be affected by a *[[1st-Circle Spells#Charm|charm]]* spell as thorns pierce their body. The target can repeat the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. If the target fails three consecutive saves, the thorns become deeply rooted and the [[Conditions#Charmed|charmed]] effect is permanent until remove curse or similar magic is cast on the target.

Part of [[Headwear & Eyewear]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
