*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 1,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

While wearing this bright yellow bandana, you have a climbing speed of 30 feet, and you gain a +5 bonus to STR (Athletics) and DEX (Acrobatics) checks to jump over obstacles, to land on your feet, and to land safely on a breakable or unstable surface, such as a tree branch or rotting wooden rafters.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Gripping Spikes

Part of [[Headwear & Eyewear]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
