*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 2,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

You gain a +1 bonus to saves while you wear this simple, black eyepatch.

In addition, if you are missing the eye that the eyepatch covers and you roll a 1 on the d20 for a save, you can reroll the die and must use the new roll. The eyepatch can’t be used this way again until the next dawn.

Part of [[Headwear & Eyewear]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
