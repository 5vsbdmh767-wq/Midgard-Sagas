*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]]) 80,000 gp*

This helmet, covered in rust and verdigris, features glowing inlays in Void Speech. The helm has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. While wearing the helm, you can use an action to cast the *[[8th-Circle Spells#Antimagic Field|antimagic field]]* spell from it, except the duration is only 1 minute and doesn’t require concentration. You can use a bonus action to end the effect early.

Part of [[Headwear & Eyewear]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
