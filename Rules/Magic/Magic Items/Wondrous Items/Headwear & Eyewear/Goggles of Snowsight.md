*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 4,200 gp*

While wearing these multifaceted blue crystal goggles, you do not have disadvantage on WIS (Perception) checks to see through areas that are lightly obscured by blowing snow, sand, or other debris.

Part of [[Headwear & Eyewear]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
