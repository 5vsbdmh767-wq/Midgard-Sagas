*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 5,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

This leather headband grants you supernatural alertness. You can’t be surprised while you wear it, even while asleep, though it has no effect if you have the [[Conditions#Unconscious|unconscious]] condition for any other reason.

Part of [[Headwear & Eyewear]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
