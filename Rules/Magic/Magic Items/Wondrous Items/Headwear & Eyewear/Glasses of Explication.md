*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 1,000 gp*

While wearing these reading glasses, you can understand and read any written language that you can see (no action required), provided the writing is complete and legible. The glasses don’t allow you to speak or understand spoken languages you hear or to write languages you don’t know know. The glasses also can’t decode secret messages hidden within written text or the meaning of magical glyphs that aren’t part of a written language (such as those created by the *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Glyph of Warding|glyph of warding]]* or *[[Rituals 7th–9th Circle#Symbol|symbol]]* spells).

For every hour that you use the glasses within the same 24 hour period, you must make a DC 13 CON save or be afflicted with painful headaches. While affected with a headache in this way, you have disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks. This effect ends once you finish a short or long rest.

Part of [[Headwear & Eyewear]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
