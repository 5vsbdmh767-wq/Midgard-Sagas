*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] 120,030 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

The deep blue pearls forming the core of these earrings come from oysters that survive being struck by lightning. While wearing these earrings, you gain the following benefits:

- You have resistance to lightning and thunder damage.

- You can understand Primordial. When it is spoken, the pearls echo the words in a language you can understand, at a whisper only you can hear.

- You can’t be [[Conditions#Deafened|deafened]].

- You can breathe air and water.

- As an action, you can cast the *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Sleet Storm|sleet storm]]* spell (save DC 15) from the earrings. The earrings can’t be used this way again until the next dawn.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Voltaic Organ · Conductive Blood · storm oyster pearls

Part of [[Headwear & Eyewear]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
