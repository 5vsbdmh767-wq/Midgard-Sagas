*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])* %% price pending %%

This painted, wooden mask bears the visage of a snarling, fiendish face. While wearing the mask, you can use a bonus action to feed on the fear of a [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]] creature within 30 feet of you. The target must succeed on a DC 13 WIS save or take 2d6 psychic damage. You regain hit points equal to the damage dealt. If you are not injured, you gain temporary hit points equal to the damage dealt instead. Once a creature has failed this save, it is immune to the effects of this mask for 24 hours.

Part of [[Headwear & Eyewear]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
