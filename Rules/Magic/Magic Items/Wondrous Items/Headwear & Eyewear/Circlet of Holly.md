*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]]) 500 gp*

While wearing this circlet, you gain the following benefits:

- ***Language of the Fey.*** You can speak and understand Sylvan.
- ***Friend of the Fey.*** You have advantage on ability checks to interact socially with Fey.
- ***Poison Sense.*** You know if any food or drink you are holding contains poison.

Part of [[Headwear & Eyewear]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
