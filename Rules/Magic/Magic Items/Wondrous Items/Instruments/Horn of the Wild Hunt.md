*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Legendary]] 45,000 gp*

This black horn appears normal during daylight, but in darkness it glitters like a starlit sky. The horn has 4 charges and regains 1d4 charges daily at sunset.

When you blow this horn, you can expend 1 charge to cast *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Conjure Animals|conjure animals]]*, or 3 charges to cast *conjure fey* (conjuring a **spectral hunter**).

Part of [[Instruments]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
