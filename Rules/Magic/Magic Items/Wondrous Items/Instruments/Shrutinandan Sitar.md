*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] 200,050 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]] by a [[Bard]])*

An exquisite masterpiece of craftsmanship, this instrument is named for a prestigious musical academy. You must be proficient with stringed instruments to use this instrument. A creature that plays the instrument without being proficient with stringed instruments must succeed on a DC 17 WIS save or take 2d6 psychic damage.

The exquisite sounds of this sitar are known to weaken the power of demons. Each creature that can hear you playing this sitar has advantage on saves against the spells and special abilities of demons.

***Spells.*** You can use an action to play the sitar and cast one of the following spells from it, using your spell save DC and spellcasting ability: *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Create Food and Water|create food and water]]*, *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Fly|fly]]*, *[[5th-Circle Spells#Insect Plague|insect plague]]*, *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Invisibility|invisibility]]*, *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Levitate|levitate]]*, *[[1st-Circle Spells#Protection from Evil and Good|protection from evil and good]]*, or *[[Rituals 4th–6th Circle#Reincarnate|reincarnate]]*. Once the sitar has been used to cast a spell, you can’t use it to cast that spell again until the next dawn.

***Summon.*** If you spend 1 minute playing the sitar, you can summon animals to fight by your side. This works like the *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Conjure Animals|conjure animals]]* spell, except you can summon only 1 elephant, 1d2 tigers, or 2d4 wolves.

Part of [[Instruments]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
