*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 2,200 gp*

You must have proficiency with instruments (woodwinds) to use these pipes. They have 3 charges. You can use an action to play them and expend 1 charge to create an eerie, spellbinding tune. Each creature within 30 feet of you that hears you play must succeed on a DC 15 WIS save or become [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]] of you for 1 minute. If you wish, all creatures in the area that aren’t hostile toward you automatically succeed on the save. A creature that fails the save can repeat it at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. A creature that succeeds on its save is immune to the effect of these pipes for 24 hours. The pipes regain 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn.

Part of [[Instruments]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
