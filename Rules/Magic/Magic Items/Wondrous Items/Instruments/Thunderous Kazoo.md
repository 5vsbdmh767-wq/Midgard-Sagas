*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 1,000 gp*

You can use an action to speak the kazoo’s command word and then hum into it, which emits a thunderous blast, audible out to 1 mile, at one Large or smaller creature you can see within 30 feet of you. The target must make a DC 13 CON save. On a failure, a creature is pushed away from you and is [[Conditions#Deafened|deafened]] and [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]] of you until the start of your next turn. A Small creature is pushed up to 30 feet, a Medium creature is pushed up to 20 feet, and a Large creature is pushed up to 10 feet. On a success, a creature is pushed half the distance and isn’t [[Conditions#Deafened|deafened]] or [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]]. The kazoo can’t be used this way again until the next dawn.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Resonant Trunk

Part of [[Instruments]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
