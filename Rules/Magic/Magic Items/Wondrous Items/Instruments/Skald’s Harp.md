*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]] by a [[Bard]])* %% price pending %%

This ornate harp is fashioned from maple and engraved with heroic scenes of warriors battling trolls and dragons inlaid in bone. The harp is strung with fine silver wire and produces a sharp yet sweet sound. You must be proficient with stringed instruments to use this harp. When you play the harp, its music enhances some of your bard class features.

***Celebrate Life.*** When an ally spends a Hit Die during your [[Bard#Bardic Performance: Celebrate Life|Celebrate Life]] performance while you play this harp, it also gains 10 temporary hit points, once per performance. The temporary hit points last for 1 hour. %% house rule: replaces Song of Rest, 22 Nov 2026 %%

***Clarity of Thought.*** When you play this harp as part of your [[Bard#Bardic Performance: Clarity of Thought|Clarity of Thought]] performance, you and any friendly creatures within 30 feet of you also have resistance to thunder damage and have advantage on saves against being [[Conditions#Paralyzed|paralyzed]]. When this property has been used for a total of 10 minutes, this property can’t be used again until the next dawn. %% house rule: replaces Countercharm, 22 Nov 2026 %%

Part of [[Instruments]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
