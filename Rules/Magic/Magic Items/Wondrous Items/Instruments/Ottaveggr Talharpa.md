*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Common]] 60 gp*

This [[Northlands]] lyre is strung with cords made of braided jotun hair. As an action, you can play the talharpa to grant all your allies within 60 ft. who can hear it advantage on saves spells and effects that inflict the [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]] condition and on STR (Athletics) checks for 1 hour. Once this ability has been used, it cannot be used again until the next dawn.

Part of [[Instruments]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
