*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 2,200 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

You must be proficient with percussion instruments to use these drums. The drums have 3 charges. You can use an action to play them and expend 1 charge to create a baleful rumble. Each creature of your choice within 60 feet of you that hears you play must succeed on a DC 13 WIS save or have disadvantage on its next weapon or spell attack roll. A creature that succeeds on its save is immune to the effect of these drums for 24 hours. The drum regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Resonant Trunk

Part of [[Instruments]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
