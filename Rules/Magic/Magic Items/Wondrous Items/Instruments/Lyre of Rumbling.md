*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 8,800 gp*

*(Requires Attunement)*

You must have proficiency with instruments (strings) to use this lyre. The lyre has 4 charges and regains 1d3 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn.

***Bass Thrum.*** As an action, you can expend 1 charge to play a deep chord in 15-foot cone that reverberates through the ground. A creature in the area must succeed on a DC 15 STR save or be knocked [[Conditions#Prone|prone]].

***Power Chord.*** As an action, you can expend 1 charge to play a ringing chord in 15-foot cone that is powerful enough to move creatures. A creature in the area must succeed on a DC 15 STR save or be pushed 5 feet away from you.

***Screeching Wail.*** As an action, you can expend 1 charge to play a high-pitched chord in 15-foot cone that assaults the ears of your enemies. A creature in the area must make a DC 15 CON save, taking 2d6 thunder damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

Part of [[Instruments]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
