*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 4,430 gp*

This wooden horn is carved from lightning-struck yew. It has 6 charges and regains 1d6 charges each day at dawn. It can be blown as an action to cast a spell—*[[1st-Circle Spells#Fog Cloud|fog cloud]]* (1 charge), *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Gust of Wind|gust of wind]]* (2 charges), *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Sleet Storm|sleet storm]]* (3 charges)— using your spell save DC or 10 + your INT or WIS modifier, whichever is higher.

Part of [[Instruments]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
