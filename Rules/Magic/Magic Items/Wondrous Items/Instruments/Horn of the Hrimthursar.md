*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 14,000 gp*

This ivory horn, also known as the Horn of Frozen Breath, is typically carved from the bone of a white dragon, remorhaz, or another arctic monster. When you sound the horn, it creates a blast of freezing wind in a 60-foot cone. Each creature in the area must make a DC 15 CON save, taking 4d6 cold damage and becoming [[Conditions#Incapacitated|incapacitated]] until the start of your next turn on a failed save, or half as much damage and not [[Conditions#Incapacitated|incapacitated]] on a successful one. Once you blow the horn, it can’t be used again until the next dawn.

Part of [[Instruments]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
