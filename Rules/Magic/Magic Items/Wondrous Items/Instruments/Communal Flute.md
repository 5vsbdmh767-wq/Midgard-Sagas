*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]] by a [[Bard]])* %% price pending %%

This flute is carved with skulls and can be used as a spellcasting focus. If you spend 10 minutes playing the flute over a dead creature, you can cast the *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Speak with Dead|speak with dead]]* spell from the flute. The flute can’t be used this way again until the next dawn.

Part of [[Instruments]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
