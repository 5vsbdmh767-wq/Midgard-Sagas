*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 22,000 gp*

*(Requires Attunement)*

You must have proficiency with instruments (percussion) to use these cymbals. While wielded as a set, you have a +3 bonus to AC. You can also make CHA (Performance) checks in place of a CHA (Intimidation) checks.

The instrument has 3 charges and regains 1d3 charges daily at dawn. If you take any amount of thunder damage, the cymbals also regain 1 charge. As an action, you can expend 1 charge and clash the cymbals, making a CHA (Performance) check to create an agonizing sound in a 30-foot cone that is audible to 600 feet. Each creature in the area must make a CON save against a DC equal to your Performance check. On a failed save, a creature takes 4d10 thunder damage and is [[Conditions#Stunned|stunned]] until the start of its next turn. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and isn’t [[Conditions#Stunned|stunned]]. Creatures and objects made of glass or crystal have disadvantage on the saves and take 8d10 thunder damage instead of 4d10.

Part of [[Instruments]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
