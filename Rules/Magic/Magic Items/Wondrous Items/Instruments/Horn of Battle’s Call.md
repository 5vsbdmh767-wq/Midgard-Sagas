*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 8,000 gp*

This leather-wrapped horn is bound with stout iron bands. When you blow this horn as an action, choose up to five allies within 120 ft. of you who can hear you. Each target has advantage on melee weapon attacks until the start of your next turn and gains 10 temporary hit points. The [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]] condition ends for each target. Once you blow the horn, it can’t be used again until the next dawn.

Part of [[Instruments]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
