*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 3,000 gp*

While holding this fan, you can use an action to cast the *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Gust of Wind|gust of wind]]* spell (save DC 13) from it. Once used, the fan shouldn’t be used again until the next dawn. Each time it is used again before then, it has a cumulative 20 percent chance of not working and tearing into useless, nonmagical tatters.

Part of [[Tools, Keys & Curiosities]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
