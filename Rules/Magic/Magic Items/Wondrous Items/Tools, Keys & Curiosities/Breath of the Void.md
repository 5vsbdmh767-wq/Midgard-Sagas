*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 5,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

While wearing this amulet affixed with a spongy onyx gem, you can use an action and speak a command word so that, for 8 hours, you don’t require air, and you remain immune to the effects of Void terrain.

You can expend a number of hours remaining in this duration to breathe a 30-foot line of shadow. Each creature in the area must make a DC 13 DEX save, taking 1d8 necrotic damage per hour expended on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

After using the amulet, you can’t use it again until the next midnight.

Part of [[Tools, Keys & Curiosities]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
