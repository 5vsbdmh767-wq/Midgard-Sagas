*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 8,020 gp*

This item looks like a typical hooded brass lantern, but shadowy forms crawl across its surface and it radiates darkness instead of light. The lantern can burn for up to 3 hours each day. While the lantern burns, it emits darkness as if the *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Darkness|darkness]]* spell were cast on it but with a 30-foot radius.

Part of [[Tools, Keys & Curiosities]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
