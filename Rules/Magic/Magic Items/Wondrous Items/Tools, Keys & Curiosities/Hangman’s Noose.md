*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 4,020 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

Certain hemp ropes used in the execution of final justice can affect those beyond the reach of normal magics. This noose has 3 charges. While holding it, you can use an action to expend 1 of its charges to cast the *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Hold|hold]]* spell from it. Unlike the standard version of this spell, though, the magic of the hangman’s noose affects only undead. It regains 1d3 charges daily at dawn.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Demonic Thread · Spectral Essence

Part of [[Tools, Keys & Curiosities]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
