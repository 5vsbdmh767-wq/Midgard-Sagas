*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] 50,000 gp*

This curious device comes in two parts: a quartz amulet housing a copper arrowhead, and a small copper orb covered in quartz nodules. When you speak into the amulet, your voice comes through the copper orb. This works indefinitely for a distance up to a half-mile; it works at any distance, as *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Sending|sending]]*, to transmit up to 250 words of speech. Unlike *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Sending|sending]]*, the orb doesn’t allow an ability to respond, nor does it convey any magical understanding of language.

To “recharge” its transmission limit, the orb and its companion amulet must be submerged in a salt bath for 24 hours.

Part of [[Tools, Keys & Curiosities]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
