*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 2,000 gp*

When holding this shovel, as an action, you can speak its command word to make it seek out gems, metal, or other valuable items, worth at least 1,000 gp in total. The shovel trembles slightly when within 300 feet and facing the general location of such items, and it starts to pull more vehemently once within 30 feet.

Once the location is pinpointed, the shovel can be treated as adamantine for 1 minute to more easily dig up the treasure. Once this feature is used, the item can’t be used again until the next dawn.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Treasure-Sensitive Nose · Stone-Eating Acid
