*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 650 gp*

This seashell is intricately carved with protective runes. If you are carrying the shell and are reduced to 0 hit points or [[Conditions#Incapacitated|incapacitated]], the shell activates, creating a bubble of force that expands to surround you and forces any other creatures out of your space. This sphere works like the *[[5th-Circle Spells#Wall of Force|wall of force]]* spell, except that any creature intent on aiding you can pass through it. The protective sphere lasts for 10 minutes, or until you regain at least 1 hit point or are no longer [[Conditions#Incapacitated|incapacitated]]. When the protective sphere ends, the shell crumbles to dust.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Regenerative Coral

Part of [[Tools, Keys & Curiosities]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
