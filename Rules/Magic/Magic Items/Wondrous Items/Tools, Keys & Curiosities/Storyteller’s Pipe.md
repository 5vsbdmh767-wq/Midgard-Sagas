*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 16,010 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

This long-shanked wooden smoking pipe is etched with leaves along the bowl. Although it is serviceable as a typical pipe, you can use an action to blow out smoke and shape the smoke into wispy images for 10 minutes. This effect works like the *[[1st-Circle Spells#Silent Image|silent image]]* spell, except its range is limited to a 10-foot cone in front of you, and the images can be no larger than a 5-foot cube. The smoky images last for 3 rounds before fading, but you can continue blowing smoke to create more images for the duration or until the pipe burns through the smoking material in it, whichever happens first.

***Choking Smoke.*** You can use an action to speak the pipe’s command word then blow on the pipe, which emits choking smoke in a 30-foot cone. The smoke spreads around corners, and its area is heavily obscured. A wind of at least 20 miles per hour disperses the smoke, otherwise it lasts for 1 minute. Each creature that enters the smoke or that starts its turn in the area must succeed on a DC 15 CON save or be unable to breathe or speak as it coughs uncontrollably for 1 minute. A creature affected in this way can’t cast spells with verbal components. A creature can repeat the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. If a creature spends 3 rounds coughing from the smoke, it suffers one level of [[Conditions#Exhaustion|exhaustion]]. A creature can’t suffer more than one level of exhaustion from exposure to this smoke. Once used, this property can’t be used again until the next dawn.

Part of [[Tools, Keys & Curiosities]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
