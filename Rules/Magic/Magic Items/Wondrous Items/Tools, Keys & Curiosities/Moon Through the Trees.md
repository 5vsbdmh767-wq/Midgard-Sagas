*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]]) 16,000 gp*

This charm is comprised of six polished river stones bound into the shape of a star with glue made from the connective tissues of animals. The reflective surfaces of the stones shimmer with a magical iridescence.

While you are within 20 feet of a living tree, you can use a bonus action to become [[Conditions#Invisible|invisible]] for 1 minute. While invisible, you can use a bonus action to become visible. If you do, each creature of your choice within 30 feet of you must succeed on a DC 15 CON save or be [[Conditions#Blinded|blinded]] for 1 minute. A blinded creature can repeat this save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. If a creature's save is successful or the effect ends for it, the creature is immune to this charm's blinding feature for the next 24 hours.

Part of [[Tools, Keys & Curiosities]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
