*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 1,010 gp*

This fluted silver tube, barely two inches long, bears tiny runes etched between the grooves. While holding this tube, you can use an action to cast the *[[1st-Circle Spells#Magic Missile|magic missile]]* spell from it. Once used, the tube can’t be used to cast *[[1st-Circle Spells#Magic Missile|magic missile]]* again until 12 hours have passed.

Part of [[Tools, Keys & Curiosities]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
