*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 500 gp*

These three crystal figures of terns can interlock into a circle as if nestling together. Each one can be removed and buried in snow or a similarly loose material like sand or soil. As an action, you can call to a buried crystal bird within 100 feet of you. It bursts up into the air, filling a 10-foot-tall, 5-foot-radius cylinder with snow, before falling back to the ground. The area within the snow burst is heavily obscured until the start of your next turn. The three snowbirds can be placed separately and activated simultaneously to obscure multiple areas.

Part of [[Tools, Keys & Curiosities]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
