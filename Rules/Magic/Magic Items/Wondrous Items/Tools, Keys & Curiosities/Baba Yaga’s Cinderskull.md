*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])* %% price pending %%

Warm to the touch, this white, dry skull radiates dim, orange light from its eye sockets in a 30-foot radius. While attuned to the skull, you only require half of the daily food and water a creature of your size and type normally requires. In addition, you can withstand extreme temperatures indefinitely, and you automatically succeed on saves against extreme temperatures.

***Scorching Skull.*** While holding the skull, you can use an action to cast a 3rd-circle scorching ray from it. When you make the spell’s attacks, you do so with an attack bonus of +7. The skull can’t be used in this way again until the next dawn.

Part of [[Tools, Keys & Curiosities]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
