*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] 4,010 gp*

*(Requires Attunement)*

This small hand mirror sits inside an unassuming, folding case. While holding the mirror, as an action, you can target a creature you can see within 30 feet, and it must make a DC 15 CHA save. A creature can choose to fail this save. On a failed save, the creature’s semblance is captured in the mirror and visible on its surface. As an action, you can then cast the *[[1st-Circle Spells#Disguise Self|disguise self]]* spell from it to take on the illusory appearance captured within the mirror, and you have advantage on any checks made to impersonate the creature. The illusion ends when you are [[Conditions#Incapacitated|incapacitated]] or use an action to end it.

The mirror can only contain one captured semblance at a time. If you capture a new semblance, it replaces any held in the mirror.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Scintillating Hide · Photoadaptive Hide · shapechanger blood
