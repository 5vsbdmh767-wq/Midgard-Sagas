*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])* %% price pending %%

These prosthetic fangs can be positioned over your existing teeth or in place of missing teeth. While wearing these fangs, your bite is a natural melee weapon, which you can use to make unarmed strikes. When you hit with it, your bite deals piercing damage equal to 1d4 + your STR modifier, instead of the bludgeoning damage normal for an unarmed strike. You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic bite.

While you wear the fangs, a successful DC 9 DEX (Sleight of Hand) checks conceals them from view. At the GM’s discretion, you have disadvantage on CHA (Deception) or CHA (Persuasion) checks against creatures that notice the fangs.

***Poisoned Bite.*** You can use an action to cause the fangs to fill with a green poison. The poison remains active for 1 minute or until an attack using these fangs hits a creature. That creature must succeed on a DC 13 CON save or take 2d4 poison damage and become [[Conditions#Poisoned|poisoned]] until the end of its next turn. The fangs can’t be used this way again until the next dawn.

Part of [[Tools, Keys & Curiosities]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
