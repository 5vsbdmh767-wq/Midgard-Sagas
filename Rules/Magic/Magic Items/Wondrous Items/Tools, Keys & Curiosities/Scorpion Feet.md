*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Legendary]] 100,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

These thick-soled leather sandals offer comfortable and safe passage across shifting sands. While you wear them, you gain the following benefits:

- Your speed isn’t reduced while in magical or nonmagical difficult terrain made of sand.

- You have advantage on all ability checks and saves against natural hazards where sand is a threatening element.

- You have immunity to poison damage and advantage on saves against being [[Conditions#Poisoned|poisoned]].

- You leave no tracks or other traces of your passage through sandy terrain.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Camouflaged Hide (desert) · giant scorpion stinger · Anchoring Root

Part of [[Tools, Keys & Curiosities]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
