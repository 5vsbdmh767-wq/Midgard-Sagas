*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 200 gp*

This is a small, hollow figurine of a shrike, made of gold or silver. As an action, you can toss it into the air, where it takes flight and streaks toward a target creature that you can see within 60 feet. The target must succeed on a DC 15 CON save or be [[Conditions#Stunned|stunned]] until the end of your next turn. Each time a [[Conditions#Stunned|stunned]] target takes damage, it can repeat the save, ending the stunned effect on a success. The figurine is destroyed in the process.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Trampling Hoof

Part of [[Tools, Keys & Curiosities]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
