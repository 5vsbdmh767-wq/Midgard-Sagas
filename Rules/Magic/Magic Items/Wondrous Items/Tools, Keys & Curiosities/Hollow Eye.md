*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 4,040 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires attunement]])*

This small wooden talisman is shaped like a large eyeball with an empty void in place of the pupil. While holding the eye, as an action, you can spend 1 of your hit dice (as if spending it during a short rest) to drip a small quantity of your own blood into the eye and cast either the *[[4th-Circle Spells#Arcane Eye|arcane eye]]* spell or the *[[Rituals 4th–6th Circle#Divination|divination]]* spell with no components. Once you use the eye once, it can’t be used again until the next dawn.

Part of [[Tools, Keys & Curiosities]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
