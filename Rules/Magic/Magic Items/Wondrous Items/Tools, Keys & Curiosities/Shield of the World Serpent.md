*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] 30,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires attunement]])*

This shield is shaped from a scale of Jormungandr. While wielding the shield, you cannot be pushed or pulled against your will. At the start of each of your turns, you may end any grapple or restraining effect on you.

Part of [[Tools, Keys & Curiosities]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
