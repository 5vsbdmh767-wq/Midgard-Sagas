*Wondrous Item, Rarity Varies 50 gp (copper) or 1,020 gp (silver) or 4,020 gp (bone) or 50,040 gp (crystal)*

This arcane master key is the prized possession of many an intrepid thief. Several types of skeleton key exist, each type made from a distinct material.

***Bone (Rare).*** While this key is on your person, you have advantage on ability checks made to disarm traps or open locks. The key has 3 charges. While holding it, you can use an action to expend 1 or more of its charges to cast one of the following spells from it: *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Arcane Lock|arcane lock]]* (2 charges), *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Detect Poison and Disease|detect poison and disease]]* (1 charge), or *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Knock|knock]]* (1 charge). When you cast these spells, they are silent. The key regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the key crumbles into dust and is destroyed.

***Copper (Common).*** While this key is on your person, you have advantage on ability checks made to disarm traps or open locks.

***Crystal (Very Rare).*** While this key is on your person, you have advantage on ability checks made to disarm traps or open locks. The key has 5 charges. While holding it, you can use an action to expend 1 or more of its charges to cast one of the following spells from it: *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Arcane Lock|arcane lock]]* (2 charges), *[[1st-Circle Spells#Detect Magic|detect magic]]* (1 charge), *[[4th-Circle Spells#Dimension Door|dimension door]]* (3 charges), or *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Knock|knock]]* (1 charge). When you cast these spells, they are silent. The key regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the key shatters and is destroyed.

***Silver (Uncommon).*** While this key is on your person, you have advantage on ability checks made to disarm traps or open locks. In addition, while holding the key, you can use an action to cast the *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Knock|knock]]* spell. When you cast the spell, it is silent. The key can’t be used this way again until the next dawn.

Part of [[Tools, Keys & Curiosities]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
