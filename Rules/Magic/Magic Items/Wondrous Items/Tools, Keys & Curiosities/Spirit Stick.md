*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 2,000 gp*

This twisted, Y-shaped branch is carved with eldritch runes and adorned with tiny beads, bones, and feathers. As an action while holding the item, you can discern the direction and distance to the nearest [[Curses, Diseases, and Hazards#Spirit Crossing|spirit crossing]] hazard, as long as it is on the same plane of existence.

In addition, while holding the item, you can detect the presence of any ethereal Undead, such as ghosts or specters, within 60 feet of you, though you don’t know their specific locations. The item hums with a low vibration that only you can hear or feel, and this increases in intensity the closer you get to the creature.

Part of [[Tools, Keys & Curiosities]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
