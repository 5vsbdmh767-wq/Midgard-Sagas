*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 2,200 gp*

This one-foot-long rope of woven fox tails channels the power of the trickster [[Loki]] to escape difficult situations. While you hold it or have it on you, you have advantage on checks or saves made to avoid or end the [[Conditions#Grappled|grappled]] or [[Conditions#Restrained|restrained]] conditions.

***Unraveling.*** As an action, you can unravel one of the nine knots in the rope to cast one of the following spells, using CHA as your spellcasting ability: *[[5th-Circle Spells#Bragi’s Theatrical Fall|Bragi’s theatrical fall]]*, *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Calm Emotions|calm emotions]]*, *[[1st-Circle Spells#Charm|charm]]*, *[[4th-Circle Spells#Confusion|confusion]]*, *[[1st-Circle Spells#Disguise Self|disguise self]]*, *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Enthrall|enthrall]]*, *[[4th-Circle Spells#Greater Invisibility|greater invisibility]]*, *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Mirror Image|mirror image]]*, or *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Misty Step|misty step]]*. Each of these spells may be cast only once by the item. When the last knot is unraveled, the rope loses its magic.

Part of [[Tools, Keys & Curiosities]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
