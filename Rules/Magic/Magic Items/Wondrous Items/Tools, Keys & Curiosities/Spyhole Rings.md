*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 2,000 gp*

These paired rings must be used together. As a bonus action, you can affix each of the rings to a flat surface within 1 mile of one another. Once both are in place, they act like a spyhole: you can look through the nearer ring to see what lies beyond the second ring. You can only see what would be visible from the second ring’s vantage point.

Once the “spyhole” is created, it works in both directions, so creatures on the other end could potentially look through to see you as well. As a bonus action, you can detach an affixed ring from its surface.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Everwatchful Eyes · Teleport Tracer
