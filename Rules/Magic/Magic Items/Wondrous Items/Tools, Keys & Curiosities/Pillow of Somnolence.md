*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 1,100 gp*

If you lie down with your head on this magic pillow and close your eyes, you can complete a short rest in only 5 minutes, rather than 1 hour. Once used, this property of the item can’t be used again until the next dawn.

The pillow has 1d3 charges. As part of a long rest, you can expend 1 charge to complete the rest in only 4 hours, rather than 8. Once used, this property of the item can’t be used again until the next dawn. Once all the pillow’s charges are expended, the pillow becomes nonmagical.

Part of [[Tools, Keys & Curiosities]] in [[Wondrous Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
