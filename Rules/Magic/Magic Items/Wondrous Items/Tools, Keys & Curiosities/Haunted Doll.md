*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Legendary]] 200,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

This dolly is made of rags and stuffed with straw. It has button eyes and patchwork clothing, and its head is covered with real human hair. While attuned to it, your weapon attacks deal an extra 2d6 necrotic damage.

***Banshee Wail.*** As an action while holding the doll, you can cause it to release a mournful wail. Creatures within 30 feet of you and that can hear you must make a DC 15 CON save. On a failure, a target takes 10d10 psychic damage. On a success, it takes half as much damage and is [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]] of you for 1 minute. A frightened creature can repeat the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success. Once you use this feature, it can’t be used again until the next dawn. The wail has no effect on Constructs or Undead.

***Create Undead.*** As a reaction when you reduce a Humanoid to 0 HP with a weapon attack while attuned to the doll, you can cause that creature’s spirit to rise as a **specter** under your control. As a bonus action, you can mentally command this specter if it is within 120 feet of you. You decide what action it takes and where it moves on its next turn, or you can issue a general command, such as to guard a particular corridor. If you issue no commands, it only defends itself against hostile creatures. The specter dissipates at the next dawn or when it is reduced to 0 HP. Once you use this feature, it can’t be used again until the next dawn.

***Sentience.*** The doll is a sentient object with INT 12 (+1), WIS 14 (+2), and CHA 14 (+2). It has hearing and darkvision to a range of 120 feet. It can also speak, read, and understand Common and communicate telepathically with an attuned creature. The doll’s voice swings wildly from calm and comforting to angry, distraught, or even panicked.

The doll’s personality is that of a mother who died in childbirth and whose spirit then possessed it to remain near her child. (Additional details, including whether the child is still alive as an adult or long since passed, at GM’s discretion.)

The possessing spirit is filled with grief and wants to protect the PC attuned to the doll. However, it ultimately wishes to find a home with another child, whom it can play with and protect. If the attuned PC acts too far outside the spirit’s wishes, such as by refusing to help a child in need or ignoring the doll for too long, the spirit feels rejected, and as punishment, it withholds attunement for 24 hours—or longer, possibly even permanently.
