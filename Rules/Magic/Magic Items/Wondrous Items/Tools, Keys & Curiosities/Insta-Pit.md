*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 8,000 gp*

As an action, you can throw this heavy black blob at a point within 60 feet. It unfolds as it travels through the air, opening into a 10-foot-radius extradimensional pit when it strikes the surface. The pit is 30 feet deep. Any creature in the area of the extradimensional space’s surface must make a DC 15 DEX save. Creatures not adjacent to the pit’s edge have disadvantage on this save. On a failed save, the creature falls into the pit and is [[Conditions#Prone|prone]]. On a successful save, the creature manages to move to an unoccupied space adjacent to the pit.

The pit remains in place for 1 minute or until you use a bonus action to return the condensed blob to your hand. When the pit disappears, any creature still in the pit is ejected harmlessly to the corresponding space on the surface. Once used, the item can’t be used again until the next dawn.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Stone-Eating Acid · Astralsupial Pouch
