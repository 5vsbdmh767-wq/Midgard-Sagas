*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Legendary]] 100,000 gp*

This tiny, enameled carousel the size of a jewelry box has strange and skeletal creatures instead of colorful horses. If wound by the key in the back, the carousel spins slowly, and the creatures move up and down as the music box inside plays tinkly, off-key circus music.

If you place it on the ground and speak the command word, the carousel expands to a life-size, 20-foot-radius, 10-foot-tall carousel, and it remains in this state until you speak the command word again. The carousel plays pipe organ music (still off key) and rotates in the same manner as the music-box version. However, if you mount one of the carousel creatures and speak a different command word, the creature animates. Its creature type is Construct, but otherwise, it uses the statistics listed in the entries below.

The creature is friendly to you and your allies, understands your language, and obeys your spoken commands. If you issue no commands, it defends itself from hostile creatures but takes no other actions.

The duration is specific to each creature, and at the end of the duration, it becomes inanimate again. If it is returned to its spot on the carousel, it can be used again after 7 days. If it is reduced to 0 HP, it is destroyed.

The carousel contains 4d4 + 2 creatures, and only one of any given type listed can be present. Roll on the **Dark Carousel Creatures** table to determine which are available:

- **Basilisk.** The serpentine creature has a black tongue and wicked fangs. If animated, it uses the **giant constrictor snake** stat block and can carry up to three Medium creatures or four Small creatures. It remains for up to 8 hours.
- **Bat.** This giant bat has its mouth open in a scream and its wings spread wide as if in flight. If animated, it becomes a **giant bat** for up to 8 hours.
- **Black Unicorn.** This creature is frozen mid-gallop, its blood-tipped horn lowered as if to attack. If animated, it uses the **war horse** stat block and remains for up to 24 hours.
- **Black Widow.** This spindly giant spider has a gleaming black carapace with a red hourglass on its abdomen. If animated, it becomes a **giant spider** for up to 12 hours.
- **Chimera.** A vicious creature with three heads—one goat, one lion, and one dragon—and wide leathery wings is frozen mid-leap. If animated, it uses the **lion** stat block, has a flying speed of 80 feet, and remains for up to 12 hours.
- **Cloaker.** This horrific creature resembles a cross between a bat and a manta ray. If animated, it uses the **giant bat** stat block and remains for up to 8 hours.
- **Death Dog.** This beast has two snarling heads and glowing amber eyes. If animated, it becomes a Large **death dog** for up to 12 hours.
- **Dire Wolf.** This white wolf has red eyes and a snarling face. If animated, it becomes a **dire wolf** for up to 12 hours.
- **Gargoyle.** This winged creature resembles a bipedal bat with the head of a dog, and it seems to be made of stone. If animated, it uses the **giant bat** stat block and remains for up to 8 hours.
- **Harpy.** This creature has the head and torso of a woman but the wings and lower half of an eagle. If animated, it uses the **giant eagle** stat block and remains for up to 8 hours.
- **Hell Hound.** This black canine has a mane and tail that resemble flames, and its open mouth glows from within. If animated, it uses the **dire wolf** stat block and remains for up to 12 hours.
- **Nightmare.** The mane and tail of this black horse are painted to resemble flames. If animated, it becomes a **nightmare** for up to 8 hours.
- **Raven.** This black bird has an unnatural gleam in its obsidian eye. If animated, it uses the **giant eagle** stat block and remains for up to 8 hours.
- **Skeletal Bear.** This hulking skeletal figure wears a saddle pad of red and black tattered fabric. If animated, it uses the **brown bear** stat block and remains for up to 24 hours.
- **Skeletal Horse.** This boney equine sports a tattered black saddle adorned with silver chains. If animated, it uses the **war horse** stat block and remains for up to 24 hours.
- **Spirit Naga.** This snake-like creature coils around the carousel, occupying several spaces. If animated, it uses the **giant constrictor snake** stat block and can carry up to three Medium creatures or four Small creatures. It remains for up to 8 hours.
- **Tarrasque.** This is a miniaturized version of the legendary creature, though still quite large compared to the other carousel creatures. If animated, it uses the **mammoth** stat block (without the Trunk Grab bonus action). It remains for up to 4 hours.
- **Vrock.** This animal looks like a humanoid vulture with wide feathered wings and pale skin. If animated, it uses the **giant eagle** stat block and remains for up to 8 hours.
- **Worg.** While this creature bears a resemblance to a wolf, there is no mistaking it for one. If animated, it becomes a **worg** for up to 12 hours.
- **Wyvern.** This green and black draconic figure stands rampant, its toothy maw open in a roar. If animated, it uses the **giant lizard** stat block with a flying speed of 80 feet, and it remains for up to 8 hours.

(See *Monster Vault* for all stat blocks.)
