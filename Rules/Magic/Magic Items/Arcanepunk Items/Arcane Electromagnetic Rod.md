*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 8,800 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

This rod resembles a jagged lightning bolt. Sparks pop from a sapphire fixed to the top of the rod. The rod has 7 charges. Once placed into the ground using an action, you can spend 1 charge as a bonus action to activate the rod to emit a 30-foot-radius electromagnetic field that lasts until the start of your next turn. All creatures within range have disadvantage on spell concentration checks. Any creature beginning its turn within the radius must make a successful DC 15 STR (Athletics) check or be pulled 10 feet closer to the rod. The field does not affect you. To keep the field active, you can spend another charge as a bonus action. The rod regains 1d6 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn.

**Overcharge.** When you expend the rod’s last charge, you can overcharge the item. Creatures that fail their STR (Athletics) check also take 2d10 lightning damage. Roll a d10. The rod’s gem shatters on a 1, and its magical properties are inert until repaired.

**Repairing.** The gem must be replaced with a similarly colored gem. The rod must then be struck by lightning, natural or magical, to reactivate.

Part of [[Arcanepunk Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
