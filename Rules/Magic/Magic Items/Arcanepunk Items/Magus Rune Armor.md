*[[Armor|Armor]] (Scale Mail), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 4,400 gp*

An opalite gem adorns the center of the chest of this red scale armor trimmed in gold. This armor has 7 charges. When you cast a spell while wearing the armor, you can expend 1 charge to activate protective runes as a bonus action. You gain a bonus to AC equal to the circle of the cast spell, which lasts until the start of your next turn. Used charges refresh daily at dawn.

**Overcharge.** When you expend the armor’s last charge, you can overcharge the item. Double the bonus to AC. Roll a d10. The armor’s gem shatters on a 1, and its magical properties are inert until repaired.

**Repairing.** The gem must be replaced with an opalite gem, which you must imbue with magic using an available spell slot.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Magic-Absorbing Organ · Throat Scales
