*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 1,100 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

This silver gauntlet has five gems on the inside of its palm in a circle, which are the color of fire (orange), acid (green), lighting (bright white), thunder (violet), and cold (blue). The gauntlet has 5 charges, one for each of its gems. After a successful attack, you can expend 1 charge to cover the target in an alchemical plasm. The next time the target takes damage, they take an additional 2d6 damage of the type corresponding to the expended gem. The gauntlet regains 1d4 used charges daily at dawn.

**Overcharge.** When you expend the gauntlet’s last charge, you can overcharge the item. The target becomes vulnerable to the damage type corresponding to the expended gem until the end of your next turn. Roll a d10. All of the gauntlet’s gems shatter on a 1, and its magical properties are inert until repaired.

**Repairing.** The gems must be replaced with similarly colored gems. As part of repairing the item, each gem must be touched with the corresponding element, such as fire from a burning ember or cold from a block of ice.

Part of [[Arcanepunk Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
