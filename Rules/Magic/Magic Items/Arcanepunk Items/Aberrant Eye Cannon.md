*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] 66,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

This slimy, wiggling tentacle has a [[Conditions#Petrified|petrified]] eye of an Aberration on one end, with fragments of purple, glowing gemstones embedded in it. The cannon has 7 charges. Once attuned, the tentacle slithers itself onto your shoulder or arm. As an action, you can point it at a creature of your choice within 50 feet and expend 1 or more charges to use one of the following properties:

- **Charm (1 Charge).** The targeted creature must succeed on a DC 15 WIS save or be [[Conditions#Charmed|charmed]] by you for 1 hour or until you or one of your allies harms the creature.

- **Blast (2 Charges).** The targeted creature must succeed on a DC 15 DEX save or take 4d6 force damage. If this damage reduces the creature to 0 HP, it is reduced to a pile of fine gray dust.

- **Paralyze (1 Charge).** The targeted creature must succeed on a DC 15 CON save or be [[Conditions#Paralyzed|paralyzed]] for 1 minute. The target can repeat the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.

The eye cannon regains 1d6 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn.

**Overcharge.** When you expend the eye cannon’s last charge, you can overcharge the item, which has different effects, depending on the chosen property. Charm and Paralyze double the duration. Blast doubles the damage dealt. Roll a d10. The eye cannon’s [[Conditions#Petrified|petrified]] Aberration eye shatters on a 1, and its magical properties are inert until repaired.

**Repairing.** The eye cannon can only be repaired by finding another Aberration eye, which must then be petrified before being fitted into the eye cannon.
