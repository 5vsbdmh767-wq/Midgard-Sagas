Fantastical foods and beverages are magic items that look, smell and taste like ordinary food and drink, but they never spoil. They count as potions.

***Eating One.*** It takes at least 1 minute to eat or drink one. If you do so as part of a short rest and spend Hit Dice to regain hit points, each Hit Die restores twice as many hit points. You gain the food's own benefit either right after eating it or at the end of that short rest (your choice). Only one fantastical food or beverage works on you at a time.

***Cooking Them.*** A cook needs the recipe. Otherwise, making one during downtime works the same as brewing a potion (see [[Batch Crafting#Brewing|Brewing]]).

A [[Bezoars|bezoar]] in your stomach stops these foods from working.
