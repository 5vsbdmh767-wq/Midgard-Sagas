Fabled items grow in power along with their owner. This section lists some fabled magic items. Each entry provides key information about the item, including its type, any attunement factors, and a description of its properties.

## Properties and Attunement

%% source: Player's Guide 2 %%

A fabled magic item begins as a common magic item and does not require attunement to use, granting a useful minor trait to its owner. To access its higher-level traits, a PC must attune to it, using standard [[Using Magic Items#Attunement|attunement rules]]. Once attuned, that PC can use the item’s increasingly powerful properties as they themselves gain experience. If a PC higher than 1st level attunes to the item, its level-dependent properties, up to the PC’s current level, are immediately available. Unless otherwise noted, the item’s properties remain in effect as long as the PC is wearing, carrying, or touching it. If a PC chooses not to attune to it, the item remains a common magic item, granting only its minor property, regardless of the PC’s level.

At the GM’s discretion, a fabled magic item’s more powerful properties might become available only after the PC completes a particular quest or event related to the item rather than simply reaching each required level.

## Stories
%% source: Vault of Magic %%

Every fabled item is one of a kind and comes with a story tied to its powers. When a character first touches the item, a hint of that story comes to them: a name, a place or some other clue. That hint can send the character off to research the item, and the GM can make that research a condition of attuning to it. Unless the GM decides otherwise, a character who attunes to a fabled item learns its story, if they didn't know it already.

The names in each story, even the item's own name, can be changed to fit the campaign or a character's history.

## Variant: Heavier Attunement
%% source: Vault of Magic %%

Fabled items can outgrow other magic items as their owner levels up. A GM who wants to rein that in can make a fabled item take up more than one attunement slot as its wielder grows: one slot for a character of 12th level or lower, two slots from 13th level, and, if the GM likes, three slots from 17th level. This suits a world where legendary items and artifacts are rare.
