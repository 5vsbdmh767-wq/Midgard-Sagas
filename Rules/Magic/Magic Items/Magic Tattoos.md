%% source: Player's Guide 2 %%

Along with the other magic items in this chapter, several magic tattoos are presented, which are a special type of wondrous item. Magic tattoos can only be applied by highly skilled artists who distill special magical inks and create specific implements for use in their craft. It is incredibly rare for an individual to possess the combination of artistry, magical ability, and esoteric knowledge required to successfully create a magic tattoo—tracking down such a master, and them having the time to fit you in, is usually an especially difficult process. The GM has final say on whether or not magic tattoos are available in your game.

Mechanically, magic tattoos work just like other permanent magic items and follow the typical rules and pricing guidelines found under [[Using Magic Items]]. Despite their location on a creature’s skin, a tattoo’s abilities remain intact regardless of any surface damage or injury done to the tattooed area.

***Attunement.*** Magic tattoos always require attunement (and therefore take up an attunement slot) to use their properties. Attuning to a magic tattoo requires an hour, which can be done as part of a rest, just like a typical magic item, but you don’t need to make any special effort to remain physically in contact with it during the attunement process (since it’s inscribed on your skin). If you become unattuned to a magic tattoo inscribed on your skin, you choose whether it remains visible on your skin as a nonmagical cosmetic feature or whether the markings disappear entirely. If you reattune to a magic tattoo, the markings reappear on your skin as they were originally inscribed. If your body is destroyed and rebuilt by magic or your soul is transferred to a new body as the result of a spell like

*[[Rituals 7th–9th Circle#Clone|clone]]*, *[[Rituals 4th–6th Circle#Reincarnate|reincarnate]]*, *[[Rituals 7th–9th Circle#True Resurrection|true resurrection]]*, or similar magic you can still reattune to your magic tattoo as part of a long rest. When you do so, the tattoo reappears on your new body.*

***Appearance.*** The various magic tattoos listed might include suggestions for the visual themes, placement, or overall size of the tattoo, but these details can and should be customized to whatever design best suits you. In fact, a magic tattoo need not even appear as a traditional inked illustration, and it could easily be reflavored as a scar, brand, birthmark, or similar cosmetic feature that suits your game world.

## Arming Tattoo

*Wondrous Item, Common 60 gp*

*(Requires Attunement)*

These magic tattoos often depict arrows, bows, targets, or similar ranged weapon motifs.

When you wield a weapon with the Ammunition property, this tattoo summons a piece of ammunition for that weapon to your hand when you need it. Another piece appears immediately after you fire the first, allowing you to attack at the same pace you would with normal ammunition. Ammunition created by the tattoo counts as magical for the purposes of overcoming resistance or immunity to nonmagical damage. If the weapon has the Loading property, you must still spend the required time to load the ammunition.

Unfired ammunition disappears when you stow the weapon and fired ammunition disappears immediately after each attack is resolved.

## Iron Tattoo

*Wondrous Item, Uncommon 2,000 gp*

*(Requires Attunement)*

These magic tattoos often depict shields, armor, shells, towers, or similar symbols associated with defense and protection.

The tattoo has a number of hit points equal to 5 times your PB. When you take damage, you can choose to transfer any amount of that damage to the tattoo (no action required), reducing the tattoo’s current HP. If the tattoo is ever reduced to 0 HP, it is destroyed and disappears from your skin. The tattoo regains all expended HP daily at dawn.

The tattoo will automatically reduce your damage taken if you are unconscious, but it will stop when it has 1 HP remaining. That last hit point can only be used while you are conscious.

## Lucky Tattoo

*Wondrous Item, Rare 10,500 gp*

*(Requires Attunement)*

These magic tattoos often depict symbols of fortune like dice, clovers, coins, rabbits, and horseshoes. However, designs vary widely depending on cultural background and individual superstitions.

Whenever you gain Luck, you can choose to store it in this tattoo. The tattoo can hold a number of Luck equal to your PB. Luck stored in the tattoo doesn’t count against your maximum Luck. When you spend Luck, you can spend any combination of Luck stored in the tattoo and Luck accrued normally, up to a maximum of 5 Luck total spent on the same roll.

## Mystic Tattoo

*Wondrous Item, Varies Varies*

*(Requires Attunement by a Spellcaster)*

These magic tattoos often depict arcane symbols like pentacles or glyphs, as well as spiraling geometric symbols associated with meditation.

As a reaction when you cast a spell using a spell slot of a circle equal to or lower than the tattoo’s associated circle, you can expend the energy in the tattoo instead of expending the slot. Once used, the tattoo can’t be used again until the next dawn.

## Pocket Tattoo

*Wondrous Item, Common 100 gp*

*(Requires Attunement)*

These magic tattoos often depict treasure chests, coin purses, safes, or discrete symbols like a simple *X* to mark the spot.

As an action, you can touch an item of Medium size or smaller that isn’t being worn or held. When you do so, the item disappears and is stored in the tattoo. You can then use an action to retrieve a stored item, having it reappear either in your hand or in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of you. The tattoo can store a single Medium item, four Small items, or eight Tiny items at one time.

If the tattoo is destroyed or otherwise loses its magical properties, any items stored within it appear in the nearest unoccupied space that can accommodate them. Items stored in the tattoo aren’t shielded from divination spells or similar detection magic.

## Striking Tattoo

*Wondrous Item, Uncommon 2,000 gp*

*(Requires Attunement)*

These magic tattoos often depict simple lines, bands, or geometric patterns wound around limbs.

While you are attuned to this tattoo, your unarmed strikes deal a minimum of 1d4 bludgeoning damage. If the damage die for your unarmed strikes is already a d4 or greater (such as from the monk’s Martial Arts feature), this tattoo instead increases the size of your current damage die by one step (up to d12). For example, a d4 damage die becomes a d6, a d6 becomes a d8, and so on.

Additionally, when you successfully hit with an unarmed strike, you can convert any amount of the bludgeoning damage dealt to force damage. You can use this property a number of times equal to your PB, regaining all expended uses daily at dawn.

## Tattoo of the Dawn

*Wondrous Item, Rare 8,200 gp*

*(Requires Attunement)*

These magic tattoos most commonly depict radiant suns, but images of torches, lanterns, and angels are also popular.

As an action, you shed sunlight from your body in a 20-foot radius and dim light for an additional 10 feet. Any Fiends or Undead that end their turn in the area of the light take 2d6 radiant damage.

You can use this property for up to 5 minutes, either all at once or in shorter spans of 1 minute each. The tattoo regains 1d4 + 1 expended minutes daily at dawn.

## Tattoo of the Emissary

*Wondrous Item, Very Rare 30,000 gp*

*(Requires Attunement)*

These tattoos are only given to those owing allegiance to or currying the favor of a powerful Fey, typically one of the infamous lords or ladies that dwell in the shadow realms or the bright lands. The appearance of the tattoo and its exact benefits depend on the nature of the fey that inspired it.

While you are attuned to this tattoo, you gain the following benefits:

- As a bonus action, until the start of your next turn, you know the location of any Fey creatures within 60 feet of you.

- You have advantage on checks against being charmed or frightened. In addition, as an action, you undergo a transformation, taking on the physical characteristics associated with the Fey linked to the tattoo and gaining one of the following benefits (choosing when you first gain the tattoo). This transformation lasts for 1 minute.

***Animals.*** You have advantage on STR checks and STR saves, and your melee attacks deal an extra 1d4 damage as you are infused with bestial might.

***Frost.*** You gain a +2 bonus to AC as you are encased in a thin coating of ice.

***Plants.*** You regain a number of hit points equal to your current level at the start of each of your turns as you are infused with the vitality of nature.

***Shadow.*** Creatures have disadvantage on attack rolls against you as you are wrapped in swarming shadows.

***Sky.*** Your speed is doubled as you are carried by swift winds.

## Transforming Tattoo

*Wondrous Item, Uncommon 2,100 gp*

*(Requires Attunement)*

These magic tattoos often depict theatrical masks, butterflies, or other symbols of transformation.

As an action, you can alter your appearance to that of another Humanoid for up to 1 hour. Your new appearance must be the same size as you and have the same arrangement of limbs, but the rest of your features are up to you. The tattoo does not affect your clothing or equipment, only transforming your flesh. While transformed, you are affected by spells and features as if you had the Shapechanger tag.

If you are attempting to impersonate a specific individual, creatures familiar with that individual can discern that you are an imposter by using an action to make an INT (Investigation) or WIS (Insight) check, with advantage, against your CHA (Deception or Performance) check. At the GM’s discretion, those closest to the Humanoid being impersonated—such as a spouse or sibling—automatically succeed on this check.
