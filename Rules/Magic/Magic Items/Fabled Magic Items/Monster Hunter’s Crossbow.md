*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Heavy Crossbow), Fabled Priceless (5th-level and higher properties require attunement)*

This crossbow is made of polished wood and iron, inlaid with silver runes. Its attacks count as magical for the purpose of overcoming resistance and immunity to nonmagical attacks and damage.

In addition, when you hit an Aberration, Dragon, Fiend, Monstrosity, or Undead (or a creature with the Lycanthrope tag) with this weapon, the target takes an extra 2d6 piercing damage.

***Slayer’s Prowess (Requires Attunement).*** As your level increases, you gain the following benefits while wielding this weapon.

**5th Level.** When you hit with this weapon, you can change the extra 2d6 piercing damage to a different damage type, choosing from the following: fire, necrotic, or radiant.

**9th Level.** You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon.

In addition, when you hit a creature with this weapon, the target must succeed on a DC 15 CHA save or be banished (as the *[[4th-Circle Spells#Banishment|banishment]]* spell). You must maintain concentration on this effect as you would the spell. Once used, this property can’t be used again until the next dawn.

**13th Level.** You now gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this weapon, instead of +1, and its extra damage is now 3d6, instead of 2d6.

In addition, when you hit with this weapon, you can choose to force the target to succeed on a DC 17 CON save or be [[Conditions#Incapacitated|incapacitated]] until the end of your next turn.

**17th Level.** You now gain a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this weapon, instead of +2, and its extra damage is now 4d6, instead of 3d6.

Part of [[Fabled Magic Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
