*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Greataxe), Fabled Priceless (5th-Level and Higher Properties [[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Require Attunement]])*

This greataxe has a dulled, brushed texture and shows many scars, but it occasionally seems to pulse as its impatient power bubbles out. While wielding this magic weapon, you can’t cast or concentrate on spells, but you are resistant to the [[Conditions#Charmed|charmed]] and [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]] conditions and are immune to the effects of the *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Calm Emotions|calm emotions]]* spell.

***Sentience.*** This is a sentient weapon with the following statistics: INT 11, WIS 9, CHA 17. It has hearing and darkvision out to a range of 120 feet and can communicate telepathically with its wielder by transmitting intense, chaotic emotions that unsettle the mind.

***Personality.*** The mind of *Mayhem* is chaos incarnate. Whenever you find yourself in a position to make a decision, it floods your mind with thoughts urging you toward chaos.

***Chaos Reigns (Requires Attunement).*** As your level increases, you gain the following benefits while wielding this weapon. While attuned to *Mayhem,* if you consistently choose order over chaos (GM’s discretion), you must succeed on a DC 13 CHA save or your attunement to it ends. You can attempt to reattune to it only once per dawn.

**5th Level.** When you hit with an attack using this weapon, the target takes an extra 1d8 psychic damage.

**9th Level.** You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon. In addition, it has 3 charges and regains all expended charges daily at dawn. As a reaction when you damage a creature with it, you can expend 1 charge to force the target to succeed on a DC 15 WIS save or suffer the effects of the *[[4th-Circle Spells#Confusion|confusion]]* spell for up to 1 minute, requiring no concentration.

**13th Level.** You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon, instead of +1. You are immune to the [[Conditions#Charmed|charmed]] and [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]] conditions.

**17th Level.** You gain a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon, instead of +2. While wielding this weapon, as a reaction when you are hit by a weapon attack, you can cause another creature within range of the attack (which can include the attacker) to take an amount of psychic damage equal to the amount of damage you took from the attack. You can use this property a number of times per day equal to your PB, regaining all expended uses at the next dawn.

Part of [[Fabled Magic Items]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
