*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Paired Handaxes), Fabled Priceless (5th-Level and Higher Properties [[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Require Attunement]])*

*These paired handaxes have well-worn, coppery wooden handles and dull gray blades that show signs of heavy use. A fallen tree is engraved upon the head of Deadfall, and a thunderhead is engraved on the head of Fetch.*

*Deadfall* and *Fetch* are a set of paired handaxes first owned by the dwarven ranger Deblim Flintcoat. Deblim came from a long line of lumberjacks and coal miners in the Red Hills but was filled with a wanderlust uncommon in his people. Shortly after leaving home with a pair of axes given to him by his parents, he met and became a traveling companion of the wizard Anishan Fateshaper who is believed to have enchanted the axes for his friend.

You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with both *Deadfall* and *Fetch*. When holding both handaxes, you can activate them as an action to gain advantage on WIS (Survival) checks for 1 hour. Once used, this property can’t be used again until the next dawn.

***Swiftly Hewn (Requires Attunement).*** As your level increases, you gain the following benefits while wielding both axes. You cannot use any of these abilities unless wielding both axes.

**5th Level.** When you score a critical hit with *Deadfall*, the target must succeed on a DC 13 STR save or be knocked [[Conditions#Prone|prone]]. When throwing *Fetch*, you can make a ranged attack without incurring disadvantage for throwing at long range.

**9th Level.** After you take an action to make a melee weapon attack with one of these handaxes and a bonus action to make a melee weapon attack with the other one, you can make an additional attack with either handaxe (no action required).

**13th Level.** *Deadfall* gains 3 charges. As an action, you can spend 1 charge and slam *Deadfall* to the ground, causing violent vibrations to spread out from you in a 30-foot cone. Creatures standing within the area of effect take 6d6 thunder damage and must make a DC 15 CON save. On a failure, affected creatures fall [[Conditions#Prone|prone]]. On a success, a creature takes half as much damage and does not fall prone. *Deadfall* regains all expended charges daily at dawn. *Fetch* returns to your hand immediately after it is used to make a ranged weapon attack.

**17th Level.** Melee weapon attacks made with *Deadfall* deal an additional 2d6 thunder damage to Constructs and Plants. Ranged weapon attacks with *Fetch* do not incur disadvantage when made within 5 feet of a hostile creature.
