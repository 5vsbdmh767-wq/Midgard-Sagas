*Staff, Fabled Priceless*

*(5th-Level and Higher Properties Require Attunement)*

Warts dot the length of this green staff, an emerald frog sitting at its top. While holding this magic staff, you can speak with frogs and toads as if you shared a language.

***Toad Dominion (Requires Attunement).*** As your level increases, you gain the following benefits while holding the staff.

**5th Level.** You are resistant to poison damage and to the [[Conditions#Poisoned|poisoned]] condition. In addition, you have a swimming speed equal to your walking speed and can breathe air and water. The staff has 5 charges and regains all expended charges at the next dawn. While holding the staff, as an action, you can expend 1 or more of its charges to cast one of the following spells from it, using CHA as the spellcasting ability: *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Conjure Animals|conjure animals]]* (frogs and toads only, 3 charges), *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Enlarge/Reduce|enlarge/reduce]]* (2 charges), *[[1st-Circle Spells#Longstrider|longstrider]]* (1 charge), and *[[1st-Circle Spells#Thunderwave|thunderwave]]* (1 charge).

**9th Level.** The staff now has 10 charges. In addition to the previously listed spells, you can cast the following additional spells from the staff, expending the necessary number of charges: *[[5th-Circle Spells#Contagion|contagion]]* (5 charges) and *[[4th-Circle Spells#Polymorph|polymorph]]* (frog or toad only, 4 charges). In addition, as a bonus action, you can expend 1 charge to make your skin secrete a mild toxin for 1 minute. For the duration, a creature that touches you or hits you with a melee attack while within 5 feet of you must succeed on a DC 15 CON save or be [[Conditions#Poisoned|poisoned]] until the end of its next turn.

**13th Level.** You can make a long jump equal to your walking speed and a high jump equal to half your walking speed, with or without a running start. In addition, as an action, you can expend 3 charges to call forth a rain of hundreds of frogs in a 10-foot radius centered on a point you can see within 60 feet. Each creature in the area must make a DC 17 DEX save, taking 8d6 bludgeoning damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. The area then becomes difficult terrain for 1 minute.

**17th Level.** You are immune to poison damage and to the poisoned condition. The staff now has 20 charges. In addition to the previously listed spells, you can cast the following additional spells from the staff, expending the necessary number of charges: *[[8th-Circle Spells#Animal Shapes|animal shapes]]* (8 charges) and *[[7th-Circle Spells#Transmogrification|transmogrification]]* (7 charges).
