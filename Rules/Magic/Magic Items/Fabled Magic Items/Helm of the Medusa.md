*Wondrous Item, Fabled Priceless*

*(5th-Level and Higher Properties Require Attunement)*

This tarnished bronze helm covers the wearer’s eyes and is embossed with snakes. While wearing it, you have darkvision to a range of 30 feet. If you already have darkvision, wearing the helm increases its range by 30 feet.

***Aspect of the Medusa (Requires Attunement).*** As your level increases, you gain the following benefits while wearing this helm.

**5th Level.** You are resistant to poison damage and to the [[Conditions#Petrified|petrified]] and [[Conditions#Poisoned|poisoned]] conditions. In addition, you can communicate with snakes as if you shared a language. As an action, you can cast the *[[1st-Circle Spells#Animal Friendship|animal friendship]]* spell, but the target must be a snake. Once used, this property can’t be used again until the next dawn.

**9th Level.** As a bonus action, you can animate the snakes on the helm to attack a creature you can see within 10 feet. The target must succeed on a DC 15 DEX save or take 1d6 piercing damage plus 2d6 poison damage. In addition, as a reaction when a creature you can see targets you with a melee weapon attack, the snakes animate to intervene, and the attacker has disadvantage on the attack. You can use each of these properties a number of times equal to your PB, regaining all expended uses at the next dawn.

**13th Level.** You are immune to poison damage and to the [[Conditions#Petrified|petrified]] and [[Conditions#Poisoned|poisoned]] conditions.

**17th Level.** As an action, you can turn your magical gaze toward a creature you can see within 30 feet that can also see you. That creature must make a DC 17 CON save. If the save fails by 5 or more, the creature is instantly petrified. Otherwise, a creature that fails the save begins to turn to stone and is [[Conditions#Restrained|restrained]]. The [[Conditions#Restrained|restrained]] creature must repeat the save at the end of its next turn, becoming petrified on a failure or ending the effect on itself on a success. The petrification lasts for 1 minute, or until the creature is freed by the *[[5th-Circle Spells#Greater Restoration|greater restoration]]* spell or other magic.
