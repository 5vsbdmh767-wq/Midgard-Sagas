*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Warhammer), Fabled Priceless (5th-Level and Higher Properties [[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Require Attunement]])*

This warhammer, unblemished and with a seemingly eternal shine, appears to be made of a silver alloy and has intricate runes carved into its surfaces. While wielding this magic weapon, you have advantage on WIS (Insight) checks made to determine if a creature is lying.

***Sentience.*** This is a sentient weapon with the following statistics: INT 11, WIS 17, CHA 15. It has hearing and darkvision out to a range of 120 feet and can speak, read, and understand Common, plus one other language determined by the GM. It can also communicate telepathically with its wielder.

***Personality.*** *Arbiter* considers the rule of law and the fair application of justice to be paramount. It speaks in a solemn, straightforward, and sometimes vainglorious manner. It is honest to a fault and supremely confident in its abilities as a weapon and a judge. ***Judge, Jury, Executioner (Requires Attunement).*** As your level increases, you gain the following benefits while wielding this weapon. While attuned to *Arbiter*, if you frequently act contrary to its ideals (GM’s discretion), you must succeed on a DC 13 CHA save or else your attunement to it ends. You can only attempt to reattune to it once per dawn.

**5th Level.** Your base damage with this weapon is 1d10/1d12, instead of 1d8/1d10.

**9th Level.** You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon. In addition, as an action, you can cast the *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Zone of Truth|zone of truth]]* spell from the weapon at will (save DC 15), requiring no verbal or somatic components or concentration.

**13th Level.** You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon, instead of +1. When you roll a 20 on an attack roll with it, the target takes an extra 1d10 (or 1d12 if used with two hands) bludgeoning damage. In addition, it has 3 charges and regains all expended charges daily at dawn. As an action, you can expend 1 charge to cast the *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Spirit Guardians|spirit guardians]]* spell from the weapon (save DC 16), requiring no concentration.

**17th Level.** You gain a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon, instead of +2. When you roll a 20 on an attack roll with this weapon against a creature, the target takes an extra 2d10 (or 2d12 if used with two hands) bludgeoning damage, instead of 1d10 (or 1d12). If the target has 100 HP or fewer after taking this damage, it dies.
