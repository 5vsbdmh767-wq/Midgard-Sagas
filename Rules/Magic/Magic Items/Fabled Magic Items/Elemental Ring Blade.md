*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Longsword), Fabled Priceless (5th-Level and Higher Properties [[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Require Attunement]])*

This sword has a long cylindrical hilt with five metal rings fitted over the hilt just below the cross guard, each of a different composition. You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic sword. As an action, you can activate one of the rings for 1 minute. Your attacks with the sword deal an extra 1d6 of a damage type determined by which ring is active (see the **Ring Type** table). Once used, you can’t activate this property of the sword again until the next dawn.

*Ring Type*

| Ring | Damage Type | Ring | Damage Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brass | Fire | Gold | Thunder |
| Bronze | Lightning | Silver | Cold |
| Copper | Acid |  |  |

***Elemental Evolution (Requires Attunement).*** As your level increases, you gain the following benefits while wielding this sword.

**5th Level.** While a ring is active, you are resistant to its damage type. You can now use a bonus action to activate the ring, and when you do so, you can activate up to two rings, gaining the benefits of both. In addition, the rings can now be active for up to 2 minutes all at once or in multiple shorter activations. Each activation uses a minimum of 1 minute from the duration. The sword’s rings regain all expended minutes at the next dawn.

**9th Level.** You can now activate up to three rings at a time, and the total duration the rings can be active increases to 3 minutes.

**13th Level.** You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this sword, instead of +1. In addition, you can activate up to four rings at a time, and the total duration the rings can be active increases to 4 minutes.

**17th Level.** When you activate the rings, you can activate all five rings at once, and the total duration the rings can be active increases to 5 minutes.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: can't be crafted; fabled items are beyond mortal crafting.
