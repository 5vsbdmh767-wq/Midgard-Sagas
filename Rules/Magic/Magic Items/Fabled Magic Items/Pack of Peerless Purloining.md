*Wondrous Item, Fabled Priceless (5th-Level and Higher Properties [[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Require Attunement]] by a [[Rogue]])*

This dark canvas haversack is unremarkable, and even emptying the pockets produces nothing more than a suit of common clothes, a suit of fine clothes, and some lint. While you carry the pack, you can cast *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Nondetection|nondetection]]* on yourself from it once until the next dawn.

***Pilfering Pockets (Requires Attunement).*** As your level increases, the pack provides you with additional benefits.

***5th Level.*** The pack functions as a more compact *[[Handy Haversack|handy haversack]]*. Its central pouch can hold up to 4 cubic feet or 40 pounds of material, while each of its two side pockets can hold up to 10 pounds of material, not exceeding 1 cubic foot each. The backpack always weighs 5 pounds, regardless of its contents.

When first opened, the central pouch contains a bullseye lantern (filled but unlit), a steel mirror, and 50 feet of silk rope. One of the side pockets holds a set of *[[Dancing Lockpicks|dancing lockpicks]]*. If you lose the lockpicks or if they become nonmagical, another set appears in the pocket 1d4 hours after you notice.

Placing an object in a pouch or pocket follows the normal rules for interacting with objects. Retrieving an item from the pack requires an action. When you reach into the pack for a specific item, that item is always magically on top.

If a sharp object pierces or tears the pack, it ruptures and is destroyed. If the pack is destroyed, its contents are lost forever, although exceptionally powerful magic items lost in the bag tend to turn up again somewhere. Turning the pack inside out produces nothing of value; items that you previously placed inside the pack don’t spill out. If a breathing creature is placed within the pack, the creature can survive for up to 10 minutes until it begins to suffocate.

Placing the pack inside an extradimensional space created by a *[[Bag of Holding|bag of holding]]*, *[[Portable Hole|portable hole]]*, *[[Handy Haversack|handy haversack]]*, *portable vault*, or similar item instantly destroys both items and opens a gate to the Astral Plane. The gate originates where the one item was placed inside the other. Any creature within 10 feet of the gate is sucked through it and deposited in a random location on the Astral Plane. The gate then closes. The gate is one-way only and can’t be reopened.

***9th Level.*** You discover an additional pouch on the pack that holds up to 4 cubic feet or 40 pounds of material. When discovered, it contains a *[[Potion of Due Notice|potion of due notice]]*, a *[[Potion of Climbing|potion of climbing]]*, and a *[[Potion of Invisibility|potion of invisibility]]* (all clearly labeled). If you consume one of these potions and return the empty vial to the pocket, the pouch contains a new dose of that potion 1d4 days later. ***13th Level.*** While wearing the pack, if you use the Evasion class ability and take no damage from an area effect, you can choose to become [[Conditions#Invisible|invisible]] as the *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Invisibility|invisibility]]* spell for 2d4 minutes, or until you decide to end the effect. ***17th Level.*** You can open and unfold all of the pouches and pockets of the pack to create a 5-foot-square door over the course of 1 minute. Once assembled, the door opens to a demiplane, which appears as a room 30 feet in each dimension, made of stone. This room contains every item you have ever placed in the *Pack of Peerless Purloining*. It also contains a 12-foot rowboat with oars, a 24-foot wooden ladder, a 2-person tent, and a donkey cart with no donkey.

You can remain in this demiplane indefinitely but can’t close the door unless you are outside. If someone else closes the pack while you are inside, you become trapped in the room, but the air supply is seemingly infinite.
