*Wondrous Item, Fabled Priceless (5th-Level and Higher Properties [[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Require Attunement]] by a [[Bard]])*

This gold-trimmed carnival mask is expressionless and completely covers the wearer’s face. While wearing it, you can cast the *[[1st-Circle Spells#Disguise Self|disguise self]]* spell from it as often as you wish. In addition, your speed increases by 10 feet and you can double your normal jumping distance (though no further than your remaining movement allows).

***Thrilling Heroics (Requires Attunement).*** As your level increases, wearing the mask affords you the following additional benefits.

***5th Level.*** Your speed increases by 20 feet instead of 10 feet, and you can walk on water or other harmless liquid as easily as solid ground. If you move across a harmful liquid such as lava or acid, you still take damage from the substance. If you end your move on a liquid, the water walking effect ends until you return to dry ground.

***9th Level.*** You can cast the *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Alter Self|alter self]]* spell from the mask as often as you wish. In addition, your CHA score increases by 2, to a maximum of 20.

***13th Level.*** When you use the mask to cast *[[1st-Circle Spells#Disguise Self|disguise self]]*, you can also cast *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Enhance Ability|enhance ability]]* upon yourself, applying any two effects from the list, instead of one. This effect lasts until you finish a short or long rest and does not require concentration. Once used, this *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Enhance Ability|enhance ability]]* effect can’t be used again until the next dawn.

***17th Level.*** As an action, when you use the mask to impersonate a specific Humanoid, you can exchange physical locations with it via teleportation, provided it is within 1 mile of you. The Humanoid arrives in whatever clothing they are currently wearing, with whatever gear they have on their person, in whatever condition they happen to be in (such as [[Conditions#Unconscious|unconscious]] or dead). The target can choose to make a DC 17 WIS save to resist being teleported to your location. On a success, you still teleport, arriving in the nearest empty space to them. You can use this effect three times until the next dawn.
