Some healing potions use watered-down troll blood. Some alchemists bottle it pure instead. Undiluted troll blood heals a great deal, but it can also cause strange mutations. Troll blood comes in two versions: the raw potions on this page, and a refined brew made by master alchemists. Neither is the same as the [[Trollsblood Elixir|trollsblood elixir]].

***Drinking It.*** At the start of each of your turns, you regain hit points, up to a total set by the potion. If you take acid or fire damage, you don't regain hit points at the start of your next turn. While the potion is working, you die only if you start your turn with 0 hit points and don't regain any. You can also reattach a severed body part by holding it in place for 1d4 rounds. The potion can't regrow a missing part.

***Harvesting It.*** A successful DC 15 WIS (Medicine) check collects blood from a freshly killed troll. It works like a [[Potion of Troll Blood|potion of troll blood]] but lasts only 2d4 days. Stronger potions take blood that has been processed and concentrated. At the GM's discretion, an especially powerful troll might give blood that works like a stronger potion.

## Alchemists' Troll Blood
%% from the Alchemy Recipes page %%

Master apothecaries can refine troll blood into a thick, metallic-tasting brew that regrows lost body parts. Drinking it takes an action. A severed finger, leg, tail or other part grows back over 2 minutes, or knits back at once if you hold the severed part to the stump. It carries none of the raw potions' risks below. The recipe is a forbidden one. Making it is a master-level recipe; see [[Alchemy Recipes#Troll Blood * (Banned)|Troll Blood]] in the alchemy recipes.

## Instant Death and Mutations

These rules apply to the raw potions only. A troll blood potion keeps healing you up to its total even after you reach your hit point maximum. Too much healing, especially all at once, can mutate or kill you. You can't heal past your hit point maximum, but the extra still counts for the rules below.

***Instant Death.*** If the healing the potion still has left would be at least twice your hit point maximum, you die. For example, a cleric with a 12 hit point maximum and 6 hit points left drinks a potion that heals 30. The cleric is back to 12 with 24 healing left over. That's twice the cleric's maximum, so the cleric dies.

***Mutations.*** Each time the potion heals you past your hit point maximum, make a CON save (DC 10 + the number of hit points over your maximum). On a failure, you develop a mutation. Each potion says which kind (minor or major) and how many it can cause. The GM rolls d100 on the table below. A mutation lasts 1d4 + 1 hours.

### Minor Mutations

| d100 | Mutation |
|---|---|
| 01–20 | Thick, gnarled skin gives you a +1 bonus to AC. You also become vulnerable to acid and fire damage, and a critical hit against you rolls the damage dice three times instead of twice. |
| 21–30 | A keen sense of smell gives you advantage on WIS (Perception) checks that rely on smell. You take 2d6 psychic damage whenever you fail a WIS save. |
| 31–40 | You gain darkvision out to 60 feet and take a −4 penalty on DEX saves. |
| 41–50 | Claws burst from your fingertips. They are natural weapons for your unarmed strikes and deal 1d6 + your STR modifier slashing damage. You take a −4 penalty on attack rolls with any other weapon. |
| 51–60 | Your jaw stretches and your teeth keep growing and falling out. You are proficient with your bite, which deals 1d8 + your STR modifier piercing damage. When you make a melee attack, you can use a bonus action to bite the same target. You can't speak, and you can't cast spells with verbal components. |
| 61–70 | Your muscles double in mass. You count as one size larger for carrying capacity and for what you can push, drag or lift. Your speed drops by 10 feet. |
| 71–80 | Your mind clouds with fever and rage. You have advantage on STR checks and saves, and your melee weapon attacks using STR deal 2 extra damage of the weapon's type. Each melee attack you make has a 50 percent chance of hitting a different creature within 5 feet of your target instead, which can be an ally. |
| 81–90 | Your blood thickens. Damage you take is reduced by 1, and you take a −4 penalty on CON saves. |
| 91–00 | You reek. A humanoid or beast that starts its turn within 5 feet of you must succeed on a DC 13 WIS save or use its reaction to move 5 feet away from you. You have disadvantage on DEX (Stealth) checks. |

### Major Mutations

| d100 | Mutation |
|---|---|
| 01–20 | Your form shifts constantly. You have resistance to bludgeoning, piercing and slashing damage from nonmagical attacks, and disadvantage on saves against spells. |
| 21–30 | An extra arm sprouts from your body. It can't wield weapons or hold more than 5 pounds, but it gives you advantage on STR checks and saves. It throws off your balance, giving you disadvantage on DEX checks and saves. |
| 31–40 | You bleed heavily when hit, spraying magical blood. A creature within 5 feet that hits you with a melee attack must succeed on a DC 15 DEX save or take 1d6 necrotic damage. Damage you take is increased by 1. |
| 41–50 | Your scent and presence draw humanoids and anger beasts. You have advantage on CHA checks when dealing with humanoids, and beasts with INT 4 or lower are hostile to you on sight. |
| 51–60 | You grow a small second head. You have advantage on WIS (Perception) checks and on saves against being [[Conditions#Blinded\|blinded]], [[Conditions#Charmed\|charmed]], [[Conditions#Deafened\|deafened]], [[Conditions#Frightened\|frightened]], [[Conditions#Stunned\|stunned]] and knocked [[Conditions#Unconscious\|unconscious]], and you can't be [[Conditions#Surprised\|surprised]] while conscious. When you miss with an attack roll, you take half the damage you would have dealt. |
| 61–70 | You twitch and jerk without control. You gain a +2 bonus to AC, but on your turn you can take an action or a bonus action, not both. |
| 71–80 | Your body reshapes for a new way of moving. You gain a burrowing or swimming speed (GM's choice) equal to your walking speed, and your speed on land is halved. |
| 81–90 | Your blood turns toxic and you are [[Conditions#Poisoned\|poisoned]]. While [[Conditions#Poisoned\|poisoned]] this way, you are immune to disease and to poison and necrotic damage. |
| 91–00 | Your body keeps regenerating slowly. You regain 3 hit points every 10 minutes while the mutation lasts. This can still trigger instant death. |
