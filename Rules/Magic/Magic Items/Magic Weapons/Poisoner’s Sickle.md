*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Sickle), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 2,000 gp*

You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magical weapon. When you hit with an attack with this weapon, you can choose for the damage to be poison or slashing. On a critical hit, the target is [[Conditions#Poisoned|poisoned]] until the end of its next turn.

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
