*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Any Sword), Rarity Varies, Price Varies*

You gain a bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. The bonus is determined by the item’s rarity (see **Slayer’s Blade Rarity** table).

This blade is especially effective against a certain creature type (GM’s discretion): Aberrations, Celestials, Elementals, Fey, Fiends, or Undead. When you hit a creature of the relevant type with it, the target takes an extra 3d6 damage of the weapon’s damage type.

**Slayer’s Blade Rarity**

| Bonus | Rarity | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| +1 | Rare | 8,000 gp |
| +2 | Very Rare | 30,000 gp |
| +3 | Legendary | 50,000 gp |

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
