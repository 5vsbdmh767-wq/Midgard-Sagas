*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Club), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 1,100 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

This magic weapon has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. When you hit a target on your turn, you can take a bonus action to spend 1 charge and attempt to shove the target. The club grants you a +1 bonus on your STR (Athletics) check to shove the target. If you roll a 20 on your attack roll with the club, you have advantage on your STR (Athletics) check to shove the target, and you can push the target up to 10 feet away.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Trampling Hoof

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
