*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Morningstar), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 2,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

Ten spikes stick out of the head of this magic weapon. While holding the morningstar, you can fire one of the spikes as a ranged attack, using your STR modifier for the attack and damage rolls. This attack has a normal range of 100 feet anda long range of 200 feet. On a hit, the spike deals 1d8 piercing damage. Once all of the weapon’s spikes have been fired, the morningstar deals bludgeoning damage instead of the piercing damage normal for a morningstar until the next dawn, at which time the spikes regrow.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Throwing Spine (manticore)

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
