*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Glaive), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 5,000 gp + base weapon cost ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. Its default form is that of a quarterstaff, but as a bonus action, you can transform it into a glaive, or back again.

As a glaive, the weapon can also make a ranged weapon attack, firing its blade with a range of 30/60. On a hit, the blade deals 1d10 piercing damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 15 DEX save or be knocked [[Conditions#Prone|prone]] from the force.

The blade housing can hold up to three glaive blades, and if the blades are recovered, they can be reloaded as a bonus action. Once fired, a new blade automatically replaces it from within the blade housing if there are any available. Blades can be replaced with standard nonmagical glaive blades.

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
