*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Any Sword), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])* %% price pending %%

This enchanted blade is infused with the spirits of fallen warriors who carried it in battle long ago. You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. If you die while attuned to the sword, you gain the effect of the *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Gentle Repose|gentle repose]]* spell. This effect lasts until another creature attunes to the sword.

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
