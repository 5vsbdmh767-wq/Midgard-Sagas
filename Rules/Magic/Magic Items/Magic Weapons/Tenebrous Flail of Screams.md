*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Flail), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 8,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

The handle of this flail is made of mammoth bone wrapped in black leather made from bat wings. Its pommel is adorned with raven’s claws, and the head of the flail dangles from a flexible, preserved braid of entrails. The head is made of [[Conditions#Petrified|petrified]] wood inlaid with owlbear and raven beaks. When swung, the flail lets out an otherworldly screech. You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. When you hit a creature with this flail, the target takes an extra 1d6 psychic damage. When you roll a 20 on an attack roll made with this weapon, the target must succeed on a DC 15 WIS save or be [[Conditions#Incapacitated|incapacitated]] until the end of its next turn.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Resonant Trunk · mammoth bone and owlbear beak
