*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (greatclub), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 1,000 gp + 2 sp*

This greatclub is fashioned from the clapper of a giant’s bell. When you score a critical hit with this greatclub, you can choose for the target to be [[Conditions#Stunned|stunned]] until the start of your next turn instead of dealing the extra damage from the critical hit.

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
