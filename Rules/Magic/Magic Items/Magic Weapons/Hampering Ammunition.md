*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Any Ammunition), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Common]] 10 gp*

*+ ammunition base cost* This ammunition slows a target’s movement. A creature hit by the ammunition must succeed on a DC 10 CON save or have their base movement speed reduced by 5 feet for 1 minute. An affected target can repeat this save at the end of each turn to end the reduction.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Adhesive Slime

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
