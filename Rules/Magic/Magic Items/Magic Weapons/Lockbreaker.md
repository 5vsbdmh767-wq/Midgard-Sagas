*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Dagger), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Common]] 50 gp*

You can use this stiletto-bladed dagger to open locks by using an action and making a STR check. The DC is 5 less than the DC to pick the lock (minimum DC 10). On a success, the lock is broken.

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
