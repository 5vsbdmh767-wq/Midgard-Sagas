*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Battleaxe or Greataxe), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]]* %% price pending %%

You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. While you wield the axe, you have advantage on STR (Athletics) checks to shove a creature, and you can shove a creature up to two sizes larger than you.

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
