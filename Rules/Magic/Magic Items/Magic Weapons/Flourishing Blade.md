*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Any Sword), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 5,000 gp + base weapon cost ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

As a bonus action, you can make a bewildering flourish with it, targeting one creature you can see within 5 feet. The target must succeed on a DC 15 WIS save or have disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls until the end of its next turn.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Scintillating Hide · Fey Tongue

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
