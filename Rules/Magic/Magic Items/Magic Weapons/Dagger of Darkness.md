*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Dagger), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 1,000 gp*

While holding this dagger, you can use a bonus action to speak the command word, gathering writhing darkness around your hand and sending a bolt of darkness at a creature you can see within 30 feet of you. Make a ranged spell attack using your INT modifier. If the target is in dim light or darkness, you have advantage on the attack roll. On a hit, the target takes 2d8 necrotic damage and is [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]] until the end of its next turn. The dagger can’t be used this way again until the next dawn.

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
