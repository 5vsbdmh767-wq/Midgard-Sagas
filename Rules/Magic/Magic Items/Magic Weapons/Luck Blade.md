*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Any Sword), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Legendary]] Priceless ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. While carried, you also gain a +1 bonus to saves.

***Luck.*** While you carry this sword, you can call on its luck (no action required) to reroll one attack roll, ability check, or save you dislike. You must use the second roll. This property can’t be used again until the next dawn.

***Wish.*** The sword has 1d4 − 1 charges. While holding it, you can use an action to expend 1 charge and cast the *[[9th-Circle Spells#Wish|wish]]* spell from it. This property can’t be used again until the next dawn and never recharges. The sword loses this property if it has no charges.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Idolatrous Heart · Gluckshund Heart · Elemental Essence

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
