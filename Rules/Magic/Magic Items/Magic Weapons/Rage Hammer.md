*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (maul or warhammer), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 1,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires attunement]] by a barbarian)*

When you enter a Rage, you can make a melee attack with this weapon as part of the bonus action used to enter the Rage. After using this property, you can’t use it again until you complete a short rest.

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
