*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Any Sword or Axe), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Common]] 50 gp + base weapon cost*

A pleasant scent emerges from this weapon. While it is on your person, you have advantage on CHA (Persuasion) checks made to interact with humanoids and fey.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Fragrance Gland
