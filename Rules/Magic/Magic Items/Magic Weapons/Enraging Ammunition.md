*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Any Ammunition), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]]* %% price pending %%

When you hit a creature with a ranged attack using this magical ammunition, the target must succeed on a DC 13 WIS save or become enraged for 1 minute. On its turn, an enraged creature moves toward you by the most direct route, trying to get within 5 feet of you. It doesn’t avoid opportunity attacks, but it moves around or avoids damaging terrain, such as lava or a pit. If the enraged creature is within 5 feet of you, it attacks you. An enraged creature can repeat the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
