*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Any), Rarity Varies Price Varies*

You have a bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. The bonus is determined by the weapon’s rarity.

**Weapon Rarity**

| Bonus | Rarity | Price |
|---|---|---|
| +1 | Uncommon | 1,000 gp + weapon base cost |
| +2 | Rare | 5,000 gp + weapon base cost |
| +3 | Very Rare | 15,000 gp + weapon base cost |

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Bizarre Cartilage · Colossal Tendon · Throwing Spine. Use as many as the version's rarity calls for.

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
