*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Dagger), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]]* %% price pending %%

You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. When you hit a creature with an attack using this weapon, you can use a bonus action to inject paralyzing venom in the target. The target must succeed on a DC 15 CON save or become [[Conditions#Paralyzed|paralyzed]] for 1 minute. It can repeat the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. Creatures immune to poison are also immune to this dagger’s paralyzing venom. The dagger can’t be used this way again until the next dawn.

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
