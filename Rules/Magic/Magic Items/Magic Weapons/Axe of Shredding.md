*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Battleaxe), Varies Price Varies ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

You have a bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. The bonus is determined by the item’s rarity. Attuning to this weapon grants you proficiency with it and grants it the Finesse property. You can use the axe as a spellcasting focus for your spells.

This axe doubles as a magical lute, and you can simply will the axe’s strings into existence (no action required). Songs played on the axe are amplified and distorted, though still pleasing to hear. When you make a CHA (Performance) check while playing the axe, treat any d20 roll of 9 or lower as a 10.

If you score a critical hit with the axe, your music fills the target’s head, forcing it to make a CHA save against your spell save DC. On a failed save, the creature is so enthralled by the music that it drops anything it’s holding and becomes [[Conditions#Incapacitated|incapacitated]] until the start of your next turn. While incapacitated in this way, the only thing the creature can do is dance to the music.

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
