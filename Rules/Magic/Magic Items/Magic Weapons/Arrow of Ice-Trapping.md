*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Arrow), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 350 gp*

This arrow deals an additional 1d6 cold damage on a successful hit. If a creature takes damage from the ammunition, it must make a DC 15 STR save. On a failed save, the target becomes [[Conditions#Restrained|restrained]] in bands of ice. It can repeat its save at the end of each of its turns to end this effect on itself. While it is restrained, the target takes 1d6 cold damage at the start of each of its turns. The target or a creature within 5 ft. of it can take an action to make a DC 15 STR (Athletics) check, freeing the target on a success.

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
