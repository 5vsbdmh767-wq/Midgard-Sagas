*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (maul or warhammer), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Common]] 50 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires attunement]])*

Immediately after you score a critical hit with this hammer, it casts *thunderclap*, using your level to set the cantrip’s damage. Your allies are immune to the damage dealt by this casting of the spell.

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
