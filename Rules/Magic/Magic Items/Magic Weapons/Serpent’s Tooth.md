*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Spear), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])* %% price pending %%

When you hit with an attack using this magic spear, the target takes an extra 1d6 poison damage. In addition, while you hold the spear, you have advantage on DEX (Acrobatics) checks.

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
