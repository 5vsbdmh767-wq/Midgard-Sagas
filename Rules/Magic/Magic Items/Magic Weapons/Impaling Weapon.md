*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Any Piercing Weapon), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 1,500 gp + base weapon cost*

When you roll a 20 on your attack roll with this magic weapon, the weapon (or its ammunition) becomes stuck in the target. At the end of each of the target’s subsequent turns, it automatically takes the base weapon damage (no modifiers). The target, or another creature within 5 feet of it, can remove the weapon by succeeding on a DC 12 STR (Athletics) or DC 15 WIS (Medicine) check. If removed using a STR check, the target takes the base weapon damage on the weapon’s way out.

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
