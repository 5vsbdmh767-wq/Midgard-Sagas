*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Spear), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])* %% price pending %%

You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. Fashioned in the style of a whaling spear, this long, barbed weapon is made from bone and heavy, yet pliant, ash wood. Its point is lined with decorative engravings of fish, clam shells, and waves. While you carry this spear, you have advantage on any WIS (Survival) checks to acquire food via fishing, and you have advantage on all STR (Athletics) checks to swim. When set on the ground, the spear always spins to point west.

When thrown in a westerly direction, the spear deals an extra 2d6 cold damage to the target.

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
