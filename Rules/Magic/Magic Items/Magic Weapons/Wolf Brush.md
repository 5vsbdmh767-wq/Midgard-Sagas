From a distance, this weapon bears a passing resemblance to a fallen tree branch. This unique polearm was first crafted by a famed martial educator and military general from the collected weapons of his fallen compatriots. Each point on this branching spear has a history of its own and is infused with the pain of loss and the glory of military service. When wielded in battle, each of the small, branching spear points attached to the polearm’s shaft pulses with a warm glow and burns with the desire to protect the righteous.
