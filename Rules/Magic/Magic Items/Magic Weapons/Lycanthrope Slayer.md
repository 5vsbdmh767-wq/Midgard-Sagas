*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Any Sword), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] 30,000 gp + base weapon cost*

This blade is made of silver-plated steel and covered in intricate runes. When you hit a creature possessing the Shapechanger tag with this weapon, it deals an extra 1d6 damage of the weapon’s type (as a silvered weapon), plus 1d6 force damage. The creature must then succeed on a DC 15 CON save or immediately return to its true form (if not already).

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
