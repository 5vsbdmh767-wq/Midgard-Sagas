*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Flail), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 5,000 gp*

You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

The flail appears to be a normal, well-made flail, but when wielded in combat, its bludgeoning head grows to giant-sized proportions without affecting its wielder’s ability to hold it or attack with it. When an attack made with this flail hits, it deals an additional die of damage. The flail’s head returns to normal proportions after the attack.

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
