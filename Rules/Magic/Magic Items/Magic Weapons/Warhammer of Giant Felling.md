*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (warhammer), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] 30,000 gp*

This warhammer is inscribed with runes and pictograms dedicated to battles between dwarves and giants. You have a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

If you hit a creature that is a larger size than you with this weapon, that creature must succeed on a DC 15 STR save or take 3d6 thunder damage and be knocked [[Conditions#Prone|prone]].

If you hit a creature that is your size or smaller, that creature must succeed on a DC 15 STR save or take 2d6 thunder damage and be both knocked [[Conditions#Prone|prone]] and [[Conditions#Restrained|restrained]] as it is driven into the ground. The target, or a creature within reach of it, can use an action to make a DC 15 STR (Athletics) check to free it.

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
