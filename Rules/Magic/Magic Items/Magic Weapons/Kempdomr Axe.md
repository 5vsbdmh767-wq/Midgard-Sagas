*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (battleaxe, bearded axe, breidox, or greataxe), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires attunement]]) 5,000 gp + weapon base cost*

This axe channels the might of the jotun. You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

This weapon has 5 charges. When you hit an enemy with a melee attack using this weapon, you can expend 1 charge to deal an extra 3d8 force damage and push the target 10 feet away from you for every size category it is smaller than gargantuan (gargantuan creatures are not pushed). The weapon regains 1d4 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn.

***Giant’s Boon.*** While you are attuned to the weapon and wearing either a *[[Belt of Giant Strength|belt of giant strength]]* or *[[Gauntlets of Ogre Power|gauntlets of ogre power]]* to which you are also attuned, the weapon regains all expended charges daily at dawn. In addition, the bonus to attack and damage rolls increases to +2, and the extra force damage you deal when you expend a charge increases to 4d8.

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
