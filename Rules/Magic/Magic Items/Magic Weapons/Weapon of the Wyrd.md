*[[Weapons|Weapon]], [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 4,000 gp + base weapon cost*

This weapon is infused with Wyrd magic, making it unsettling to the average Humanoid, though it makes an ideal pact weapon. If you perform the warlock special ritual to transform this weapon into your pact weapon, any creature you hit with it must succeed on a DC 14 WIS save or gain the [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]] condition until the end of its next turn, with the weapon as the source of its fear. You can use this effect three times until the following midnight.

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
