*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Flail, Mace, or Morningstar), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 4,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])* %% cursed: listed at half price %%

You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon. The weapon appears to be a mace of disruption, and an *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Identify|identify]]* spell reveals it to be such.

***Curse.*** This weapon is cursed. Becoming attuned to it extends the curse to you. As long as you remain cursed, you are unwilling to part with the weapon, keeping it on your person at all times. While attuned to this weapon, you have disadvantage on attack rolls made with weapons other than this one.

The first time you use this weapon to kill a creature that has an INT score of 5 or higher, you begin having nightmares and disturbing visions that disrupt your rest. Each time you complete a long rest, you must make a WIS save. The DC equals 10 + the total number of creatures with INT 5 or higher that you’ve reduced to 0 hit points with this weapon. On a failure, you gain no benefits from that long rest, and you gain one level of [[Conditions#Exhaustion|exhaustion]].

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
