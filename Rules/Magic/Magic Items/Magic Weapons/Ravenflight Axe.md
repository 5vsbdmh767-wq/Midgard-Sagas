*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (handaxe), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 2,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

When you attack a creature with this handaxe and one of your allies is within 5 feet of the target, the weapon deals an extra 1d6 slashing damage.

*Swarm Attack.* Once per day, you can release the weapon from your hand as an action, at which point it transforms into 60-foot cone of swarming ravens. Make a ranged weapon attack with the axe against each creature of your choice in the cone’s area. Immediately after completing the last attack, the weapon reappears in your hand. This property can’t be used again until the next dawn.

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
