*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Club), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 2,000 gp*

This simple, polished club has a studded iron band around one end. When you attack a [[Conditions#Poisoned|poisoned]] creature with this magic weapon, you have advantage on the attack roll.

When you roll a 20 on an attack roll made with this weapon, the target becomes poisoned for 1 minute. If the target was already poisoned, it becomes [[Conditions#Incapacitated|incapacitated]] instead. The target can make a DC 13 CON save at the end of each of its turns, ending the poisoned or incapacitated condition on itself on a success.

> [!note] Faustin the Drunkard
> A cruel moneylender turned zealot made this club. After a hangover he took as divine punishment, he blamed all the world’s ills on drink, became a cleric, and set his thugs on taverns and brewers. He is long dead, but his club still turns up and causes trouble. %% source: Vault of Magic (paraphrased sidebar) %%
