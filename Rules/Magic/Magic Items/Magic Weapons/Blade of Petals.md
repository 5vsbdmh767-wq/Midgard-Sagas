*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Shortsword), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 8,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

This brightly-colored shortsword is kept in a wooden scabbard with eternally blooming flowers. The blade is made of dull green steel, and its pommel is fashioned from hard rosewood. As a bonus action, you can conjure a flowery mist which fills a 20-foot area around you with pleasant-smelling perfume. The scent dissipates after 1 minute.

A creature damaged by the blade must succeed on a DC 15 CHA save or be [[Conditions#Charmed|charmed]] by you until the end of its next turn. A creature can’t be [[Conditions#Charmed|charmed]] this way more than once every 24 hours.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Primeval Sap · Fragrance Gland
