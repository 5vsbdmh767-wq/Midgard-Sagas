*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Any Ammunition), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 100 gp*

When you hit a creature with a ranged attack using this magical ammunition, the target takes only half the damage from the attack, and the target is [[Conditions#Restrained|restrained]] as the ammunition bursts into entangling strands that wrap around it. As an action, the restrained target can make a DC 13 STR check, bursting the bonds on a success. The strands can also be attacked and destroyed (AC 10; hp 5; immunity to bludgeoning, poison, and psychic damage).

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
