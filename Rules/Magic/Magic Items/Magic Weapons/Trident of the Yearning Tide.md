*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Trident), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])* %% price pending %%

The barbs of this trident are forged from mother-of-pearl and its shaft is fashioned from driftwood. You gain a +2 bonus on attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. While holding the trident, you can breathe underwater.

The trident has 3 charges for the following other properties. It regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn.

***Call Sealife.*** While holding the trident, you can use an action to expend 3 of its charges to cast the *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Conjure Animals|conjure animals]]* spell from it. The creatures you summon must be beasts that can breathe water.

Yearn for the Tide. When you hit a creature with a melee attack using the trident, you can use a bonus action to expend 1 charge to force the target to make a DC 17 WIS save. On a failure, the target must spend its next turn leaping into the nearest body of water and swimming toward the bottom. A creature that can breathe underwater automatically succeeds on the save. If no water is in the target’s line of sight, the target automatically succeeds on the save.

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
