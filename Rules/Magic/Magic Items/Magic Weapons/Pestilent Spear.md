*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Spear), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 2,000 gp*

The head of this spear is deadly sharp, despite the rust and slimy residue on it that always accumulate no matter how well it is cleaned. When you hit a creature with this magic weapon, it must succeed on a DC 13 CON save or contract the sewer plague disease.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: otyugh gut

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
