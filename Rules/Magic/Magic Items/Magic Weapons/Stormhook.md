*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (dart, javelin, or spear), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 4,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires attunement]])*

When you hit a creature with a ranged attack made with this magic weapon, the target is pulled 10 feet closer to you. If the target has a flying speed, that speed is reduced to 0 until the start of its next turn.

After making a ranged attack with this weapon, it returns to your hand.

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
