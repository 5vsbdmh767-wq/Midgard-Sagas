*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Scythe), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 5,000 gp + base weapon cost ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

This magic weapon is a long wooden handle with a blade made of purple and black void energy. You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon, which deals necrotic damage instead of slashing damage.

As a bonus action, you can call on the blade, infusing the blade with mind-twisting horrors for 1 minute. When you hit a creature with the scythe while it is imbued in this way, the target must succeed on a DC 17 WIS save or be [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]] of you for 1 minute. The target can repeat the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. The scythe can’t be used this way again until the next midnight.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Void Essence · Unliving Claw
