*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Handaxe), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 2,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. When you draw this weapon, you can use a bonus action to cast the *[[Cantrips#Thaumaturgy|thaumaturgy]]* spell from it. You can have only one of the spell’s effects active at a time when you cast it in this way.

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
