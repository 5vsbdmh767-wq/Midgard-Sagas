*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Maul), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] 15,000 gp + base weapon cost ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic maul.

As an action, you can strike the ground with it, causing the ground around you to violently ripple. Each creature within 10 feet of you must make a DC 15 STR save, taking 2d6 bludgeoning damage and falling prone on a failed save, or half as much damage and not falling prone on a successful one.

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
