*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Longsword), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]]) 4,015 gp*

The story of the ill-fated Margreve Knights, adventurers who perished in the heart of the woods, is little remembered except in the name of this strange sword. To all appearances, this sword has been shorn down the middle of the blade.

You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. This longsword has the Finesse property, and you score a critical hit on a d20 roll of 19 or 20 with it.

Legend says that the second half of the blade lies forgotten in the [[The Margreve|Margreve]], and if the halves are rejoined, it becomes a vorpal sword.

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
