*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (longsword, rapier, scimitar, or shortsword), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 4,400 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires attunement]])*

This sleek magic weapon projects a shadowy double image of itself when wielded. The weapon has 3 charges and regains 1d3 charges each day at sunset.

***Elusive Strike.*** When you miss with a melee attack made with this weapon, you can spend 1 charge to reroll the attack roll and use the new result.

***Phantasmal Parry.*** While holding this weapon, you can spend 1 charge to cast the *[[1st-Circle Spells#Shield|shield]]* spell.

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
