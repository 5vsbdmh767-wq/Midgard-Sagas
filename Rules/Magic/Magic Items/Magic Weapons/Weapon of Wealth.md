*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Any), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 1,500 gp*

This magic weapon has a slot somewhere on it (such in its haft or, if a mace or similar, in its head) sized for standard coins. As a bonus action, you can insert a coin, which is consumed with a clink as it disappears. This offering gives the weapon certain properties for 10 minutes, depending on the type of coin inserted, as indicated on the **Coin Properties** table. The properties are not cumulative.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Treasure-Sensitive Nose · Metal-Crushing Beak

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
