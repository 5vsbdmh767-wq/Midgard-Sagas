*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Dagger or Any Sword with the Finesse Property), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]]) 15,000 gp + base weapon cost*

When you hit with an attack using this magic weapon, you gain insight into the target’s insecurities, allowing you to hurl devastating insults, and the target takes an extra 1d6 psychic damage. The target must be able to hear you (though it need not understand you). When you score a critical hit with this weapon, the target must also succeed on a DC 15 CHA save or have disadvantage on all its attacks until the end of your next turn.

In addition, while you hold the weapon, you have resistance to psychic damage.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Fey Tongue · Imitative Vocal Cords · Hag’s Blood

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
