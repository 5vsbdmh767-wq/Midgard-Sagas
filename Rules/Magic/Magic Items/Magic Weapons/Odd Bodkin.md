*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Dagger), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 8,000 gp*

This dagger has a twisted, jagged blade. You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. When you hit a creature other than a construct or an undead with this weapon, it loses 1d4 hit points at the start of each of its turns from a jagged wound. Each time you successfully hit the wounded target with this dagger, the damage dealt by the wound increases by 1d4. Any creature can take an action to stanch the wound with a successful DC 11 WIS (Medicine) check. The wound also closes if the wounded creature receives magical healing.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Rending Claws · Hardened Blood
