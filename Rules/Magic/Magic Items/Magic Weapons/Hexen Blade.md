*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Shortsword), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 16,030 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

The colorful surface of this sleek adamantine shortsword exhibits a perpetually shifting, iridescent sheen. You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

The sword has 5 charges and regains 1d4 +1 charges daily at dawn. While holding it, you can use an action and expend 1 or more of its charges to cast one of the following spells from it, using spell save DC 15: *[[1st-Circle Spells#Disguise Self|disguise self]]* (1 charge), *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Hypnotic Pattern|hypnotic pattern]]* (3 charges), or *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Mirror Image|mirror image]]* (2 charges).

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
