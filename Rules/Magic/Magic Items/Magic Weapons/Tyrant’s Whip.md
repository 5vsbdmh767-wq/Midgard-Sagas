*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Whip), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 1,010 gp*

This wicked whip has 3 charges and regains all expended charges daily at dawn. When you hit with an attack using this magic whip, you can use a bonus action to expend 1 of its charges to cast the *[[1st-Circle Spells#Command|command]]* spell (save DC 13) from it on the creature you hit. If the attack is a critical hit, the target has disadvantage on the save.

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
