*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Warhammer), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 8,000 gp*

When you roll a 20 on an attack roll with this weapon, the target is [[Conditions#Stunned|stunned]] until the end of its next turn. The repercussions continue the following round, forcing the target to succeed on a DC 13 CON save or be [[Conditions#Stunned|stunned]] until the end of its next turn.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Resonant Trunk · Ringing Claw

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
