*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Dagger), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 8,000 gp*

You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

You can use an action to cause thick, black poison to coat the blade. The poison remains for 1 minute or until an attack using this weapon hits a creature. That creature must succeed on a DC 15 CON save or take 2d10 poison damage and become [[Conditions#Poisoned|poisoned]] for 1 minute. The dagger can’t be used this way again until the next dawn.

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
