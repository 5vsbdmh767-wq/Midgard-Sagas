*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (battleaxe or greataxe), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 1,500 gp + weapon base cost*

This axe is forged from a dark grey metal and has blue-white runes inscribed on the head. This weapon deals an extra 3d8 thunder damage to any object made of ice that it hits.

The weapon has 3 charges. As an action, you can expend 1 charge and strike the weapon against an icy surface to create a 60-foot line of thunderous energy. Each creature in the area must succeed on a DC 15 STR save or fall [[Conditions#Prone|prone]]. Objects in the area that are made of ice take 3d8 thunder damage. The weapon regains 1d3 charges daily at dawn.

Part of [[Magic Weapons]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
