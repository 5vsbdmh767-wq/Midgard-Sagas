Treasure feels more useful and personal when it's obviously and immediately tailored to one character. This section contains suggestions for how to customize the magic items a PC can acquire based on class. Use these examples to help personalize magic items for your PCs.

The tables below make suggestions beyond just magic weapons and armor. Each recommended item includes a note explaining how and why the item synergizes with class abilities.

Note that the prices of some items can be prohibitive if gained through purchase. It's ok to sprinkle more expensive items into treasure hauls to reward one PC. However, limit that to one per character. A single PC with multiple high-price magic items is considerably more powerful than one without, which complicates your job to challenge everyone in the party.

Each item notes the book it comes from. Items from the *Player’s Guide*, *Player’s Guide 2*, the *Labyrinth Worldbook*, and *Trade Secrets* are linked where the wiki has them.

## Tier 2 Recommended Magic Items

Options open up in tier 2, and there are several generally good kinds of magic items that don’t need calling out in this table.

Every hero wants items that boost their primary ability score even temporarily. Look for *amulet of health*, *gauntlets of ogre power*, or *headband of intellect*, and potions of ability score boost.

Whenever possible, front-line combatants should acquire magic weapons and magic armor to hit more often and be hit less often. Damage bonuses are nice, but unless you’re guaranteed to hit, they’re unreliable.

Spellcasters should try to pick up a *pearl of power*. Those who rely on spell attack rolls should also look for a *wand of the war mage*.

As always, potions that cure you or buff your rolls are never a bad idea.

Finally, as a party, pool funds to invest in a *bag of holding* and a *lantern of revealing*.
