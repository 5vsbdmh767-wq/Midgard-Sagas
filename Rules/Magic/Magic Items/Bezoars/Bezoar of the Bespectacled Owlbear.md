*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 200 gp*

While this bezoar sits in your stomach, you have advantage on saves against poison, and you have immunity to the [[Conditions#Poisoned|poisoned]] condition. In addition, you can’t be [[Conditions#Surprised|surprised]] while you are conscious.

***Curse.*** Voices constantly tell you terrible things, maybe true and maybe not but certainly off-putting. The first time a creature damages you, you must succeed on a DC 15 WIS save or be [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]] of that creature until it or you fall [[Conditions#Unconscious|unconscious]] or until the creature is no longer within your line of sight for 1 minute. Once [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]] by a creature, you can’t be frightened by that creature again until 24 hours have passed.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Bezoar (bespectacled owlbear)
