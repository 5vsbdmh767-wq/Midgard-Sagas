*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Common]] 50 gp*

While this bezoar sits in your stomach, you are immune to the [[Conditions#Petrified|petrified]] condition, and you have darkvision out to a range of 60 feet. If you already have darkvision, your vision is unaffected by this bezoar.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Bezoar (cockatrice)
