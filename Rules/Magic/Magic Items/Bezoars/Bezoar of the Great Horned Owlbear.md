*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 650 gp*

While this bezoar sits in your stomach, you are immune to the [[Conditions#Charmed|charmed]] and [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]] conditions. In addition, creatures must be at least two sizes larger than you to grapple you.

***Curse.*** Your scent, demeanor, personality, or attitude irritates creatures around you. When interacting socially with creatures with an INT of 5 or higher, you have disadvantage on CHA checks. Creatures with an INT of 4 or lower are immediately hostile toward you, and the first such creature to attack you each day has advantage on the attack roll.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Bezoar (great horned owlbear) · Colossal Tendon
