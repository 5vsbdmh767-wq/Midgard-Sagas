*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]]* %% price pending %%

While this bezoar sits in your stomach, small spikes sprout from your skin. While you are grappling a creature or while a creature is grappling you, the creature takes 1d4 piercing damage at the start of your turn. In addition, you have proficiency with the spikes, and you can make a ranged weapon attack with a spike, hurling it as if it had the thrown property with a normal range of 30 feet and a long range of 120 feet. If the attack hits, the target takes piercing damage equal to 1d8 + your STR modifier. You can throw up to 12 spikes. Used spikes regrow when you finish a long rest.

Part of [[Bezoars]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
