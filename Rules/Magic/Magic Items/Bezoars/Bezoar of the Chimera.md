*Wondrous Item, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 200 gp*

While this bezoar sits in your stomach, red scales cover your body, two small horns curl from your temples, and your nose widens and flattens. When you aren’t wearing armor, your AC equals 13 + your DEX modifier, and with a 10-foot running start, you can long jump up to 25 feet. In addition, you have advantage on STR and DEX saves made against effects that would knock you [[Conditions#Prone|prone]], and you have advantage on WIS (Perception) checks that rely on smell.
