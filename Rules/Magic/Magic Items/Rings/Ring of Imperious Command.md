*Ring, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 8,800 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

Embossed in gold on this heavy iron ring is the image of a crown. The ring has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn.

While wearing this ring, you have advantage on CHA (Intimidation) checks, and you can project your voice up to 300 feet with perfect clarity. In addition, you can use an action and expend 1 of the ring’s charges to command a creature you can see within 30 feet of you to kneel before you. The target must make a DC 15 CHA save. On a failure, the target spends its next turn moving toward you by the shortest and most direct route then falls [[Conditions#Prone|prone]] and ends its turn.

Part of [[Rings]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
