*Ring, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 4,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]] by a Giant or Humanoid)*

While wearing this ring, your size changes to match the size of those around you. If you are a Large creature and start your turn within 100 feet of four or more Medium creatures, this ring makes you Medium. Similarly, if you are a Medium creature and start your turn within 100 feet of four or more Large creatures, this ring makes you Large. These effects work like the effects of the *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Enlarge/Reduce|enlarge/reduce]]* spell, except they persist as long as you wear the ring and satisfy the conditions.
