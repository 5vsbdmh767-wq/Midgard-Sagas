*Ring, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 1,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

This blackened steel ring is cold to the touch. While wearing this ring, you have darkvision out to a range of 30 feet, and you have advantage on DEX (Stealth) checks while in an area of dim light or darkness.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Shadowporting Blood
