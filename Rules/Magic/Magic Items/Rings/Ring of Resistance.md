*Ring, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 8,000 gp*

*(Requires Attunement)*

You are resistant to one damage type while wearing this ring. The gem in the ring indicates the type, which the GM chooses or determines randomly.

**Ring of Resistance Damage Type**

| d10 | Damage Type | Gem | d10 | Damage Type | Gem |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Acid | Pearl | 6 | Necrotic | Jet |
| 2 | Cold | Tourmaline | 7 | Poison | Amethyst |
| 3 | Fire | Garnet | 8 | Psychic | Jade |
| 4 | Force | Sapphire | 9 | Radiant | Topaz |
| 5 | Lightning | Citrine | 10 | Thunder | Spinel |

Part of [[Rings]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
