*Ring, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Legendary]] 55,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires attunement]])*

This ring is made from a twisted piece of wood charred by lightning, and it bears runes of the storm and the sea. While wearing it, you are resistant to cold, lightning, and thunder damage.

In addition, the ring has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. You can expend its charges to use the properties below.

***Storm Strike.*** You can expend 2 charges when you deal damage with a melee attack. In addition to the weapon’s normal damage, the target takes 8d6 lightning damage, and is [[Conditions#Blinded|blinded]] until the start of your next turn.

***Windborne Leap.*** You can expend 1 charge to cast the *jump* spell on yourself.

Finally, it has the following property.

***Jarlpact Benefit.*** If an ally within 120 feet is attuned to and wearing any other ring of the jarlpact, you have advantage on STR checks and STR saves.

Part of [[Rings]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
