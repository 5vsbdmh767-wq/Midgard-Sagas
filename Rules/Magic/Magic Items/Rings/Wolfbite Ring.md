*Ring, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 4,400 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

This heavy iron ring is adorned with the stylized head of a snarling wolf. The ring has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn.

When you make a melee weapon attack while wearing this ring, you can use a bonus action to expend 1 of the ring’s charges to deal an extra 2d6 piercing damage to the target. Then, the target must succeed on a DC 15 STR save or be knocked [[Conditions#Prone|prone]].

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Frenzying Oil · wolf fangs

Part of [[Rings]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
