*Ring, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] 66,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

This ring appears to be made of golden chain links. It has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn.

When you hit a creature with a melee attack while wearing this ring, you can use a bonus action and expend 1 of the ring’s charges to cause mystical golden chains to spring from the ground and wrap around the creature. The target must make a DC 17 WIS save. On a failure, the magical chains hold the target firmly in place, and it is [[Conditions#Restrained|restrained]]. The target can’t move or be moved by any means. The target can repeat the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. However, if the target fails three consecutive saves, the chains bind the target permanently. A successful dispel magic (DC 17) cast on the chains destroys them.
