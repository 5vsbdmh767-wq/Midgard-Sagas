*Ring, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 4,000 gp*

While wearing this ring, as an action, you can become Tiny for 1d4 hours. This functions as the “reduce” effect of the *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Enlarge/Reduce|enlarge/reduce]]* spell for Small PCs, and for Medium PCs, your size reduces to one-fourth of what it was in all dimensions, and your weight reduces to one‑sixteenth of normal. This reduction reduces your size by one category for Small PCs and by two for Medium PCs.

While reduced, you have disadvantage on STR checks and STR saves. Also, your weapons shrink to match your new size, and your attacks with them deal 1d4 less damage (this can’t reduce the damage below 1). You revert to your normal size at the end of its duration or if you use an action to do so.

Once used, the ring can’t be used again until the next dawn.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Insectoid Carapace · Bizarre Cartilage
