*Ring, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 4,000 gp*

This ring has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. While wearing the ring, you can use an action to expend 1 charge to cast one of the following spells:

- *[[1st-Circle Spells#Animal Friendship|Animal friendship]]* (save DC 13)

- *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Fear|Fear]]* (save DC 13), targeting only Beasts or creatures with the Animal tag that have an INT score of 3 or lower

- *[[1st-Circle Spells#Speak with Animals|Speak with animals]]*

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Animal Lord Essence · Fey Tongue

Part of [[Rings]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
