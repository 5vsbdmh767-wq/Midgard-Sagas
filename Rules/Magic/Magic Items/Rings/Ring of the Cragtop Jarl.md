*Ring, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] 33,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires attunement]])*

This polished granite ring bears runes of strength. While wearing the ring, when you attack with a weapon that deals bludgeoning damage, you score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20.

In addition, the ring has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. You can expend its charges to use the properties below.

***Bones of the Mountain.*** You can expend 1 charge to cast *[[1st-Circle Spells#False Life|false life]]*.

***Mountain Strike.*** You can expend 1 charge when you deal bludgeoning damage with a melee or ranged attack to push the target 10 feet.

Finally, it has the following property.

***Jarlpact Benefit.*** If an ally within 120 feet is attuned to and wearing any other ring of the jarlpact, you have advantage on STR checks and STR saves.

Part of [[Rings]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
