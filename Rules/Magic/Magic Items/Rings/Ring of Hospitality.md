*Ring, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Common]] 25 gp*

This ring is fashioned to show two hands shaking in greeting. While wearing this ring, you have advantage on CHA (Persuasion) checks when you request shelter from another creature that can understand you.

Part of [[Rings]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
