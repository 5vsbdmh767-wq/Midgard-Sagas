*Ring, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 2,000 gp*

While you wear this polished brass ring, you have advantage on CHA (Intimidation) checks and on saves against being [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]]. If you fail a save against being [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]], you can choose to succeed instead. If you do, the ring’s magic ceases to function until the next dawn.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Idolatrous Heart

Part of [[Rings]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
