*Ring, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Common]] 50 gp*

While wearing this ebony ring in dim light or darkness, you have advantage on DEX (Stealth) checks. When you roll a 20 on a DEX (Stealth) check, the ring’s magic ceases to function until the next dawn.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Shadowporting Blood
