Spirit honey is ordinary honey made by ordinary bees that feed on poisonous or magical plants. The bees concentrate the plants' essence into a sweet that is powerful and bends the mind. Many alchemists and hedge witches think it's worth the risk, which is where a lot of stories about mad alchemists and odd witches come from. Spirit honeys count as potions.

***Eating It.*** When you eat spirit honey, you gain its benefits and a long-term madness that lasts d10 × 10 hours.

***Insight.*** If you keep the madness for its full duration, you gain the honey's insight at the end. If the madness is cured early, you lose the honey's benefits and gain no insight.

***Calming the Madness.*** The *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Calm Emotions|calm emotions]]* spell suppresses the madness while it lasts, but you also lose the honey's benefits for that time. It doesn't stop you from gaining the insight.
