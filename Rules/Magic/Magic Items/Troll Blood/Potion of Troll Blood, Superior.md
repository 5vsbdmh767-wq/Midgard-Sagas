*Potion, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]]* %% price pending %%

When you drink this potion, you regain 5 hit points at the start of each of your turns. After it has restored 50 hit points, the potion’s effects end.

***Mutations.*** This potion can cause up to 1 minor mutation and I major mutation.

Part of [[Troll Blood]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
