*[[Weapons|Weapon]] (Greataxe), Fabled Priceless (5th-Level and Higher Properties [[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Require Attunement]])*

When you must choose between two options (such as turning left or right) or engage in an activity where the odds are 50/50 (such as flipping a coin) while wielding the axe, you get the more favorable outcome (as determined by the GM).

***Good Fortune (Requires Attunement).*** As your level increases, you gain the following benefits while wielding this weapon.

***5th Level.*** You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this weapon. In addition, when you miss on a melee weapon attack made with *Second Chance*, you can spend your reaction to successfully hit instead. Once you use this feature, you can’t do so again until you finish a short or long rest.

***9th Level.*** If you would drop to 0 HP as a result of taking damage while this greataxe is within 30 feet of you, you instead drop to 1 HP. Once this property has been used, it cannot be used again until the next dawn.

***13th Level.*** You now gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this weapon, instead of +1. In addition, once per turn when you roll a 1 on an attack or damage roll made with *Second Chance*, you can reroll the die. You must use the new result.

***17th Level.*** You now gain a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this weapon, instead of +2. In addition, once per long rest when you fail a save, you can choose to succeed instead.

*Checkered patterns decorate this double-headed axe whose blades are carved like dragons—one grinning and the other grimacing.* Only warriors of incredible skill have a chance to survive multiple years of their profession, and luck plays no small part in ensuring a soldier lives to see an old, restful end. Champions with seemingly endless luck often sit at the heart of legend, enchanting audiences with tales of everriskier exploits and thrilling narrow escapes. Weapons in the hands of such warriors often take on a measure of their wielder’s luck, becoming precious tools to keep their masters fighting in the rare moments luck runs out.

## Suggested Mighty Deeds

Potential mighty deeds for this weapon include the following:

- Emerge as the sole survivor of a battle or disastrous event.

- Miraculously win a dangerous wager with a powerful being like a god, ancient dragon, or other extraplanar being.

- Risk your life and the lives of others to turn the tides by pulling off a nigh-impossible stunt or gambit.
