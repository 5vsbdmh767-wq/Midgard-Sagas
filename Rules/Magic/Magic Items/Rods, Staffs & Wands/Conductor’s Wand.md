*Wand, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 4,400 gp*

*(Requires Attunement)*

While holding the wand, as a bonus action, you can create a brief piece of music at a volume of your choosing that can be heard up to 60 feet away. As a bonus action on subsequent turns, the music continues, and the melody and tune can be altered at will, whether to continue playing an entire song or to play a different piece of music.

This wand has 5 charges and regains 1d4 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn. As an action, you can expend 1 charge to target a creature you can see within 60 feet. If the target can hear your music, it must succeed on a DC 15 CHA save or dance to the music until the end of its next turn. On a failed save, the target’s speed is halved, and it can’t use actions, reactions, or bonus actions for the duration.

If you expend the last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the wand crumbles into ashes and is destroyed.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Imitative Vocal Cords · Resonant Trunk
