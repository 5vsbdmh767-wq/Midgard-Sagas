*Staff, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 120,000 gp*

*(Requires Attunement by a Spellcaster)*

This staff can be wielded as a magic quarterstaff that grants a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with it.

The staff has 7 charges and regains 1d6 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn. While holding the staff, as an action, you can expend 1 or more of its charges to produce one of the following effects. If you expend the last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the staff comes apart in a cloud of dark feathers.

***Bird’s Eye.*** While holding the staff, as a bonus action, you can expend 1 charge to gain advantage on WIS (Perception) checks that involve vision for 1 hour.

***Flight.*** While holding the staff, as a bonus action, you can expend 2 charges to gain a flying speed of 60 feet for 10 minutes.

***Summon Flock.*** While holding the staff, as an action, you can expend 3 charges to summon birds that appear in unoccupied spaces that you can see within 60 feet of you. They remain for 10 minutes. Choose one of the following options for what appears:

- 1d2 **giant eagles** or **giant vultures**.

- 1d8 **axe beaks** or **giant owls**.

- 1d12 **eagles**, **hawks**, **owls**, **ravens**, or **vultures**. All summoned creatures are friendly to you and your companions. They act on your initiative, taking their turns immediately after yours, and disappear when reduced to 0 HP or when the duration ends. They obey any verbal commands you give them (no action required by you). If you don’t give any commands, they defend themselves from hostile creatures but otherwise take no actions.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Celestial Feather · Bezoar
