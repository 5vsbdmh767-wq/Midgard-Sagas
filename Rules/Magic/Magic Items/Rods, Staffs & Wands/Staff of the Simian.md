*Staff, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 13,000 gp*

*(Requires Attunement)*

This staff can be wielded as a magic quarterstaff that grants a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with it. While carrying it, you have a +1 bonus to DEX saves.

The staff has 7 charges and regains 1d6 expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the staff comes apart in a series of tiny wooden monkeys and apes that quickly scamper away.

***Prehensile Tail.*** While holding the staff, as a bonus action, you can expend 1 charge to manifest a spectral monkey tail. You can use this to pick up and hold any Tiny object that isn’t otherwise being worn or carried. You can also use it to interact with objects, leaving your hands free to wield weapons or carry heavier objects. You can’t wield a weapon with the tail. The tail lasts for 1 hour or until you use a bonus action to dismiss it. As a bonus action, while the tail is active, you can attempt to distract an opponent during combat. One target creature you can see within 5 feet must succeed on a DC 15 DEX save or have disadvantage on all attacks against you until the start of your next turn.

***Simian Affinity.*** While holding the staff, as a bonus action, you can expend 1 charge to communicate with primates as if you share a language. This lasts for 1 hour or until you use a bonus action to dismiss it.

***Simian Movement.*** While holding the staff, as a bonus action, you can expend 1 charge to gain a climbing speed of 30 feet. In addition, your long jump is up to 30 feet, and your high jump is up to 15 feet, with or without a running start. This lasts for 1 hour or until you use a bonus action to dismiss it.

***Summon Apes.*** You can use an action to spend 2 charges to summon either 1d2 **apes** or 1d8 **baboons**, which appear within 60 feet in unoccupied spaces that you can see. A summoned creature disappears when it drops to 0 HP or when the spell ends. The summoned creatures are friendly to you and your companions and act on your initiative, taking their turns immediately after yours. They obey any verbal commands you give them (no action required by you). If you don’t give any commands, they defend themselves from hostile creatures but otherwise take no actions.

Part of [[Rods, Staffs & Wands]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
