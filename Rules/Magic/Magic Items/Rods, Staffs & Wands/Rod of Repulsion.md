*Rod, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 8,000 gp*

*(Requires Attunement)*

While holding this rod, as an action, you can target one creature that you can see within 20 feet. The target must succeed on a DC 15 STR save or be pushed 15 feet away from you. Alternatively, as an action, you can force all creatures within 5 feet of you to succeed on a DC 15 STR save or be pushed 10 feet away from you.

If this movement is interrupted by the target encountering a solid surface (such as being pushed into a stone wall), the target also takes 1d6 bludgeoning damage for each 5-foot increment it moved.

Part of [[Rods, Staffs & Wands]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
