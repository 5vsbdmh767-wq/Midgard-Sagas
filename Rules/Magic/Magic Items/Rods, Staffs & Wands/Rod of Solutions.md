*Rod, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 60,000 gp*

*(Requires Attunement)*

While holding the rod, as an action, you can dispense up to 10 gallons of clean water from the flared nozzle at its top. This water can be either poured or projected at a point you can see within 30 feet (at roughly 1 gallon per round) and can be moved around in a 10-foot-cube area. The water is able to extinguish exposed, nonmagical flames. If you instead target a creature, that creature must succeed on a DC 15 STR save or be pushed 10 feet away from you.

In addition, the rod has four embedded gemstones that change color when rotated: blue, green, red, and yellow. While holding it, as an action, you can rotate and depress one of the gemstones, locking in one of the following effects, targeting a point within 60 feet you can see. Each effect lasts for 1 minute, and once depressed, a gemstone can’t be used again until the next dawn.

***Blue.*** Glue coats a surface in a 20-foot square within range. A creature in the area must make a DC 15 STR save. On a failed save, the target is [[Conditions#Restrained|restrained]] until the end of its next turn. On a successful save, the target’s speed is halved until the end of its next turn. The area is difficult terrain for the duration.

***Green.*** Poison gas fills a 10-foot cube within range. Each creature in the area must succeed on a DC 15 CON save or be [[Conditions#Poisoned|poisoned]] until the end of its next turn. Any creature entering or ending their turn within the area must also make the save.

***Red.*** Oil coats a surface in a 20-foot square within range, making it difficult terrain. Each creature in the area must succeed on a DC 15 DEX save or fall [[Conditions#Prone|prone]]. Any creature entering or ending their turn within the area must also make the save.

***Yellow.*** Acid mist fills a 10-foot cube within range. A creature in the area must make a DC 15 DEX save, taking 2d6 acid damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a success. A creature that enters the area or ends it turn there must also succeed on a DC 15 CON save or be poisoned.

Part of [[Rods, Staffs & Wands]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
