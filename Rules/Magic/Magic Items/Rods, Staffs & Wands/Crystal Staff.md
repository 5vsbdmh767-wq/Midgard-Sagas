*Staff, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] 60,070 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]] by a [[Sorcerer]], [[Warlock]], or [[Wizard]])*

Carved from a single piece of solid crystal, this staff has numerous reflective facets that produce a strangely hypnotic effect. The staff has 10 charges. While holding it, you can use an action to expend 1 or more of its charges to cast one of the following spells from it, using your spell save DC and spellcasting ability: *[[1st-Circle Spells#Color Spray|color spray]]* (1 charge), *confound senses* (3 charges), *[[4th-Circle Spells#Confusion|confusion]]* (4 charges), *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Hypnotic Pattern|hypnotic pattern]]* (3 charges), *jeweled fissure* (3 charges), *prismatic ray* (5 charges), or *[[7th-Circle Spells#Prismatic Spray|prismatic spray]]* (7 charges). The GM can swap any spell here that isn’t on this wiki for another of a similar circle related to light or confusion.

The staff regains 1d6 + 4 expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the crystal shatters, destroying the staff and dealing 2d6 piercing damage to each creature within 10 feet of it.

Part of [[Rods, Staffs & Wands]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
