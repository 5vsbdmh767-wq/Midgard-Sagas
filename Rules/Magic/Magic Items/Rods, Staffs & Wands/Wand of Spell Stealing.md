*Wand, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] 39,500 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]] by a Spellcaster)*

You gain a +2 bonus to your spell attack and spell save DC while holding this wand. It has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the wand harmlessly implodes with a loud pop and is destroyed.

***Counterspell.*** As a reaction when a creature within 60 feet of you is casting a spell, you can expend 1 charge to cast the *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Counterspell|counterspell]]* spell (5th circle). If successful, the targeted spell has no effect (as *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Counterspell|counterspell]]*) and is instead stored within the wand. The wand can store up to nine circles’ worth of spells at a time. When found, it contains 1d8 circles of stored spells (GM’s discretion).

***Spells.*** As an action while holding the wand, you can cast any spell stored within it, using your spell save DC and spellcasting ability modifier. Spells cast from the wand in this fashion are no longer stored within it, freeing up space.

Part of [[Rods, Staffs & Wands]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
