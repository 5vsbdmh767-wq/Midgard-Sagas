*Staff, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]] by a [[Cleric]]) 45,000 gp*

This staff can be wielded as a magic quarterstaff that grants a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with it.

This staff has 9 charges and regains 1d6 + 3 expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the staff’s last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the staff is destroyed in a fiery burst of brimstone. The staff has the following additional properties:

***Spells.*** While holding this staff, you can use an action to expend 1 or more of its charges to cast one of the following spells from it, using your spell save DC: *clutch of ruin* (2 charges), *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Destructive Resonance|destructive resonance]]* (2 charges), or *[[6th-Circle Spells#King of Nothing|king of nothing]]* (6 charges). You can also use an action to cast the *[[Cantrips#Corrosion|corrosion]]* spell from the staff without expending any charges. Each of these spells is detailed later in this chapter.

***Voice of Ruin.*** While holding this staff, you can use an action and expend 1 charge to empower your weapon and spell attacks. For 1 minute, each weapon or spell attack you make against an object or Construct is a critical hit.

Part of [[Rods, Staffs & Wands]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
