*Rod, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])* %% price pending %%

This rod is topped with a red ram’s skull with two backswept horns.

As an action, you can spend one or more Hit Dice, up to half of your maximum Hit Dice. For each Hit Die spent in this way, you roll the die and add your CON modifier to it. You lose hit points equal to the total, and a target within 60 feet of you must make a DC 17 DEX save, taking necrotic damage equal to the total on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. You can’t use this feature if you don’t have blood. Hit Dice spent on this feature can’t be used to regain hit points during a short rest. You regain spent Hit Dice as normal.

Part of [[Rods, Staffs & Wands]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
