*Staff, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] 60,050 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]] by a [[Cleric]], [[Sorcerer]], or [[Wizard]] of a Non-evil Alignment)*

Crafted from polished bone, this strange staff is carved with numerous arcane symbols and mystical runes. The staff has 10 charges. While holding the staff, you can use an action to expend 1 or more of its charges to cast one of the following spells from it, using your spell save DC: *[[1st-Circle Spells#False Life|false life]]* (1 charge), *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Gentle Repose|gentle repose]]* (2 charges), *heartstop* (2 charges), *[[4th-Circle Spells#Death Ward|death ward]]* (4 charges), *[[Rituals 4th–6th Circle#Raise Dead|raise dead]]* (5 charges), *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Revivify|revivify]]* (3 charges), *shared sacrifice* (2 charges), or *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Speak with Dead|speak with dead]]* (3 charges). You can also use an action to cast the *bless the dead* or *[[Cantrips#Spare the Dying|spare the dying]]* spell from the staff without using any charges.

The staff regains 1d6 + 4 expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the bone staff crumbles to dust.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Eternal’s Humor · Celestial Blood · undead bone

Part of [[Rods, Staffs & Wands]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
