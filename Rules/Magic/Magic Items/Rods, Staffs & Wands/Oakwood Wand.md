*Wand, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Common]] 110 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]] by a [[Druid]])*

You can use this wand as a spellcasting focus. The wand has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. While holding it, you can expend 1 charge as an action to cast the *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Detect Poison and Disease|detect poison and disease]]* spell from it. When you cast a spell that deals cold damage while using this wand as your spellcasting focus, the spell deals 1 extra cold damage.

Part of [[Rods, Staffs & Wands]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
