*Staff, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 11,030 gp*

*(Requires Attunement by a Spellcaster)*

This short, stubby staff with a jester’s hat at its crown has 10 charges and regains 1d6 + 4 expended charges daily at dawn. The rod has the following additional properties. If you expend the last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the staff implodes harmlessly with the accompanying sound of flatulence.

***Commanding Presence.*** While holding the staff, as a bonus action, you can expend 2 charges to give yourself advantage on CHA checks for 1 hour.

***Spells.*** While holding the staff, as an action, you can expend 1 or more of its charges to cast one of the following spells from it, using your spell save DC and spellcasting ability modifier: *[[1st-Circle Spells#Charm|charm]]* (1 charge), *[[1st-Circle Spells#Hideous Laughter|hideous laughter]]* (1 charge), or *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Hypnotic Pattern|hypnotic pattern]]* (3 charges).

***Unnerving Laughter.*** While holding the staff, as a reaction when you are targeted by a melee attack from a creature you can see within 10 feet, you can expend 1 charge and impose disadvantage on the attack roll.

Part of [[Rods, Staffs & Wands]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
