*Staff, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]] by a [[Druid]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Warlock]], or [[Wizard]])* %% price pending %%

This ebony staff is decorated in silver and topped with a jagged piece of obsidian. This staff can be wielded as a magic quarterstaff. While holding the staff, you have advantage on CHA checks made to influence or interact socially with fey creatures.

The staff has 10 charges for the following properties. The staff regains 1d6 + 4 charges daily at dawn. If you expend the last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the staff dissolves into a shower of powdery snow, which blows away in a sudden, chill wind.

***Rebuke Fey.*** When you hit a fey creature with a melee attack using the staff, you can expend 1 charge to deal an extra 2d6 necrotic damage to the target. If the fey has a good alignment, the extra damage increases to 4d6, and the target must succeed on a DC 15 WIS save or be [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]] of you until the start of your next turn.

***Spells.*** While holding this staff, you can use an action to expend 1 or more of its charges to cast one of the following spells from it, using your spell save DC and spellcasting ability: *[[1st-Circle Spells#Charm|charm]]* (1 charge), conjure woodland beings (4. charges), *[[4th-Circle Spells#Confusion|confusion]]* (4 charges), *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Invisibility|invisibility]]* (2 charges), or *[[7th-Circle Spells#Teleport|teleport]]* (7 charges). You can also use an action to cast the *[[Cantrips#Vicious Mockery|vicious mockery]]* cantrip from the staff without using any charges.

Part of [[Rods, Staffs & Wands]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
