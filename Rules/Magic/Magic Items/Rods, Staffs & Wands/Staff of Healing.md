*Staff, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 22,500 gp*

*(Requires Attunement by a [[Bard]], [[Cleric]], or [[Druid]])*

This staff has 10 charges. While holding it, you can use an action to expend 1 or more of its charges to cast one of the following spells from it, using your spell save DC and spellcasting ability modifier: *[[1st-Circle Spells#Cure Wounds|cure wounds]]* (1 charge per spell circle, up to 4th), *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Restoration|restoration]]* (2 charges), or *[[5th-Circle Spells#Mass Cure Wounds|mass cure wounds]]* (5 charges).

The staff regains 1d6 + 4 expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the staff vanishes in a flash of light, lost forever.

Part of [[Rods, Staffs & Wands]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
