*Staff, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]] by a [[Sorcerer]] or [[Wizard]])* %% price pending %%

This plain, wooden staff has 5 charges and regains 1d4 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn. When you cast a spell while holding this staff, you can expend 1 or more of its charges as part of the casting to increase the circle of the spell. Expending 1 charge increases the spell’s circle by 1, expending 3 charges increases the spell’s circle by 2, and expending 5 charges increases the spell’s circle by 3.

When you increase a spell’s level using the staff, the spell casts as if you used a spell slot of a higher circle, but you don’t expend that higher-level spell slot. You can’t use the magic of this staff to increase a spell to a slot level higher than the highest spell circle you can cast. For example, if you are a 7th-level wizard, and you cast *[[1st-Circle Spells#Magic Missile|magic missile]]*, expending a 2nd-circle spell slot, you can expend 3 of the staff’s charges to cast the spell as a 4th-circle spell, but you can’t expend 5 of the staff’s charges to cast the spell as a sth-level spell since you can’t cast 5th-circle spells.

Part of [[Rods, Staffs & Wands]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
