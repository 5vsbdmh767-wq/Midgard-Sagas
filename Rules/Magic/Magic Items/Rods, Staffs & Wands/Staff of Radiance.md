*Staff, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] 75,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]] by a Divine spellcaster)*

This staff can be wielded as a magic quarterstaff that grants a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with it. While attuned to the staff, the wielder has resistance to necrotic damage.

This staff has 9 charges and regains 1d6 + 3 expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the staff’s last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the staff is destroyed in a fiery burst of brimstone. The staff has the following additional properties:

***Spells.*** While holding this staff, you can use an action to expend 1 or more of its charges to cast one of the following spells from it, using your spell save DC: *[[1st-Circle Spells#Guiding Bolt|guiding bolt]]* (1 charge), *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Daylight|daylight]]* (3 charges), or *[[6th-Circle Spells#Sunbeam|sunbeam]]* (6 charges). You can also use an action to cast the *[[Cantrips#Light|light]]* or *[[Cantrips#Sacred Flame|sacred flame]]* cantrips from the staff without expending any charges.

***Voice of Heaven.*** While holding this staff, you can use an action and expend 1 charge to empower your weapon and spell attacks. For 1 minute, each weapon or spell attack you make deals a number of additional d6s of radiant damage equal to your PB.

Part of [[Rods, Staffs & Wands]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
