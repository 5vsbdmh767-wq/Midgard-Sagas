*Staff, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] 120,050 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]] by a [[Cleric]], [[Druid]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Warlock]], or [[Wizard]])*

This staff of [[Conditions#Petrified|petrified]] wood is topped with a stylized carving of a bat with spread wings, a mouth baring great fangs, and a pair of ruby eyes. It has 10 charges and regains 1d6 + 4 charges daily at dawn. As long as the staff holds at least 1 charge, you can communicate with bats as if you shared a language. Bat and bat-like beasts and monstrosities never attack you unless magically forced to do so or unless you attack them first.

While holding the staff, you can use an action to expend 1 or more of its charges to cast one of the following spells from it, using your spell save DC: *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Darkness|darkness]]* (2 charges), *[[5th-Circle Spells#Dominate|dominate]]* (8 charges), or *[[5th-Circle Spells#Flame Strike|flame strike]]* (5 charges).

Part of [[Rods, Staffs & Wands]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
