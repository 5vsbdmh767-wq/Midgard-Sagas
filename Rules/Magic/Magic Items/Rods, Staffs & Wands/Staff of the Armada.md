*Staff, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]] by a [[Wizard]])* %% price pending %%

This gold-shod staff is constructed out of a piece of masting from a galleon. The staff can be wielded as a magic quarterstaff that grants you a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with it. While you are on board a ship, this bonus increases to +2.

The staff has 10 charges and regains 1d8 +2 expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the staff loses all of its magic and becomes a i nonmagical quarterstaff.

***Spells.*** While holding the staff, you can use an action to expend 1 or more of its charges to cast one of the following spells from it, using your spell save DC and spellcasting ability: *[[4th-Circle Spells#Control Water|control water]]* (4 charges), *[[1st-Circle Spells#Fog Cloud|fog cloud]]* (1 charge), *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Gust of Wind|gust of wind]]* (2 charges), or *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Water Walk|water walk]]* (3 charges). You can also use an action to cast the *[[Cantrips#Ray of Frost|ray of frost]]* cantrip from the staff without using any charges.

Part of [[Rods, Staffs & Wands]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
