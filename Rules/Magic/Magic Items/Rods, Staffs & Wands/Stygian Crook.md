*Staff, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]] by a [[Druid]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Warlock]], or [[Wizard]])* %% price pending %%

This staff of gnarled, rotted wood ends in a hooked curvature like a twisted shepherd’s crook. The staff has 10 charges. While holding it, you can use an action to expend 1 or more of its charges to cast one of the following spells from it, using your spell save DC: *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Bestow Curse|bestow curse]]* (3 charges), blight (4. charges), *[[5th-Circle Spells#Contagion|contagion]]* (5 charges), *[[1st-Circle Spells#False Life|false life]]* (1 charge), or *[[Rituals 4th–6th Circle#Hallow|hallow]]* (5 charges).

The staff regains 1d6 + 4 expended charges daily at dusk. If you expend the last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the staff turns to live maggots and is destroyed.

Part of [[Rods, Staffs & Wands]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
