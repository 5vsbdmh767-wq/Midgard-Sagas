*Staff, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]] by a [[Sorcerer]], [[Warlock]], or [[Wizard]])* %% price pending %%

Fashioned from twisted ash wood, this staff’s head is carved in the likeness of a serpent preparing to strike. You have resistance to poison damage while you hold this staff.

The staff has 10 charges for the following properties. It regains 1d6 + 4 expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the carved snake head twists and magically consumes the rest of the staff, destroying it.

***Spells.*** While holding the staff, you can use an action to expend 1 or more of its charges to cast one of the following spells from it, using your spell save DC: *[[5th-Circle Spells#Cloudkill|cloudkill]]* (5 charges), *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Detect Poison and Disease|detect poison and disease]]* (1 charge), *[[Conditions#Poisoned|poisoned]] *[[1st-Circle Spells#Volley|volley]]** (2 charges), or *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Protection from Poison|protection from poison]]* (2 charges). ***Serpent Form.*** While holding the staff, you can use an action cast *[[4th-Circle Spells#Polymorph|polymorph]]* on yourself, transforming into a serpent or snake that has a challenge rating of 2 or lower. While you are in the form of a serpent, you retain your INT, WIS, and CHA scores. You can remain in serpent form for up to 1 minute, and you can revert to your normal form as an action. Once used, this property can’t be used again until the next dawn.
