*Wand, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Common]] 28 gp*

The top half of this wooden wand is covered in thorns. The wand has 3 charges. While holding it, you can use an action to expend 1 of its charges and point the wand at a humanoid you can see within 30 feet of you. The target must succeed on a DC 10 CHA save or be forced to cry for 1 minute. Crying doesn’t prevent a target from taking actions or moving.

The wand regains all expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the wand’s last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the wand transforms into a *[[Wand of Giggles|wand of giggles]]*.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Fearful Eyedrops

Part of [[Rods, Staffs & Wands]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
