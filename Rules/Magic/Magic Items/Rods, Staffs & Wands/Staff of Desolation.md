*Staff, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]] by a [[Sorcerer]], [[Warlock]], or [[Wizard]])* %% price pending %%

This staff can be wielded as a magic quarterstaff that grants a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with it. When you hit an object or structure with a melee attack using the staff, you deal double damage (triple damage on a critical hit).

The staff has 10 charges. While holding it, you can use an action to expend 1 or more of its charges to cast one of the following spells from it, using your spell save DC: *[[1st-Circle Spells#Thunderwave|thunderwave]]* (1 charge), *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Shatter|shatter]]* (2 charges), *[[6th-Circle Spells#Circle of Death|circle of death]]* (6 charges), *[[6th-Circle Spells#Disintegrate|disintegrate]]* (6 charges), or *[[8th-Circle Spells#Earthquake|earthquake]]* (8 charges).

The staff regains 1d6 + 4 expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the last charge, roll a d20.On a 1, the staff crumbles to dust and is destroyed.

Part of [[Rods, Staffs & Wands]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
