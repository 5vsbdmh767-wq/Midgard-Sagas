*Wand, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]] by a Spellcaster) 8,800 gp*

This wand has 5 charges and regains 1d4 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the wand’s last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the wand wilts, crumbling into dirt, and is destroyed.

***Creeper Clutch.*** While holding the wand, as an action, you can expend 1 charge, targeting a creature you can see within 60 feet of you. Creeping vines erupt from the ground around the target, which must succeed on a DC 15 STR save or be [[Conditions#Restrained|restrained]] for 1 minute. A [[Conditions#Restrained|restrained]] creature can repeat the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success. While a creature is restrained in this way, as a bonus action, you can summon thorns to sprout from the vines, and the restrained creature must make a DC 15 CON save, taking 2d4 piercing damage on a failed save and half as much damage on a successful one.

***Spells.*** While holding the wand, as an action, you can expend some of its charges to cast one of the following spells (save DC 15): *[[1st-Circle Spells#Entangle|entangle]]* (1 charge) and *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Spike Growth|spike growth]]* (2 charges).

Part of [[Rods, Staffs & Wands]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
