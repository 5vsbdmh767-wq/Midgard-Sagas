*Wand, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 4,400 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires attunement]] by a spellcaster)*

This rod is made of blackened, desiccated skin shrunk tightly around a humanoid forearm and hand. This wand has 5 charges and regains 1d4 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn.

***Withering Cold.*** While holding it, you can use an action to expend 1 charge causing a shadowy ray to streak from one of the rod’s fingertips toward a creature you can see within 60 feet of yourself. The target must succeed on a DC 15 CON save or for 1 minute, it takes an additional 2d6 necrotic damage whenever it takes cold damage. At the end of each of the target’s turns, it repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success.

***Cast Spell.*** You can cast *[[Cantrips#Ray of Frost|ray of frost]]* from it using your spell attack modifier. The damage scales with your level.
