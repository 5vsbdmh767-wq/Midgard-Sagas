*Wand, Uncommon 1,000 gp* This wand has 3 charges. While holding it, you can expend 1 charge as an action to cast the *[[1st-Circle Spells#Detect Magic|detect magic]]* spell from it. The wand regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn.

Part of [[Rods, Staffs & Wands]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
