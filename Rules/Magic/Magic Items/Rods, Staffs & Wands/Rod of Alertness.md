*Rod, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] 121,000 gp*

*(Requires Attunement)*

This rod has a flanged head and the following properties.

***Alertness.*** While holding the rod, you have advantage on WIS (Perception) checks and on rolls for initiative.

***Spells.*** While holding the rod, you can use an action to cast one of the following spells from it: *[[1st-Circle Spells#Detect Evil and Good|detect evil and good]]*, *detect* *magic*, *[[Rituals 1st–3rd Circle#Detect Poison and Disease|detect poison and disease]]*, or *[[2nd-Circle Spells#See Invisibility|see invisibility]]*.

***Protective Aura.*** As an action, you can plant the haft end of the rod in the ground, whereupon the rod’s head sheds bright light in a 60-foot radius and dim light for an additional 60 feet. While in that bright light, you and any creature that is friendly to you gain a +1 bonus to AC and saves and can sense the location of any [[Conditions#Invisible|invisible]] hostile creature that is also in the bright light. The rod’s head stops glowing and the effect ends after 10 minutes, or when a creature uses an action to pull the rod from the ground. This property can’t be used again until the next dawn.

Part of [[Rods, Staffs & Wands]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
