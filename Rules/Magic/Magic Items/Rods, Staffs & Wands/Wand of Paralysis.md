*Wand, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 4,400 gp*

*(Requires Attunement by a Spellcaster)*

This wand has 7 charges. While holding it, you can use an action to expend 1 of its charges to cause a thin blue ray to streak from the tip toward a creature you can see within 60 feet of you. The target must succeed on a DC 15 CON save or be [[Conditions#Paralyzed|paralyzed]] for 1 minute. At the end of each of the target’s turns, it can repeat the save, ending the effect on itself on a success.

The wand regains 1d6 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the wand’s last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the wand crumbles into ashes and is destroyed.

Part of [[Rods, Staffs & Wands]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
