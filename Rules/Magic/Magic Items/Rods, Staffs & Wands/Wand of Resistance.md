*Wand, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Common]] 50 gp*

This slim, wooden wand is topped with a small, spherical cage, which holds a pyramid-shaped piece of hematite. The wand has 3 charges. While holding it, you can use an action to expend 1 of its charges to cast the *[[Cantrips#Resistance|resistance]]* spell. The wand regains all expended charges at dawn. If you expend the wand's last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the wand transforms into a *[[Wand of Guidance|wand of guidance]]*.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Huecambra Gem

Part of [[Rods, Staffs & Wands]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
