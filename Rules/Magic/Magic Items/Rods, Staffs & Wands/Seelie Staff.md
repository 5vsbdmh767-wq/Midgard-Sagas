*Staff, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 16,050 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]] by a [[Druid]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Warlock]], or [[Wizard]])*

This white ash staff is decorated with gold and tipped with an uncut crystal of blue quartz. This staff can be wielded as a magic quarterstaff. While holding the staff, you have advantage on CHA checks made to influence or interact socially with fey creatures.

The staff has 10 charges for the following properties. The staff regains 1d6 + 4 charges daily at dawn. If you expend the last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the staff dissolves into a shower of fragrant flower petals, which blow away in a sudden wind.

***Rebuke Fey.*** When you hit a fey creature with a melee attack using the staff, you can expend 1 charge to deal an extra 2d6 radiant damage to the target. If the fey has an evil alignment, the extra damage increases to 4d6, and the target must succeed on a DC 15 WIS save or be [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]] of you until the start of your next turn.

***Spells.*** While holding this staff, you can use an action to expend 1 or more of its charges to cast one of the following spells from it, using your spell save DC and spellcasting ability: *[[1st-Circle Spells#Charm|charm]]* (1 charge), *[[4th-Circle Spells#Conjure Woodland Beings|conjure woodland beings]]* (4 charges), *[[1st-Circle Spells#Disguise Self|disguise self]]* (1 charge), *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Pass Without Trace|pass without trace]]* (2 charges), or *[[5th-Circle Spells#Tree Stride|tree stride]]* (5 charges). You can also use an action to cast the *[[Cantrips#Vicious Mockery|vicious mockery]]* cantrip from the staff without using any charges.

> [!note] Seelie Staff in Midgard
> Elves and fey treat the bearer of a seelie staff as an envoy or defender of the Summer Court of [[Silendora]], with respect and some fear. They assume the court gave it to you. If they learn otherwise, they may try to take it back for their masters, or report the thief so justice can follow later. %% source: Vault of Magic (paraphrased sidebar) %%
