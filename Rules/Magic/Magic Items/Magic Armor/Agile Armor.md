*[[Armor|Armor]] (Any Medium or Heavy), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Common]]* %% price pending %%

This magically enhanced armor is less bulky than its nonmagical version. While wearing a suit of medium agile armor, the maximum DEX modifier you can add to determine your AC is 3, instead of 2. While wearing a suit of heavy agile armor, the maximum DEX modifier you can add to determine your AC is 1, instead of 0.

Part of [[Magic Armor]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
