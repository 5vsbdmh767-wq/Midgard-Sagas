*[[Armor|Armor]] (Shield), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] 30,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

While wielding this shield, you can speak its command word as a bonus action to cause it to animate. The shield leaps into the air and hovers in your space to protect you as if you were wielding it, leaving your hands free. The shield remains animated for 1 minute, until you use a bonus action to end this effect, or until you are [[Conditions#Incapacitated|incapacitated]] or die. Then the shield falls to the ground or flies into your hand if you have one free.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Creator’s Essence · Whirling Gizmo · Abnormal Leather

Part of [[Magic Armor]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
