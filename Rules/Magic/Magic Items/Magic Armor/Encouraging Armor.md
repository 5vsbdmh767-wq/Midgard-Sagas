*[[Armor|Armor]] (Light, Medium, or Heavy), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])* %% price pending %%

While wearing this armor, you gain a +1 bonus to AC. In addition, each friendly creature within 10 feet of you that can see you gains a +1 bonus to attack rolls and saves. If you are a paladin with the Aura of Courage feature, this bonus increases to +2.

Part of [[Magic Armor]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
