*[[Armor|Armor]] (Any medium or heavy), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 4,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires attunement]])*

While wearing this armor, you aren’t [[Conditions#Unconscious|unconscious]] when you drop to 0 HP. You still make death saves normally. You can’t use the Help action to make a Medicine check to stabilize yourself.

Part of [[Magic Armor]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
