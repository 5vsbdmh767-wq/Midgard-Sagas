*[[Armor|Armor]] (Any Medium or Heavy), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 5,000 gp + base armor cost*

While wearing this armor, as a reaction to being hit by a melee attack, the attacker must succeed on a DC 14 CON save or take bludgeoning damage equal to half the damage dealt to you.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Abnormal Leather · Eruptive Sac

Part of [[Magic Armor]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
