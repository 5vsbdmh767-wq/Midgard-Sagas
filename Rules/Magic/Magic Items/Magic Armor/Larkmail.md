*[[Armor|Armor]] (Chain Mail), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 16,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

While wearing this armor, you gain a +1 bonus to AC. The links of this mail have been stained to create the optical illusion that you are wearing a brown-and russet feathered tunic. While you wear this armor, you have advantage on CHA (Performance) checks made with an instrument.

In addition, while playing an instrument, you can use a bonus action and choose any number of creatures within 30 feet of you that can hear your song. Each target must succeed on a DC 15 CHA save or be [[Conditions#Charmed|charmed]] by you for 1 minute. Once used, this property can’t be used again until the next dawn.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Imitative Vocal Cords · lark feathers

Part of [[Magic Armor]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
