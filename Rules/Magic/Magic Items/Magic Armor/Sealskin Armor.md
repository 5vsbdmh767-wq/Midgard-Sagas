*[[Armor|Armor]] (Any light), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 2,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires attunement]])*

You have a swimming speed equal to your normal speed when wearing this armor. Also, you have advantage on any checks and saves to avoid or end being [[Conditions#Grappled|grappled]].

Part of [[Magic Armor]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
