*[[Armor|Armor]] (Leather or Hide), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])* %% price pending %%

While wearing this armor festooned with thin draconic scales, you gain a +1 bonus to AC. You can use the scales as a melee weapon while wearing the armor. You have proficiency with the scales and deal 1d4 slashing damage on a hit (your STR modifier applies to the attack and damage rolls as normal). Swarms don’t have resistance to the damage dealt by the scales. In addition, if a swarm occupies your space, you can attack with the scales as a bonus action.

Part of [[Magic Armor]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
