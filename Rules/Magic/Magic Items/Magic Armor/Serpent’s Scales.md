*[[Armor|Armor]] (Scale Mail), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 8,040 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

While wearing this armor made from the skin of a giant snake, you gain a +1 bonus to AC, and you have resistance to poison damage.

While wearing the armor, you can use an action to cast *[[4th-Circle Spells#Polymorph|polymorph]]* on yourself, transforming into a giant poisonous snake. While you are in the form of a snake, you retain your INT, WIS, and CHA scores. In addition, you don’t need to maintain concentration on the spell, and the transformation lasts for 1 hour, until you use a bonus action to revert to your normal form, or until you drop to 0 hit points or die. The armor can’t be used this way again until the next dawn.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Abnormal Leather · giant snake skin

Part of [[Magic Armor]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
