*[[Armor|Armor]] (Light, Medium, or Heavy), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 5,000 gp + armor base cost ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

You are resistant to one type of damage while you wear this armor. The GM chooses the type or determines it randomly from the options below.

**Armor of Resistance Damage Type**

| d10 | Damage Type | d10 | Damage Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Acid | 6 | Necrotic |
| 2 | Cold | 7 | Poison |
| 3 | Fire | 8 | Psychic |
| 4 | Force | 9 | Radiant |
| 5 | Lightning | 10 | Thunder |

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Abnormal Leather · Elemental Gemstone

Part of [[Magic Armor]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
