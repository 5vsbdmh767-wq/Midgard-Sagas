*[[Armor|Armor]] (Padded), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 2,020 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

This padded black armor is fashioned in the furtive style of shinobi shōzoku garb. You have advantage on DEX (Stealth) checks while you wear this armor.

***Darkness.*** While wearing this armor, you can use an action to cast the *[[2nd-Circle Spells#Darkness|darkness]]* spell from it with a range of 30 feet. Once used, this property can’t be used again until the next dawn.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Shadowporting Blood

Part of [[Magic Armor]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
