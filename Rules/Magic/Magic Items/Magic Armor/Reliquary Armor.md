*[[Armor|Armor]], [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 20,000 gp ([[Using Magic Items#Attunement|Requires Attunement]])*

A religious relic was embedded in this armor as part of the crafting process. While wearing this armor, you gain a +1 bonus to AC and you know Celestial. You have advantage on saves against curses while attuned to it, and you can cast *[[3rd-Circle Spells#Remove Curse|remove curse]]* on any other creature once. This effect recharges at dawn.

If the GM deems that the relic is sacred to your religion, you can double your PB for saves when you call out loudly to your deity for help as a reaction. You can use this effect once until it recharges at the next dawn.

Part of [[Magic Armor]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
