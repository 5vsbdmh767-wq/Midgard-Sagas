*[[Armor|Armor]] (Leather), [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Common]]* %% price pending %%

This suit of leather armor has a shiny, greasy look to it. While wearing the armor, you have advantage on ability checks and saves made to escape a grapple. In addition, while squeezing through a smaller space, you don’t have disadvantage on attack rolls and DEX saves.

Part of [[Magic Armor]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
