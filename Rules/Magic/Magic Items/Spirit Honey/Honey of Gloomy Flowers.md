*Potion, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]]* %% price pending %%

This spirit honey is made from flowers growing in the [[The Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]], in areas where the veil between the Material Plane and the Shadow Realm is thin, or in areas infused with shadow magic. When you consume this honey, you can see normally in darkness, both magical and nonmagical, to a distance of 60 feet. In addition, you can use an action to force shadows to cover the eyes of a creature you can see within 60 feet of you. The target must succeed on a DC 15 CON save or be [[Conditions#Blinded|blinded]] until the end of its next turn. You can’t blind another creature in this way again until you finish a short or long rest.

***Insight.*** At the end of the spirit honey’s duration, the terrain in a 150-foot cube around you changes to your liking. This works like the *[[Rituals 4th–6th Circle#Hallucinatory Terrain|hallucinatory terrain]]* spell, except its duration is equal to the number of hours you spent under the effects of this spirit honey.

Part of [[Spirit Honey]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
