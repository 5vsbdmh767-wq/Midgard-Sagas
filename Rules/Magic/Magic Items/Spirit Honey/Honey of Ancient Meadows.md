*Potion, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 650 gp*

This spirit honey is made from flowers found in ancient magical forests and meadows that have seen little to no humanoid traffic or influence. When you consume this honey, you are immune to the [[Conditions#Charmed|charmed]] condition, and you have advantage on CHA checks to interact socially with fey for the duration. In addition, you have blindsight out to a range of 30 feet while you are in contact with bare ground or plant life.

***Insight.*** At the end of the spirit honey’s duration, you can name or describe a person, place, or object and gain a brief summary of the significant lore about the thing you named. This works like the *[[5th-Circle Spells#Legend Lore|legend lore]]* spell, except the thing you name doesn’t have to be of legendary importance.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: no monster parts; spirit honey from flowers in ancient meadows.

Part of [[Spirit Honey]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
