*Potion, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Very Rare]] 1,500 gp*

Favored by intelligent undead, this rich stock is brewed from the flesh and bones of humanoids and typically kept sealed in an earthenware jar. When you consume this broth, you can reduce your [[Conditions#Exhaustion|exhaustion]] level by up to 2, and you have advantage on saves against disease, poison, and environmental effects for 8 hours. If you are undead, you also regain 3d6 hit points.

Part of [[Fantastical Foods]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
