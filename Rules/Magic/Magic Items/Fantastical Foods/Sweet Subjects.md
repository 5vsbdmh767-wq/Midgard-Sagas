*Potion, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 350 gp*

This aromatic, floral mead is pale yellow and sweet to taste. For 1 hour after drinking this mead, any beast that sees or smells you must succeed on a DC 13 WIS save or be [[Conditions#Charmed|charmed]] by you for the duration. If you or one of your companions harms a beast charmed in this way, the creature is no longer charmed and is immune to the effects of this mead for 24 hours.

Part of [[Fantastical Foods]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
