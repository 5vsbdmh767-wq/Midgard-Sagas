*Potion, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 200 gp*

The intense spice of this stuffed pepper makes your mouth feel as if it is on fire. When you consume this food, you have resistance to fire damage for 1 hour. In addition, once before the duration ends, you can use a bonus action to exhale fire in a 15-foot cone. Each creature in the cone must make a DC 13 DEX save, taking 4d4 fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Breath Pouch (a fire-breathing dragon)

Part of [[Fantastical Foods]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
