*Potion, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 100 gp*

This amber mead is flecked with edible gold and has a sour taste. When you drink this mead, you immediately reduce your [[Conditions#Exhaustion|exhaustion]] level by one. If you don’t have any levels of exhaustion, this mead has no effect. Once consumed, you can’t benefit from golden dawn again until 24 hours have passed.

[[Crafting Magic Items|Made with]]: Celestial Blood

Part of [[Fantastical Foods]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
