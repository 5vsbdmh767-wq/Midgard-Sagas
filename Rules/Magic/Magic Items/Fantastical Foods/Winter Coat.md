*Potion, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Uncommon]] 100 gp*

This syrupy mead is deep brown in color and smells of honeyed ham. When you drink this mead, you sprout a thick, shaggy coat of fur. The fur lasts for 8 hours, after which time it falls off harmlessly. This fur mimics the effects of cold weather clothing, allowing you to tolerate temperatures as low as -50 degrees Fahrenheit without any additional protection.

Part of [[Fantastical Foods]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
