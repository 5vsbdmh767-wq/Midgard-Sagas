*Potion, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]] 350 gp*

Drinking this rich, translucent, umber liquid fills your body with strength and vigor. When you consume this broth, you can reduce your exhaustion level by up to 2, and if you are not (or no longer) exhausted, you have advantage on STR checks and CON saves for 1 hour.

Part of [[Fantastical Foods]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
