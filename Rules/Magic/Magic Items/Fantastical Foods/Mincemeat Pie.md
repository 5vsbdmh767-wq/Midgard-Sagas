*Potion, [[Using Magic Items#Rarity|Rare]]* %% price pending %%

This savory pie has a hint of sweetness. For 8 hours after you consume this food, you gain 5 temporary hit points each time you reduce a creature to 0 hit points. Once you have gained a total of 20 temporary hit points, you can’t gain more temporary hit points from this consumption of mincemeat pie.

Part of [[Fantastical Foods]]. See also [[Magic Items A–Z]] and [[Magic Items by Rarity]].
