During a naval engagement, PCs may choose to act as they would in any other combat. Throwing fireballs or raining down a volley of arrows is effective against regular foes, but note that most engagements begin at (2d6 + 2) x 100 ft., so most character abilities are not in range when engagements commence. PCs may fill a role in the ship’s hierarchy or help as emergencies arise. PCs may choose the order in which a ship’s stations act, with the following shipboard actions available:

#### Attack (Broadside)
**Action Point:** 1
**Crew Position:** Gunner

Attack with primary attack at a target in the ship's Broadside arc. 

#### Attack (Fore/Aft)
**Action Point:** 2
**Crew Position:** Gunner

Attack with secondary attack in the ship's Fore/Aft arc. Ship must be equipped with weapons capable of firing in this arc to use this action. When rolling damage for this attack, roll one dice lower than the primary weapons, if it is the same type, to account for the reduced room. A ship can hold more cannons (or whatever) on it's Broadside than Fore/Aft. 

#### All Stop
**Action Point:** 1
**Crew Position:** Master of Tops

The master of tops attempts a DC 12 DEX (Water Vehicles) check to strike the sails and drop the anchor, bringing the ship’s speed to 0 ft. after it completes its movement this round. If the check fails, the ship continues moving for another round before the anchor catches. This check cannot be attempted in deep water. 

#### Come About
**Action Point:** 1
**Crew Position:** Helmsman

The helmsman attempts a severe change of direction with a DC 15 DEX (Water Vehicles) check, and if successful the ship turns 90° instead of the normal 45°. If the check fails, the ship doesn’t turn at all. This check is made with disadvantage if it crosses against the direction of the wind. 

#### Cut and Run
**Action Point:** 0
**Crew Position:** Boatswain or Captain

The ship is no longer anchored, but will drift until new anchors are acquired. The ship's max speed is reduced to
half for this turn.

#### Disable Station
**Action Point:** 2
**Crew Position:** Gunner

The gunner fires to disable stations on the enemy vessel. This attack is made with disadvantage. If the attack hits, instead of doing damage to the hull, one of the stations is damaged according to the ==Disable Station Effects== table. 1d6 crew members in that area are unconscious and dying. Characters in the area take half the damage unless they succeed on a DEX saving throw with a DC equal to the enemy gunner’s roll to hit, in which case they take one-quarter of the damage done by the attack.

#### Escape a Grapple
**Action Points:** 2
**Crew Position:** Boatswain

The boatswain leads a number of crew in cutting lines that tie their vessel to another. By making a successful DC 13 CHA (Intimidation or Persuasion) check and working with a number of crewmembers equal to one per 10-ft. length of the ship, the ropes are cut and the vessel may maneuver. It takes an additional round to complete for every two crew members below the required number. If the check fails, the action takes an additional round to complete.

#### Evasive Maneuver
**Action Points:** 2
**Crew Position:** Helmsman

The helmsman makes an INT (Water Vehicles) check contested by the enemy helmsman. If successful, enemy gunners have disadvantage to hit the ship. 

#### Force Position
**Action points:** 2
**Crew Position:** Helmsman

The helmsman makes an INT (Water Vehicles) check opposed by the enemy helmsman to force the enemy into a disadvantageous position. If successful, the enemy ship has disadvantage on their WIS (Water Vehicles) check to determine initiative next round. If the helmsman fails, the enemy gains advantage to the check next round. 

#### Full Speed Ahead 
**Action Points:** 2
**Crew Position:** Master of Tops

The master of tops uses their action to adjust the sails and gain a burst of speed. The ship may Dash this turn. If the ship is powered by oars, the rowers (make one roll for the group) must succeed on DC 12 CON saving throw or gain a level of exhaustion. 

#### Grapple
**Action Points:** 2
**Crew Position:** Boatswain

The boatswain leads a number of crew in throwing grappling hooks onto an enemy vessel within 5 feet and tying the ships together. While tied, neither ship may move or maneuver. This action requires a successful DC 13 CHA (Intimidation or Persuasion) check and a number of crewmembers equal to one per 10-ft. length of the enemy ship. It takes an additional round to complete for every two crew members below the required number. If the check fails, the action fails, and the other ship may move normally. Jumping from one grappled ship to another requires a successful DC 10 STR (Athletics) check. If this check is failed by 5 or more, the sailor falls into the water

#### Hold Her Steady
**Action Points:** 1
**Crew Position:** Helmsman

The helmsman holds the ship steady, giving its gunnery crews advantage to hit one enemy ship, but all enemy ships gain advantage to hit their ship. 

#### Motivate 
**Action Points:** 1
**Crew Position:** Boatswain or Captain

By shouting orders to the crew and succeeding on a DC 13 CHA (Intimidation or Persuasion) check, the officer gives advantage on a specific check made before the end of this round. 

#### Patch
**Action Points:** **All** for 2d6 hull patch, **2** for 1d6 hull patch, **1** to for a hull patch equal to the carpenters proficiency bonus. 
**Crew Position:** Carpenter

The carpenter leads a damage repair crew of four others to put in a temporary patch on the hull or repair a damaged station. A successful WIS (Carpenter’s Tools) restores 2d6 hit points to the ship or restores a station to working order. This action can only be used once per hole or damaged station. The DC to restore hit points to the ships is 11 + 2 per hole the ship has suffered, and 15 to repair a station. It takes an additional round to complete this repair for every two crew members below the five required.

#### Ram
**Action Points:** All
**Crew Position:** Helmsman

If moving with the wind, the helmsman can attempt to ram another vessel by making a successful DEX (Water Vehicles) check against the other ship’s AC. If successful, roll 1 hit die + distance traveled vs opponents hit die. If the ship is equipped with a ram, it takes only half damage. Both ships become hopelessly grappled and cannot be separated without several minutes of work. If the attack missed, the ships graze and both take one quarter damage. The Ram shipboard action cannot be made against the direction of the wind. 

#### Sheer
**Action Points:** 2 AP
**Crew Position:** Helmsman

Only effective against ships with oars, the helmsman brings their vessel within 5 feet of the enemy vessel, sheering off all the oars on one side of the enemy vessel, bringing its speed to 0 ft. for three rounds until new oars can be deployed. In order to succeed, the helmsman must make a successful DEX (Water Vehicles) check against the other ship’s AC.

#### Weighing Anchor
**Action Point:** All
**Crew Position:** Master of Tops

The crew raises the ship's anchor(s). The ship's max speed is reduced to half for this turn.
