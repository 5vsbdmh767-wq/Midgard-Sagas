Roll double the damage dice and reroll 1's. Critical Hits cause [[Injuries]].

When using the [[Called Shot]] rule, a crit can cause particularly devastating injuries, such as the loss of an eye, or the loss of a hand. The attack must be declared a called shot, and the location decided, before the crit is rolled. 

For example: A player calls a Called Shot on a creatures hand/arm, adding a +2 to the monsters AC for the attack. If the attack hits normally, the attack inflicts the "Contused Arm Bone" injury. However, if the player rolls a natural 20, a critical hit, the attack will inflict the "Destroy Arm/Hand" injury, removing, or otherwise damaging the limb in a way that makes it unusable until appropriately healed, usually with the regenerate spell.
