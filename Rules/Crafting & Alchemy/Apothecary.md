*Alchemists' Association · Restorative Mixtures*

> [!info] Overview
> Apothecaries focus on **restorative mixtures** — healing potions, antidotes, and antitoxins. These alchemists are among the most selfless and widely respected. During a crisis affecting public health, apothecaries are expected to work to combat or manage the situation, even expending their own resources.

---

## Core Principles

> [!note] Apothecary Principles
> **Altruistic Aid** — Help any who ask, regardless of alignment or affiliation. So long as terms are agreed to and payment received, aid will be given.
>
> **Do No Harm** — An apothecary vows to not create any alchemical substance with the intent to harm another living thing.
>
> **Natural Protection** — When nature itself comes under serious threat, apothecaries are the first to respond. Many are caretakers of the natural world.

---

## Discipline & Expert Ability

> [!tip] Mental Adaptability (All Alchemists)
> When making an ability check to craft an alchemy recipe, you may use **Intelligence** or **Wisdom**.

> [!tip] Preservative Practices (Expert Rank)
> Ingredients you collect or purchase, as well as items you create using an apothecary recipe, **lose the *delicate* property**. If you fail in the attempt to make an item from a delicate recipe, you still lose ingredients as normal.

## Crafting Mechanics

### Time Reduction (Rush)
- **−25% time:** +2 to crafting DC
- **−50% time:** +5 to crafting DC *(disadvantage if at equal rank)*

### On Failure
If you fail the ability check, **only the first ingredient** is consumed. Other ingredients are preserved. You may reattempt after 1 day.

### Alternative Creation
If the item is of lower rank than you, or you have ample time to reattempt, the GM may rule you **automatically succeed** — but you must still choose a source for enchanting recipes.

### Ingredient Properties — Failure Effects
| Property | Failure Consequence |
|---|---|
| Corrosive | 1d6 acid damage |
| Delicate | All ingredients destroyed |
| Flammable | 1d6 fire damage |
| Poisonous | Poisoned until long rest |
| Psychedelic | Incapacitated until long rest |
| Resonant | Noise draws nearby creatures |
| Sharp | 1d6 piercing damage |
| Staining | Auto-fail to hide attempt |
| Stupefying | Stunned until long rest |
| Volatile | 1d6 force damage |
