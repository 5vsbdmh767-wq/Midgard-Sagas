%% house rule: magic item ingredients, 24 Nov 2026 %%

Every magic item that can be crafted needs **key ingredients**: parts harvested from monsters (see [[Monster Harvesting]]). An item's page lists them on its "Made with" line, main ingredient first. You still need the knowledge, the time and the coin to make the item. The ingredients are what carry its magic.

## How Many, and From What

| Rarity | Key ingredients | Main ingredient comes from | Harvest DC of that creature |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common | 1 | any creature | 12 or higher |
| Uncommon | 1 | a CR 5 or higher creature | 13 or higher |
| Rare | 2 | a CR 9 or higher creature | 14 or higher |
| Very Rare | 3 | a CR 13 or higher creature | 15 or higher |
| Legendary | 3 | a CR 17 or higher creature | 16 or higher |

Only the main ingredient has to come from a creature that strong. The others can come from any creature that produces them.

Consumables take one fewer ingredient, to a minimum of one. That covers potions, ammunition, foods, scrolls and anything else used up when it's used.

Some items come in versions of different rarity. Their pages list the ingredients in order of importance; use as many as the version's rarity calls for.

## Reading a "Made with" Line

- A capitalized name is a specific harvested material, such as Voltaic Organ or Abnormal Leather. Each comes from a particular kind of creature and has uses of its own.
- A plain description, such as *dragon bone* or *Fey hair*, means any harvest of that kind from a fitting creature.
- A creature or place in brackets narrows it down, as in *Elemental Gemstone (fire)*.
- A few items are made from something that isn't a monster, such as a flower or a traded memory. Their line says so.
- Artifacts and fabled items can't be crafted.

## Who Can Work Them

The more harvested materials an item holds, the better its maker has to be. A capable craftsman can work one into an item. Two take a master. Three is legendary work.

The key ingredients are needed on top of the item's usual cost. The cost savings for harvested materials described in [[Monster Harvesting]] still apply where that page says they do.
