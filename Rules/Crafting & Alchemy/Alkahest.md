*Alchemists' Association · Destructive Mixtures*

> [!info] Overview
> Alkahests focus on **destructive mixtures** full of entropy and disruption. Typical products are poisons, corrosives, explosives, and flammables. More than any other alchemist, alkahests deal in dangerous substances — and hold themselves accountable for it.

---

## Core Principles

> [!note] Alkahest Principles
> **Considered Clientele** — These alchemists hold themselves responsible for vetting whether a potential client is someone the association wishes to do business with.
>
> **Paranoia with Purpose** — Never be complacent when handling or storing any materials or creations.
>
> **Unwavering Response** — The reputation of alkahests is the most fragile of the three alchemy schools. Any problems, especially those of a public nature, must be addressed with care, precision, and speed.

---

## Discipline & Expert Ability

> [!tip] Mental Adaptability (All Alchemists)
> When making an ability check to craft an alchemy recipe, you may use **Intelligence** or **Wisdom**.

> [!tip] Careful Process (Expert Rank)
> If you fail an ability check to create an item using an alkahest recipe, you **ignore damage** from volatile ingredients or recipes.

## Crafting Mechanics

### Time Reduction (Rush)
- **−25% time:** +2 to crafting DC
- **−50% time:** +5 to crafting DC *(disadvantage if at equal rank)*

### On Failure
If you fail the ability check, **only the first ingredient** is consumed. Other ingredients are preserved. You may reattempt after 1 day.

### Alternative Creation
If the item is of lower rank than you, or you have ample time to reattempt, the GM may rule you **automatically succeed** — but you must still choose a source for enchanting recipes.

### Ingredient Properties — Failure Effects
| Property | Failure Consequence |
|---|---|
| Corrosive | 1d6 acid damage |
| Delicate | All ingredients destroyed |
| Flammable | 1d6 fire damage |
| Poisonous | Poisoned until long rest |
| Psychedelic | Incapacitated until long rest |
| Resonant | Noise draws nearby creatures |
| Sharp | 1d6 piercing damage |
| Staining | Auto-fail to hide attempt |
| Stupefying | Stunned until long rest |
| Volatile | 1d6 force damage |
