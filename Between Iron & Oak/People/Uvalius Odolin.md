*Clockwork mage of [[Zobeck]], deceased.*

A geomancer from [[Magocracy of Allain#Bemmea|Bemmea]], a specialist in ley magic who came to [[Zobeck]] to learn clockwork magic, and a minor, part-time lecturer at the [[Arcane Collegium]]. His colleagues thought him a mediocre academic. He was a homebody who tinkered day and night in the workshop of his house, a small stone tower near the Collegium, and he could be counted on to forget a lecture and to leave something valuable lying on a table. Most evenings he had a pint at the Hedgehog with his friends Ovras and Friganis.

##### His Death
Uvalius was killed in his own house by his own creations, when a surge of ley magic sent them haywire. A memory gear in the house named his killer: [[Shurnas Kradore]], who admitted manipulating the ley node but claimed he meant only to ruin Uvalius, not kill him. His friends at the Hedgehog put out a notice asking for someone to find out what happened, and his colleagues at the Collegium started circling before the body was found.

The same surge woke M3L0-D.
