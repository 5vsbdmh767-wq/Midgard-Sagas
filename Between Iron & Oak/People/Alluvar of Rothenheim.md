*Griffon Knight-Commander, Marshall of the Margreve, commander of the Griffon Court.*

An elf of the old empire, with long black hair, green eyes, and a faint, knowing smile, who wears crystal plate and rides a great black griffon. He commands the [[Griffon Court]] and is at least an honorary general of the Fifth Legion, the Flying Fifth, also called the Griffon Guard, which once trained the elves' knights and captains at the Court.

##### What Folk Say
Alluvar is believed to be the only one who still knows the command words and holds the keys to the sealed tunnels of the Flying Fifth. His wife [[Xendra]], his son [[Ollivary]], and his squire Fillibera of Liadmura round out the garrison. See [[Griffon Court]].

##### With the Party
He came down on his black griffon to meet the party on the far side of the [[Rushfens]] and walked them in himself. The briar wall opened for him without a sound and closed behind them. In the hall he told them they smelled bad, had food brought, and then had the ledger brought out: every surfacing of the [[The Crumbling Tomb|Crumbling Tomb]] since the elves left Midgard, more than four hundred of them. Only once before had anyone walked out of it. His name was Qeldar.

Before the night was over he called [[Chairo]] "Tender" to her face, then went off on his rounds with the ledger.
