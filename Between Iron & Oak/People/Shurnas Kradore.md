*Scholar of [[Magocracy of Allain#Bemmea|Bemmea]].*

A Bemmean scholar who followed [[Uvalius Odolin]] to [[Zobeck]].

##### With the Party
A memory gear in Uvalius's house named Shurnas as his killer. The party found him at his grotto on the edge of the [[The Margreve|Margreve]], where he admitted manipulating the ley node that killed Uvalius but claimed he only meant to ruin him, not kill him. He refused to answer for it, fought the party, and escaped through his magic circle. His apprentice, [[Arius Azafaris]], was spared.

See [[Story So Far#Death of a Mage|Story So Far]].
