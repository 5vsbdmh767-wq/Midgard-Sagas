*A "family" of fey and others living in the trees at the [[Fingers of Derende]].*

Deep in [[The Margreve]] a mixed band lives in leafy huts built high in the boughs of the trees called the fingers of Derende, joined by walkways with no rails and reached by rope ladders. Folk who live near enough to know of them call them a threat. Each member wears a grim charm made from natural things and dead animals: a brooch of a dead mouse and burned pine cones, a fox skull wrapped in grass, a serpent's spine worn as a headband.

##### Vil'Opon
A dryad, second in command of the family. Her skin is branded with angular glyphs.

She met the party at the Fingers and the meeting ended without a fight. She asked to take [[Chairo]] in, and when the party said no, she let the question stand. She told them Chairo carries "a piece of the tender." When Rozen offered to cleanse the growth spreading through the place, she took it as an insult:

> "This is the forest's natural state, before the gods tamed it."

Lesshy went up to her alone and came back with a book of bark bound in dried vine.

##### With the Party
The family is still waiting on an answer about Chairo, and the party knows it will have to go back. See [[Story So Far#The Fingers of Derende|Story So Far]].
