*The eldest child of [[Incot and Wheedon|Incot]], who travels with the party.*

A red-haired, blue-eyed girl with a confident walk and a wry smirk, the eldest daughter of pig keepers in the hamlet of Incot. When the adults of Incot died in their sleep, she gathered the other children, took what money their parents had, and set out to bring them to their relatives in Wheedon. That is where the party found her.

Things happen around Chairo. The trail got easier once she joined the party, game was easy to find, and the forest seemed to bend around her. When she laid eyes on [[Eagehrt Mathye]] at Wheedon, the whole wood went still.

##### With the Party
When the other children were settled in Wheedon, Chairo stayed with the party. [[Family Starless|Vil'Opon]] of Family Starless said she carries "a piece of the tender" and asked to take her in. [[The Sage of the Tomb|The sage in the Crumbling Tomb]] took one look at her and asked what she was. At the [[Griffon Court]], [[Alluvar of Rothenheim|Alluvar]] called her "Tender" to her face. She didn't react.
