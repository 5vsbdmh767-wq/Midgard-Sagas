*Guildmaster of the [[Arcane Collegium]], [[Zobeck]].*

A clockwork mage, consul, and member of the Free City Council. See [[Zobeck]] for more on him.

##### With the Party
As the party prepared to leave Zobeck, Orlando examined the Ley Line Resonator they had recovered from [[Uvalius Odolin]]'s house, pointed them toward "the Fingers," and briefed them on shadow fey sightings tied to killings on the [[Great Northern Road]].
