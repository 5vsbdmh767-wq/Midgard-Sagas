*Shadow fey huntress.*

A shadow fey with small horns, fangs, and long white hair, who carries a hunting bow. She led a hunting party out of [[The Shadow Realm]] into [[The Margreve]], and by her own account they were there to bring back game for a court feast.

##### With the Party
Her hunters were after [[Eagehrt Mathye]], who had killed some of them, and the party crossed her trail while dealing with him. The party fought her and her hunters. Her hunters were killed; Suzenga turned invisible and got away, and the shadow fey know what the party did.
