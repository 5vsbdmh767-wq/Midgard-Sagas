*A shade riding the [[The Crumbling Tomb|Crumbling Tomb]].*

The shade of a Nurian scholar-priest, dead some six centuries. He has ridden the tomb across the forest ever since, and he does not know how long it has been. The party did not tell him that the elves have gone.

Listening to him costs something. Lesshy paid for it.

##### What He Told the Party
Hedging, he said that Derende is not entirely dead, and guessed at some relation to the Green Walker of Kush. He was more certain that "the Tender" is a title and not a name: it describes a job, and a job that exists has someone doing it.

He noticed [[Chairo]] and asked straight out what she was. The party told him what they knew.
