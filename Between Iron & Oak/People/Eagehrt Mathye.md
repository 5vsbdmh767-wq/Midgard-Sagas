*Noble of [[Zobeck]], deceased.*

Arrogant, selfish, and very sure of his own importance. He came into [[The Margreve]] with a retinue of guards, looking for a rare root called collybiscus, and he hated every day he spent there.

##### Incot and Wheedon
In the hamlets of [[Incot and Wheedon]] he offered the villagers a gold piece and a good meal for a little of their blood, and in a hungry year they took it gladly. The people of Wheedon thanked him for it. At Incot, every adult went to bed one night and did not wake up.

##### With the Party
The party brought the children of Incot to Wheedon as Eagehrt was leaving it. After a tense standoff at Wheedon, the party killed him before he could use the blood he had collected. Frost bloomed over his body and consumed it, and the marks on the villagers who had sold him blood burned out, leaving frostbite scars. The party learned the collybiscus was meant for a divination ritual. It was never collected.
