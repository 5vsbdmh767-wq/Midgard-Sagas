*Treant, owner of the [[Great Northern Road#The Bluebell|Bluebell Coaching Inn]] on the Great Northern Road.*

The elves who built the Bluebell long ago left it in the keeping of a gnarled, oak-like treant, and Leafhair has kept it ever since. Few travelers know the inn has an owner at all. Under him it has become a meeting place for the folk of the deep woods: alseids, pineys, bearfolk, sprites, gnomes, and stranger things, and humans in the taproom are often outnumbered.

He rarely shows himself except in winter. Through the growing season he lives in a patch of deep forest near the inn that is overgrown with violets, and travelers are warned to leave that patch alone. After the first frost he comes in and settles in a corner of the taproom, where he is sometimes mistaken for part of the building. He sings long, mournful songs for the trees that have died and been burned in the hearth. Ask him about forest lore and he will tell you the truth, at length. He disappears for a few days after midsummer and after midwinter every year, and nobody says where.

##### With the Party
Rozen works for him, and it was on Leafhair's business that Rozen was at the Bluebell the night the party first met.
