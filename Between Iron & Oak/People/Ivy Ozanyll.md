*Clockwork mage and lecturer at the [[Arcane Collegium]], [[Zobeck]].*

A short woman with a striking blonde streak through her chestnut hair. Around the Collegium she has a reputation for being cold, precise, and efficient, and for not caring much about anything except where she is going next.

##### With the Party
When word got round that [[Uvalius Odolin]] had stopped answering his door, Ivy was one of the colleagues who came looking for the people sent to his house. She wanted one particular device of his out of it and offered good money for it. As far as she was concerned, the rest of the place could be stripped bare, so long as she got what she was after.

She crossed the party. That has not been settled.
