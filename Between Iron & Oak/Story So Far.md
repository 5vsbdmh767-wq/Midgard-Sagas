### The Bluebell and Levoča
The party first met at the [[Great Northern Road#The Bluebell|Bluebell]], the old coaching inn on the [[Great Northern Road]] where travelers share the taproom with alseids, pineys, bearfolk, and stranger things. Rozen was there on the business of his master, [[Leafhair Rootglum]], the treant who owns the inn. Lesshy was there on ordinary ranger work, stopping for the night on an escort job. Sidious was there for his own reasons.

From there they went to [[Levoča]], a village on the edge of the forest where the animals had turned violent and something was coming to doors at night. It was the Hollow Man, a thing of twisted wood with a scythe called the Harvester, collecting heads for the Singing Tree, an old and sick tree out in the woods that wanted voices to sing with again. The party went up against both the Hollow Man and the Singing Tree.

A villager enlisted them, and Leafhair separately sent Rozen and Lesshy to look into it. The villager mentioned a shadow fey who had passed through a few weeks before. The party rallied the villagers to fight alongside them and tried to reason with the maddened animals rather than kill them. They killed the Hollow Man and burned him, burned the Singing Tree, and kept his sickle, the Harvester. In Levoča they are local legends.

### Death of a Mage
In [[Zobeck]], [[Uvalius Odolin]], a clockwork mage and part-time lecturer at the [[Arcane Collegium]], turned up dead in his house, killed by his own creations when a surge of ley magic sent them haywire. The same surge woke M3L0-D.

His friends Ovras and Friganis had posted a notice at the Hedgehog Tavern. The party went through his house in Broomskill Alley, fought their way past haywire constructs and animated furniture, and found him beaten to death by his own creations, slumped against the construct that would become M3L0-D. A memory gear named his killer: [[Shurnas Kradore]]. They also recovered a brass sphere about the size of a large ball and more than 20 pounds, the Ley Line Resonator, which grew heavier while the furniture was animated.

[[Ivy Ozanyll]], another clockwork mage of the Collegium, wanted something out of Uvalius's house and was willing to pay for it. She offered 500 gp for his regulation device. The party declined, and the offer is still open. She crossed the party.

The party found Shurnas at his grotto on the Margreve's edge. He admitted manipulating the ley node but claimed he meant to ruin Uvalius, not kill him. He refused to answer for it, fought, and escaped through his magic circle. His apprentice, [[Arius Azafaris]], was spared and now works at the Hedgehog. On the way back toward Zobeck, the party killed two invasive thunderboars at the forest's edge, and Allia joined them.

### Leaving Zobeck
The party left through the market, and had a run-in with the Watch at Uvalius's house in Broomskill Alley, where they met [[Vikka]], a ratfolk Watch officer. [[Orlando]], guildmaster of the [[Arcane Collegium]], examined the Resonator, pointed the party toward "the Fingers," and briefed them on shadow fey sightings tied to killings on the [[Great Northern Road]]. Thelamandrine of the Winter Court's embassy separately told Sidious to look into the same reports. The Spyglass Guild let the party know it wants them as contacts in the Margreve.

### The Vengeful Heart
On the [[Great Northern Road]] the party dealt with an owlbear and, improvising, a griffon. In the [[The Margreve|Margreve]] they found the hamlet of [[Incot and Wheedon|Incot]], where every adult had died in their sleep. The children had left on their own, led by the eldest, [[Chairo]], a red-haired pig keeper's daughter, and the party took them to their relatives in Wheedon. On the way, the forest itself seemed to bend around her. At Incot, Lesshy spoke with the plants, which said the adults had all died at the same moment, from something that came from a distance, underground.

The cause was [[Eagehrt Mathye]], a noble from Zobeck travelling with a retinue, who had paid villagers a gold piece for a little of their blood. He was in the forest looking for a rare root called collybiscus. The shadow fey were hunting him, led by [[Suzenga]]. The party fought Suzenga and her hunters; her hunters were killed, and she vanished, invisible, and got away. At Wheedon, after a tense standoff, the party killed Eagehrt before he could use the blood he had collected. Frost bloomed over his body and consumed it. The marks on the villagers who had sold him blood burned out, and they screamed, then lived, left with frostbite scars. His last guard was charmed, tied up, and killed. The party talked the frightened villagers down and learned that the collybiscus was meant for a divination ritual. They set out to keep it from being collected, and it never was.

Chairo stayed with the party.

### Briarblack Hill
On the way, the Resonator went still at [[Western Margreve|Briarblack Hill]] and turned pure white. The party searched the hill thoroughly and found petrified white roots and remains in the ground.

### The Fingers of Derende
At the [[Fingers of Derende]] the same petrified white material stood as actual trees. Lesshy spoke with them, and felt something of the void reach for him. The party met [[Family Starless|Vil'Opon]], a dryad of Family Starless, and the matter was settled without a fight. She asked to take Chairo in. The party refused, but never actually gave her an answer.

When Rozen offered to cleanse the growth spreading through the place, Vil'Opon took offence: "This is the forest's natural state, before the gods tamed it." She told the party that Chairo carries "a piece of the tender." Lesshy went up to her alone and came back with a bark-bound book. The party knows they will have to go back.

### The Crumbling Tomb
The assassin vines in the front half of the tomb nearly killed everyone, and Sidious only pulled through on a natural 20. There was writing in Nurian that no one could read, and the party came away with nothing.

The back half walked the Shadow side, carrying the party with it. Inside was a shade, a Nurian scholar-priest dead some six centuries who has ridden the tomb ever since without knowing how long it has been. Listening to him cost something. Lesshy felt the void take hold and, given the choice between mind and body, chose his body. Whatever came away with him is still there under his shoulder.

[[The Sage of the Tomb|The sage]] told them, hedging, that Derende is not entirely dead, and guessed at some relation to the Green Walker of Kush. He was more certain of one thing: "the Tender" is a title, not a name. It describes a job, and a job that exists has someone doing it. He noticed Chairo and asked straight out what she was, and the party told him what they knew. He spoke only to Sidious, and gave the party a lead toward the records kept by the garrison of the Griffon Court. For most of the crossing the party rode on the tomb's roof, going back inside as it surfaced.

When the tomb surfaced again, it rose in the [[Rushfens]], about a dozen miles below the [[Griffon Court]].

### The Rushfens Crossing
Twelve miles of flooded forest, at about a mile an hour. In the thorn pans [[The Party|Rozen]] got through to a graack, a huge grey wading bird, and the party talked it into helping them against the mudcrawlers that were stalking them. Its scream paralyzed anyone close and deafened the whole party. It killed one mudcrawler and ate it while the party killed the second, then harvested its carapace and viscera. They also came away with a bog oak haft.

At the deep pool someone pulled a knife out of a log, and a phibbit, a small, vain, brightly coloured frog-fey, took it very badly. Allia gave it a flower from her hair, and it forgave them and showed them the way across. They came through unhurt.

On the far side, [[Alluvar of Rothenheim]], Marshall of the Margreve, came down on his black griffon to meet them himself, and walked them in. The great briar wall around the Court opened for him without a sound and closed behind them the same way.

### The Griffon Court
Alluvar led the party up the crag road. In the yard his son [[Ollivary]] was saddling a griffon. In the hall, Alluvar told them they smelled bad, had food brought, and then had the ledger brought out.

The garrison has recorded every time the tomb has surfaced since the elves left Midgard, more than four hundred times. In all of them, someone has walked out of it only once before. The party were the second.

The one before was Qeldar, long ago. Alluvar thought he was an elf but wasn't sure, only that he was something of shadow. Qeldar stayed at the Court and preached about bringing the bright fey and the shadow fey together, two sides of the same coin, no light without shadow. Then he stole something from them and left.

Before the evening was out, Alluvar called Chairo "Tender" to her face. Then he went off on his rounds and took the ledger with him.

The party split up for the night. Allia stayed in the great hall. Lesshy and Rozen went down to the forge, where a halfling smith works, to ask what could be made from the mudcrawler carapace and the storm boar. Sidious went upstairs to a room overlooking the eastern clearing. At dusk nine pilgrims had come up the old road, and now they were beginning some kind of rite out there. Alluvar made little of them.
