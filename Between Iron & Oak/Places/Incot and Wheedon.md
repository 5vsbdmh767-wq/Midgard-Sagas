*Two small hamlets in [[The Margreve]], a day or so apart along the same trail.*

##### Incot
A handful of daub-and-wattle huts with thatched roofs on top of a low hill, with a well, an animal pen, and a small garden. The six families of Incot had little to spare, and going into the last winter they were pooling and rationing what food they had.

Every adult in Incot went to bed one night and did not wake up. The children waited to see whether anyone would come or whether their parents would wake, and then the eldest, [[Chairo]], took what money there was and led them down the trail toward Wheedon, where they had family.

When Lesshy spoke with the plants of Incot, they said the adults had all died at the same moment, from something that came from a distance, underground.

##### Wheedon
The next village along the trail, larger than Incot and with a proper road leading out of it. The children of Incot were taken in by their relatives here.

[[Eagehrt Mathye]] had been through Wheedon too, paying a gold piece and a good meal for a little of each villager's blood, and the people of Wheedon thought him a blessing. He was leaving as the party arrived. The party killed him there after a tense standoff, before he could use the blood. The marks on the villagers who had sold it burned out, and they are left with frostbite scars.
