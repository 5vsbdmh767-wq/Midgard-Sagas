*Groves of strange trees found scattered through [[The Margreve]].*

The fingers of Derende grow in small copses, sometimes two or three together and sometimes a dozen strong. They are tall, spindly trees with pale grey and white bark and great knots along their length, so that the trunks look jointed, and their long, dark leaves curl like overgrown nails. A full-grown finger stands sixty or seventy feet tall. Woodcutters say their roots go down forever and that an axe barely marks them. Wood that does come away is twice as hard as ordinary timber and makes a fine club or staff.

Woodcutters also tell of the two shapes the groves take. In some, one tree grows perfectly straight and points at something in the sky, while the trees around it curl. In others, several trunks grow tangled together like fingers holding something small and delicate, and they shift their grip from day to day as if a wind were blowing that nobody else can feel. Things live in the boughs of the fingers, and living trees gather near them, and the sound of an axe brings them. People who sleep too close to a grove wake up strange.

Nobody can tell you where the name came from.

##### With the Party
At one of these groves the party met Vil'Opon of [[Family Starless]], who live in huts high in the branches. The meeting ended without a fight, and with a question about [[Chairo]] still unanswered. [[The Party|Lesshy]] came down from the trees with a bark-bound book. The party knows it will have to go back.
