*A farming and hunting village on the edge of [[The Margreve]].*

About seventy-five people live in Levoča, farming the cleared ground and hunting the woods behind it. On paper the village is led by Brother Arkadi, a follower of [[Yarila & Porevit|Porevit and Yarila]], though everyone knows he is a weak-willed old man and the village mostly runs itself. A mile or so north, at Dead-Nettle Cottage, lives Mother Molvor, an old herbalist. The villagers are a little superstitious about her and go to her anyway when they need a remedy or good advice.

##### With the Party
The party came to Levoča soon after meeting at the Bluebell. The animals had turned violent, a girl had gone missing, and something was coming to doors at midnight. It was the Hollow Man, a thing of twisted wood carrying a scythe called the Harvester, taking heads for the Singing Tree, an old and sick tree out in the woods that wanted voices to sing with again. The party went up against both. They rallied the villagers to fight with them, tried to reason with the maddened animals, killed and burned the Hollow Man, burned the Singing Tree, and kept the Harvester. In Levoča they are local legends.
