*A wandering tomb of the [[Western Margreve]].*

Covered in lichen and gripped by ivy, the Crumbling Tomb sits in the permanent dusk under the canopy behind a pair of mithral-barred doors. At dawn the ivy drags it down into the earth, leaving only a chip of stone to mark the spot, and at dusk it rises again somewhere else. Most people who hear the story don't believe it. The shadow fey tell a legend that whoever lies inside will one day rise and take a side in some great fight over the forest. Others say the tomb is looking for something.

The garrison of the [[Griffon Court]] has kept a record of every time it has surfaced since the elves left Midgard. In more than four hundred surfacings, only twice has anyone walked out of it.

##### With the Party
The front half of the tomb is guarded by vines that very nearly killed the whole party. Its walls are covered in Nurian writing that none of them could read.

The back half walks the Shadow side, and the party went with it. Inside they found [[The Sage of the Tomb|the sage]], a shade who has ridden the tomb for six centuries. Lesshy came away changed. When the tomb surfaced again it rose in the [[Rushfens]], and the party walked out.
