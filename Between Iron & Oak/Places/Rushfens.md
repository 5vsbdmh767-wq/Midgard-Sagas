*Flooded forest southeast of the [[Griffon Court]], below the Black Hills.*

Miles of drowned woodland, where the great trees cling to low hummocks above two feet of muddy water and the smell of algae, swamp gas, and rot covers everything. Dead boughs choke the muck. In places they wall off pools as still as a winter sky, and in others they funnel the water into channels that run like rapids, deep enough to knock over anyone wading and too deep for anyone small to cross without swimming. The ground changes from week to week. A good day's travel is about a mile an hour.

##### With the Party
The tomb set the party down in the Rushfens, about a dozen miles below the Griffon Court, and they walked out. In the thorn pans they talked a graack, a huge grey wading bird with a deafening scream, into helping them against the mudcrawlers that were stalking them, and spent two hours harvesting what was left. They also came away with a bog oak haft, black and iron-hard from centuries in the water. At a deep pool, pulling a knife out of a log offended a phibbit, and a flower from Allia's hair made it right. On the far side, [[Alluvar of Rothenheim]] came down on his black griffon to meet them and walked them in.
